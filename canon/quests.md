# Active Quests

## Find and disable Starfall / rescue Aeris
- **Given by:** [[Sigil]], [[Crone]], [[Aeris]]
- **Status:** In progress — Funnel route reopening; Emperor Shen has retreated to Starfall
- **Details:** [[Aeris]] is imprisoned inside [[Starfall]], her mortal body aging and weakening. [[Lyngbakr]] repaired the [[Hole in the Sky]], but Starfall and Aeris remain unresolved. [[Fharan]] warned that Starfall's next target is [[Sigil]], the sun. In session 032, [[Emperor Shen]] and [[Vizier Jade]] fled to Starfall after the Broyish capital uprising. [[Courteous Cam]] and [[Otto]] are reopening the [[The Funnel|Funnel]] route toward [[Axis Mundi]]. The party's [[Antumbra]] in the League's supply chain may still sabotage Starfall when fed as fuel.

## Kill the Tome Keeper
- **Given by:** [[Rakshasa]]
- **Status:** In progress
- **Details:** The [[Rakshasa]] (Ceril's father) is trapped at [[The Garden]] by his duty to hunt escaped demons. The [[Tome Keeper]], an [[Arcanoloth]] who holds the [[Tome of Satariel]], can track the Rakshasa's location and could summon other demons to find him. Killing the Tome Keeper would free the Rakshasa to visit his child. [[Tome Keeper's Pyramid]] was located in the eastern waters during session 023.

## Defeat Yalet Aurum

## Stop the Demi-Spell / confront Boral Erendel
- **Given by:** [[Crone]], [[Ceril]] (own initiative)
- **Status:** In progress
- **Details:** [[The Order of Seasons]] is pursuing the [[Demi-Spell]] to reset the world. [[Boral Erendel]] has gone rogue with an eugenics version of the plan — creating thousands of empty high elf forms to reincarnate all spirits into "perfect" bodies. [[Crone]] confirmed there is time to stop the Demi-Spell but warned the party is more likely to die than run out of time. [[Ceril]] proposed capturing or assassinating the [[Rakshasa]] as leverage against [[Keys]] to halt the Demi-Spell entirely.
