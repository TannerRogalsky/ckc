# The Croakborne Carnival

The Croakborne Carnival is a ship flying the flag of [[The Order of Seasons]]. Its significance lies in [[Ceril]]'s earlier service aboard it and his acquaintance with [[Illidrielle Gandara]].

## Ownership and Command

The vessel served The Order of Seasons. Its individual owner, captain, and crew roster are not established.

## Campaign History

Before the party's search for [[Penumbra]], Ceril served aboard The Croakborne Carnival with a half-elf woman. He recognized that former shipmate when she and a tiefling took Penumbra from [[Castle Kaedon]]. The pair were later identified as Illidrielle Gandara and [[Keys Caeradel]] at [[The Academy]].

## Final Status

Its later voyages, commanders, whereabouts, and fate are unrecorded. Its last established affiliation remains The Order of Seasons.
