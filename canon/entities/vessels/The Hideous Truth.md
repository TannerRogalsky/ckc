# The Hideous Truth

The Hideous Truth is a small galley captured from hobgoblin pirates by the party. Initially concealed as a reserve vessel, it later carried the rescuers into [[Broy]] while [[The Opal]] traveled in [[Kerben]]'s magical egg.

## Description

The galley was much smaller than The Opal, roughly a third of its size. When captured, it carried no papers, identifying colors, black flag, or useful log. Its former Fae name referred to the end times. The party's chosen canonical name reflects [[Red Caesar]]'s proposal after the capture.

The first hiding place was a jungled grotto beneath a moon-shaped cape. Canopy extended over the water, and foliage placed over the hull concealed the ship from passing vessels and [[Broyish Empire]] aerial scouts. Vines and undergrowth later strengthened that camouflage while making a relaunch require clearing work.

## Ownership and Command

Hobgoblin pirates operated the galley under a [[Warg Lord]] who remained below decks. [[Ceril]] suspected that they had seized it from earlier owners, but no record identified those people or established their fate.

After the party captured it, the vessel became a reserve for [[Obould]]'s company. The company lacked enough crew to operate it alongside The Opal and the [[Gheister]], so possession did not immediately make it an active third ship. The party later brought it back into use for the Broy rescue.

## Campaign History

Following the boarding attack on The Opal, the party pursued the pirate galley aboard the Gheister. They found the upper deck empty and the Warg Lord below. Kerben killed the Warg Lord, and the party recovered gold from crates thrown overboard by its destructive howl.

The companions debated selling the ship, seeking League assistance, or keeping it. Uncertain earlier ownership troubled Kerben, while Red Caesar argued that salvage rights permitted retaining it. They chose a hidden reserve against future damage to The Opal, renamed it The Hideous Truth, covered it with foliage, and marked the grotto for a return.

On the way to [[The Karat Cake]], the party checked the hiding place. The galley remained there beneath additional vines, which they deliberately left intact rather than clearing it for immediate use.

Red Caesar, Ceril, and Kerben switched to The Hideous Truth to approach Broy without displaying The Opal's familiar hull. Kerben carried The Opal and its entire crew in the egg, preserving it as a possible escape resource. The rescuers landed and entered the capital; their later departure used magical travel rather than a recorded voyage back aboard the galley.

## Final Status

The party last used The Hideous Truth to enter Broy. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is established, so custody remains attributed to the party. Its exact later location and condition are unknown; its earlier concealment in the grotto is a historical state.
