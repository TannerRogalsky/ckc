# Gun Balloon

Gun balloons are armed aerial craft used by the [[Broyish Empire]]. The party captured one during an attack on [[The Opal]] and later used the captured craft in its return to [[Broy]].

## Description

An imperial gun balloon has a hot-air envelope above a cramped gondola bearing imperial markings. Mounted weapons include spike cannons and rotary guns, and soldiers can descend from the gondola onto ships below. The captured specimen was damaged during its seizure but remained usable after initial repairs.

## Ownership and Command

The attacking craft belonged to the Broyish Empire and were operated by imperial pilots and soldiers. [[Red Caesar]] seized one for the party; its capture made it a shared transport resource rather than establishing his individual ownership. The party later abandoned that balloon during its descent into the [[Dawn Palace]].

## Campaign History

Three gun balloons attacked The Opal as the party returned from [[Xarag's Island]]. Imperial soldiers boarded from their gondolas. The party destroyed one balloon, while [[Vokenar]] compelled another pilot to fly north and leave the fight.

Red Caesar entered the remaining gondola and used a resilient sphere to force its occupants out. The disruption tipped burning equipment and interrupted the air supply, bringing the balloon down against The Opal's stern. The party defeated the remaining boarders and retained the damaged craft. Initial work made it usable, though full repairs required supplies from port. The capture offered an alternative to sea travel as the waves grew more dangerous.

On the party’s later return to Broy, Red Caesar, [[Ceril]], and [[Kerben]] ascended over the city in the balloon. Wind magic carried it beyond its ordinary ceiling so they could reach and dispel the illusion hiding the repaired sky. They then abandoned the craft above the Dawn Palace and descended with Ceril's flight and [[Tango]]'s help.

When considering their descent into the [[Gray Wastes]], the companions recalled the captured balloon but could not establish whether they had recovered it after Broy. They used The Opal instead.

## Final Status

The last established use ended with the balloon abandoned over the Dawn Palace. Its possible recovery remained uncertain during the later descent into the Gray Wastes. Final custody, location, and condition are not established; it cannot be treated as a confirmed resource aboard The Opal at campaign end.
