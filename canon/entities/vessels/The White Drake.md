# The White Drake

The White Drake is the principal ship of [[The League of New Stark]], commanded by [[Lady Jacinthe]]. It served as a refuge, diplomatic meeting place, and carrier of the [[Penumbra]] supply the party sabotaged, then hosted Lady Jacinthe and [[Obould]]'s wedding after the final battle.

## Description

The ship bears white and cyan colors. Its permanent teleportation circle opens into a guarded receiving chamber, with an exterior docking point serving arriving travelers. Guest quarters, lounges, laundry service, and a wine cellar support life aboard for visitors, servants, and refugees.

Lady Jacinthe's quarters were protected by guarded approaches, locked doors, and trusted retainers. Art aboard included a portrait of her christening the ship; [[David Harvey]] later hung [[Jack Harvey's Portrait]] there. [[Vokenar]] established the ship as a sanctuary for his magical return journeys.

## Ownership and Command

The White Drake belongs to The League of New Stark and serves as its main ship. Lady Jacinthe commands it, with [[Ema-Tep]] among its leadership. Obould retired from [[The Opal]] to join Lady Jacinthe in leading the League; that change did not establish him as The White Drake's captain.

## Crew and Passengers

- [[Damien Ouranous]] — Served with the League and received visitors aboard. His service ended with his death during the attack on the moon.
- [[David Harvey]] — League associate who placed his family's recovered portrait aboard the ship.
- [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] and [[Ulrich Fjoller]] — Left The Opal to prepare for their child. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian favored The White Drake as a safe home, but the record does not confirm their eventual permanent residence there.

The ship also carried refugees from flooded regions, together with the guards, servants, and household staff needed to sustain that community.

## Campaign History

The party first met Lady Jacinthe aboard The White Drake in the early campaign. Its role as a League meeting place continued when [[Red Caesar]] brought companions through its teleportation circle. Lady Jacinthe offered hospitality and arranged their return to [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]] through Ema-Tep. The visit included a banquet and a private discussion of the League's aims.

During Obould’s captivity, Red Caesar and [[Ceril]] arrived seeking news of their imprisoned captain. Refugees were living aboard, and Damien Ouranous gave the visitors lodging while Lady Jacinthe refused an audience. Ceril investigated her guarded quarters and the ship's household services. A portrait and brandy taken aboard enabled the divination that exposed [[Vizier Jade]]'s coercion of Lady Jacinthe over Obould's survival.

While The White Drake was docked in Broy, locating magic revealed Penumbra hidden in its hold. Kerben boarded invisibly and used [[Zulu]] as a distraction to enter the hold and plant [[Antumbra]] among the stones. The contaminated supply was intended for the Broyish Empire and [[Starfall]]. Its eventual use destroyed Starfall at [[Axis Mundi]], confirming the sabotage's outcome.

Obould retired from The Opal and proposed to Lady Jacinthe aboard The White Drake. She accepted, and they committed to leading the League together. Vokenar gave Jack Harvey's Portrait to David Harvey, who hung it aboard, and made the ship a recall sanctuary. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian and Ulrich Fjoller departed to prepare for their child, considering the ship as a safe home.

Weeks after the final battle, The White Drake followed the changing waters and anchored near the former Lyngbakr Lagoon and [[Castle Kaedon]]. It hosted Obould and Lady Jacinthe's wedding, attended by the surviving adventurers and League associates. Vizier Jade represented Broy, opening the possibility of renewed relations with the League.

## Final Status

The White Drake was last seen as a League ship under Lady Jacinthe's command, hosting the wedding near the former Lyngbakr Lagoon. The record establishes the celebration and diplomatic contact without fixing the ship's later voyages or confirming every proposed resident's future aboard it.
