The tree of all worlds, extending from [[Arkadia]] through [[Stark]] into the [[Gray Wastes]]. Its roots and branches span the planes.

[[Ceril]] first visited Gaokerena in session 032, before learning its name. In Arkadia it appeared from afar as a golden pillar reaching into the heavens. Its trunk combined many kinds of bark and wood, its branches bore many kinds of leaves and fruit, and ivory towers surrounded a forum at its base. [[Sigil]], [[Aeris]], and [[Crone]] gathered there.

In session 036, Ceril recalled the tree while discussing how the planes might connect. Traveling east aboard [[The Opal]], [[Vokenar]] sighted a distant pillar joining the Gray Wastes' ground and sky, with branches and billowing leaves. Sigil identified it to him as Gaokerena, the tree of all worlds. The party had not yet reached its lower reaches.

Gaokerena's relationship to [[Axis Mundi]] was still unconfirmed at the end of session 036. Ceril's earlier return to Stark used a closing wound in Arkadia's ocean basin, away from the tree.

## Session 037

The party reached Gaokerena in the [[Gray Wastes]] and found a broad island of forest and wildlife around its lower trunk. [[Ceril]] learned that his planting to feed [[Aeris]] in session 024 had revived the tree's diminished lower growth. The surrounding vegetation had diversified with extraordinary speed, forming a self-sustaining ecosystem.

[[Crone]] was recovering among the tree's roots in [[Arkadia]], receiving restorative pulses from it. [[Sigil]] left Ceril a message within the tree; he used Transport via Plants to open a passage to Arkadia, and [[Vokenar]] followed. Aeris granted [[Ceril's Star]] there.

The path past Gaokerena led to nearby [[Axis Mundi]] and [[Starfall]], both overgrown by Ceril's plants. The tree and the weapon's site are connected by this forest, but are distinct landmarks.
