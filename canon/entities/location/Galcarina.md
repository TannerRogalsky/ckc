The tree of all worlds, extending from [[Arkadia]] through [[Stark]] into the [[Gray Wastes]]. Its roots and branches span the planes.

[[Ceril]] first visited Galcarina in session 032, before learning its name. In Arkadia it appeared from afar as a golden pillar reaching into the heavens. Its trunk combined many kinds of bark and wood, its branches bore many kinds of leaves and fruit, and ivory towers surrounded a forum at its base. [[Sigil]], [[Aeris]], and [[Crone]] gathered there.

In session 036, Ceril recalled the tree while discussing how the planes might connect. Traveling east aboard [[The Opal]], [[Vokenar]] sighted a distant pillar joining the Gray Wastes' ground and sky, with branches and billowing leaves. Sigil identified it to him as Galcarina, the tree of all worlds. The party had not yet reached its lower reaches.

Galcarina's relationship to [[Axis Mundi]] remains unconfirmed. Ceril's earlier return to Stark used a closing wound in Arkadia's ocean basin, away from the tree.
