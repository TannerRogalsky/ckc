# Mana Sickness

Mana Sickness was a contagious magical illness spreading from the cursed [[Ninki Nanka]] through the [[Mana Sea]] near [[Southport]]. The party ended its continuing source, and Southport had recovered by the campaign's conclusion.

## Description

The illness could affect children, elderly people, experienced spellcasters, and people with no inclination to cast spells. It was not merely a consequence of attempting magic before one was ready.

[[Red Caesar]] described victims' darkened eyes and souls becoming diffuse within the weave. Advanced cases fell into an impenetrable slumber. The contagion's source was a curse that made Ninki Nanka continually radiate a poisonous magical atmosphere.

## Effects and Limitations

[[Deep Roses]] could cure individual victims, but treatment did not prevent reinfection while the source remained. Herbs used for the temporary sickness of inexperienced spellcasters addressed a different problem and were insufficient for this plague.

Removing Ninki Nanka's curse stopped the continuing contagion. That did not establish immediate recovery for everyone already ill; ending the source and treating its victims were separate parts of the solution.

## Campaign History

The party first learned of the plague while helping [[Trent Indorra]] and [[Ebbie Indorra]] in [[Broy]]. Their child was affected, and their search for a cure had led them to obtain unsuitable herbs. The conversation distinguished the contagious illness from a familiar sickness associated with premature spellcasting.

[[Ceril]] later identified Deep Roses as the cure. The recovered flowers reached Southport through [[Father Warrick]] and the freed priests of the [[Temple of Sigil]], alongside the returning Indorra family. Individual cures alleviated the suffering, but the town continued facing reinfection.

At Southport, [[Mayor Yoris]] explained that even non-spellcasters and elderly residents were falling ill. [[Janeera]] identified Ninki Nanka as the source and linked the condition to fallen [[Penumbra]] reacting with the dragon's body. The party found the frightened, suffering dragon in a cave filled with the poisonous atmosphere it emitted.

Ceril removed the curse with Greater Restoration. The poison stopped immediately and the air cleared, ending the continuing source of infection. Mayor Yoris, relying on Janeera's advice, expected the town's full recovery to take months.

## Final Status

Southport had fully recovered by the epilogue following the removal of Ninki Nanka's curse and the treatment of existing victims. The recorded recovery concerns Southport; it does not establish the fate of every possible case elsewhere.
