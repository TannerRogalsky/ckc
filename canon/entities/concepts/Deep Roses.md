Rare herbs that grow exclusively underwater in the [[Mana Sea]]. They cure individual cases of [[Mana Sickness]], and priests freed from the [[Temple of Sigil]] helped supply them to [[Southport]]. The treatment could not prevent reinfection until [[Ceril]] removed the curse from [[Ninki Nanka]].
