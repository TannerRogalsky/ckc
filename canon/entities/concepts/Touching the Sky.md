# Touching the Sky

Touching the Sky is a titan rite of climbing from the mountains to make physical contact with [[Aeris]]'s sky-body. Completed by [[Domyx]] and earlier by [[Emperor Shen]], it connected a tradition of personal ambition with the campaign's conflict over divine power.

## Description

The rite was open to anyone willing to attempt the ascent. What mattered was reaching the sky, rather than merely beginning the climb. Among the titans, the achievement demonstrated exceptional ability and maximized ambition without carrying an inherent moral judgment.

Aeris is the sky itself, so the successful climber physically touches a divine being of the highest power over [[Stark]]. The party's discussion described this as a feat that inhabitants of [[Arkadia]] could not claim. The contact left Domyx's palms permanently sky-blue.

Titan custom permitted a successful climber's descent into Stark as a solitary, final departure from the homeland. Leaving without that achievement was a grave taboo. The custom did not establish that every climber shared the same intentions toward the gods or the world below.

## Campaign History

Domyx completed the rite before descending from the titan mountains. During that descent, [[Ulrich Fjoller]] warned that another blood relative of [[Clan Akathia]] had touched the sky earlier, had been erased from the family's records, and still lived somewhere in Stark.

[[Sigil]] later identified that unnamed ancestor as the party's greatest enemy, perhaps its only great enemy. Domyx described the ancestor as banished from his homeland. He and [[Vokenar]] wondered whether the earlier climber's ambition had extended to reaching Arkadia itself; that original aim remained speculation.

At the [[Dawn Palace]], [[Vizier Jade]] revealed Emperor Shen as Domyx's grandfather. Their matching sky-colored palms and Emperor Shen's acknowledgment of the earlier ascent established him as the hidden relative.

At [[Axis Mundi]], Emperor Shen explained that touching the sky had led him to decide that no later climber should draw power from Aeris. He had used [[Starfall]] to break the sky and sought a divine dynasty through Domyx. These were Emperor Shen's choices, not necessary consequences or moral authority conferred by the rite.

## Final Status

Domyx survived the campaign and returned to [[Thalassia]] after Emperor Shen's defeat. The homeland's houses were sharing authority and its people were beginning to question restrictive traditions. The record does not establish another ascent or a settled new role for Touching the Sky in that changing society.
