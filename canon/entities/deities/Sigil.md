# Sigil

Sigil is the sun goddess of life, energy, and motion, one of the three sisters of the [[Aesir]]. She guided [[Vokenar]], sustained the injured [[Crone]], and survived [[Emperor Shen]]'s intended attack on the sun. At the campaign's end, she welcomed Vokenar back to [[Arkadia]].

## Identity and Background

Sigil is the sister of [[Aeris]] and Crone, and the sun is her physical celestial body. The creation account recalled by the party makes Aeris the eldest sister and creator of the sun and moon; Sigil is the power who created nature and life. Her domain is grounded in the present.

The sisters defeated the [[Vanir]] before the creation of the present heavens. [[Domyx]] recalled that ancient struggle when the party encountered the fallen gods' stone avatars beneath [[Stark]] in [[canon/sessions/session_036#Chunk 0000]].

During Vokenar's long descent from Arkadia, Sigil helped train him outside ordinary time and space. In [[canon/sessions/session_011#Chunk 0000|his training flashback]], she urged patience against Crone's demands for faster progress.

## Personality and Motivations

Sigil values safety, health, and the ethical judgment needed to protect living beings. She defended Vokenar's kindness and morality as essential to his mission: without them, she argued, he might never have accepted the task and could have become a cutthroat pirate.

Her concern for the party continued through the final confrontation. She questioned the wisdom of placing mortals in such danger and urged them to return safely. When Crone proposed renewed training after Vokenar's homecoming, Sigil again asked that he first be allowed to rest.

## Relationships

- [[Aeris]] — Her elder sister, whose faith in unpredictable mortal choices sometimes differed from Sigil's concern about the risks they faced. Together they received Ceril and Vokenar before the final battle.
- [[Crone]] — Her sister and fellow mentor. Their arguments over Vokenar's training did not diminish Sigil's care for her after Starfall wounded the moon.
- [[Vokenar]] — Her pupil and divine champion. She guided his decisions, defended his ethics, and returned him safely to his companions when Crone was attacked. Entropie recognized her power in him.
- [[Ceril]] — A mortal ally whom she received in Arkadia, guided through [[Gaokerena]], and supported in the work surrounding Vokenar's return. His protective shield was a gift from Aeris.
- [[Emperor Shen]] — The enemy who attacked both of her sisters and intended to destroy the sun next. His final shot was prevented before it could be fired.
- [[Entropie]] — A returning Vanir who recognized Sigil as the goddess who had defeated it in the earlier divine conflict.

## Abilities

Sigil governs life, energy, and motion. Her continual Locate Creature awareness lets her perceive living creatures active in Stark and provide practical guidance about where they are. That awareness does not amount to perfect knowledge of everything mortals do: she was surprised by parts of the party's account before the final battle.

When Vokenar contacts her through his mantle, its colored gems become white diamonds and a brilliant sun rises from them for his sight alone. Only he hears her answers and must relay them to his companions. Her true presence in Arkadia could overwhelm Ceril with light until she relieved him of its effects.

Sigil can sustain Crone's recovery through the bond between life and death. She explained that, while one of the younger sisters survives, she can help restore the other. This aid eventually overcame the injury she had initially described as beyond healing. Aeris's power can flow through Sigil into mortal work that fosters life.

## Campaign History

### Guidance for the Sky Mission

In the [[chunks/sessions_001-0010|early campaign notes]], Sigil assured the party that restoring the sky was not hopeless and that they needed to give [[Penumbra]] to [[Lyngbakr]]. Her counsel supported the mission before its method had been demonstrated.

When contacted at [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]], she advised using the shortest favorable route toward the elves first. She revealed that [[The Order of Seasons]] had moved its Penumbra beyond the jungles, confirmed that [[Alamar]] was an ally who would still withhold truths, and said allies would come to the lagoon. She identified Domyx's sky-touching ancestor, later established as Emperor Shen, as the party's greatest enemy. The consultation is recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0004]].

During the later debate over [[Antumbra]], Sigil told Vokenar that [[Obould]] was in less danger than appearances suggested and that [[Vizier Jade]] had further tricks concealed in the situation. She did not explain those tricks or guarantee his safety. Crone then pressed Vokenar to resume training away from the ship.

### The Moon Attack and Its Aftermath

Between worlds, Sigil watched Vokenar's training and disputed Crone's relentless approach. She explained that the Penumbra returned from [[Castle Kaedon]] had strengthened Aeris enough to begin healing herself, while further fragments would hasten the process. Crone objected that delay still allowed new harm.

When Starfall struck the moon, Sigil immediately distinguished Crone's injury from Vokenar's coinciding strike. She tended her sister, helped Vokenar scry Emperor Shen, and remained with Crone while sending him back to the world with a hand on his shoulder and a warning to be safe. [[canon/sessions/session_026#Chunk 0000]] preserves the training and attack.

[[Fharan]] subsequently confirmed that Starfall's next target was the sun. After Lyngbakr repaired the [[Hole in the Sky]], Ceril visited Arkadia and found Sigil devoting her strength to Crone. She explained the sisters' capacity to restore one another, but could not herself return Vokenar from where they stood; Ceril needed to recover his body and work below. Her explanation is recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_032#Chunk 0000]].

### The Threat to the Sun

As the party descended toward the [[Gray Wastes]], Sigil named the vast tree Vokenar sighted as Gaokerena, the tree of all worlds. She later left Ceril a message inviting him through it and received him and Vokenar alongside Aeris in Arkadia. She questioned the danger they faced while Aeris defended trusting their choices. Aeris granted [[Ceril's Star]], and Sigil urged the visitors to return safely.

An imperial captive disclosed that Emperor Shen intended to fire at the sun that day. Emperor Shen delayed his intended shot so Domyx could witness it. That delay allowed the party to reach [[Axis Mundi]], where Antumbra destroyed Starfall before it fired at Sigil. During the battle that followed, Entropie recognized Sigil's power in Vokenar and identified her as its earlier conqueror. The immediate divine threat ended when the resurrected Emperor Shen was destroyed as the last Vanir.

## Final Status

Sigil appeared well in Arkadia with Aeris and the recovered Crone during [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002|Vokenar's homecoming]]. The attack on her sun-body had been prevented, and she was helping [[Ceril]] weave life into the newly exposed lands below.

She welcomed Vokenar home and urged a period of rest before Crone's proposed century of training. His acceptance of that training established their next commitment, without depicting the planned assault on the deeper hells.
