# Crone

Crone is the moon goddess of time, death, and the passage of things, one of the three sisters of the [[Aesir]]. A martial mentor to [[Vokenar]] and defender against demons, she survived the shattering of the moon and recovered to prepare him for a further campaign in the deeper hells.

## Identity and Background

Crone is the sister of [[Aeris]] and [[Sigil]], and the moon is her physical celestial body. The creation account recalled by the party makes Aeris the eldest sister and creator of the moon and sun. The sisters defeated the [[Vanir]] in the ancient struggle for [[Stark]]; the fallen gods' stone avatars remained beneath the world. [[Domyx]] recalled that history when the party encountered them in [[canon/sessions/session_036#Chunk 0000]].

During Vokenar's long descent from [[Arkadia]], Crone trained him outside ordinary time and space. She refined elemental opponents that grew stronger with him and demanded that he fight faster, while her sisters urged greater patience. [[canon/sessions/session_011#Chunk 0000|The training flashback]] records her concern that he would reach the world unprepared.

Her martial presence could be imposing: when summoned under a full moon, she wore chainmail and dragged a greatsword stained with demon blood. Her later frailty reflected the attack on the moon rather than an enduring inability to fight.

## Personality and Motivations

Crone values readiness, discipline, and the protection of the worlds against hostile powers. She presses Vokenar harder than Sigil, distrusts reassurance that might make him complacent, and teaches him never to lower his guard. Her harshness is supported by her training methods and by her insistence that he continue preparing for danger.

Her commitment to restoring Aeris outweighed concern for her own wounds: even while critically injured, she ordered Vokenar to keep his original mission. Her respect for him became more visible as he persisted, and she welcomed his continuing purpose when he returned home.

She condemned mortal ambition that refused to accept limits and doubted the elves would relinquish their desire to witness [[Genesis Mundi]]. That judgment was counsel about their motives, rather than proof that every elf would ultimately choose renewal.

## Relationships

- [[Aeris]] — Her elder sister, formerly her partner in holding demons back at the boundaries between worlds. Crone prioritized Aeris's restoration because it remained the central mission and offered hope for the other divine injuries.
- [[Sigil]] — Her sister and fellow mentor, with whom she disputed the balance between martial pressure and ethical preparation. Sigil remained with her after the moon attack and devoted her power to Crone's recovery.
- [[Vokenar]] — Her warrior pupil. She honed him through elemental trials, demonic battles, and relentless sparring, then renewed their training after his return to Arkadia.
- [[Ceril]] — A mortal ally whose restoration of [[Gaokerena]] provided living growth through which divine help could reach the party. He also defended the possibility of Crone's recovery against claims that the world needed to be remade.
- [[Emperor Shen]] — The attacker who used [[Starfall]] to shatter her moon-body. His violence left her aging, bleeding, and critically weak, but did not extinguish her.
- [[Vanir]] — Ancient rivals of the sisters whose returning manifestations threatened their survival and the worlds they defended.

## Abilities

Crone governs time, death, and the passage of things. The phase of the moon matters when contacting her, though the precise limits were not established. Her influence includes the tides; after recovering, she could help hasten the replenishment of Arkadia's oceans. Her governance of time does not extend into [[Ceril's Star]].

She is a formidable armed combatant and fights demons at the thinning boundaries between worlds. Her training can employ elemental constructs, direct sparring, and even temporary cooperation with demons to test a pupil's vigilance. A crescent-moon image of her also appeared on ceilings during Ceril's Moonbeam.

Her survival is tied to the other sisters' aid. Sigil explained that life and death form a union and that, while one younger sister survives, she can help restore the other. Gaokerena's restorative light also sustained Crone during her recovery.

## Equipment and Resources

- Chainmail and a greatsword — Her armor and blood-stained weapon when [[Vokenar]] called her at [[The Garden]]. He temporarily handled the sword and returned it before she departed. She also carried a bastard sword during his later training.
- **Training sword** — Supplied to [[Vokenar]] for their renewed training in Arkadia. The ending does not identify it as the same weapon she had carried earlier.

## Campaign History

### Counsel and Renewed Training

Crone told the party that a single person had caused [[The Cataclysm]] and achieved the intended result. She was the source of that revelation, rather than the identified perpetrator. The account is preserved in the [[chunks/sessions_001-0010|early campaign notes]]; later evidence linked the attack on the sky to Emperor Shen and Starfall.

At the Garden, Vokenar summoned Crone during a full moon. She arrived exhausted from fighting. To Vokenar, the walls opened onto a shimmering silver moon; his companions could not see her, but her blood soaked the bedding and marked her passage. She warned that the party was more likely to die than run out of time before the [[Demi-Spell]], and explained that they needed enough [[Penumbra]] to restore Aeris's ability to heal rather than every fallen fragment.

She approved considering the [[Rakshasa]] as leverage against the spell's researchers and advised proving strength to [[Farraday]], then offering its life in exchange for help reaching the captive. She judged that the elves would not abandon their desire to witness Genesis Mundi simply because the sky was repaired. These were proposed methods and her assessment of their intentions; they did not establish a completed bargain. Her counsel is recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_015#Chunk 0002]].

Crone later demanded that Vokenar leave [[The Opal]] and resume training for the coming battle. He departed expecting to be away for several weeks. Between worlds, they fought demons together and sparred without respite. Sometimes she joined the demons against him. She accused Sigil of insufficient pressure, while Sigil defended the ethics that had led him to accept his mission.

### The Shattered Moon and Recovery

During a sparring exercise, Vokenar's old Akasha blade passed Crone's defenses just as a much greater injury struck her. White pearlescent blood poured out and her body rapidly aged. Sigil made clear that Vokenar had not caused the wound. His scrying showed Emperor Shen firing Starfall at the moon; [[Kerben]] and Ceril saw its celestial destruction from the ship. The simultaneous scenes are established in [[canon/sessions/session_026#Chunk 0000]].

As Sigil tried to direct Vokenar toward the fallen moon fragments, Crone interrupted. She ordered him to remain focused on restoring Aeris and not waver from the mission. He returned to his companions after Crone slumped into her blood, leaving her critically wounded in Sigil's care.

Lyngbakr's repair of the [[Hole in the Sky]] fulfilled that priority. When Ceril reached Arkadia afterward, Sigil explained why the wounded Crone could still be restored. Ceril later used that assurance to challenge [[Keys Caeradel]]'s belief that losing the moon made another world necessary. He argued for allowing the healing already underway rather than casting the Demi-Spell.

### The World Tree and the Return Home

Approaching Gaokerena, Vokenar contacted Crone and saw her lying among its roots, held in place as restorative light flowed into her. Still weak, she confirmed that the tree was the right destination. She explained that Emperor Shen was delaying his attack on Sigil to confront Domyx, and said the goddesses could not intervene directly in their creation. She directed the party toward help through the tree Ceril had restored. She also suggested that [[Vizier Jade]]'s will would resist Emperor Shen's control, without promising when or how. [[canon/sessions/session_037#Chunk 0000]] records that guidance.

The destruction of Starfall prevented the attack on Sigil. During the ensuing final confrontation, the party entered Ceril's Star and saw the moon beginning to reform as Crone healed. The last returning Vanir was defeated, and Vokenar eventually returned to Arkadia.

## Final Status

By [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002|Vokenar's homecoming]], Crone had recovered to fighting condition and intended to raise Arkadia's depleted waters more quickly. She welcomed him alongside Aeris and Sigil.

She offered a century of training toward an assault on a pit fiend's gathering armies in the deeper hells beneath the [[Gray Wastes]]. Vokenar accepted and their training resumed. The proposed assault and its outcome were not depicted.
