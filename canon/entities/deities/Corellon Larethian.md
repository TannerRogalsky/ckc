The primordial elf, understood in elven lore as both the first elf and a deity whose gender can change. [[Boril Erendel]] invoked this ideal in the Larethian form he assumed at [[House Erendel]]: an androgynous high elf body clad in silver armor. Boril Erendel's claims of elven supremacy were his own doctrine.
