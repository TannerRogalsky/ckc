# Corellon Larethian

Corellon Larethian is the primordial elven deity invoked through [[Boril Erendel]]'s Larethian form. The campaign establishes the deity through elven lore rather than a direct encounter.

## Identity and Background

Elven lore understands Corellon Larethian as the first elf and a deity whose gender can change. This is an account of primordial elven identity, rather than an established allegiance to Boril Erendel's doctrine.

## Campaign History

At [[House Erendel]], Boril Erendel assumed an androgynous high elf body clad in silver armor and called it his Larethian form. The party's later examination connected the name to Corellon Larethian in [[canon/sessions/session_035#Chunk 0002|its review of elven lore]].

Boril Erendel's claims of elven supremacy were his own. His transformation did not establish that he was Corellon Larethian or that the deity endorsed his plans.
