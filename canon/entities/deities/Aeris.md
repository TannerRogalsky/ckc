# Aeris

Aeris is the goddess of the sky, chaos, potential futures, and fate, and the eldest of the three sisters of the [[Aesir]]. The party restored her wounded celestial body and defeated the forces holding her mortal fragment in [[Starfall]]. She survived to welcome [[Vokenar]] home and support the renewal of life below.

## Identity and Background

Aeris is the sister of [[Sigil]] and [[Crone]]. In the creation account recalled by the party, chaos and emptiness gave rise to Aeris, who then created the sun and moon. Her domain was described as broader than those of her younger sisters, and her power as greater. The sisters defeated the [[Vanir]] before the creation of the present heavens; the defeated gods' physical avatars fell beneath [[Stark]], while divine sparks survived elsewhere. These accounts were recalled in [[canon/sessions/session_026#Chunk 0000|the discussion after the moon's destruction]] and [[canon/sessions/session_036#Chunk 0000|Domyx's recollection of the ancient divine war]].

The sky is Aeris's physical celestial body. [[Touching the Sky]] therefore means literal contact with her, rather than merely approaching an image or avatar. Her visible presence can take the form of a black void veiled in stars. The mortal form imprisoned in Starfall was a stolen concentration of her soul, separated from the divinity still reaching the world through the sky.

During Vokenar's long descent from [[Arkadia]], Aeris helped her sisters prepare him for an approaching war. At that earlier point, they did not yet understand the wrong he would have to remedy. [[canon/sessions/session_011#Chunk 0000|His training flashback]] preserves that uncertainty.

## Personality and Motivations

Aeris can be receptive to persuasion and willing to answer questions beyond the limits others expect of divine counsel. Her guidance nevertheless depends on what she can perceive and on the condition of the aspect being contacted.

She defends entrusting the future to mortal choices. When Sigil questioned the danger imposed on the party, Aeris argued that mortal souls possess freedom and unpredictability even the goddesses cannot fully control or foresee. Her gratitude toward [[Ceril]] was expressed through a lasting gift and help with his restoration of the living world.

## Relationships

- [[Sigil]] — Her younger sister, whose concern for mortal safety sometimes conflicts with Aeris's confidence in mortal choice. Sigil also helped sustain the sisters through their injuries.
- [[Crone]] — Her younger sister and fellow divine mentor. When the moon was shattered, Crone insisted that Vokenar continue restoring Aeris rather than abandon that mission for her own rescue.
- [[Ceril]] — Her devoted mortal ally, charged with restoring her body and protecting Vokenar. His care fed her captive form and helped restore [[Gaokerena]]; their bond continued through her gifts and the renewal of life after the campaign.
- [[Vokenar]] — A warrior she helped bring into the world and prepare for its dangers. He and Ceril reached her captive form together, and she later helped make his return to life possible.
- [[Emperor Shen]] — Her captor, who touched the sky, stole a concentration of her soul, and broke her celestial body to deny others her power. His campaign against the sisters carried forward the resentment of the Vanir.
- [[Vanir]] — Ancient enemies of the sisters whose returning manifestations renewed the divine conflict. [[Entropie]] appeared as a hostile counterpart to Aeris during the final confrontation.

## Abilities

Aeris governs chaos, potential futures, and fate, and provides divine counsel through such means as Ceril's shield. Her answers can appear as changes in starlight rather than speech. Divination through her does not establish perfect knowledge of every mortal event or future.

Her sky-body can heal once enough [[Penumbra]] has restored its strength. Returning fallen fragments through [[Lyngbakr]] accelerated that healing. Her celestial and captive mortal aspects could not freely share everything each knew: the latter had to learn mortal magic over fifty years and could barely summon Ceril and Vokenar to her prison.

After the sky was repaired, Aeris gave Ceril time to prepare Vokenar's reincarnation without advancing events elsewhere. She later taught the party to combine lesser magical reserves into stronger workings within their star refuge. Whether her knowledge of [[Saratan]] exceeded the books available to the party was not established.

## Equipment and Resources

- **Protective shield** — Given to [[Ceril]], who retains it as both protection and a conduit for contacting Aeris.
- [[Ceril's Star]] — A lasting celestial refuge Aeris bestowed on Ceril through the power of the restored Gaokerena. It shelters its occupants outside ordinary time and became Ceril's home after the adventure. Aeris could visit the party there.

## Campaign History

### Captivity and the Search for Healing

Decades before the party reached Starfall, Emperor Shen captured Aeris's mortal fragment and used its stolen divine power to attack the sky. In [[canon/sessions/session_024#Chunk 0000|her account of captivity]], she estimated that sixty to eighty years had passed since the man who touched her had taken her. She was aging and weakening like a mortal, while her remaining soul still flowed into the world through the sky. Starfall's first shot used the power she had carried into captivity; later shots required pieces of her sky-body.

Aeris supported the party's search for those fragments. After the defeat of the apparent [[Morel Chainsunder]], Ceril consulted her through his shield and received guidance to destroy the dwarves' binding magic. During Lyngbakr's ascent after the [[Castle Kaedon]] expedition, she appeared clothed in stars but answered only by brightening or fading. She confirmed that their work could repair the [[Hole in the Sky]], advised against Ceril and Vokenar intervening personally, and directed them away from a further western search. Her constrained communication was later explained by the tightening of her captivity after the partial repair.

She then drew Ceril and Vokenar to her imprisoned form at the center of the vast ring combining earth, water, air, and fire. She believed restoring the sky could renew her divinity and potentially save the mortal fragment, but warned them not to underestimate her captor. Ceril grew apples, pears, citrus, and grain to feed her; Vokenar left a Continual Flame as a sign of their commitment. The encounter ended before a proposed attempt to banish her from the device could be tried.

### Sabotage and the Restored Sky

When the party considered planting [[Antumbra]] among [[The League of New Stark]]'s Penumbra deliveries, Ceril consulted Aeris's star-filled divine presence. She confirmed that the sabotage would not harm her trapped fragment, that [[Obould]] was alive, and that they should proceed with [[Red Caesar]]'s plan. Her guidance addressed their uncertainty without establishing when the sabotaged stone would reach the weapon.

Lyngbakr later consumed [[Jack Harvey]]'s condensed hoard and repaired the Hole in the Sky. This restored the visible heavens, but Aeris's mortal captivity continued at Starfall, now located at [[Axis Mundi]]. In [[canon/sessions/session_032#Chunk 0000|Ceril's visit to Arkadia]], she acknowledged that restoring her celestial body fulfilled one of his charges. The other, protecting Vokenar, had been interrupted by his death. She directed Ceril to recover the preserved body and gave him time to prepare the magic that returned Vokenar to life.

### Gaokerena and the Final Confrontation

Ceril's earlier planting, strengthened by Aeris's power, had restored Gaokerena's lower growth and spread into a living forest around Axis Mundi. Ceril and Vokenar traveled through the tree to meet Aeris and Sigil in Arkadia. Aeris defended mortal choice and explained the Vanir's surviving resentment before bestowing Ceril's Star for the coming danger. The gift and its connection to the restored tree are established in [[canon/sessions/session_037#Chunk 0000]].

Antumbra destroyed Starfall before its intended shot at Sigil. The fighting continued without depicting the physical release of Aeris's mortal fragment. During the battle against Emperor Shen's resurrected Vanir form, Ceril invoked the star refuge and Aeris taught the companions how to renew their magic there. The final destruction of Emperor Shen ended the returning gods' immediate threat.

## Final Status

In [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002|the epilogue]], Aeris appeared well beside Sigil and the recovered Crone in Arkadia and welcomed Vokenar home. Her survival and restored divine activity establish the rescue mission's successful outcome; the physical release of the mortal fragment from Starfall was not separately depicted.

She described the companions' futures and continued helping the life Ceril had fostered below. Her gratitude endured in that growth and in his star refuge. Ceril's eventual choice about entering a later world remained open.
