# Blibdoolpoolp

Blibdoolpoolp was the god of the [[Kuo-Toa]] encountered and defeated by the party at [[Otyugh Isle]]. Its manifestation showed how a people's collective belief could make a divine being real.

## Identity and Background

Seen through glass, Blibdoolpoolp appeared as a massive shadow in the water, holding an even larger entity in its claws. The early account does not identify that other entity.

The Kuo-Toa had made Blibdoolpoolp real through belief. That origin was recalled when the party encountered a different collective manifestation in [[Darvenlast]].

## Campaign History

The [[chunks/sessions_001-0010|early campaign notes]] record the party's encounter with Blibdoolpoolp in the island dungeon and their defeat of the god. The record provides no separate later restoration or return.

In [[canon/sessions/session_012#Chunk 0004]], the memory helped [[Red Caesar]] understand the apparent [[Morel Chainsunder]]: the Kuo-Toa's belief had created a being, while the dwarves' shared fear sustained a leader's identity through their congregation. During the later return to the island's surrounding waters, the party was again reminded that it had killed Blibdoolpoolp.
