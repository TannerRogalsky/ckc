# Deep World Wizard

Deep World Wizard is the recorded designation for two distinct unnamed mages who defended [[Darvenlast]] against the party. Both were killed during its advance through the city's residential approaches.

## Description

The mages combined destructive spells, counterspelling, force magic, and short-range teleportation with resilience in close combat. Fireball, Blight, and Hellish Rebuke characterized their hostile magic.

## Campaign History

The first mage fought alongside an [[Etten]] champion called from a residence, rather than magically summoned. Their destructive magic threatened [[Red Caesar]], [[Ceril]], and [[Domyx]], but the party defeated both defenders. After the mage teleported into [[Kerben]] and [[Zulu]]'s reach, Domyx killed him, enduring a final fiery rebuke. See [[session_012#Chunk 0000]].

A second mage joined fighters and a priest in an ambush deeper in the residential district. His blighting magic injured Domyx, while teleportation let him threaten the party's rear. The companions overcame the ambush, and Domyx again delivered the fatal blow despite the mage's dying fiery retaliation. See [[session_012#Chunk 0001]].
