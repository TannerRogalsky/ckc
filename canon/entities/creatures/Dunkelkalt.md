# Dunkelkalt

Dunkelkalt was the Vanir That Eats The Sun And Moon, a two-headed [[Vanir]] who returned alongside [[Entropie]] at [[Axis Mundi]]. [[Kerben]] killed him during the confrontation with the party.

## Description

His winged form combined humanoid faces with a muscular, doglike body. Both heads were crowned: one shimmered blue, while the other was matte black with red eyes. He and Entropie stood as opposites to the [[Aesir]] sisters [[Aeris]], [[Sigil]], and [[Crone]].

Dunkelkalt embodied opposed cold and fiery powers and resisted many forms of magical energy. Exodus Mundi temporarily cast victims outside reality into a hostile, cold, suffocating expanse of stars.

## Campaign History

He returned with Entropie after [[Emperor Shen]]'s first death, renewing the ancient divine conflict and attacking the party for serving the goddesses. Exodus Mundi repeatedly cast [[Domyx]] outside reality, disrupting his efforts to hold the god.

Kerben killed Dunkelkalt. His dying attempt to cast [[Red Caesar]] outside reality failed, and his form sank into the ground, leaving Entropie to face the companions. See [[session_037#Chunk 0002]].
