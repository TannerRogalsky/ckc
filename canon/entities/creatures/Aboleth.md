# Aboleth

The Aboleth encountered beneath the [[Mana Sea]] was an extraplanar aberration that fed on memories and controlled local creatures.

## Description

Long tentacles extended from its soft, slippery body, and a piranha-like mouth grinned as it explored its victims' minds. It concealed itself underwater through invisibility, dragged captives below the surface, and painfully relived their memories without erasing them. It enjoyed anguish as readily as pleasure.

Its psychic domination linked [[Kuo-Toa]], drow, and driders into a hierarchy of servants, drawing the latter out of their underground refuge.

## Campaign History

The party first encountered its influence through displaced drow and driders, then faced coordinated Kuo-Toa musketeers in the drowned forest. They broke several servants free and allowed them to flee while the hidden Aboleth seized companions beneath the water.

[[Ceril]] exposed it with starlight. [[Domyx]] resisted its attempt to make him a replacement servant, enlarged himself to escape its grasp, and restrained it while freeing [[Kerben]]. Kerben killed the creature. Its final words regretted that it could not return to space with the world it had learned from their memories.

## Final Status

The encountered Aboleth died in the Mana Sea. Its surviving Kuo-Toa servants fled after their control was broken.
