An extraplanar aberration that established itself beneath the [[Mana Sea]]. It psychically enslaved local [[Kuo-Toa]] and a group of drow and driders drawn from the underground, linking each dominated creature into a layered mental hierarchy.

The Aboleth concealed itself underwater, pulled victims into its tentacles, and fed on their memories without erasing them. The party freed several thralls by breaking the creature's control and killed it before continuing toward [[Ninki Nanka]].
