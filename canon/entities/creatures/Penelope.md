# Penelope

Penelope was the giant bat ridden by a dwarf [[Bat Rider]] knight in [[Darvinblast]]. Her puffy, chinchilla-like face looked more like a cat's than a bat's and was described as uncomfortably cute.

## Campaign History

When her rider surrendered, he gave Penelope a consoling pet and asked the party to spare her regardless of what they did to him. The record does not establish a later fate for Penelope.
