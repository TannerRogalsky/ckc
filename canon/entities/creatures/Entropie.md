# Entropie

Entropie was the Vanir That Eats the Stars, a star-filled [[Vanir]] who returned with [[Dunkelkalt]] at [[Axis Mundi]]. Defeat of his manifested form did not prevent a final Wish that resurrected [[Emperor Shen]] as the last Vanir.

## Description

Entropie resembled a dark counterpart to [[Aeris]]. He and Dunkelkalt stood opposite the [[Aesir]] sisters Aeris, [[Sigil]], and [[Crone]]. Entropie identified Sigil as the goddess who had brought him down in their ancient conflict.

He distorted luck, wielded destructive stellar energy, and resisted the divine radiance and deathly magic used by the companions. [[Ceril]] recognized and contested his manipulation of fate.

## Campaign History

Entropie manifested after Emperor Shen's first death, attacking the party for its service to the sister goddesses. [[Vokenar]] channeled Sigil's power against him. After [[Kerben]] killed Dunkelkalt, the companions defeated Entropie's manifested form, with [[Domyx]] delivering the final blow. See [[session_037#Chunk 0002]] and [[session_037#Chunk 0003]].

## Final Status

Before dissipating into his remaining stars, Entropie cast an uncounterable Wish that resurrected Emperor Shen's body as the last Vanir. The party destroyed that final opponent. The record did not establish a later separate manifestation of Entropie. See [[session_038#Chunk 0000]] and [[session_038#Chunk 0002]].
