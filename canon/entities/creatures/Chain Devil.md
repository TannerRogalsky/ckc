# Chain Devil

A Chain Devil was summoned into [[Castle Kaedon]]'s prison cells to oppose the party alongside a [[Bearded Devil]].

## Description

Its body consisted entirely of chains, hot to the touch. Chains pulled inward toward black pits where its eyes should have been before beams of red light emerged, inflicting supernatural fear.

[[Red Caesar]] knew Chain Devils as slave masters of hell who used their chains to string captives up and drag them back when they tried to escape.

## Campaign History

Summoning marks appeared among the cells and partitions. The Chain Devil's gaze briefly frightened [[Domyx]], who turned his attention to the Bearded Devil while the others fought.

[[Vokenar]] saw an invisible [[Nalfeshne]] bolstering the Chain Devil from an adjoining room. The devil's cooperation with a demon contradicted the usual rivalry between their kinds.

Domyx overcame the next fearful gaze, and [[Ceril]]'s divination helped him land the finishing blow. The creature fell and its chains collapsed.
