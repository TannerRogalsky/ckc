# Arcanoloth

Arcanoloth is the kind of fiend to which [[Farraday]] belonged, described in the party's account as a demon.

## Campaign History

The party learned that an Arcanoloth commanded a portion of the ocean and held the [[Tome of Satariel]]. The unnamed keeper was later identified as Farraday, [[Kerben]]'s former employer and [[Jack Harvey]]'s benefactor.

At [[Tome Keeper's Pyramid]], Kerben destroyed the tome and dealt the final blow to Farraday's body on [[Stark]].

## Final Status

Farraday was forced back to hell after his body on Stark was destroyed. This outcome concerns the encountered individual rather than Arcanoloths as a whole.
