# Haunted Living Tent

The Haunted Living Tent was a gargantuan, stationary creature on [[The Carrot Cake]]'s midway that swallowed visitors into an extra-dimensional house.

## Description

Its walls expanded and settled as if breathing. The opening exposed rows of teeth around a spiraling light and drew victims inward, their bodies vanishing as points of light.

Inside was a pleasant house with furniture, bay windows, and an apparent green landscape. A [[Magen]] sat drinking tea while furnishings became poltergeist weapons, battering guests against the walls. The windows and apparent exits were painted illusions; holes in the structure revealed the darkness of the Astral Plane.

The boundary blocked the party's ordinary escape magic. Those inside and outside could damage the same creature without being able to reach one another normally.

## Campaign History

The tent swallowed [[Kerben]], [[Red Caesar]], and [[Vokenar]], then later [[Ceril]]. [[Domyx]] and [[Zulu]] remained outside while the captives attacked the house's supports.

Domyx climbed onto the roof and cut through it, joining the damage Kerben had made from within. The tent deflated and its interior disintegrated into starry particles. The seated Magen vanished first, saying goodbye.

Red Caesar and Kerben seized [[Jack Harvey's Portrait]] before the house dissolved. All the trapped companions returned safely to the real world, and the portrait went into [[The Opal]]'s hold.

## Final Status

The tent was destroyed. The party escaped with the portrait; the Magen inside disintegrated with the house.
