# Nyquil

Nyquil was [[Ceril]]'s owl familiar and a long-distance scout, lost to [[Akasha]] rain on [[Continental Stark]] before Ceril renewed his familiar-summoning abilities.

## Description

The owl communicated telepathically with Ceril through faint images and metaphor. It could scout far beyond the distance at which they could communicate directly, then return with its observations.

## Campaign History

Nyquil followed an imperial flying scout over the northern mountains and out to sea. Its report showed a golden-haired human in a turban and the [[Broyish Empire]]'s white and gold colors. It also followed an injured dwarf to [[The Pit]], where members of [[Figma Brickfinger's Union]] collected him after his surrender.

During the journey across Continental Stark, Akasha rain killed the owl, leaving burns and a lingering glow on its body. Ceril found and consumed the remains.

[[Gossa]] subsequently made Ceril a volcanic-rock amulet that allowed him to summon an owl as a magma elemental, with claws, fire breath, and an explosive burst on destruction. Ceril later used a molten owl to scout [[Castle Kaedon]].

## Final Status

The original owl's death was established. Later owl summoning restored Ceril's scouting capability, but the record does not describe the familiar's fate at campaign end.
