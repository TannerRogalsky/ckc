# Carrion Crawler

Carrion Crawlers were large, arachnid-like creatures infesting [[Castle Kaedon]]'s granaries and entrance foyer.

## Description

Thin, clear tentacles writhed like a mass of worms and could paralyze prey. The granary specimens were filthy and coated in spilled alcohol.

## Campaign History

The party first encountered them among giant scorpions and [[Sturges]] in a storehouse near [[Castle Kaedon Arena]]. [[Kerben]] killed one while the companions cleared the infestation.

More attacked in the overgrown foyer alongside a [[Flabbergast]], [[Severed Hands]], and giant scorpions. The last crawler briefly paralyzed [[Red Caesar]], but he recovered and [[Vokenar]] cut it in half with his sword. It continued twitching after death.

## Final Status

The encountered Crawlers were killed, clearing the infested rooms.
