# Saratan

Saratan are gigantic turtle- or whale-like beings easily mistaken for islands. [[Lyngbakr]] belongs to this species, and records mention a famous member named [[Wyrm]].

## Description

A magical aura makes observers perceive them as islands. The party's account describes them as generally benign, driven more by hunger and instinct than curiosity.

The party considered [[Aeris]] a possible source of knowledge about them beyond what books supplied.

## Campaign History

Lyngbakr's apparent island concealed a living being connected to the party's efforts to repair the sky with [[Penumbra]]. His individual history and eventual return to [[Arkadia]] are recorded in his article.
