# Gnoll

Gnoll packs held highlands near [[Brimbolyn]] and guarded a pure [[Penumbra]] fragment that the party sought.

## Description

These fiendish, hyena-like people carried dangerous poison and fought in coordinated packs. Encountered warriors used bone bows, whips, and flails; fangs, thanes, and pack lords filled distinct fighting roles. Pack lords drove their followers into renewed attacks.

[[Ceril]] recalled that their people had survived [[The Cataclysm]] by racing inland and violently displacing other inhabitants from hills and high ground.

## Campaign History

[[Courteous Cam]] gave the party a map to the fragment, explaining that its guards recognized its value without knowing how to use it. [[Red Caesar]] detected an encircling ambush before [[Domyx]] advanced into it.

[[Vokenar]]'s wall of fire obstructed attackers and burned incoming arrows. Domyx killed a captive pack lord with a punch. The party brought down the other leaders and remaining warriors, and Red Caesar's locating magic found no further members of the packs nearby.

The companions subsequently recovered the untouched Penumbra. Another Gnoll was detected passing near their later wyvern encounter without approaching.

## Final Status

The packs guarding the fragment were killed. The later passing individual was not confronted; the victory did not establish the fate of the wider people.
