# Storm Phoenix

The Storm Phoenix was a being of concentrated elemental power that [[Theotropa]] asked the party to release after [[The Cataclysm]] drove it mad.

## Description

Its original form was an amber core. As a great bird it moved like lightning between clouds, briefly re-forming before flashing elsewhere. Its attacks could arc from one nearby person to another.

Theotropa explained that defeating its bird form would return it to the core, which the party should cast into the sea. The possibility of keeping it as a future companion remained uncertain.

## Campaign History

[[Ceril]] located it through nature communion south of [[The Garden]]. It settled aggressively on [[The Opal]]'s crow's nest. [[Domyx]] drew it nearer with [[Iklwa Isondo]], and [[Kerben]] timed his shots between its lightning movements.

Kerben's final shot dispersed the bird, leaving amber on the deck. He wanted to keep the core and perhaps raise a phoenix, but the others insisted on fulfilling Theotropa's instructions and threw it overboard.

When the amber touched seawater, it became a vertical lightning bolt that split outward into the heavens. Ceril recognized this as the return of some weather balance after decades of diminishing lightning. Theotropa confirmed that the elements were freer outside the phoenix and gave the party the promised [[Penumbra]].

## Final Status

The concentrated creature and its amber core dispersed into lightning. No retained egg or later reborn bird was established; its power returned to the wider world.
