# Lightning Elemental

Lightning Elementals were living masses of electricity encountered in [[Hole Shebang]] and [[King of the Hole]] within [[The Karat Cake]].

## Description

They crackled with electrical arcs, sprang into the air from the ground, and moved rapidly between targets. Their discharges could paralyze victims, and striking their bodies risked an electrical backlash.

Their electricity restored animated toys and [[Shambling Mound]]s rather than harming them, sustaining nearby enemies.

## Campaign History

The first encountered elemental appeared among Hole Shebang's wires alongside a [[Death Spark]]. Its electricity repeatedly repaired the animated toys. [[Ceril]] dispersed it with moonlight, ending that support and allowing the party to destroy the remaining swarm.

Another appeared in King of the Hole alongside a Shambling Mound and continued replenishing it as the contestants fought. After the Shambling Mound fell, [[Domyx]] destroyed the elemental with the [[Gith Shard Glaive]], enduring its electrical backlash.

## Final Status

Both encountered elementals were destroyed. Their defeat ended the replenishment they supplied during those battles.
