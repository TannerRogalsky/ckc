# Victor, the Amphibious Beast

Victor, the Amphibious Beast was [[Kerben]]'s large crocodilian companion, suited to travel on land and in water.

## Description

Powerful jaws allowed him to seize and twist enemies. Kerben recalled meeting him in mosquito-filled swamps.

## Campaign History

He accompanied the party through the [[Mana Sea]], sheltering with Kerben during a destructive bird swarm. [[Vokenar]] tended his injuries.

A drow cultist's freezing magic struck him and Vokenar unconscious during the drider encounter. He was resummoned after the fight. During the subsequent battle, his bite finished the last moss golem as it tried to attack the rear of the party.

He also attacked the [[Aboleth]]'s musketeers while the companions broke their control and freed the surviving servants.

## Final Status

He last accompanied Kerben's Mana Sea expedition, restored after the drider encounter. The record does not establish his later condition or whereabouts.
