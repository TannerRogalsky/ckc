[[Kerben]]'s large crocodilian companion, first encountered in mosquito-filled swamps. Victor, the Amphibious Beast can move effectively over land and through water and fights by seizing enemies in powerful jaws. Victor, the Amphibious Beast accompanied the party through the [[Mana Sea]], surviving the drider and moss golem encounters and helping break the [[Aboleth]]'s control over its thralls.
