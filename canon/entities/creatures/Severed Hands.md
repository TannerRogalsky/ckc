# Severed Hands

Severed Hands were crawling swarms encountered in [[Castle Kaedon Arena]]'s exterior grounds and elsewhere in [[Castle Kaedon]].

## Description

Blackened hands scuttled like spiders through grass and seaweed, using fingers and thumbs as five legs. They seemed to coordinate through gestures before directing attacks at particular opponents.

They appeared to arrive with wind and fog from the great cataract, though that origin was uncertain. Broad blasts could scatter the swarms.

## Campaign History

A swarm approached through the arena grounds while [[Mezzoloth]]s emerged from nearby stables. [[Red Caesar]] drove the hands toward the cliff with thunderous force, and [[Kerben]] cut away the fingers still holding on. The remains fell into the sea.

More emerged in an acid-damaged checkpoint alongside black puddings. [[Ceril]] gathered them into a roiling mass with Maelstrom as the party cleared the room. He also destroyed the remaining hands in the entrance foyer during its infestation.

The swarms encountered in these battles were destroyed.
