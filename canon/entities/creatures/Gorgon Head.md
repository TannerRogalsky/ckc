# Gorgon Head

Gorgon Heads were floating, snake-wreathed monstrosities encountered in [[Magic Hat]] and [[King of the Hole]] within [[The Carrot Cake]].

## Description

Each had a woman's head without a body, with fused serpentine material trailing below the neck. Snake hair struck at nearby victims, poisonous rays threatened others, and its gaze could turn people to stone.

A destroyed head could burst in a wave of black-and-white energy that also threatened petrification.

## Campaign History

A head emerged with [[Flame Skull]]s from Magic Hat's water features. [[Red Caesar]] destroyed it with an electrical shock. Its death burst began turning [[Vokenar]] and [[Domyx]] to stone, but both overcame the transformation.

More appeared during King of the Hole. [[Ceril]] destroyed the head in the opening round. During the fourth required round, separate heads threatened the northern contestants and those near the lighthouse. Ceril dispelled his encroaching petrification, Vokenar repeatedly cleansed himself, and Red Caesar resisted the transformation through his own endurance.

Domyx brought down the southern head with [[Iklwa Isondo]]. Red Caesar finished the northern one, ending that round.

## Final Status

The encountered Gorgon Heads were destroyed. The companions overcame their threatened petrification.
