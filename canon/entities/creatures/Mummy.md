# Mummy

Mummies were undead humanoids encountered around [[The Carrot Cake]]'s entrance, [[Magic Hat]], and [[King of the Hole]].

## Description

They attacked with rotting fists and a dreadful glare that could paralyze victims. Their gaze immobilized prey rather than establishing the permanent stone transformation associated with [[Gorgon Head]]s.

## Campaign History

At the park entrance, [[Vokenar]] turned two Mummies away. A seated [[Magen]] refused to help them, and they eventually sank back into the rain-softened mud while the party defeated the remaining attackers.

Another accompanied vampire servants in Magic Hat's courtyard and was overcome before further enemies emerged from the water features.

Two appeared during the opening King of the Hole round, part of [[Farraday]]'s entertainment system. One briefly paralyzed [[Red Caesar]], but his wards prevented its draining effect and he recovered. He destroyed one, escaped the other's pursuit across the water, and returned to finish it after Vokenar's fire had weakened it.

## Final Status

The arena pair were destroyed. Two entrance Mummies withdrew into the mud rather than being killed; their later fate was not established.
