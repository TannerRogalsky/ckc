# Psychic Goop

Psychic Goops were gray oozes formed from decayed human remains in [[Castle Kaedon Arena]], later encountered elsewhere around [[Castle Kaedon]].

## Description

Their bodies arose where surviving brain matter dominated the remains. They sensed minds rather than visible bodies, detecting opponents even when they hid or moved out of sight.

They attacked with distant mental pressure and appeared able to coordinate through a shared intellect. Some magic inflicted on them fed their agony back to the caster, while physical attacks avoided that retaliation.

## Campaign History

The party first found them among the arena's ancient spectators, alongside other oozes formed from the dead. Their collective assaults pressed hardest on [[Ceril]] until the companions destroyed the final threats in the stands.

More joined a cliffside ambush with water elementals and [[Animated Armor]]. [[Red Caesar]]'s lightning provoked painful retaliation. Ceril healed him and used starry attacks that avoided the same response.

## Final Status

The encountered Psychic Goops were destroyed. Their appearance beyond the arena showed that they were not confined to the bleachers.
