# Kuo-Toa

Kuo-Toa are an amphibious people encountered at [[Otyugh Isle]] and later in the [[Mana Sea]]. They worship [[Blibdoolpoolp]].

## Description

Their offering was called Frankensense. The party encountered both temple claimants and musket-bearing individuals, whose circumstances were distinct.

## Campaign History

At Otyugh Isle, Kuo-Toa wanted to occupy the island dungeon. A frightened-looking member of their people was among the statues at the [[Temple of Sigil]].

A later return revealed that nineteen Kuo-Toa and twelve priests had been petrified when a cockatrice interrupted their struggle for the temple. [[Father Warrick]] was restored and proposed gradually awakening the Kuo-Toa and teaching them about [[Sigil]]. Their restoration was not confirmed.

In the Mana Sea, an [[Aboleth]] controlled Kuo-Toa musketeers and forced them to attack the party. The companions broke their compulsion and let the freed servants flee, then killed the controller.

## Final Status

The temple group was last established as petrified, with a plan for later restoration. The surviving musketeers escaped the Aboleth's control and fled; these encounters do not establish the fate of the wider people.
