# Vampiric Nightbringer

The Vampiric Nightbringer was the vampire lord occupying [[Hole on Wheels]]' front car in [[The Karat Cake]].

## Description

Its faceless head had only a screaming mouth and moved in abrupt, unnatural shifts. Broad white, angelic wings rose from its back, and wounds shed pale feathers instead of blood.

It commanded vampire spawn and familiars throughout the train, drained vitality, dominated minds, and released oppressive magic from its wings. Sunlight suppressed its regeneration.

## Campaign History

The party fought through its servants to the front car. The Vampiric Nightbringer drained [[Domyx]] and took control of [[Red Caesar]]'s will.

Red Caesar opened the front window with acid, and [[Ceril]] tore the roof open with lightning, admitting sunlight. [[Vokenar]] broke the Vampiric Nightbringer's hold over Red Caesar with the [[Moon Blade]] before the wizard could be compelled to attack a companion.

A spawn dragged Domyx beneath the moving train. He survived, climbed back aboard, and returned to the front while the others kept fighting the lord under the exposed roof.

Domyx's final glaive strike destroyed the Vampiric Nightbringer. It dispersed into pale feathers that blackened and drifted out through the ceiling.

## Final Status

The Vampiric Nightbringer was destroyed, ending its control of the train.
