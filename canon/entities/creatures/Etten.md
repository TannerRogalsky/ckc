# Etten

The Etten was a two-headed giant brought into the defense of [[Darvinblast]] by the [[Deep World Wizard]].

## Description

Each head had tusks and a grayish-black beard, and the heads spoke to one another during the fight. It wielded an enormous battle axe and morning star, the latter capable of knocking opponents down. Its second head made attempts to choke it ineffective.

## Campaign History

The Etten emerged with the Deep World Wizard from the city's residences and confronted [[Domyx]]. Domyx brought it down while the rest of the party fought the wizard.
