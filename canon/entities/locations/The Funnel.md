The primary mining operation area within [[The Pit]]. Near the entrance to [[The Darvenlast]].

In session 032, [[Courteous Cam]] and [[Otto]] led its deepest expedition. A previous breakthrough exposed a vast air-filled abyss beneath [[Stark]], but the route collapsed. They agreed to redirect work toward reopening it, offering the party a possible route to [[Axis Mundi]] within several weeks.

## Session 035

[[Figma Brickfinger]] and [[Courteous Cam]] confirmed that the route had been reopened. The miners reached a spacious region with unfamiliar air but withdrew rather than venture through its dangers. With magical travel to [[Axis Mundi]] blocked, the party prepared to descend by this route from [[The Pit]].
