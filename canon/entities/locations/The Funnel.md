# The Funnel

The Funnel is [[The Pit]]'s principal mining area and the starting point of the reopened descent into the [[Gray Wastes]]. Its excavations gave the party a physical route toward [[Axis Mundi]] when magical entry failed.

## Geography and Access

The mining area lies near [[Darvenlast]]'s entrance. Wooden lifts and pulley systems led below the sea to deeper excavation. Beyond natural caverns and the remains of earlier expeditions, a purple worm's tunnel crossed the lower planar threshold.

## Description

Deep underground, brackish water moved through broad natural chambers. Light weakened and changed color, while gravity diminished with descent. The route passed the stone remains of defeated [[Vanir]] avatars before reaching the separate lower plane.

## Inhabitants and Control

[[Figma Brickfinger's Union]] controlled the broader mining enterprise. [[Courteous Cam]] and [[Otto]] oversaw its deepest expedition and redirected work toward the breakthrough. Miners withdrew from the unfamiliar space rather than treating it as surveyed or safe.

## Campaign History

Otto had reached an air-filled abyss beneath [[Stark]], but the passage collapsed. Courteous Cam agreed to reopen it when [[Red Caesar]] sought a way to the weapon below. The work was expected to take weeks.

[[Figma Brickfinger]] and Courteous Cam later confirmed the route's reopening. The party chose it after failed teleportation and planar travel to Axis Mundi.

Otto guided the companions through the descent before turning back. They found the remains of a pre-[[The Cataclysm]] dwarven expedition that had sought the underworld against the Union's orders, and encountered hostile fomorians. [[Kerben]] tracked the worm's path, leading them across the boundary on foot and in flight.

## Final Status

The party successfully used the reopened route in session 036. The later flood into the Gray Wastes did not separately establish the continued accessibility of this tunnel or the condition of the upper mining operation.
