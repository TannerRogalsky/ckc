# Continental Stark

Continental Stark is the great elven continent containing [[Brimbolyn]], described as the surviving expanse of the Old World. It is distinct from both the city at its center and the world of [[Stark]].

## Geography and Access

Dense jungle, perpetual fog, and a thick canopy obscure the interior. Bogs, sweltering terrain, and burning [[Akasha]] rainstorms make travel hazardous. Overgrown railways cross the land, with much of the older network buried or rusted.

## Description

The continent's jungle shelters threats such as [[Fuchsia Fungus]]. Magical terrain-shaping and rail construction altered the landscape, while newer cleared lines remained in use around Brimbolyn.

## Notable Areas

- [[Brimbolyn]] — The central magical city and principal destination of the party's inland journey.
- Rail network — Older neglected tracks and active lines connecting towns and noble courts; these do not constitute a single wholly abandoned system.

## Campaign History

Even before [[The Cataclysm]], railway construction was controversial for its destruction of species and injury to the natural world. Ceril's response to the cleared and magically altered terrain reflected that older conflict.

The party crossed the continent's dangerous interior and reached the guarded active railway on its way to the city. The surviving network supported [[The Order of Seasons]]'s influence, but the continent, city, and Order remained distinct.

## Final Status

The campaign later records continuing visits to Brimbolyn and its institutions, followed by renewed elven monarchy and the repurposing of [[The Academy]]. It does not establish that the continent's jungle hazards or every damaged railway were removed.
