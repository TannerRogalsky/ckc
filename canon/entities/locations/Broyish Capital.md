# Broyish Capital

The Broyish Capital is the industrial metropolis in the hills of [[Broy]], formerly the center of [[Emperor Shen]]'s rule. The party's rescue of his captives and exposure of the false sky provoked an uprising there.

## Geography and Access

The city stands above an industrial steel town. Winding stairs connect the coastal approach with the capital, while airships travel overhead.

During imperial rule, [[Oni]] sentries watched from pillars and rooftops, some invisibly. Restrictions included areas where magic failed, hotels protected by persistent dead-magic effects, and wards suppressing lesser spells. Spell scrolls were contraband. Citizens were easier to scry upon than visitors; underground fixers could fabricate citizenship papers.

## Description

Magic and electricity power automated wheeled devices, copper cables carry electrical arcs, and rushing water drives machinery amid whirring gears. The city's market had diverse non-human residents despite the Empire's xenophobic reputation.

## Notable Areas

- [[Dawn Market]] — The capital's commercial district.
- [[Dawn Palace]] — The imperial seat and the site of consequential imprisonments and rescues.

## Inhabitants and Control

The [[Broyish Empire]] governed the city under Emperor Shen and [[Vizier Jade]]. [[Naomi Ue]] operated through underground contacts, providing forged documents and later organizing a diversion. The sentries and restrictions describe the former regime, not a verified final security system.

## Campaign History

The party first entered while [[Obould]] was imprisoned by Vizier Jade. They later returned to recover [[Domyx]] and [[Vokenar]]'s preserved body.

Although the sky above Stark had been repaired, Mirage Arcana concealed that change from the population. [[Red Caesar]], [[Ceril]], and [[Kerben]] dispelled the illusion above the palace. Naomi Ue's paid diversion became part of a genuine revolt against imperial authority. Emperor Shen and Vizier Jade withdrew to [[Starfall]], leaving Domyx's opened cage behind. The companions restored him and recovered Vokenar's body from the palace pinnacle.

## Final Status

The last detailed visit, in session 032, ended with the captives recovered and the rulers gone from the palace. Broy's later charter replaced the old regime's governing framework, but the capital's subsequent local administration, repairs, and magical restrictions were not separately documented.
