# Thalassia

Thalassia is the broad region of the Prime Material Plane encompassing the lands and waters traversed by the party throughout the campaign. It includes [[Domyx]]'s titan homeland, [[Broy]], [[The Pit]], the [[Mana Sea]], [[Brimbolyn]], and the other places visited on that plane. The [[Gray Wastes]] and [[Arkadia]] are separate planes outside Thalassia.

## Geography and Access

Thalassia spans multiple continents, islands, settlements, and waterways; the titan homeland is one part of this wider region. The map below depicts the entire area.

![[images/Thalassia.jpg]]

Treacherous mountains separate the titan homeland from lower territory. Descent was possible but dangerous, while ascent was regarded as nearly impossible for outsiders.

[[Farron Acathian II]] gave Domyx a map showing part of Thalassia, elven territory connected toward [[Broy]], another continent to the north, and a hill route into the homeland. The party approached through frozen northern straits and the Arctic Plains, then followed a river inland.

## Description

The region includes Broy's industrial coast, the dwarven mining settlement of The Pit, the magical swamp of the Mana Sea, and the elven city of Brimbolyn on [[Continental Stark]]. These places retain their distinct landscapes and communities within Thalassia.

The approach to the titan homeland passed from severe cold into grassy hills, orchards, farmland, and flowers sustained by the titan realm's magic. At altitude, the sky appeared as a flat plate separating [[Stark]] from [[Arkadia]].

During the epilogue, the mountains contained new industry, timber transport, watermills, and settlements shared by titans, humans, halflings, gnomes, and dwarves.

## Notable Areas

- Titan homeland — Domyx's mountainous homeland, formerly isolated and dominated by [[Clan Akathia]].
- [[Broy]] — The eastern continent and homeland of the former [[Broyish Empire]].
- [[The Pit]] — The dwarven settlement and mining center governed by [[Figma Brickfinger's Union]].
- [[Mana Sea]] — The magical swamp near [[Southport]], formed over the drowned forests of [[Windsurf]].
- [[Brimbolyn]] — The magical city at the center of Continental Stark.
- [[Acathian Manor]] — The golden palace atop the titan homeland's highest mountains, formerly the ruling dynastic seat.
- [[Prison of Frost]] — The nearby prison from which [[Ulrich Fjoller]] was rescued.
- Northern approaches to the titan homeland — Frozen straits, Arctic terrain, and the river route through the highlands.

## Inhabitants and Control

Thalassia contains distinct communities and governments; the titan houses' authority describes the homeland rather than the entire region.

[[Clan Akathia]] formerly held dominant dynastic authority in the titan homeland. The surrounding titan community had excluded outsiders and concealed parts of its history. By Domyx's return, the houses governed cooperatively and newcomers lived openly among them.

## Campaign History

The map Farron Acathian II provided opened a route into the titan territory Domyx had left. A [[Tatzelwurm]] intensified the northern cold through blizzards and avalanches; its defeat calmed the weather. The party followed a river stained with the dragon's blood, overcame a [[Remorhaz]], and climbed into the fertile homeland.

Domyx reached the place where Ulrich Fjoller had last intercepted him, then entered Acathian Manor. [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] revealed his friend's imprisonment. Domyx rejected his family's suppression of the truth and was disowned by [[Domyx IV]], after which the companions freed Ulrich Fjoller and departed.

After Emperor Shen's final defeat, Domyx returned to find new settlements and cooperative rule. [[Charlotta Fjoller]] welcomed him, and Domyx IV apologized and began rebuilding their relationship.

## Final Status

In the titan homeland, session 038 established that authority was shared among the houses, and the former isolation had given way to cooperation with other peoples. Domyx's reconciliation did not require inheriting the old dynasty or becoming the homeland's new ruler.

These changes concern the titan homeland. Broy, The Pit, the Mana Sea, Brimbolyn, and Thalassia's other locations have their own last established conditions, recorded in their respective articles.
