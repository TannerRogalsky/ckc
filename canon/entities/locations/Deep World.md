# Deep World

The Deep World is the underground dwarven realm containing [[Darvinblast]] and numerous clans. It is distinct from the separate lower plane of the [[Gray Wastes]].

## Geography and Access

Darvinblast and other entrances connect the realm to the surface. Large illusions concealed terrain in the northern mountains during the party's early exploration; the illusion associated with Darvinblast's binding curse was later removed.

## Description

The realm has winding caves, magma veins, toxic smoke, and acrid fumes. Its established inhabitants speak Northern.

## Inhabitants and Control

Dozens of dwarf clans include the [[Steelfend Clan]] and [[Far Helm Clan]]. Many resisted outsiders, but individual households and communities formed different relationships with the party. Darvinblast's former magical control does not establish one ruler over the entire realm.

## Campaign History

The party explored Darvinblast while searching for [[Morel Chainsunder]] and [[Penumbra]]. Ending his surviving spell freed its population from a collective emotional bond. The resulting movement between the underground and [[The Pit]] changed relations among deep worlders and other dwarves.

## Final Status

Later accounts of integration in The Pit show continuing deep-world communities after Darvinblast's liberation. They do not establish that every clan became welcoming or that all entrances and surface illusions shared the same fate.
