# Gray Wastes

The Gray Wastes are the lower plane beneath [[Stark]], containing [[Axis Mundi]] and [[Gaokerena]]'s lower growth. They became the basin for Stark's excess oceans after [[Emperor Shen]]'s final defeat.

## Geography and Access

The plane occupies a lower position corresponding to [[Arkadia]] above Stark. A purple worm's tunnel beneath [[The Funnel]] provided a physically traversable threshold. [[Vokenar]] remained visible while crossing but could no longer be heard by those above the boundary.

The deeper hells are a separate realm below the Gray Wastes. The plane participates in the reality renewed by [[Genesis Mundi]]; its possible identity as another world's Stark remained speculation.

## Description

Before the flood, a vast gray desert lay beneath a second sky of clouds, lightning, and crumbling stone islands suspended on pillars. Falling rock shattered into fine dust. Gravity was weaker than on Stark, colors were muted, and the air was breathable.

Soft powder could carry [[The Opal]] under sail with magical assistance. Around Gaokerena, Ceril's plants had created fertile living land, varied vegetation, and wildlife within the desert.

## Notable Areas

- [[Gaokerena]] — The world tree and forest refuge, providing the demonstrated route to Arkadia.
- [[Axis Mundi]] — The protected weapon site reached by following firing scars east and then a forest path.

## Campaign History

The party entered through the worm's tunnel. [[Red Caesar]] recognized the desert from his encounter with [[Domyx I]] and interpreted it through his earlier learning about natural renewal.

[[Kerben]] used a summoned roc to save The Opal from its descent. The ship glided down, then crossed the powder with its sails and wind-providing genie. The companions traced [[Starfall]]'s scars through the lower sky until Gaokerena came into view.

Reaching the tree revealed the forest grown from [[Ceril]]'s effort to feed [[Aeris]]. Its flourishing life demonstrated the possibility of recovery with divine aid. Beyond it, the party reached Axis Mundi, destroyed Starfall, and faced the returning [[Vanir]].

Emperor Shen's emergence as the last Vanir drained vitality across the plane and blighted the restored vegetation. His destruction ended that influence; fertile soil and new growth returned near Axis Mundi. The lower sky continued breaking, however, and waterfalls from Stark widened into a flood.

## Final Status

The Gray Wastes were flooding in session 038. Vokenar stayed behind to send his companions home and recognized that the lower basin could receive the waters that had drowned Stark. Over the following weeks, seas above retreated and exposed old lands. The later condition of every lower landmark and route was not established.
