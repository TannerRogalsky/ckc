# The Pit

The Pit is the dwarven settlement in the northeastern dwarven continent and the seat of [[Figma Brickfinger's Union]]. Its development and integration of deep-world communities supported the party's later descent beneath [[Stark]].

## Geography and Access

The city provides a surface center for mining, construction, markets, and trade. Its teleportation circle admitted the returning party. [[The Funnel]] leads from its mining works toward the lower caverns.

## Description

Industrial development added palace wings, wider roads, and minecart tracks. After the moon's destruction, a great [[Crone]] fragment near the palace was built around and tunneled into for housing.

## Notable Areas

- [[The Palace of the Pit]] — The Union's seat of authority.
- [[The Deepworlders Delve]] — The Duergar home described in the early city directory.
- [[The Funnel]] — The mining operation and reopened descent toward the Gray Wastes.
- Market — Established craftspeople and newly arrived deep-world traders.

## Inhabitants and Control

[[Figma Brickfinger]] led the Union and managed the transition after [[Morel Chainsunder]]'s binding curse ended. Morel Chainsunder's apparent control of the deeper community was later revealed as the product of his surviving spell, not continuing rule by the living founder.

[[Sigrid Forgewelt]] forged weapons and employed deep-world workers. [[Beryzoz Helmscar]] forged armor; earlier visa records associated him with the [[Gheister]]. [[Courteous Cam]] and [[Otto]] oversaw the deeper excavation. By the later visits, deep-world diviners had become part of the city's mining and commercial society.

## Campaign History

[[Vizier Jade]] visited [[Red Caesar]] while the party was traveling toward the city. A frightened dwarf fleeing [[Darvinblast]] also sought refuge toward The Pit, emphasizing its role as a surface center.

After Darvinblast's liberation, deep worlders emerged and other dwarves entered formerly closed underground spaces. Figma Brickfinger handled the resulting unrest while the party traded and reinforced [[The Opal]].

On the ship's return, expansion was underway and minor conflicts remained manageable. Reports placed [[Broyish Empire]] airships in the wider region without confirming their presence at the city itself. The party acquired a bulbous bow and hardened sails; [[Domyx]] and [[Vokenar]] exchanged gems with Sigrid Forgewelt for magical weapons.

Later visits found further integration and housing adapted from the moon fragment. Red Caesar asked the miners to reopen the breakthrough below the world. Its completion enabled the companions' expedition through the Funnel when direct magical travel to [[Axis Mundi]] remained blocked.

## Final Status

The last established visits through session 036 show a functioning city and reopened mining descent. Integration was continuing rather than universally settled. The record does not separately document the city's response to the later retreating oceans or the continued safety of the lower route.
