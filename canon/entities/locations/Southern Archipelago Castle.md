# Southern Archipelago Castle

The Southern Archipelago Castle is an old human refuge on a craggy southern island, formerly sheltering [[Witness]]. The party defeated the murderous [[PAXO]] there and recovered one component of the [[Chart of the Witness]].

## Geography and Access

The castle stood on a craggy island in the southern archipelago, reached through water fouled by red algae and decay. Its cramped human-scale passages forced [[Domyx]] to stoop.

## Description

The structure remained largely intact after [[The Cataclysm]], but its interior bore damaged walls and murdered inhabitants. Sootlike black pollen caused headaches and oppressive psychic effects. Clearing that air with wind allowed the party to continue through the refuge.

## Notable Areas

- Armory — Steel weapons and a substantial supply of mithril survived.
- Inner rooms — The concealed chart and [[Potion of Fluid Adamantite]] were recovered during exploration.
- Treasury — A cache of old [[Esperanto]] coins remained, apparently reserved for a time when the earlier currency might again be useful.

## Inhabitants and Control

Human flood survivors had taken shelter with Witness, the construct cartographer of [[Stark]]. Members of [[The Garden]] told the party about the refuge. A dead inhabitant later testified that a wicked construct had killed the people and driven Witness away; it did not establish that all inhabitants escaped.

## Campaign History

The party investigated while seeking Witness's chart. [[Red Caesar]] questioned a corpse and learned about PAXO's attack. The testimony also described failed aid from [[Heaven's Bulb]], which he kept private while sharing the immediate threat with his companions.

Wind dispersed the harmful pollen and exposed the chart. It was one of the matching representations needed for the complete map, rather than a physically torn half. The companions collected it, mithril, the surviving potion, and the treasury coins.

PAXO ambushed them. [[Kerben]] removed its reflective faceplate while Red Caesar's conjured hand crushed its body. The party stored the unfamiliar faceplate with the other recovered goods.

## Final Status

PAXO was destroyed in session 025, ending the refuge's immediate murderous occupation. Witness had been driven away, not established dead. The party later completed the recovered chart with its counterpart, but the castle's resettlement or rebuilding was not recorded.
