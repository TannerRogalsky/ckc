# Gaokerena

Gaokerena is the tree of all worlds, extending from [[Arkadia]] through [[Stark]] into the [[Gray Wastes]]. [[Ceril]]'s planting restored its lower growth, creating a living refuge and a demonstrated passage between planes.

## Geography and Access

The tree's roots and branches span the worlds. Its lower trunk stands amid forest in the Gray Wastes, with a path leading to nearby [[Axis Mundi]] and [[Starfall]]'s former site. The connecting forest does not make the tree and weapon site one landmark.

Ceril used Transport via Plants to open a passage through Gaokerena into Arkadia, with [[Vokenar]] following him. His earlier descent from Arkadia had instead used a closing wound in its drained ocean basin, away from the tree.

## Description

In Arkadia, Gaokerena appears from afar as a golden pillar rising into the heavens. Its trunk combines many kinds of bark and wood; diverse leaves and fruit grow from its branches. Ivory towers surround a forum at its base.

Below Stark, the immense trunk joins a broad living island to the lower sky, its canopy spreading above grass, moss, shrubs, trees, and wildlife. The revived lower growth developed rapidly into a self-sustaining ecosystem.

## Inhabitants and Control

[[Sigil]], [[Aeris]], and [[Crone]] gathered around the tree in Arkadia. Crone recovered among its roots, receiving restorative pulses. Those divine associations do not establish that Ceril owned the tree because his planting nourished it.

## Campaign History

Ceril first visited the upper tree without learning its name. Later, as the party considered the planes' connections, he recalled it while Vokenar sighted a great pillar joining the Gray Wastes' ground and sky from [[The Opal]]. Sigil identified it as Gaokerena. Its exact relationship to Axis Mundi remained uncertain until the party reached the forest.

There, Ceril learned that the seeds he had planted to feed Aeris during her captivity had revived the lower tree. Her power had carried their growth far beyond the original planting. Sigil left a message within the tree, and Ceril opened the passage to Arkadia. Aeris granted [[Ceril's Star]] before he and Vokenar rejoined their companions.

The party followed the wooded route toward Axis Mundi, where Ceril's vegetation also surrounded Starfall.

## Final Status

The tree's restoration and traversable connection were established in session 037. The subsequent blighting influence and flood affected the lower forest and surrounding plane, but the record does not separately establish destruction of Gaokerena or the final accessibility of its lower passage.
