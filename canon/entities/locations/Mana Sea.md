A treacherous magical swamp near [[Southport]], formed when the rising oceans drowned the forests of [[Windsurf]]. Ancient trees and vines create cavern-like passages beneath a dense canopy, while wild magic disrupts divination and makes spellcasting hazardous. [[Deep Roses]] grow exclusively in its waters.

The party crossed poisonous fungi, driders, animated moss golems, enthralled [[Kuo-Toa]], and an [[Aboleth]] while seeking the source of [[Mana Sickness]]. Deep in the swamp, fallen [[Penumbra]] had cursed [[Ninki Nanka]] and transformed the dragon into the disease's source. [[Ceril]] ended the curse in session 033.
