# Mana Sea

![[images/Mana-Sea_Encounters.png]]

The Mana Sea is the dangerous magical swamp near [[Southport]], formed over the drowned forests of [[Windsurf]]. It was the source of [[Mana Sickness]] until [[Ceril]] freed [[Ninki Nanka]] from its curse.

## Geography and Access

A dense canopy forms a dark dome over the swamp. Interlocked trees and vines obstruct boats, while ancient growth creates cave-like passages above the drowned ground. Wild magic disrupts divination and makes spellcasting hazardous.

## Description

Humidity, magical fog, poisonous fungi, webs, and animated moss made the former forest perilous. [[Deep Roses]] provided treatment for the illness and grew underwater; the party also found them in a separate submerged garden during its southern voyage, so exclusive growth in this swamp is not established.

## Inhabitants and Control

An [[Aboleth]] controlled displaced drow and [[Kuo-Toa]], a threat distinct from the plague. Driders, cultists, and moss constructs obstructed the expedition. Their presence did not establish the Aboleth as the maker of every local hazard.

Ninki Nanka, once a defender of Windsurf, had reacted with fallen [[Penumbra]]. The curse amplified the dragon's poisonous nature and turned it into the contagion's source.

## Campaign History

The party entered while seeking the cause of Southport's recurring illness. It survived the poisoned forest and confronted the Aboleth's servants, freeing several from its influence. [[Domyx]] rescued [[Kerben]] from the controller, and Kerben killed it.

In Ninki Nanka's cave, the companions found the remains of the failed local expedition and recovered the missing part of the [[Chart of the Witness]]. [[Red Caesar]]'s protective sphere enabled Ceril to reach the suffering dragon.

Ceril removed the curse. Poison stopped streaming from Ninki Nanka and the foul air cleared. The grateful dragon left to protect its people, without establishing its exact destination.

## Final Status

The contagion's source and the Aboleth's local rule ended in session 033. Southport later fully recovered. Those outcomes do not establish that every swamp hazard disappeared or that the retreating oceans restored all of Windsurf.
