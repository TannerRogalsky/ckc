# Westerness

Westerness was the human city surrounding and leading up to [[Castle Kaedon]] before [[The Cataclysm]]. Its submerged remains made old-world history visible to the party.

## Geography and Access

The city lay around the castle in [[Kaedon]]. When [[Lyngbakr]] raised the castle, streets, occasional buildings, and woodland remains could be seen through the surrounding water.

## Description

Nature had reclaimed much of the sunken city. Encrusted, limed-over trees and branches marked its ancient woodland. The visible ruins did not constitute an established resettled town.

## Campaign History

[[Ceril]] had known Westerness by reputation as part of an evil human empire in the west, an inherited description rather than an impartial account of every resident. [[Kerben]] remembered visiting before the flood, while [[Red Caesar]] knew it from historical study.

Viewing the ruins gave those different relationships a shared physical setting. Subsequent exploration of the castle supplied evidence of ordinary sporting, brewing, artistic, and civic life beyond the earlier hostile reputation.

## Final Status

The submerged remains were directly observed in session 017 and again around Lyngbakr's later rise. The later destruction of the castle and retreat of the oceans did not separately establish Westerness's surviving extent or rebuilding.
