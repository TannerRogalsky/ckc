# Cutlass Cray

Cutlass Cray is a pirate trading city formerly built from boats joined into a floating settlement. As [[Stark]]'s oceans retreated, parts of the city settled onto newly exposed mountains and its residents adapted their homes and businesses.

## Geography and Access

Ships once gathered around the floating city under brightly colored flags, adding to its size as new boats attached. Some were anchored to a submerged continental shelf, described as a lacuna.

## Description

The settlement served as a busy gathering place for pirates, commerce, entertainment, worship, and recruitment. Its original boat-built setting changed during the epilogue rather than continuing as an entirely floating city.

## Notable Areas

- [[The Brine & Bodak]] — A popular establishment run by [[Transel]], where [[Octavia Crayborne]] formerly performed.
- [[The Bad Hare Day Theatre]] — The theatre associated with [[Vorgan of the Stage]].
- [[The Boardwalks]] — Waterfront managed by [[The Marid]], where [[Otto]] was recruited.
- [[The Corsairs' Court]] — Octavia Crayborne's venue.
- [[The W. H. Boutique]] — The boutique of the well-traveled [[The Wonder Hulk]].
- [[Savar Brews]] — The potion and beverage shop of [[Gilder Savar]].
- [[Bookbinders Cray]] — Library run by [[Pleasance MacLenth]].
- [[The Church of the Thirty Lights]] — Church led by [[Pastor Borm]], a gold wyrmling.

## Inhabitants and Control

Octavia Crayborne was identified as the city's mayor and a descendant of its builders. The Marid served as Port Authority. The proprietors above supplied significant local services, but their earlier associations do not establish where every business remained after the city settled.

Gilder Savar was a lich when [[Ceril]] renewed contact with him; they had traveled together during his mortal life. Vorgan of the Stage later joined [[The Opal]] as a performer.

## Campaign History

The party repeatedly used Cutlass Cray for shopping, repairs, research, and recruitment. Its venues connected the travelers with sailors and performers as well as the records needed to understand [[Lyngbakr]].

[[Vokenar]]'s final banishment returned [[Kerben]], carrying The Opal and everyone aboard, to the city. Over the following weeks, lowering seas exposed the mountains beneath parts of the settlement. Residents relocated buildings and worked to sustain the community in the changing terrain.

## Final Status

In session 038, Cutlass Cray was adapting to its new partly grounded setting. Octavia Crayborne's mayoralty was established, but the eventual placement and survival of every former floating venue were not individually recorded.
