# Hole in the Sky

The Hole in the Sky was the great breach between [[Stark]] and [[Arkadia]] through which [[Lyngbakr]] fell. The party's search for [[Penumbra]] culminated in the turtle sealing it.

## Geography and Access

The opening connected the upper plane with Stark and admitted Arkadia's water. It was nearly overhead when the party fed Lyngbakr the Penumbra recovered from [[Castle Kaedon]], with falling water around its edges.

## Campaign History

[[Sigil]] identified [[Emperor Shen]], an earlier practitioner of [[Touching the Sky]], as the party's greatest enemy. That guidance first linked the damaged heavens with titan ambition and contact with [[Aeris]]. Later revelations established [[Starfall]] as the weapon that could pierce divine boundaries and cause [[The Cataclysm]].

The party's early deliveries strengthened Lyngbakr enough to begin closing the wound. More material was still needed. [[Red Caesar]] and [[Ceril]] eventually brought [[Jack Harvey]]'s condensed hoard to the turtle, supplying enough sky-material to complete the work.

Lyngbakr rose toward Arkadia with Ceril and [[Alamar]]. Black sky-matter filled smaller wounds and then the central opening, leaving just enough space for their passage. The sky sealed beneath them and became whole blue sky, the first Red Caesar had seen in his life.

## Final Status

The breach was sealed in session 031. Repairing it did not itself restore the shattered moon or destroy Starfall; those were separate outcomes. The later flood into the [[Gray Wastes]] came through the lower boundary, rather than an established reopening of this breach.
