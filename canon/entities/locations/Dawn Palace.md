The imperial palace in the [[Broyish Capital]], used by [[Emperor Shen]] and [[Vizier Jade]]. In session 032, Shen held [[Domyx]] in a magical cage there, attempting to coerce him into accepting succession. After [[Red Caesar]], [[Ceril]], and [[Kerben]] exposed the capital's false sky and [[Naomi Ue]]'s contacts began a public uprising, Jade faked Domyx's death and freed him. Shen and Jade then fled the palace for [[Starfall]].
