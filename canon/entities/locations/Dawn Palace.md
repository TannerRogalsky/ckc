# Dawn Palace

Dawn Palace is the former imperial palace in the [[Broyish Capital]], where [[Emperor Shen]] and [[Vizier Jade]] held [[Domyx]] captive before their withdrawal to [[Starfall]].

## Campaign History

Emperor Shen imprisoned Domyx in a magical cage and tried to force him into accepting dynastic succession. When he ordered Domyx's death, Vizier Jade concealed Feign Death beneath an illusion of lethal magic.

Meanwhile, [[Red Caesar]], [[Ceril]], and [[Kerben]] exposed the city's false sky, and [[Naomi Ue]]'s contacts helped begin a public uprising. Vizier Jade opened Domyx's cage before departing with Emperor Shen. The companions recovered Domyx and [[Vokenar]]'s preserved body from the palace pinnacle.

## Final Status

The rulers had fled and the captives were recovered by the end of the session 032 visit. Later Broyish reform does not separately establish the palace's new occupants, use, or ownership.
