# Otyugh Isle

Otyugh Isle is the island site of [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]] and the [[Temple of Sigil]]. Its dungeon and petrified clergy connected the party's early discoveries with later aid for [[Southport]].

## Notable Areas

- [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]] — Alamar's former refuge near Lyngbakr and Castle Kaedon.
- [[Temple of Sigil]] — The island temple where priests and invading Kuo-Toa had been petrified.
- Island dungeon — A site sought by Kuo-Toa and associated with the encounter with their god.

## Campaign History

The party encountered [[Kuo-Toa]] seeking a home and defeated [[Blibdoolpoolp]]. It also obtained a map leading toward a Temple of Sigil.

On the later temple visit, [[Ceril]] and [[Domyx]] found twelve priests and nineteen Kuo-Toa petrified by a cockatrice roughly seventy years earlier. Ceril restored [[Father Warrick]], who freed several more priests. The restored clergy turned toward Southport's illness.

## Final Status

The island's last direct visit in session 025 established the priests' departure, not restoration of every statue or permanent control of the dungeon. The lagoon's later destruction and exposure as a caldera did not separately settle the temple's final condition.
