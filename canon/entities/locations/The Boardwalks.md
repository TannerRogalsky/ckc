# The Boardwalks

The Boardwalks are the waterfront area in [[Cutlass Cray]] run by [[The Marid]], who also serves as the city's Port Authority. [[Domyx]] and [[Vokenar]] found [[Otto]] repairing a boat here and recruited him.
