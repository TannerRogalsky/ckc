The hidden laboratory and residence of [[Boral Erendel]] in the wilderness beyond [[Brimbolyn]]. Its crumbling stone dome has been replaced in places by magical force, and arcane plant life glows around the overgrown structure.

When the party arrived in session 034, several duplicates of Boral Erendel watched from concealed positions around the building. Another created figure resembled [[Vokenar]], showing that the laboratory's replacement-body work extended beyond identical copies of Boral Erendel.
