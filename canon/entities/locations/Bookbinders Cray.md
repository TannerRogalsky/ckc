# Bookbinders Cray

Bookbinders Cray is a library in [[Cutlass Cray]] run by [[Pleasance MacLenth]]. Its records include accounts of [[Lyngbakr]] and the [[Saratan]], and its librarian offered the party a bespoke spell.
