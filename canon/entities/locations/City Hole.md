# City Hole

City Hole is the electrically lit hub cavern linking destinations in [[The Carrot Cake]]. Its barrel portals gave the party access to the park's connected zones.

## Geography and Access

A portal from the midway courtyard leads into the cavern. Barrels labeled for [[Hole Shebang]], [[Magic Hat]], [[King of the Hole]], and [[Hole on Wheels]] connect the destinations. The arrival barrel is labeled City Hole and returns to the midway.

## Description

Copper lanterns illuminate the small interior space through electrical power. A wireframe gate at the far end leads farther into the zone.

## Campaign History

[[Red Caesar]] recognized that the portals used teleportation-circle magic. He also identified the name's play on City Hall. Their investigation distinguished the cavern's technological lighting from the magic connecting the park.
