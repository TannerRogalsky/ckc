The eastern continent and homeland of the [[Broyish Empire]]. It is known for advanced technology, white-and-gold imperial colors, and the pursuit of [[Penumbra]] as an energy source.

The coastline features cliffs, mountainsides, waterfalls, and rushing rivers. The sky shows small holes from which waterfalls pour — remnants of the broken sky from [[The Cataclysm]].

The coastal approach to the [[Broyish Capital]] passes through an industrial steel town with whirring gears, copper electrical cables, and water-powered machinery. Winding stairs lead further up into the imperial capital. [[Oni]] sentries guard the approach at multiple positions, some invisible.

During the party's earlier visit, [[Kerben]] guarded [[The Opal]] while the others entered the capital.

In session 034, [[Emperor Shen]]'s absence left Broy militarily weak and politically uncertain. Imperial waters were largely empty, commerce cautiously resumed, and the old government had no clear successor.

## Session 038

[[Vizier Jade]] returned to a population uncertain about its future after [[Emperor Shen]]'s departure and death. She and [[Red Caesar]] established a new charter emphasizing laws, succession, and limits on a ruler's power. Red Caesar wrote it on the erased pages of the [[Demi-Spell]]. Vizier Jade subsequently submitted to imprisonment under the new legal order.
