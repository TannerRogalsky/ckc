# Broy

Broy is the eastern continent and homeland of the former [[Broyish Empire]]. After [[Emperor Shen]]'s defeat, [[Red Caesar]] and [[Vizier Jade]] helped establish a new legal charter for its rebuilding.

## Geography and Access

The coast features cliffs, mountainsides, waterfalls, and rushing rivers. During earlier visits, water also fell through small holes in the sky left by [[The Cataclysm]]; [[Lyngbakr]]'s later repair of the heavens ended that broken-sky setting.

The approach to the [[Broyish Capital]] passes through an industrial steel town before winding stairs ascend into the hills.

## Description

Broy is distinguished by advanced machinery and magical engineering. The old regime used white-and-gold colors and pursued [[Penumbra]] as an energy source. Coastal industry includes whirring gears, copper electrical cables, and water-powered machinery.

## Notable Areas

- [[Broyish Capital]] — The industrial metropolis in the hills above the coastal steel town.
- [[Dawn Market]] — A diverse commercial district with relic merchants and gunsmiths.
- [[Dawn Palace]] — The former emperor's palace in the capital.

## Inhabitants and Control

Emperor Shen formerly ruled through the Empire, with Vizier Jade as his powerful representative. [[Oni]] guarded the approach, sometimes invisibly. Their historical deployment does not establish the final charter's security arrangements.

## Campaign History

On an earlier visit, [[Kerben]] guarded [[The Opal]] while his companions entered the capital. Broy's machinery and defenses made imperial power visible even as its pursuit of Penumbra threatened the repair of the sky.

Emperor Shen's withdrawal left the continent militarily weakened and politically uncertain. Imperial waters emptied and commerce cautiously resumed without a clear successor to the old government.

After his death, Vizier Jade returned to a population uncertain about its future. She and Red Caesar developed laws governing succession and limiting a ruler's power. Red Caesar erased the [[Demi-Spell]] and wrote the charter on its surviving paper. The charter was established and displayed; Vizier Jade then submitted to imprisonment under its laws.

## Final Status

Broy was rebuilding under the new charter in session 038. Vizier Jade accepted accountability for her earlier crimes. Red Caesar's involvement was established, but the suggested political titles and a particular constitutional label were not all confirmed appointments or settled descriptions of the new government.
