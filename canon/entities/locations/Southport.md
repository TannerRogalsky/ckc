# Southport

Southport is the port city west of [[The Garden]] that fully recovered from [[Mana Sickness]] after the party ended its source. Its hospitality, food network, and gratitude helped sustain the campaign's final preparations.

## Geography and Access

The city lies near the [[Mana Sea]], the swamp through which the party followed the contagion to its source.

## Inhabitants and Control

[[Mayor Yoris]] governed with guidance from the deceased [[Janeera]], whose spirit could be contacted through preparations she had arranged before death. [[Rochella Golf]] returned and reunited with her family. [[Trent Indorra]] and [[Ebbie Indorra]] sought treatment for their children; [[Father Warrick]] and the restored priests helped supply care.

## Campaign History

Mana Sickness brought repeated illness despite individual treatment with [[Deep Roses]]. The agricultural network strengthened by [[Ceril]], [[Kerben]], and the Garden kept the city supplied after eastern war disrupted ordinary trade.

Mayor Yoris's earlier expedition had vanished in the Mana Sea. Janeera's guidance and the party's investigation connected the sickness with [[Ninki Nanka]]'s reaction to fallen [[Penumbra]]. The companions recovered the missing chart component and Ceril freed the dragon from its curse, ending new infections.

The city initially expected existing victims to need months of continuing treatment. In gratitude, its people expanded [[The Opal]]'s dormitories, installed a voice-pipe system, and supplied eleven new deckhands. The ship's crew contributed mithril to the work.

## Final Status

Session 038 confirmed full recovery after the party's work with Janeera and removal of the contagion's source. The earlier recovery estimate was superseded by that established outcome, rather than remaining a pending campaign objective.
