# The Bad Hare Day Theatre

The Bad Hare Day Theatre is the [[Cutlass Cray]] theatre run by [[Vorgan of the Stage]]. He later joined [[The Opal]] to gather experiences for new theatrical material; his departure did not establish a successor or closure of the theatre.
