# Rasharan's Rock

Rasharan's Rock was an evacuation destination associated with the discovery of [[Alamar]] in the magical coma imposed by [[Vlerro]]. The early account does not clearly identify whose planned evacuation it describes.

By the session 007 account, the place no longer existed. Its former location, the reason for its disappearance, and any later recovery were not established.
