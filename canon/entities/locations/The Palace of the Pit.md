# The Palace of the Pit

The Palace of the Pit is the seat of [[Figma Brickfinger's Union]] in [[The Pit]].

## Campaign History

During the city's industrial expansion, new wings were being added to the palace. The surrounding settlement widened roads and installed minecart tracks as deep-world inhabitants integrated into its society.

After a great fragment of [[Crone]] fell nearby, dwarves built around it and tunneled into its base for new housing. This adaptation did not establish that the fall caused no casualties.
