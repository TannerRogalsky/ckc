# Hole Shebang

Hole Shebang is the subtropical festival island in [[The Carrot Cake]], combining carnival games, automata, and electrical machinery. The party redirected its power to light the first lamp at [[Jack Harvey]]'s sealed gate.

## Geography and Access

A midway teleportation circle is marked by electricity arcing between poles and a roller coaster. Barrel portals in [[City Hole]] connect the interior approach with the park's other destinations.

## Description

Sandy beaches, stages, puppet automata, and games surround an electrical hub. Arcs between poles made movement dangerous before the party redirected the supply. Hostile animated creatures emerged from barrels and ruined scenery, while some games and friendly attendants continued working without the electrical system.

## Notable Areas

- Sky High Striker — A strength-testing attraction on an outer island.
- Al-Qadif's Tower of Treasures — An automated bottle game offering valuable prizes.
- Interior games — Smack a Bodak and an eight-ball table, attended by a friendly [[Magen]].
- Electrical hub — The breaker controlling the dangerous arcs and the island's contribution to the gate.
- Southern stage — A performance area with curtains, backstage boards, and concealed approaches.

## Inhabitants and Control

Magen attendants described the island as part of Jack Harvey's technological vision. Animated toys, [[Death Spark]], [[Roger Ribbons]], and [[Mary Andrews]] threatened visitors. [[Phase Cat]] appeared to be local wildlife, and [[Smoke Elemental]] formed amid burned debris; not every threat was a coordinated park guardian.

## Campaign History

The party followed the island's wiring to its central hub while surviving the electrical hazards and animated defenders. Carnival games still awarded prizes: [[Domyx]] won a spell scroll, and [[Kerben]] earned substantial gold at the bottle game and Smack a Bodak.

Domyx reached the breaker and diverted the island's power. The arcs ended and electrical lights went dark, but more defenders appeared. The party overcame them and continued through the burned passages and stage. [[Ceril]]'s healing brought new grass and flowers through the debris.

The companions returned to the midway and confirmed that one gate lamp had lit. Their success advanced access to the sanctum while leaving three other destinations to investigate.

## Final Status

At the end of session 028, the electrical hazards were shut down and the gate's first lamp was lit. Some magical games and animated activity survived the diversion. The record does not establish permanent closure, repair, or new control of the island.
