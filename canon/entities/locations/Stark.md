The world beneath [[Arkadia]] where [[Vokenar]] arrived after his long fall from the heavens. [[The League of New Stark]] operates among its surviving islands.

In session 031, [[Lyngbakr]] repaired the [[Hole in the Sky]], closing the open wound above Stark and revealing a whole blue sky. At that time, [[Starfall]] remained hidden beneath Stark at [[Axis Mundi]].

## Session 036

The party descended through [[The Funnel]], passed the stone remains of the defeated [[Vanir]]'s physical avatars, and crossed a physically traversable boundary into the [[Gray Wastes]]. This lower plane corresponds to [[Arkadia]] above Stark. [[Gaokerena]] extends through all three realms.

## Session 038

After [[Emperor Shen]]'s final defeat, the broken sky of the [[Gray Wastes]] admitted Stark's excess ocean water. Within weeks, former islands again resembled mountaintops and new lands opened to settlement. [[Cutlass Cray]] settled onto emerging mountains. [[Ceril]] helped restore vegetation, while [[Broy]] and the titan homeland developed new forms of government. The world continued without casting the [[Demi-Spell]].
