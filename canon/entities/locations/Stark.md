# Stark

Stark is the world beneath [[Arkadia]], flooded by [[The Cataclysm]] and preserved through the party's restoration of the heavens. Its drowned lands began reappearing as excess oceans drained into the [[Gray Wastes]].

## Geography and Access

Arkadia lies across the upper planar boundary; the Gray Wastes occupy a separate plane below. [[Gaokerena]] extends through all three realms. The reopened descent beneath [[The Funnel]] led past the stone remains of defeated [[Vanir]] avatars to a physically traversable lower threshold.

Stark is the world containing [[Continental Stark]], [[Broy]], [[Thalassia]], and its surviving islands, not another name for the elven continent alone.

## Description

For much of the campaign, settlements and former high ground survived as islands above the drowned landscape. [[The League of New Stark]] operated among them. Broken heavens admitted water until [[Lyngbakr]] repaired the sky.

## Campaign History

[[Vokenar]] reached Stark after his long fall from Arkadia. The party's search for [[Penumbra]] ultimately enabled Lyngbakr to seal the [[Hole in the Sky]], revealing whole blue heavens. [[Starfall]] nevertheless remained at [[Axis Mundi]] below, capable of renewing the divine threat.

The companions entered the Gray Wastes through the Funnel and reached the weapon. Its destruction and [[Emperor Shen]]'s final defeat ended that immediate danger, but the damaged lower sky continued breaking. Stark's excess water poured into the lower plane.

Over the following weeks, formerly isolated islands again appeared as mountaintops and new lands opened to settlement. Parts of [[Cutlass Cray]] settled onto exposed mountains. [[Ceril]] helped restore vegetation, while Broy and the titan homeland adopted new forms of government.

## Final Status

The world continued with its existing history and losses; the [[Demi-Spell]] was erased rather than cast. Retreating seas made restoration and settlement possible without establishing that every drowned community had been rebuilt.
