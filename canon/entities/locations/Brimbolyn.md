# Brimbolyn

Brimbolyn is the magical city at the center of [[Continental Stark]], closely associated with [[The Order of Seasons]] and its former [[Demi-Spell]] project. The campaign records its protective institutions, social exclusions, and later transition within the renewed elven monarchy.

## Geography and Access

Cliffs and extensive elven terrain-shaping protect the continent's coastal approach. Brimbolyn lies inland beyond the jungle; its lights were visible to the party from the coast.

Magically conducted trains connect the city with eleven towns and noble courts, including Kirboku and [[House Kiirnodel]]. A tram leads through jungle to [[The Academy]]. During the party's first visit, [[Knights of the Four Seasons]] inspected arrivals at the rail junction, used Augury to judge their intentions, and placed protective Sanctuary magic on admitted visitors. The protection could be forfeited through aggression.

## Description

The city combines magical infrastructure and bright illumination with less forgiving alleyway districts where gangs roam. Before [[Queen Caeradwyn]]'s fall, the surrounding elven lands were marked by aristocratic rule, slavery, and large-scale alteration of the terrain.

## Notable Areas

- [[Elvish Marketplace]] — A trading district for magical goods, including mithril work and art.
- [[The Academy]] — The Order's research center, connected by tram and later repurposed.
- [[House Kiirnodel]] — The noble house whose court was connected to the city's transport network.
- [[Yalet Aurum]]'s monument — A great stone face in the nearby jungle, built by the Galeb Duurr around his golden body; outside the city itself.

## Inhabitants and Control

Elves and tieflings predominated during the party's visits; humans were rare. [[Marshal Zem]] led the protective militia. The Order's influence shaped the city, but its academy, the Knights, the noble houses, and the surrounding continent remain distinct entities.

## Campaign History

After the Order took [[Penumbra]] from [[Castle Kaedon]], the party sought its researchers in Brimbolyn. [[Sigil]] indicated that the material had been moved beyond the jungles to a highly protected site. The party eventually reached the city by train and negotiated with the researchers at the Academy.

[[Emperor Shen]] later fired [[Starfall]] from [[Broy]] at [[Crone]] above western elven territory. The companions placed Brimbolyn in the path of the falling debris and feared extensive casualties. Subsequent visits found the Academy grieving its losses even after completion of its spell; the earlier forecast was not a complete account of the city's damage.

The party returned during the dispute over whether the finished Demi-Spell should be cast. Its surrender and eventual erasure allowed the world's restoration to proceed without remaking reality.

## Final Status

The epilogue established a renewed elven monarchy under [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] and the Academy's retirement from its original mission. The record does not provide a complete account of Brimbolyn's repairs, surviving population, or later security arrangements.
