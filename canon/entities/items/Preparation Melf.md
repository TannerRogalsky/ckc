# Preparation Melf

Preparation Melf is a flask of sticky acid crafted by [[Kerben]] from the unnamed black dragon defeated at [[Castle Kaedon]]. [[Ceril]] considered that dragon likely to be one of [[Xarag]]'s offspring.

## Description

The flask is a throwable acid weapon. When it breaks against a target, its sticky contents continue burning until removed.

## Campaign History

[[Domyx]] asked whether Kerben could make something useful from the dragon's remains. Kerben examined its resilient, acid-resistant scales and used recovered material, his poisoner's kit, and supplies from the shared hold to produce the flask. The ruined wyrmlings offered little suitable material.

Before the northern voyage, Kerben also worked black dragon acid into a weapon-delivered poison. He used that preparation to prevent the [[Rimefire Hydra]] from regrowing its heads. The account does not explicitly identify that poison as the original Preparation Melf flask.

## Final Status

Kerben is the flask's maker and last established holder. No clearly identified throw, transfer, or destruction of the original flask is recorded, so its disposition at campaign end remains uncertain. Use of the later black dragon poison does not by itself establish that this flask was consumed.
