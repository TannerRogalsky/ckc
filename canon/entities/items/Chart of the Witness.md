A magically encoded world chart created by [[Witness]], who walked over mountains and across the ocean floor to map all of [[Stark]] before [[The Cataclysm]].

The party recovered one half from the [[Southern Archipelago Castle]] in session 025. A prior Southport expedition carried the other half into the [[Mana Sea]] and died near [[Ninki Nanka]]'s cave. In session 033, the party recovered that half, and [[Red Caesar]] joined and decoded both pieces.

The completed chart depicts both the old world's topography and the surviving islands of the present. It includes deep ocean trenches but no direct route to [[Axis Mundi]], supporting the belief that the world's hollow interior lies beyond a planar boundary rather than merely underground.
