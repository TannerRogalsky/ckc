# Chart of the Witness

The Chart of the Witness is a magically encoded map of [[Stark]] made by [[Witness]] before [[The Cataclysm]]. The party recovered and decoded its complementary portions, gaining a view of both the drowned world and its surviving islands.

## Description

Witness mapped the world by walking over its highest mountains and across the ocean floor. The completed chart records elevations and ocean trenches, allowing the old landscape to be read against present-day [[Thalasia]].

The chart's two portions each occupy a complete surface but contain only part of the information. They require assembly and magical decoding; they are not simply two pieces of a torn sheet.

## Campaign History

The party recovered the first portion from the [[Southern Archipelago Castle]], where [[Red Caesar]] cleared away dangerous black pollen to reveal it. They placed the incomplete chart among their maps aboard [[The Opal]].

An earlier expedition from [[Southport]] had taken the complementary portion into the [[Mana Sea]]. [[Vokenar]]'s Arcane Eye located it among the expedition's remains near [[Ninki Nanka]]'s cave. After the party reached those remains, they recovered the chart and returned to The Opal for their first portion.

Red Caesar interpreted and magically decoded the two maps together. The resulting topography showed deep trenches that might offer places to begin digging, but no direct entrance to [[Axis Mundi]]. Its continuous surface supported the party's suspicion that the world's hollow interior lay beyond a planar boundary rather than somewhere they could simply teleport underground.

## Final Status

The completed chart remains among the party's maps aboard The Opal at campaign end. Red Caesar's work as its decoder does not establish individual ownership.
