A powerful fighting gauntlet forged by [[Sigrid Forgewelt]] from [[Domyx]]'s former hammer. It wraps the hand Domyx used for [[Touching the Sky]], leaving his blue palm exposed and channeling force through his blows.

When attuned, the sky-blue coloration on Domyx's hand spreads across one side of his body and takes on the appearance of the living heavens, including the constellations visible at night.
