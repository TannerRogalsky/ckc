# Cestus of the Clear Sky

The Cestus of the Clear Sky is a sky-infused fighting gauntlet forged by [[Sigrid Forgewelt]] for [[Domyx]]. It transforms his connection to [[Touching the Sky]] into a weapon and a visible reflection of the restored heavens.

## Description

The gauntlet resembles simple leather wrappings across the back of the hand Domyx used to touch the sky, leaving his blue palm exposed. Once he attunes to it, the blue spreads across one side of his body and takes on the appearance of the living sky, including its nighttime constellations.

Its magic gathers clouds into his fist and adds force to his blows. Domyx can draw on his restorative nimbus while striking, recovering vitality and strengthening the blow together.

## Campaign History

After the sky was restored, Domyx returned to Sigrid Forgewelt in [[The Pit]]. He traded the hammer she had previously made for him and his diamonds for a new weapon. She fashioned the cestus around his sky-touched hand, making his connection to the heavens central to its form and power.

Domyx carried it into the confrontation with [[Boril Erendel]], where gathering clouds enlarged his striking fist. During the descent beneath [[The Funnel]], its blows drove a stone giant back into the rock. He later used it against [[Emperor Shen]] at [[Axis Mundi]].

## Final Status

The cestus remains with Domyx at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
