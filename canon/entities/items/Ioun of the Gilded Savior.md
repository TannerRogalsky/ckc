# Ioun of the Gilded Savior

The Ioun of the Gilded Savior is a restorative Ioun stone acquired by [[Vokenar]] in [[Cutlass Cray]]. He bonded it to [[Red Caesar]], allowing his own healing to aid the wizard as well.

## Description

The stone is a solid-gold sphere. Once bonded, it traces a rapid figure-eight orbit between its wearer and a chosen companion, appearing as a faint halo with a ringing buzz. The halo glows when its magic activates.

It shares restorative power when its wearer heals himself and can empower an occasional decisive strike.

## Campaign History

Vokenar sought the stone primarily for its shared healing during the party's equipment purchases before confronting [[Farraday]]. He chose Red Caesar as its bonded companion.

Its restorative link helped protect Red Caesar during the fight in [[Tome Keeper's Pyramid]]. At [[House Erendel]], the stone carried healing to him while Vokenar sustained the party, keeping the wizard from collapse and helping preserve his protective magic. The bond continued aiding Red Caesar during the final confrontation at [[Axis Mundi]].

## Final Status

The stone remains in Vokenar's custody at campaign end, following its last established use. No transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded. The epilogue does not describe whether it accompanied his youthful return to [[Arkadia]].
