# Gith Shard Glaive

The Gith Shard Glaive is an enchanted halberd crafted by [[Sigrid Forgewelt]] for [[Domyx]], using the star gem obtained after the encounter with [[Aeon]].

## Description

The weapon incorporates a ruby and a Githzerai star gem containing condensed spatial magic. Sigrid Forgewelt's understanding of unusual metals and enchantments allowed her to work that magic into its design.

Its cleaving force can project beyond the halberd's physical reach as a bolt of psychic energy. In use, its head can appear to detach into dark purple, star-filled energy, strike a distant foe, and return to the weapon.

## Campaign History

Domyx commissioned the glaive during the party's return to [[The Pit]], supplying the star gem, a ruby, and gold. It extended his ability to fight beyond ordinary close combat.

On the northern voyage, he carried it onto the ice against the [[Rimefire Hydra]]. Its projecting strikes later helped him reach distant and airborne enemies in [[The Carrot Cake]], including the [[Flame Skull]] threats, and it remained in use during the expedition through the [[Mana Sea]].

Domyx subsequently acquired the [[Cestus of the Clear Sky]] as his principal close weapon. That commission used his former hammer, leaving the glaive distinct from the weapon he traded.

## Final Status

The glaive remains with Domyx at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
