# Ioun of Crimson Dreams

The Ioun of Crimson Dreams is a defensive Ioun stone acquired by [[Red Caesar]] in [[Cutlass Cray]] before the confrontation with [[Farraday]].

## Description

The stone is a crimson octahedron that strengthens its attuned wearer's defenses. It can protect against acid, thunder, cold, fire, or lightning, with the wearer choosing a different energy as circumstances change.

## Campaign History

After the party's return from the [[Mana Sea]], Red Caesar acquired and attuned to the stone during their equipment purchases in Cutlass Cray. Its adaptable protection broadened his defenses for the campaign's remaining dangers.

## Final Status

The stone remains with Red Caesar at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
