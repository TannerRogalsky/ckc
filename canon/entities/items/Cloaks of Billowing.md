# Cloaks of Billowing

The Cloaks of Billowing are enchanted cloaks [[Red Caesar]] purchased for the entire party at the [[Elvish Marketplace]] in [[Brimbolyn]].

## Description

The cloaks billow dramatically at their wearers' command without requiring attunement.

## Campaign History

Red Caesar bought the set during the party's return to Brimbolyn, seeking matching magical cloaks for his companions.

When the party entered a tavern in [[Broy]], [[Vokenar]] set his cloak billowing and the others joined him. The display drew attention to the unfamiliar visitors, including [[Naomi Ue]], whom they approached for help finding [[Obould]].

The cloaks remained in use during the later campaign. Red Caesar unfurled his while revealing [[Antumbra]] to his companions and again used it during the descent beneath [[The Funnel]].

## Final Status

The cloaks remain with their respective party members at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction of the set is recorded.
