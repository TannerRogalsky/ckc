# Crone's Contempt

Crone's Contempt is a magical potion from [[Janeera]]'s collection, given to [[Kerben]] before the party entered the [[Mana Sea]].

## Description

The potion temporarily adds force to repeated weapon strikes.

## Campaign History

In [[Southport]], [[Mayor Yoris]] offered potions from Janeera's collection to help the party investigate the source of [[Mana Sickness]]. The companions initially considered giving Crone's Contempt to [[Domyx]], then chose Kerben because its power suited his repeated attacks. [[Red Caesar]] took [[Green Slaadi Whiskey]] as the other advance reward.

## Final Status

Kerben is the last confirmed recipient. No consumption, transfer, or loss is recorded. With no established user or later custody statement, the potion's whereabouts at campaign end remain uncertain.
