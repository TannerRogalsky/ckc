# Mangonel

The Mangonel is a siege catapult recovered from [[Castle Kaedon]] and later mounted aboard [[The Opal]]. It preserves evidence of the castle's celebrations before its ruin.

## Description

The machine has a rear weight-adjustment mechanism that allows its launch force to be calibrated. Decorative fireworks found nearby came from [[Broy]], suggesting that it had once launched fireworks during peaceful celebrations rather than serving only as a weapon.

## Campaign History

During the confrontation with the castle's adult black dragon, the castle's magic animated the mangonel and made it turn and fire at [[Kerben]]. The enchantment then faded, leaving the mechanism usable.

Kerben later adjusted it for [[Domyx]]'s weight and launched him across a gap to the castle's southern platform. The catapult survived the unusual use intact. Recovering it for the ship was initially a proposal, but the party subsequently brought it aboard The Opal and mounted it there.

## Final Status

The mangonel remains an established resource of The Opal's company, under Kerben's later command. No subsequent removal, loss, or destruction of the machine is recorded.
