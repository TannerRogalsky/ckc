# Orkland Pin of Courage

The Orkland Pin of Courage is an award earned by [[Obould]] for saving orc tribes in southern battles on the high seas.

## Description

The pin commemorates Obould's service during early skirmishes decades before the campaign. He valued it as a personal keepsake of that history.

## Campaign History

The pin was among the treasures [[Xarag]] stole from [[The Opal]]'s hold. The party recovered it from [[Xarag's Island]] while Obould remained captive and placed it back in the ship's shared hold.

After his rescue, Obould retrieved the pin together with [[Obould's Wedding Band]]. Their return preserved possessions the party could otherwise have sold or traded during his absence.

## Final Status

The pin was returned to Obould and remains his keepsake at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
