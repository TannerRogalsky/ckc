# Tomb of Lenth the Rugged

The Tomb of Lenth the Rugged is a gilded sarcophagus recovered from [[Ninki Nanka]]'s abandoned hoard and entrusted to [[Pleasance MacLenth]] at [[Bookbinders Cray]].

## Description

The tomb bears [[Lenth the Rugged]]'s name in the language of [[Westerness]]. Its slightly open lid revealed ancient bones and tattered robes. A familiar scent recalled the perfume [[Deception by Lenth]].

## Campaign History

The party found the tomb among the dragon's treasures in the [[Mana Sea]]. After ending the source of [[Mana Sickness]], they recovered it and intended to return it to Lenth the Rugged's family.

[[Red Caesar]] carried it on a floating disc to Pleasance MacLenth in [[Cutlass Cray]]. She confirmed that Lenth the Rugged was her grandfather and accepted the recovered remains as family history. Consulting her books, she described the late-life romance that had drawn him back toward [[Southport]].

Pleasance MacLenth kept the tomb for preservation and display in the library.

## Final Status

The tomb remains with Pleasance MacLenth at Bookbinders Cray at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
