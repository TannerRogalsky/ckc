A gilded tomb inscribed with the name of [[Lenth the Rugged]], containing his ancient bones and tattered robes. It was found among [[Ninki Nanka]]'s abandoned hoard in the [[Mana Sea]]. In session 034, [[Red Caesar]] returned it to [[Pleasance MacLenth]] for preservation and display at [[Bookbinders Cray]].
