# Branch of the Itinerant

The Branch of the Itinerant is an enchanted wand acquired by [[Ceril]] at the [[Elvish Marketplace]] in [[Brimbolyn]].

## Description

The branch strengthens its attuned wielder's spellcasting as a magical focus. Pointing it along a chosen path makes flowers bloom along that path.

## Campaign History

During the party's return to Brimbolyn, Ceril sought a wand rather than a staff to improve his magic. The elven sellers offered the branch in exchange for a gemstone, substituting it for the mithril initially considered for the trade.

## Final Status

The branch remains with Ceril at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
