# Wyvern Poison

Wyvern Poison is an injury poison crafted by [[Kerben]] from recovered wyvern glands with help aboard the [[Goblin Traders]]' ship. He spent the prepared dose against the [[Tatzelwurm]].

## Description

The purified poison can coat a weapon or ammunition and remains ready until the next strike delivers it. Its purpose is to inflict a potent burst of poison harm.

## Campaign History

Kerben collected wyvern glands during the party's earlier journey. [[Gunk Grodley]] directed him to his wife in the traders' hold, where equipment and preserving ingredients let him refine the glands into his first completed poison. He traded other goods and supplied gold for the work.

On the northern journey, Kerben coated a bullet with Wyvern Poison and fired it into the Tatzelwurm. The preparation was expended during that attack. The dragon's later defeat provided material for [[Tatzelwurm Gizzard Juice]].

## Final Status

The prepared dose was consumed against the Tatzelwurm. No replacement supply is recorded.
