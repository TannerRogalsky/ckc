# Sunset's Solace

Sunset's Solace is a spell-storing shield crafted by [[Sigrid Forgewelt]] for [[Vokenar]]. It broadens the magic he can carry into an expedition.

## Description

The shield requires attunement and contains an embedded ruby. Parts of the gem glow to indicate the magic held within it.

Spells can be cast into the shield and released later. Vokenar can replace stored magic during travel, preparing situational spells or restorative power for the next destination.

## Campaign History

During the visit to [[The Pit]] for ship improvements, Vokenar sought equipment that supported spellcasting rather than another weapon. Sigrid Forgewelt provided the shield in exchange for a rare gem. It initially held Slow, giving him access to magic beyond his usual healing and protection.

On the northern journey, he used its stored Prayer of Healing after the [[Tatzelwurm]] battle. The shared restorative aura let the party recover quickly while [[Ceril]]'s summoned bestial spirit remained available.

## Final Status

The shield remains with Vokenar at campaign end, following its last established use. No transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded. The epilogue does not describe whether it accompanied his youthful return to [[Arkadia]].
