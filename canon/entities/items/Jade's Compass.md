# Jade's Compass

Jade's Compass is a valuable, nonmagical seafarer's compass formerly belonging to [[Vizier Jade]]. Recovered from [[Jack Harvey]]'s submerged vault, it was later given to [[Naomi Ue]] as payment for help in [[Broy]].

## Description

The compass is made of gold with jade inlays, rubies, electrum metalwork, and a diamond reticle. Vizier Jade's name is engraved on its back, and it has a fitting where a gold chain could have been attached.

Years underwater left it water-damaged and tarnished, although it remained a valuable object. [[Red Caesar]] found no active enchantment.

## Campaign History

[[Kerben]] and [[Domyx]] found the compass while exploring the flooded vault beneath [[The Carrot Cake]]. Its age and condition suggested that someone had carried it inside years earlier and become trapped. Remembering [[Theo Harvey]]'s account of Vizier Jade's missing servant, the party suspected [[The Tyrant]] rather than treating the inscription as proof that Vizier Jade had visited herself. They subsequently encountered The Tyrant within the vault.

While preparing to rescue Domyx and [[Vokenar]] in Broy, Red Caesar bargained with Naomi Ue for a diversion near the imperial palace. She accepted gold, the compass, and plans from The Carrot Cake. Her people were to act when the city's false sky changed and then withdraw into hiding.

## Final Status

The compass was transferred to Naomi Ue as payment. No later return, transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded; the party no longer held it.
