# Obould's Wedding Band

Obould's Wedding Band is an inscribed ring recovered from [[Xarag's Island]] and presented by [[Obould]] to [[Lady Jacinthe]] when he renewed their engagement.

## Description

The ring was kept in a satin-lined box. Its inscription and personal significance made it one of the treasures Obould particularly valued.

## Campaign History

[[Xarag]] stole the ring when the dragon broke into [[The Opal]]'s hold. The party recovered it from the dragon's island while Obould was still a prisoner and returned it to the ship's hold.

After his rescue, Obould retrieved it along with the [[Orkland Pin of Courage]]. It reminded him of his postponed marriage to Lady Jacinthe and their earlier choice to pursue separate responsibilities. He took the band with him to [[The White Drake]] to address that unfinished bond.

When Obould permanently retired from The Opal, he knelt and offered the ring to Lady Jacinthe. She accepted, and they agreed to lead [[The League of New Stark]] together. Their marriage aboard The White Drake followed after the party's victory.

## Final Status

The band was presented to Lady Jacinthe for the renewed engagement, followed by their marriage. No later loss or transfer is recorded.
