A wedding ring with an inscription, kept in a satin-lined box. It was among the few items [[Obould]] truly cared about when [[The Opal]]'s hold was broken into by the [[Xarag]], and the party recovered it from [[Xarag's Island]] in session 026.

In session 034, Obould used the ring to propose again to [[Lady Jacinthe]]. She accepted, and the two agreed to lead [[The League of New Stark]] together.
