# Deep Roses

Deep Roses are underwater medicinal plants used to cure [[Mana Sickness]]. Their collection and delivery helped [[Southport]], although lasting recovery required [[Ceril]] to end the contagion spreading from [[Ninki Nanka]].

## Description

The plants are roses found growing beneath the water, including a garden submerged by [[The Cataclysm]] in the southern archipelago. Ceril recognized them as the remedy needed for Mana Sickness and could encourage their growth for harvesting.

They cure individual cases, but do not protect patients from renewed infection while the source remains active. Maintaining enough plants for continuing treatment demands sustained cultivation.

## Campaign History

In [[Broy]], [[Trent Indorra]] and [[Ebbie Indorra]] were seeking medicine for their sick children in Southport, near the [[Mana Sea]]. They had bought unsuitable herbs. Ceril identified Deep Roses as the cure they actually needed and offered to gather them.

During the southern voyage, Ceril found the plants in the submerged garden occupied by the [[Bane Siren]]. He encouraged their growth and collected a supply for [[The Opal]]'s hold, then resumed harvesting after the siren's defeat.

Priests freed from the [[Temple of Sigil]] helped bring Deep Roses to Southport. The treatment cured individual patients, but they became ill again while the contagion continued. Ceril's removal of Ninki Nanka's curse ended new infections and allowed the ongoing treatment to produce lasting recovery.

## Final Status

Deep Roses had entered Southport's treatment supply through the freed priests, and the town fully recovered by the epilogue. The remaining quantity of the delivered medicine and any separate remainder aboard The Opal are not established. The record does not indicate that the plants themselves were exhausted or destroyed.
