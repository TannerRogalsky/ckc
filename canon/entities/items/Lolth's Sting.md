# Lolth's Sting

Lolth's Sting is a poison acquired by [[Kerben]] from the [[Goblin Traders]]. It weakens a victim's ability to fight and can render a vulnerable target unconscious.

## Description

The poison is jet-black and oily. Its value lies in incapacitating or impairing a target rather than inflicting direct injury.

## Campaign History

Aboard the traders' ship, [[Gunk Grodley]] directed Kerben to his wife in the hold, where she prepared and sold poisons. Kerben traded his bulette eggs for a bottle of Lolth's Sting.

The merchant hoped to raise the creatures as shipboard defenders. Kerben warned her that keeping them aboard could endanger the vessel, but made the trade because he could not reasonably raise them aboard [[The Opal]] either.

On the northern journey, Kerben applied it through [[Shark's Edge]] against the [[Tatzelwurm]]. The poison failed to incapacitate the dragon, but the dose was spent. A suggestion to replenish his poisons later did not establish a replacement.

## Final Status

The acquired dose was consumed against the Tatzelwurm. No later replacement supply is recorded.
