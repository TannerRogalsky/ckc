# Spelljammer

The Spelljammer is a crystalline device given by [[Obould]] to [[Kerben]] for mounting aboard [[The Opal]]. It enabled the ship's later voyages beyond its own plane.

## Description

The device resembles a sizable geode, with a rocky exterior and bristling, iridescent crystals inside. Mounted aboard a vessel, it enables travel through the astral and to other planes.

[[Vokenar]]'s consecrations aboard The Opal remain enduring enchantments, although they become temporarily inactive while the ship travels through the astral.

## Campaign History

Southern orcish guilds gave the device to Obould, who kept it until The Opal could withstand its power. At his wedding to [[Lady Jacinthe]], he reaffirmed Kerben's permanent captaincy and handed over the Spelljammer as a means of exploring other worlds.

Kerben first chose to rebuild [[40 Karats]] with [[Theo Harvey]]'s help. In later years, he returned to The Opal and sailed among the stars, fulfilling the device's purpose without establishing a final destination.

## Final Status

The Spelljammer remains aboard The Opal under Kerben's command during his later journey among the stars. No transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded, and the voyage's ultimate destination remains open.
