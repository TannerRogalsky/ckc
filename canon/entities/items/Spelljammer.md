A geode-like device with a rocky exterior and iridescent crystals inside, given by [[Obould]] to [[Kerben]] at Obould's wedding to [[Lady Jacinthe]]. Southern orcish guilds had given it to Obould, who kept it until [[The Opal]] could withstand its power.

Mounted aboard the ship, it enables travel through the astral and to other planes. Kerben initially chose to rebuild [[40 Carats]], then eventually sailed into the stars. [[Vokenar]]'s consecrations aboard The Opal remain enduring enchantments but are temporarily inactive during astral travel.
