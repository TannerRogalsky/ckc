# Potion of Clairvoyance

The Potion of Clairvoyance is a magical preparation associated with [[Lenth the Rugged]] and recovered by the party at the [[Southern Archipelago Castle]]. [[Ceril]] later confirmed that he carried it.

## Description

The clear liquid bends light like a lens when swirled, making objects behind it appear farther away. Drinking it grants temporary remote sight or hearing through a magical sensor, without requiring the drinker's continued concentration.

## Campaign History

The party found the potion in an ornate bottle in the castle's storeroom, alongside the [[Potion of Eels]]. [[Red Caesar]] identified its magic and connected it to Lenth the Rugged's innovations.

In [[Cutlass Cray]], Ceril referred to it among his available supplies. He still recalled carrying it at [[Axis Mundi]], although he did not choose a use for it during that conversation.

## Final Status

Ceril is the last confirmed holder. No consumption, transfer, or loss is recorded. With no established user or later custody statement, the potion's disposition at campaign end remains unconfirmed.
