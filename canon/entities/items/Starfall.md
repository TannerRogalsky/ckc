# Starfall

Starfall was an ancient elemental weapon controlled by the [[Broyish Empire]], used by [[Emperor Shen]] to attack the heavens. The party's [[Antumbra]] sabotage destroyed it at [[Axis Mundi]], preventing its intended shot at [[Sigil]].

## Description

The device was a vast stone ring combining the four elements of [[Stark]]: clouds coursed across it, water moved within it, and portions of its surface burned. Its fusion of those elements produced a stream of energy powerful enough to pierce the barriers between worlds and wound divine beings. [[Aeris]] described it as combining everything needed to rebuild the world in a single stream.

At its center, [[Aeris]]'s captive mortal form appeared as a collection of stars drawn from the black sky overhead. Her stolen power fueled the weapon's first attack on the heavens; later shots required [[Penumbra]], fragments of her sky-body. Starfall could also make Emperor Shen invulnerable while it remained active.

The ring emitted a pulsing rumble and dangerous energy, with a hot wind resisting those who approached. Its surface could surrender fire to nearby magic, and plants grew from its earth. Before its destruction, [[Ceril]]'s cultivation had grown into trees that surrounded the ring and held its supports.

## Campaign History

### Origins and the Broken Sky

The party received differing accounts of Starfall's beginnings.

[[Vokenar]]'s Legend Lore described a device created in Hell to free demons by piercing the membrane between worlds, a barrier akin to divine skin:

> created in hell to free demons into the world above

Source: [[chunks/session_022/chunk_0001|Vokenar's Legend Lore revelation]].

The divination also said that Starfall had been brought into the world above centuries earlier and lodged in [[Broy]], but magical concealment prevented it from identifying who brought the weapon or why.

[[The Tyrant]] later described Starfall as born with [[Stark]] and composed of the world's four elements:

> It is the core, very center of Stark, comprised of its four elements. It was born when the world was born.

Source: [[chunks/session_031/chunk_0003|The Tyrant's account]].

The first account places Starfall's creation in Hell; the second ties its birth to the birth of Stark. The transcript does not reconcile those claims, leaving the precise origin unresolved.

Aeris explained that Starfall had connected Stark with [[Arkadia]] before being repurposed as a weapon. Around [[The Cataclysm]], roughly sixty years before the party reached the weapon, Ceril and witnesses from [[Castle Kaedon]] saw streaks of light crossing the heavens from the east. Their matching recollections led the party to suspect that Starfall had opened the [[Hole in the Sky]], allowing Arkadia's oceans to flood the world. Later divination supported that conclusion, and Emperor Shen admitted that he had tried to destroy the sky without fully succeeding.

[[Vizier Jade]] confirmed that the Empire was gathering Penumbra to fire the weapon again. In a vision shared by Ceril and Vokenar, Aeris revealed her imprisonment and explained how her stolen physical form had been turned against her godhood. She believed the ring lay deep beneath the world; the party had not yet established its precise location. Ceril encouraged the plants around her, giving the captive goddess food and leaving living growth that later transformed the site.

### The Attack on the Moon

During his training with [[Crone]], Vokenar witnessed Emperor Shen load Penumbra into Starfall and fire at the moon. A silent white streak widened into the colors of the four elements as it crossed the sky above [[Brimbolyn]]. [[Kerben]] observed it from [[The Opal]]. The attack shattered most of the moon, leaving a narrow crescent, and endangered Crone.

The shot was also an act of war against the elves. Falling debris, tsunamis, and disrupted tides followed; the party feared that damaging the moon could disturb time itself. The Tyrant subsequently identified Axis Mundi, in the [[Gray Wastes]] beneath Broy, as Starfall's location. [[Fharan]] confirmed that the next target was Sigil. [[Lyngbakr]]'s repair of the Hole in the Sky did not disable the weapon or free Aeris from it.

### Sabotage and Destruction

[[Red Caesar]], [[Keys Caeradel]], and [[Illidrielle Gandara]] created Antumbra as a sabotaged Penumbra sphere. Kerben planted it in the supply aboard [[The White Drake]] destined for the Empire. The party later concluded that using it as fuel would permanently destroy both the sphere and Starfall, but could not initially confirm whether it had reached or been used by the weapon.

Permanent protection identified as Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum prevented direct magical entry to Starfall. In the Gray Wastes, Ceril identified scars that brightened as the lower sky darkened, and Red Caesar distinguished firing paths from natural features. Their curvature complicated precise triangulation, but following them guided The Opal eastward. They reached [[Gaokerena]] and then the ring, now overgrown by Ceril's earlier planting.

Emperor Shen was preparing to fire at Sigil and remained protected by Starfall. Vizier Jade had reserved Antumbra as the final ammunition and arranged for him to load it himself. When he pushed it into the ring, the opening became black and star-filled, briefly trapped his hand, and exploded. The weapon was utterly destroyed, its protection of Emperor Shen ended, and the shot at Sigil never occurred.

## Final Status

Starfall's wreckage lay at Axis Mundi; no usable weapon survived. The confrontation continued against the [[Vanir]] without a separate account of Aeris's mortal fragment leaving the wreckage. After Emperor Shen's final defeat, Aeris appeared well in Arkadia, establishing the rescue's successful outcome. The moon was also seen reforming as Crone recovered.
