# The Jewel of Alfheimer

The Jewel of Alfheimer is a landscape painting by [[Aramil Kiirnodel]], recovered from [[Castle Kaedon]] and returned to elven sellers in [[Brimbolyn]] through a trade by [[Red Caesar]].

## Description

The framed painting depicts Brimbolyn before [[The Cataclysm]], showing a lush jungle within a continent and no visible surrounding floodwater. Much of its terrain resembles the preserved landscape the party later observed in the elven lands.

The title is embossed on the frame, and Aramil Kiirnodel's signature appears at the lower right. Its artistry and provenance made it a valuable recovered work.

## Campaign History

[[Domyx]] uncovered the painting while brushing dust from a wall at Castle Kaedon. The party placed it in the shared hold aboard [[The Opal]].

At the [[Elvish Marketplace]], Red Caesar traded it for the [[Boots of the Alvargard]], seeking greater mobility. The elven sellers welcomed the painting's return and intended to hang it.

## Final Status

The painting was transferred to the elven sellers at the Elvish Marketplace. No later disposition is recorded; the party no longer held it.
