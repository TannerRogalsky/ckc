# Penumbra

Penumbra is the physical substance of the fallen sky, sought as magical fuel and as the material needed to restore [[Aeris]]'s broken heavens. The party delivered its major recovered deposits to [[Lyngbakr]], culminating in the repair of the [[Hole in the Sky]].

## Description

Living Penumbra appears as black material filled with points of light like stars. Fragments can join together: the great stone recovered from [[Castle Kaedon]] consisted of triangular pieces fused into a blocky, fractal mass, while [[Jack Harvey]]'s vault held both crystalline shards and sand-like deposits. Near Lyngbakr, the castle's fragment became almost weightless.

Penumbra stores extraordinary magical energy. Drawing on that energy can exhaust it, leaving mundane obsidian where living sky once remained. An early beach find was already fading and had no useful magical function; the [[Darvenlast]] quarry contained a much larger example of depletion.

[[Akasha]] is Penumbra's liquid counterpart. [[Red Caesar]] demonstrated that immense pressure could turn collected Akasha into a small, star-filled sphere of genuine Penumbra. His later spell, [[Obvolvo Caelum]], could condense existing deposits into a portable orb without discarding their sky-material. [[Antumbra]] was a separately altered sphere intended to destroy anything that consumed its energy, rather than a property of ordinary Penumbra.

## Campaign History

### Early Finds and Competing Uses

[[Sunspite]] used Penumbra as a magical battery while seeking to sink Castle Kaedon. [[Keys Caeradel]] and [[Illidrielle Gandara]] carried away a castle fragment; [[Ceril]] recognized Illidrielle Gandara's affiliation with [[The Order of Seasons]] before the party met the researchers at [[The Academy]]. Another fragment recovered from a defeated golem was carried by [[Vokenar]].

The Duergar of [[The Deepworlders Delve]] drew power from Penumbra. Divine guidance directed the party to find [[Morel Chainsunder]] before recovering the Darvenlast supply. Ending his surviving binding spell revealed the hidden quarry. Decades of use had drained the enormous stone's exterior into ordinary black glass, but Vokenar located a surviving, star-filled core. [[Figma Brickfinger]]'s workers cut away the dead shell, and [[Kilosaurus]] hauled the remaining stone to [[The Opal]]. The depletion raised concern that treating Penumbra as fuel was consuming the sky rather than healing it.

The party also recovered an untouched fragment from [[Gnoll]] territory and delivered its gathered supply to Lyngbakr at [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]], following the goddesses' direction. [[Sigil]] revealed that the Order had moved its own fragments to a protected place beyond [[Brimbolyn]]'s jungles.

### The Academy and Castle Kaedon

At the Academy, the party learned that the Order used Penumbra to accelerate the [[Demi-Spell]], sparing researchers some of the life-consuming cost of its development. The Order agreed to pause new collection while retaining its existing stock. In return, the party promised its collection if repairing the sky failed.

[[Lady Jacinthe]] warned that the [[Broyish Empire]] was gathering similar stones for a great weapon. Its demand for Penumbra later proved to be fuel for [[Starfall]], setting the Empire's purpose against the party's restoration work.

After defeating the [[Nalfeshne]], the party recovered the fused mass from Castle Kaedon's throne room. It was carried aboard the [[Gheister]] and pushed into the sea above Lyngbakr, who consumed it and other fragments shaken from the ruins. This helped the turtle begin repairing the sky, but did not supply enough material to complete the work.

Vokenar kept a separate piece, polished it into a reflective focus, and fixed it to the back of his shield for Scrying. Red Caesar's subsequent synthesis at the Academy produced Penumbra from Akasha; work with Keys Caeradel and Illidrielle Gandara then created the sabotaged Antumbra used against Starfall.

### Jack Harvey's Hoard and the Restoration

Lighting all four lamps in [[The Karat Cake]] opened the way to Jack Harvey's inner sanctum. [[Vizier Jade]] and [[Emperor Shen]] exploited the party's rest to search for the hoard while the companions were trapped away from the opened entrance. During that interruption, Red Caesar completed Obvolvo Caelum.

Inside Jack Harvey's underwater vault, the party found Penumbra crystallized across the walls. Red Caesar condensed the shards and fine deposits into a small orb containing more sky-material than all the party's earlier finds combined.

Red Caesar and Ceril delivered the orb to Lyngbakr while their companions delayed the Empire at the lagoon. Released from its condensed form, the Penumbra flowed into the turtle. Lyngbakr rose into the heavens with Ceril and [[Alamar]], sealed the central hole and smaller wounds, and returned to [[Arkadia]].

## Final Status

The major supplies gathered for restoration were consumed by Lyngbakr and returned to the repaired sky. Antumbra was consumed separately in Starfall's destruction. Neither outcome establishes that every Penumbra fragment in the world was used up.

Vokenar retains the polished piece used as his shield's divination focus; no later loss or transfer is recorded. Its transport during his youthful return to Arkadia is not described. The Order's previously retained stock was never established as transferred to the party, and its remaining quantity is unrecorded.
