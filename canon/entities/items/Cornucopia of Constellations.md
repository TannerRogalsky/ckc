# Cornucopia of Constellations

The Cornucopia of Constellations is an elven feast charm commissioned by [[Ceril]] before the party's descent through [[The Funnel]]. It supplies materials for his magic and strengthens Heroes' Feast into Goddess's Feast.

## Description

Worn at the neck, the cornucopia lets Ceril grow the food needed for a magical feast. Its conjured meal can appear as stars that roll onto a picnic blanket, arrange themselves into constellations of food, and become edible.

The feast strengthens its participants' vitality and protects them from poison and other chosen energies. Eating another meal later that day can change the additional protections.

## Campaign History

Ceril used diamonds to commission the charm from the elves, seeking a continuing supply of feast materials rather than purchasing them afresh for each casting. The commission also enhanced the feast's sustaining magic.

Before entering The Funnel, Ceril fed the companions and [[Tango]], choosing protection against necrotic and psychic forces for the descent. The feast's protections continued to aid them as they approached [[Axis Mundi]].

## Final Status

The cornucopia remains with Ceril at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
