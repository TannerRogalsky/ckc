# Potion of Proof Against Storms

The Potion of Proof Against Storms is a protective draught given to [[Domyx]] by a dissenting dwarf in [[Darvinblast]]. Domyx later drank it before entering [[Hole Shebang]].

## Description

The potion temporarily protects its drinker against thunder and lightning.

## Campaign History

After seeing Domyx protect his companions, an unseen dwarf called from a window and asked how he found his courage. Their exchange led the dwarf to question whether [[Morel Chainsunder]]'s plan served all dwarves or only a privileged few. He urged a return toward the sun and tossed the potion down to Domyx, who kept it out of respect for the gift. [[Red Caesar]] identified its protection.

Domyx drank it before following the party through the portal to Hole Shebang, anticipating the electrical hazards ahead.

During the confrontation with the last [[Vanir]], Domyx recalled a storm-resistance potion again and discussed drinking it. That recollection did not establish a replacement or a second consumption.

## Final Status

The originally recovered draught was consumed before Hole Shebang. The later recollection leaves any replacement supply unconfirmed; no second use is established.
