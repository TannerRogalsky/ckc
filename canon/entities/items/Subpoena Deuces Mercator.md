# Subpoena Deuces Mercator

Subpoena Deuces Mercator is a reusable magical scroll acquired by [[Kerben]] from [[Gunk Grodley]] of the [[Goblin Traders]], using the party's shared treasure.

## Description

Reading the scroll summons the traders' ship to find its user at sea. A short magical message can accompany the summons, allowing the user to request particular supplies. The traders must still travel to reach the caller.

## Campaign History

After helping Kerben obtain poisons, Gunk Grodley offered a way to trade without returning to port. With the party's approval, Kerben exchanged a rare gem from [[The Opal]]'s hold for the scroll.

The companions also considered its value if they became stranded after losing a ship: the summons could bring an ally who could offer supplies or passage.

## Final Status

Kerben is the last confirmed recipient. No activation, transfer, or loss is recorded. With no established user or later custody statement, the scroll's whereabouts at campaign end remain uncertain.
