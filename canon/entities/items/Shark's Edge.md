# Shark's Edge

Shark's Edge is an enchanted falchion carried by [[Kerben]]. Its ability to reveal water temperature became a practical tool for the northern voyage.

## Description

The falchion is made from a megalodon tooth and strengthens its wielder's strikes. Looking at visible water while carrying it reveals the water's temperature.

Its shark theme was associated with [[Crone]]'s purview over the moon and tides. A proposed ability to sense the tide was replaced by temperature perception; both powers were not established together.

## Campaign History

Kerben acquired the weapon while serving as acting captain of [[The Opal]], before the voyage north. He used it to mark their chosen route on a chart, then read warmer water and weaker ice with it as the ship approached [[Thalasia]].

It remained one of his weapons during later expeditions. He used it within the [[Haunted Living Tent]], against [[Farraday]], and while escaping a purple worm beneath [[The Funnel]].

## Final Status

Shark's Edge remains with Kerben at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
