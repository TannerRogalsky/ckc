# Ranger Scroll

The Ranger Scroll is a partially used Conjure Barrage scroll recovered from abandoned adventuring equipment at [[The Karat Cake]] and given to [[Kerben]].

## Description

The scroll originally held two inscriptions of Conjure Barrage. Its magic summons a barrage of ammunition, taking the form of spectral musketmen and gunfire when used by Kerben.

## Campaign History

In the park's midway courtyard, [[Domyx]] lifted a table and found papers, a backpack, a rusted knife, and leather armor. The belongings might have come from an unsuccessful adventurer, but no body established the owner's fate. Domyx gave the ranger scroll among them to Kerben.

During the later imperial naval attack, Kerben used one inscription before retreating aboard [[The Opal]]. Spectral musketmen appeared alongside the crew and fired across the nearest escort's deck, throwing defenders into the sea.

## Final Status

One inscription was expended during the naval attack. With no later use, transfer, or loss recorded, the scroll and its remaining inscription stay with Kerben at campaign end.
