# Teleport Keys

The Teleport Keys are teleportation-circle encodements available to the party, identifying destinations for suitable spellcasting or scrolls. [[Red Caesar]] used them to maintain contact with distant allies.

## Description

Each key identifies a destination circle; travel requires the appropriate teleportation magic. The encodements and their physical magical anchors give the party a means of targeting established circles.

### Known Destinations

- [[House Kiirnodel]] in [[Brimbolyn]].
- [[The White Drake]].
- The dwarvish market in [[The Pit]].
- Broyish Steeltown, the industrial town on the approach to the [[Broyish Capital]].

## Campaign History

[[Feronia Caeradel]] granted House Kiirnodel's encodements during the party's first visit, giving them permission and a destination for later returns.

After learning Teleportation Circle, Red Caesar knew the House Kiirnodel, The White Drake, and dwarvish market destinations. He used The White Drake's circle to rendezvous with [[Lady Jacinthe]] and later named all three routes while the companions planned travel after [[Castle Kaedon]].

The party used House Kiirnodel's circle during their return to Brimbolyn. In [[Broy]], [[Vanzia Vynnfae]] supplied a teleportation-circle scriptum for the industrial town. Red Caesar later referred to the keys' physical anchors while planning the return to Broy.

## Final Status

The encodements remain known to the party, with Red Caesar their established user. No withdrawal of access or destruction of the keys is recorded. Their destinations do not establish a way to bypass the permanent abjurations around [[Axis Mundi]].
