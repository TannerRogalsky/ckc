Teleportation-circle encodements known to the party. Each identifies a destination for suitable teleportation-circle magic or a scroll of that spell.

## Known Destinations

The party had keys for at least four destinations:

- [[House Kiirnodel]] in [[Brimbolyn]].
- [[The White Drake]].
- Dwarvish Market in [[The Pit]].
- Broyish Steeltown, the industrial town on the approach to the [[Broyish Capital]].

## History

[[House Kiirnodel]] granted its circle's encodements in session 014. By session 016, [[Red Caesar]] knew the House Kiirnodel, The White Drake, and Dwarvish Market destinations, and he named all three again in session 021. The party used the House Kiirnodel destination in session 022. In session 023, Red Caesar received a teleportation-circle scriptum in [[Broy]]. He referred to the keys' physical magical anchors while planning the return to Broy in session 032.
