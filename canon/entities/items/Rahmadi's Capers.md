# Rahmadi's Capers

Rahmadi's Capers is a collectible comic series about the adventures of [[Rahmadi]], the legendary goblin rogue. The party recovered its fourth issue at the [[Magic Hat]].

## Description

The recovered issue is a hand-drawn periodical with gold trim, depicting Rahmadi as a dashing adventurer. A collector was said to be willing to trade for a complete set.

## Campaign History

While resting at the Magic Hat hotel in [[The Karat Cake]], [[Vokenar]] sent Arcane Eye through its rooms. The magical eye found the fourth issue lying open on a bed, and the party added it to the shared hold aboard [[The Opal]].

The discovery suggested a possible collection and trade, but neither a complete set nor an exchange was established.

## Final Status

The fourth issue was last recorded among the party's shared treasure. No later individual holder, completed collection, trade, or loss is established; its particular custody at campaign end remains uncertain.
