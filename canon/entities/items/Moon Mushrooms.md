# Moon Mushrooms

Moon Mushrooms are hallucinogenic fungi from the jungles of [[Continental Stark]], harvested by [[Ceril]] for [[Kerben]]'s developing poison research.

## Description

The mushrooms are translucent and resemble the moon; broken specimens suggest half-moons or crescents. Their inviting fragrance provokes a strong instinctive desire to approach and eat them.

Ingestion can cause hallucinations severe enough to blind the consumer. Correctly handled specimens can be harvested, dried, and stored as poisonous ingredients.

## Campaign History

During the party's journey through Continental Stark, Ceril recognized the mushrooms' hallucinogenic danger. [[Vokenar]] resisted their lure.

Kerben had already begun collecting wyvern glands and was interested in developing poisons. Ceril gathered enough mushrooms for that research, and the supply was placed in [[The Opal]]'s shared hold.

## Final Status

The harvested supply remains in [[The Opal]]'s shared hold, its last established custody. No later consumption, transfer, or completed preparation using these mushrooms is recorded; their remaining quantity and condition are unknown.
