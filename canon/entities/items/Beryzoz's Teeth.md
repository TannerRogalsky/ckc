A necrotic-resistance ring traded to [[Red Caesar]] by [[Beryzoz Helmscar]]. Once per day it can cast Speak with Dead; the ring's teeth enter the corpse's mouth so the dead can speak through them.

## Session 036

[[Red Caesar]] used the ring with [[Kerben]]'s animal speech to question a dead expedition dog beneath [[The Funnel]]. The dog recalled a clear sky before [[The Cataclysm]], explained that its dwarven companions were seeking the underworld, warned of creatures emerging from the walls, and directed the party east.
