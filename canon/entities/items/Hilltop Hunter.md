# Hilltop Hunter

Hilltop Hunter is [[Kerben]]'s enchanted musket, commissioned from [[Sigrid Forgewelt]] in [[The Pit]]. Its magic strengthens his marksmanship, particularly against distant targets.

## Description

The musket requires attunement. Its shots ignite with bright green force energy as they travel farther from the wielder, lending additional power to distant strikes.

A small layer of moss or grass grows along the top of the weapon and changes with the seasons, including flowers in spring.

## Campaign History

After the expedition to [[Darvinblast]], Kerben sought an improvement to his musket at Sigrid Forgewelt's forge. She enhanced the existing gun into Hilltop Hunter.

It became a recurring part of his equipment. He was still using it during the confrontation with [[Boril Erendel]], after assuming permanent command of [[The Opal]].

## Final Status

Hilltop Hunter remains with Kerben at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
