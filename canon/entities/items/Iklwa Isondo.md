# Iklwa Isondo

Iklwa Isondo is an enchanted mithril trident made by [[Lesanderol Nokiirna]] for [[Domyx]]. Its magnetic link to his lodestone greaves allows it to return after a throw and pull targets toward him.

## Description

The lightweight weapon is shaped as a thick throwing and stabbing spear. An iron coil around it connects to Domyx's magnetic greaves, drawing it back to his body after it is thrown.

When the weapon catches a target, its return can pull that target along with it. This power draws others toward Domyx; it does not let him pull himself toward distant structures.

## Campaign History

At the [[Elvish Marketplace]] in [[Brimbolyn]], Lesanderol Nokiirna examined Domyx's weapons and greaves before devising the magnetic return. Domyx exchanged mithril, his earlier trident, gold-trimmed books, and a piece of pure gold for the finished weapon. He stayed to watch the smith's work and test it.

During the exploration of [[Castle Kaedon]], Domyx used its pulling power to bring a consenting [[Kerben]] back through a doorway, protecting him from the creatures inside. He also used the returning weapon against the castle's black dragon wyrmlings. Its magnetism did not provide a way to cross the gaps between the castle's broken platforms.

On the northern journey, Domyx used it to hook himself onto a [[Remorhaz]]'s head and draw the creature's attention away from [[Vokenar]].

## Final Status

Iklwa Isondo remains with Domyx at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
