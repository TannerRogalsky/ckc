# Kaboom Ring

The Kaboom Ring is a returning thrown weapon acquired by [[Domyx]] before the descent through [[The Funnel]]. It replaced his ordinary thrown javelins as a reusable way to strike distant foes.

## Description

The weapon works as a boomerang, returning after it is thrown, and requires no attunement. Its impact releases a thunderous burst around the target and can briefly deafen nearby creatures.

## Campaign History

Domyx exchanged his remaining diamonds for the weapon during the party's final preparations before entering The Funnel. With the [[Cestus of the Clear Sky]] occupying his principal close-combat role, he chose a secondary weapon he could recover rather than carrying a supply of javelins.

He used the Kaboom Ring against [[Emperor Shen]] at [[Axis Mundi]] before closing with the cestus.

## Final Status

The Kaboom Ring remains with Domyx at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
