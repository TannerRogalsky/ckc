# Hopping Mad Sash

The Hopping Mad Sash is a magical championship sash awarded to [[Domyx]] after the party completed every [[King of the Hole]] challenge at [[The Karat Cake]], including the optional bonus wave.

## Description

The sash carries an enchanted rabbit tail and requires no attunement. It improves its wearer's leaping and can release a forceful shockwave when the wearer collapses or suffers a damaging fall.

## Campaign History

The arena's [[Magen]] announcer made the reward by detaching its own rabbit tail and enchanting it. The construct knelt and raised the tail to Domyx in deference, giving the arena's rabbit theme a practical form.

At [[Axis Mundi]], Domyx seized [[Dunkelkalt]] and pulled the titan down with him. His landing activated the sash's shockwave while he kept hold of his opponent.

## Final Status

The sash remains with Domyx at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
