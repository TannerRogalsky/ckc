# League Banner

The League Banner is a signaling banner given to the party by [[Lady Jacinthe]] of [[The League of New Stark]].

## Description

When flown, the banner can be detected through divination by League members, allowing them to locate the party.

## Campaign History

Lady Jacinthe offered the banner aboard [[The White Drake]] during the party's early dealings with the League. Its usefulness lay in connecting travelers at sea with League allies.

After capturing [[The Hideous Truth]], the party considered raising it to attract the League. The discussion established the banner as an available means of contact, but did not confirm that it was flown.

## Final Status

The banner was last recorded among the party's available resources. No confirmed use, transfer, or loss is established; its particular holder and whereabouts at campaign end remain uncertain.
