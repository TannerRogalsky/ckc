# Para and Bellum

Para and Bellum are a pair of copper blades given to [[Red Caesar]] by [[Master Lee]] during his education at [[Heaven's Bulb]]. They became conduits for his arcane magic.

## Description

The paired blades take their names from the maxim meaning “If you want peace, prepare for war.” Their copper forms channel Red Caesar's magic, including the return of stolen life force as arcs of electricity.

## Campaign History

A flashback to Heaven's Bulb recalled Master Lee giving Red Caesar the blades. The gift accompanied warnings about the dangers beyond the conclave.

Red Caesar used them to channel Vampiric Touch against a [[Wyvern]]. Their magic later formed a resilient sphere around [[Theo Harvey]], preventing his escape.

At [[Axis Mundi]], Red Caesar manifested a magical sword modeled on one of the paired blades and sent it against [[Emperor Shen]]. The summoned weapon was an expression of his magic rather than an established transfer of the copper blades.

## Final Status

Para and Bellum remain with Red Caesar at campaign end. No later transfer, loss, or destruction is recorded.
