# Potion of Soothing Gaze

The Potion of Soothing Gaze is a healing potion recovered from [[Castle Kaedon]] and claimed by [[Red Caesar]].

## Description

Drinking the potion restores the drinker's vitality and lets that healing reach two other creatures the drinker looks at.

## Campaign History

While the party explored a damaged room, [[Domyx]] disturbed unstable flagstones and brought down debris from the apothecary above. The potion fell into his lap amid the rubble.

After its magic was identified, Red Caesar claimed it and added it to his inventory. Its discovery supplied a means of shared healing, but did not establish that anyone drank it.

## Final Status

Red Caesar is the last confirmed recipient. No consumption, transfer, or loss is recorded. With no established user or later custody statement, its disposition at campaign end remains unconfirmed.
