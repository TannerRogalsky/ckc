# Akasha

Akasha is a silvery-white substance from the oceans of [[Arkadia]], encountered as dangerous rain, mist, and a dense liquid beneath [[Stark]]'s seas. It suppresses magic and can be compressed into [[Penumbra]], making it central to [[Red Caesar]]'s research and the sabotage of [[Starfall]].

## Description

Liquid Akasha resembles quicksilver. Rainstorms over [[Continental Stark]] leave it pooled on the ground before it soaks into the earth; the jungle's fog also carries its energy. Contact can burn exposed creatures with radiant light. Denser than ordinary seawater, it settles into a deeper ocean layer.

Akasha is the substance of abjuration, counteracting magic and elemental energy, with properties likened to the material of outer space. The oceans that poured through the [[Hole in the Sky]] carried it down from Arkadia and stripped much of the drowned old world's magic. Survivors sought to replace that loss by binding magical auras to new objects through dweemers.

In [[Castle Kaedon]], silvery Akasha mist gathered into specters and wraiths and reacted to spellcasting by draining the caster's life. The substance incorporated into [[Vokenar]]'s blade similarly reacts against magic and elementals. Its forge master may have obtained it from the dwarven mountains, but that particular origin was not confirmed.

Containing Akasha requires care: it can escape ordinary vessels or deaden the energy around it. [[The Academy]] used specially tempered glass and magical containment for its experiments. In its concentrated solid form, it becomes the black, star-filled material known as Penumbra.

## Campaign History

### Collection and the Drowned World

On Continental Stark, the party sheltered from Akasha rain while Vokenar collected a bottled sample for study. Similarities between the liquid and his enchanted blade suggested a relationship among the fallen oceans, Arkadia, and the broken sky.

The Akasha-filled rooms of Castle Kaedon revealed its ability to feed undead manifestations and drain spellcasters. The party learned that the deep layer of the substance had neutralized much of Stark's former magic. They proposed that Penumbra was crystallized Akasha, storing the power the liquid had absorbed. [[Lyngbakr]] later reached the deeper sea, found it familiar and comfortable, and agreed to gather some for their investigation.

### Synthesis and Sabotage

At the Academy in [[Brimbolyn]], Red Caesar worked with [[Keys Caeradel]] and [[Illidrielle Gandara]] using collected Akasha and the Academy's reservoir. Otiluke's Resilient Sphere provided a sealed boundary, while adapted size-changing magic increased the pressure within it. A lake's worth of liquid yielded a palm-sized, jet-black sphere with stars inside: genuine Penumbra.

They named the process the Caesarean method. Red Caesar estimated that an established operation could make another sphere every five to ten days, but it required substantial facilities and supplies. This was a manufacturing breakthrough, not a portable source of unlimited Penumbra.

The collaborators then altered a separate sample into [[Antumbra]], concealing instability within its compressed Akasha so that drawing on its energy would destroy the consuming system. They kept it apart from their own Penumbra stock. The altered sphere was planted in the Empire's fuel supply for Starfall.

Red Caesar later completed [[Obvolvo Caelum]] after his younger self, encountered within a [[Heaven's Bulb]] memory, identified Mending as the missing principle. The spell smoothed the transition between liquid and solid states, offering a refinement of the synthesis work as well as a way to condense existing Penumbra.

### The Pyramid and the Final Battle

Akasha-laced seawater flooded the base of [[Tome Keeper's Pyramid]] unusually close to the surface. The party wondered whether repairing the sky had drawn it upward; that explanation remained a theory. Red Caesar used Dust of Dryness to condense a sample into a single-use grenade.

At [[Axis Mundi]], Antumbra's unstable Akasha destroyed Starfall when [[Emperor Shen]] loaded it into the ring. After the weapon's destruction, Red Caesar threw the grenade into a group of spectral celebrants serving the emperor. Its released light destroyed them, expending the preparation. Vokenar subsequently returned to his Akasha-infused blade during the final confrontation with the emperor's [[Vanir]] form.

## Final Status

Red Caesar's grenade was consumed, and the Akasha compressed into Antumbra was spent in Starfall's destruction. These were distinct preparations, not the exhaustion of Akasha as a substance.

Vokenar remains the last established holder of his original bottled sample and retains the Akasha-infused blade he used in the final battle. The sample's later condition and the transport of these possessions during his youthful return to Arkadia are not described. The record does not establish the disappearance of the environmental deposits or a final accounting of the Academy's supply.
