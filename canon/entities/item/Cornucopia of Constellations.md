An elven commission acquired by [[Ceril]] before the party's descent through [[The Funnel]]. Worn at the neck, the cornucopia supplies the feast materials he needs and strengthens Heroes' Feast into Goddess's Feast.

The feast grants greater vitality and protection from poison, plus resistance to two chosen energy types. A later meal during the same day can change the two chosen protections.
