An ancient titan god of the [[Vanir]] and ancestor of [[Domyx]], encountered by [[Red Caesar]] in the [[Gray Wastes]] through Contact Other Plane in session 036. He appeared as a mountain-sized, cross-legged figure in orange robes. Domyx understood the encounter as contact with a surviving god or divine spark of his ancestral people.

Domyx I said the titans lost their war against the goddesses and now wait for the cycle to begin again. He claimed that the titans had won in earlier worlds and vowed they would rule after the next renewal. He accused the goddesses of controlling mortal choices without understanding the suffering they cause. These are Domyx I's claims, not independently established truths.

Red Caesar initially mistook Domyx I for [[Emperor Shen]], then compared Emperor Shen to a diminished imitation of him. Domyx I did not recognize the emperor's name and dismissed would-be imitators.

As Domyx I spoke of renewal, Red Caesar felt drawn to reveal the [[Demi-Spell]]. He endured the psychic pressure rather than show it. [[Vokenar]] restored Red Caesar from the incapacitating trance, ending the encounter without directions to [[Axis Mundi]].
