The Vanir That Eats the Stars, a star-filled [[Vanir]] who manifests at [[Axis Mundi]] after [[Emperor Shen]]'s death in session 037. Entropie resembles a dark counterpart to [[Aeris]] and can distort luck, wield destructive stellar energy, and resist the divine radiance used by the party.

Entropie and [[Dunkelkalt]] stand as opposites of the [[Aesir]], the three sister goddesses [[Aeris]], [[Sigil]], and [[Crone]]. Their hostility toward the party follows from its service to those goddesses.

Entropie identifies [[Sigil]] as the sister goddess who brought it down in the ancient conflict. [[Ceril]] recognizes and contests its manipulation of fate, while [[Vokenar]] channels Sigil's power against it.

After [[Kerben]] defeats [[Dunkelkalt]], the party destroys Entropie's manifested form, with [[Domyx]] delivering the final blow. Before dissipating, Entropie casts a Wish that brings forth the last Vanir, likely [[Domyx I]]. The final manifestation's identity remains provisional. Like the other returning gods, it threatens the party as servants of the three sister goddesses.
