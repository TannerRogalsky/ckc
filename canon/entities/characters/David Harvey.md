# David Harvey

David Harvey is a [[Harengon]] working with [[The League of New Stark]] to recover a homeland for his displaced people. He offered a bounty for his cousin [[Theo Harvey]] and later received [[Jack Harvey's Portrait]] for the family.

## Identity and Background

He is a small, fuzzy, orange, rabbit-like person reaching about a humanoid's knee. He described the devastation of the Harengon population by [[The Cataclysm]], while noting that their rapid reproduction helped recovery.

## Relationships

- [[Theo Harvey]] — His cousin, who disclosed the hidden warrens to the [[Broyish Empire]] and joined its forces. David Harvey wanted him captured alive so the League could learn what he had revealed.
- [[Jack Harvey]] — His great-uncle and the entrepreneur behind [[The Karat Cake]]. David Harvey said he had not followed those theatrical ambitions; Theo Harvey had been more interested in them.
- [[Lady Jacinthe]] — Praised her protection of his displaced people and joined her in assembling Theo Harvey's bounty.

## Equipment and Resources

- [[Jack Harvey's Portrait]] — Given to him by [[Vokenar]], who preferred to keep the relic with the Harvey family. He hung it aboard [[The White Drake]], where it remains entrusted to him with no later transfer recorded.

## Campaign History

David Harvey approached [[Domyx]] aboard The White Drake and asked the party to bring Theo Harvey back as a prisoner. Imperial humans occupied the island above the [[Harengon Warrens]]. The extensive tunnels reached below the old ocean level into an area once called [[Esperanto]]; their development belonged to an earlier period of closer trust in the Empire, before the Harengon turned to the League. Many refugees were now stranded on boats, awaiting a safe return. See [[session_022#Chunk 0001]].

The party captured Theo Harvey but took him toward Broy rather than delivering him to David Harvey. The capture alone therefore did not complete the requested return or establish payment of the bounty. See [[session_022#Chunk 0002]].

David Harvey later heard that Theo Harvey had escaped. He was disappointed, but welcomed the removal of the imperial foothold above the warrens and intended to send the Harengon back to reclaim them. He rejected Theo Harvey's reading of Jack Harvey as merely a pirate, insisting that his great-uncle had been trying to save the world. The intended return was not shown completed. See [[session_025#Chunk 0000]].

## Final Status

David Harvey was last encountered aboard The White Drake, where he accepted and hung Jack Harvey's Portrait. No later personal fate or completed return of his people to the warrens was recorded. See [[session_034#Chunk 0000]].
