# Tuna

Tuna is a speaking sphinx, half cat and half falcon, and [[Alamar]]'s companion. Formerly associated with [[Castle Kaedon]] and [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]], he was with Alamar in [[Arkadia]] at campaign end.

## Relationships

- [[Alamar]] — Companion at the castle and lagoon refuge, later together with him in Arkadia.
- [[Rella Kel'Navvi]] — Welcomed his decision to remain at the lagoon, saying that Tuna loved dogs.

## Campaign History

Tuna was associated with Alamar and Castle Kaedon when the party investigated Alamar's magical sequestering.

During the later return to the lagoon, Tuna was living with Alamar beside his bonfire and growing shelter. Alamar recalled that they had eaten owlbear eggs for a week after an encounter there. When Rella Kel'Navvi volunteered to stay and help Alamar, Tuna welcomed having a dog nearby. These exchanges are recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0003]].

In the epilogue, Tuna flew near Alamar as he approached to welcome [[Vokenar]] back to Arkadia. The reunion is recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002]].

## Final Status

Tuna was alive in Arkadia with Alamar at campaign end. His passage from Stark to Arkadia was not described.
