Qian Hu is a merchant in the [[Dawn Market]] who claims to be the sole survivor of the pre-[[The Cataclysm]] world, with his soul carried forward across seventeen successive forms. His shop displays ancient-world artifacts and a large golden dragon statue. He is invisible to unworthy eyes and appears only to those he considers important.

Qian Hu knew [[Master Lee]] before [[The Cataclysm]]. He recognized [[Red Caesar]] as a member of [[Heaven's Bulb]] and someone of importance. He trades in diamonds and legendary relics of the old world.
