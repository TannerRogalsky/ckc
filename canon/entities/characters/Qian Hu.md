# Qian Hu

Qian Hu is a merchant and mechanic in [[Broy]]'s [[Dawn Market]], trading in diamonds and legendary relics. He claimed to be the sole surviving soul of an ancient world, carried through seventeen successive forms.

## Identity and Background

His shop displayed ancient artifacts beneath a large golden dragon statue. His account described a former world where machines and magic worked together; its extraordinary history and his successive identities were his own claims.

## Relationships

- [[Master Lee]] — Qian Hu said he had known him under an earlier identity and recalled teleporting to freedom rather than waiting for doors to open.
- [[Red Caesar]] — Recognized as a wizard, traveler, and member of [[Heaven's Bulb]], supposedly through Legend Lore. Their first conversation left Red Caesar uncertain how to understand the merchant's claims.

## Abilities

Qian Hu said only worthy or important people could see him. He also claimed to use Legend Lore to recognize significant visitors.

## Campaign History

Red Caesar visited his shop, but the party lacked sufficient wealth for its most exceptional relics and made no purchase. Qian Hu invited him to return and hinted that a special item could grant access to a distant mountain, without establishing how that journey worked. [[canon/sessions/session_023#Chunk 0002]]

A later opportunity to revisit the shop was discussed, but Red Caesar declined after recalling the unpleasant earlier exchange. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0001]]

## Final Status

Qian Hu was last personally encountered trading in the Dawn Market. His claimed origin and the wider implications of his successive forms remained unresolved. [[canon/sessions/session_023#Chunk 0002]]
