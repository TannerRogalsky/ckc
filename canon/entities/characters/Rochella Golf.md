# Rochella Golf

Rochella Golf is a former passenger aboard [[The Opal]] who returned to [[Southport]] after years away and reunited with her family. She acquired the [[Boston Golf]] fortune-teller automaton as a recovered family heirloom.

## Campaign History

During the voyage, she told [[Domyx]] that she wanted to see her children, fearing they believed her dead. She suspected her husband had moved on and recalled that their relationship had already deteriorated before she left. Those fears did not establish what had actually happened at home. [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0003]]

She eventually returned to Southport and reunited with her family. The party later sold her the Boston Golf automaton, restoring the heirloom to its family. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0000]]

## Final Status

Rochella Golf was last established in Southport with her family and the recovered automaton. Her earlier fears do not establish a confirmed separation from her husband.
