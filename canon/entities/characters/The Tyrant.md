# The Tyrant

The Tyrant was [[Vizier Jade]]'s undead, beholder-like servant, lost during her search for [[The Carrot Cake]]. Its revelation of [[Starfall]]'s location gave the party a route toward the weapon before an unseen force destroyed it.

## Identity and Background

The Tyrant appeared as an enormous dead head with a central eye, a great mouth, leathery and bony skin, and withered eyestalks bearing differently colored eyes. [[Penumbra]] encrusted its body when the party found it in [[Jack Harvey]]'s flooded vault.

According to its own account, Starfall created it and it embodied the weapon's energy. It described Starfall as the core of [[Stark]], formed from the world's four elements when the world itself was born, and as a failsafe preceding [[Genesis Mundi]].

## Personality and Motivations

The Tyrant was confused and despairing when encountered. It grieved for the divine beings it called its mothers, recognized a fragment of [[Crone]] in [[Vokenar]]'s manifested sword, and blamed the party for destroying the world and harming them. Vokenar's explanation that Vizier Jade and [[Emperor Shen]] were responsible briefly diverted its hostility without resolving its confusion.

It believed its continued existence depended on Vizier Jade's pleasure and expected her to dispose of it. It also expressed disbelief that she had reached Starfall, which it thought had been kept secret from her.

## Relationships

- [[Vizier Jade]] — Former mistress and the power on which it believed its survival depended. It accused her of casting it out, called for her aid, and feared that she would destroy it.
- [[Crone]] — One of the divine mothers it mourned. Recognizing her presence in Vokenar's sword exposed its grief over the assault on the moon.
- [[Starfall]] — Claimed source of its existence and energy. Its knowledge of the weapon ultimately supplied the party with the location they needed.

## Abilities

The Tyrant communicated telepathically. Its eyestalks projected debilitating and lethal magic, while its central eye created a negative aura in which healing did not work. It remained physically dangerous through its great mouth and teeth despite its confusion.

## Campaign History

[[Theo Harvey]] told [[Domyx]] that Vizier Jade had sent The Tyrant to find The Carrot Cake and that it had never returned. He described the park's illusions and arcane traps as capable of trapping anyone who entered. Later discussion considered whether the search had included other locations and whether the park's limited opening could explain the failed return. None of these earlier explanations established its death.

The party eventually found The Tyrant alive as an undead being inside Jack Harvey's underwater Penumbra vault. [[Jade's Compass]] lay nearby. The compass suggested that an agent connected to Vizier Jade had entered years earlier, and the party suspected The Tyrant had brought it; its presence did not establish the servant's custody of the compass or a personal visit by Vizier Jade.

The Tyrant attacked while calling for Vizier Jade and grieving for its divine mothers. After the party exhausted its defenses against hostile magic, [[Red Caesar]] charmed it and ended the fight long enough to question it. The Tyrant explained its relationship to Starfall, and Vokenar persuaded it to identify the weapon's hiding place beneath the world, at its center in [[Axis Mundi]].

As it gave the name, an unseen outside force snapped its jaw, tore its body apart, and condensed the remains into a small knot of bone. Its warning about Vizier Jade's control suggested her intervention, but the party did not witness the source of the force. The disclosure and destruction are recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_031#Chunk 0003]].

## Final Status

The Tyrant was destroyed after revealing Axis Mundi. Its information survived and directed the party toward Starfall; responsibility for the force that killed it remained an inference rather than a witnessed act.
