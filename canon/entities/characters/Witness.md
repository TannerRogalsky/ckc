# Witness

Witness is a silvery warforged cartographer who completed a map of [[Stark]] and went on to chart [[Arkadia]]. His preserved work helped the party recover the geography of the drowned world.

## Identity and Background

Witness is a powerful, humanoid construct. He identified [[Vizier Jade]] as his creator, saying that she had given him life. His cartography records the old world's mountains, lowlands, and ocean depths, preserving the landscape that existed before [[The Cataclysm]]. Once his survey was complete, he entrusted its encoded portions to people who had cared for him and left Stark to chart another plane.

## Personality and Motivations

Witness regarded the completion of Stark's chart as a reason to explore new places rather than remain in a world where he felt he had little left to do. His continued mapping of Arkadia made that purpose explicit.

He remained grateful to Vizier Jade for his creation. In their conversation in Arkadia, he told [[Damien Ouranous]] that he had hoped she would become independent, recalling her desire for peace and the repeated wars that drew her back into work in which her talents were useful. These were his recollections of her motives.

## Relationships

- [[Vizier Jade]] — His creator, whom he credited with giving him life and remembered with gratitude despite her involvement in war.
- [[Janeera]] — Trusted keeper of one portion of his chart, entrusted with it during the rising waters.
- [[PAXO]] — Hostile warforged that drove him from the human refuge at [[Southern Archipelago Castle]] and killed its inhabitants.
- [[Damien Ouranous]] — Traveling companion in Arkadia, with whom he discussed exploration, his creator, and their former world.

## Abilities

Witness mapped terrain by walking across mountain peaks and along the ocean floor. Rising water did not stop the survey: whether land lay above or beneath the sea had little bearing on how he traveled and recorded it. Pursuit by hostile warforged forced him into hiding and changed his method, but did not prevent him from completing the work.

He encoded his map into complementary portions that required assembly and magical interpretation to recover their full information.

## Equipment and Resources

- [[Chart of the Witness]] — His completed chart of Stark. He entrusted its portions to Janeera and the people who had sheltered him. The party later recovered and decoded both portions; the chart remained among their maps aboard [[The Opal]].

## Campaign History

Witness lived among humans sheltering in Southern Archipelago Castle after the flood. A dead inhabitant told [[Red Caesar]] that a new, wicked construct had driven their ally away and killed the people there. The party subsequently encountered and destroyed PAXO, identifying the threat that had ended the refuge. They recovered one portion of Witness's chart, but found no body establishing that Witness had died. Their investigation is recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_025#Chunk 0001]].

During [[Ceril]]'s journey across Arkadia's drained ocean bed, he observed Witness walking with Damien Ouranous and overheard their conversation. Witness had finished charting Stark and had begun exploring the upper plane. He spoke about Vizier Jade's creation of him and his wish to discover new places, as recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_032#Chunk 0000]].

His chart continued to aid the travelers on Stark after his departure. In [[Southport]], Janeera explained that Witness had entrusted her with one portion during the rising waters. An expedition that did not return had subsequently carried it into the [[Mana Sea]]. The party recovered it near [[Ninki Nanka]]'s cave, and Red Caesar joined and decoded both portions to reveal the full chart.

## Final Status

The epilogue confirmed that Witness had reached Arkadia and was charting it after completing his survey of Stark. His crossing of paths with Ceril was also confirmed. Travel beyond these worlds remained a possibility, rather than an established later journey.
