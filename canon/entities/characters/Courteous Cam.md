# Courteous Cam

Courteous Cam is a dwarf farmer and trader near [[The Pit]] who supplied [[Ceril]] with agricultural knowledge and a lead to [[Penumbra]]. He later helped reopen [[The Funnel]], making the party's descent beneath Stark possible.

## Personality and Motivations

He was curious about the liberated deep worlders and interested in growing elven Darkwood for lighter equipment. The northern climate and shortage of spellcasters limited that ambition. He also knew how dwarves harnessed natural power through water mills and redirected lightning storms.

## Campaign History

Courteous Cam traded dwarven root-crop seeds, growing knowledge, and a Call Lightning scroll to Ceril in an exchange involving a Plant Growth scroll and elvish Darkwood bark. He supplied a map for the Bounty in the Boneyard lead: a Penumbra fragment in northern [[Gnoll]] territory, held by defenders who knew its value but not its use. The party followed the map and recovered the fragment. See [[session_013#Chunk 0000]] and [[session_013#Chunk 0003]].

Later, he and [[Otto]] oversaw the Funnel's deeper excavation. Otto had broken through the bottom of Stark into unfamiliar air and a black abyss, but the passage collapsed. Courteous Cam agreed to redirect the work toward reopening it after [[Red Caesar]] explained the continuing danger from [[Emperor Shen]]. Earth-moving magic, explosives, and advanced methods were already available; Red Caesar's proposed Penumbra power system was not implemented. Recurring [[Mana Sickness]] also slowed the workers, and Courteous Cam promised to report when the route approached readiness. See [[session_032#Chunk 0002]].

## Final Status

Courteous Cam reported the reopened passage alongside [[Figma Brickfinger]]. The workers had withdrawn from dangers beyond it rather than explore further, leaving the descent to the party. He admitted that finishing the excavation had required breaking union rules. This was his last established activity, in [[session_035#Chunk 0002]]; no later personal fate was recorded.
