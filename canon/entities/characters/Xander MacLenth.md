# Xander MacLenth

Xander MacLenth was a human pirate whose ghost lingered in the abandoned [[Far Helm Clan]] home. [[Vokenar]] released him to the rising tide, and the epilogue confirmed his passage to the afterlife.

## Identity and Background

He was the son of the legendary [[Lenth the Rugged]]; his surname also connected him to [[Pleasance MacLenth]]'s family, though their exact relationship was not established. He and his pirate crew tried to seize the coastal site from a dwarf family and were killed by its defenders, including younger family members. A connection to [[Gammix]] and [[Tammix]] was suggested but not confirmed.

## Personality and Motivations

Calling himself a "pirate through and through," he had wanted to spend his wealth before his "watery grave" and retire to drink away his days in [[Cutlass Cray]]. Unlike his father, he had no ambition to leave a great legacy. When Vokenar did not recognize Lenth the Rugged's name, he mocked the old man's supposed fame and tried unsuccessfully to lift an empty rum flagon for a last drink.

## Campaign History

Xander MacLenth waited for the rising waters of [[The Cataclysm]] to sweep him into the afterlife. Time had become indistinct to him, and the sea rose too slowly for his liking. He told Vokenar that the dwarves had taken his wealth and suggested pursuing and killing them. Vokenar did not adopt that proposal; he created and directed water over the ghost, who welcomed it and dissolved into the tide. [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0001]]

## Final Status

His release truly carried him into the afterlife, as confirmed in the closing account. [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002]]
