# Feronia Caeradel

Feronia Caeradel is a drow of former House Caeradel, [[Keys Caeradel]]'s mother, and a former principal authority for enacting [[The Order of Seasons]]' [[Demi-Spell]]. Associated with [[House Kiirnodel]], she ultimately carried the crystal containing her former husband's soul after the spell was relinquished and erased.

## Identity and Background

Before [[The Cataclysm]], she and [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] encountered [[Ceril]] fleeing the elven lands and allowed him to continue. On meeting him again, she said that his soul survived many worlds unchanged and that he had never been meant to remain bound. She predicted that he could become like a god in a renewed world; this was her interpretation of his possible future, not a completed transformation.

## Relationships

- [[Keys Caeradel]] — Son and Demi-Spell author. She shared responsibility for the project and later received the crystal containing his father's soul.
- [[Rakshasa]] — Former husband in another world and form. She acknowledged his repeated transformations, rejected the assumption that demons never changed, and remained uncertain about what he had become.
- [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] — Fellow authority at House Kiirnodel and participant in Ceril's earlier escape and the party's later negotiations.
- [[Ceril]] — Survivor whose freedom and future she recognized before the campaign, then identified again during his return to Brimbolyn.

## Equipment and Resources

- [[Rakshasa]] — His soul remained contained in a crystal worn by Feronia Caeradel at campaign end. Keys Caeradel had earlier bound it into an amulet at his father's request; the crystal's custody does not establish release or a restored body.

## Campaign History

At House Kiirnodel, Feronia Caeradel explained that [[Genesis Mundi]] was the natural renewal between worlds, while the Demi-Spell would accelerate and guide it. She and Rizolvir Kiirnodel defended their intent to treat different peoples fairly. He warned that the [[Broyish Empire]] was gathering [[Penumbra]], probably for a weapon. Feronia Caeradel asked the party to check on [[Boril Erendel]] at [[The Garden]], worried about his independent work. See [[session_014#Chunk 0004]].

When [[Red Caesar]] and Ceril later arrived through the house's teleportation circle, Feronia Caeradel recognized them and lowered its protective blast shield. They reported Boril Erendel's growing extremism. See [[session_022#Chunk 0001]].

After Boril Erendel's defeat, she greeted the party beside Rizolvir Kiirnodel outside [[The Academy]] and explained the Rakshasa's relationship to her family. Keys Caeradel was meeting his father inside. He later left carrying his father's bound soul and intended to bring it to her. See [[session_035#Chunk 0002]].

## Final Status

The epilogue established that Feronia Caeradel wore the crystal containing the Rakshasa's soul, completing that transfer. The Demi-Spell was erased rather than enacted; no separate later office or personal destination was established for her. See [[session_038#Chunk 0002]].
