# Mayor Yoris

Mayor Yoris is the adolescent mayor of [[Southport]], governing with posthumous counsel from [[Janeera]]. He helped the party end the source of the town's recurring [[Mana Sickness]].

## Personality and Motivations

He felt responsible for warriors lost on an earlier expedition into the [[Mana Sea]] and refused to send another group without confidence it could survive. He sought lasting relief for his people rather than indefinite dependence on treatment.

## Relationships

- [[Janeera]] — The deceased seer whose prepared spirit contact advised his government, predicted the party's arrival, and directed the search for the plague's source.
- [[Southport]] — The community he governed through its recurring illness and subsequent recovery.

## Abilities

He could invoke protective magic against poison and disease and use Janeera's prepared ritual to consult her spirit.

## Campaign History

Mayor Yoris welcomed the companions on Southport's beach after the sky was repaired, dressed in military regalia and protecting them before they came ashore. Janeera had foretold the arrival of those who would heal the heavens. [[canon/sessions/session_032#Chunk 0002]]

He explained the town's repeated reinfection and directed the party to Janeera. His previous expedition had vanished while seeking [[Ninki Nanka]], carrying part of [[Witness]]'s chart. With the companions better prepared, he supplied potions from Janeera's collection and supported their journey. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0000]]

After they freed the dragon and ended the source of the contagion, he delivered the remaining promised potions, offered continued hospitality, and arranged free improvements to [[The Opal]]. Existing patients still needed sustained treatment. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0003]]

## Final Status

His last personal appearance was in Southport after the expedition, assisting its recovery. The closing account confirmed the town's eventual full recovery; it did not provide a separate later account of his office or whereabouts. [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002]]
