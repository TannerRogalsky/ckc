The adolescent mayor of [[Southport]], governing with posthumous counsel from [[Janeera]]. He welcomed the party after the sky was repaired and asked them to end the recurring [[Mana Sickness]]. He had previously sent warriors into the [[Mana Sea]] and refused to risk another group without confidence they could survive.

After the party ended the plague's source, Mayor Yoris granted them potions from Janeera's collection and arranged free upgrades to [[The Opal]].
