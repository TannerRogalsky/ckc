# Queen Caeradwyn

Queen Caeradwyn was the former ruler of the elves, killed roughly a century before the party's present. Her death broke a bloodline curse affecting [[Castle Kaedon]] and helped bring down the aristocratic order, while a deeper legacy of madness persisted.

## Identity and Background

Her rule upheld a rigid aristocracy in [[Brimbolyn]] that enslaved most elves as well as lower-status humans and dwarves. [[Alamar]] described her as carrying her people's blood in its purest form and sustaining an ambition over centuries.

## Abilities

Her bloodline carried both magical power and a curse that drove [[King Maniasis]] insane. Alamar described a wider curse spanning physical, metaphysical, divine, and profane forces: women periodically claimed to be her reincarnation, spreading madness in Brimbolyn and elsewhere. That account did not prove that she had literally returned.

## Campaign History

Queen Caeradwyn's death broke the curse on King Maniasis, opened the way for [[Aramil Kiirnodel]] to gain power, and helped trigger revolutionary upheaval. Refugees, including [[Ceril]], fled the collapsing old order before [[The Cataclysm]]. [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0000]]

Alamar later explained that her influence ran deeper than the ended bloodline enchantment. The recurring claims of reincarnation and the fiends and undead occupying Castle Kaedon formed part of the curse that had afflicted his life. [[canon/sessions/session_015]]

## Final Status

Queen Caeradwyn was dead long before the campaign. Alamar's testimony preserved uncertainty about the continuing reach of her curse without establishing a literal resurrection or a final end to every effect.
