# Illidrielle Gandara

Illidrielle Gandara was a half-elf collaborator on [[The Order of Seasons]]' [[Demi-Spell]] and the [[Antumbra]] sabotage. After dying on the day the moon shattered, she counseled [[Ceril]] in [[Arkadia]] and ultimately accepted that the spell would remain unused.

## Identity and Background

She had sailed with Ceril aboard [[The Croakborne Carnival]] before her Academy work. Ceril also recognized her and [[Keys Caeradel]] as the collectors who took [[Penumbra]] from [[Castle Kaedon]].

## Personality and Motivations

Illidrielle Gandara challenged the claim that repairing the sky left everyone free to choose, arguing that repair also imposed a choice. She regarded the Demi-Spell as a mortal undertaking capable of affecting even the gods, whose possible responsibility for the catastrophe she questioned without proving. She explicitly rejected the idea that continued flooding should select only the strongest survivors.

She nevertheless accepted evidence for an alternative and proposed a pause in the Order's collection of Penumbra. After her death, she valued Keys Caeradel's restraint and wanted him to find a life beyond grief and the spell, rather than remake everything merely to bring her back.

## Relationships

- [[Keys Caeradel]] — Longtime friend and research colleague. His grief at her death intensified his wish to cast the spell; her message encouraged him to relinquish it and move forward.
- [[Ceril]] — Former shipmate who carried her warning about [[Boril Erendel]] and her wishes for Keys Caeradel back from Arkadia.
- [[Red Caesar]] — Collaborated with him on Penumbra synthesis and Antumbra while continuing to differ over the existing world's future.

## Abilities

Her illusion magic concealed Keys Caeradel's destabilization of a synthesized Penumbra sample, allowing Antumbra to resemble ordinary material. The altered sample remained separate from the Order's own supplies.

## Campaign History

At [[The Academy]], she recognized Ceril and helped lead the moral discussion about the Demi-Spell. She proposed the temporary halt to new Penumbra collection and access to teleportation circles while the party tested its sky-repair plan. See [[session_014#Chunk 0003]].

She later worked with Red Caesar and Keys Caeradel to make Antumbra for [[Starfall]]. Red Caesar solved synthesis, Keys Caeradel altered the sample, and she concealed the change. [[Kerben]] planted it aboard [[The White Drake]]. She privately considered synthesis a major advance for the Demi-Spell as well as an answer to the shared imperial threat. See [[session_025#Chunk 0000]].

Ceril met her with other dead Order members in Arkadia. She remembered screaming on the day the moon shattered but did not know exactly what killed her; falling debris remained a likely explanation. She reported that Keys Caeradel had completed the spell before her death and honored the promise not to cast it. She warned that Boril Erendel was already constraining future incarnations to elven bodies, asked Ceril to stop him, and preferred his death. Her message to Keys Caeradel was that she thought well of him and would see him again someday. See [[session_032#Chunk 0000]].

Ceril conveyed her wishes after Boril Erendel's defeat. That testimony helped persuade Keys Caeradel to entrust the completed spell to the party and seek a new life. See [[session_035#Chunk 0002]].

## Final Status

Illidrielle Gandara remained in Arkadia and greeted [[Vokenar]]'s return, asking whether Ceril and the others had survived. She accepted that the Demi-Spell would not be cast despite her work on it. Her death's exact cause remained unresolved. See [[session_038#Chunk 0002]].
