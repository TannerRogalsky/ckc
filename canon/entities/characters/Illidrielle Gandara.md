A half-elf member of [[The Order of Seasons]] who works alongside [[Keys Caeradel]] at [[The Academy]]. She was previously encountered by [[Ceril]] at [[Castle Kaedon]], where she and Keys Caeradel stole a piece of [[Penumbra]]. She recognized Ceril when they met at the Academy.

She leads the party's discussion about the [[Demi-Spell]] and its moral implications, arguing that fixing the sky is "another way of depriving people of choice." She negotiated the accord with the party to stop collecting [[Penumbra]] temporarily.

In session 025, Illidrielle collaborated with [[Keys Caeradel]] and [[Red Caesar]] at [[The Academy]] to create [[Antumbra]], a poisoned form of Penumbra intended to sabotage the [[Broyish Empire]]'s [[Starfall]] device. She and Keys Caeradel used an illusion-concealed method to poison the material without contaminating their own samples. The resulting Antumbra was planted aboard [[The White Drake]] by [[Kerben]].

## Session 035

Her death weighed on [[Keys Caeradel]] and the subdued atmosphere at [[The Academy]]. [[Ceril]] said he had spoken with Illidrielle Gandara beyond the mortal world, where she was happy and wanted Keys Caeradel to move on. This helped persuade him to relinquish the [[Demi-Spell]] and seek a new life. The account does not establish how she died.
