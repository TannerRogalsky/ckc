# Gunk Grodley

Gunk Grodley is a dapper goblin trader and captain of the [[Goblin Traders]]' ship. His tall black top hat, velvety outfit, and confident bearing marked his approach to [[The Opal]].

## Campaign History

While The Opal sailed from [[Brimbolyn]], Gunk Grodley came aboard to offer accessories, ammunition, potions, and magical wares, including goods forbidden in elven lands. He directed [[Kerben]] to his wife in the hold for poison-making and sales.

He also offered the reusable [[Subpoena Deuces Mercator]], allowing its user to call the traders at sea with a short request. Kerben acquired it from the shared treasure. The traders departed after the exchange; no later individual appearance or change of command was established for Gunk Grodley. See [[session_015#Chunk 0000]].
