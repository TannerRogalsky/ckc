# Otto

Otto is a warforged boat repairman recruited at [[The Boardwalks]] of [[Cutlass Cray]]. He served the company of [[The Opal]] and later helped reopen the descent through [[The Funnel]].

## Identity and Background

[[Courteous Cam]] described Otto as a warforged who had developed a soul, challenging [[Red Caesar]]'s assumption that he was simply a machine. [[canon/sessions/session_032#Chunk 0002]]

## Relationships

- [[Domyx]] — Recruited Otto with [[Vokenar]], agreeing to help find his dwarf friends as the condition of joining the crew.
- [[Courteous Cam]] — Collaborated with him in directing the Funnel's deepest excavation.

## Abilities

Otto repaired boats and worked on the difficult subterranean excavation. He could travel deeper into the Funnel than many of the dwarves, though even he reached a limit beyond which he would not proceed.

## Campaign History

Domyx and Vokenar found him fixing a boat in Cutlass Cray and secured his help in exchange for assisting his search for his friends. [[canon/sessions/session_004]]

During the excavation beneath Stark, Otto reported a vast abyss and helped Courteous Cam commit the work to reopening a route toward [[Axis Mundi]]. He later guided the party partway down the Funnel before refusing to descend further; they continued through the caverns toward the [[Gray Wastes]]. [[canon/sessions/session_032#Chunk 0002]] [[canon/sessions/session_036]]

## Final Status

Otto was last established guiding the initial descent into the Funnel. The record does not follow his individual whereabouts beyond his decision to stop descending. [[canon/sessions/session_036]]
