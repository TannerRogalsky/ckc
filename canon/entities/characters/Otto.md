A boat repairman encountered on [[The Boardwalks]] of [[Cutlass Cray]]. Recruited by [[Domyx]] and [[Vokenar]] to join the party's crew on the condition that they help him find his dwarf friends.

In session 032, Otto helped [[Courteous Cam]] direct the Funnel's deepest excavation. He reported a vast abyss below Stark and committed the operation to reopening a route toward [[Axis Mundi]].

## Session 036

Otto guided the party partway down [[The Funnel]], reaching farther than many of the other dwarves could before refusing to descend further. The party continued through the deep caverns toward the [[Gray Wastes]].
