# Father Warrick

Father Warrick is a senior priest of [[Sigil]] restored by [[Ceril]] after decades of petrification at the [[Temple of Sigil]] on [[Otyugh Isle]]. His freed clergy helped bring medicine to [[Southport]] during its [[Mana Sickness]] outbreak.

## Identity and Background

He survived [[The Cataclysm]] before leaving Southport with fellow priests who had taken vows of poverty. They sought a peaceful home among the island's druids. When [[Kuo-Toa]] arrived to claim the temple, a cockatrice interrupted the confrontation by petrifying priests and invaders alike. Father Warrick lost approximately seventy years to the stone.

He recalled a good life with [[Janeera]] in Southport and wondered whether he should have remained there.

## Personality and Motivations

Father Warrick hoped to reopen the temple and gradually awaken the Kuo-Toa, teaching them about Sigil rather than assuming they could not change. News of Southport's plague redirected his immediate purpose toward helping its people.

## Abilities

His restorative and abjuration magic enabled him to free several other priests after Ceril restored him with Greater Restoration.

## Campaign History

The party identified his clothing and religious equipment as belonging to a senior cleric who might help restore the others. Ceril freed him first, and Father Warrick began awakening fellow priests. The scene did not establish that every priest or any Kuo-Toa had been restored. Learning of Southport's illness, he sought to hurry to the mainland with the freed clergy. See [[session_025#Chunk 0000]].

## Final Status

The restored priests later delivered [[Deep Roses]] to Southport, curing individual cases while reinfection continued. Their aid was established; Father Warrick's proposed reopening of the temple and gradual restoration of the Kuo-Toa were not shown completed. His last personal conversation was at the temple, with the clergy's subsequent work reported in [[session_033#Chunk 0000]].
