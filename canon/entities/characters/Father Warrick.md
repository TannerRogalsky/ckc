A high-ranking priest of [[Sigil]] who was petrified for approximately seventy years at the [[Temple of Sigil]] on [[Otyugh Isle]].

Father Warrick survived [[The Cataclysm]] and belonged to a group of priests who left [[Southport]] to establish a new temple. They made peace with local druids before a cockatrice petrified all twelve priests and nineteen invading [[Kuo-Toa]].

In session 025, [[Ceril]] restored Father Warrick first. Father Warrick then used his abjuration abilities to free several more priests. The priests returned to Southport, where their work helped distribute [[Deep Roses]] and contain [[Mana Sickness]].
