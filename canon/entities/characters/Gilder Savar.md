# Gilder Savar

Gilder Savar is the lich proprietor of [[Savar Brews]] in [[Cutlass Cray]]. Once a human companion of [[Ceril]] and [[Vanzia Vynnfae]], he preserved himself to continue perfecting his brewing over centuries.

## Identity and Background

He fled [[Brimbolyn]] with Ceril and Vanzia Vynnfae before [[The Cataclysm]]. During that flight he was starving and asked to be left behind; Ceril used Skywrite to mark his position for possible aid. After escaping the elven lands, Gilder Savar chose lichdom rather than let old age end his work, applying preserving magic to his own corpse. See [[session_013#Chunk 0000]] and [[session_034#Chunk 0000]].

## Personality and Motivations

He sought the time needed to perfect his craft, explaining that the best drinks could require centuries to distill. Continuing that work was his stated reason for undeath.

## Abilities

He brewed magical drinks and protective or emergency potions. His craft also supported poison-making: he fashioned Mana Worm Poison from [[Kerben]]'s [[Mana Sea]] fungi, capable of making a spellcaster's magic misfire dangerously.

## Equipment and Resources

- [[Savar Brews]] — His brewing business and the party's source of potions, consumables, and later poison craft.

## Campaign History

Ceril did not initially recognize his lich form when they reunited, though Gilder Savar recognized his old companion. He explained his transformation and traded potions for gold and stories. See [[session_027#Chunk 0000]].

The party later confirmed that he was the same human brewer from Ceril's escape, rather than a separate namesake. He prepared Kerben's Mana Worm Poison in exchange for a purple-worm skin rug. See [[session_034#Chunk 0000]].

## Final Status

He was last established brewing at Savar Brews, having chosen lichdom to continue that craft. No later departure, death, or change of purpose was recorded beyond [[session_034#Chunk 0000]].
