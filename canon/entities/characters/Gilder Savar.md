Gilder Savar is a lich who preserved himself through magic applied to his own corpse. He runs [[Savar Brews]] in [[Cutlass Cray]].

Before becoming a lich, Gilder Savar traveled alongside [[Ceril]] and [[Vanzia Vinfei]] during their escape from [[Brimbolyn]] decades before the current campaign. Ceril did not recognize him in his lich form, but Gilder Savar recognized Ceril. He revealed that he chose undeath so he could live long enough to perfect his craft — "the best drinks take centuries to distill into perfection."

He provided Ceril with potions and consumables in exchange for gold and story-sharing, including protective and emergency-use supplies.
