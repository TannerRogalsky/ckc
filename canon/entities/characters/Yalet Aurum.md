# Yalet Aurum

Yalet Aurum is a golden-bodied Galeb Duurr living within a great stone monument outside [[Brimbolyn]]. Powerful, boastful, and fond of riddles, he ultimately staged his own decline to end his younger brother [[Yalet Mora]]'s pursuit of vengeance.

## Identity and Background

His body and head are solid gold. He encased himself within a towering stone face in the jungle, with shrubs growing like hair, and spoke through its openings in a booming voice.

## Personality and Motivations

Yalet Aurum claimed superiority over his brother: where Yalet Mora produced small rocks, he produced mountains. His later attempt to give his brother closure was still accompanied by a boast that he surpassed him even in morality. He wanted the visitors to care for Yalet Mora while he remained to reshape his own surroundings.

## Relationships

- [[Yalet Mora]] — His younger brother, who regarded him as an arrogant bully and wanted to take his golden head. Yalet Aurum settled the feud by letting him believe he had outlasted his older brother.
- [[Red Caesar]] — A visitor he challenged with a riddle, warned about Penumbra collection, and later trusted with the truth behind the staged defeat.

## Abilities

He commanded powerful earth magic and could produce mountains and gold. [[Penumbra]] was one of the few stones over which he said he had no power. A fallen fragment of [[Crone]] strengthened him rather than injuring him; his claims of resulting invulnerability were not independently demonstrated.

## Campaign History

Yalet Aurum posed a riddle: if he could produce only mountains and his brother only small rocks, what could his brother make someone that he could not? The party answered "bolder," playing on boulders and courage. Laughing, he vomited up chunks of gold as their reward. He confirmed the [[The Order of Seasons]]' interest in Penumbra and warned them to act faster than an elf. [[canon/sessions/session_014#Chunk 0003]]

When the party later brought Yalet Mora to confront him, Yalet Aurum appeared crushed beneath a moon fragment. The companions refused to kill an apparently helpless opponent. He surrendered a massive golden brain and claimed to be dying, allowing his brother to leave satisfied. Attempts to heal or reshape the apparent wound accomplished nothing. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0002]]

Once Yalet Mora had gone, Yalet Aurum revealed that the moonstone had empowered him. The decline was a performance meant to give his insecure brother closure, and both remained alive. He asked the companions to take care of Yalet Mora while continuing to boast of his superiority.

## Final Status

Yalet Aurum was alive and strengthened beneath the moon fragment when the party left. He intended to reshape the surrounding land and felt the moon's influence slowly lifting as Crone recovered. His later alterations were not recorded. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0002]]
