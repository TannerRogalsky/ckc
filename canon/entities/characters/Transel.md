Transel is a beach troll the party encountered on an island during a hunt for [[The League of New Stark]]. The party killed the other troll, but [[Red Caesar]] charmed Transel and chose to help him escape the island. The party built him a raft, and he followed them to [[Cutlass Cray]], where he became bartender at [[The Brine & Bodak]]. He later came to run the establishment.
