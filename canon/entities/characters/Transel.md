# Transel

Transel is a beach troll spared by the party who became bartender, and later proprietor, of [[The Brine & Bodak]] in [[Cutlass Cray]].

## Campaign History

During a troll hunt for [[The League of New Stark]], the party killed the other troll but [[Red Caesar]] charmed Transel and chose to help him leave the island. They built a raft, and he followed them to Cutlass Cray. [[canon/sessions/session_026]]

[[Domyx]] later found him training at The Brine & Bodak, assisted by a hobgoblin. Transel spoke only Runic and was working to overcome the language barrier rather than causing trouble. He subsequently came to run the establishment. [[canon/sessions/session_027#Chunk 0000]]
