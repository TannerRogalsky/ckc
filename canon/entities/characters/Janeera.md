A deceased orc seer who prepared magic allowing [[Southport]]'s mayor to summon her spirit for counsel. More than fifty years earlier, [[Witness]] entrusted her with part of his world chart before leaving Stark to map another plane.

Janeera foresaw the party's arrival after the sky was repaired and guided them toward [[Ninki Nanka]], whom she identified as the source of [[Mana Sickness]]. She had previously sent a warrior expedition with her chart fragment, but the Mana Sea killed them. Her potion collection supplied the party for the successful expedition.
