# Janeera

Janeera was an orc seer of [[Southport]] whose prepared magic allowed [[Mayor Yoris]] to summon her spirit for counsel. Her guidance and preserved chart helped the party end the source of [[Mana Sickness]].

## Identity and Background

More than fifty years before the party's arrival, [[Witness]] entrusted her with part of his world chart before leaving Stark to explore another plane. Her preparations preserved both that knowledge and access to her counsel after death.

## Campaign History

Janeera foresaw the party's arrival after the sky was repaired. Her summoned spirit identified [[Ninki Nanka]] as the source of the spreading illness and explained that a previous warrior expedition carrying her chart fragment had died in the [[Mana Sea]]. Her potion collection supplied the new expedition. See [[session_033#Chunk 0000]].

## Final Status

The party recovered the missing chart portion and freed Ninki Nanka from the Penumbra-born curse, ending the source of new infections. Janeera remained deceased; her successful guidance did not establish resurrection. See [[session_033#Chunk 0003]].
