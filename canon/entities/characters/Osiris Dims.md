# Osiris Dims

Osiris Dims is an aasimar born in [[Brimbolyn]], where he helped [[Vokenar]] learn about surviving aasimar and [[The Order of Seasons]]. His own existence illustrated what changing the past through the [[Demi-Spell]] might put at risk.

## Identity and Background

He was forty-five when he met Vokenar. His aasimar mother had fallen from [[Arkadia]] and married a half-elf who showed her kindness. His father later became ill, and the couple's opposition to the Order prevented them from staying in the city. Osiris Dims had not seen them for some time and suspected they might have gone to [[Southport]], without knowing their destination. He regarded remaining in Brimbolyn as honoring their wish for him to have a better life.

## Campaign History

He welcomed Vokenar's interest in other aasimar and mentioned [[Gossa]]. He regarded the Magic City and the Order of Seasons as inseparable and understood the Demi-Spell as a way to undo past damage. He reported hearing that new resources might allow it within a year and directed Vokenar to the Academy for details. [[canon/sessions/session_014#Chunk 0002]]

The warning that opponents of the Order had suffered, and the account of a past attempt to kill everyone who knew the Demi-Spell, came from Gossa in the subsequent conversation. Vokenar wondered whether [[Morel Chainsunder]] had been responsible; that identity was not established.

## Final Status

Osiris Dims was last encountered in Brimbolyn. His parents' destination and his own later course remain unknown. [[canon/sessions/session_014#Chunk 0002]]
