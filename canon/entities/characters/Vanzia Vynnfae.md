# Vanzia Vynnfae

Vanzia Vynnfae is a high elf paladin and former revolutionary who escaped the elven lands with [[Ceril]]. She later took an official position in the [[Broyish Empire]], believing it could prevent a return to the tyranny of [[Queen Caeradwyn]].

## Identity and Background

After the queen's death, she fled west from [[Brimbolyn]] with Ceril and [[Gilder Savar]]. Ceril's sight of lines of light crossing the sky helped guide their course before [[The Cataclysm]]. Decades later she had made a new life in Broy.

## Personality and Motivations

She felt that fleeing had shirked her revolutionary duty to those still captive. Her eventual allegiance to the Empire reflected a desire to keep the former elven order from rising again and to live somewhere Queen Caeradwyn could never regain power. Her playful claim of absolute honesty during the reunion was a joke, not an established binding oath.

## Relationships

- [[Ceril]] — Her trusted escape companion from the old world, reunited with her after decades apart.
- [[Naomi Ue]] — Her associate in a network clearing criminal records through access to imperial paperwork.
- [[Vizier Jade]] — Her imperial employer, whose custody of Obould she described to the party.

## Campaign History

Vanzia Vynnfae fled the collapsing elven order with Ceril. They left the exhausted Gilder Savar behind with Ceril's plea for help, and continued west after pursuers chose to spare them. [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0000]]

The party encountered her working with Naomi Ue in the [[Broyish Capital]]. She recognized Ceril, teased him about their shared history, and confirmed that [[Obould]] was a prisoner of war and leverage against [[The League of New Stark]]. She offered to guide them toward him, warning that an audience with Vizier Jade would come first. She also offered citizenship papers and explained their greater exposure to imperial scrutiny. [[canon/sessions/session_023#Chunk 0001]]

## Final Status

Vanzia Vynnfae was last personally encountered working within Broy's imperial system. Her later position after the emperor's defeat is not established. [[canon/sessions/session_023#Chunk 0001]]
