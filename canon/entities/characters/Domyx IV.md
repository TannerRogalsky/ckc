Domyx IV is the former ruler of [[Clan Akathia]] and father of [[Domyx]] and [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]]. He formerly ruled the clan with strict enforcement of titan isolationist laws and has kept the truth about [[Emperor Shen]] hidden from his children and the broader clan.

## Plot Events

In session 024, Domyx IV confronted his returned son [[Domyx]] at the exterior of [[Acathian Manor]]. Initially showing a mix of genuine relief and anger, he attempted to negotiate a quiet resolution — offering to cover up Domyx's return and send him back to Stark. When Domyx refused to forget the truth about their grandfather and renounced his family name by discarding his Akathian medallion, Domyx IV disowned him, declaring "You are an Akathian no more. You are my son no more." He departed in sorrow, breaking their relationship until their later reconciliation.

## Session 038

When [[Domyx]] returned to [[Acathian Manor]], Domyx IV apologized for decades of mistreatment and acknowledged the corrupt influence of his father, [[Emperor Shen]]. He had felt relief at Emperor Shen's death, mixed with shame and grief, and learned that [[Farron Acathian II]] had also died.

No single house now ruled the titan homeland. Domyx IV accepted that people should make independent choices instead of following tradition blindly. He and Domyx reconciled and carried a heavy log together while his son began telling the story of his travels.
