# Domyx IV

Domyx IV is the former ruler of [[Clan Akathia]], father of [[Domyx]] and [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]], and son of [[Emperor Shen]]. After enforcing his people's isolation and disowning Domyx, he acknowledged his mistreatment and reconciled with him as the titan houses adopted cooperative rule.

## Personality and Motivations

His former rule prioritized strict enforcement of titan traditions. He concealed Emperor Shen's crimes, arguing that protecting the people's sense of their history and sanctity mattered more than revealing its mistakes. He blamed [[Ulrich Fjoller]] for encouraging Domyx's dangerous curiosity. His eventual apology acknowledged that this inherited obedience and secrecy had harmed his family.

## Relationships

- [[Domyx]] — Son whose return brought genuine relief as well as anger over the clan's rules. Disowning broke their relationship until Domyx IV's apology and their later shared work.
- [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] — Daughter who concealed her pregnancy and escaped his increasingly severe rule with Ulrich Fjoller.
- [[Emperor Shen]] — Father whose corrupt influence shaped his rule. His death brought relief, grief, and shame at that relief.

## Campaign History

When Domyx returned to [[Acathian Manor]], Domyx IV offered to conceal the violation of clan law, spend an evening together, and send him back below. Domyx refused to forget Emperor Shen's responsibility for the broken sky and challenged the suppression of other Akathians living outside the mountains. Domyx IV acknowledged his father's monstrous conduct but insisted that their people must be protected from the knowledge.

Domyx renounced the clan by discarding his medallion. Domyx IV declared that he was no longer an Akathian or his son, then departed in sorrow. See [[session_024#Chunk 0003]].

## Final Status

After Emperor Shen's final defeat, Domyx IV apologized for decades of mistreatment and admitted the corruption of their lineage. He also learned of [[Farron Acathian II]]'s death. No single house ruled the homeland any longer; he accepted that its people should make their own choices and that he, too, must change. Father and son reconciled and carried a heavy log together while Domyx began telling his story. The reunion did not establish restored clan membership or a new governing office for either.
