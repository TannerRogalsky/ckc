# Lady Acelia

Lady Acelia was [[Alamar]]'s younger sister and the youngest daughter of the Rodgard family, born before [[King Maniasis]] succumbed to madness. Her personal fate remained uncertain, while [[Lady Acelia's Chalice]] survived in [[Castle Kaedon]].

## Identity and Background

After the crown was abdicated and the royal family dispersed, she was believed to have traveled north to pursue peace with the dwarves. Another Rodgard relative retained a place in [[Kaedon]]'s senate; she was not identified as that senator. See [[session_021#Chunk 0000]].

## Equipment and Resources

- [[Lady Acelia's Chalice]] — Former oak drinking cup with jade insets and her name scratched in a child's handwriting. [[Vokenar]] recovered it from a flower-painted guest room, and [[Ceril]] later claimed it from the party's treasure.

## Final Status

[[Red Caesar]] considered it likely that [[The Cataclysm]] had killed her, but did not know. The survival of her chalice established a trace of her childhood rather than confirmation of her death. See [[session_021#Chunk 0000]].
