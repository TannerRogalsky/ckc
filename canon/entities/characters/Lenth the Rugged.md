# Lenth the Rugged

Lenth the Rugged was a legendary pirate, prolific romantic adventurer, and inventor of magical potions. The father of [[Xander MacLenth]] and grandfather of [[Pleasance MacLenth]], he sought the lasting legacy his son preferred to spend on rum and a comfortable retirement.

## Identity and Background

According to Xander MacLenth, his father had a wife on every continent: one of each race, with some "of elemental varieties" besides. He had "sowed his wild oats across the land," leaving a family history as sprawling as his travels. These were his son's accounts of his reputation, rather than a verified roster of marriages. [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0001]]

Pleasance MacLenth kept many volumes of his adventures. Volume thirty-three described his travels in the Southlands and a romance near [[Southport]] so lavishly that a single erotic encounter occupied two chapters. She understood that relationship as the reason he returned to Southport for the final chapter of his life. Her extravagant suggestions that he had adventured into astonishing old age exaggerated the account; he was understood to have died in his early sixties. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0000]]

## Personality and Motivations

Lenth the Rugged wanted fame and a lasting legacy. Xander MacLenth described himself instead as "a pirate through and through," wanting to spend all his wealth before his "watery grave" and drink out his remaining days in [[Cutlass Cray]]. When [[Vokenar]] had never heard of the supposedly legendary father, the ghost mocked the "old fool": if even his name was unknown, perhaps his grand ambition had failed too. [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0001]]

## Abilities

He was a noted innovator of magical potions. [[Deception by Lenth]] bottled a perfume based on his own personal musk, helping its wearer deceive others. His other inventions included the [[Potion of Eels]] and the [[Potion of Clairvoyance]]. Even his recovered tomb retained the perfume's alluring scent: floral and musky, with a powdery finish. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0000]]

## Campaign History

Vokenar learned the family stories from Xander MacLenth's ghost and brought the name back to the crew. The party did not know the legend and did not pursue a separate investigation of his legacy. His magical preparations nevertheless surfaced during their travels. [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0001]]

Much later, they recovered the [[Tomb of Lenth the Rugged]] from [[Ninki Nanka]]'s abandoned hoard. The gilded sarcophagus bore his name in the language of [[Westerness]]; its partly open lid revealed old bones and tattered robes. They intended to return the remains to his family. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0003]]

[[Red Caesar]] carried the tomb to [[Bookbinders Cray]] on a floating disc. Pleasance MacLenth recognized its scent and inscription, confirmed that it held her grandfather, and enthusiastically consulted her family histories. She read the salacious Southport episode aloud to an uncomfortable Red Caesar before accepting the tomb for display in her library. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0000]]

## Final Status

Lenth the Rugged's remains were returned to Pleasance MacLenth at Bookbinders Cray. Whatever Xander MacLenth thought of his father's fame, the pirate's family histories, magical inventions, and unmistakable perfume outlived him.
