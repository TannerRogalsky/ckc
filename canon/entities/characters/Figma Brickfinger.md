# Figma Brickfinger

Figma Brickfinger leads [[Figma Brickfinger's Union]] at [[The Pit]]. She managed the upheaval after the deep worlders' liberation and later confirmed that [[The Funnel]] had been reopened, withdrawing her workers from the dangers beyond it.

## Personality and Motivations

She prioritized political stability as formerly separated communities began sharing the city. She acknowledged the party's help while warning it not to worsen an already difficult diplomatic transition.

## Campaign History

After [[Morel Chainsunder]]'s curse fell, Figma Brickfinger posted guards around the revealed [[Penumbra]] and helped the party prepare it for transport. Her dwarves removed the drained obsidian shell, leaving the smaller star-filled core. She acknowledged a debt to the party and warned that the road to political settlement would be long. See [[session_013#Chunk 0000]].

[[Vokenar]] later visited her at [[The Palace of the Pit]] to ask about integration. She reported movement and disorder alongside a determination to rebuild. The deep worlders no longer competed for underground space as before, though minor conflicts persisted. New palace wings, wider roads, and minecart tracks marked industrial expansion. She relayed scout reports of [[Broyish Empire]] airships while admitting she had not seen them herself. See [[session_022#Chunk 0000]].

## Final Status

At her last established encounter, she confirmed the Funnel's requested excavation was complete and said she had recalled the workers from the dangers beyond the breakthrough. [[Courteous Cam]] described the spacious region and unfamiliar air beyond it. The party took responsibility for continuing the descent; no later change to her leadership was recorded. See [[session_035#Chunk 0002]].
