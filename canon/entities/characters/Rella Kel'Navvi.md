# Rella Kel'Navvi

Rella Kel'Navvi is a speaking blink dog and companion of [[Ceril]]. A former crew member aboard [[The Opal]], he volunteered to remain with [[Alamar]] at [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]].

## Identity and Background

Rella Kel'Navvi had lived among high elves before spending much of the time after [[The Cataclysm]] alone. The party found him during its adventure at [[Otyugh Isle]], and he joined them afterward.

## Relationships

- [[Ceril]] — Named Rella Kel'Navvi using his own surname. Rella Kel'Navvi addressed him as master, recognized his anxiety about returning to elven lands, and offered hope that their society might have changed.
- [[Alamar]] — Rella Kel'Navvi volunteered to serve him and help keep him safe at the lagoon until the party needed him again.
- [[The Opal]] — Former ship's hound and janitor, whose small errands and cleaning eased the crew's daily work.

## Abilities

Rella Kel'Navvi can speak and teleport by blinking. Aboard The Opal, he used this ability to move trash and misplaced objects where they belonged, making repetitive chores nearly invisible to the crew. He was familiar with the lagoon's terrain and attentive to Ceril's unease.

## Campaign History

As the party approached the lagoon again, Rella Kel'Navvi pointed out smoke and a bonfire to [[Domyx]], revealing that someone was living at the previously abandoned site. His warning led the ship to visit Alamar's developing refuge.

He volunteered to stay there with [[Kilosaurus]] and three deckhands; [[Tuna]] welcomed having a dog nearby. Before the party sailed toward the elves, Rella Kel'Navvi spoke to Ceril about his apprehension. Drawing on his own experience among high elves, he suggested that the world after the Cataclysm could differ from the society Ceril had escaped. Ceril agreed that it could hardly be worse, and Rella Kel'Navvi bade him travel safely before blinking away to remain at the lagoon. Their farewell is recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0004]].

Rella Kel'Navvi was still at the growing settlement during the later preparations to explore [[Castle Kaedon]]. When Ceril finally returned with the party and [[Jack Harvey]]'s hoard, Rella Kel'Navvi greeted him joyfully, running and blinking around him before taking a lookout position on a watch post. The reunion is recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_031#Chunk 0003]].

## Final Status

Rella Kel'Navvi was last directly seen alive at Lyngbakr Lagoon in session 031, shortly before the imperial assault. The epilogue established that the attack killed some residents and that many escaped, but did not identify his fate. His survival, escape, or later reunion with Ceril remains unconfirmed.
