# PAXO

PAXO was a murderous warforged that drove [[Witness]] from [[Southern Archipelago Castle]] and killed the humans sheltering there. The party destroyed PAXO while recovering Witness's chart.

## Identity and Background

PAXO was a cloaked, roughly human-sized construct made of metal pieces, with oddly fluid, humanlike movements. Its smooth, reflective faceplate distinguished it from an ordinary armored person. It introduced itself by name when it ambushed the party.

Its creator, origin, and reason for coming to the castle were not established.

## Personality and Motivations

PAXO announced that the remaining lifeforms had to be eradicated and attacked accordingly. That declared objective was consistent with the massacre at the refuge, but the record did not explain who or what had given it that purpose.

## Relationships

- [[Witness]] — The refuge's former ally, whom PAXO hunted away before the party arrived.

## Abilities

PAXO projected concentrated heat beams and possessed enough physical force to crush human bodies. Its faceplate also emitted a supernatural slowing effect, making the surrounding dust and those caught before it move more slowly. These capabilities ended with its destruction.

## Equipment and Resources

Its reflective faceplate was made from an unfamiliar alloy resembling quicksilver or mercury mixed with gold, silver, and other fine metals. Electricity could change its shape and outward appearance, producing effects resembling illusions. [[Kerben]] removed it during PAXO's defeat; the party examined it as a potentially valuable curiosity and placed it in [[The Opal]]'s hold.

## Campaign History

The party entered the castle to investigate Witness's former refuge and found human bodies bearing concentrated burns and crushing injuries. Using [[Beryzoz's Teeth]], [[Red Caesar]] questioned a dead inhabitant, who described a wicked new construct that had driven Witness away and killed the people there.

After the party recovered a portion of the [[Chart of the Witness]], PAXO ambushed Kerben and [[Ceril]] in the cramped interior. Its heat beams matched the wounds found on the victims. The companions fought it together, with Red Caesar's Bigby's Hand restraining its metal body.

Kerben pried off the faceplate with [[Shark's Edge]] as the conjured hand crushed the exposed body into a twisted mass of metal. PAXO's destruction ended the threat at the abandoned refuge. The investigation and defeat are recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_025#Chunk 0001]].

## Final Status

PAXO was destroyed in session 025. Its faceplate was recovered into the party's shared hold; no later sale, transfer, or use was recorded. Its maker and the cause of its murderous purpose remained unknown.
