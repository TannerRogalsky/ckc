# Gossa

Gossa is an aasimar teacher from [[Academia Lux]] who later became a merchant and collector of old-world art in [[Brimbolyn]]'s [[Elvish Marketplace]]. Her reunion with [[Vokenar]] connected his earlier life in [[Arkadia]] with other survivors below.

## Identity and Background

She taught Vokenar in Arkadia, suggesting that becoming a Guardian was not his only path and invoking [[Sigil]]'s guidance. She remained in Arkadia when others fell, then traveled below in search of survivors. See [[session_011#Chunk 0000]] and [[session_014#Chunk 0002]].

## Personality and Motivations

Gossa preserved beauty from the old world, preferring art to gems in trade. She trusted [[The Order of Seasons]]' promised answer to the disaster while admitting that she understood little of its technical basis and that its members kept secrets. She urged Vokenar to learn more before assuming the Order was an enemy, despite acknowledging the risks to people born since the Cataclysm.

## Abilities

Skills learned in Arkadia allowed her to craft magical accessories. For [[Ceril]], she made a volcanic-rock necklace enabling him to summon his familiar as a magma elemental, with claws, fire breath, and an explosive burst when destroyed.

## Campaign History

Gossa immediately recognized Vokenar at the market. He explained that the goddesses had sent him to repair the sky. She warned that an earlier opponent of the [[Demi-Spell]] had tried to kill those who knew it and sought further flooding; Vokenar's suspicion that this was [[Morel Chainsunder]] was not confirmed. See [[session_014#Chunk 0002]].

Ceril later exchanged his Mask of Ogun for the magma-familiar amulet. Gossa displayed the artwork behind her shop. She was last established trading and collecting there; no later personal fate was recorded. See [[session_014#Chunk 0003]].
