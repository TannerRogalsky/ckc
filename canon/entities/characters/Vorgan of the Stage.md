Vorgan of the Stage runs [[The Bad Hare Day Theatre]] in [[Cutlass Cray]].

In session 034, [[Kerben]] found Vorgan preparing the final performance of a play about the moon's destruction. Vorgan joined [[The Opal]] as its performer, intending to learn from the crew's travels and return with new material for the theatre.
