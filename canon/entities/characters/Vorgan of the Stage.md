# Vorgan of the Stage

Vorgan of the Stage runs [[The Bad Hare Day Theatre]] in [[Cutlass Cray]]. He joined [[The Opal]] as an entertainer to learn from its voyages and bring new material back to the theatre.

## Personality and Motivations

He wanted to experience the wider world, gather stories, and help sustain [[40 Carats]] through new performances. His role aboard the ship was to support morale, rather than a claim of established martial prowess.

## Campaign History

[[Kerben]] found Vorgan of the Stage preparing the final performance of a play about the moon's destruction. He recruited him as the ship's performer, linking future travels to the theatre's storytelling. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0000]]

## Final Status

Vorgan of the Stage was last personally established joining The Opal. His intended return to the theatre with new material was not separately recorded. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0000]]
