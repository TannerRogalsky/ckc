# Lyngbakr

Lyngbakr is a gigantic, speaking [[Saratan]] who helped the party restore [[Stark]]'s broken sky. Formerly stranded below the heavens, he returned to [[Arkadia]] after sealing the [[Hole in the Sky]].

## Identity and Background

Lyngbakr fell from Arkadia through the Hole in the Sky. Fields of heather on his back gave him his name, and his immense body supported [[Westerness]] and [[Castle Kaedon]]. He was noted in [[Bookbinders Cray]]'s records as a member of the Saratan, gigantic turtle- or whale-like beings whose magical aura makes people perceive them as islands.

## Personality and Motivations

Lyngbakr wanted to return to Arkadia and understood that he had to pass through the opening before it healed shut. He cooperated with the party's restoration work, asked what they needed him to do, and thanked them for helping him. When the lagoon came under attack, he urged Red Caesar to help his companions and insisted on taking Alamar with him because Alamar would not survive being left behind.

## Relationships

- [[Ceril]] — Telepathic contact and collaborator in repairing the sky. Ceril sought divine guidance for their work and accompanied Lyngbakr into Arkadia.
- [[Alamar]] — Bound to the castle's location by magic that Alamar believed also constrained Lyngbakr. Lyngbakr ultimately carried him into Arkadia.
- [[Red Caesar]] — Supplied the condensed hoard that made the final repair possible. Lyngbakr also agreed to gather Akasha for his research.
- [[Vokenar]] — Fellow seeker of a return to Arkadia, who asked Lyngbakr about his own needs and requested his protection for the lagoon settlement.

## Abilities

Lyngbakr communicates telepathically, including with Ceril and Red Caesar, and produces a rumbling, whale-like song. His Saratan nature gives him an island-like appearance.

Consuming [[Penumbra]] restores his strength and enables him to rise from the sea and fly toward the heavens. Large feedings can require weeks of digestion. Near the damaged sky, silver-and-black mist and black sky-matter leave his body and restore the missing heavens, weakening him through the effort. He can also submerge the land he carries, concealing it beneath the waves, and dive beyond the continental shelf into the deeper sea of [[Akasha]].

## Campaign History

### Recovering strength

The party encountered Lyngbakr at [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]] after the adventure at [[Otyugh Isle]]. Learning that he had fallen from Arkadia connected him to their investigation of the broken sky. Castle Kaedon's spires initially poked out of the water. The party cleared those spires of demons, including [[Sunspite]], before discovering Alamar cloistered within. Later Penumbra feedings raised more of the castle and allowed access to [[Castle Kaedon Arena]]. [[Sigil]] later assured them that supplying him with Penumbra offered a way to repair it.

They fed him the purified [[Darvenlast]] core and fragments recovered from gnoll territory through the lagoon's chute. Alamar expected digestion to take weeks, perhaps a month, before Lyngbakr could rise farther and expose more of Castle Kaedon. Between explorations, he could descend to hide the castle.

By the next visit, Lyngbakr had finished digesting the earlier feeding. The party supplied the drained fragment from [[Theotropa]]. The island and surrounding pillars rose together, exposing roughly twice as much of the castle and bringing its turrets into view.

### Repairing the heavens

The great Penumbra mass recovered from the [[Nalfeshne]]'s throne room restored enough strength for Lyngbakr to emerge above the sea, his body dwarfing the lagoon and [[Cutlass Cray]]. He shook buildings, fiends, animated bones, and further Penumbra from his back, then swallowed the scattered stones. Akasha destroyed the fallen creatures and rose as silver mist. Castle Kaedon broke apart during his renewed flight; its complete destruction was later confirmed in [[canon/sessions/session_031#Chunk 0004]].

Lyngbakr contacted Ceril because the sky remained broken and he did not know what to do next. Ceril sought [[Aeris]]'s guidance. Lyngbakr then climbed toward the Hole in the Sky, emitting mist that repaired its edges. The breach stopped growing and shrank by about a third, but the effort weakened him again and exhausted the Penumbra available at the castle.

At Red Caesar's suggestion, Lyngbakr explored the waters beyond the continental shelf and found a second sea of Akasha beneath the ordinary ocean. Those waters felt familiar and comfortable, like the oceans he remembered in Arkadia. He agreed to gather Akasha for Red Caesar's investigation of whether Penumbra could be its crystallized form. Vokenar asked him to watch over Alamar's settlement, and Lyngbakr submerged again. These exchanges are recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_021#Chunk 0002]].

When Ceril and Red Caesar returned with [[Jack Harvey]]'s condensed hoard, Lyngbakr was hiding deep beneath the largest waterfall near the lagoon. He said that a man had tried to kill him and forced him into the depths; the attacker was not identified in that account. Red Caesar released the hoard into star-like motes, which Lyngbakr consumed, giving him enough material to finish the repair.

Lyngbakr brought them to the surface during the imperial attack. Red Caesar remained on Stark while Alamar joined Ceril aboard the turtle. Lyngbakr ascended, shedding sky-matter that filled the smaller wounds before closing the great breach. He passed into Arkadia with his passengers just before the sky sealed beneath them.

Exhausted after returning the Penumbra to the heavens and restoring Arkadia's ocean floor, Lyngbakr rested while Ceril and Alamar continued inland. He directed them to seek Aeris, whose body they had healed, as recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_032#Chunk 0000]].

## Final Status

The epilogue confirmed that Lyngbakr had returned home to Arkadia and was swimming in its replenishing oceans. The sky's repair and his return were accomplished, despite [[Emperor Shen]]'s false claim that he had killed the turtle.
