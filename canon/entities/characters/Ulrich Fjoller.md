# Ulrich Fjoller

Ulrich Fjoller is a titan, [[Domyx]]'s close friend, and the partner of [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]]. Rescued from execution by his clan, he served aboard [[The Opal]] before leaving with Lorelai Lapis-Acathian to prepare for their child's birth.

## Identity and Background

A former loyalist of [[Clan Akathia]], he researched its history and uncovered an erased relative who had [[Touching the Sky|touched the sky]] before Domyx. [[Charlotta Fjoller]] is his sister.

## Personality and Motivations

His loyalty to Domyx and concern about the suppressed family history led him to break a grave titan taboo by following his friend down the mountains. Even under imprisonment, he refused to disclose why he had gone. After his rescue, protecting his expected family required a life beyond the clan.

## Relationships

- [[Domyx]] — His close friend, whom he warned despite the risk of exile or execution. Domyx returned to rescue him and helped him learn shipboard routines.
- [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] — His partner and the mother of their expected child; she concealed her pregnancy and sought his release.
- [[Charlotta Fjoller]] — His sister, to whom he had disclosed that the suppressed history could undermine the clan.
- [[Clan Akathia]] — His former allegiance, whose authorities imprisoned and sentenced him after his unauthorized descent.

## Abilities

Ulrich Fjoller could research clan records, keep written accounts, and update charts. Initially inexperienced at sea, he learned shipboard work and later manned a cannon and advised Kerben during the naval escape. [[Trent Indorra]] had proposed teaching him the gunner's duties, without establishing a completed appointment as master gunner.

## Campaign History

Ulrich Fjoller intercepted Domyx after his descent, warning that another sky-touching blood relative had been erased from the records and still lived in Stark. He believed the shared blood endangered Domyx. [[canon/sessions/session_013#Chunk 0000]]

After returning home, he was imprisoned for months and sentenced to execution. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian secretly told Domyx of the sentence and her pregnancy. Charlotta Fjoller later recalled his warning that [[Emperor Shen]] was some kind of monster, though he had lacked time to establish precisely what that meant. [[canon/sessions/session_016#Chunk 0000]] [[canon/sessions/session_024#Chunk 0003]]

At the [[Prison of Frost]] near [[Acathian Manor]], [[Ceril]] dispelled the magical barrier holding him. Ulrich Fjoller greeted Domyx and Lorelai Lapis-Acathian with relief and asked whether the world below would be safe. He and Lorelai Lapis-Acathian escaped to The Opal and joined its company. [[canon/sessions/session_024#Chunk 0004]]

He took up recordkeeping and chart work, then helped Kerben escape the imperial fleet with cannon fire and naval advice. [[canon/sessions/session_025#Chunk 0000]] [[canon/sessions/session_031#Chunk 0000]]

## Final Status

Ulrich Fjoller and Lorelai Lapis-Acathian left The Opal to prepare for a child still expected in the coming months. Southport was discussed as a home, but she favored [[The White Drake]]. He hoped titan society might adapt without establishing reconciliation with the clan. Their eventual arrival and the birth were not recorded. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0000]]
