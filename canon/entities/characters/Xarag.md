# Xarag

Xarag was the black dragon that broke into [[The Opal]]'s hold and stole its treasure early in the voyage. The party later killed him at [[Xarag's Island]] and recovered the stolen goods, ending a longstanding threat tied to their ship and [[Obould]].

## Identity and Background

Xarag was the large black dragon encountered near the southern island that bears his name. His lineage connected him to the dragons later encountered at [[Castle Kaedon]], although [[Ceril]] identified that relationship as probable rather than certain.

An adult black dragon at the castle spoke of its father and treated its accompanying wyrmlings as its own children. That adult was a separate individual from Xarag.

## Personality and Motivations

Xarag recognized the returning party by the scent of their orc captain and taunted them for seeking treasure he claimed to have spent. The hoard they subsequently recovered showed that the stolen goods remained hidden on his island.

## Relationships

- [[The Opal]] — His earlier target. Damaging its hold and stealing its treasure gave the party a lasting reason to seek him out.
- [[Obould]] — Among the stolen goods were his personal gems, an award for saving southern orc tribes, and an inscribed wedding band.
- [[Kerben]] — Helped track and confront Xarag, delivered the killing shots, and salvaged material from his corpse.

## Abilities

Xarag's corrosive breath and acid magic were formidable threats, and he could call clouds of biting black gadflies to harry his enemies. His scales and acid-bearing remains supplied useful materials after his death.

## Equipment and Resources

Xarag kept a hoard in a concealed hollow in the island's cliff face. It included gold, mithril, and bags of gems, along with [[Orkland Pin of Courage]] and [[Obould's Wedding Band]]. These were stolen possessions that the party recovered and returned to The Opal's hold.

Some mithril appeared to have come from another ship, possibly an elven vessel; that provenance was not confirmed. Black dragon eggs were present near Xarag when the party returned to the island.

## Campaign History

### The theft and the castle dragons

Early in the voyage, Xarag wrecked The Opal's hold and took its treasure. The party initially considered itself too weak to confront him, leaving the theft as unfinished business.

During the later exploration of Castle Kaedon, an adult black dragon and four wyrmlings rose from the water to attack. Ceril considered them probably related to Xarag. The adult said that its father would be pleased to find the castle, suggesting a new haunt for their lineage. The party defeated this separate group, with [[Domyx]] killing the adult. Kerben used material from that dragon to craft [[Preparation Melf]]; it did not come from Xarag's later corpse. The encounter is recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_018#Chunk 0001]] and [[canon/sessions/session_018#Chunk 0002]].

### The return to Xarag's Island

After investigating [[Southern Archipelago Castle]], the party returned to Xarag's Island to recover the ship's treasure. The dark island was surrounded by luminescent algae, mist, and glowing cyan tide pools. Kerben followed claw marks from the water toward a nest, and [[Red Caesar]]'s divination guided the party when the visible trail faded. Xarag was resting but awake, with eggs nearby.

The party's acid-resistance preparations helped it survive Xarag's breath and corrosive magic. The dragon recognized Obould's scent and mocked the returning companions while attacking them and harrying Kerben with gadflies. Kerben's final shots brought him down into the glowing water, where he writhed before dying.

Kerben salvaged the remains, enabling Red Caesar to preserve two acid-resistance potions that would endure later reuse of Tasha's Cauldron. Domyx then found the cliff hollow containing the hoard. The party recovered gold, mithril, gems, and Obould's personal items into the ship's shared hold. Xarag's death and the recovery are recorded in [[canon/sessions/session_026#Chunk 0001]].

## Final Status

Xarag was killed at his island in session 026. The stolen treasure was recovered, and Obould later reclaimed his award and wedding band. The record did not establish the fate of the eggs left near Xarag's resting place.
