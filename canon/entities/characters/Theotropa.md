# Theotropa

Theotropa leads [[The Garden]], a druid sanctuary whose fleet supplies communities with food and livestock. She supported the repair mission after the party restored lightning and later asked [[Ceril]] to help cultivate newly exposed lands.

## Identity and Background

[[Alamar]] remembered a kindly leader of the Garden bearing the same name before [[The Cataclysm]]. His recollection suggested continuity across the ages but did not establish whether the present Theotropa was that same individual. [[canon/sessions/session_015#Chunk 0004]]

## Personality and Motivations

Theotropa sought to help surviving communities while protecting the druids and their waters. She hesitated to drain Penumbra without knowing the source or cost of its power. Although receptive to divine guidance, she wanted tangible progress before trusting a mission whose voices she could not hear. She understood destructive lightning and wildfire as forces from which new life could grow.

## Relationships

- [[The Garden]] — The sanctuary and aid network under her leadership, extending friendship and supplies to human settlements.
- [[Ceril]] — Offered him a place in the Garden after the mission and requested his help restoring vegetation and supporting settlers.
- [[The League of New Stark]] — A possible source of protection for the Garden's boats as dangerous creatures threatened the waterways; a completed protective agreement was not established.

## Equipment and Resources

The Garden built a fleet bearing cyan-and-green banners marked with a tree. Its druids raised crops and livestock for communities in need. Theotropa surrendered the nearly drained [[Penumbra]] fragment the Garden had studied and offered to reserve further fragments it acquired for the party.

## Campaign History

[[Vokenar]] located the Penumbra held behind her chambers. Theotropa offered it in exchange for destroying the [[Storm Phoenix]] south of the Garden and returning its trapped elemental power to the wider world. She could not yet promise regular deliveries to [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]] because the waters remained unsafe. [[canon/sessions/session_015]]

After the party destroyed the phoenix and cast its amber core into the sea, lightning and storms returned. Theotropa recognized the success from lightning striking one of her woodlands, explained the renewing role of wildfire, and gave them the fragment. An imperial scout using a cloudlike appearance on a device had sought it during their absence, but its protective concealment left him empty-handed. She offered to reserve future Penumbra acquisitions for the companions.

## Final Status

At [[Obould]] and [[Lady Jacinthe]]'s wedding, Theotropa thanked Ceril and invited him into the Garden's restoration work. He agreed to help restore vegetation and support peoples settling the lands revealed by retreating seas before retiring to [[Ceril's Star]]. [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002]]
