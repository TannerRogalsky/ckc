# Boril Erendel

Boril Erendel was a high elf spell researcher whose independent [[Demi-Spell]] project sought to force every spirit into a perfected high elf body. Formerly associated with [[The Order of Seasons]], he was killed at [[House Erendel]], ending his active replacement-body laboratory.

## Personality and Motivations

Boril Erendel resented the Order's confined working arrangements, comparing them to slavery. At [[The Garden]], he valued a view and time for art and poetry. His separation from [[Keys Caeradel]] was also ideological: where Keys Caeradel allowed some randomness in the next world, Boril Erendel demanded order and control.

He believed differences of ancestry produced fear and war, and regarded other peoples as inferior accidents of birth. His proposed solution was compulsory incarnation into high elf bodies, which he judged perfect even when challenged about his own imperfections. He later claimed that elves had preceded all other peoples and that the gods divided their power to protect divine rule. That account was his justification for supremacy, rather than established creation lore.

## Relationships

- [[The Order of Seasons]] — Former research colleagues whose constraints and limits on his changes led him to pursue an independent project.
- [[Keys Caeradel]] — Fellow architect whose tolerance of chaos he rejected. Boril Erendel threatened to proceed without him if his additions were refused.
- [[Feronia Caeradel]] — Worried about his absence and private project, asking the party to persuade him to return to [[House Kiirnodel]].
- [[Vokenar]] — His manipulation of reincarnation forced the cleric into a high elf body. He later revealed that Vokenar retained his aasimar nature; Vokenar recovered its powers while rejecting the forced transformation and delivered Boril Erendel's fatal wound.
- [[Corellon Larethian]] — The divine ideal invoked by his androgynous Larethian form; the transformation did not establish that he was the deity.

## Abilities

Boril Erendel was a powerful spellcrafter able to create duplicate defenders, prepare empty bodies, and influence reincarnation. [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] described his magic as potentially surpassing [[Queen Caeradwyn]]'s, a judgment rather than a demonstrated comparison.

His Larethian form combined destructive magic with an altered, silver-armored body. Its white hair, mask, and androgynous appearance expressed the elven perfection he sought to impose.

## Equipment and Resources

- [[The Garden]] — Former side office where he developed additions to the Demi-Spell away from the Order's supervision.
- [[House Erendel]] — Replacement-body laboratory beneath [[Brimbolyn]], defended by copies of himself and mirrors showing visitors as intended elven replacements. Empty flesh vessels were found there; the mirrors and building were destroyed after his death.

## Campaign History

Early in the party's travels, Boril Erendel advised looking east for answers about empire and imperial structure. He also helped arrange access to elven waters. Feronia Caeradel later sought his return from the Garden. See [[session_001]] and [[session_014#Chunk 0004]].

When [[Red Caesar]] and [[Ceril]] visited his Garden office, Boril Erendel outlined his intended changes to the Demi-Spell and claimed to have prepared thousands of empty high elf bodies. They did not inspect that claimed stockpile. Both rejected his scheme; Ceril considered capturing or killing the [[Rakshasa]] to pressure Keys Caeradel into halting the spell, but the proposal was not carried out. See [[session_015#Chunk 0002]].

Red Caesar reported Boril Erendel's extreme ideas and added spell pages to Rizolvir Kiirnodel, who agreed to send someone to retrieve him. Later, [[Illidrielle Gandara]] warned Ceril that the replacement-body scheme was already affecting the incarnation cycle. Vokenar's return in an imposed high elf body exposed its present consequences. The party decided to confront the laboratory after dealing with [[Farraday]]. See [[session_022]], [[session_032]], and [[session_033]].

At House Erendel, the party discovered several copies of Boril Erendel and a created figure resembling Vokenar. They defeated the three defending copies and entered a chamber of mirrors that projected his desired forms onto them. Boril Erendel revealed Vokenar's retained aasimar inheritance, then emerged in his Larethian form and fought to enforce his vision of perfection. See [[session_034]] and [[session_035#Chunk 0000]].

## Final Status

Vokenar's restored radiance killed Boril Erendel. Before fading away, he urged Vokenar to retain both bloodlines. The mirrors returned to ordinary reflections, and the party destroyed them and collapsed the laboratory, ending that active project. See [[session_035#Chunk 0001]].
