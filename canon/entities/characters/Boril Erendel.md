A former member of [[The Order of Seasons]] who worked at [[The Garden]] before establishing his replacement-body laboratory at [[House Erendel]]. He was killed in session 035. Advised that to understand empire and imperial structure, one should "look east if you want your answers."

[[Feronia Caeradel]] worries about his whereabouts and side project at the Garden, asking the party to convince him to return to [[House Kiirnodel]] so she can have a word with him.

In session 015, [[Red Caesar]] and [[Ceril]] visited Boril Erendel at his side office at the Garden. He revealed he left the Order because they had him "locked up in a side room" doing spellcraft without a view, "as though we were still slaves." He has been working on his own version of the [[Demi-Spell]] to make it "better" than [[Keys Caeradel]] would allow. While Keys Caeradel wants chaos to reign to some extent, Boril Erendel wants order to prevail. His plan is eugenics: he has already created thousands of empty high elf forms, and when the Demi-Spell is cast, all spirits will be incarnated into these perfect high elf bodies. He believes this would eliminate war, which "arises from differences" and "fears of the other." He told Red, "better, yes, and better than myself, even" — implying humans like Red would not be preserved in their current form. He said if Keys Caeradel doesn't allow him to integrate his ideas, he may forge ahead on his own.

Red and Ceril were horrified by his plan. Ceril called him an apostate whose inherent dangers magnify the Demi-Spell's evil "ten times." He proposed capturing or assassinating the [[Rakshasa]] as leverage against Keys Caeradel to halt the Demi-Spell entirely.

In session 022, [[Red Caesar]] reported to [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] that Boril Erendel had gone "off the rails" at [[The Garden]], adding nearly a hundred pages of his own design to the [[Demi-Spell]] and developing extreme political ideas. Rizolvir agreed to send someone to retrieve him.

By session 033, the party understood that Boril Erendel's active laboratory lies beneath [[Brimbolyn]]. They chose to confront him after dealing with [[Farraday]].

In session 034, [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] described Boril Erendel as an exceptionally powerful high elf whose magic may surpass [[Queen Caeradwyn]]'s. The party reached [[House Erendel]] and discovered several duplicates of Boril Erendel guarding the site, along with another created figure resembling [[Vokenar]].

## Session 035

The party defeated Boril Erendel's three clone guards and confronted him inside [[House Erendel]], where mirrors showed visitors as his intended high elf replacements. He claimed that elves preceded all other peoples and that the gods had divided their original power; this was his justification for forced reincarnation, rather than an established account of creation.

Boril Erendel emerged in a silver-armored, androgynous Larethian form, invoking [[Corellon Larethian]] as an ideal of elven perfection. He revealed that [[Vokenar]] retained his aasimar heritage alongside his high elf body. The party killed Boril Erendel, with Vokenar's restored radiance delivering the final blow. Boril Erendel urged Vokenar to retain both bloodlines before fading away. The mirrors returned to ordinary reflections, and the party destroyed the mirrors and collapsed the laboratory.
