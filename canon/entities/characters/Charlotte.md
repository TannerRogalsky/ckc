Sister of [[Ulrich Fjoller]] and sister-in-law to [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]]. She works at [[Acathian Manor]] preparing food and performing general maintenance duties. She was present when [[Domyx]] confronted his family about the truth of [[Emperor Shen]]'s crimes.

## Plot Events

In session 024, Charlotte approached [[Domyx]] upon his arrival at [[Acathian Manor]] and warned him that his return was against clan rules. When Domyx revealed the truth about his grandfather breaking the sky, Charlotte was shocked. She overheard [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] emerge from hiding, confirming that Lorelai Lapis-Acathian had heard everything about their grandfather. Charlotte backed away from the confrontation as Lorelai Lapis-Acathian revealed her pregnancy and plan to flee with Ulrich.
