# Ninki Nanka

Ninki Nanka is an ancient green dragon and former defender of the elves of [[Windsurf]]. Freed from the curse that made the dragon the source of [[Mana Sickness]], Ninki Nanka departed to protect its people.

## Identity and Background

Before [[The Cataclysm]], Ninki Nanka made peace with the local elves and defended their lands alongside a copper dragon. The dragon was not inherently hostile and regarded protection as an ongoing responsibility. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0000]]

## Campaign History

Fallen [[Penumbra]] reacted with the dragon's bodily fluids, transforming its poisonous nature into the source of regional Mana Sickness. Wounded, terrified, and isolated in a cave within the [[Mana Sea]], Ninki Nanka radiated an increasingly dense magical poison that kept help away.

[[Ceril]] reached the dragon within a protective sphere and removed the curse with Greater Restoration. The poison ceased immediately, ending the continuing source of contagion. Ninki Nanka thanked him and acknowledged the end of its suffering. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0003]]

## Final Status

Ninki Nanka abandoned the hoard and left the cave to protect its people. Its precise destination and the identity of those people were not established. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0003]]
