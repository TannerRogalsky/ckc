An ancient green dragon who defended the elves of [[Windsurf]] alongside a copper dragon before [[The Cataclysm]]. Ninki Nanka is not inherently hostile and regards the protection of those lands as a continuing responsibility.

Fallen [[Penumbra]] reacted with Ninki Nanka's bodily fluids and cursed the dragon into becoming the source of [[Mana Sickness]]. The curse left Ninki Nanka wounded, terrified, and isolated in a cave deep within the [[Mana Sea]], where an ever-thickening poisonous atmosphere prevented anyone from approaching.

In session 033, [[Ceril]] reached Ninki Nanka inside a protective sphere and removed the curse with Greater Restoration. The poison ceased immediately, ending the continuing source of Mana Sickness. Ninki Nanka thanked Ceril, abandoned the hoard, and departed to protect the dragon's people.
