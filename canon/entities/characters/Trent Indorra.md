A sailor and mage from [[Southport]] who briefly joined [[The Opal]]'s crew alongside his wife [[Ebbie Indorra]].

Trent Indorra and Ebbie Indorra left Southport while [[Mana Sickness]] threatened their twelve children. [[Kerben]] recruited them aboard The Opal, where Trent Indorra served capably as master gunner after previous experience on several ships, including a [[Broyish Empire]] vessel.

The couple later disembarked near Southport to reunite with their family and help treat the plague. Trent Indorra transferred his master gunner duties to [[Ulrich Fjoller]] before leaving.
