# Trent Indorra

Trent Indorra is a sailor and mage from [[Southport]] who briefly served as master gunner aboard [[The Opal]]. He and his wife, [[Ebbie Indorra]], returned to their family after seeking help for their children's [[Mana Sickness]].

## Relationships

- [[Ebbie Indorra]] — His wife and companion in the search for medicine for their twelve children.
- [[Lodestar]] — His wife's sister, later reunited with the family in Southport.
- [[Kerben]] — Recruited the couple and appointed Trent Indorra master gunner during his acting command.
- [[Ulrich Fjoller]] — Trent Indorra proposed teaching him the gunner's duties before leaving; that offer did not establish a completed appointment.

## Abilities

He was a mage with experience aboard several ships, including a [[Broyish Empire]] vessel. His knowledge encompassed rigging, repairs, and shipboard work, although modern cannons were newer to him.

## Campaign History

Trent Indorra and Ebbie Indorra left Southport while illness threatened their children. After the party rescued them near Broy, Kerben recruited them aboard The Opal. Trent Indorra served as master gunner while Ebbie Indorra provided medical care. [[canon/sessions/session_023]] [[canon/sessions/session_025#Chunk 0000]]

The couple disembarked with the freed priests near the route to Southport to reunite with their family and treat the plague. Before leaving, he offered to train Ulrich Fjoller in the gunner's work. The family was later found together in Southport, their children lively enough to run and play. [[canon/sessions/session_025#Chunk 0000]] [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0000]]

## Final Status

Trent Indorra was last established in Southport with Ebbie Indorra, their children, and Lodestar. The epilogue confirmed the community's full recovery from the plague. [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002]]
