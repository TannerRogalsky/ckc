A former vizier of [[Emperor Shen]] from [[Broy]], now allied with the party after enabling [[Starfall]]'s destruction. Appeared on the deck of [[The Opal]] through powerful illusion magic, not projection. Knew of [[Heaven's Bulb]] and initially sought to recruit [[Red Caesar]] to the Empire, claiming the party members were a liability to him. Knew [[Master Lee]] personally before the cataclysm. Warned that the Empire also seeks [[Penumbra]]. Asked Red Caesar to keep their meeting secret.

In session 014, she appeared again on [[The Opal]]'s deck as the party sailed from [[Continental Stark]]. She distinguished the Empire's military effort to map Stark from her personal surveillance of [[Red Caesar]]: "I am keeping an eye on individuals of great importance who will rise to great power. That includes yourself." She warned him about [[Obould]]: "You cannot be allowed to die. Your captain, if left unchecked, will lead you into great disaster. I am certain. It is my sole task, moving forward, to make sure that you can survive." She offered Red Caesar a scroll to restrict her meddling, but Red Caesar declined, saying his trust was bond enough. She departed via Teleport Circle, saying she won't appear again until the party arrives in the capital.

In session 021, [[Vokenar]] scryed on [[Obould]] and found Vizier Jade leading his capture deep in the mountains of the [[Broyish Empire]]. She appeared in a long flowing dress with black hair, commanding armed humans in white and gold with rifles, muskets, and cutlasses. Obould was in manacles, being led up stone stairs through angular archways far inland. Vizier Jade cast See Invisibility to search for the scrying sensor, then cast a dispelling spell that ended Vokenar's scrying. She is apparently high-ranking in the Empire.

In session 022, Vizier Jade appeared via illusion magic in [[Lady Jacinthe]]'s private room aboard [[The White Drake]]. She confirmed [[Obould]] was safe but living, and gave Jacinthe a one-month ultimatum: provide the Empire with Penumbra or information leading to it, or Obould's safety could not be guaranteed. Every piece of useful intelligence might extend the deadline. She told Jacinthe she was "not the first" to make such a plea and "won't be the last." Vizier Jade departed of her own volition using her own magics.

In session 023, the party entered the imperial palace and was granted audience with Vizier Jade in her chamber. Vizier Jade demonstrated powerful mind-reading magic, reading the party's thoughts directly and catching [[Red Caesar]]'s attempted deception about penumbra synthesis. She demanded fifteen pieces of penumbra in exchange for [[Obould]]'s freedom — the same deal she made with [[The League of New Stark]]. She brought Obould before the party encased in a resilient sphere, alive but humiliated; she cast a spell forcing him to dance in place as entertainment before ending the effect. Vizier Jade revealed that [[The League of New Stark]] had been giving her penumbra, extending Obould's stay of execution to three months. She also revealed that the [[Broyish Empire]] knows [[The League of New Stark]] possesses at least fifteen pieces of penumbra. Vizier Jade is willing to accept larger penumbra for longer extensions. She dismissed the party and allowed them to depart on their own terms, conserving energy and keeping them alive as a resource.

## Session 030

Vizier Jade entered [[Red Caesar]]'s dream alongside [[Emperor Shen]], using his memories as a search space while the party rested on [[Hole on Wheels]]. She recognized that Red Caesar was consciously present inside the memory and warned Emperor Shen to be careful with what they said and did. Vizier Jade's magic allowed the Empire to probe Red Caesar's past and more recent memories after the party's actions opened access to [[Penumbra]] inside [[The Carrot Cake]].

## Session 031

Her lost servant [[The Tyrant]] was found in [[Jack Harvey]]'s underwater vault, and [[Jade's Compass]] was recovered nearby. The Tyrant's presence confirms that Vizier Jade's earlier search reached the vault but failed to retrieve the [[Penumbra]].

At [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]], Vizier Jade arrived with [[Emperor Shen]] and the caged [[Obould]] to demand the Penumbra. After [[Domyx]] swapped himself into Obould's cage and [[Emperor Shen]] departed with him, Vizier Jade remained to face [[Vokenar]]. She tried to banish Vokenar to [[Arkadia]], then admitted she had once fought for the world but had grown tired of watching divine wars and mortal suffering repeat. She killed Vokenar with a word of death and departed with his body.

## Session 037

At [[Axis Mundi]], Vizier Jade encouraged [[Emperor Shen]] to demonstrate his power to [[Domyx]], buying time for [[Red Caesar]] to establish private telepathic communication. Red Caesar proposed that they build a better [[Broyish Empire]] together. She agreed with his hope for Broy and warned him against repeating Emperor Shen's mistakes.

She asked Red Caesar to restrain her so that Emperor Shen would load [[Starfall]]'s final ammunition himself. She accepted the spell deliberately, and Emperor Shen petrified her for apparent failure. The final ammunition proved to be the [[Antumbra]], deliberately kept until this moment, and destroyed Starfall when he loaded it.

[[Vokenar]] restored her from petrification after Emperor Shen's mortal defeat. Her betrayal cost her the magic and abilities he had held under his control. She warned that his death would bring back the [[Vanir]], but her attempt to cast Dimension Door failed. Her permanent Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum continued to prevent both magical entry and escape. She remained sheltered nearby when the last Vanir, likely [[Domyx I]], manifested.
