# Vizier Jade

Vizier Jade is the former vizier of [[Emperor Shen]] and an architect of [[Broy]]'s reconstruction with [[Red Caesar]]. Once the party's imperial adversary, she enabled [[Starfall]]'s destruction, lost her former powers, and ultimately accepted imprisonment under the laws they established.

## Identity and Background

She appeared as a black-haired woman in a long flowing dress, commanding imperial soldiers in white and gold. She knew [[Heaven's Bulb]] and [[Master Lee]] from the old world. [[Witness]] identified her as his creator and remembered her wish for peace, repeatedly interrupted by wars that drew her back into work suited to her talents.

## Personality and Motivations

Vizier Jade sought people she expected to become important, especially Red Caesar, whose survival she made a personal concern. Her protection was entangled with surveillance, manipulation, and recruitment into the Empire. She kept useful opponents alive while exploiting their loyalties.

She told [[Vokenar]] that she had once fought for the world but grown exhausted by divine wars and mortal suffering. She accused the gods of treating mortals as toys and wanted the cycle ended. Her later choice at Axis Mundi rejected Emperor Shen's course while preserving the hope of building a better Broy. She warned Red Caesar that the emperor had once held a similar dream and that he must avoid the same turn toward destructive power.

## Relationships

- [[Emperor Shen]] — Her former ruler, who controlled powers on which she depended. She served his campaign against the gods, then betrayed him and paid with petrification and the loss of her magic.
- [[Red Caesar]] — An initially favored prospect whom she privately recruited and monitored. His trust deteriorated through her use of his captain as a hostage. Their eventual cooperation destroyed Starfall and produced Broy's new charter, alongside her accountability for past crimes.
- [[Obould]] — Her prisoner of war, whose humiliation and threatened execution became leverage against both the adventurers and [[Lady Jacinthe]].
- [[Lady Jacinthe]] — Coerced into directing imperial attention toward the party and providing Penumbra to prolong Obould's life.
- [[Vokenar]] — Killed by her after challenging her war against the gods. He later restored her from petrification and accepted her assistance against Emperor Shen's final form.
- [[The Tyrant]] — Her undead servant, lost during the search for [[The Karat Cake]]. It feared her control and destruction; the force that killed it was associated with her only by inference.
- [[Master Lee]] — An old acquaintance she described as a good man.
- [[Witness]] — The warforged cartographer she created, who remembered her gratefully despite her involvement in war.

## Abilities

Her former magic supported convincing illusory appearances, teleportation, intrusive mind-reading, divination detection, and the disruption of others' observation. She could compel behavior, seal a prisoner's mouth, attempt banishment, and kill with a word. Her intrusion into Red Caesar's memories made those memories a search space for imperial intelligence.

Emperor Shen sealed away her former powers after her betrayal. Restoration from petrification did not restore them, and her later attempt to escape magically failed. Her knowledge of the emperor's protections remained useful even when her spellcasting was gone.

## Equipment and Resources

Her former imperial office gave her soldiers, prisoners, palace access, and influence over Penumbra shipments. Her permanent Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum protected [[Axis Mundi]] against magical entry and departure. The ward continued enclosing the battlefield after she lost her powers, preventing the companions' immediate withdrawal as well as earlier attempts to enter.

[[Jade's Compass]] was recovered near The Tyrant in Jack Harvey's vault and later transferred to [[Naomi Ue]] as rescue payment. Its presence connected the vault to the earlier search without proving that Vizier Jade had personally visited or carried it there.

## Campaign History

### Recruitment and surveillance

Vizier Jade first approached Red Caesar aboard [[The Opal]] through powerful illusion magic. She offered safe passage east, warned that his companions were liabilities, revealed the Empire's interest in [[Penumbra]], and asked him to keep their meeting secret. [[canon/sessions/session_011]]

On a later visit as the ship left [[Continental Stark]], she distinguished imperial mapping efforts from her personal watch over individuals destined for power. She warned that Obould might lead Red Caesar into disaster and offered a scroll restricting her interference. He declined, saying his trust was sufficient. She departed by teleportation and expected their next meeting in the capital. [[canon/sessions/session_014]]

### Obould's captivity

Vokenar's scrying found her escorting the manacled Obould inland through Broy. She detected the sensor and dispelled it. She then appeared illusorily in Lady Jacinthe's quarters aboard [[The White Drake]], demanding Penumbra or useful information and guaranteeing Obould's safety for only a month. Intelligence and deliveries could buy more time. [[canon/sessions/session_021]] [[canon/sessions/session_022#Chunk 0001]]

In the palace audience, she read the party's thoughts and exposed Red Caesar's attempted bluff about synthesis. She displayed Obould within a protective sphere, compelled him to dance, and silenced his warning before returning him to captivity. Her price for his freedom was fifteen fragments, with larger pieces able to buy longer reprieves. She said the League possessed at least that many, and its deliveries had already extended his survival to roughly three months. She allowed the party to leave, retaining them as a possible resource. [[canon/sessions/session_023#Chunk 0002]]

### The restored sky and a death

Vizier Jade and Emperor Shen intruded into Red Caesar's memories while the party rested aboard [[Hole on Wheels]], searching for newly accessible Penumbra in The Karat Cake. She recognized his conscious presence and cautioned the emperor about what they revealed. [[canon/sessions/session_030#Chunk 0003]]

The Tyrant was found in [[Jack Harvey]]'s underwater vault, showing that her earlier search had reached it without recovering the stock. At [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]], she and the emperor used the caged Obould to demand the material. After [[Domyx]] exchanged places with him and the emperor departed, she remained with Vokenar. Her attempt to banish him failed. Following their argument about hope and divine suffering, she killed him and took his body. [[canon/sessions/session_031#Chunk 0004]]

### Betrayal and accountability

At Axis Mundi, she encouraged Emperor Shen's demonstration to Domyx while Red Caesar established a private telepathic bond. They agreed to work toward a better Broy. She asked Red Caesar to restrain her so the emperor would handle Starfall's final ammunition himself, deliberately accepted the spell, and pretended to resist. The emperor petrified her for the apparent failure. He then loaded the concealed [[Antumbra]], destroying Starfall. [[canon/sessions/session_037#Chunk 0001]]

Vokenar freed her from stone after the emperor's mortal defeat. Bereft of the powers the emperor had controlled, she warned of the returning [[Vanir]]. Her own permanent ward blocked escape, and she remained under cover during the fighting. She then explained how the stelae sustained Emperor Shen's resurrected form and could be permanently destroyed. [[canon/sessions/session_037#Chunk 0002]] [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0000]]

After the final victory, she escaped with the ship's people through Vokenar's sacrifice and attended Obould and Lady Jacinthe's wedding with the leaderless Broyish contingent. Red Caesar proposed an initial public role for her while he helped manage a government constrained by laws and succession safeguards. She agreed to reconstruction while acknowledging that she must answer for her past. [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002]]

## Final Status

Vizier Jade survived without her former magic and helped Red Caesar establish Broy's charter on the erased pages of the [[Demi-Spell]]. She then accepted several years in a Broyish jail for offenses under the new order. The record establishes the political settlement and her imprisonment; the proposed titles and later transfer of leadership were not separately confirmed. Cooperation in rebuilding did not erase responsibility for her earlier crimes. [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002]]
