# Lodestar

Lodestar is a human diviner formerly of [[Heaven's Bulb]] and the sister of [[Ebbie Indorra]]. Her guidance in [[Southport]] helped the party recognize [[Ninki Nanka]] as someone who could be saved.

## Identity and Background

She studied divination at Heaven's Bulb while [[Red Caesar]] studied abjuration there. [[Master Lee]] encouraged her to build a life beyond the school because her power exceeded what the institution could contain. Prophetic stars eventually guided her to Southport and back to her sister's family.

## Relationships

- [[Ebbie Indorra]] — Her sister, with whom she reunited in Southport, among [[Trent Indorra]] and their children.
- [[Red Caesar]] — A former fellow student; their reunion allowed them to discuss life beyond Heaven's Bulb and later reminisce together.
- [[Master Lee]] — Her former teacher, who encouraged her departure and independence.

## Abilities

Lodestar follows prophetic stars and uses divination to offer warnings and assess distant events. Her counsel pointed the party toward preserving restorative magic for an afflicted creature beyond their own group.

## Campaign History

In Southport, Lodestar warned Red Caesar to reserve Greater Restoration for someone outside the party. He shared her advice with the companions, and [[Ceril]] ultimately used the spell to free Ninki Nanka from the Penumbra affliction spreading disease through the region. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0000]]

After their return, Lodestar reported that [[Emperor Shen]] was resting, weakened, and apparently unable to access his former magic. She saw imperial forces retreating and believed there would be a respite until at least the next moon. She did not know why he had weakened. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0003]]

## Final Status

Her last established appearance was in Southport, reunited with the Indorra family and advising the companions after their expedition. The record does not establish her later destination. [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0003]]
