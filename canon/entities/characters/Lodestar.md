A human diviner from [[Heaven's Bulb]] and sister of [[Ebbie Indorra]]. Lodestar studied there alongside [[Red Caesar]], following divination while he studied abjuration. [[Master Lee]] encouraged her to leave Heaven's Bulb and build a life beyond it.

Lodestar followed prophetic stars to [[Southport]], where she reunited with Ebbie Indorra and the Indorra family. She warned the party to preserve Greater Restoration for someone beyond their group, guidance that enabled [[Ceril]] to save [[Ninki Nanka]]. Afterward, she divined that [[Emperor Shen]] had weakened, the eastern imperial forces were retreating, and he would remain inactive until at least the next moon.
