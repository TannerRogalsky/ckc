# Beryzoz Helmscar

Beryzoz Helmscar is a gnomish armorer in [[The Pit]] who supplied distinctive equipment to the party. Repeated ship thefts eventually brought him into a dispute with [[Kerben]], settled through compensation from the revived [[40 Carats]].

## Identity and Background

He removed his own skin and replaced it with metal, giving him a grotesque appearance. His wish to speak with his dead flesh helped explain his interest in magic for speaking with the dead. See [[chunks/session_013/chunk_0000]].

## Abilities

Beryzoz Helmscar forged enchanted armor, including [[Vivarian Zodex]], whose protection extended to Kerben's nearby animal companions and whose rotating chest slot held an egg. He also supplied [[Beryzoz's Teeth]], a ring of his own teeth offering protection from necrotic magic and allowing conversation with corpses through teeth that entered their mouths.

## Equipment and Resources

- [[The Pit]] — His armor business and the site of the party's purchases.
- [[Beryzoz's Teeth]] — Formerly his own teeth, sold to [[Red Caesar]]. A replacement set was later removed for a proposed sale; that sale was not established.

He owned ships but repeatedly lost them to thieves. Captaining or stealing one did not make the party owners of his remaining vessels.

## Campaign History

The party's earlier dealings with him concerned visas: he had last issued them to the [[Gheister]], and [[Redrick Wenn]]'s dwarvish visa was stolen at The Pit. See [[session_009]].

On a later visit, he sold Vivarian Zodex to Kerben and Beryzoz's Teeth to Red Caesar. He was already lamenting further lost boats and did not yet know Kerben had taken one. See [[session_013#Chunk 0000]].

When Red Caesar returned, Beryzoz Helmscar asked whether his old teeth had experienced worthy adventures. Pleased by the answer, he removed his replacement teeth after Red Caesar proposed buying another set once he had the money. See [[session_034#Chunk 0002]].

## Final Status

In the epilogue, costly divinations identified Kerben among many people who had stolen his ships. Beryzoz Helmscar confronted him, and they settled the grievance with a season pass to the revived 40 Carats. See [[session_038#Chunk 0002]].
