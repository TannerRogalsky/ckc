# Boston Golf

Boston Golf was a figure in the old acting company associated with [[The Carrot Cake]]. A fortune-teller automaton portraying him survived in the ruined [[Magic Hat]] and was recovered as a family heirloom.

## Campaign History

The party found the broken machine containing his likeness: a porcelain-skinned figure with onyx eyes, citrine and ruby ornamentation, and an imitation turban. Its label identified Boston Golf. [[Red Caesar]] noticed its resemblance to [[Rochella Golf]], suggesting a family connection before the relationship was established. The party collected the figurine to show her. See [[session_029#Chunk 0000]].

The party later sold the automaton to Rochella Golf in [[Southport]], where it was identified as her grandfather's family heirloom. See [[session_033#Chunk 0000]].

## Final Status

The recovered likeness remained with Rochella Golf. Boston Golf's own later life and fate were not established by the automaton's survival.
