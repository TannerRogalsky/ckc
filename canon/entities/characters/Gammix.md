# Gammix

Gammix is a dwarf fighter of the [[Steelfend Clan]] who became a temporary ally after [[Red Caesar]] broke the magic controlling his clan. He fought with a heavy crossbow alongside his brother [[Tammix]].

## Campaign History

After initially fighting the party under [[Morel Chainsunder]]'s compulsion, Gammix and Tammix offered to help defeat him. Red Caesar dispelled observation and coercive magic affecting the brothers and their wider clan. Gammix survived a boiling-oil ambush with [[Ceril]]'s help and continued fighting beside the party. See [[session_011#Chunk 0002]].

## Final Status

After the atrium battle, the brothers led the party to their clan's household and chose to remain to protect their family. Their earlier statement that they had no family left was not explained. This departure preceded the later confrontation with Morel Chainsunder, rather than following the city's final liberation. No later individual fate was established. See [[session_011#Chunk 0003]].
