# Domyx I

Domyx I is an ancient titan god of the [[Vanir]] and ancestor of [[Domyx]], contacted by [[Red Caesar]] in the [[Gray Wastes]]. He remains distinct from [[Emperor Shen]] and the emperor's resurrected final form.

## Identity and Background

He appeared as a mountain-sized, cross-legged figure in orange robes. Domyx understood his presence as a surviving god or divine spark, despite the destruction of the ancestral avatars in the old war against the [[Aesir]].

## Personality and Motivations

Domyx I said the titans had lost to [[Aeris]], [[Sigil]], and [[Crone]] and were waiting for the world's cycle to begin again. He claimed victory in earlier worlds and vowed the titans would rule after the next renewal. He accused the goddesses of controlling mortal choices while failing to understand the suffering they caused. These were his claims about [[Genesis Mundi]] and divine rule, not independently established truths.

## Campaign History

Red Caesar used Contact Other Plane while seeking directions to [[Axis Mundi]]. He initially mistook Domyx I for Emperor Shen, then compared the emperor to a diminished imitation. Domyx I did not recognize the emperor's name and dismissed would-be copies of himself.

As Domyx I spoke of renewal, Red Caesar felt drawn to reveal the concealed [[Demi-Spell]]. He endured the psychic pressure instead, and [[Vokenar]] restored him from the incapacitating trance. The encounter ended without directions or disclosure of the spell. See [[session_036#Chunk 0000]] and [[session_036#Chunk 0001]].

## Final Status

His later fate was not given. Entropie's final wish resurrected Emperor Shen's body as the last Vanir; the destruction of that opponent did not establish Domyx I's destruction. See [[session_038#Chunk 0000]].
