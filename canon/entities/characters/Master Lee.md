# Master Lee

Master Lee is a teacher at [[Heaven's Bulb]] who shaped [[Red Caesar]]'s magical education and departure into the wider world. His later appearances in memories do not establish his present condition or whereabouts.

## Relationships

- [[Red Caesar]] — His former pupil, whom he taught both magic and physical discipline. He challenged Red Caesar to consider the cost of understanding the world and ultimately expressed pride in his readiness to leave.
- [[Lodestar]] — A former divination student whom he encouraged to seek a life beyond Heaven's Bulb because her power exceeded what the school could contain.
- [[Vizier Jade]] — Knew him personally and described him as a good man.
- [[Qian Hu]] — Claimed to have known him while living under an earlier identity; the merchant's extraordinary account remains attributed to him.

## Equipment and Resources

- [[Para and Bellum]] — Two copper blades he gave Red Caesar as the apprentice prepared to face life outside Heaven's Bulb.

## Campaign History

Master Lee warned Red Caesar of the dangers beyond Heaven's Bulb and gave him Para and Bellum. A later recollection showed their conversation before Red Caesar left: the pupil wanted to understand how magic, people, and society worked, even if the answers proved painful. Master Lee said he had taught him all he could and expressed his pride. [[canon/sessions/session_002]] [[canon/sessions/session_025#Chunk 0001]]

A magical intrusion into Red Caesar's recurring memories placed Master Lee and the younger apprentice before [[Emperor Shen]] and Vizier Jade. Red Caesar intervened when the emperor threatened the figures. After the intruders departed, Master Lee asked the older Red Caesar to explain what had happened. This encounter occurred within an altered memory, rather than an ordinary reunion. [[canon/sessions/session_030#Chunk 0003]]

The texts Master Lee had required Red Caesar to study later helped him recognize the Gray Wastes and their connection to [[Genesis Mundi]]. [[canon/sessions/session_036#Chunk 0001]]

## Final Status

Master Lee's actual later status is unknown. Reports from former pupils and acquaintances, and Red Caesar's encounters with him in memories, preserve his influence without establishing his survival or fate.
