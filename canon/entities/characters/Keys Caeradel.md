# Keys Caeradel

Keys Caeradel is a tiefling researcher who completed the [[Demi-Spell]] at [[The Academy]], then entrusted it to the party rather than decide the world's future alone. After its erasure, he sought a purpose beyond settled elven society, with no confirmed later destination.

## Identity and Background

He is the son of [[Feronia Caeradel]], a drow of former House Caeradel, and the [[Rakshasa]], her former husband in another world and form. Keys Caeradel spent years visiting his father in a cave without feeling that he truly knew him. He and [[Otto]] had once traveled on a dragon in search of his mother. See [[session_014#Chunk 0003]] and [[session_014#Chunk 0004]].

## Personality and Motivations

He rejected godhood and undeserved power, describing himself as a scientist trying to save the world. His original willingness to shape its renewal rested on the judgment that a new cycle could do more good than preserving the suffering of the present. He allowed some chaos in that future, unlike [[Boril Erendel]]'s attempt at total control.

The moon's destruction and [[Illidrielle Gandara]]'s death intensified his wish to cast the spell even after the sky was repaired. He ultimately accepted that neither grief nor expertise entitled him to choose alone what every life would become.

## Relationships

- [[Feronia Caeradel]] — Mother and a principal authority for the Demi-Spell project at [[House Kiirnodel]]. He intended to visit her after relinquishing the spell and brought his father's bound soul into her custody.
- [[Rakshasa]] — Father whom he sought to bring to the Academy. Their reunion began with magical confinement, then became voluntary containment of the father's soul in a wearable gem.
- [[Illidrielle Gandara]] — Longtime friend and collaborator. Her death weighed on him, while her message from Arkadia encouraged him to move forward rather than undo the world for her return.
- [[Red Caesar]] — Research partner in Penumbra synthesis and [[Antumbra]], despite their opposing views on forced renewal. Red Caesar ultimately retained and erased his completed spell.
- [[Ceril]] — Recognized him as a Penumbra collector from [[Castle Kaedon]], negotiated the initial reprieve, and later conveyed Illidrielle Gandara's wishes and the gods' assurance that the moon could heal.

## Abilities

Keys Caeradel combined spell writing with extensive daily divinations, reviews, and records to guide the Demi-Spell's development. He understood [[Akasha]] containment and assisted Red Caesar's synthesis of [[Penumbra]]. He destabilized a separate sample for Antumbra while Illidrielle Gandara concealed its alteration. Magic Jar let him bind his father's soul into a gem at the Rakshasa's request.

## Equipment and Resources

- [[The Academy]] — Former research base, providing scribes, laboratory access, and Akasha collection for the Demi-Spell and synthesis experiments.
- [[Demi-Spell]] — Completed and sufficiently powered work surrendered intact to the party. It was later erased rather than cast.
- [[Rakshasa]] — His father's soul was briefly carried in an amulet around Keys Caeradel's neck, then transferred to Feronia Caeradel, who wore the crystal at campaign end.

## Campaign History

At the Academy, Keys Caeradel and Illidrielle Gandara were recognized as the collectors who had taken Penumbra from Castle Kaedon. He defended his project but accepted a temporary pause in the Order's new collection while retaining its stock. The party promised its collected Penumbra if the sky-repair strategy failed, and received teleportation-circle access. He also asked it to bring his father to him. See [[session_014#Chunk 0003]].

Red Caesar later shared a synthesis method in exchange for laboratory access and an equal division of production. Keys Caeradel helped produce genuine Penumbra, then altered a separate sample into Antumbra to sabotage [[Starfall]]. Illidrielle Gandara concealed the change. The collaboration advanced both Red Caesar's immediate plan and the Demi-Spell despite their disagreement over renewal. See [[session_025#Chunk 0000]].

Illidrielle Gandara reported in [[Arkadia]] that he had completed the spell before her death and honored the agreement not to cast it. Later, [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] agreed to discourage casting while the party pursued its remaining work. See [[session_032#Chunk 0000]] and [[session_034#Chunk 0002]].

After Boril Erendel's defeat, Keys Caeradel reunited with the Rakshasa but held him in a force cage. He argued that repairing the sky had not undone death, suffering, or the shattered moon. The party answered with the value of lives formed since [[The Cataclysm]], the moon's capacity to heal, and Illidrielle Gandara's wish that he find a life beyond the spell.

Keys Caeradel conceded that the next world's shape should not be his sole decision. At his father's suggestion, he gave the completed spell to the companions as a choice they could preserve or reject. The Rakshasa requested an amulet instead of a traveling cage; Keys Caeradel bound his soul in the gem, wore it, and departed intending to see his mother and seek former companions. See [[session_035#Chunk 0002]].

## Final Status

Keys Caeradel sought a new purpose away from elven society. Wandering, helping [[Southport]], exploring the emerging lands, or undertaking further magic remained possibilities, with no confirmed destination or completed successor to the Demi-Spell. The Rakshasa's soul remained in Feronia Caeradel's crystal. Red Caesar erased the Demi-Spell and used its paper for Broy's charter. See [[session_038#Chunk 0002]].
