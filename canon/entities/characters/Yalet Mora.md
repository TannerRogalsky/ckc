# Yalet Mora

Yalet Mora is a stone-bodied crew member aboard [[The Opal]] and the younger brother of [[Yalet Aurum]]. His long resentment of his brother ended with a sense of victory built on a deception he did not know about.

## Personality and Motivations

He viewed Yalet Aurum as an arrogant bully and wanted compensation for years of humiliation. His plan to grow strong enough to take his brother's golden head gave that resentment a goal, but he eventually accepted what he believed was his brother's approaching death as closure.

## Relationships

- [[Yalet Aurum]] — His older brother, who boasted of making mountains while Yalet Mora could make only small rocks. Yalet Mora left their final encounter believing he had outlasted him, unaware that the apparent decline had been staged.
- [[Red Caesar]] — His drinking companion and ally in the planned confrontation. Red Caesar encouraged him to recognize the potential formed by his own choices and experiences.
- [[The Opal]] — The ship and company he served, including helping keep it afloat during the naval escape.

## Abilities

He could roll in boulder form and produce small rocks. His practical work aboard the ship included manning the bilge pump. He also proposed recruiting a wind-aligned creature to drive the sails when ordinary winds failed.

## Campaign History

Yalet Mora invited Red Caesar to drink in the mess and proposed that they train, grow stronger, and take Yalet Aurum's golden head. Red Caesar agreed there might be something in it for both, and they toasted the plan. [[canon/sessions/session_015#Chunk 0000]]

During the [[Broyish Empire]]'s naval attack, he worked the bilge pump while [[Kerben]] commanded the retreat. [[canon/sessions/session_031#Chunk 0000]]

The party later brought him to confront his brother, apparently dying beneath a moon fragment. They refused to attack a helpless opponent despite Yalet Mora's demands. Yalet Aurum surrendered a golden brain, which went into the ship's hold, and claimed he regretted being a poor brother. Yalet Mora thanked Red Caesar and returned to The Opal satisfied that his older brother would die. Afterward, the companions learned that the moon fragment had strengthened Yalet Aurum and that the scene was staged. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0002]]

## Final Status

Yalet Mora was last established returning to The Opal with a sense that the feud had ended. Both brothers were alive, but he was not told the truth of Yalet Aurum's performance in the recorded scene. [[canon/sessions/session_034#Chunk 0002]]
