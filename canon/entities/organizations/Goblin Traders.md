# Goblin Traders

Goblin Traders is a seagoing goblin trading crew led by [[Gunk Grodley]]. Its mobile shop supplied [[Kerben]] with poisons and a means of calling the traders back without returning to port.

## Purpose and Principles

The crew trades accessories, ammunition, potions, scrolls, and other supplies on the open sea. Its wares include poisons and goods prohibited in the elven lands. Poison crafting and magical ammunition distinguish its services from ordinary provisioning.

## Leadership and Membership

- [[Gunk Grodley]] — Leads the crew and handles general sales on deck.

Gunk Grodley's unnamed wife works in the hold as a poison crafter and dealer, including helping customers refine materials they bring. Another crew member manufactures bullets, arrows, and magical ammunition.

## Holdings and Resources

The crew operates an unnamed trading ship with red sails and black markings. Its workshops and stock travel aboard, allowing it to reach customers at sea.

[[Subpoena Deuces Mercator]] provides a magical summons and a brief message to the traders. The vessel must still travel to meet its caller; the scroll does not transport it instantly.

## Campaign History

The traders approached [[The Opal]] after passing an elven patrol's inspection near [[Continental Stark]]. Kerben visited their ship while his companions were ashore. Gunk Grodley's wife helped him turn recovered wyvern materials into [[Wyvern Poison]] and obtain further poisons.

With the party's approval, Kerben traded a gem from the shared hold for Subpoena Deuces Mercator. It offered future supplies or passage if the companions became stranded. The traders then sailed onward.

## Final Status

The last established encounter is in [[canon/sessions/session_015#Chunk 0000|session 015]], when the crew departed after trading. No later summons, loss of its vessel, or change of command is recorded.
