# The League of New Stark

The League of New Stark is a maritime organization protecting independent kingdoms and communities. Led by [[Lady Jacinthe]] and later jointly with [[Obould]], it opposed imperial expansion and supported the party's work while enduring coercion over [[Penumbra]].

## Purpose and Principles

Lady Jacinthe described the League's mission as protecting human kingdoms and townships without surrendering their independence to a single coercive empire. She supported the party's freedom to establish a base and pursue its investigation within the League's maritime sphere.

## Leadership and Membership

- [[Lady Jacinthe]] — Established leader and principal political contact, whose concern for Obould exposed the League to imperial pressure.
- [[Obould]] — Joined her in leadership after retiring permanently from [[The Opal]]. Their renewed engagement was followed by marriage.
- [[Damien Ouranous]] — Envoy who sought information about [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]] and Penumbra and negotiated protection with the party. He died when the moon was struck; he is a former representative.

The party sought a letter of marque identifying it as members in exceptional standing. That objective and its later cooperation do not alone establish formal League office or ordinary membership.

## Relationships

- [[Broyish Empire]] — Rival whose coercion and attacks threatened the League's protected communities. The rescue of Obould ended the specific hostage pressure, while the old regime's later defeat opened a different political relationship.
- [[Broy]] — Vizier Jade attended Obould and Lady Jacinthe's wedding as its representative. The contact offered an opening for cooperation, without establishing a formal alliance.
- [[The Opal]] — The party's vessel benefited from League protection and shared Obould's personal ties. His former captaincy does not make it League property.

## Holdings and Resources

[[The White Drake]] is the League's principal ship of note and a venue for its leaders' meetings. The [[League Banner]] allows League members to divine a vessel flying it.

Lady Jacinthe described the League's strength as distributed among communities and forces rather than dependent on one central base. Even The White Drake could become unsafe if the Empire openly hunted its leaders.

## Campaign History

### Protection and imperial pressure

Lady Jacinthe warned the party about the Broyish Empire's treatment of people under League protection. The League shot down an imperial airship that had spotted the party's operations near Lyngbakr Lagoon. Damien Ouranous sought information about the emerging island and its stones so the League could assess and defend the area.

Lady Jacinthe agreed to strengthen defenses in central and western waters while the party continued its investigation. Her intelligence about an imperial stone-powered cannon helped clarify why Penumbra had become a political as well as divine concern.

[[Vizier Jade]] used Obould's captivity to force Lady Jacinthe and the League to supply Penumbra or information leading to it. Lady Jacinthe chose to deliver the material to prolong his life. The party concealed [[Antumbra]] among that supply without informing her, intending it to sabotage [[Starfall]].

### Rescue and shared leadership

The companions rescued Obould at the lagoon, ending the leverage of that captivity. When Red Caesar later disclosed the sabotage, Lady Jacinthe learned that her shipment had unknowingly carried Antumbra toward its target.

Obould retired from The Opal and proposed to Lady Jacinthe. She accepted, and they agreed to lead the League together. Their marriage aboard The White Drake brought League associates and guests from Broy, including Vizier Jade, into the same celebration after Emperor Shen's defeat.

## Final Status

The epilogue in [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002|session 038]] establishes Obould and Lady Jacinthe's marriage and shared future leading the League. Contact with Broy suggests a possibility of cooperation, while the record leaves any later treaty, enlarged membership, or revised political structure unconfirmed.
