# Aesir

Aesir is the divine faction of the three sister sky goddesses, [[Aeris]], [[Sigil]], and [[Crone]]. Victors in an ancient struggle with the [[Vanir]], they were the powers whose survival the party defended against the returning titan gods.

## Identity and Background

Before the present heavens were created, the sky goddesses and titan gods fought for the world between its highest and lowest realms. The Aesir won, and the defeated Vanir's physical avatars fell beneath [[Stark]], where their stone remains survived.

## Leadership and Membership

- [[Aeris]] — One of the three sister goddesses.
- [[Sigil]] — One of the three sister goddesses.
- [[Crone]] — One of the three sister goddesses.

Their sisterhood is established; the record does not describe a separate faction hierarchy.

## Relationships

- [[Vanir]] — Ancient rivals whose returning manifestations renewed the divine conflict. [[Dunkelkalt]], associated with consuming the sun and moon, and [[Entropie]], associated with consuming the stars, stood as hostile counterparts to the sisters.
- [[Domyx I]] — An ancestral titan god who accused the goddesses of predetermining mortal choices and ruling without understanding the suffering they caused. His accusations and predictions of further divine wars remain his own account.

## Campaign History

[[Emperor Shen]] named the Aesir and Vanir while explaining [[Domyx]]'s divine ancestry. Domyx later recalled his childhood account of their war when the party encountered the fallen titan avatars beneath Stark.

At [[Axis Mundi]], Emperor Shen's dying call brought back Dunkelkalt and Entropie. They attacked the party as servants of the sisters. Their defeat was followed by Entropie's resurrection of Emperor Shen as the last Vanir, whose final destruction ended the immediate returning threat.

## Final Status

In [[canon/sessions/session_038#Chunk 0002|session 038]], all three sisters welcome [[Vokenar]] back to [[Arkadia]]. Crone is recovering and able to guide the return of its waters, while Aeris supports the renewed growth below. The ending establishes their survival and continued divine activity; Domyx I's predicted later wars are not established outcomes.
