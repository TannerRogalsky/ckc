# Dancing Blades

Dancing Blades was a thieves guild in pre-Cataclysm [[Kaedon]]. Its political representation and survival under the republic illuminate the consequences of [[Alamar]]'s departure from monarchy.

## Identity and Background

The former kingdom had tried to root out the guild. An unnamed guild senator later credited Alamar's intervention with allowing it to survive. Dancing Blades was among the local syndicates represented in the republic's political order.

## Leadership and Membership

- [[Clockwork]] — A guild member whose name appeared on a prisoner list in [[Castle Kaedon]]. The list establishes his earlier imprisonment, without settling his later fate.

The guild also had an unnamed senator who acted as a defense advocate when needed. His willingness to risk himself for a client shows political representation and personal obligation, rather than establishing a complete guild hierarchy.

## Campaign History

The party learned of the guild's place in Kaedon's republic through testimony recovered in Castle Kaedon. A dead noblewoman's account recalled the syndicates involved in the transition from monarchy.

Later, [[Red Caesar]] questioned the guild senator's remains using [[Beryzoz's Teeth]]. The senator explained that he had been visiting a client in a cell as an advocate when the rising waters trapped them. He had not been serving a prison sentence. He confirmed that he had concealed wealth, but supplied no location before the questioning ended.

## Final Status

The guild's last direct account comes from the dead senator in [[canon/sessions/session_020#Chunk 0001|session 020]]. Its pre-Cataclysm activity and the deaths at the castle are established; the record does not settle whether the guild survived elsewhere.
