An entertainment company and traveling troupe founded by [[Jack Harvey]], formerly employing [[Kerben]]. Its legacy includes [[The Carrot Cake]], a distinct amusement park bearing the company's branding. Members and materials survived [[The Cataclysm]].

In session 038, [[Theo Harvey]] gathered former performers from [[Cutlass Cray]] and [[The Garden]] to repay Kerben for saving him. Kerben chose to rebuild and lead the company before eventually departing to explore other worlds. The receding oceans reopened places along the troupe's old routes.
