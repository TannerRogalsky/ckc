# Clan Lapis

Clan Lapis is a titan clan known for seeking precious gems through careful exploration of the mountains. [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] married into it from [[Clan Akathia]], connecting it to [[Domyx]]'s family.

## Purpose and Principles

The clan's work resembles exploration more than industrial mining. Its people enter hills and mountains to find gems while avoiding extensive damage to the terrain, rather than constructing the mines and tunnels associated with dwarven excavation.

## Leadership and Membership

The clan has a king, whose name and individual role are not established. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian's marriage into the clan is historical; her later relationship with [[Ulrich Fjoller]] does not establish the clan's response or her continuing membership.

## Final Status

The clan is last directly described in [[canon/sessions/session_016#Chunk 0000|session 016]], during Lorelai Lapis-Acathian's contact with Domyx. Its later leadership and institutional fate are not recorded.
