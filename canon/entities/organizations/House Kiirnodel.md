# House Kiirnodel

House Kiirnodel is an elven aristocratic house in [[Brimbolyn]] and a principal supporter of [[The Order of Seasons]]' [[Demi-Spell]] project. Its leaders spared [[Ceril]] during his earlier escape and later negotiated with the party over the future of the existing world.

## Identity and Background

The house was active after [[Queen Caeradwyn]]'s death, when the old elven aristocratic order was weakening. [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]], accompanied by [[Feronia Caeradel]], offered Ceril refuge and spoke of growing a better society from the old empire's ashes. Although they had orders to capture him, they allowed him to continue fleeing.

## Purpose and Principles

During the campaign, the house devoted power and political support to the Demi-Spell. Rizolvir Kiirnodel described its creation and eventual casting as his life's work.

The hosts also distinguished communities preserved by institutions from the violence of their rulers. Their warnings about the [[Broyish Empire]] did not deny that many humans depended on it for survival.

## Leadership and Membership

- [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] — Established leader of the house and son of [[Aramil Kiirnodel]], committed to supporting the Order's project.
- [[Feronia Caeradel]] — A drow of former House Caeradel and mother of [[Keys Caeradel]]. She worked from House Kiirnodel and described herself as chiefly responsible for enacting the Demi-Spell, while the Academy's researchers handled its practical development.

## Relationships

- [[The Order of Seasons]] — The house supplied major support for the Demi-Spell. Its leadership, the Order's researchers, and the [[Knights of the Four Seasons]] had connected but distinct roles.
- [[Ceril]] — Rizolvir Kiirnodel and Feronia Caeradel spared him before The Cataclysm. Feronia Caeradel later said she recognized his future and predicted that his soul would survive many worlds unchanged; this remains her account.
- [[Boril Erendel]] — An Order member whose long absence and private work worried the house's leaders. They asked the party to persuade him to return from [[The Garden]].
- [[Broyish Empire]] — An opposing power that, according to Rizolvir Kiirnodel, threatened both the Order's renewal project and the party's aims. His reports of imperial violence and a suspected Penumbra-powered weapon were warnings, rather than firsthand proof of every claim.

## Holdings and Resources

The house's estate in Brimbolyn was covered with pink, purple, and blue flowers. Its teleport circle connected it to the wider magical transport network.

The house granted the party [[Teleport Keys]] for return travel and later provided a map to [[House Erendel]]. Those grants established assistance and access, rather than house ownership of everything the visitors carried.

## Campaign History

The party visited the estate after meeting the Academy's researchers. Ceril recognized the two aristocrats from his escape. Their conversation connected the Demi-Spell to [[Genesis Mundi]] and revealed the house's intelligence that the Empire was collecting [[Penumbra]], probably for a weapon. Rizolvir Kiirnodel said the Empire opposed their attempt to bring about Genesis Mundi.

The hosts requested an investigation of Boril Erendel's work and provided the means to return. When Red Caesar reported Boril Erendel's extreme plans, Rizolvir Kiirnodel promised to send someone to retrieve him. On a later visit, Rizolvir Kiirnodel agreed to stop urging Keys Caeradel to cast the Demi-Spell while the companions confronted Boril Erendel. He supplied the map needed to find him.

After Boril Erendel's defeat, Rizolvir Kiirnodel and Feronia Caeradel received the party at the Academy. Rizolvir Kiirnodel confirmed that he had continued discouraging immediate casting, even though the completed spell and the elves' losses made the decision urgent.

## Final Status

The house's last direct involvement is in [[canon/sessions/session_035#Chunk 0002|session 035]], through its leaders' participation at the Academy. The epilogue later establishes Rizolvir Kiirnodel as king of the elves, after the Demi-Spell is erased and the Academy repurposed. That personal accession does not settle the house's subsequent membership or its formal role in the monarchy.
