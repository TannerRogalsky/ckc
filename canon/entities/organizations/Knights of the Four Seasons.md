# Knights of the Four Seasons

Knights of the Four Seasons is the militia protecting [[The Order of Seasons]] and its magi. The party encountered its admission controls at the rail junction leading into [[Brimbolyn]].

## Identity and Background

Members wear cloaks of different colors bearing the Order's common emblem: a full moon flanked by gibbous, half, and crescent phases, framing it like parentheses.

## Leadership and Membership

- [[Marshal Zem]] — The marshal who represented the militia and supervised the party's admission. The encounter establishes his command role without describing the entire force's hierarchy.

## Holdings and Resources

The knights guard the rail approach to Brimbolyn and inspect arriving travelers and their equipment. Augury helps them judge whether admitting visitors is safe. An enhanced Sanctuary ward protects admitted visitors from attack until they initiate aggression themselves.

## Campaign History

The militia was on heightened alert because of [[Broyish Empire]] activity when the party arrived. Marshal Zem inspected the companions, arranged divinations, and admitted them under the protective ward. He also described the rail network linking the city, noble courts, and outlying communities.

## Final Status

The knights are last directly encountered in [[canon/sessions/session_014#Chunk 0002|session 014]], continuing their protective work for the Order. The later end of the Demi-Spell project does not establish whether the militia dissolved or changed its allegiance.
