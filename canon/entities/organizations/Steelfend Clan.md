# Steelfend Clan

Steelfend Clan is a dwarven household in [[Darvenlast]] that was freed from [[Morel Chainsunder]]'s magical control. Its hospitality gave the party refuge during the struggle underground.

## Leadership and Membership

- [[Gammix]] — Clan member who joined the party's fight against Morel Chainsunder.
- [[Tammix]] — Gammix's brother and fellow ally.

An unnamed matron, who speaks Western, received the party on the household's behalf. The family included women, children, and other brothers. Gammix and Tammix initially said they had no family left, but later reunited with these relatives; the discrepancy was not explained.

## Holdings and Resources

The clan house occupied an entire wing of the deep-world complex. The matron held deep-world coinage, some previously controlled by Morel Chainsunder, which she gifted to the party after liberation.

## Campaign History

Gammix and Tammix had gone underground hoping for a better life. When they offered to help defeat Morel Chainsunder, [[Red Caesar]] removed the observation and punitive enchantments affecting them. The clan had undergone a shared ceremony, allowing his dispelling to reach beyond the two brothers.

After further fighting, the brothers led the party to their family home. The matron confirmed that the entire clan had felt Morel Chainsunder's watch disappear. She particularly thanked Red Caesar, offered hospitality and coinage, and hoped her people could rejoin society above.

## Final Status

The party left the clan's refuge at the beginning of [[canon/sessions/session_012#Chunk 0000|session 012]]. Its liberation and surviving household are established, but its eventual return above and later settlement are not recorded.
