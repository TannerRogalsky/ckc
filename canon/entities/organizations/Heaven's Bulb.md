# Heaven's Bulb

Heaven's Bulb was a human refuge and magical school associated with [[Master Lee]], [[Keeper Rufus]], and [[Red Caesar]]. Its teaching shaped Red Caesar's surviving powers and his desire to understand the world, although its institutional fate remains unsettled.

## Identity and Background

The conclave was active before and during [[The Cataclysm]]. Protective wards held back the surrounding waters, and maintaining its machinery taught Red Caesar how pressure could contain them. Its sheltered passageways and false ceiling remained vivid in his memories.

Red Caesar's recollections showed Master Lee and Keeper Rufus contemplating the dangers beyond the refuge. Master Lee also asked what he hoped to become when he and the institution no longer needed one another.

## Purpose and Principles

The community sought to make its students stronger and better able to inherit the new world. Its surviving members were described as unusually powerful, and members eventually went out into the wider world.

Departure was not uniformly an expulsion. [[Lodestar]] said Master Lee encouraged her to leave because her abilities had outgrown what the school could contain. Red Caesar remembered life inside as participation in a machine, where people were valued for what they produced; his own ambition was to understand magic, people, and society beyond it.

## Leadership and Membership

- [[Master Lee]] — Teacher and guiding figure who counseled students about life outside. His leadership is established, while the record does not settle whether he founded the institution.
- [[Keeper Rufus]] — Associated with Master Lee and the warnings about the world's dangers.
- [[Red Caesar]] — Former student trained in abjuration, whose practical and magical education remained consequential after his departure.
- [[Lodestar]] — Former divination student who followed Master Lee's advice and eventually reunited with her family in [[Southport]].

## Holdings and Resources

The institution's compound combined machinery with powerful protective magic. Its warding runes inspired Red Caesar's later protective abilities, and its teaching included familiarity with divine magic. Those remembered capabilities do not establish the compound's condition at campaign end.

## Campaign History

At the [[Southern Archipelago Castle]], Red Caesar questioned a dead flood refugee who identified Heaven's Bulb through his aspirant earrings. The victim said its people had tried to help but had been too weak. This testimony described a failed rescue, rather than a deliberate refusal to assist. Red Caesar kept that account of his community private.

Later, [[Vizier Jade]] and [[Emperor Shen]] entered a magical recreation of Heaven's Bulb within Red Caesar's dream, searching his memories for newly accessible [[Penumbra]]. He intervened to protect Master Lee and his younger self. The intruders' presence established an attack on his memories, without showing that the physical school had been invaded.

After they left, Red Caesar used True Seeing on his younger self. A remembered crack in the false ceiling helped the younger self identify Mending as the missing component of [[Obvolvo Caelum]]. Their collaboration completed the spell.

Red Caesar subsequently met Lodestar in Southport. Her account of leaving at Master Lee's encouragement gave him another view of the community's scattered former students.

## Final Status

The last direct conversation with a former member occurs in [[canon/sessions/session_033#Chunk 0000|session 033]]. Master Lee's dream appearance is not evidence of his continued physical survival, and the record does not establish the school's later leadership, occupancy, or survival through the ending. Red Caesar's proposed opportunity to continue its legacy was not a confirmed institutional revival.
