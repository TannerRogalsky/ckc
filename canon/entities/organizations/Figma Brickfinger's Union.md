# Figma Brickfinger's Union

Figma Brickfinger's Union is the dwarven organization governing [[The Pit]] from [[The Palace of the Pit]]. It helped the party recover [[Penumbra]] and open the route beneath [[Stark]], while confronting the consequences of the deep-worlders' liberation.

## Identity and Background

The union had fought the duergar inhabitants of [[The Deepworlders Delve]] before reaching an uneasy truce. The underground community's insularity and devotion to Penumbra kept the relationship strained.

Before [[The Cataclysm]], the union prohibited expeditions seeking the underworld. Some dwarves nevertheless defied those orders and descended below [[The Funnel]].

## Leadership and Membership

- [[Figma Brickfinger]] — Chair and established leader of the union, responsible for its political dealings and the work around the Funnel.

Union guards and workers enforced its authority and carried out excavation and stonework. [[Courteous Cam]] helped reopen the Funnel, but that collaboration does not establish a formal union office.

## Relationships

- [[The Deepworlders Delve]] — Former enemies and uneasy neighbors. Their release from the underground curse required both communities to renegotiate access and coexistence.
- [[Red Caesar]] — Helped end the deep-world problem and sought Figma Brickfinger's assistance with Penumbra and the descent toward [[Axis Mundi]]. Figma Brickfinger acknowledged a debt to the party while asking it to avoid worsening local tensions.

## Holdings and Resources

The union operates The Palace of the Pit and commands guards and workers in The Pit. Its excavators and stoneworkers could clear deep passages and reduce the quarry's depleted Penumbra exterior so that the surviving kernel could be transported.

## Campaign History

An injured dwarf fleeing [[Darvinblast]] surrendered in The Pit and was collected by union members. What happened to him afterward was not established.

After the curse broke, deep-world inhabitants emerged above while surface dwarves entered spaces previously closed to them. Figma Brickfinger faced a difficult political transition and asked the party to leave before its presence aggravated the unrest. She nevertheless guarded the quarry and supplied workers to help free its usable Penumbra.

When [[Vokenar]] returned to The Pit, Figma Brickfinger reported that integration remained unsettled but workable. Former deep-world inhabitants were rebuilding their lives, new cooperation opened underground space, and roads, rail tracks, and the palace were expanding. She preferred the smaller disputes to the earlier confrontation. Scouts still reported imperial airships nearby, though she had not seen them herself.

The party later returned to find the Funnel reopened. Figma Brickfinger and Courteous Cam reported that the diggers had reached unfamiliar, spacious air but withdrawn from the dangers beyond. Courteous Cam admitted that the work had required breaking union rules. The opening gave the companions a physical route toward Axis Mundi.

During the descent, the party questioned a dead expedition dog. Its memories placed the earlier forbidden expedition before The Cataclysm, warned of stone creatures, and helped the companions find an eastward route through the caverns.

## Final Status

The union was still active under Figma Brickfinger when the Funnel opened in [[canon/sessions/session_035#Chunk 0002|session 035]]. The dog's historical testimony in [[canon/sessions/session_036#Chunk 0000|session 036]] establishes an earlier prohibition, rather than a later change of leadership. The union's condition after the campaign's final flood is not recorded.
