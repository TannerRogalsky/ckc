# Session 013

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_013/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

The end of [[Darvenlast]]'s curse had revealed a quarry whose enormous [[Penumbra]] fragment had powered the deep-world inhabitants' magic. While the party rested aboard [[The Opal]] and considered how to move it, the effects of liberation spread through [[The Pit]]. Former underground residents emerged above, while surface dwarves entered places previously closed to them. The exchange created unrest that [[Figma Brickfinger]] and representatives of the freed community would have to address.

#### Ceril's escape from the elven lands

A flashback returned to [[Ceril]] about a century earlier, after [[Queen Caeradwyn]]'s death and before [[The Cataclysm]]. The queen's fall broke the curse affecting [[King Maniasis]], [[Alamar]]'s father, and weakened the aristocratic order that had enslaved elves and other peoples. Refugees fled toward the new western republic, hoping for a society without a king or queen. Ceril had no clan name; his given name came from the community that raised him in captivity.

Ceril traveled west through storms and damaged settlements with [[Vanzia Vynnfae]], a high elf paladin and revolutionary, and an exhausted human, [[Gilder Savar]]. Others in their original group had died or separated. Vanzia Vynnfae wanted to meet those who had killed the queen, but also worried that leaving had abandoned her revolutionary duties and other captives. Ceril remained wary of pursuit. The continuing rain and the sun's absence made it difficult to tell how long they had been traveling.

When Vanzia Vynnfae asked what the stars advised, Ceril looked upward and saw thick streaks of light cutting from east to west. She could not see them herself, but recognized them as the phenomenon he had described before. They differed from ordinary shooting stars, seeming to appear along lines from a common eastern origin toward varying points in the west. Ceril had been watching them for some time; they became more frequent before The Cataclysm.

Gilder Savar could no longer keep pace and asked to be left behind rather than continue holding the elves back. Ceril put a hand on his shoulder and used Skywrite to leave a plea for help above him. Vanzia Vynnfae pointed out that enemies might see it as readily as allies, but Ceril answered that they had nothing more to give him and someone else might be able to help. They continued west, leaving his immediate fate uncertain.

A high elf aristocrat then called after them, accompanied by a drow. He offered refuge among elves who wanted to rebuild their society after the queen's fall. Ceril rejected the offer, judging the old civilization corrupt at its root. The drow reminded the aristocrat that they had orders to capture him, but the high elf instead warned Ceril to run faster because other pursuers would not be so slow. He insisted that [[House Kiirnodel]] could grow something better from the old empire's ashes. Ceril and the aristocrat committed each other's faces to memory before the storm separated them and Ceril fled onward with Vanzia Vynnfae.

#### Domyx's interrupted descent

A second flashback followed [[Domyx]] down from the titan mountains after [[Touching the Sky]]. The descent was supposed to be a solitary, final departure for someone who had proved his worth. Leaving the homeland without that achievement was a grave taboo, so Domyx was astonished when his closest friend, [[Ulrich Fjoller]], emerged from the snow to intercept him.

Ulrich Fjoller had found evidence in the library that contradicted the family's claim that Domyx was the first of [[Clan Akathia]] to touch the sky. Another blood relative had done so earlier, been removed from the records, and, according to Ulrich Fjoller, still lived somewhere in [[Stark]]. He did not know why the history had been suppressed, but believed the shared blood put Domyx in danger. He urged him to find the missing relative.

Domyx recognized the importance of the discovery and the risk his friend had taken. Ulrich Fjoller feared execution if his forbidden descent was discovered, and hurried back up the mountain in the hope of avoiding it. [[Domyx IV]] watched from above, although the scene did not establish whether he saw the return. Domyx continued downward carrying the secret, with no clear lead to pursue yet.

#### Recovering the quarry fragment

Back in the present, [[Obould]] convened the party aboard The Opal. He acknowledged that their knowledge of Penumbra made them best placed to choose the next course, even though he would have been content to sell their goods in [[Cutlass Cray]]. Before sailing, however, the ship needed reinforcement to carry both the enormous fragment and the triceratops. He committed shared resources to a laminated hull and strengthened deck, with work expected to occupy at least a day. He had also recruited three dwarven deckhands.

The party agreed to ask Figma Brickfinger for help with the stone. [[Red Caesar]] wanted both the favor he thought their work had earned and a better understanding of how she governed a functioning society through the upheaval. [[Kerben]] brought the triceratops with ropes, leads, and a yoke, and named it [[Kilosaurus]], drawing on its weight and cow-like appearance.

At the quarry, Figma Brickfinger met them with guards she had posted overnight against theft. She acknowledged owing the party for ending the deep-world problem and admitted that she might need to soften her own attitude after decades of hostility. The diplomatic work ahead remained difficult. She agreed to help move the fragment, but asked the party to examine its magic before her workers began cutting.

Decades of use had drained much of the fragment's divine energy, turning its exterior into ordinary obsidian. [[Vokenar]], remembering his own arrival through a smaller piece of sky, sensed a surviving kernel within it. Detect Magic helped locate that center, and Kerben confirmed that the chipped outer material was mundane black glass. Figma Brickfinger's workers shaved the edges away until a much smaller, star-filled sphere remained. Red Caesar checked the discarded pieces and found no magic worth preserving in them. The contrast between dead stone and living sky raised concern about what other collectors might be doing to Penumbra.

Kilosaurus hauled the remaining core back to The Opal, where the reinforcing work was underway. Figma Brickfinger asked the party to obtain what it needed and leave without worsening the city's tension. She would welcome a later return, but preferred that the dwarves handle the immediate reconciliation themselves.

#### Trade and the next route

At the dwarven market, Kerben visited [[Sigrid Forgewelt]]'s forge and had his musket enchanted as the [[Hilltop Hunter]]. Its distant shots carried glowing green force, and a small patch of vegetation along its top would change with the seasons. He then returned to [[Beryzoz Helmscar]], whose boat the party had previously stolen. The merchant complained of losing another boat without identifying Kerben as responsible. He offered to replace Kerben's vulnerable skin with metal; Kerben chose armor instead.

Using jade, Beryzoz Helmscar made the [[Vivarian Zodex]], a breastplate that protected Kerben and nearby animal companions. It also held Kerben's egg-like object in a rotating socket at the chest. Red Caesar exchanged his amethyst for [[Beryzoz's Teeth]], a protective ring made from the merchant's own teeth. Beryzoz Helmscar had obtained it to try to speak with his discarded flesh after replacing his skin. The ring could protect against necrotic harm and place its teeth in a corpse's mouth to enable Speak with Dead.

Vokenar deferred purchases and offered his ruby to anyone who needed it. Domyx learned that the local smiths did not work the softer elven mithril and kept that material for another opportunity. Ceril likewise did not pursue new armor. His share of the earlier treasure included the holy mask of Ogun, but the local market offered little demand for art.

Outside the underground market, Ceril met [[Courteous Cam]] among the farms around The Pit. The trader was curious about the dwarves' liberation, and Ceril was willing to recount it. Courteous Cam also wanted to grow elven Darkwood for lighter equipment, but worried about the northern climate and shortage of spellcasters. Ceril traded a Plant Growth scroll for dwarven growing knowledge, mixed root-crop seeds, and a Call Lightning scroll. Their exchange connected Ceril's wish to spread plants across the world with the dwarves' own use of natural power, including water mills and redirected lightning.

Having seen the party haul a piece of sky, Courteous Cam offered another lead. A Penumbra fragment lay in the northern hills held by [[Gnoll]]s. He said the gnolls knew it was valuable without knowing how to use it, and warned that recovering it would require a fight. He supplied a map, and Ceril offered a premium for further Penumbra discoveries.

The party planned a circuit around the landmass: investigate the [[Far Helm Clan]] treasure map, recover the gnoll-held fragment, and then deliver its gathered Penumbra to [[Lyngbakr]] near [[Castle Kaedon]]. Vokenar worried about carrying the material into elven territory after the earlier theft, while Ceril argued for securing the northern piece before other collectors took it. Visiting the elves remained a priority after those nearer opportunities.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_013/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### The Far Helm Clan's abandoned home

The party set out on the planned circuit, expecting the overall journey back toward Castle Kaedon to take roughly seventeen days. The reinforced ship retained warmth for the northern waters, and the post-Cataclysm climate was less sharply divided than it had once been. Through their looking glass they could see an unusual glow over the elven coast, suggesting the magic city of [[Brimbolyn]] farther inland. Tall cliffs and terrain apparently shaped by magic made that side of the continent difficult to approach, so they continued toward the mapped dwarven site.

The Far Helm Clan location stood beyond a stretch of shallow water, exposed sand, and coastal rock. The party anchored offshore and walked to a half-built home abandoned decades earlier. Red Caesar dated its weathering to the period when the deep-world movement had risen: the clan appeared to have given up its intended household to join the cult. Much of the treasure was gone, but a sapphire remained, along with silver and copper coins useful for the ship and its crew.

#### Xander MacLenth's request

Vokenar found no active magic in the building. He then used See Invisibility and discovered a human ghost lingering in a back corner, unaware of the party until addressed. The spirit was [[Xander MacLenth]], a pirate who wanted the rising waters of The Cataclysm to carry him into the afterlife. Time had become indistinct to him; he measured his wait through the slow rise of the sea.

Xander MacLenth said that he and his crew had tried to seize the cave from a dwarven family. Even its younger members had fought well enough to kill all the pirates. His surname connected him to [[Pleasance MacLenth]]'s family, although the exact relationship was not established. He had hoped to spend his wealth and retire to drink in Cutlass Cray, rather than leave a great legacy. His father, [[Lenth the Rugged]], had pursued that kind of fame, supposedly taking wives across continents and peoples. Finding that Vokenar had never heard of him, Xander MacLenth mocked his father's failed ambition. He could not even grasp the empty rum flagon beside him for a last drink.

The ghost asked Vokenar to raise the water a little inside the cave. Vokenar tried to bargain for hidden treasure, but Xander MacLenth claimed the dwarves had taken everything and suggested pursuing and killing them instead. Vokenar pretended to have forgotten the spell and walked away, hoping to coax another answer. He did not ultimately withhold help: he created and directed water over the spirit, who welcomed the tide and dissolved into it.

The scene suggested that Gammix and Tammix themselves might have helped defend the family home while younger, but did not confirm that connection. Back aboard, Vokenar told his companions about the ghost and Lenth the Rugged. They gained no further firm knowledge of the legendary pirate and did not commit to a separate investigation of his legacy.

#### The gnolls' ambush

After more travel and rest, the party reached the northern coast and followed Courteous Cam's map into gnoll country. Kerben brought [[Tango]] to scout above them. Ceril recalled that the gnolls had survived the rising waters by occupying higher ground and violently displacing other peoples, and that their packs could carry dangerous poison. He prepared Protection from Poison before they advanced through the high weeds and steep rock formations.

Red Caesar used Locate Creature to sense nearby gnolls. When their positions began to surround the party, he stopped Domyx from walking farther into the ambush. Guided by his warning, Vokenar, Kerben, and Domyx spotted three concealed groups. The party could prepare at a distance rather than enter the tight killing ground the packs had chosen.

Kerben tested the Hilltop Hunter against the ambushers, while Domyx advanced through the rough terrain and drew several attackers toward him. Poisonous bites and bone weapons made that engagement dangerous. Ceril brought moonlight down around Domyx, who could withstand his companion's magic, and assumed his starry form to support him. Vokenar's fire cleared weeds and struck a cluster of enemies, then became a wall that divided the battlefield and opened a route forward.

The pack leaders drove their followers into repeated attacks, while Kerben used the grass for concealment and the others continued attacking from range. The new breastplate helped protect him when the gnolls tried to answer his gunfire. Domyx held one side of the fighting and seized a pack lord to keep it within Ceril's moonlight. The battle remained underway as he prepared to strike the captive foe.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_013/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Breaking the gnolls' encirclement

Domyx killed the grappled pack lord with a punch and moved by cloud magic toward the remaining fight. Another leader forced its way through Vokenar's wall of fire and turned to attack him, but Domyx intercepted and killed it. The flames also consumed arrows that enemies tried to send through the barrier. The gnolls' attempted encirclement had become a struggle to reach the party through difficult ground and hostile magic.

The party finished the pack leaders and then the remaining warriors. Red Caesar divided his missiles between weakened opponents, Ceril's light pursued the last attackers, and Kerben's gunfire ended the battle. Red Caesar checked through Locate Creature and found no other gnolls nearby. The group accepted healing and continued without a rest, allowing Kerben to retain his tracking magic.

#### Wyverns above the Penumbra

Beyond the battlefield, Domyx spotted a large Penumbra fragment tucked against the rocks. Unlike the depleted quarry stone, this piece was already full of stars and appeared untouched. It rested on a pile of gnomish gold in long, wafer-like bars. Before the party could reach it, Vokenar and Red Caesar saw two [[Wyvern]]s diving from the peaks.

The creatures were winged, armless relatives of dragons with dangerous poison in their scorpion-like tails. Kerben knew how to extract that poison and make the rest of the carcass safe to eat; Ceril recognized the sting as the principal danger. Red Caesar learned that their coloring reflected camouflage rather than elemental protection.

Domyx caught one wyvern's wing in his trident and pulled it to the ground, limiting its ability to pass the party and attack freely. Kerben opened fire on the other, drawing on his new weapon and Assassin training. Ceril summoned an aerial heron to support Domyx and attacked in starry form, while Red Caesar used his lightning wand.

The wyverns' attacks nevertheless endangered several companions. Red Caesar extended his ward to absorb a bite aimed at Kerben, then suffered a poisonous sting himself. The grounded creature badly poisoned Domyx, who released it and used cloud magic to rejoin the others in a defensive position. Vokenar spread healing through Aura of Vitality and took wing, while Kerben switched to close combat when the other wyvern came within reach. His attempt to ensnare it failed.

Ceril cured Domyx's poisoning and gave him temporary protection against further venom. The party continued pressing the two creatures together, with the summoned heron assisting. The poison had hurt them, but their healing and mutual protection kept the fight from breaking the group apart.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_013/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### The wyverns fall

The wyvern battle continued at close quarters. When one creature turned to bite Kerben, Domyx caught its neck in his trident and killed it before the attack landed. He then helped bring the remaining wyvern down while Vokenar healed him and Kerben wounded the creature with his melee weapons. Ceril removed Red Caesar's poisoning and continued supporting the fight with his heron.

Red Caesar's Locate Creature briefly detected another gnoll passing nearby without approaching. He deliberately ended that watch to use Vampiric Touch against the wyvern, drawing life force into himself as electrical energy through [[Para and Bellum]]. Domyx again intervened when the creature threatened Kerben, and Vokenar flew above it and drove his sword downward for the finishing blow. His healing aura restored the party afterward.

Courteous Cam's map had accurately led them to the sheltered dead end, even including a rough depiction of a wyvern. With the defenders gone, the party recovered the pure Penumbra and gnomish gold and brought them back to the ship. The wyvern remains also offered meat and material for Kerben's developing poison work.

#### Life aboard The Opal

During the onward voyage, Domyx visited the mess to reconnect with the crew. [[Mobley]] had prepared the evening meal, and Domyx resumed Titans Tarot, the game he had made part of welcoming new crew members. He wanted people to feel comfortable with one another, particularly amid the different personalities aboard.

[[Rochella Golf]] joined him without much interest in the game. She wanted to reach [[Southport]] and see her children after years away, fearing that her family thought her dead. She suspected her husband had moved on, recalling that their relationship had already been deteriorating before she left. Domyx did not press her to join the initiation ritual; he treated the distinction between passenger and crew member as hers to cross if she wished.

[[Yalet Mora]] suggested finding a creature aligned with wind to keep the sails moving through the doldrums. Domyx welcomed the idea as a useful addition to their mixed company, and recruiting such an ally became a prospective objective. [[Rella Kel'Navvi]] was already contributing through small errands and cleaning, using his teleportation to make routine work aboard the ship easier.

As they approached the waters where the party had first found him, Rella Kel'Navvi drew Domyx's attention to smoke and a bonfire at [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]]. Someone appeared to be living there despite the site's earlier abandonment. Domyx told Obould, who diverted the ship toward the lagoon. The party went ashore to investigate, with the captain joining them.

#### The lagoon and Castle Kaedon

Obould hoped to develop the island as a base and initially proposed removing its occupants by piracy if necessary. Vokenar refused to kill innocent people, and Red Caesar urged the captain to stay his blade. The confrontation disappeared when the apparently vulnerable resident stood and greeted them: it was Alamar, accompanied by [[Tuna]].

Alamar had built shelter, water storage, and a small household using objects and books brought from Castle Kaedon. Earlier feedings of Penumbra had apparently enlarged the area through which he could move, and he had spent the weeks since the party's departure exploring and improving the lagoon. He had repurposed the old feeding chute to send stones toward Lyngbakr and opened the cave approach, lighting it with torches. Vokenar praised what he had accomplished.

Life there had been safer than repeated encounters with the castle's undead. Alamar had dealt with occasional fiends and an owlbear, whose eggs had fed him and Tuna for a week. His increased freedom did not mean that the old bond was gone, however. He believed that the powerful magic linking him, Lyngbakr, and the castle's location enabled his survival rather than simply imprisoning him. At the limit of his permitted range he became sleepy, as though his old Sequester were taking hold, and had to return before losing his senses. He suspected that Lyngbakr faced the same limit.

Obould brought the gathered Penumbra from the ship in the larger boat Kerben had stolen. Alamar reported having seen eastern airships searching for Castle Kaedon, which he believed belonged to the [[Broyish Empire]]. Their broad, erratic movements suggested to him that they had failed to locate it; the party did not independently establish that. Vokenar worried that raising the castle farther would make it easier to discover.

They discussed concealing it with Mirage Arcana, but none of them could yet supply that magic. Alamar offered a more immediate precaution: Lyngbakr could descend between visits, keeping the castle beneath the waves until it was time to explore. He warned that [[The League of New Stark]] might already know its position, even if the Broyish Empire did not.

The party sent all its collected Penumbra down the chute. Alamar returned from the depths to report that Lyngbakr had begun breaking it down. Consumption and digestion would take weeks, perhaps a month, after which the turtle should be able to rise farther. The party would need to pursue other business before returning to explore newly exposed parts of the castle.

Discussing the next journey, Red Caesar explained the Order of Seasons' intention to use Penumbra to power the Demi-Spell and reset the world. Alamar spoke of that renewal as [[Genesis Mundi]], expecting it to restart memories and souls as well as reality. He thought it could undo terrible losses but also erase good that had grown since the disaster. He admitted that he did not understand its entire scope and advised respecting the goddesses' opposition. Red Caesar distinguished the Order's commitment from the intentions of elves generally.

Alamar also said that he had not reached the deeper castle, but sensed something powerful near its former throne room. He described it as though a new king had taken the remaining Penumbra into a lair beneath the waves. This was a hunch, not a confirmed occupant or discovery.

#### Developing a second base

Alamar asked whether anyone could remain to keep him company, build, and help defend the lagoon. Concealment alone seemed unlikely to protect a resident who needed food, supplies, and fire. Obould supported making the site a practical second base rather than depending on permanent secrecy.

The party agreed to leave Kilosaurus, Rella Kel'Navvi, and three deckhands. The triceratops could haul stone and help defend the settlement; Vokenar also wanted it to enjoy the open world after its confinement underground and aboard ship. Rella Kel'Navvi volunteered to stay in familiar terrain and serve Alamar until needed again. Tuna welcomed having a dog nearby. The party kept [[Raxxy]] aboard because it still needed its navigator.

Alamar revealed that he could use Wind Wall against airships, though he kept his other abilities close to his chest, irritating Red Caesar. Vokenar provided Continual Flame torches so that the settlement could have light without a smoky open fire. With a gold bar supplied for the materials, he also taught Alamar the spell so that he could extend the lighting himself. Ceril prepared Wind Wall as a precaution for the party's own travels.

With its Penumbra delivered, the party reconsidered the elves, [[The Garden]], and possible visits to the League. Ceril challenged Obould when the captain slipped back into hostile generalizations about elves. They had several plausible destinations but no agreed route, so Vokenar began a consultation with [[Sigil]].

### Chunk 0004

[[chunks/session_013/chunk_0004|Source transcript]]

#### Sigil's guidance for the next voyage

Vokenar laid out his mantle, whose colored gems turned to white diamonds. A brilliant sun rose from them for his eyes alone, and Sigil greeted him warmly. Only he could hear her answers, which he relayed to his companions.

He explained the alternatives before them: recover Penumbra from the elves, visit the druids at The Garden, or try to deal with Broy. Sigil expected a broad circuit through the elven lands, The Garden, and perhaps the League before their return to the lagoon. She advised grounding the choice of direction in the shortest practical path.

Kerben considered the winds and season, with Ceril's weather magic offering another means of judging their course. Going north to the elves first would be quicker, then they could curve south toward The Garden and possibly Cutlass Cray or [[The White Drake]]. An eastern journey would keep them away from Castle Kaedon too long. Favorable winds could bring them to the elven coast in about four days.

Asked where the Order's Penumbra lay, Sigil said it had been moved farther inland, beyond the jungles, to one of the organization's most protected places. Red Caesar then asked whether Alamar was concealing his intentions. Sigil answered that he was an ally and had not lied, but had not told them every truth and would not. The answer supported trusting his cause without resolving the secrets he kept. Vokenar also asked whether their route would bring strong allies for the lagoon; Sigil promised that allies would appear, without identifying them or establishing any recruitment yet.

#### The warning about Domyx's ancestor

For the last question, Domyx asked whether the person who had touched the sky before him might be an enemy. His companions had not previously heard the full reason for that concern. Through Vokenar, Sigil described Domyx's ancestor as their greatest enemy, perhaps their only great enemy. She did not name him. Her light then returned to the mantle, ending the consultation.

Vokenar turned to Domyx and asked what his ancestor had done to the sky. Domyx explained Ulrich Fjoller's warning about the blood relative erased from the clan's records. He still knew little about that person. Among his people, excellence in a chosen pursuit could define a life, and touching the sky represented exceptional climbing and ambition rather than an inherently good or evil act. Domyx speculated that his ancestor might have wanted more than the feat itself, while Vokenar wondered whether he had tried to reach [[Arkadia]]. Neither possibility was established.

The discussion clarified the stakes of the physical act: [[Aeris]] was the sky itself, just as Sigil was the sun and [[Crone]] the moon. Touching the sky meant direct contact with a divine being rather than merely attaining an impressive height. Domyx's own climb had not further broken the world, but the ancestor's ambition now carried an unresolved warning.

#### Farewells at Lyngbakr Lagoon

Alamar returned toward the castle, leaving mysteries he did not fully understand to the party. Their immediate course was still to seek the elves' Penumbra. Red Caesar also intended to look for [[Yalet Aurum]], Yalet Mora's golden-headed brother, and take his head; that remained an intention.

Before departure, Rella Kel'Navvi approached Ceril, sensing his unease about returning to the elven lands. Having lived among the high elves himself, the blink dog suggested that the world after The Cataclysm might offer something different from the society Ceril had escaped. Ceril agreed that it could hardly be worse. Rella Kel'Navvi offered that small hope, said farewell, and blinked away to remain at the lagoon.

Obould prepared the boat for the return to The Opal. Ceril departed with the party, leaving the new residents behind as they set their course toward the central elven continent.

### Summary

- Flashbacks revealed [[Ceril]]'s escape with [[Vanzia Vynnfae]] and [[Gilder Savar]], his sight of strange east-to-west sky-lines, and [[Ulrich Fjoller]]'s warning to [[Domyx]] about an erased sky-touching relative.
- [[Obould]] reinforced [[The Opal]] and recruited three dwarf deckhands. [[Kerben]] named the triceratops [[Kilosaurus]].
- [[Figma Brickfinger]] helped remove the depleted obsidian shell from the Darvenlast [[Penumbra]], and Kilosaurus hauled its surviving core aboard.
- Kerben acquired the [[Hilltop Hunter]] and [[Vivarian Zodex]]. [[Red Caesar]] traded an amethyst for [[Beryzoz's Teeth]]; Ceril traded a Plant Growth scroll for dwarven seeds, growing knowledge, and a Call Lightning scroll.
- The party recovered a sapphire and coins at the [[Far Helm Clan]] home. [[Vokenar]] found [[Xander MacLenth]]'s ghost and released him with created water.
- Following [[Courteous Cam]]'s map, the party defeated gnoll packs and two wyverns, then recovered untouched Penumbra and gnomish gold.
- Domyx renewed the crew's Titans Tarot gatherings, heard [[Rochella Golf]]'s wish to return home, and welcomed [[Yalet Mora]]'s proposal to recruit a wind-aligned companion.
- [[Alamar]] had developed [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]] and reported imperial aerial searches and the continuing magical limits on his movement.
- The party fed all its collected Penumbra to [[Lyngbakr]], then left Kilosaurus, [[Rella Kel'Navvi]], and three deckhands to help build and defend the lagoon. Vokenar taught Alamar Continual Flame.
- [[Sigil]] confirmed Alamar as an ally with undisclosed truths, placed the Order's Penumbra deep in protected territory, and warned that Domyx's unnamed ancestor was their great enemy.
- Ceril sailed with the party toward the elves. Lyngbakr's digestion, the ancestor's identity, and the larger consequences of the Order's plan remained unresolved.

The session turned a liberated city's hidden resource into usable aid for the sky. Figma Brickfinger's cooperation secured the quarry core while leaving the dwarves' political recovery to their own communities. The dead obsidian around that core also showed a cost of treating Penumbra as a general source of power: divine material could be consumed rather than returned to its proper place. The intact northern fragment offered a contrasting example, and both discoveries became part of Lyngbakr's next feeding.

The two flashbacks gave personal stakes to the planned return toward the elves. Ceril's memories included both the cruelty of enslavement and an individual aristocrat who resisted an order to recapture him. His decision to leave Gilder Savar with a plea for help preserved a difficult moment of limited means rather than a simple tale of escape. Domyx's memories showed that his family's public history could be false and that Ulrich Fjoller had risked his life to warn him. Sigil's final answer made the suppressed relative's significance much more alarming without yet supplying his identity or actions.

The party's practical gains supported those larger concerns. New equipment protected Kerben and his companions, the teeth ring opened another route to testimony, and Ceril's trade joined his botanical interests to dwarven methods of using nature. The northern fighting demonstrated how early warning, shared protection, and healing could keep them together against poisonous opponents. Vokenar's care for Xander MacLenth and his objection to killing the lagoon's residents continued his commitment to helping people without requiring them to be useful or harmless in their past lives.

At the lagoon, Alamar's work changed the relationship from a rescue and supply mission into the beginnings of a supported settlement. Leaving companions and builders there made a second base possible, while his mobility, secrecy, and reports of aerial searches left reasons for caution. The party departed with no Penumbra cargo to defend and a wind-favored route toward the Order of Seasons. It had time to use while Lyngbakr digested, but still needed to return to the castle and decide how to answer a world-renewal plan whose costs remained uncertain.
