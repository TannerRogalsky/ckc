# Session 021

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_021/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### The guest rooms and Lady Acelia's belongings

The party resumed the exploration of [[Castle Kaedon]] by taking the left-hand passage from the foyer. [[Kerben]] remained toward the rear because of the wraiths' life drain, while [[Ceril]]'s summoned crab helped scout ahead. The passage opened into a dilapidated guest room with partitioned beds and exits to the north and west.

A recent ceiling collapse connected the room to the upper corridor where the party had fought [[Sunspite]] outside [[Alamar]]'s bedroom. Among the fallen masonry, [[Vokenar]] found Sunspite's severed head, which he had cut off at the end of that earlier battle. Roses had grown over and through it. [[Red Caesar]] considered the possibility of questioning the remains, but did not cast Speak with Dead.

[[Domyx]] investigated the humid adjoining room and found a bath, latrines, and exercise equipment. Steam still rose from water in the bath, while openings overlooking the ocean admitted light from the worsening storm. Kerben recognized that adding salt and resting there could provide natural relief and renewed resilience. The group noted the bath as a place to recover, then continued searching before using it.

Beside a guest bed decorated with painted flowers, Vokenar discovered [[Lady Acelia's Chalice]], an expertly carved oak cup with jade insets and a name scratched in a child's handwriting. Red Caesar identified [[Lady Acelia]] as Alamar's younger sister and the youngest daughter of the Rodgard family, the last child born before [[King Maniasis]] succumbed to madness.

Red Caesar recalled that the royal family had dispersed after the crown was abdicated. Alamar had gone adventuring south, while Lady Acelia was believed to have gone north to begin peace efforts with the dwarves. Other members of the Rodgard family retained a place in the republic through a senator, thought to have come from [[Taylin]]. Red Caesar did not know that senator's name and did not believe the senator had been Lady Acelia. Her fate was uncertain; he considered it likely that [[The Cataclysm]] had claimed her.

The chalice joined the party's recovered treasures. Red Caesar also found gilded puzzle toys among the beds. Their character suggested belongings from a noble childhood, possibly Alamar's, but the party did not establish their owner.

#### Akasha in the presentation hall

Continuing north, the party entered a presentation hall with a raised stage now crowded with barrels and broken wood. Stained-glass windows lit the room in many colors, while rain and hail struck the glass outside. Cracks admitted both a cold breeze and an unusual silvery-white mist. It spread low through the room without greatly obscuring the party's view.

The mist began gathering into spectral human forms. A [[Wraith]] appeared behind Kerben near a small bar, and a wight crawled from the wreckage near the stage. The hall filled with undead capable of draining life, creating a particularly dangerous situation for Kerben in his already weakened condition.

Vokenar filled the hall with Daylight, appearing as an orange glow like a sunrise that grew warmer through the battle. The light hindered the undead, but casting the spell brought an unexpected cost. The mist collected around Vokenar, glowed against him, and drained his vitality as it consumed the magic. He recognized its light as the same [[Akasha]] carried in his blade and associated with the oceans of [[Arkadia]].

This recognition changed the party's approach. Any spell cast within the mist could trigger the drain. Kerben abandoned his intention to add hunting magic to his attacks, unwilling to lose more of his remaining strength. Ceril relied on his star form and its luminous arrows, which he could use without casting another spell. Domyx and the crab spirit nevertheless suffered further draining attacks from the undead.

Red Caesar chose to accept the hazard once in order to remove it for everyone. He used Gust of Wind to create a sweeping vortex that gradually drove the Akasha out of the room. The casting cost him vitality, and he felt the material resisting and counteracting the magically produced wind even as that wind displaced it.

While the mist cleared, Vokenar's divine radiance destroyed most of the undead. Ceril's crab finished a surviving specter, and the party concentrated on the remaining wraith. Kerben destroyed it with gunfire, taking particular care to ensure it was gone. Once Red Caesar's wind had cleared the Akasha, the party could use magic in the hall without suffering the mist's reaction.

The encounter completed a substantial part of their investigation into Akasha. They understood it as a substance of abjuration that canceled magic and elemental matter. The fallen oceans of Arkadia had settled beneath the ordinary sea, forming a denser layer that had destroyed much of the old world's submerged magic. This explained why merely recovering ancient enchanted objects from the seabed would often be insufficient: the material surrounding them had already stripped away what made them magical. Survivors' efforts to bind new enchantments to objects were, in this respect, attempts to recreate what had been lost.

Red Caesar asked how Akasha interacted with [[Penumbra]], since one appeared to cancel power while the other stored it. They had not directly observed the two together. The discussion instead produced a theory: Penumbra might be Akasha in a crystallized form, storing the power the substance absorbed and forming the solid sky between Stark and Arkadia.

Red Caesar proposed collecting and distilling Akasha to manufacture Penumbra, which could reduce their dependence on finding scattered fragments. The party adopted that investigation as a new objective. It remained an untested possibility, requiring a supply of Akasha from deep beneath the sea. Red Caesar suggested that the [[Broyish Empire]] might possess submersibles as well as airships, but they had no evidence that such vessels existed.

#### Refugees, preserved rooms, and magical locks

The party searched the hall before taking a rest. Its remaining wine was spoiled, and much of its former wealth had been removed. Vokenar interpreted the arrangement of goods on the raised stage and the marks left on tables as evidence that common people had sheltered there after the nobles fled. They appeared to have used the highest available surfaces to stay above the rising water. Their refuge might have lasted days or longer, while the prisoners in the neighboring dungeon remained locked away.

Beyond the northern door was a bedroom opening onto a storm-lashed patio. Curtains and thick glass afforded some shelter, and this room had survived better than other parts of the castle through its patchwork of protective magic. Red Caesar urged the party to spread out while searching, mindful of the Nalfeshne's previous ambushes.

Ceril found blank stationery and a bottle of perfume in a bedside drawer. Red Caesar identified the bottle as [[Deception by Lenth]], a magical preparation that temporarily enhanced the wearer's ability to deceive. It had once belonged to [[Lenth the Rugged]], whom the party knew as [[Pleasance MacLenth]]'s father. Domyx took the perfume, although he remarked that deception was not normally his approach.

The bedroom's eastern door bore an arcane lock. Red Caesar determined that this defense was temporary and would expire if they waited long enough. He associated it with the [[Nalfeshne]]'s efforts to seclude itself, like the locked northern passage they had already encountered.

The group first inspected the unlocked southern storeroom. Its emptied food barrels and depleted wine racks added to the picture of people consuming the castle's supplies while trying to survive the flood, possibly over weeks. Beneath its floorboards, Red Caesar found hidden mithril bars. Their concealment suggested someone had hoped to use them for trade and escape to elven lands, but the owner's identity and eventual fate were unknown.

The storeroom also held two magical potions in ornate bottles. The [[Potion of Eels]] contained tiny black eels, apparently an illusory effect, and could greatly improve the drinker's agility for a time. Kerben took it. The [[Potion of Clairvoyance]] appeared to bend light through its liquid and could allow remote seeing or hearing. The party recovered it as well. Both were associated with Lenth the Rugged's innovations.

A further locked door prompted Red Caesar to recognize the broader defense as Guards and Wards, a spell familiar from [[Heaven's Bulb]]. It could obstruct a region with locks, obscure its passages, and conceal doors behind illusions. The party therefore considered bypassing the doors altogether with Stone Shape or the Passwall magic in Kerben's keyring.

They returned to the room beyond the right-hand corridor, where the Hezru had entered during the preceding session. It was empty, contained several latrines, and had another arcane-locked door. The visible approaches had been explored, leaving the Nalfeshne apparently sealed inside the central rooms. Ceril's summoned crab expired during these investigations.

Red Caesar asked what lay above the inaccessible space. The party established that an upper study stood over it and that the lower chamber was partly buried in the island's stone. This offered a different approach: enter from above, where the demon might not expect them.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_021/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### A passage through the ceiling

The group weighed three approaches: wait for the protective locks to expire, open a guarded doorway, or bypass the defenses through the ceiling. They chose the third, hoping to repay the Nalfeshne's ambushes with one of their own.

First, they rested in the salt bath Kerben had identified. During the recovery they refined the plan to leave the lower rooms, approach from the upper study, and use Kerben's keyring to open a passage through the floor. If the demon was watching the usual entrances, their departure might look like frustration or retreat. They did not know whether it could actually observe them there.

Kerben's Stone Cunning helped locate the chamber below. The party secured ropes so everyone could descend safely when Passwall opened the floor. Vokenar prepared Calm Emotions against the demon's fear, while Red Caesar established Circle of Power to protect the party from hostile magic. Ceril could expose an invisible target with his starlight if necessary. Kerben summoned a new bestial spirit crab for the confrontation; Ceril let him provide this companion rather than summoning another himself.

The keyring opened the passage, and Domyx descended first. Papers and books from the study fell into a proper throne room with stone pillars, benches, stained glass, and a red carpet rising toward a dais. A large chunk of Penumbra rested near the southern wall. Only a few wisps of Akasha remained, allowing the party to use its magic without the earlier hall's draining hazard.

The Nalfeshne was present and caught unprepared. Its boar-like head carried an overgrowth of tusks and horns, and its muscular, furred body was surrounded by smoky blue energy. The party entered around it before it could arrange another ambush or bring a roomful of defenders against them.

#### Defeating the Nalfeshne

Kerben moved to a safer firing position, bringing his crab and then Zulu down the ropes. Ceril's radiant magic marked and pressured the demon, while Domyx confronted it directly. The Nalfeshne was unusually powerful even by Domyx's standards, but he enlarged himself to meet it on more equal terms and kept its attention away from the more vulnerable members of the group.

The demon repeated its intention to return the palace to Hell and demanded to know why the party wanted the stones. Its own explanation for occupying the castle amounted to its business in Hell. It did not reveal a fuller purpose for the Penumbra.

The Nalfeshne teleported within the chamber to threaten different opponents and used the remaining magic it had not spent during its earlier attacks. Red Caesar's attempt to counter its acid magic failed, but his defensive wards limited its effects. Vokenar added a Guardian of Faith to the room, partly to give Kerben another place of protection. The guardian previously left in the corridor had already expended its remaining force on an unidentified intruder during the party's exploration.

The preparation proved decisive when the Nalfeshne released its terrifying blue energy. Calm Emotions protected those close enough to Vokenar from supernatural fear, and Red Caesar's Circle of Power blunted the accompanying psychic assault. Red Caesar described his protection as a labyrinth through which hostile magic had to pass, losing its force before reaching those inside.

Denied the panic it expected, the demon struck Domyx repeatedly. Vokenar's protection helped him withstand the assault, while Ceril's Moonbeam continued wearing down their enemy. The Nalfeshne then turned toward Red Caesar, whose Shield held against its claws. Its shifting position no longer gave it the uncontested escape and surprise it had enjoyed in the corridors.

Kerben continued the attack with his companions. His summoned crab reached the Nalfeshne, clamped its pincers around the demon's neck, and delivered the final blow. The Nalfeshne dissolved in blue smoke.

Vokenar healed the badly battered Domyx, who also used his own healing preparation. The party had destroyed the castle's principal fiendish occupier and could finally attend to the Penumbra they had come to recover.

#### Recovering the stone and feeding Lyngbakr

The Penumbra differed from earlier pieces: several triangular fragments appeared to have fused together into a blocky, almost fractal mass. Vokenar kept watch over it while the others searched, concerned that someone else might try to take it through teleportation.

Red Caesar recovered a black, nonmagical periapt from the Nalfeshne's former position. Its sardonyx gems and unfamiliar workmanship made it a valuable art object from Hell. Domyx removed a ruby and a sapphire from the worn throne's armrests. The party placed these treasures in their shared hold.

Castle Kaedon now felt quiet and inert. Its magical aura and preserving abjurations seemed slightly weaker, and the arcane locks had disappeared. The demon's defeat therefore changed the castle's enchantments as well as ending its immediate threat; it did not simply restore all the old protective magic.

The party chose to deliver the Penumbra promptly, before another claimant could interfere. Near [[Lyngbakr]], the stone weighed almost nothing, making its removal much easier despite its size. Domyx helped carry it out, with Ceril and Vokenar responsible for the recovered fragment.

They boarded the [[Gheister]], crowded into one end because the Penumbra occupied much of the boat. Through the storm they could see the turtle's shadow and the submerged contours of [[Westerness]]. Lyngbakr had drifted while they explored, too gently for them to feel it inside the castle. The [[Hole in the Sky]] remained almost directly overhead, with falling water around its edges explaining why so much Penumbra had collected here.

Over Lyngbakr's head, a rumbling whale-like song awaited their offering. The group pushed the stone overboard, completing its delivery.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_021/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Lyngbakr rises and the sky begins to heal

Starlight inside the falling Penumbra briefly illuminated Lyngbakr's face before the turtle swallowed it. The sea began to pulse around the castle, and the island rose. Domyx struggled to steady the Gheister as the displaced water nearly overturned it.

When the waves settled enough for the party to look up, Lyngbakr was hovering above the sea, carrying Castle Kaedon and part of the surrounding city. Its enormous body dwarfed the familiar islands and settlements. The vortex beneath it drew the longboat back toward the rising land.

Lyngbakr shook itself with enough force to break buildings and collapse one of the castle's turrets. Bones, fiends, and other animated inhabitants were thrown off its back, revealing that more creatures had remained in the wider ruins than the party had encountered. Further fragments of Penumbra fell with them. Lyngbakr immediately lowered its head and swallowed the scattered stones in a great gulp of water. Where the fallen creatures struck the sea, Akasha destroyed them and rose as silver mist.

Restored to greater strength, Lyngbakr contacted Ceril telepathically. It understood that the goddesses had wanted the stones to restore its power, but the sky remained broken and it did not know what to do next. It could find no more Penumbra nearby.

Ceril sought [[Aeris]] through his shield. She appeared as a dark figure clothed in stars, more distant than in their earlier conversations. Instead of speaking freely, she answered by brightening or fading. Ceril received confirmation that their work could repair the sky and that something prevented her from communicating as she normally did. She did not explain that obstruction, and she advised against Ceril and Vokenar intervening personally to help her.

The party asked whether they needed to seek Penumbra beyond the supplies already known among the elves and in Broy. Aeris answered yes. Asked whether they should head farther west, she answered no. The maps had already shown Westerness near the former continent's western edge; her answer directed their search back toward the central lands and the east, without resolving every remaining objective.

While they spoke, Lyngbakr ascended beyond the storm clouds toward the Hole in the Sky. Silver-and-black mist streamed from its body and repaired the wound's edges. For the first time the hole stopped growing and became visibly smaller, reduced by roughly a third. The waterfalls around it diminished as less of Arkadia's ocean poured through.

Lyngbakr returned weakened by the effort. It could tell that the sky was being repaired, but the process had drained the power it had just recovered. The castle and surrounding ruins had yielded all the Penumbra it could gather there, so more fragments would be needed.

The party discussed how the turtle should spend the interval. Vokenar cautioned against leaving it exposed in the air, especially to an empire already capable of attacking the heavens. Red Caesar doubted that an ordinary airship could threaten so vast a creature, but wanted to give Lyngbakr useful work.

At Red Caesar's suggestion, Lyngbakr tested how far it could dive beyond the continental shelf. It returned with news of a second sea beneath the ordinary ocean: a layer of Akasha that felt familiar and comfortable, like its former home. It could reach that layer and agreed to gather some for the party's investigation into making Penumbra.

Vokenar asked what Lyngbakr needed for itself. The turtle wanted to return to Arkadia and observed that it would need to pass through the opening before the sky healed shut. It also wondered whether carrying Penumbra close to the heavens might work for another creature capable of reaching them. That remained a possibility rather than a tested alternative.

Before leaving, Vokenar asked Lyngbakr to watch over Alamar's new settlement. The turtle dived again, carrying the castle beneath the waves. The party also considered using collected Akasha to obscure or magically protect the lagoon, but did not create such a defense. Red Caesar's immediate purpose for the material remained distillation.

#### Obould's disappearance and the crew's resources

The party returned to [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]], where Alamar and [[Raxxy]] hurried to meet them. Alamar had seen the castle rise and break apart and was relieved that the explorers had escaped. The shrinking sky brought welcome news, but Raxxy had a different report.

[[Obould]] was missing. Earlier that day he had taken a longboat, telling the crew he intended to look for the party in the castle. Only afterward had they realized that he was rowing east, in the opposite direction. He had been absent all day, and the ship's spyglasses could no longer find him. [[The Opal]] remained intact, but the captain had left his crew without an explanation. Alamar promised to keep the lagoon safe and left the crew to address its captain's disappearance.

Red Caesar believed Obould had gone toward [[The White Drake]] because of [[Lady Jacinthe]]. He described the captain as a passionate man whose love might have driven him into trouble, and insisted that the crew should go after him. Their long association made Obould their responsibility even if he had chosen to leave. When Vokenar asked who would command the ship, Red Caesar declined the role and deferred to Kerben.

Before settling the journey, they reviewed their resources and outstanding aims. Their available wind magic, including Ceril's Control Winds, could now improve The Opal's sailing without the wind creature they had once considered seeking. The group treated that objective as fulfilled. A bulbous bow, hardened sails, and more cannons still required a port and payment; they had not yet installed those upgrades.

The mangonel recovered from the castle was now mounted aboard The Opal. The party also set aside gold for ship improvements and divided much of the accumulated treasure. Ceril claimed Lady Acelia's Chalice, Domyx took the equestrian manuals, and Red Caesar retained the piece of the stone giant's head. Kerben accepted a sapphire with a possible weapon improvement in mind. Mithril, other gems, fireworks, and art objects were distributed or kept in the hold for later trade.

They discussed where those valuables could be useful. Dwarven craftspeople could work gems into weapons, the elves could use mithril for equipment, and [[Muudeep]] at [[The Garden]] traded art for magical items to wear. [[Cutlass Cray]] offered a wider range of exchanges. None of these prospective purchases had yet happened.

Kerben's growing collection of tickets to [[The Karat Cake]] remained in his bunk. The castle's discoveries had narrowed the search for [[Jack Harvey]]'s old amusement park to a region of the former continent, giving that objective a clearer place on their map.

Other commitments remained open. Domyx still intended to rescue [[Ulrich Fjoller]], imprisoned while [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] secretly carried his child. Ceril's proposed bargain involved finding the fiend threatening the [[Rakshasa]] and using that leverage to influence [[Keys Caeradel]] and the [[Demi-Spell]]. The party had not carried out that plan or gained a new lead on the earlier sky-touching member of [[Clan Akathia]].

The party also reconsidered the streaks Ceril and the castle's dead had seen before The Cataclysm. The name [[Starfall]], recently learned from Lady Jacinthe, offered a possible explanation for a weapon fired from the east that broke the sky. They adopted finding the weapon as an objective, while retaining its responsibility for The Cataclysm as a conjecture. The name and the matching accounts did not yet prove the cause.

#### Scrying into Broy

Ceril challenged the plan to sail straight for The White Drake without first locating Obould. He considered Commune with Nature, but its limits made it unsuitable for finding the captain. Other possible messages or divinations would require preparation. Vokenar then realized that he already had Scrying available.

He established a polished piece of Penumbra on the back of his shield as the spell's reflective focus. Obould had left his characteristic jeweled clothes aboard, providing a further connection to him. Vokenar could feel that the captain was trying to resist observation, but the scrying succeeded.

Obould was far from The White Drake, on an unfamiliar mountainside so deep inland that no ocean was visible. Stone stairs climbed through angular, ornamented archways. He still wore his armor, but his hands were manacled behind his back. Humans dressed in white and gold followed with rifles trained toward him; others carried muskets and cutlasses. A black-haired woman in a long dress led the group.

The distance was greater than Obould could plausibly have traveled in his small boat during the time available. The party did not know how he had reached the mountains so quickly.

Near the end of the observation, the woman cast See Invisibility and searched for the scrying sensor. Once she found it, she dispelled the magic. Vokenar could no longer watch the captain.

Red Caesar recognized the description as [[Vizier Jade]] and identified the mountains as part of the Broyish Empire. He named her to his companions. Obould was thus in imperial custody, rather than merely visiting the League.

Red Caesar recalled the captain's oath to suffer in Lady Jacinthe's place. He believed something had happened to her and that Obould had gone to meet that obligation. The scene established the captain's captivity and resistance to being watched, but did not yet settle Lady Jacinthe's condition or the full reasons for his departure.

#### Loyalty, the divine mission, and a divided route

The revelation produced a sharp disagreement. Ceril questioned whether Obould had offered himself voluntarily and whether he would even want a rescue. He proposed changing ships at The White Drake so he could continue the mission to repair the sky, then withdrew angrily from the discussion.

Red Caesar answered that Kerben and Domyx were family after years of operating together. He did not demand the same obligation from the newer companions, but felt the older crew could not abandon its captain. He wanted to avoid becoming part of a conflict between the League and the Empire, while insisting that Obould's involvement made intervention necessary.

Domyx supported seeking information from The White Drake before deciding what the rescue should mean. If Obould had deliberately taken this course, the party needed to understand his intentions rather than arrive to find him rejecting their help.

Vokenar refused to abandon the companions who had done so much for his and Ceril's quest. He also pointed out that they needed to visit Broy for Penumbra regardless. He favored learning whether Lady Jacinthe had been endangered and whether Obould had been protecting the crew or buying it time. These were possible explanations, not answers established by the scrying.

The urgency of captivity had to be balanced against travel and preparation. The Opal had suffered some damage during Lyngbakr's movement and had accumulated ordinary wear. Vokenar argued for visiting [[The Pit]], the nearest place to obtain ship repairs and upgrades. Better sails could repay some of the detour in faster travel, while strengthening the vessel mattered before approaching an empire with airships and unknown weapons.

They found a way to combine the priorities: some of the party could use teleportation circles to seek information at The White Drake and trade at other established destinations, while the others sailed The Opal to The Pit. They could reunite through a dwarven teleportation circle rather than require every traveler and the ship to make each stop. Red Caesar discussed visits to the elven settlement and market as part of this separate route. The plan remained under discussion as they considered a later stop at The Garden and the journey east.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_021/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### Plans for the sea voyage

The route discussion continued with The Garden as a possible departure point for the voyage toward Broy. Information from The White Drake could still change their priorities, and other objectives might be approached if they lay along the route. No one departed during this closing scene.

Ceril's search for the fiend holding the [[Tome of Satariel]] offered one such possibility. The party now described that keeper as an [[Arcanoloth]] whose territory lay somewhere at sea. If it dominated an area, it might be the most powerful outsider there and therefore detectable through Commune with Nature. Ceril considered using that divination during travel to find a lead. They had not located the keeper or established its identity.

#### Rest, growth, and the captain's succession

The party then took a long rest and reached the next stage of its development. The rest restored the vitality lost to the undead and the Akasha mist, including Kerben's injury from the previous session. His diminished condition had shaped both the exploration and the final fight, but no longer remained at the close.

Domyx still kept the captain's succession arrangement private. Obould had recorded him as the ship's inheritor, but Domyx was uncertain whether the instruction applied to incapacity or only to death. He did not challenge Kerben's ordinary responsibility as first mate to captain the vessel during Obould's absence. The timing of the private arrangement followed by the captain's unexplained departure suggested that Obould might have anticipated leaving, although Domyx did not know this for certain.

The companions also discussed the capabilities accompanying their growth. Domyx gained a divine battle cry that could strengthen nearby allies. Ceril could change the constellation expressed by his luminous form, making his star magic more flexible. Kerben chose further rogue training, improving his ability to avoid a dangerous blow, disengage after striking, and incorporate poisons into his attacks. This gave his interest in developing his own poisons a clearer practical use.

Vokenar gained Divine Intervention, allowing him to call directly for clerical magic beyond his prepared spells. He considered using it to establish a hallowed refuge, raise the dead, or seek Legend Lore about objects and places such as Starfall or The Karat Cake. These were newly available possibilities; he did not perform those interventions during this session.

The party ended rested and stronger, with resources to improve its equipment and ship. Castle Kaedon's expedition had succeeded and the sky had visibly begun to heal, but Obould's captivity now pulled the next journey toward Broy. The companions had remained together through their disagreement, with a plan to divide the immediate travel and gather information before committing to the rescue.

### Summary

- The party recovered Lady Acelia's Chalice, gilded toys, mithril bars, Deception by Lenth, and the Potions of Eels and Clairvoyance. Domyx took the perfume, Kerben the Potion of Eels, and Ceril later claimed the chalice.
- An Akasha-filled hall revealed how the substance drained spellcasters and had stripped magic from much of the drowned old world. The party defeated its undead and began investigating whether Akasha could be distilled into Penumbra.
- After resting in the salt bath, the party used Kerben's keyring to enter the Nalfeshne's throne room through its ceiling. Prepared protection against fear and hostile magic helped them prevail; Kerben's summoned crab killed the demon.
- The party recovered the large fused Penumbra, a Hell-made sardonyx periapt, and gems from the throne. The castle's arcane locks disappeared as its magical aura weakened.
- Feeding Lyngbakr restored enough strength for it to rise and partly repair the Hole in the Sky. The wound stopped growing and shrank by about a third.
- Aeris confirmed the direction of the quest but could communicate only tersely. Lyngbakr agreed to gather Akasha from the second sea beneath the ocean.
- At the lagoon, the party learned Obould had departed secretly. Vokenar's scrying revealed him in imperial manacles, led through Broy's mountains by Vizier Jade.
- The crew debated its obligation to rescue him and planned to combine teleportation for information and trading with a voyage to The Pit for ship repairs and upgrades.
- A final long rest removed the party's life drain and accompanied significant growth in its abilities. Kerben acted as captain, while Domyx kept Obould's succession instruction private.

The expedition's purpose and result finally converged. Clearing Castle Kaedon yielded the stone needed to restore Lyngbakr, and the turtle's ascent demonstrated that gathering Penumbra could actually repair the sky. The result was substantial but incomplete: the great wound remained, Lyngbakr was weakened again, and the party still needed more material. Its growing understanding of Akasha offered a possible new source, while Aeris's changed manner made the divine situation less transparent.

The castle also left the party with a fuller image of the world that had drowned. Noble belongings preserved individual histories, while abandoned rooms and exhausted stores suggested the commoners' attempt to survive after wealthier residents fled. Those discoveries gave the recovered treasures context beyond their trade value.

Obould's disappearance shifted the problem from dungeon exploration to loyalty and politics. Red Caesar's certainty that they owed their captain a rescue met Ceril's concern that the mission and Obould's own choice might be neglected. Vokenar's commitment to the party and Domyx's insistence on learning more helped turn that disagreement into a workable plan. At the close, Obould remained captive, Lady Jacinthe's situation remained unverified, and the crew had not yet begun its divided journey. It had, however, recovered its strength, secured useful resources, and found a route that could address preparation and information together.
