## Session 036

### Chunk 0000

- [[Ceril]] provides a feast with the [[Cornucopia of Constellations]], and [[Otto]] guides the party partway down [[The Funnel]] before turning back.
- [[Kerben]] discovers an old expedition camp and recovers diamonds, a sapphire, a potion of speed, and a potion of silver wind. Further exploration yields more diamonds.
- With [[Red Caesar]]'s [[Beryzoz's Teeth]], Kerben questions a dead dog from an expedition before [[The Cataclysm]]. Its dwarven companions sought the underworld against [[Figma Brickfinger's Union]]'s orders. It warns of stone creatures and directs the party east.
- The party defeats two gem-encrusted fomorians and recovers further gems. [[Domyx]] suffers a warping curse that weakens his body and mind and leaves hollows in his skin.
- As light and gravity weaken, the party reaches enormous stone heads beneath a fiery ceiling. Domyx identifies the fallen [[Vanir]], titan gods defeated by the [[Aesir]] before the creation of the heavens.
- Red Caesar uses the Contact Other Plane page from the [[Tome of Satariel]], supported by Domyx's ancestral song. The contact incapacitates him and brings him before [[Domyx I]] in the [[Gray Wastes]].
- Domyx I claims the titans will rule after another world cycle and condemns the goddesses' control over mortals. Red Caesar feels pressure to reveal the [[Demi-Spell]].

### Chunk 0001

- [[Red Caesar]] endures the psychic pressure rather than reveal the [[Demi-Spell]]. [[Vokenar]] restores him from the trance. He returns without directions, but with evidence that the fallen gods' divine sparks survive their destroyed avatars.
- [[Kerben]] follows the tracks of a colossal burrowing creature. The party defeats the purple worm, whose tunnel opens onto a second sky.
- The party recovers [[Domyx II]]'s gold amphora and diamond-set gold coronet, together with a gold bugle bearing the family's insignia.
- Vokenar crosses the planar boundary first, and the party follows into the [[Gray Wastes]]. Suspended stone islands crumble into a gray desert far below.
- Red Caesar recognizes the lower plane from his encounter with Domyx I and recalls that it is part of the reality recycled by [[Genesis Mundi]].
- [[Axis Mundi]] and [[Starfall]] lie far to the east, approximately beneath [[Broy]]. Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum blocks magical entry at the destination. Red Caesar proposes locating Starfall by tracing its old shots through the lower sky.
- The party plans to board [[The Opal]], store it in Kerben's egg, and have [[Tango]] carry it to the desert. [[Domyx]] braces the deployed ship on a collapsing rock while the others board; [[Raxxy]] rouses the crew.
- The ship slides into the open sky and Kerben's attempt to store it again fails. He summons a roc with his bird feather token to avert the fall.

### Chunk 0002

- The roc returns [[Kerben]] to [[The Opal]] and guides its fall into a glide. Its sails and wind-providing genie carry it safely onto the soft dust, where it continues sailing east.
- [[Domyx]] finds the ship largely undamaged. [[Brim the Bullywog]] reports dust flowing through its bilge.
- Kerben carries [[Ceril]] and [[Red Caesar]] aloft on the roc. Ceril identifies bright scars in the lower sky, and Red Caesar distinguishes likely [[Starfall]] shots from natural features. Their observations refine the ship's eastward course.
- The roc returns the scouts to the ship and departs freely into the [[Gray Wastes]].
- [[Vokenar]] sights a distant tree connecting ground and sky. [[Sigil]] identifies it as [[Galcarina]], the tree of all worlds, whose roots extend from [[Arkadia]] through [[Stark]] into this lower plane.

### Summary

The reopened Funnel leads the party beyond the dwarven workings into a realm where light fades, gravity weakens, and the history of the gods becomes physically visible. An expedition dog's memories help Kerben find the route and identify threats. The fallen stone avatars of the Vanir then connect Domyx's ancestral teachings to the landscape around him. His encounter with warped giants leaves a lingering affliction whose removal is not established during the session.

Red Caesar's attempt to ask a surviving god for directions becomes a confrontation with Domyx I. The titan describes the goddesses as blind rulers who determine mortal choices and claims that another world cycle will restore titan supremacy. These accusations remain his account. Red Caesar refuses to expose the Demi-Spell despite the pressure, and Vokenar brings him back. The spell remains intact and in Red Caesar's custody.

Kerben's tracking succeeds where divination did not: a purple worm's passage leads to a physically traversable boundary beneath Stark. Beyond it, the Gray Wastes reveal a second sky above an immense dust plain. The recovered heirlooms of Domyx II deepen the family's connection to these ancient depths. Axis Mundi is somewhere far east, but the party still needs a route across the lower plane.

Deploying The Opal nearly ends in disaster when its storage magic fails above the abyss. Kerben's summoned roc, the ship's sails, and the crew's wind source turn the fall into a safe descent. The fine dust supports the ship's passage, allowing the voyage to continue. Ceril and Red Caesar resume their shared navigation by reading Starfall's old firing scars overhead. Vokenar's sighting of Galcarina gives them a new landmark, but they have not yet reached the tree, Axis Mundi, or Starfall, and [[Aeris]] remains captive.

### Connections

- The descent follows the excavation requested in session 032 and completed by session 035.
- The Aesir and Vanir expand the divine ancestry [[Emperor Shen]] described to Domyx in session 032.
- Contact Other Plane is one of the three pages recovered when [[Farraday]]'s Tome of Satariel was destroyed in session 034.
- Red Caesar's refusal to reveal the Demi-Spell continues his commitment to safeguard it after [[Keys Caeradel]] relinquished it in session 035.
- The Gray Wastes confirm the lower planar boundary inferred from the [[Chart of the Witness]], while the physical crossing bypasses the need for magical entry at the warded destination.
- Galcarina is the previously unnamed tree Ceril visited among the goddesses in session 032. Its reach across the planes is now established; its exact relationship to Axis Mundi remains unknown.
