# Session 036

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_036/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Beyond the dwarves' workings

Before entering [[The Funnel]], [[Ceril]] used the [[Cornucopia of Constellations]] to provide a sustaining feast. Stars rolled onto the picnic blanket, arranged themselves into constellations of food, and became a meal the companions could eat. Ceril chose additional protection against necrotic and psychic forces for the dangers below. [[Tango]] shared the feast and accompanied [[Kerben]] as the party's flying scout. [[Red Caesar]] also supplied healing potions from his cauldron.

[[Otto]] guided them through the worked sections of the descent. Elevators carried them down the dwarves' shafts before the route gave way to natural chimneys that had to be climbed. Even Otto's resilience had limits: he eventually reached the point beyond which he would not guide them, and the five continued with Tango.

Kerben recognized that they had passed beneath the ocean floor itself. Tall natural caverns opened around them, with shallow brackish water trickling and flowing onward into the depths. [[Domyx]] noticed that it behaved unusually, slipping from his hands with little trace and appearing still even while it moved. Their lights also began to weaken. Torches shone in strange colors close to the party but failed to illuminate the distance as they would above. The changes suggested a different environment ahead, without yet establishing that they had crossed into another plane.

#### An earlier expedition

Kerben's sense of stone revealed a geometric alcove among the natural walls. Its dry camp, protected from the water by a ridge, contained old bones, worn packs, and the remains of a fire. The explorers recovered diamonds, a sapphire, and two surviving potions from among the empty bottles.

Kerben took the potion of speed, intending to use it before a dangerous encounter. Red Caesar took the potion of silver wind. The uncut diamonds suggested that the earlier travelers had mined them below rather than brought all their treasure from the surface. Kerben began watching for similar formations and soon extracted another vein of diamonds from graphite near the walls.

The bones in the camp were too damaged to question, so Red Caesar searched farther for an intact skull. He found the remains of a large dog that had accompanied the expedition. The party combined [[Beryzoz's Teeth]] with Kerben's speaking magic: Red Caesar animated the dead creature's voice, and Kerben understood its answers.

The dog remembered dwarven companions seeking an underworld or promised land against the Union's orders. It had wandered away from camp after hearing something in the night. A large stone creature peeled itself from a wall and killed it. Its memory of a clear blue sky and an intact moon placed the expedition before [[The Cataclysm]].

Its account also supplied a route. The dwarves had explored north and west and planned to continue east, beyond the pillars ahead. An earlier scout's journey, years before theirs, had inspired the expedition. Following that unfinished direction gave the party a useful lead through the branching passages, while the warning told them to watch the walls as well as the path.

#### The gem-encrusted giants

The water led downward, and Ceril noticed that his steps felt lighter. Around the next turn, two enormous forms detached themselves from the walls. Their stone and embedded gems initially suggested elementals, but they were warped giants with flesh beneath the mineral growth. The dog's warning helped Ceril and [[Vokenar]] react before the creatures fully caught the others unprepared.

The giants' clubs and uncanny gazes posed different threats. Vokenar resisted an attempt to drain his strength and clarity, but Domyx suffered a lasting warping curse. Hollows like empty gem sockets appeared across his arms and forehead. His body weakened, and his thoughts became less clear. The change was real rather than a passing illusion, and the encounter gave him an unwelcome image of what a Titan might become after repeated distortions.

Domyx tried to seize the giant and avoid looking at its gaze, but it escaped his grasp. Closing his eyes offered little certainty against a supernatural influence. Red Caesar's own exposure did not leave him with the same lasting affliction.

Ceril summoned a crab to occupy one giant while attacking with starlight, and Vokenar protected the creature and pressed the same foe. Red Caesar called Bigby's Hand from the nearby wall to strike the other. Domyx recovered enough purpose to drive his opponent back into the stone with the Cestus of the Clear Sky. The impacts dislodged gems, including chalcedony and citrine, which he caught and recovered.

The party wore away the giants' stone-covered bodies until the flesh beneath was exposed. Ceril's final arrow killed one, leaving an intact amethyst among its fallen pieces. Domyx then struck the other as it attacked Red Caesar's conjured hand, driving it into the ceiling and killing it.

Red Caesar used the remaining life of Bigby's Hand to break a diamond-bearing graphite slab from above. The companions sorted the fallen material for further treasure, then regrouped. Domyx still bore the hollows and weakening curse. Its duration was uncertain, and no removal was established before they continued.

#### The fallen Vanir

The party chose to proceed carefully rather than rush through the next passages. Gravity weakened enough for everyone to notice, and their lamps reached still less of the dark. Tango flew closer to the companions because the bird could not see far into that darkness.

The tunnel opened into an immense chamber beneath a fiery ceiling. Ash and falling sparks briefly illuminated giant stone heads ahead, then others beyond them. Domyx recalled childhood teachings about the ancient [[Vanir]] and [[Aesir]]. Before the creation of the present heavens, giant gods had fought the sky goddesses over the world between its highest and lowest reaches. The goddesses had won.

Domyx shared the account as the shapes around them gave it a physical presence. These were the fallen bodies used by the defeated gods, sunk beneath the burning upper layers and left as stone. They resembled statues but had not been carved by ordinary builders. The chamber preserved the remains of a divine conflict older than Domyx's known family history.

Its openness also deprived the party of their earlier route. They had emerged from the south, but no obvious passage showed where to go next. They considered divination for guidance through the uncharted space.

#### Contact with Domyx I

Red Caesar chose Contact Other Plane, using the intact page recovered from the [[Tome of Satariel]]. He hoped to reach one of the remaining powers associated with the fallen Titans. The spell carried a danger of overwhelming his mind. Domyx supported the attempt with an ancestral song about the Vanir, offering the encouragement he might otherwise give as a battle cry.

The contact still overcame Red Caesar. The writing vanished from the page as he read it, and his body fell into an incapacitating trance. Vokenar prepared to restore him once the failure became clear to the others.

Within the encounter, Red Caesar emerged from powdery gray dust before a vast orange-robed figure. He initially mistook the being for [[Emperor Shen]], then understood that he was facing an older power: [[Domyx I]].

The Titan warned him away from the [[Gray Wastes]] and spoke of the war the goddesses had won. He claimed that mortals had been confined above so the victors could control them, and that even Red Caesar's apparent choices had been set by the gods. He denied being Emperor Shen, describing lesser aspirants as copies of himself.

Domyx I said that he waited for another world cycle and another war of the gods. He claimed the Titans had prevailed in earlier cycles and intended them to rule the next. As he spoke, Red Caesar felt his hand moving toward the [[Demi-Spell]] concealed in his robes. The scene ended with pressure to expose the scroll rather than the directions he had sought.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_036/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### The spell remains concealed

Red Caesar refused to show Domyx I the Demi-Spell and endured the psychic pain instead. The Titan continued accusing the goddesses of blindness: they believed themselves just, he argued, without seeing what their interventions did to the world. He cast the party's sacrifice as service to their continued rule. Those accusations remained his account of the divine conflict.

Vokenar's restorative magic ended Red Caesar's trance. The wizard returned coughing gray dust and described the immense ancestor he had encountered. Domyx understood the significance of a living divine presence beyond the stone remains. The fallen heads were avatars used in the old battle; the gods' sparks could survive the destruction of those physical bodies in another plane.

The attempt had produced that revelation, but no practical directions. Red Caesar retained the concealed Demi-Spell and recovered with the others' help while the party looked for a physical way forward.

Kerben found one. Even these depths contained traces of life. Smooth grooves in the stone showed where a colossal, footless creature had traveled toward lower ground. Its rounded path suggested a serpent or worm far larger than ordinary surface animals. He led the group along its tracks.

#### The burrower rises

The grooves descended through successive shelves of rock. Kerben's stone sense detected a rumbling beneath the party that grew rapidly closer. He warned the others before the creature broke through, giving them a chance to move and prepare.

Red Caesar hastened Kerben. Ceril placed Moonbeam over the expected opening, while Domyx waited to seize whatever emerged. Kerben climbed to a position above them and prepared the Future bullet from his new ammunition.

A huge purple worm erupted around Domyx, its teeth emerging in a ring much wider than he had anticipated. Ceril's light shone into its mouth, but Domyx could not grapple the immense creature. Kerben's shot drew the worm up toward his elevated position. The clockwork magic inside the bullet pulled much of its body from the burrow, leaving it exposed to the party while still touching the ground.

The worm struck Domyx with its poisoned tail and caught Kerben in its mouth. Kerben braced himself against its inner teeth and prevented it from swallowing him, although he remained trapped there. Red Caesar tried to charm the creature, but it resisted. Domyx's attempt to hold onto its body also failed; its movement and slippery surface made it a poor mount.

Domyx instead battered and twisted the worm while the others attacked. The opening was not enough to free Kerben, who found another answer among his equipment. Using [[The Ascot]], he exchanged places with Tango. The bird could fly immediately clear, and Kerben escaped the mouth without sacrificing his companion.

The worm subsequently caught Domyx, who also prevented it from swallowing him. Ceril's light, Vokenar's magic, and the others' attacks kept weakening it. Domyx finally tightened its knotted body until it died. Its swollen digestive tract burst, spraying acid across the party and into the tunnel it had opened. The companions survived, injured but with the route below now visible.

#### Heirlooms above another sky

Ceril's surviving Moonbeam illuminated the worm's shaft. Its light met a natural glow rising from beneath the rock: a second sky appeared through the opening, where the party had expected only deeper stone.

Treasures the worm had swallowed lay among its broken remains. Domyx recognized his family's insignia on a great gold amphora belonging to [[Domyx II]]. A gold bugle and a diamond-set gold coronet were recovered alongside it. The coronet resembled his father's, though more elaborate. The finds tied his ancestral history to the depths without establishing how each piece had reached the worm.

Vokenar restored the party before the descent. The opening formed a vertical chimney with apparent handholds, but beyond its lower end lay open sky. He climbed first, preserving his wings for a possible fall. A handhold weakened by the worm's acid crumbled in his grasp. His wings caught him, and he flew down and sideways to a hanging island of stone.

He tested other possible handholds and called advice back to his companions. Then his voice abruptly ceased to reach them. Red Caesar and Ceril recognized the threshold: Vokenar had crossed out of Stark into the lower plane, even though the boundary itself was invisible.

Domyx climbed down and along the inverted stone until he reached a connecting pillar. Kerben followed with his climbing ability, while Tango and Ceril flew. Red Caesar jumped through the opening and steered toward a landing with his billowing cape. The lower gravity softened the fall, although it still hurt when he reached the rock.

#### The Gray Wastes

The companions assembled among stone islands suspended from the ceiling by natural pillars and bridges. Lightning illuminated clouds around them. Far below spread an immense gray desert. Pieces of the hanging terrain broke away and shattered into dust when they reached it, continually adding to the waste beneath.

Red Caesar recognized the place from his encounter with Domyx I and from the old texts [[Master Lee]] had made him study. The Gray Wastes were a lower plane associated with the reality recycled by [[Genesis Mundi]]. The possibility that this pocket of existence might become another world's Stark was an interpretation of that cycle, rather than a landscape they had visited in a different age.

The crossing made their destination's position clearer, but did not remove its defenses. The party understood the private sanctum around Axis Mundi and Starfall to prohibit teleportation as well as travel between planes. Entering the Gray Wastes physically had bypassed the first obstacle, leaving a long journey to the protected location.

Starfall was believed to lie far east, roughly beneath [[Broy]], while the party had descended in the northwest. Red Caesar proposed reading its old attacks from below. Shots that had passed through Stark and its heavens should also have scarred this lower sky. Their trajectories might guide the party toward the place from which they had been fired.

Ceril recalled the great tree among the goddesses and an elven image of roots reaching between worlds. Its relation to this lower realm remained to be found. The immediate problem was how to cross the distance safely from their precarious islands.

#### Launching the Opal

Domyx suggested using [[The Opal]], still carried in Kerben's magical egg. The desert's powder might support sailing, but it lay far beneath them. The companions considered other means, including a captured balloon whose present availability was uncertain and a magical gate whose reach was far too short.

They settled on another use of the egg. Kerben would deploy The Opal, let the companions board, and store it again. Tango could then carry the egg down to the desert, where the ship and its passengers could emerge safely.

Finding space to deploy the vessel was harder than the plan assumed. Kerben chose the best available platform, but it sloped toward open sky. The ship began sliding, and its weight cracked the pillars holding the rock above the desert.

Domyx braced the bow with his full strength, giving the others time to board. The crew emerged from confinement disoriented by the new surroundings. [[Raxxy]] demanded an explanation, looked over the side, and realized that there was no sea or solid ground below. She hurried to assemble the crew.

Kerben signaled Domyx to come aboard. As Domyx released the bow and scrambled up the ship, it lurched toward the edge. The chunk ended with the vessel moving and the platform failing beneath it, before the planned storage and descent could be completed.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_036/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### The roc catches the fall

The Opal slid off the rock into open sky while the platform itself broke apart. Kerben leaped after the ship and tried to return it to his egg. The storage magic failed. He and the vessel fell together, and Tango dived after them as the original plan collapsed.

Kerben used his bird feather token. Its dark feather became the first of the immense wings of a roc that formed beneath him. The bird caught him and returned him to The Opal. He then ordered it to guide the falling ship.

The roc could not lift the vessel outright. Instead, it pushed and steered it into an angled descent, aided by the lower gravity. The sails began filling with wind. The awakened crew took their accustomed stations, and one activated the genie that supplied the ship with further wind. Their ordinary work helped turn a fall into a glide.

The descent grew so gradual that they did not notice the precise moment the keel met the ground. Soft powder streamed up on both sides, and The Opal continued forward across the Gray Wastes. The roc withdrew its support and flew alongside, while Tango returned aboard.

Domyx inspected the ship. Despite the danger, it had suffered little damage. [[Brim the Bullywog]] reported dust flowing through the bilge where water normally gathered and continued attending it. The crew had survived the crossing with their ship largely intact and a means of sailing east across the powder.

#### Reading the weapon's old tracks

Kerben took Ceril and Red Caesar aloft on the roc to look for Starfall's traces. He guided the bird while Ceril examined the distant sky. The elf began picking out dark scars, and Red Caesar distinguished the likely weapon strikes from natural features.

The marks did not all follow straight lines. Starfall's shots had curved, making a simple convergence harder to calculate. As the lower sky darkened, its holes grew brighter and resembled constellations. Ceril identified them one by one, and Red Caesar used that pattern to refine the heading.

After several hours, the roc returned the three scouts to The Opal. Their observations allowed successive corrections to the ship's eastward course. The bird then flew away into the lower heavens as a free living creature, no longer under Kerben's control.

#### The tree of all worlds

Vokenar watched ahead from the bow. A thin, distant pillar joined the desert to the sky, with branches and long leaves hanging from it. He recognized the tree Ceril had described.

[[Sigil]]'s voice entered his mind and named [[Gaokerena]], the tree of all worlds. Its roots extended from Arkadia through Stark into the Gray Wastes. The sight linked the tree Ceril had visited among the goddesses to the lower plane they had now entered.

The party rested aboard after the hours of sailing. They had a course, a new landmark, and a ship capable of traversing the waste, but had not yet reached Gaokerena, Axis Mundi, or Starfall. [[Aeris]] remained to be freed, and the Demi-Spell remained intact with Red Caesar.

### Summary

- Ceril provided a protected feast, and Otto guided the party partway down the Funnel before they continued beneath the ocean floor.
- Kerben recovered gems and potions from an old camp. With Red Caesar's speaking magic, he questioned an expedition dog whose memories supplied an eastward route and warning.
- The party defeated two gem-encrusted giants. Domyx suffered a lasting warping curse that weakened him and marked his skin.
- Domyx recognized the stone avatars of defeated Vanir. Red Caesar contacted Domyx I, refused to expose the Demi-Spell, and was restored from the overwhelming trance by Vokenar.
- Kerben tracked a purple worm. Defeating it opened a shaft toward the lower plane and yielded Domyx II's amphora, a gold bugle, and an ancestral coronet.
- The party crossed physically into the Gray Wastes, where hanging islands crumbled above a vast desert of dust.
- The Opal's storage magic failed during the descent. Kerben's summoned roc, the sails, and the crew's wind source guided it safely onto the powder.
- Ceril and Red Caesar traced Starfall's old firing scars while Kerben piloted the roc, refining the ship's course.
- Vokenar sighted Gaokerena, and Sigil identified the world tree connecting the planes.

The descent brought physical evidence of histories the party had previously known through teachings and testimony. The dwarven expedition had failed long before the Cataclysm, but its dog's remaining memories supplied help in the present. The stone heads revealed the aftermath of the ancient divine war, while Domyx I's continuing presence showed that a destroyed avatar did not necessarily end a god's existence. His accusations about the victors remained contested claims, and Red Caesar protected the scroll rather than make it part of that struggle.

Kerben's tracking succeeded where the divination had not provided a route. The worm's tunnel opened a traversable boundary, and the recovered heirlooms made the family's connection to the depths more tangible. Domyx nevertheless carried the new curse onward; the record did not show it removed during the journey.

The ship's launch made the crossing a crisis for the entire crew. Domyx bought time to board, Raxxy organized the sailors, and Kerben supplied the creature that could guide the vessel when its storage failed. The Opal survived and began another kind of voyage. Ceril and Red Caesar resumed the shared navigation that had carried the party toward the repaired sky, now reading the weapon's damage beneath it. Gaokerena appeared at the close as a confirmed link between worlds, while the party's final destination and the fate of Starfall still lay ahead.
