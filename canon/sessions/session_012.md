# Session 012

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_012/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Poison and defenders in Darvinblast

The party left the refuge provided by the [[Steelfend Clan]] and continued searching [[Darvinblast]] for [[Morel Chainsunder]]. Their earlier efforts had won allies and divided the city's loyalties, but many inhabitants still treated them as enemies. [[Kerben]] renewed his stone sense to trace movement through the surrounding walls, [[Red Caesar]] prepared to detect magic, and [[Vokenar]] extended Water Walk over the party so that the city's sludgy ash and soot would not impede them.

The passages resembled narrow city streets, with homes dug into the walls above them, shuttered windows, wooden walkways, clotheslines, and pipes carrying water from hidden cisterns. Kerben realized why the residents lived above ground level when the party walked into a fetid black mist crawling along the floor. Its poison left Red Caesar and Vokenar weakened beyond the immediate injury. The raised dwellings offered some protection, but the environment itself made life underground unhealthy. [[Ceril]], mindful of his responsibility to protect Vokenar, helped shelter him within the group as they continued north.

At the next corner, a [[Deep World Wizard]] and an [[Etten]] emerged from the residences to defend the city. The two-headed giant carried enormous weapons, while the wizard combined destructive magic with dangerous close combat. [[Domyx]] confronted the Etten as Ceril raised concealing fog and Red Caesar challenged the enemy mage. A fireball badly injured Red Caesar and Ceril, but the party held together, with Kerben and [[Zulu]] joining the attack. Domyx brought down the Etten, and Ceril turned his attention to healing Red Caesar.

The wizard then struck Domyx with force magic and tried to attack Red Caesar, but Domyx disrupted him. His teleportation carried him away from that struggle and directly into Kerben and Zulu's reach. They wounded him, and Domyx pursued him to finish the battle. The wizard retaliated with a final burst of fire before falling. Vokenar healed the party afterward, and they chose to keep moving rather than stop for a rest.

#### A voice from the windows

A dwarf called down from one of the high windows, praising Domyx's willingness to risk his life for his companions and asking where he found such courage. Domyx answered that there was always a higher stand to take, whether or not he succeeded. The dwarf connected that idea to climbing back into daylight. He questioned whether Morel Chainsunder's plan had served everyone or only a privileged few, and other voices murmured above the party. He tossed Domyx a [[Potion of Proof Against Storms]], which could protect its drinker from thunder and lightning. Domyx kept the gift out of respect for its giver.

Searching the surrounding area, Vokenar and Ceril found crates of newly minted deep-world coins. Ceril argued that the money could serve their quest and treated taking it as part of their pirate life. Vokenar understood his companions' approach without agreeing with it, continuing the disagreement over theft from the preceding session. The coins went into the shared hold. In an unfinished section of the city's expanding residences, Kerben also found a valuable ruby embedded in the rock and added it to their stores.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_012/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Frightened residents and another ambush

The party first checked another inhabited passage. No one attacked, but a dwarf child called down to ask whether the strangers had come to kill or abduct them. Vokenar answered that they wanted to give the residents a choice about leaving the underground. Domyx observed that some might have been born into their confinement without ever knowing another life. The child's parents pulled them back inside, yet the party's visit without violence eased some of the watching inhabitants' fear.

As Red Caesar took the lead again, Kerben sensed movement through the walls, and the party spotted defenders preparing another ambush above them. Fighters dropped into the passages on both sides of the group, accompanied by a priest and another Deep World Wizard. The warning prevented the attackers from catching them unprepared.

The wizard's blighting magic withered Domyx, while Kerben killed the priest and the others fought their way through the surrounding defenders. Ceril summoned a flying beast spirit to harry the wizard. Red Caesar interrupted another hostile spell with Counterspell, imagining its effect through the image of [[Master Lee]] blowing a magical wind. The wizard destroyed Ceril's summoned spirit, then teleported toward the party's rear. Red Caesar and Ceril continued the assault, and Domyx ultimately struck down the mage who had blighted him, enduring another fiery rebuke as the enemy fell. The party had overcome the ambush and cleared the residential approaches.

#### A shrine and approaching reinforcements

During the search afterward, Red Caesar applied the method he had watched Kerben use to find gems. He located an intact jade deposit in unworked stone and sent it to the hold. Vokenar found a shrine furnished with valuable ivory figurines, which he and Domyx examined. The figures represented three principal dwarven gods associated with the deepest reaches of the earth, an underground counterpart to the three sister goddesses of the sky. They did not establish the figures' individual identities, but took them into their shared stores.

The party rested in the quieter passage, and Vokenar tended Domyx's injuries. The weakening caused by the poisonous mist still remained. During the rest, Morel Chainsunder's voice boomed through the city again, promising redemption and conquest of the upper world if his followers could defeat the remaining intruders.

Continuing onward, the party entered a broad area under development. There were fewer homes here, with mining tracks and carts overhead and fresh excavations in the walls. They could see reinforcements approaching from a distance: an enormous triceratops, later named [[Kilosaurus]], carried a roofed basket containing its driver, a spy, and other defenders. More fighters waited nearby.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_012/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### The triceratops and its handlers

Kerben studied the triceratops and judged it to be a trained animal rather than another subject of the magic binding the dwarves together. He wanted to spare it and, if possible, win its help after defeating its handlers. He used Speak with Animals and listened for the commands its driver gave, hoping to learn how to direct it himself.

Morel Chainsunder ordered a final stand as the battle began. Domyx rushed into the defenders, drawing their attention while Red Caesar's fire swept through the mounted group. The triceratops gored and flung Domyx amid the surrounding fighters, but Vokenar followed the fire with an icy blast that devastated the troops without catching the animal. Kerben eliminated another defender, and Domyx brought down the commandant.

#### Winning the triceratops's trust

Losing its handler did not immediately end the triceratops's aggression. It continued bucking and charged Ceril, injuring him while the party tried to avoid killing it. Red Caesar offered Guidance to Kerben, and Vokenar used Calm Emotions to quiet the beast enough for a conversation. The triceratops was concerned about the elf it had hurt and asked whether he would recover.

Through Kerben, the party learned that the animal had hatched in the fields above and been brought underground while young. It had carried people and stones and was proud to have served, but its life had become increasingly violent and painful. Its handlers had also fed it things it should not eat. It wanted to see its birthplace again and offered to carry the party, although it knew nothing of Morel Chainsunder's whereabouts.

Turning that willingness into trust proved difficult. The confused animal accidentally caught at Kerben with a horn, which his armor stopped, then became more frightened by what it had nearly done. Vokenar suggested that it could help lead the way and open blocked routes without being forced into further injury. Kerben offered a shared escape and the chance to help the people below, but the triceratops associated people with violence and almost fled.

Domyx approached gently and stroked the beast's side, calming it through his presence. With him keeping it steady, Kerben offered a crunchy stone-bark treat from the surface. The familiar food finally reassured the animal: it recognized something it had not tasted since youth and agreed to carry them. The party climbed into its basket and rested there before continuing, with Vokenar healing Domyx.

#### The bridge above the burning pit

Once Kerben's animal speech had faded, he discovered a useful posting inside the basket. Written in Northern, it listed the commands for movement, charging, and attack. He could therefore keep guiding their new ally without the spell. The route squeezed through a tunnel that appeared to have been enlarged to accommodate the beast before opening onto a stone bridge above a deep pit of burning tar and refuse.

A [[Fire Giant]] leapt from a hole in the wall, trailing flames and announcing in Giant's Runic that he had finally found a worthwhile fight. Domyx understood the challenge. The triceratops could just fit along the bridge, while Vokenar took wing and Kerben prepared to direct the mount toward the new opponent.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_012/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### The Fire Giant's challenge

The confrontation began at range. Kerben fired from the triceratops's basket, and the Fire Giant recognized his weapon as evidence that eastern gunpowder had spread since the giant last visited the surface. As the enemy advanced, Kerben brought the mount forward and commanded a charge. Its horns knocked the giant down, then pitched him over the side into the burning pit. The fall badly hurt him even though the flames could not. Kerben continued shooting from above.

Domyx jumped after the giant, enlarging as he fell and landing in a grapple that confined the enemy to fighting him. The giant's burning sword severely injured him, but Ceril healed him from above and the party continued supporting the fight. The giant proclaimed that the gods below would overcome the sister goddesses. He recognized Vokenar as an Arcadian and told him to return home, then addressed Domyx as one giant speaking to another and told him to return to the sky.

Kerben sent Zulu down into the pit as well. Although Domyx could have escaped through his cloud magic, he stayed to protect the companion. Vokenar's warding light helped him survive the giant's renewed attacks, and Ceril delivered the finishing blow. Vokenar swooped down to retrieve Zulu before the pit could hurt him further. Domyx climbed out as his enlarged body returned to its usual size.

The party found no further defenders in the chamber. Its excavations had been intended as the city's next expansion, with plans to drill deeper into the ground. Kerben and Vokenar healed Domyx, and the party continued without another rest.

#### A congregation held by fear

Around the bend lay the church's rear entrance. Unlike the earlier approach barred by two enchanted doors, this route had only one magical lock. Kerben used Knock from his keyring to open it, and Morel Chainsunder's voice invited them inside. Riding the triceratops, they entered a sanctuary where a robed congregation sat before a platform surrounded by smooth, bubbling tar. Most of the worshipers appeared to be dwarves and gnomes. Other civilians, stripped of their robes and confined in cages, seemed to have been punished for dissent.

The figure on the platform accused the party of having been sent by the sky goddesses to destroy the future his people had built over fifty years. Vokenar answered that they wanted the inhabitants to understand the world above and choose freely whether to leave or remain. He urged unity and rebuilding rather than continued separation, asking whether the congregation had actually seen the outside world or only heard its leader's account. The leader repeatedly interrupted him and insisted that only death waited above.

Vokenar dismounted and walked up the aisle to address both the figure and the congregation. He crushed a vial and restored it with Mending as a small demonstration of repair accompanying his appeal. Worried that the listeners' allegiance was wavering, the leader answered with a Flamestrike rising from geothermal heat beneath the floor. It gravely injured Domyx and the triceratops and burned Vokenar and Kerben. He then accused [[Figma Brickfinger]] of forcing his people underground and taking them from the light; the party did not establish that accusation as fact.

Domyx, badly wounded and confronting the possibility of his own death, showed Vokenar the marks left on his hands when he had touched the sky years earlier. The affected fingertips and parts of his palms mirrored the sky's changing color. At that moment they were bright blue, evidence of the daylight overhead despite the surrounding darkness. Vokenar lifted his hands before the congregation and urged them to consider a beauty some had never seen. They could go above and return if they wished; what mattered was having the choice.

The display drew murmurs from the worshipers. Domyx found a new purpose in the climb that had marked his hands, setting aside his usual bravado to show them calmly as he walked among the pews. Kerben meanwhile shot the figure on the platform, revealing an oddly colored dwarven face beneath the robes. The congregation seemed to regard its leader as too powerful to be threatened by ordinary violence, so the shots did not immediately derail the appeal.

Red Caesar turned to the prisoners. He discovered that the cages were held shut by external bolts rather than locks, removed one, and opened a way out. The frightened occupant fled before Red Caesar could enlist his help.

### Chunk 0004

[[chunks/session_012/chunk_0004|Source transcript]]

#### Unmasking the false Morel Chainsunder

Red Caesar continued toward the other cages while the leader condemned the fleeing prisoner and insisted that the surface would reject him. Ceril used divination to test the identity of the figure they had come to find. He joined Vokenar and reported that the creature before them was not Morel Chainsunder: seeking the real person did not lead to a living individual.

Vokenar used that discovery to question the congregation's trust. He pointed to their imprisoned friends and families and to the triceratops their leader had burned despite its service to the city. After healing the animal, he asked whether anyone had known the supposed Morel Chainsunder before coming below. His words visibly weakened the figure. The leader tried to silence him with magic, but Vokenar's Guarded Mind resisted the attempt, allowing him to keep speaking.

Domyx pulled back several worshipers' cowls so that they could see one another as distinct people. He asked them to think about what they wanted for themselves rather than simply following someone else's authority. They need not believe in the leader or even in Vokenar to believe in their own capacity to choose. Domyx used his sky-marked hands to explain that willpower, rather than his stature alone, had carried him upward. He was willing to face death by his own decision, but rejected their being made to die under another person's command. As the listeners reconsidered themselves and their allegiance, the figure became increasingly frightened and diminished.

Kerben kept shooting, demonstrating that the apparently invulnerable leader could bleed. Vokenar now saw that the exposed face differed from the one the party had encountered through an earlier illusion at the entrance. It looked like an ordinary member of the congregation. Red Caesar freed more prisoners, further undermining the leader's hold. Vokenar finally brought the figure down with radiant Burning Hands. The disguise fell away, leaving an unconscious congregant rather than the person the party had sought. Vokenar called on the others to heal their companion, and they began caring for one another. The recovered individual soon blended back into the gathering, apparently as bewildered as everyone else about the role he had occupied.

#### The founder's will

Ceril's divination then led him across the tar to a small office or shrine behind the sanctuary. He called Red Caesar forward to examine the magic there. A cloaked skeleton sat before a document, and Kerben judged the remains to be about half a century old. The document identified the dead man as the original Morel Chainsunder.

His last will was also the spell sustaining the community. It bound the dwarves together emotionally, allowing them to share one another's feelings and awareness. He had hoped that solidarity would eventually bring happiness, a return to the surface, and even peace with Figma Brickfinger. Instead, anger had fed back through the shared bond until it amplified fear and hostility. The imagined watcher who punished disobedience was a manifestation of their collective paranoia; an ordinary member of the community could assume the leader's identity and power. The long-dead Morel Chainsunder and the threatening figure the party had confronted were therefore distinct.

Red Caesar's Detect Magic and Identify established that the spell resided in the written document and could be broken by tearing it. He wondered whether Figma Brickfinger could redirect it toward its original good purpose. Vokenar worried about what would happen to people who had lived under its influence for so long, recalling a divine warning of bloodshed and hoping for a gentler transition. Ceril added that the curse appeared connected to the Mirage Arcana he had mapped outside. Finding the hidden [[Penumbra]] might require destroying the spell.

#### Aeris's counsel and the curse's end

Before deciding, Ceril used his shield to consult [[Aeris]]. Asked whether they could both save lives and find Penumbra, she indicated that such a path existed and reminded them that their words had already freed the dwarves, as the spell once had. On the document itself, her direction was clear: destroy the binding magic and let new magic free them.

The remaining questions turned to their wider task. Aeris placed the nearby Penumbra in the hills and warned that others might seek it. She said that other organizations understood little about using the material, which helped explain their desire to collect it. Ceril suspected that [[The Order of Seasons]] wanted it for the [[Demi-Spell]], but that connection remained his interpretation. The party recalled the fragment stolen from [[Castle Kaedon]] by [[Keys Caeradel]] and his half-elven companion. The Order of Seasons was based in [[Brimbolyn]], and Ceril had already obtained permission for the party to enter elven territory. He disliked the high elves, but regarded following divine direction there as part of his duty.

Aeris also said that more Penumbra fragments existed than the party believed, scattered on land and beneath the ocean. Ceril noted that pieces could gather together, leaving the total uncertain. Neither he nor Vokenar knew how much [[Lyngbakr]] would need to complete their work, although they reasoned that the goddesses would not have set them an impossible task. Vokenar wanted to warn Figma Brickfinger about the liberation before unrest could spread above; a peaceful aftermath was still a hope rather than an established outcome.

Following the consultation, Red Caesar looked once more at the skeleton and tore the will down the middle. The magic departed. Outside the shrine, Darvinblast grew louder as residents emerged from their homes, their fear giving way to curiosity. This ended the binding curse without establishing that every inhabitant would welcome the party.

Kerben noticed a tiny point of blue light at the top of a long vertical shaft, revealing another possible exit. The party chose to backtrack instead so that it could bring the triceratops out. During a final search, Kerben found books recording the deep-world inhabitants' own history of events around [[The Pit]], including accounts the people above might not know. The party took the texts, rode out, and arranged for its new animal ally to be returned to [[The Opal]].

#### The hidden Penumbra quarry

Ceril then led the others toward the terrain the illusion had concealed. By early afternoon they reached a previously hidden quarry, with no workers or flying scouts in sight. There they found a massive black, tetrahedral Penumbra fragment, far larger than anything they could easily carry. It appeared that the deep-world inhabitants had been drawing energy from it. The imperial scouts seen earlier might have been searching for this very site, and the vanished illusion might have been what kept them from finding it.

The discovery fulfilled the regional search, but transporting the fragment remained an immediate problem. The party rested afterward, recovering from the poisonous mist and reaching eighth level as it consolidated the growth earned through the expedition. Domyx strengthened his endurance after repeatedly risking his life, while Kerben considered how to develop his training beyond his existing ranger and rogue abilities.

### Chunk 0005

[[chunks/session_012/chunk_0005|Source transcript]]

#### Growth after Darvinblast

The party's advancement continued after the expedition. Kerben considered adding wizard magic through Arcane Trickster training, but chose the Assassin path instead, emphasizing decisive attacks and opening opportunities for poison craft. Red Caesar strengthened his spellcasting and protective ward, prepared Control Water, and chose Tongues so that unfamiliar languages would no longer leave him dependent on translation. Vokenar developed Heavy Armor Master training, increasing his strength and protection, and prepared Banishment alongside the Arcane Eye supplied by his divine domain. He also offered to teach Kerben the language of the undead.

#### Farewell and the road ahead

A brief scene returned to the party's departure from Darvinblast: [[Gammix]] and [[Tammix]], who had remained with their family, waved their new friends off. Their farewell affirmed the alliance won during the descent even as the wider city's adjustment remained uncertain.

The party divided treasure from the shared hold. Its accumulated stores included the coins, ruby, jade, ivory deity figures, and historical books recovered in Darvinblast alongside earlier acquisitions. Red Caesar selected an amethyst, Domyx took mithril, and Kerben received jade. A holy mask was identified with Ogun, the dwarven god of darkness, although its recipient was not clearly established. The gems were valuable materials for trade or possible enchantment rather than magical items in themselves.

Before setting out for Brimbolyn, the party still needed a way to bring the enormous Penumbra fragment aboard The Opal. Domyx proposed asking Figma Brickfinger's people to help move it. Speaking with her about the freed population and visiting the dwarven market to exchange their gems or improve equipment were also proposed. These preparations and the Penumbra's transport remained unfinished at the session's close.

### Summary

- The party left the [[Steelfend Clan]] refuge, endured [[Darvinblast]]'s poisonous environment, and defeated two Deep World Wizards and their accompanying defenders.
- A dissenting resident gave [[Domyx]] a [[Potion of Proof Against Storms]]. The party recovered minted coins, a ruby, jade, ivory deity figures, and deep-world historical texts.
- [[Kerben]], [[Vokenar]], Domyx, and [[Red Caesar]] helped win the trust of the triceratops later named [[Kilosaurus]], which carried them through the city and helped defeat a [[Fire Giant]].
- Vokenar defended the congregation's freedom to choose, Domyx demonstrated the living sky through his marked hands and urged self-determination, and Red Caesar released prisoners.
- [[Ceril]] exposed the apparent [[Morel Chainsunder]] as an identity sustained by shared fear. The original Morel Chainsunder had died about fifty years earlier, leaving a well-intended emotional bond that had become a curse.
- After consulting [[Aeris]], Red Caesar destroyed the written spell. The curse ended, residents emerged with less fear, and the linked illusion cleared.
- Ceril led the party to a hidden quarry containing a massive [[Penumbra]] fragment. Its transport was still unresolved.
- The party recovered and reached eighth level. Kerben chose Assassin training; Domyx increased his endurance; Red Caesar strengthened his magic and selected Tongues; Vokenar improved his armor training.
- Kilosaurus returned to [[The Opal]]. The party intended to consult [[Figma Brickfinger]] and then pursue [[The Order of Seasons]] in [[Brimbolyn]].

The descent ended by exposing a system of shared fear rather than simply defeating a living cult leader. Morel Chainsunder's original hope had been to unite his people until they could find peace, but his unmaintained spell magnified their anger and gave it a succession of apparent leaders. The party's earlier mercy and efforts to reduce hostility mattered because the authority it confronted depended on the community's feelings. Healing, prisoner releases, persuasion, and the visible vulnerability of the supposed leader all helped make that dependence apparent.

Vokenar's insistence on choice linked the frightened child's question, the triceratops's fear, and the congregation's confinement. Domyx gave that principle a personal expression: his willingness to risk death was his own decision, and his hands showed others a world their leader denied them. Ceril's divination supplied the crucial distinction between the dead founder and the present manifestation, while Red Caesar translated that knowledge into a practical end to the curse. Their cooperation did not erase differences in their methods; Vokenar still objected to taking the residents' treasure.

Winning the triceratops's trust also changed the expedition. A former instrument of the defenders became an ally through calming magic, patient care, and familiar food, then helped the party reach the church. The farewell from Gammix and Tammix supplied a smaller but clear sign of friendship beyond the spell's destruction. Neither those relationships nor the city's emerging curiosity guaranteed an orderly reconciliation with the surface dwarves.

By the close, the party had broken Darvinblast's binding curse and found the Penumbra it sought. The new fragment's size made discovery only the first part of recovery. Figma Brickfinger's response, the liberated population's adjustment, and the journey toward the Order of Seasons remained immediate concerns, while Aeris's answers broadened the search without establishing how many fragments their larger mission required.
