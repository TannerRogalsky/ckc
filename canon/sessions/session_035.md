## Session 035

### Chunk 0000

- The party defeats three clones guarding [[House Erendel]]. One succeeds in warning [[Boril Erendel]] of their arrival.
- The party reaches the laboratory's magically locked entrance.

### Chunk 0001

- [[Kerben]] opens the entrance with his magical key. Inside, tiers of mirrors show the visitors as high elf replacements; his stone sense reveals a hidden lower chamber.
- [[Boril Erendel]] reveals that [[Vokenar]] retains his aasimar heritage within his high elf incarnation.
- Boril Erendel emerges in a silver-armored Larethian form, alluding to [[Corellon Larethian]], and attacks the party. The battle remains unresolved at the chunk's end.

### Chunk 0002

- [[Vokenar]]'s restored radiance kills [[Boril Erendel]]. Vokenar retains both bloodlines and an appearance reflecting both; the mirrors return to true reflections.
- The party recovers mithril fragments, an ancient liquid-metal weapon that copies Vokenar's hammer in his hands, and a harp that plays endlessly without repeating a song. They destroy the mirrors and collapse [[House Erendel]].
- At [[The Academy]], [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] thanks the party and confirms that he has discouraged [[Keys Caeradel]] from casting the [[Demi-Spell]]. [[Feronia Caeradel]] confirms that she and the [[Rakshasa]] are Keys Caeradel's parents.
- Keys Caeradel initially confines his visiting father in a magical cage. After debating the world's future, he entrusts the completed Demi-Spell to the party and leaves with his father's soul bound, by consent, into an amulet.
- [[Red Caesar]] takes custody of the spell and learns he can permanently destroy it by draining its energy. Vokenar can enact it. [[Domyx]] can read and carry it safely but cannot cast it; [[Ceril]] refuses to study it.
- Teleportation and planar travel to [[Axis Mundi]] both fail. The party suspects lasting abjurations protecting [[Starfall]].
- At [[The Pit]], [[Figma Brickfinger]] and [[Courteous Cam]] confirm that [[The Funnel]] has reopened, although the miners withdrew from the dangers beyond.
- Before the descent, Ceril acquires the [[Cornucopia of Constellations]], commissioned from the elves, and Domyx acquires the [[Kaboom Ring]]. The party prepares to enter the Funnel.

### Summary

The confrontation at House Erendel ends Boril Erendel's attempt to impose high elf bodies on the world's souls. His mirror laboratory exposes both the scale of the project and his belief that elven perfection entitles him to overrule other peoples and the gods. Vokenar rejects that domination while accepting the revelation that his aasimar nature survives reincarnation. His restored radiance ends the battle, and he emerges with a stronger sense of belonging to both [[Arkadia]] and [[Stark]].

The party then faces the choice underlying the Demi-Spell itself. Keys Caeradel argues that repairing the sky has left the moon shattered and generations of suffering unhealed. Red Caesar and Vokenar defend the people and relationships that exist now. Ceril recalls the gods' assurance that the moon can recover naturally and relays [[Illidrielle Gandara]]'s wish that Keys Caeradel move forward with his life. Keys Caeradel concedes that the next world should not be his decision alone. His father's advice lets him surrender the spell while preserving it as a possible last resort.

The family reunion and the handover resolve the immediate conflict with the Order, but leave the party responsible for an intact world-remaking spell. Red Caesar would destroy it rather than let it be misused; Vokenar trusts him and remains willing to cast it if all else fails, while Ceril objects to Red Caesar holding it and prefers Vokenar as its custodian. The party's next destination is Axis Mundi. With magical entry blocked, the reopened Funnel offers a physical approach to the boundary below Stark.

### Connections

- Vokenar's dual heritage resolves the apparent loss of his aasimar nature after Ceril reincarnated him in session 032.
- Boril Erendel's defeat fulfills the bargain made with Rizolvir Kiirnodel in session 034.
- Destroying [[Farraday]]'s Stark body and the [[Tome of Satariel]] in session 034 enabled the Rakshasa to leave [[The Garden]] and visit Keys Caeradel.
- The moon's natural recovery answers a remaining objection to the sky's restoration without requiring the Demi-Spell.
- Red Caesar's ability to drain sky energy now gives him a way to destroy the completed spell; the [[Genesis Mundi]] remains the distinct natural cycle that the spell would accelerate and modify.
- Failed magical travel reinforces the planar interpretation of the [[Chart of the Witness]], while the Funnel's reopening completes the excavation requested in session 032.
