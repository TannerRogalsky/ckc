# Session 035

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_035/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### The copies outside House Erendel

The opening continued the confrontation outside [[House Erendel]]. [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] had warned that [[Boril Erendel]] was building a force and might try to restore his preferred version of the [[Demi-Spell]]. Three pale copies of the wizard occupied separate positions on the stepped structure. Water covered its base, and the party approached through that flooded ground. The copies immediately began preparing magic rather than offer a welcome or explanation.

[[Kerben]] fired from the water, using concealment between shots, while [[Ceril]] attacked with starlight. The copies' unnaturally marblelike skin proved to be a magical defense against weapons. Their answering bolts of cold, lightning, and acid threatened the advancing companions, particularly Kerben and [[Domyx]].

[[Red Caesar]] tried to speed Domyx's advance with Haste, but a copy dispelled the enchantment and Red Caesar's own protective armor. Losing the borrowed speed left Domyx briefly unable to act. Red Caesar then empowered [[Vokenar]] instead, allowing the cleric to reach the defenders without repeating that immediate setback.

The copies claimed the perfection and invulnerability of elves. One tried to force supremacist memories into Vokenar's mind, urging him to abandon the supposedly lesser people beside him. Vokenar resisted. Ceril meanwhile used Moonbeam to wear down a defender and break its stone-skinned protection. Their coordinated attacks began overcoming the copies' apparent immunity.

#### The warning got through

Red Caesar's magical missiles brought down the first defender. Another copy used Sending to warn Boril Erendel that the party had arrived. Red Caesar attempted to stop it, but the message passed through. The party could continue into the house, but no longer expect to surprise its owner.

Domyx tested the [[Cestus of the Clear Sky]] against another defender. His blows drove it from one of the tiers, and he followed it down, striking with a fist enlarged by gathering clouds. The weapon also allowed his restorative sky power to feed the force of an attack.

Ceril continued moving Moonbeam between the copies, and its light destroyed another of them. To reach the final defender, the beam had to pass through Vokenar's position. Vokenar accepted that danger. The radiance hurt his reincarnated body while reminding him of the nature he had possessed before his death. Still empowered by Red Caesar's speed, he leaped down with his hammer and destroyed the last copy.

The party regrouped at the entrance. Their fighting had damaged the defenders without exhausting every capability they would need inside, but several companions required healing. Vokenar, Ceril, and Kerben restored the injured while Domyx recovered his strength. They proceeded without taking a separate rest.

The entrance led to moss-covered stone doors whose worn center showed recent use. An arcane lock still sealed them. Kerben's magical key ring offered a way through, leaving that opening as the next step in the pursuit.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_035/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Reflections of Boril Erendel's desired world

After the healing, Kerben used his key ring to release the arcane lock. Inside, House Erendel opened into a great central space rather than a sequence of enclosed floors. Hanging balconies narrowed toward the top, connected by an angular spiral stair. Dozens of mirrors crowded each level. Some had been placed in orderly rows, while later additions stood wherever room could be found. A green mist rose from below, and the light entering with the party multiplied across the glass.

The mirrors showed altered versions of their visitors. Red Caesar, Kerben, and Domyx saw high elf replacements bearing Boril Erendel's features. Ceril and Vokenar were less obviously changed, but closer examination showed that even Ceril's reflection had a different, more androgynous appearance. The images followed the party's movements, presenting the forms Boril Erendel intended them to possess.

Boril Erendel's voice echoed from somewhere beneath them, praising Red Caesar's apparent improvement and disparaging Kerben's ancestry. His own reflection shifted among the mirrors without revealing his exact position. He invited the party down to fight, claiming that either victory or defeat would lead to their rebirth in the bodies prepared for them.

Vokenar confronted him over the reincarnation that had forced him into an elf's body. Boril Erendel answered that the cleric had not lost his aasimar nature: he now possessed both inheritances. Vokenar took stock of himself and recognized that his old powers remained within his soul. The radiance outside had hurt him, but some part of him had also felt at home in it. His aasimar abilities returned alongside those of his elf incarnation.

Boril Erendel used that revelation to support a much broader claim. In his account, elves had been the first people, and the gods had divided their power into the lesser peoples to prevent them from taking the divine throne. Red Caesar challenged that mythology, suggesting an earlier elemental origin and a shared passage through different forms. Their exchange established competing explanations and Boril Erendel's ideology; it did not prove his claim of elven supremacy.

#### The hidden chamber and the Larethian form

Kerben used his sense of stone to examine the space beyond the visible rooms. More chambers extended into the ground, and a false portion of the floor concealed a hatch. He also brought [[Zulu]] into the house. The dinosaur's reflection became an elf as well, showing that Boril Erendel's scheme reached beyond the party's humanoid members.

Red Caesar established Circle of Power before the companions reached the bottom. They gathered within its protection while watching the concealed floor. A platform rose through the opening, driving the green mist outward and revealing Boril Erendel.

The wizard wore silver armor and a mask, with white hair falling behind an altered, androgynous form. He called it the Larethian form and claimed mastery over the magic beneath it. Later examination would connect that name to [[Corellon Larethian]], the primordial elven deity. Boril Erendel accused Ceril of betraying the chance of a perfect world by opposing the Demi-Spell. Ceril rejected the perfection that the wizard sought to impose.

Boril Erendel demanded combat. Ceril tried to establish Moonbeam, but the wizard prevented it despite Red Caesar's attempt to protect the spell. Domyx's blows struck the silver armor, and Kerben advanced with Zulu to open it with his blades and whip. Boril Erendel's contempt for the dwarf turned to surprise when those attacks drew blood.

#### The party holds its ground

Boril Erendel answered with a prismatic spray. Zulu, exposed beyond Red Caesar's protection, fell to its acid. The companions within Circle of Power endured much less of the assault. Its boundary slowed and softened the magical rays, making their combined defense central to their survival.

The wizard rushed Red Caesar to break that protection. His great blade appeared to be made of liquid metal, and its wounds left a shimmering effect that slowed the people it struck. Red Caesar nevertheless maintained the circle while Vokenar called a Guardian of Faith into the chamber.

Red Caesar then disrupted Boril Erendel with a close magical jolt, giving Ceril an opening to establish Moonbeam without another interruption. Its light chipped away at the armor. Domyx rallied the party with a battle cry, and Kerben again attacked through the defenses the wizard believed should keep a dwarf beneath him.

Kerben used the Mana Worm Poison he had commissioned in Cutlass Cray. The dose coursed visibly through Boril Erendel's veins and caused his attempted Misty Step to fail against him. Unable to make that escape, the wizard remained amid the party's damaging magic. The poison had accomplished the purpose for which Kerben brought it, though it did not suppress every power Boril Erendel possessed.

Boril Erendel swept the chamber with destructive energy from his gaze and attacked the weakened Vokenar. Red Caesar's circle and Domyx's encouragement helped the party withstand the assault, but the cleric came close to falling. Boril Erendel also stunned Kerben with a word of power, preventing him from immediately returning to the fight.

Vokenar thanked Boril Erendel for revealing that his aasimar nature remained, even while rejecting the purpose behind the revelation. Ceril and Domyx continued pressing the wizard. Once the poison's disruption had passed, Boril Erendel succeeded in moving behind Ceril and renewed his attacks. Red Caesar was brought to the brink of collapse but held his protective spell. The confrontation continued into the next chunk with the party still standing and Boril Erendel increasingly desperate.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_035/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Vokenar's radiance ends the battle

Boril Erendel struck at Ceril while blaming him for turning away from his own people. Vokenar responded by restoring the entire party. His healing also revived Zulu, and the [[Ioun of the Gilded Savior]] carried its shared benefit to Red Caesar, lifting the wizard from near collapse without ending Circle of Power.

Vokenar then approached with the radiance of his recovered aasimar nature shining around him. Its light delivered the final wound. Before fading, Boril Erendel urged him to retain both bloodlines and called that inheritance his parting gift.

Vokenar emerged with both sets of capabilities and an appearance combining his former aasimar features with those of his high elf incarnation. He said that what had seemed a curse had given him a sense of belonging both to [[Arkadia]] and to [[Stark]], and a further reason to protect each. His gratitude for that discovery did not endorse the wizard's attempt to determine everyone else's form.

As Boril Erendel died, the mirrors returned to ordinary reflections. Kerben once again saw himself in the glass. The imposed images had lost their hold, and the party could examine the emptied house.

#### The relics and the destruction of the laboratory

Among the mirrors, the companions found empty bodies resembling the copies outside. These were described as flesh vessels that had never lived, rather than people killed and stored there. The search also yielded broken mithril pieces, which the party recovered for the hold.

Boril Erendel's liquid-metal weapon changed shape after the battle. It was an ancient divine relic, but showed no ordinary enchantment of its own. When Vokenar held it, it became a copy of his hammer, including its magical properties. He handed it back after the test. Ceril declined to handle the metal, and the relic joined the recovered treasures without establishing another permanently wielded weapon.

An enchanted harp played by itself nearby. Examining it showed that its music could continue forever without repeating a song. The companions considered using it aboard The Opal, adding another source of entertainment to the one they had just recruited.

Red Caesar investigated the building's history. It appeared to be an old noble residence of the kind [[Queen Caeradwyn]] had visited before the Cataclysm, repurposed into a laboratory after the old order fell. Its underlying structure carried historical significance rather than an innate magical power. The Larethian name recalled Corellon Larethian, a primordial elf whose divine identity could be male or female. That connection explained the ideal Boril Erendel had invoked without making him the deity.

The party destroyed the mirrors and brought down the ziggurat with magic. Red Caesar teleported the companions out as it collapsed. They left the laboratory unable to continue the work they had confronted, carrying its useful materials and relics toward their next meeting with the elves.

#### Keys Caeradel and his captive father

The party returned to Brimbolyn and reached [[The Academy]] by its tram. Rizolvir Kiirnodel and [[Feronia Caeradel]] greeted them. Rizolvir Kiirnodel thanked them for defeating Boril Erendel and confirmed that he had been discouraging [[Keys Caeradel]] from casting the Demi-Spell. The urgency among the elves remained greater than before, despite that delay.

Feronia Caeradel explained the visiting [[Rakshasa]]'s connection to her family. He had once been her husband, in another world and a different form, and was Keys Caeradel's father. She rejected the assumption that demons never changed, while admitting uncertainty about what he had become through his transformations.

The Academy was subdued. [[Illidrielle Gandara]], who had once welcomed the party with enthusiasm, was conspicuously absent. The elves had completed their great spell but had failed to protect their people from the moon's destruction. The successful research had not relieved that grief.

Keys Caeradel stood before a force cage containing his father. Their reunion had quickly turned into renewed confinement, though the Rakshasa still spoke in his son's defense. Keys Caeradel wanted to cast the Demi-Spell and argued that the promised repair had not restored the deaths, suffering, and destruction of the intervening years.

Red Caesar and Vokenar answered that repairing the sky had been the bargain. The Rakshasa challenged even that claim: the moon was part of the sky, and it remained shattered. He blamed the party for allowing its destruction. That was his accusation, rather than a new account of who had fired Starfall.

Keys Caeradel also feared that a world without the moon would eventually fail. Ceril recalled the gods' explanation that two functioning powers could help heal the third and that Crone was already recovering naturally. The shattered moon required time, he argued, rather than another remaking of reality.

#### The present world and the possible next one

Red Caesar asked what undoing the catastrophe would do to the good that had followed it. People had formed families, loved one another, and had children who belonged to the present world. Pursuing only the old losses would erase those lives as well. He challenged Keys Caeradel to consider whether a restored past could ever supply the happiness he sought.

Vokenar warned that anyone who remembered what the spell took away might become the next enemy, repeating the violence rather than ending it. He urged Keys Caeradel to turn his power toward other good that could be done. Red Caesar suggested leaving the Academy and seeking old friends, if any still lived.

Ceril delivered the message he had received from Illidrielle Gandara in the afterlife. She had wanted Keys Caeradel to move forward and find a life of his own. She was happy where she was, and he might seek happiness without calling her back through a rewritten world.

Keys Caeradel conceded that he should not be the sole person deciding what a new world would become. He asked his father what to do. The Rakshasa recommended entrusting the spell to the people who had already carried responsibility for this world's survival. If they failed, changed their minds, or reached a point at which it was necessary, the completed work could still be enacted.

Keys Caeradel accepted. He left the Demi-Spell with the party and considered traveling to find former companions. His father preferred an amulet to a cage carried along behind him. At the Rakshasa's own request, Keys Caeradel used Magic Jar to bind his father's soul within a gem and wore it around his neck. He then departed, intending to speak with his mother before beginning his next direction in life. The reunion and decision were established; the later journey and the friends he might find were still uncertain.

#### Responsibility for the scroll

Red Caesar wished to study the completed spell and carry it. Its abstract was accessible, but the text itself emanated overwhelming power. Symbols shifted into combinations he could not comprehend, and he set it aside before prolonged examination could harm him.

Vokenar could read it safely. Its writing moved among familiar and unfamiliar languages, including those of the heavens and Arkadia, but did not overwhelm him. Examination established that he was capable of enacting it.

Red Caesar discovered a different capacity. His ability to siphon the energy of Penumbra and the sky could drain the Demi-Spell until it ceased to exist. That would permanently destroy the spell. He would have to hold it for a sustained period, whose duration was not yet known.

Red Caesar shared those findings and proposed keeping the scroll. Ceril objected, preferring Vokenar as its custodian and refusing to study a document he considered unnatural. Vokenar trusted Red Caesar's judgment after their journey together. He accepted that Red Caesar could destroy the spell if necessary and offered to cast it only if they reached dire need.

Domyx likewise treated its possible use as a grave responsibility. If someone tried to enact it against their wishes, destroying that option would be preferable to allowing an irreversible remaking of everyone. He then read it himself and found that it did not harm him. His connection to the sky gave him an affinity with its purpose, although his inability to cast spells prevented him from enacting it. He could serve as a safe carrier if the others needed to pass it to him.

Red Caesar retained the intact Demi-Spell. He would rather destroy it than let it fall into the wrong hands. The party's positions remained distinct: Ceril objected to the custody, Vokenar preserved the possibility of a last resort, and Domyx offered practical support while recognizing the stakes. The decision here entrusted the spell; it did not use or erase it.

#### The blocked magical route

With the Academy's immediate conflict resolved, the party turned toward [[Axis Mundi]]. The border below Stark separated it from the surface as the sky separated Stark from Arkadia. Red Caesar's attempt to teleport there failed, reinforcing the understanding that the destination lay on another plane.

After a day had passed, Vokenar prepared Plane Shift and tried to take the party below. That also failed. Red Caesar considered longstanding abjurations, such as a permanent private sanctum, that could block teleportation and travel between planes. Emperor Shen and [[Vizier Jade]] might have protected Starfall's realm that way for years. The failed journeys supported the possibility of wards without showing their original casting or precise extent.

The dwarves' excavation offered a physical route instead. The party traveled to [[The Pit]], where [[Figma Brickfinger]] and [[Courteous Cam]] reported that [[The Funnel]] had been reopened. Spacious, unfamiliar air lay beyond the new passage, but the miners had withdrawn from its dangers rather than venture farther. Courteous Cam admitted that completing the work had required breaking some union rules. The party thanked the diggers and prepared to take responsibility for the descent.

#### The final equipment before the descent

Before going farther, the account returned to the companions' remaining equipment purchases. Ceril had commissioned the [[Cornucopia of Constellations]] from the elves. It supplied what he needed to grow the food for his magical feasts and strengthened their sustaining effects. Besides protection from poison, a feast could grant further elemental resistances, with a later meal allowing those protections to change.

Domyx used his remaining diamonds for the [[Kaboom Ring]], a returning thrown weapon. Its impact released a thunderous burst that could strike nearby foes and briefly deafen them. It supplied a reusable alternative to his javelins while leaving the Cestus of the Clear Sky as his principal close weapon.

The party rested and readied itself at the Funnel. The route had been opened, the last purchases settled, and the intact Demi-Spell traveled with them. Their entry into the dangerous spaces beyond remained the next stage of the campaign.

### Summary

- The party destroyed House Erendel's three defending copies. One warned Boril Erendel before they could enter.
- Boril Erendel's mirrors projected high elf replacements for the visitors. Vokenar learned that his aasimar nature had survived reincarnation and recovered its powers.
- Red Caesar's Circle of Power, the companions' combined attacks, and Kerben's Mana Worm Poison helped overcome Boril Erendel. Vokenar's restored radiance delivered the final wound.
- The party recovered mithril, a shape-changing divine relic, and an endlessly varied musical harp, then destroyed the mirrors and collapsed House Erendel.
- Feronia Caeradel confirmed that the Rakshasa was her former husband and Keys Caeradel's father. Keys Caeradel initially held him captive, then bound his soul into an amulet at his request.
- The party challenged the proposed remaking of the world. Ceril conveyed Illidrielle Gandara's wish that Keys Caeradel move forward and recalled the gods' assurance that Crone could heal.
- Keys Caeradel entrusted the intact Demi-Spell to the party. Red Caesar retained it and could destroy it; Vokenar could enact it; Domyx could safely read and carry it. Ceril opposed its custody and declined to study it.
- Teleportation and Plane Shift toward Axis Mundi both failed. The reopened Funnel offered a physical approach.
- Ceril received the Cornucopia of Constellations, Domyx acquired the Kaboom Ring, and the party prepared for the descent.

The confrontation ended Boril Erendel's active project while revealing an inheritance he had not wholly erased. Vokenar's recovery made his reincarnation a more complicated transformation than the apparent loss of his former nature. He rejected the wizard's coercion but accepted the part of himself that connected him to both homes. The mirrors' true reflections and the laboratory's destruction ended this immediate attempt to impose one form on other lives.

The Academy posed the larger question in another way. Keys Caeradel's grief and concern for the moon gave his desire to remake the world a continuing urgency, even after the heavens had been repaired. The party answered with the lives already present, the possibility of natural recovery, and Illidrielle Gandara's own wish for his future. Surrendering the spell allowed Keys Caeradel to leave its choice to others and seek a purpose beyond the completed research.

That resolution transferred responsibility rather than remove the danger. The scroll remained powerful, intact, and disputed within the party. Red Caesar's capacity to erase it and Vokenar's capacity to enact it supplied opposing possible uses, with Domyx able to protect its passage and Ceril refusing participation in its study. The companions carried those unsettled choices toward a realm that their ordinary magical travel could not reach. At the session's close, the miners' completed work had opened a way below, but the party had not yet explored it.
