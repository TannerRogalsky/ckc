# Session 018

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_018/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### The graveyard and cisterns

After resting near the storehouse cleared in the previous session, the party continued around the southern part of [[Castle Kaedon]]'s exposed grounds. Their earlier encounters had taught them that the apparently quiet island could conceal threats that gathered around travelers with little warning. [[Kerben]] sent [[Tango]] ahead, while [[Red Caesar]] noticed the smell of damp, musty grain from a larger neighboring building. The party identified it as another granary, likely associated with the old brewing operation.

They considered opening part of its wooden wall to admit sunlight, but [[Ceril]] explained that the carrion crawlers encountered earlier would not be especially hindered by daylight. They instead continued scouting outside. Watching for unnatural movement in the passing fog, Ceril noticed spirits rising from a field ahead. Broken headstones revealed that the overgrown ground had once been a graveyard.

At the same time, water seeped through the walls of old cisterns behind the party. The streams collected into a living vortex: a water elemental. Ceril's warning kept the travelers from advancing blindly, but they still faced restless dead on one side and the elemental on the other.

[[Vokenar]] turned the undead, severely weakening a group of specters and frightening several back into their graves. Those that retreated remained beneath the earth rather than being destroyed. Ceril attacked the spirits that continued threatening the party, while [[Domyx]] confronted the elemental. His forceful blows disrupted its shape and slowed it, helping keep it from reaching the companions behind him.

Red Caesar found that the elemental could not simply be dispelled. He instead drove a gust through the specters, pushing their mist-like forms away and restricting their approach. Kerben fired at the living water while Tango attacked a spirit. The specters could drain vitality as well as inflict immediate wounds; Tango suffered badly from that influence, and Domyx and Red Caesar were also drained during the fight.

The elemental eventually engulfed Domyx. He remained able to struggle inside it, but its swirling pressure battered him while he was trapped. He continued striking from within as the others reduced the remaining spirits. Vokenar finally hit the elemental with his sword and caused it to break down into a brief silvery-gray substance that vanished from the plane. Domyx was released, and Ceril killed the last attacking specter.

The party chose to continue without another rest. Ceril considered Greater Restoration for the draining effects, but kept that option in reserve in case similar enemies appeared later. He healed Domyx with a scroll, and Kerben resummoned Tango in a fresh, undrained form. Red Caesar also reflected that his protective sphere might have rescued Domyx from the elemental, but that possibility had not been used during the battle.

#### Brown mold and a surviving book

Continuing around the larger granary, the party found an area with horse hooks, barrels, and deteriorated leather. The building offered several approaches: a rear door, two smaller front doors, and a larger gate that could once have admitted horses. They began considering how to enter without repeating the sudden rush of toxic air from the previous storehouse.

Kerben noticed a sharply concentrated moldy smell and withdrew. Red Caesar did not recognize the danger in time. Brown mold growing around the rear area reacted to his body heat, spread rapidly toward him, and drew warmth from his legs until frostbite appeared.

Ceril understood the hazard. The mold fed on heat but could be destroyed by cold. He threw water from his canteen and froze it in the air with Shape Water. The ice struck the infestation, which shrank and died. With that solution available, the companions could clear its remnants from the surrounding walls rather than leave it ready to grow again. Vokenar offered to assist with freezing any remaining patches.

Searching among books that the mold and damp had mostly ruined, Red Caesar found a surviving Kaedonite equestrian manual. It described horse care and equestrian sports. Its immediate usefulness was limited in a world where horses had become scarce, but its value as a record of the old civilization was considerable. The party recovered it as a collector's item.

#### Scouting the granary

Kerben tried to inspect the interior through the damaged horse gate, but could not find a safe angle that revealed much. Ceril had a better view under another door. He saw ladders, the shadows of raised connecting platforms, barrels, and vegetation invading a corner, but could not yet see what was on the ceiling.

Ceril transformed into a scorpion and crawled beneath the door. From inside, he discovered clusters of [[Sturges]] hanging overhead, sensitive to the smell of blood. His small form passed unnoticed. He returned to the party, resumed his ordinary shape, and reported what he had seen.

Red Caesar proposed entering inside Otiluke's Resilient Sphere. If the creatures crowded around its impenetrable surface, the others could attack them without exposing him to the swarm. They discussed how blood might attract the sturges, while arranging to approach through different doors. Vokenar supported the plan, and the party put it into practice.

Red Caesar entered within the sphere. Kerben came through another doorway and shot into a sturge cluster. His shots wounded the creatures, but also left him exposed inside the room. Domyx used the pulling power of the [[Iklwa Isondo]] to draw his consenting companion safely back through the doorway, then closed it behind them. The trident's unusual function therefore helped protect an ally rather than merely pull an enemy closer.

#### The protected lure

The disturbed sturges converged on Red Caesar, and ochre jellies emerged from the upper grain stores. Several creatures surrounded the sphere and battered against it without understanding why they could not reach the person inside. Another jelly seeped toward the closed door.

Vokenar chose an altered Flame Strike combining ice and holy light, rather than burn down the building. The burst struck the gathered creatures around Red Caesar, whose sphere protected him from it as thoroughly as from their attacks. Ceril added moonlight and his starry arrows. He also used Wild Resurgence to renew his ability to take his starry form while the expedition continued.

The plan cleared much of the infestation, although it did not hold every creature in one place. A jelly slipped through the doorway, and Kerben's scimitar split it into two before he switched to his revolver. Domyx broke down the jellies at the entrance and kept them from overrunning Kerben. Vokenar dealt with another that turned toward him.

Red Caesar kept the sphere intact while creatures continued trying to reach him. Their persistence made it an effective lure despite the uncertainty over the original blood-scent proposal. By the chunk's close, the jellies had been dealt with, but the party was still clearing the remaining sturges inside and around the doorway.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_018/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Tickets and a noble's testimony

Ceril finished the sturges still gathered inside the granary with moonlight and his starry attacks. Red Caesar dropped his sphere once the room was clear. Kerben, Tango, and Domyx then destroyed the final swarm near the doorway, completing the fight. The party again continued without resting and searched the exposed peninsula.

Ceril found a human woman's body beneath dead brown mold in one corner. Her remains were badly affected by frost, and her clothing suggested that she had been a noble. A roll of tickets tucked into her garments bore the name of [[The Carrot Cake]]. Kerben had encountered tickets for that attraction before; the newly recovered ones were added to those already in the shared hold.

Red Caesar used [[Beryzoz's Teeth]] to question the corpse. The teeth entered her mouth and enabled her to speak, with the answers audible to the whole party. Asked how she had died, she said that something had fallen on her as the castle came apart. She had crawled into the corner, bleeding and waiting to join her already dead husband. The mold's later damage to her body was therefore distinct from the cause of her death.

She praised [[Alamar]], saying that their republic would not have become what it was without him. The discussion recalled how he had abdicated the crown and helped bring about a senate, transforming [[Kaedon]] into a republic involving its local syndicates. The [[Dancing Blades]], the church, and the Bloomshield family were among the groups involved in that change. The discovery expanded the party's understanding of Alamar beyond his present confinement and the history of his father.

The woman had lived in [[Taylin]], not far from the castle. Her household had spent much of its wealth making this final visit before a planned move to [[Esperanto]], leaving no hidden family treasure for the party to recover. She had also hoped to visit The Carrot Cake and travel through its attractions. The park reportedly used great magic to connect distant mountaintops, allowing visitors to see far-separated parts of the world. Her intended destination lay on a peak north of the castle but south of the elven lands. That account gave Kerben a more useful lead into [[Jack Harvey]]'s expanded amusement business.

For the final question, Red Caesar asked what she had seen before the fatal collapse. The woman remembered streaks of light crossing the sky, one and then another, followed by the castle breaking apart and water rushing in. Other people had evacuated before her; she had stayed behind to help her husband.

Her description stirred Ceril's memory of the lights he had seen while escaping through the mountains before the Cataclysm. The streaks had come from the eastern horizon, crossed the sky, and stopped somewhere farther away. The coincidence strengthened the possibility that those lights preceded, and perhaps caused, the disaster. Red Caesar suspected that falling sky material had struck the woman, but her testimony did not confirm that interpretation.

The conversation also placed Esperanto south of their current map. Kerben asked about its former attractions, including the museum connected to his earlier travels. Because its desert lands had been unusually low, the party inferred that the rising waters would have reached them early. They did not visit the site or directly establish what remained beneath the ocean.

#### The hollow castle and another ambush

With that peninsula explored, the party turned toward the eastern cliff edge. Smooth white rock dropped into the sea, and disconnected sections of land stood farther south on pillars heavily encrusted with barnacles and seaweed. Water walking alone would not solve the route, because they first had to descend from the cliffs and find a way back up.

Kerben used his stone-sensing awareness to look for climbing holds and a reasonable path. It revealed more than access routes: substantial hollow spaces lay beneath their feet. Much of the castle might be buried below the surface, suggesting that the exposed grounds were only part of the complex they would eventually need to enter.

His sensing also picked up movement early enough to warn the others. Two water elementals rushed from the ocean up the sides of the island. Ceril noticed that apparently intact statues beside the path were really [[Animated Armor]], unlike the rusted remains elsewhere. Psychic goops also became threats as the encounter unfolded.

Red Caesar remembered how the earlier castle guardians had been held together by magic and dispelled one suit of armor. Ceril did the same to the other. Both collapsed without a prolonged fight, confirming that active enchantment sustained them.

Vokenar raised a freezing barrier to slow the water elementals. Kerben shattered one partly frozen creature with his gunfire, while Domyx held the other in the dangerous cold and prevented it from escaping toward the party. The psychic goops repeated their mental assaults. Red Caesar struck several with lightning, but the pain they sent back left him badly hurt; Ceril healed him and used starry attacks that avoided the same magical retaliation.

The companions finally destroyed the remaining elemental and goops. Unlike after the graveyard and granary encounters, they now chose to rest before advancing farther. Their next route led around the castle's broken edges toward isolated platforms and the entrance to its deeper spaces.

#### Dragons rising from the sea

Ceril sent [[Nyquil]], his owl familiar, to inspect the scattered structures. The report suggested a way into the castle to the north and further broken rooms beneath partly collapsed ceilings. Vokenar used his temporary wings to scout overhead while the others climbed along the edge and onto the interior of an exposed section.

From the air, Vokenar noticed ripples in water that had been unusually still. A dark shape formed below, then a young black dragon burst upward in a spiraling rush, accompanied by four wyrmlings. They shed the water and unfurled their wings above the disconnected islands.

Ceril recognized them as likely descendants of the larger black dragon the party had encountered earlier. The young dragon also referred to its father's knowledge of their ship and suggested finishing what that earlier encounter had begun. The threat was therefore linked to the party's old enemy, rather than an entirely unrelated creature appearing at the castle.

Vokenar opened with a radiant Sacred Spear that struck all five dragons. Domyx used the returning Iklwa Isondo to bring down two of the wyrmlings. Red Caesar tried Charm Monster on the young dragon, but failed to turn it aside. Kerben attacked from behind a pillar, drawing the dragon's attention. Its acid breath ate through the stone cover and injured him.

Domyx enlarged himself so that he could contest the dragon more directly, but failed to grapple it. The creature pushed past him and moved toward Ceril. It announced that its father would be pleased to find the castle and proposed it as a new haunt. Its remarks also identified the accompanying wyrmlings as its own children, making the group part of a continuing draconic lineage.

Ceril formed a wall of fire around the confined space where the dragon had advanced. The partly collapsed ceiling and the party's position limited its escape. Domyx could attack through his companion's flames, while Kerben moved in and out with his blades and Red Caesar helped protect Ceril when the trapped creature tried to break his hold on the spell.

The remaining wyrmlings were killed, and the young dragon weakened inside the burning corridor. It nevertheless remained alive at the end of the chunk. Kerben had withdrawn toward a siege engine in the room, while Ceril maintained the barrier keeping their opponent contained.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_018/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### The dragon's last attack

As Kerben moved near the [[Mangonel]], the castle's magic briefly animated it. The machine turned on him and fired, striking him before its enchantment faded. He was startled and hurt, but remained able to fight. The attack also demonstrated that the mechanism still worked. Kerben considered recovering it for [[The Opal]] after the exploration, although the party did not move it aboard during this session.

The dragon gathered another breath of acid and directed it at Ceril. His shield could not withstand the blast, and he fell unconscious. The creature then turned its attention toward Vokenar, mocking the elf it had just brought down.

Vokenar used Aura of Vitality to restore Ceril while Domyx struck the final blow. The young dragon fell as Ceril regained consciousness. In the same closing moment, Domyx returned to his ordinary size and Vokenar's temporary flight ended, his wings disappearing once he was safely over solid ground. These changes marked the end of their temporary transformations rather than a permanent loss of their powers.

The party gathered around the defeated creature and accepted further healing. The larger dragon associated with the earlier attack on their ship had not been killed here; this victory was over its offspring and the accompanying wyrmlings.

#### Dragon craft and peaceful remnants

Domyx asked whether Kerben could make anything useful from the dragon's remains. Kerben examined the larger carcass, noting the resilience of its scales and the poor prospects for eating acid-tainted meat. Using recovered material, his poisoner's kit, and supplies from the shared hold, he prepared [[Preparation Melf]], a throwable flask of sticky acid that would continue burning until removed. The destroyed wyrmlings offered little suitable material.

The companions debated resting again, but chose to continue their exploration when play resumed. They searched the room before leaving its remaining questions for the next session.

Kerben found decorative fireworks packed in insulating wineskins beside the mangonel. They had come from [[Broy]] to Westerness in more peaceful times. Their presence suggested that the machine had once launched celebratory fireworks rather than serving solely as a siege weapon. The fireworks were recovered into the shared hold; moving the mangonel itself remained a plan.

Domyx wandered aside to catch his breath and brushed dust from a wall before leaning against it. That uncovered [[The Jewel of Alfheimer]], a framed landscape of [[Brimbolyn]] before the Cataclysm. It showed a lush jungle within a continent, with no surrounding floodwater. Parts of the scene resembled the preserved terrain the party had recently seen in the elven lands.

The signature identified the artist as [[Aramil Kiirnodel]], the former leader of Brimbolyn, and the painting's title was embossed on its frame. The party recognized it as a valuable surviving artwork and added it to the hold. The possibility that the elves might want it back did not result in an immediate sale or return.

A door on the room's north side remained arcane-locked. Kerben's magical keyring could deal with it when they chose to proceed, but they did not open it before the session ended. The party had defeated the dragon and found clues and treasures from the old world, while the deeper castle and its Penumbra remained ahead.

### Summary

- Ceril detected a graveyard ambush before it fully closed around the party. Vokenar turned several specters back into their graves, and the companions defeated the remaining spirits and a cistern-born elemental.
- The elemental engulfed Domyx before Vokenar destroyed it with his sword. Kerben resummoned Tango after the specters drained the bird's vitality.
- Ceril froze a heat-feeding brown mold infestation that had injured Red Caesar. An intact Kaedonite equestrian manual survived among damaged books.
- Red Caesar's sphere protected him while drawing sturges and jellies together in the larger granary; the party cleared them without destroying the building.
- A dead noble's body yielded The Carrot Cake tickets. Through Beryzoz's Teeth, she described her life in Taylin, planned travel, Alamar's contribution to the republic, and the castle's collapse after eastern sky-lights.
- Her testimony matched Ceril's earlier memory. The lights' connection to the Cataclysm remained a strong lead rather than a completed explanation.
- Kerben discovered hollow castle spaces underground and warned of another ambush. The party dispelled animated armor and defeated elementals and psychic goops before resting.
- A young black dragon and four wyrmlings attacked. Ceril fell to acid breath, Vokenar restored him, and Domyx killed the larger attacker; the original dragon remained distinct.
- Kerben crafted Preparation Melf and recovered Broyish fireworks. Domyx found Aramil Kiirnodel's painting, The Jewel of Alfheimer.
- The mangonel remained on site, and the northern arcane-locked door was unopened. No new Penumbra was recovered.

The expedition joined physical exploration with testimony from the civilization the party was uncovering. The horse manual, tickets, fireworks, and landscape preserved ordinary interests beyond the castle's present infestation. The dead woman's admiration for Alamar also gave the party a clearer account of the republic that followed the old monarchy. Her decision to remain with her husband during the evacuation made the catastrophe a personal loss as well as a historical event.

The same testimony advanced two continuing investigations. The Carrot Cake became a destination with a geographical lead and a history of magical travel, while the eastern streaks connected Ceril's own memory to another witness's account of the collapse. Neither clue supplied the full answer. The party inferred Esperanto's early flooding and suspected falling sky material at the castle, but did not verify those possibilities through a visit or a recovered fragment.

The threats became more varied as the party reached the broken interior. Their preparation defeated a granary infestation with little direct exposure, their understanding of enchantments let them dismantle guardians, and the dragon still came close to killing Ceril. Shared healing kept the expedition intact. At the close, the party had an unopened route deeper into the castle, more knowledge of the old world, and several new acquisitions, but its Penumbra objective remained unfinished.
