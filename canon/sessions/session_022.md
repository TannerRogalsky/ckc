# Session 022

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_022/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### The divided journey and the return to The Pit

The party carried out its plan to divide the immediate journey. [[Kerben]] captained [[The Opal]] toward [[The Pit]] with [[Domyx]] and [[Vokenar]], while [[Red Caesar]] took [[Ceril]] by Teleportation Circle to [[The White Drake]] to investigate [[Obould]]'s captivity. Ceril still objected to treating the rescue as their priority, but chose to accompany Red Caesar rather than remain aboard in frustration. He intended to keep voicing his concern without sabotaging the effort.

The transcript first followed the sailing group. After six days they reached dwarven territory, where Kerben kept his stolen [[Gheister]] discreetly away from people who might recognize it. [[Brim the Bullywog]] leapt ashore to explore, delighted by the room to move in a place unfamiliar to him. He intended to meet them again in The Pit.

The crew purchased the ship improvements for which it had reserved gold. A bulbous bow would help The Opal brush past or break through rocky hazards, opening routes too dangerous for its previous structure. Hardened sails would improve its travel. The work required a stay in port, during which the companions could visit the city and market.

Vokenar went to [[The Palace of the Pit]] to ask [[Figma Brickfinger]] how the freed deep-world dwarves were settling into the community. The palace was busy with expansion, and the city was widening roads and laying tracks for minecarts and trains. Figma Brickfinger described an unsettled but workable process: small conflicts persisted, yet the newcomers wanted to rebuild their lives, and cooperation had opened underground space formerly constrained by rivalry. She preferred these manageable disputes to the old confrontation between the two populations.

Vokenar offered help if needed and explained that the crew was preparing to travel toward the [[Broyish Empire]]. Figma Brickfinger had not personally seen its airships, but further scout reports placed imperial aircraft in the surrounding region. Their presence was continuing, even if they appeared to avoid The Pit itself.

The market also reflected the integration. Deep-world dwarves brought different skills to existing shops, and [[Sigrid Forgewelt]] had readily employed them. She had held no enmity toward them before their liberation and saw no reason to refuse their help now.

Domyx brought his star gem, a ruby, and gold to Sigrid Forgewelt for a weapon improvement. She used the unusual material to produce the [[Gith Shard Glaive]], an enchanted halberd whose cleaving power could project a bolt of psychic energy at a more distant opponent. This extended his reach beyond ordinary close combat. The new halberd became part of his expanding equipment.

Domyx also exchanged the castle's equestrian materials for enchanted javelins, having first read the manuals. Sigrid Forgewelt valued that information because the dwarves had hatched bulettes and were using the animals to pull equipment. The treasure recovered from an old arena thus found a practical use in the city's new industry.

Vokenar wanted equipment that supported his spellcasting more than another weapon. Sigrid Forgewelt provided [[Sunset's Solace]], a shield with an embedded ruby that could store magic cast into it for later use. It initially held Slow, offering a capability beyond his usual healing and protective spells. During travel he could replace stored magic with preparations suited to the next destination.

Vokenar also began considering how to use Divine Intervention to hallow different parts of The Opal. The ship improvements and equipment purchases were established here; the precise wards he would place aboard were explained later.

#### Lady Jacinthe's refusal

The narrative then returned to the other group's parallel journey. Red Caesar and Ceril arrived through The White Drake's teleportation chamber, with Ceril wearing a bear mask rather than concealing himself in an animal form. [[Damien Ouranous]] greeted Red Caesar and met his unfamiliar companion.

Red Caesar asked for an audience with [[Lady Jacinthe]] about a personal matter involving their captain. Damien Ouranous warned that she was inconsolable and receiving no visitors, but agreed to inquire. He returned with permission for them to stay and with a message: they should, for all practical purposes, treat Obould as deceased. She would not discuss him, and the mention of his name had reportedly brought renewed weeping.

The message did not verify Obould's death. It conflicted with the living prisoner Vokenar had recently observed, leaving the visitors uncertain what Lady Jacinthe knew or why she would not speak to them. Damien Ouranous gave them the key to a guest room and promised that they would be told if she became willing to receive them.

Red Caesar and Ceril considered using invisibility to reach her quarters. Before casting it, Ceril made an ordinary reconnaissance of the ship. With his conspicuous equipment left in the room, he could blend into the movement of refugees and servants. He found guards at corners, a guarded staircase, locked doors above it, and people near Lady Jacinthe who appeared to be trusted retainers from her former life as a princess. Her quarters were difficult to enter even for an unseen intruder.

Ceril therefore turned toward obtaining a personal object for Scrying. He followed the laundry workers and overheard that Lady Jacinthe was sending for wine more urgently than clothes. Her sommelier had been kept busy trying to meet her requests.

Ceril took clothes from the laundry and used them to look like another member of the staff. In the wine cellar he approached an elderly half-orc sommelier who was hurriedly assembling alternatives to the particular drink he could not find. Ceril claimed the lady wanted more immediately and asked for a bottle she had enjoyed previously.

The sommelier produced a partly consumed bottle of prized brandy from [[Taylin]], associated with Lady Jacinthe's earlier life. When asked his name, Ceril gave a false crew identity and persuaded the distracted man that he belonged there. He took the bottle back to Red Caesar instead of delivering it upstairs.

Ceril explained the defenses around Lady Jacinthe and proposed using the brandy to reach her through divination. For his first Scrying he established mercury in his shield as the reflective focus. The attempt failed when Lady Jacinthe resisted observation, and they saw no useful scene.

Red Caesar proposed strengthening the next attempt with a likeness of their target. A portrait somewhere aboard could provide what they needed, so he left the room to find one.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_022/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Stealing a likeness and witnessing the ultimatum

Red Caesar found a portrait showing Lady Jacinthe christening The White Drake. He asked a guard to let him borrow it for his bare guest room, but the guard refused to move a priceless painting. The visitors would have to obtain it by another means.

Red Caesar went to a lounge and made a show of struggling with a porthole before casting Thunderwave against it. The blast shattered nearby windows, overturned furniture, and drew guards from the staircase. Ceril added a Fog Cloud that could pass for mist entering through the broken glass, then took the unguarded portrait.

While carrying it back by a less direct route, Ceril met Damien Ouranous. He claimed that the guards had asked him to move the painting away from possible damage. Damien Ouranous believed the explanation and lent his own cape to cover Lady Jacinthe's favorite artwork. His help protected what he thought was the painting's safe removal; he did not knowingly authorize its theft.

Damien Ouranous then found Red Caesar amid the shattered windows. Red Caesar acknowledged responsibility, paid for the damage with Kaedonite coins, and helped clean the lounge. While he was occupied, Ceril checked the borrowed cape for tracking magic. It carried conjuration, but gave him no indication that it was a trap.

With the portrait and brandy together, Ceril's second Scrying succeeded. He found Lady Jacinthe in her quarters, composed rather than continually weeping. She went to the door to cry loudly for the people outside, while the newly delivered wine stood untouched. Her apparent inconsolability was a performance intended to keep others away.

Once satisfied that she had privacy, Lady Jacinthe called her visitor back. A magical duplicate of [[Vizier Jade]] appeared. Lady Jacinthe asked after Obould, and Vizier Jade confirmed that he was alive and being fed, although unhappy.

The exchange exposed the price of his safety. Lady Jacinthe offered to continue reporting the party's movements and directing imperial attention toward Vokenar and Ceril, who were searching for [[Penumbra]]. Her scouts could help, but she feared the party would eventually discover her betrayal.

Vizier Jade cared about the information and the stones, not how Lady Jacinthe maintained her relationships. She demanded cooperation even if Lady Jacinthe had to abandon her pretense. Obould's safety was guaranteed for only a month; useful information could buy more time, but afterward she would promise nothing.

Lady Jacinthe warned that, if Obould were harmed, she and her allies would die trying to bring down Vizier Jade and the Empire. After the projection departed, she wondered aloud whether they could reach their destination within the month. Her staged grief had concealed negotiations, but her concern for Obould was real.

Ceril told Red Caesar everything he had seen. The revelation connected the rescue directly to the Penumbra mission he had thought it was distracting them from. The League's leader was being pressed to redirect their discoveries into imperial hands.

The two briefly considered retaliation against Lady Jacinthe. They did not attack her: doing so would make the League an enemy and would hardly serve Obould's interests. Red Caesar also reflected on Alamar's abdication and whether one good ruler might govern better than a divided group, without settling that political question.

Before leaving, Ceril wrote a full account for Damien Ouranous and concealed the message with the portrait, expecting him to find it after their departure. They did not wait for a response or establish that he had already read it. Red Caesar then opened a teleportation circle to [[Brimbolyn]].

#### House Kiirnodel and the elven market

They arrived in [[House Kiirnodel]]'s flower-filled courtyard inside a protective sphere. [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] challenged the unexpected visitors, while [[Feronia Caeradel]] recognized Ceril and his human companion. Rizolvir Kiirnodel recalled Ceril as one of the old world's survivors they had helped save, and Feronia Caeradel lowered the barrier.

Their possession of the circle's rarely distributed key troubled Rizolvir Kiirnodel, but Red Caesar offered no clear explanation of how they had obtained it. Instead, he reported on [[Boril Erendel]] at [[The Garden]] and the ideas entering the [[Demi-Spell]].

Rizolvir Kiirnodel said Boril Erendel had recently contributed nearly a hundred pages. As a political representative, he could not fully judge their magical content. Red Caesar urged him to have the work examined, describing Boril Erendel's isolation and extreme ideas. Rizolvir Kiirnodel offered to send an expedition to bring him back, but Red Caesar emphasized review of the writing rather than insisting on his removal.

Feronia Caeradel suggested that [[Keys Caeradel]] could examine it, though he was already overwhelmed with work. Rizolvir Kiirnodel wanted to hear Boril Erendel's side and might agree with some of it. The conversation established concern and possible responses, without showing an expedition dispatched or the offending work changed.

At the [[Elvish Marketplace]], Red Caesar sought mobility, magic, and gifts for the party. He purchased [[Cloaks of Billowing]] for all his companions, allowing their cloaks to move dramatically at will.

He traded [[The Jewel of Alfheimer]], the landscape recovered from Castle Kaedon, for [[Boots of the Alvargard]]. The boots improved his movement, and the elves welcomed the return of the painting. This exchange occurred with the elven market's sellers.

Ceril acquired the [[Branch of the Itinerant]], a wand-like focus that strengthened his spellcasting and could make flowers bloom along a path he indicated. They also arranged mithril plate armor for the absent Vokenar, designed to let him move more fluidly and avoid danger while protected. Red Caesar's search for spell scrolls remained unsatisfied by the markets they discussed.

#### Reunion and Vokenar's revelations

The improved Opal sailed from dwarven territory toward The White Drake, and Red Caesar and Ceril returned by teleportation to reunite with their companions nearby. They reported Lady Jacinthe's negotiations and Vizier Jade's threat. By this reunion, the party reckoned that about twenty-one days remained in the stated month, making the next route a matter of urgency.

Vokenar explained the divine protections he had established during the group's separation. He had hallowed different parts of The Opal, warding them against entry by celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead, and against the fear, charm, or possession those creatures could inflict. The common area allowed people to understand one another through Tongues, while the deck strengthened courage. Darkness protected the valuables in the hold by making unauthorized searching more difficult. The kitchen protected its occupants against fire, and the crow's nest offered protection against lightning, for which [[Raxxy]] thanked him. These effects helped the people aboard; they did not make the whole ship's structure immune to fire.

On other days Vokenar had used Legend Lore to investigate [[Genesis Mundi]] and [[Starfall]]. The first revelation confirmed that Genesis Mundi was a natural renewal of reality that would eventually occur without mortal intervention. Each recurrence erased the reality and magic by which its earlier cycles might be investigated, so even the divination could not count them. Certain important threads could return: souls, structures, or shapes of land might reappear in the next world.

The Demi-Spell was a means of bringing that event sooner and changing its parameters. It could guide the renewed world toward particular rules or resemblance to the old one. The underlying event and the spell that influenced it remained distinct.

The revelation about Starfall contradicted the earlier account that it had fallen from the heavens. It had been created in Hell to free demons into the world above and could pierce the membranes dividing worlds, barriers comparable to the protection of a god's body. This explained how it could wound Aeris and strongly supported the conclusion that it had caused [[The Cataclysm]].

Starfall had been brought out of Hell centuries earlier and placed in [[Broy]]. Divination concealed who had moved it and why. It now used Penumbra for the enormous energy it required. An earlier use of souls as fuel was considered, but was less firmly established than the weapon's present dependence on Penumbra.

The revelations sharpened the political stakes. The League and Empire had fought long before the search for the stones; Obould's captivity now gave the Empire a way to use that older conflict to obtain the fuel for a weapon capable of harming the divine world.

#### David Harvey's request

During the reunion at The White Drake, a small, orange [[Harengon]] approached Domyx and introduced himself as [[David Harvey]]. He worked with [[The League of New Stark]] to restore a home for his people, whose numbers and settlements had been devastated by The Cataclysm.

David Harvey asked the party to capture his cousin [[Theo Harvey]] alive. Theo Harvey had disclosed the Harengon's hidden warrens to the Empire and joined its forces, which now occupied the island above them. David Harvey and Lady Jacinthe had assembled a large bounty for his return as a prisoner, so killing him would defeat the purpose of the task. The League wanted to learn what he had told the Empire.

The warrens went deep below the old sea level and connected to tunnels near [[Esperanto]]. Some Harengon still lived in that larger network. They had begun developing it while more closely aligned with the Empire, then turned to the League. The tunnels did not yet provide a route into imperial territory, although Domyx hoped investigating them might reveal useful shortcuts.

Asked about [[Jack Harvey]], David Harvey identified him as his great-uncle. He had not followed Jack Harvey's theatrical ambitions and knew little about what remained of [[40 Karats]]. Theo Harvey was a better prospect for that information: he had begun as a traveling minstrel and wanted an empire of spectacle like Jack Harvey's before pursuing piracy and imperial patronage.

David Harvey praised Lady Jacinthe for protecting his displaced people, many of whom were now stranded on boats. His account added the dependence of that community to the party's knowledge of her coercion.

Domyx also placed his own mountain homeland north of the elven lands on the map. The party then considered the southern winds, possible shopping stops, and the need to head east. Red Caesar insisted on the eastern course, while Domyx argued for the warrens island as a nearby investigation that might improve their route and answer Kerben's interest in Jack Harvey. They chose to visit it before continuing toward Broy.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_022/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### The island's invisible defenders

After further hearing how much the Harengon relied on the League's protection, the party sailed for the [[Harengon Warrens]] island. Ceril recognized it as one of the islands he had observed and charted from the place where he had survived a previous shipwreck. He had not known that tunnels beneath these scattered landforms could connect surviving communities.

The surface seemed unexpectedly empty. Thick-trunked trees stood among high grass and brush, without an obvious imperial camp or entrance to the warrens. Red Caesar used Locate Creature to find a nearby Harengon. The presence was above ground ahead of them, yet no one was visible.

Red Caesar warned the others that an unseen opponent was waiting. Vokenar heard more movement and saw sand disturbed by several invisible figures adjusting their positions. The party detected the prepared ambush before fully walking into it.

Red Caesar opened the fight with Fireball in the area of movement. A soldier fell and became visible, but others continued maneuvering unseen. Red Caesar enlarged Domyx, whose new glaive let him pressure opponents through both close strikes and projected psychic force.

The imperial scouts and marshals concentrated their guns and blades on Red Caesar and Ceril, attempting to remove the spellcasters instead of wasting their effort on Domyx. Red Caesar's shields and Vokenar's protection helped withstand the firing. As the soldiers attacked, their invisibility fell away.

Theo Harvey directed them, then introduced himself as the bearer of Jack Harvey's unfinished ambitions. His rapid speech and distracting movements tried to sway Red Caesar, but failed to draw him away from the party. He also tried to sell the appeal of a freer pirate life, only to hear that Red Caesar already considered himself a pirate.

Vokenar summoned a celestial defender from a locket that opened to release light. The manifested ally took the form of a warrior on a winged horse, protecting and supporting the party while attacking a marshal. Ceril defended himself and brought radiant magic against Theo Harvey.

Red Caesar then used Steelwind Strike to move through several enemies as a flash of blue-white magical energy. The attack killed soldiers and badly hurt Theo Harvey, who remained alive and visibly impressed. Domyx continued pressing the other defenders, reducing their position further.

#### Capturing Theo Harvey and stopping the retreat

The party still needed Theo Harvey alive. Red Caesar enclosed him in a resilient sphere formed from a strand of [[Para and Bellum]]'s magic, which hardened around him before he could escape. Maintaining the sphere ended the enlargement of Domyx, who returned to his ordinary size.

Theo Harvey struck against the inside of the barrier and demanded release, but Red Caesar told him to wait. With their leader trapped, the surviving imperial soldiers decided to retreat to a nearby longboat rather than continue defending his plan.

Red Caesar recognized the large yellow circles on their capes as imperial insignia. Vokenar argued that allowing them to return east would reveal too much of the party's magic and capabilities. Ceril agreed and surrounded the retreating soldiers with a ring of fire before they could reach their escape.

Vokenar added divine fire to the enclosure, and his celestial defender approached the survivors. The last soldier burned to death. The imperial detachment was destroyed, with Theo Harvey the surviving captive.

Theo Harvey began bargaining from the sphere. He offered to double the League's payment and exchange valuable knowledge of the Empire for his freedom. Red Caesar initially answered that they intended to return him to The White Drake, then turned to searching for the warrens.

Vokenar's See Invisibility revealed displaced earth that had been magically hidden beside a tree. Working around it, the group uncovered a hole beneath a wooden panel disguised by illusion. The spell exposed the invisible earth; it did not itself reveal every part of the separate illusion. They had found the entrance, but did not descend into the network or establish a new route through it.

Theo Harvey described David Harvey's settlement plan as doomed. If the waters kept rising, he argued, the entrances would eventually flood, and his people would be safer working for the Empire. This was his assessment, not an outcome the party had verified.

He also criticized the League's loose alliances and charity. In his view the Empire had power, jobs, and a use for Harengon skills, while the League would eventually collapse for lack of contribution. He urged the party to hear the imperial side before handing him to people he feared would torture or kill him. His warnings did not establish the League's actual treatment of captives.

The party saw some practical value in carrying him east, without accepting his trustworthiness. Red Caesar considered him a bargaining chip because the Empire had committed soldiers to supporting him. Domyx warned that his quick tongue could turn any arrangement against them. Vokenar doubted that Theo Harvey would help, but also doubted they had time to backtrack to deliver him.

Their interest in Jack Harvey gave the prisoner another opening. Theo Harvey said he had dedicated his life to his grandfather's projects and whispered to Vokenar that The Karat Cake was alive and well, timing the revelation with the sphere's expiration. It was a claim that the old park survived, rather than a location or proof from the party's own exploration.

They decided to take him toward Broy as a prisoner. The bounty depended on delivery to David Harvey and the League, which they had not accomplished.

A search found little else of value on the island. Theo Harvey said the Harengon had taken their few supplies onto linked longboats and were waiting at sea until David Harvey could tell them it was safe to return. They might have drifted toward Cutlass Cray, but he did not know that they had reached it. The party discussed collecting plant seeds, without establishing a new acquisition.

#### A prisoner aboard The Opal

Theo Harvey immediately made custody a social problem as well as a physical one. Aboard The Opal he approached crew members and presented himself as someone the party had hired. [[Yalet Mora]] found him pleasant and accepted that explanation until Red Caesar corrected it: Theo Harvey was a prisoner who had tried to kill them.

Raxxy distrusted him and offered to keep watch at the brig. The crew had little established practice for holding prisoners, so careful guard duty mattered. Red Caesar considered the ship itself a useful barrier while at sea, but feared that a visit to a crowded port would create new opportunities for Theo Harvey to disappear.

Yalet Mora proposed leaving The Opal anchored offshore while a longboat carried the shoppers into Cutlass Cray. Red Caesar preferred to postpone the stop. He used Control Winds to drive them onward, and the party sailed east with its valuables and captive still aboard.

Several days passed without ending their concern. Theo Harvey repeatedly tried to win over crew members or behaved in ways that looked like preparations for escape. [[Mobley]] even enlisted him to help make carrot stew, and the prisoner adopted a cook's outfit while moving cheerfully around the work. His friendliness did not change his status or show that the crew had agreed to release him.

As they approached the eastern coast, rocks and long promontories made the water hazardous. The new bulbous bow brushed past and broke through obstacles that might otherwise have forced The Opal to turn back. The ship still scraped in places, but the improvements had made this approach possible.

An unnatural, nearly white pillar of light appeared on the horizon, different from the diffuse orange illumination of Brimbolyn. During Ceril's watch, Theo Harvey spoke from behind the bars and urged him to admire the human realm, presenting it as a new future superior to the old world's projects.

The Opal drew nearer to the coastline. Red Caesar remembered Vizier Jade's earlier assurance that he could visit safely, but the party had not yet tested what that promise meant for all of them. The session ended before anyone intercepted them or admitted them farther into imperial territory.

### Summary

- The party divided between sailing to The Pit and teleporting to The White Drake. Ceril accompanied Red Caesar despite his continuing objection to prioritizing Obould's rescue.
- The Opal received a bulbous bow and hardened sails. Domyx acquired the Gith Shard Glaive and enchanted javelins, while Vokenar acquired Sunset's Solace.
- Figma Brickfinger described progress in deep-world integration and industrial expansion, alongside continuing reports of imperial aircraft.
- Ceril obtained Lady Jacinthe's brandy and portrait for Scrying. The successful observation revealed her concealed negotiations with Vizier Jade, who threatened Obould's safety after a month unless she helped locate Penumbra.
- At House Kiirnodel, Red Caesar warned about Boril Erendel's contributions to the Demi-Spell. At the elven market he traded The Jewel of Alfheimer for Boots of the Alvargard and bought Cloaks of Billowing; Ceril acquired the Branch of the Itinerant, and the group arranged mithril armor for Vokenar.
- Vokenar hallowed The Opal and used Legend Lore to distinguish natural Genesis Mundi from the Demi-Spell. His Starfall divination established its infernal origin, capacity to pierce divine barriers, and use of Penumbra.
- David Harvey offered a bounty for Theo Harvey's live return. The party defeated Theo Harvey's imperial allies, captured him, and found the warrens entrance without exploring its tunnels.
- Theo Harvey claimed that The Karat Cake survived and offered imperial connections. The party carried him east as a guarded prisoner, leaving his delivery to the League incomplete.
- Concern over his escape led the crew to skip Cutlass Cray. The session closed on the approach to Broy's dangerous coastline, where the new bow proved its worth.

The divided journey brought the rescue and divine mission back together. Obould's captivity was the leverage by which Vizier Jade sought Penumbra, so the crew could not regard the captain's situation as entirely separate from repairing the sky. Lady Jacinthe's apparent refusal to help concealed both coercion and betrayal: she was trying to preserve Obould while exposing the party's movements. Ceril's investigation uncovered that connection, and he shared it fully with Red Caesar.

The preparation also produced lasting changes. The Opal became faster, more capable of crossing hazardous water, and protected by Vokenar's sanctified spaces. New equipment broadened the companions' abilities. The divinations clarified the distinction between a natural renewal of reality and the spell that could accelerate and direct it, while revealing why imperial demand for Penumbra threatened more than a contest over resources.

Theo Harvey complicated the eastward voyage. Capturing him served the League's request, but taking him to Broy substituted possible leverage and information for immediate delivery. His claim about The Karat Cake linked the encounter to Kerben's older interests, while his success in charming crew members exposed the weakness of relying on the sea alone as a prison. At the close, Obould remained captive, the League's bounty remained uncollected, and the party had reached imperial waters with a useful but unreliable prisoner.
