# Session 029

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_029/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### A warning from the ship

At [[The Carrot Cake]]'s main rotunda, the companions could see the result of their work in the [[Hole Shebang]]: one lamp beside [[Jack Harvey]]'s sealed gate was lit. They prepared to enter the [[Magic Hat]], hoping the hotel would provide a safe place to recover.

Before they departed, [[Kerben]] felt his entrance alarm activate. [[Ulrich Fjoller]] and [[Raxxy]] hurried in from [[The Opal]] with a warning. Ulrich Fjoller had seen a balloon in the sky, and [[Broyish Empire]] scouts were near the island. Unless the ship moved, they risked being found in the surrounding waters.

Raxxy thought the Empire might simply be checking many places with its extensive forces. [[Red Caesar]] was less reassured. [[Vizier Jade]] had previously sent [[The Tyrant]] to find the park, and it had never returned. That earlier search might have left the Empire with a particular interest in this region, even if the park's limited opening had prevented an earlier expedition from entering.

The party could not establish how much the Empire knew. Their protections against scrying complicated any attempt to track them, and a glimpse of an unfamiliar interior would not automatically identify its location. Nevertheless, the ship's layered magic and Kerben's protections offered no reason to linger needlessly.

Kerben initially offered to continue through the park alone while the others handled the vessel. [[Vokenar]] argued that his skills would be more useful at the helm. Kerben accepted that division of responsibility, healed [[Domyx]] before leaving, and gave Vokenar his unused healing potion. He departed with Ulrich Fjoller, Raxxy, and [[Zulu]], who was reluctant to abandon the games. Red Caesar, Vokenar, Domyx, and [[Ceril]] continued into the hotel district.

#### The fortune teller and the card dealer

The portal resembled a top hat. Beyond it, matching portals connected the Magic Hat to the park's other districts. A checkerboard-tiled path crossed a carefully landscaped courtyard, where artificial turf had cracked and real plants had grown through. Water features reflected green, purple, and orange lights, with the carrot emblem's orange glow dominating the scene.

Two kiosks stood beside the route. In one, a rabbitlike [[Magen]] endlessly shuffled cards. The other housed a broken fortune-telling machine. Vokenar examined its finely made automaton: porcelain skin, onyx eyes, citrine and ruby ornamentation, and a large imitation turban. A label identified it as [[Boston Golf]].

Red Caesar noticed its striking resemblance to [[Rochella Golf]]. The figure appeared to portray a member of the old acting company, and a family connection seemed possible, though the resemblance did not settle the relationship. The party collected the valuable figurine from the ruined machine, intending to show it to Rochella Golf.

The Magen dealer invited Red Caesar to a game of skill and judgment. Players drew from an unevenly weighted deck and tried to approach forty without exceeding it. Dealer and visitors used the same deck, and the dealer allowed several companions to play against him together.

Red Caesar took a blank page from his spellbook and worked out probabilities before the game began. He shared the strategy with the others. Vokenar contributed experience from the complicated chess played in [[Arkadia]], helping Red Caesar think through the wagering rules and opportunities to draw blindly.

Ceril objected to the enterprise on moral grounds and refused to participate. Red Caesar insisted that he was pursuing a sure result rather than gambling, but the eventual play still required judgment under uncertainty. His calculations carried the group through most of the contest; a fortunate low draw for the dealer also helped preserve one narrow win.

The party won all seven rounds. A single scroll printed out as its prize, carrying six divine spells. Those identified in the conversation included Inflict Wounds, Warding Bond, Beacon of Hope, and Locate Creature, along with powerful healing magic. Red Caesar could not learn that clerical magic himself, so he gave the scroll to Vokenar.

The defeated dealer theatrically raised a revolver to its head. Pulling the trigger produced a little flag bearing the carrot emblem rather than an injury. Its performance ended the game with another flourish of the park's unsettling humor.

#### Enemies beneath the water

While the others played, Ceril kept his attention on the party's larger purpose: reaching the substantial [[Penumbra]] Jack Harvey had hidden away. Looking past the courtyard ponds' reflections, he noticed figures stirring below the surface.

One was undead; three were living humans maintained underwater by supernatural protection. Ceril recognized the pattern of mortals serving a vampire as familiars. Their apparent water-breathing and unnatural agility suggested protective spells from a master, though that master did not appear in the courtyard.

Ceril attacked with Moonbeam before they emerged and warned the others. His starry arrows and Vokenar's radiance weakened the human familiars, while Domyx stood between them and Ceril. Domyx's magnetic greaves drew a thrown dagger away from Ceril. Red Caesar used disruptive magic against the attackers, and the companions overcame the familiars and the accompanying [[Mummy]].

The party briefly considered whether the mortal attackers might be saved or questioned. That possibility soon vanished. A second water feature bubbled, releasing three tiny, brightly colored [[Flame Skull]]s and a [[Gorgon Head]]. The skulls were fiendish creatures despite their undead appearance; the gorgon was a living monstrosity, a woman's head wreathed in snakes with more serpentine material where a neck should have joined a body.

The newcomers seemed momentarily confused, then attacked. Ceril recognized the gorgon's poisonous rays and the danger of its petrifying gaze. Domyx's [[Gith Shard Glaive]] sent its striking energy far enough to reach the airborne skulls, letting him pressure threats beyond ordinary melee.

Red Caesar and Domyx recalled how well their different fighting styles worked together. Their bond had grown from fighting back to back aboard the Opal, and Red Caesar's first use of enlargement on Domyx had helped turn an initially slower friendship into a close partnership.

The Flame Skulls made the courtyard far more dangerous. Red Caesar countered one fireball, but two others exploded among the companions. Vokenar was badly injured, and the blasts destroyed the fallen familiars' bodies, removing any remaining chance to question them.

Vokenar retaliated with a cold fireball. His [[Moon Blade]] stripped away the enemies' protection against the cold, leaving the skulls frozen and weakened. Ceril finished them with Moonbeam, then used his starry chalice's healing to restore Vokenar. He also tended Domyx as the party concentrated on the surviving gorgon.

Domyx repeatedly struck the head with his glaive while Vokenar shielded allies against its poison and Red Caesar pressed it with magic. Finally Red Caesar placed his blades beside its head and sent an electrical shock through it. The gorgon burst apart.

Its death released a black-and-white wave across the courtyard. Domyx and Vokenar began stiffening into stone. The enemies were defeated, but the petrification remained unresolved as the chunk ended.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_029/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Recovery and hospitality

Vokenar placed a hand over his heart and used Lesser Restoration before the petrification became complete. Light pulsed through him, clearing the effect.

Domyx had to resist the transformation himself. Stone climbed across his body, and he briefly pretended to have become a complete statue. Then he burst free through flying fragments of marble. He remained himself, with no lasting petrification established.

The party took a short rest in the courtyard. While the others recovered, Red Caesar investigated the hotel's entrance, still wearing his salvaged Hooksy mask. It was now night. Above the canyon walls, stars remained visible, but the shattered moon no longer supplied its former light.

A Magen maître d' stood at the door beside another lamplighter switch. It welcomed the party as paying guests whose tickets entitled them to free rooms. According to the attendant, strange intruders had entered the park without paying, and it had barred the hotel against them. It thanked the companions for clearing some of the threats outside and assured them that none waited within.

Red Caesar asked about the switch. The Magen explained that Jack Harvey had hurriedly installed these devices shortly before everything went wrong. They were a late addition, designed to release a sealed doorway when the park's power was redirected. Flipping this one would dim the hotel and interfere with its amenities, so the attendant recommended enjoying the rooms first.

Red Caesar agreed to wait. The maître d' used Knock to release the entrance's Arcane Lock and admitted the companions.

#### A night at the Magic Hat

Inside, a spiral staircase connected three floors and roughly thirty rooms. The hotel retained a chocolate fountain, soft-serve ice cream, shrimp cocktail, drinks, and a minibar. After their long passage through hostile attractions, the functioning comforts offered a plausible refuge.

The party searched while settling in. Ceril found a long purple-worm-hide rug edged with amethysts. Its valuable material suggested a southern origin for this part of the resort, but did not establish its exact location. He also collected tobacco from a magically preserved box. The leaves came from a hybrid of tobacco and carrot; the preservation ended when the container was opened.

Vokenar relaxed and sent an Arcane Eye through the rooms. It discovered a gold-trimmed periodical lying open on a bed: [[Rahmadi's Capers]], issue four, a hand-drawn comic about the adventures of the goblin rogue [[Rahmadi]]. They learned of a collector willing to trade for a complete set, though no such collection or exchange was completed here.

The rug, tobacco, and comic joined the party's shared treasure. With the Opal away, those goods were carried with the adventurers rather than delivered physically to the ship.

The maître d' brought plates of favorite foods to the rooms, having read the visitors' thoughts to personalize its service. The hospitality was intrusive as well as attentive, but the promised safety held. The companions completed a long rest.

By morning, Kerben had sent an animal messenger. It reached the hotel and left its message with the maître d', who delivered it to the group. Kerben reported that the Imperial scouting was more deliberate than they had first supposed: the Empire was investigating this particular location. Red Caesar's suspicion had been justified. Kerben would remain outside for a while and urged them to finish their work quickly, preferably that day.

#### Breakfast and the second switch

Ceril prepared another Heroes' Feast as a continental breakfast, renewing the party's protection before they resumed the park's challenges. Red Caesar brewed healing potions in his cauldron and distributed them among the four companions, retaining two himself and giving Domyx two. The preparations would last only until the next sunrise.

During breakfast, Red Caesar considered whether willing animals could drink potions of fire breath and provide a disruptive diversion, perhaps during a later operation in [[Broy]]. The companions discussed possible ways to recruit or direct them, but created no animal force and carried out no such plan.

The maître d' grew eager to clear the rooms before checkout. It summoned unseen servants, renewed Guards and Wards, and locked the entrance as part of its housekeeping and security.

Having enjoyed the hotel, Red Caesar finally flipped its lamplighter switch. The attendant protested dramatically that it loved life, powered down, and then immediately powered up again. It revealed the apparent death as a joke: its own existence did not depend on the hotel's electricity.

The switch was the Magic Hat's contribution to the central gate. The party trusted that the diversion had advanced their objective and went directly to the next district rather than first returning to inspect the lamps.

#### The King of the Hole's rules

The companions chose [[King of the Hole]]. Its portal deposited them on a small, grassy island beneath the morning sun, with ocean on every side. The terraced hill and surrounding outcrops could be surveyed without a prolonged search.

A differently dressed Magen greeted them through a carrot-shaped voice amplifier. It recognized that they were switching the lamps to reach Jack Harvey's vault, unlike ordinary visitors who had come only to play.

The announcer asked their names and delivered prepared introductions containing personal knowledge about their backgrounds and purposes. Its presentation muddled some identities, but references to [[Master Lee]], [[Heaven's Bulb]], divine origins, and the quest to restore the heavens suggested more than casual observation. Red Caesar suspected that divination had supplied the information before their arrival. He did not determine the exact method.

The Magen explained that the arena's forces had been arranged by [[Farraday]], Jack Harvey's benefactor, who wanted the park to remain entertaining after the apocalypse. The attendant would place enemies to make each encounter difficult.

Five carrot-shaped beacons stood around the island. Starting a round required pressing three buttons simultaneously, and the same combination of three could not be reused. The arrangement forced the contestants to separate across the hill and neighboring ground before enemies appeared.

Red Caesar examined a beacon and found electrical lighting combined with several magical effects. Hoping to simplify the challenge, he asked whether Domyx could move one closer. Domyx pulled a beacon from the ground, but the announcer warned that carrying it too far would break the enchantment. Domyx put it back rather than risk losing their route to the vault.

The announcer clarified the larger bargain: four completed rounds would grant access to Jack Harvey's lamplighter. A fifth was optional. The contestants could rest between rounds or begin another immediately; overlapping challenges were allowed but carried no additional promised reward.

It also said that the fights would be recorded or projected through illusion for entertainment in the main square. Whether anyone was actually watching remained uncertain.

#### The first arena round

The companions spread to their chosen positions and pressed three beacons together. Carrot emblems formed summoning circles, bringing forth two Phase Cats, two Mummies, a Gorgon Head, and a Flame Skull.

Ceril took his starry dragon aspect and flew into a better position, then used guiding light and arrows to support Domyx against the cats. Domyx's glaive struck across the intervening ground. Their combined efforts eliminated both cats.

Red Caesar initially faced the Mummies alone. He shocked one and used Misty Step to reach a ledge, hoping the climb would slow them. They reached him despite the obstacle, and one briefly paralyzed him with its glare. His ward prevented the threatened draining effect from taking hold, and his shield magic protected him while he recovered.

The cats' teleportation and the arena's cliffs scattered the others. Vokenar called forth Spirit Guardians, appearing as small implements of the three goddesses: suns, moons, hammers, and blades within a rotating triangular glow. The magic hindered the gorgon near him. He also sent fire toward the Mummies to support Red Caesar across the battlefield.

Ceril was struck by the Flame Skull and healed himself. Domyx used Cloud Jaunt to reach the gorgon, then struck it and cleaved toward the distant skull. The round remained underway at the chunk boundary.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_029/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Finishing the first round

Red Caesar destroyed one Mummy and drank his last longstrider potion. Its increased speed let him draw away from the survivor. With Vokenar's Water Walk, he could race across the sea beside the island, leaving the slower undead struggling to pursue him.

The Flame Skull attempted a fireball against Domyx and Vokenar, but Red Caesar countered it. The gorgon injured Vokenar badly enough to disrupt his guardians, and he healed himself before continuing.

Ceril's guiding light and starry arrow destroyed the Gorgon Head. Domyx threw Iklwa Isondo at the airborne Flame Skull and reeled it closer, then shattered it with another throw. Vokenar's fire had weakened the remaining Mummy; Red Caesar returned, shocked it, and ended the round.

The group had already begun moving toward a fresh combination of beacons while the final enemy pursued Red Caesar. Vokenar healed the companions nearby. Rather than stop for a rest, they immediately activated the next round, carrying some of their ongoing magic into it.

#### Lightning among the vines

For the second round, the announcer concentrated enemies around Ceril and Vokenar near the hill's pinnacle. Hooksies, Death Sparks, a [[Shambling Mound]], and a Lightning Elemental appeared together. The arrangement exploited the contestants' separated starting positions, leaving Domyx and Red Caesar farther from the main attack.

Vokenar opened with a burst of holy radiance and used a healing scroll on himself as the enemies crowded him. The Lightning Elemental sent part of its power into the Shambling Mound. Electricity restored the plant creature instead of damaging it, turning the enemy group into another self-replenishing threat.

The Mound tried to engulf Ceril with electrified vines. His starry luck helped him pull free. Red Caesar cast Haste on himself, then used his enhanced speed and Water Walk to sprint across the sea and climb the terraced ground, bringing him back toward the others.

Ceril struck the plant with acidic magic, used Misty Step to escape, and took his starry dragon aspect to rise above the grounded enemies. Vokenar continued pulsing with radiance, weakening the clustered creatures even while he remained under pressure.

Domyx ran close enough to use Cloud Jaunt to exchange places with Vokenar. The swap pulled Vokenar out of the crowd and put Domyx among the enemies. Domyx destroyed the remaining Death Sparks with his glaive and resisted the Mound's attempt to swallow him.

Red Caesar used the Wand of Blighting Bolts against the Mound. Black, lightninglike power coiled around the vines, withering and shrinking the creature. Ceril supported Vokenar from the air, and Vokenar answered from his safer position with a radiant fireball. Domyx's laurels protected him within the blast.

The Lightning Elemental continued feeding power into the Mound. Red Caesar's next fireball destroyed one Hooksy; its mask flew free, and he caught it. He placed the new mask on the back of his head, supplementing the one already over his face.

Ceril's starry arrow finally destroyed the Mound, and Vokenar finished the remaining Hooksy. Domyx then cut into the Lightning Elemental with the Gith Shard Glaive. Its electrical backlash hurt him as he struck, but he destroyed it without collapsing.

#### Preparing the third round

Domyx drank one of his newly brewed healing potions. Vokenar gave further healing while the others moved toward another beacon combination.

They discussed resting, but Red Caesar's Haste still had time remaining. The group chose to keep moving and preserve that advantage. Vokenar restored the wounded companions quickly enough for them to resume without a short rest.

The announcer barely had time to begin its next speech before the contestants pressed their buttons. This time it sought to catch Red Caesar, whose rapid movement had drawn attention, though it still confused him with Ceril. Another Shambling Mound and more Hooksies appeared as the third round began.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_029/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### A divided battlefield

The third round again separated the companions' threats. A Shambling Mound and Death Sparks occupied the upper hill near Vokenar, while a Hooksy, a [[Cloaker]], and a Phase Cat threatened Red Caesar on lower ground.

Domyx ran toward Red Caesar. Ceril used flight and starry arrows to contribute across the terraces, while Vokenar called up Spirit Guardians around himself. Some Death Sparks attacked Vokenar; others restored the Mound with electricity.

Red Caesar used Steel Wind Strike against the lower enemies, badly wounding the cat, then raced away before they could surround him. The Hooksy could not match his water-borne speed. As it entered the sea, its face changed into a snorkel-equipped form and its body became a little yellow submarine.

Domyx intercepted the Cloaker and tore a hammer-sized hole through its wing. It nevertheless wrapped around him and carried him up the hillside. He forced his way free and rallied Red Caesar with a shout of encouragement.

Above them, Ceril placed a Wall of Fire around Vokenar and the enemies threatening him. The fire destroyed the Death Sparks, while Vokenar's guardians and hammer kept pressure on the Mound. Red Caesar spent the remaining power of his blighting wand to wither it further.

Ceril then finished the Mound with a starry arrow and destroyed the Phase Cat with another burst of light. He dismissed the firewall once that part of the island was clear.

#### The Hooksy's hidden offspring

The Cloaker generated illusory duplicates and tried to attach itself to Red Caesar, but his shield magic stopped it. Domyx's spear destroyed several of the images. Red Caesar approached Hooksy, whose hostility was undiminished by his two masks. The imitation provided no protection from a creature described as hating its own appearance.

When Red Caesar's Haste began to expire, the Cloaker wrapped around Domyx again. This time Domyx struck it from inside, where the remaining images could not conceal its actual body. The creature carried him farther up the hill while Hooksy climbed away on its hooks.

Vokenar grew wings and flew into a position from which he could strike both enemies. His radiant fireball destroyed the Cloaker, releasing Domyx safely. The protective laurels prevented the spell's harm from passing through the Cloaker's shared injuries into him.

The same blast killed Hooksy, but the fight did not end. This was a rare variant: its body burst open in a spray of blood, and two smaller Hooksies emerged from within. Sticky, placental material and spiderlike legs exposed the disturbing organic life hidden in the supposed automaton.

Red Caesar weakened the new creatures with another fireball. Domyx crushed one with his hammer before further nested components could take form. He knocked the other onto its back, and Ceril dissolved it with Primal Savagery's acid.

The acid prevented larger hidden forms from emerging, but tiny, crab-sized Hooksies still scuttled into the sea. They posed no immediate threat to the party; their escape was not their destruction.

Confetti burst overhead as the announcer proclaimed the third round won.

#### Rest and a returning companion

The group considered another immediate round, but chose to recover first. Vokenar used Divine Intervention to call on Prayer of Healing, restoring the companions and providing the effects of a short rest quickly enough to retain longer-lasting preparations. Red Caesar's Haste had already ended, though his longstrider potion remained useful.

They planned a fresh set of beacons for the fourth required round. Ceril considered the lighthouse as a vantage point. Its lamp contained a finely blown, carrot-shaped glass bulb with an electrical filament, valuable enough to collect. Domyx unscrewed it and added it to their shared treasure.

As they prepared to start the next encounter, a silhouette appeared in one of the portals and was presented as Kerben's arrival. The glimpse suggested a returning companion, but no conversation or account of the ship followed.

The session ended before the fourth round began. The three completed rounds had not yet earned the arena's lamplighter, and Kerben had not yet explained the ship's circumstances.

### Summary

- Imperial scouts approached the park's island. Kerben left with Ulrich Fjoller, Raxxy, and Zulu to move the Opal while the other four companions continued.
- At the Magic Hat, the party recovered a Boston Golf figurine resembling Rochella Golf. Red Caesar's card-game strategy earned a six-spell divine scroll, which he gave to Vokenar; Ceril declined to participate.
- The party defeated vampiric human familiars, a Mummy, Flame Skulls, and a Gorgon Head. Vokenar and Domyx overcame the gorgon's petrifying death burst.
- The hotel's Magen provided safe rooms and personalized food. The party completed a long rest and collected a purple-worm-hide rug, carrot tobacco, and Rahmadi's Capers issue four.
- Kerben's morning message reported a targeted Imperial search. After another Heroes' Feast, Red Caesar redirected the hotel's power with its lamplighter switch.
- At King of the Hole, the announcer attributed the arena's challenges to Farraday. Four completed rounds would grant access to the lamplighter; a fifth was optional.
- The party completed three rounds, using mobility, protective magic, and coordinated attacks to overcome separated starting positions and lightning-fed plant creatures.
- One rare Hooksy released smaller forms on death. The companions destroyed the larger offspring, while tiny survivors escaped into the sea.
- After a divinely aided short rest and the collection of the lighthouse's carrot bulb, the session closed on an apparent arrival announced as Kerben.

Magic Hat supplied the recovery the party had hoped for, despite the courtyard's dangerous occupants. Its attendant maintained a distinction between paying visitors and intruders, and the companions benefited from both that protection and the resort's surviving comforts. The hotel also reinforced how much the Magen could learn about visitors through their thoughts and magic. Personalized hospitality became unsettling when the arena's announcer displayed knowledge of private histories and goals.

The Imperial warning gave that respite a cost. By morning, Kerben's report established that the search was directed at this location, increasing the pressure to reach Jack Harvey's vault quickly. The companions moved the hotel's power into the gate system and pressed through the arena's first rounds with little delay.

The arena tested how well they could reunite after being forced apart. Red Caesar's extraordinary speed, Ceril's starry flight, Domyx's Cloud Jaunt, and Vokenar's protection let them turn isolation into coordinated pressure. Yet the park still contained surprises: electricity sustained living plants, Hooksies adapted to the sea, and one creature concealed smaller organisms inside itself. At the close, the four contestants had completed three rounds and recovered briefly. The apparent arrival suggested a reunion, while the required final round and the ship's situation remained ahead.
