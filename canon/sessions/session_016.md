# Session 016

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_016/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Lorelai Lapis-Acathian's secret appeal

After the return from [[The Garden]], the party considered the changed island beside [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]]. The castle now appeared to stand wholly above the water, although the much larger creature supporting the island remained partly submerged. The party had not yet entered the castle. They returned to their ship for the night, while [[Ceril]] and [[Kerben]] looked for ways to improve the lagoon's food supply. Ceril used his knowledge of plant growth, with Kerben contributing his experience as a naturalist.

That night, [[Domyx]] experienced an unusually vivid dream. He stood aboard an empty [[The Opal]] in a storm, struggling to manage the sails and steering without [[Obould]] or the crew. His sister, [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]], appeared with long white hair and a circlet. She had married into [[Clan Lapis]], whose people sought precious stones through careful exploration of the mountains. Domyx remembered their work as gentler than the extensive excavation associated with dwarven mining.

Lorelai Lapis-Acathian had contacted him secretly. She had expected to reach him during the day and had not understood that he was asleep. The contact was magical, but Domyx did not know precisely what means she had used. It also crossed the customary separation between their people and those who had departed as sky-touchers. Domyx worried about what she risked by speaking to him; she insisted that no one knew about the conversation.

She brought news of [[Ulrich Fjoller]], the friend who had descended to warn Domyx about a concealed ancestor. As Domyx thought of Ulrich Fjoller, an image of him appeared in the dream, but it was a memory rather than another participant in the conversation. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian explained that [[Domyx IV]] had known about Ulrich Fjoller's descent almost as soon as he returned. Ulrich Fjoller had been imprisoned for months, refused to explain his conduct, and specifically asked that Domyx not be told about his imprisonment.

Lorelai Lapis-Acathian had honored that request until the situation became intolerable: Ulrich Fjoller had been sentenced to execution. She still did not know what he had discovered or why their father considered it so dangerous. Domyx declined to tell her the ancestor's story. He feared that spreading the secret would endanger her and provoke a crisis within [[Clan Akathia]], and he still did not understand why the family had buried it. She accepted that boundary and asked him to keep both their conversation and her knowledge of his whereabouts secret.

She wanted Domyx to return to the consulate and save Ulrich Fjoller, whether by persuading their father or arranging a clandestine escape. Domyx was uncertain how his people would respond to a sky-toucher returning after departure; their customs offered no clear precedent that settled the matter. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian believed there might still be a few months in which to act and hoped to delay the execution, but she could not give him a secure deadline.

Another revelation made delay more dangerous. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian was pregnant with Ulrich Fjoller's child. Revealing her pregnancy, she told Domyx that his nephew would need a father. Their relationship had remained secret, but her condition would soon make concealment impossible. If their father could not be reconciled to it, she and Ulrich Fjoller might both have to leave their society. Domyx promised to help his sister and his loyal friend. Their father's booming summons to her poetry lesson interrupted the conversation, and the magical contact ended.

#### A growing base and an unexpected envoy

By morning, work at Lyngbakr Lagoon was visibly transforming the settlement. Wooden buildings, a watchtower, food storage, and a cistern were under construction. Obould had gone ashore to inspect the work. The people helping to establish the base seemed content, although [[Red Caesar]] wondered whether sailors accustomed to plunder would grow dissatisfied with a quieter life of construction and island work. The volunteers were among the crew's less enthusiastic pirates, and Obould had made arrangements for their compensation. Red Caesar checked their morale and tried to lend a hand, but found that his unfamiliarity with the work made him an obstacle rather than a useful laborer.

[[Vokenar]] helped with scouting beyond the settlement, learning about the island's terrain and potential dangers. The wildness of [[Stark]] differed from the world he had been taught about at [[Academia Lux]] a century earlier. While exploring, he noticed a ship approaching from a direction the settlement had scarcely been watching. Its white hull, red sails, and white-and-blue insignia identified it as a vessel of [[The League of New Stark]].

Vokenar brought Red Caesar to meet the newcomers. There was too little time to gather Obould and the whole crew before the ship arrived, and they preferred to speak away from the base. Vokenar enabled them to walk across the water to the vessel, where they climbed aboard under the eyes of armed sailors. The reception remained peaceful. Their visitor was [[Damien Ouranous]], the aasimar envoy they had met earlier in the campaign, recognizable in his polished armor and white cape.

Damien Ouranous asked what they were building at the lagoon and what they knew about the second island and its rising castle. Red Caesar quickly suspected that the envoy knew more than these questions suggested. Damien Ouranous admitted that he was assessing how freely they would speak before sharing his own information. The meeting therefore began with a negotiation over trust, rather than a simple request for a report.

The envoy disclosed that the League had shot down a [[Broyish Empire]] airship to protect the party's base and the neighboring castle. It had been a League action, openly traceable to the League, rather than an attack that could be attributed to independent pirates. Further imperial incursions might turn such confrontations into a wider war. The League understood its role as protecting the human kingdoms and townships within its maritime sphere, but the Empire was increasingly challenging that arrangement. Damien Ouranous also noted that ships of [[The Order of Seasons]] had left their own territories despite earlier assurances about where they would remain.

Red Caesar emphasized the freedom and stability that made League waters attractive. He regarded the party as independent contractors who could establish a base within that sphere without surrendering their autonomy. Their need for protection was consequently part of the League's ordinary mandate, as well as a matter of particular interest to its leaders. Vokenar described their work as an investigation into the loss of his former home and the restoration of what the [[The Cataclysm]] had damaged. They were collecting unusual stones whose power might help repair that damage. The newly exposed island could offer further clues, but its dangers remained largely unknown.

Damien Ouranous explained why the stones mattered to [[Lady Jacinthe]]. She had seen an obsidian-like stone aboard an imperial airship and understood it to contain dangerous energy. Without a better purpose for such objects, he would favor destroying them. Vokenar offered a different possibility: the power might serve as a way to close or mend something broken in the catastrophe. The visitors did not reveal a complete method of restoring the sky, nor did they pretend to possess one. Their case was that [[Penumbra]] might be useful in repairing the old disaster and should not be destroyed merely because the Empire coveted it.

The League was willing to protect their investigation, but needed enough information to recognize threats and avoid working at cross-purposes. Damien Ouranous wanted to chart the island and spend a day or two looking around. Vokenar agreed while asking him to keep his notes vague and restrict fuller information to Lady Jacinthe when necessary. The envoy accepted that limit. Concentrating obvious military protection around the site could itself advertise its importance, so they also discussed the value of contacting Lady Jacinthe promptly if danger increased. Damien Ouranous went ashore to begin his visit; the transcript did not establish that his survey was completed that day.

#### A return to The White Drake

The envoy's arrival prompted Red Caesar to make use of his newly learned Teleportation Circle. He had considered using the spell for renewed contact with several allies, including the League, the orcs, and the Academy. Now he chose to visit Lady Jacinthe directly. Vokenar and Domyx accompanied him, while Kerben remained at the lagoon hunting.

The three visitors arrived in a guarded chamber around a permanent circle aboard [[The White Drake]]. The guards received them without hostility, and Lady Jacinthe welcomed their return. Their appearance interrupted routine paperwork and gave her a welcome opportunity to speak with familiar allies. This was a renewed meeting, not their first introduction.

Lady Jacinthe described the Empire's growing pressure on the League. Its forces had already clashed with League interests and attacked near human settlements. She understood the imperial project as an attempt to bring all humans under a single coercive rule, rather than allow the independent communities the League defended. Red Caesar and the others shared her concern for freedom and the possibility of building lives beyond imperial control. He doubted that a small pirate crew should be a priority for a distant emperor, while she warned that the Empire's ambitions made its reach increasingly consequential.

The visitors needed to stay overnight, and Lady Jacinthe offered hospitality, a banquet, and accommodation. She asked whether Obould had come with them and was disappointed to learn that he had remained at the lagoon dealing with the base. She then made light of missing his stories by claiming she had heard them all before. The exchange underscored the personal connections that accompanied their political dealings.

Red Caesar discovered an important limit to his new means of travel. Teleportation Circle had carried them to an established destination, but there was no permanent receiving circle at Lyngbakr Lagoon or aboard their own ship. Resting and recovering his magic would not solve that problem. They could not simply reverse the journey in the same way.

Lady Jacinthe introduced them to [[Ema-Tep]], her yuan-ti second-in-command and the officer responsible for transportation. Ema-Tep could return them through matching plants using Transport via Plants. Plantains growing at the lagoon offered a suitable connection to those available aboard The White Drake. The discussion recalled Ceril's recent work with the island's plants and the Garden's effort to preserve and spread a wide variety of species. Their cultivation at the lagoon had therefore created a practical route home as well as a food resource.

Ema-Tep offered that passage on the condition that the party look into a dangerous siren in southwestern waters. Its song had become unusually compelling, and the creature was said to have been corrupted by hellish influence. Vokenar accepted the request. The conversation established an undertaking to find and investigate the threat, without fixing an immediate deadline or recording its defeat.

The League also passed along a separate lead: a human castle south of the Garden where a powerful construct had once appeared. Its abilities had drawn people who wanted to worship it, but it had rejected that role, helped them, and moved on. Such a traveler might hold information about the old world. The visitors received the report as a possible avenue for investigation; they had neither located the construct nor established its identity.

#### Imperial intelligence and the return to the lagoon

Before the visitors departed the following day, Lady Jacinthe returned to the question of the stones. She described the object she had seen as black like the night heavens and thought it possible that the Empire possessed more than one. Her spies had also reported a massive magical cannon. A weapon of that kind would need an extraordinary source of power, and the stones could provide it. That was a troubling possibility, but the conversation did not confirm the cannon's fuel or explain precisely what the Empire planned to do with it.

Red Caesar discussed the strategic consequences if the Empire opened a major campaign. Lady Jacinthe explained that the League's strength lay in dispersed kingdoms and forces capable of resisting across a broad area. It had no single centralized base whose loss would end the struggle. Even The White Drake would become unsafe if the Empire openly hunted its leaders, and a mobile, distributed resistance would matter more than reliance on one stronghold.

The visitors gave her a clearer account of their purpose. They hoped to restore the sky, learn what had caused the Cataclysm, and prevent a repetition. They had received divine guidance that they would eventually encounter someone responsible for the old disaster, but they did not yet have all the answers. Domyx suspected that his homeland might hold part of the explanation. He did not name the hidden ancestor or identify the emperor as that person.

Lady Jacinthe agreed to strengthen defenses in central and western waters, both to protect the League's communities and to occupy the Empire while the party pursued its investigation. The prospect of war remained real, but her support gave the party room to continue work that might address dangers beyond the immediate political conflict.

Ema-Tep then opened the route through the plantains, returning Red Caesar, Vokenar, and Domyx to Lyngbakr Lagoon. The castle beside the lagoon still awaited exploration. Alongside that investigation and the search for Penumbra, Domyx now carried a private commitment to rescue Ulrich Fjoller before his execution and protect Lorelai Lapis-Acathian as her pregnancy made their secret increasingly difficult to keep.

### Summary

- Lorelai Lapis-Acathian secretly contacted Domyx and revealed that Ulrich Fjoller had been imprisoned after warning him about their concealed ancestor and was now condemned to execution.
- Lorelai Lapis-Acathian was pregnant with Ulrich Fjoller's child. Domyx promised to help them, while withholding the ancestor's secret to avoid placing her in greater danger.
- Construction and food cultivation continued at Lyngbakr Lagoon; the neighboring castle remained unexplored.
- Damien Ouranous reported that the League had shot down an imperial airship to protect the site, an intervention that could contribute to open conflict with the Broyish Empire.
- The party agreed to a limited League survey, with fuller information restricted to Lady Jacinthe when necessary.
- Red Caesar transported Vokenar and Domyx to The White Drake. Their meeting with Lady Jacinthe renewed the alliance and clarified the League's resistance to imperial expansion.
- Ema-Tep arranged a return through plantains in exchange for investigating a corrupted siren; the League also offered a lead concerning a wandering construct and a southern human castle.
- Lady Jacinthe connected reports of a massive imperial magical cannon with the stones she had seen, presenting Penumbra as a possible power source rather than a confirmed one.
- Lady Jacinthe promised stronger regional defenses, and Ema-Tep returned the visitors to the lagoon.

The dream transformed Ulrich Fjoller's earlier warning into an urgent obligation. His silence under imprisonment had protected Domyx, but the impending execution and Lorelai Lapis-Acathian's pregnancy made that protection unsustainable. Domyx promised intervention without resolving whether persuasion, escape, or departure from clan society would be necessary. The conversation also kept the ancestor's identity uncertain: he knew that a secret existed and that his father had suppressed it, but had not established the full explanation.

The League negotiations made the party's relative safety more concrete and more precarious. Its defenders had already acted against an imperial airship, and their interest in Penumbra placed the lagoon within a larger contest over dangerous power. Cooperation brought intelligence, transport, and protection while requiring careful disclosure. The visitors returned with new investigative leads and a stronger alliance, but neither the siren, the southern castle, the rescue, nor the exposed castle beside the lagoon had been resolved.
