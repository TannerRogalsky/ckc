# Session 024

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_024/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Kerben's secret bargain

[[Kerben]] now carried [[Shark's Edge]], a magical falchion fashioned from a great shark tooth. Its distinctive power let him judge the temperature of water he could see, adding a new tool to his navigation and tracking. His poison craft also included a preparation from black dragon acid that could continue corroding a target after the initial strike.

The session returned to an earlier evening while Kerben was protecting [[The Opal]] in Broyish waters and his companions were ashore. Acting as captain had shown him how much [[Obould]] quietly managed. Delegating to the crew, maintaining the rigging, setting bearings, and keeping the vessel ready required more than being its most capable scout. Kerben's gruff manner made replacing the captain's ease with people particularly difficult.

Passing through the bunks, he met [[Theo Harvey]] in the improvised brig, where wooden posts enclosed part of a bed. The prisoner praised the ship's growing reputation and offered a bargain. He claimed that returning to [[The League of New Stark]] would probably mean execution, while surrendering to the [[Broyish Empire]] would certainly do so. Those were his expectations, rather than sentences the party had independently confirmed.

Theo Harvey knew that Kerben had worked for [[Jack Harvey]] at his museum. He described his own ambition to emulate the older entrepreneur, whose life he had researched without ever learning everything he wanted to know. Kerben shared memories and a ticket connected to [[The Carrot Cake]], and Theo Harvey produced an old carrot tea recipe. They traded rum and tea while discussing Jack Harvey's ability to assemble people with the right skills for each project.

Theo Harvey supplied a more specific account of the park than he had given [[Domyx]]. Notes stolen from the Imperial Library described it as a demi-plane, accessible only from the first day of the fifth moon through the last day of the ninth. Outside that window it could not simply be found by sailing to its apparent position. He attributed [[The Tyrant]]'s failure to return to being trapped when that access closed.

He also said that Jack Harvey had gathered fragments of the sky after [[The Cataclysm]]. The collection had been placed somewhere safe in anticipation that another person might eventually need the pieces to save the world. That made the park a possible source of [[Penumbra]], and the account fit Kerben's memories of his former employer.

In exchange for his freedom and the [[Gheister]], Theo Harvey offered an exact map and the opening dates. He proposed leaving the information behind while making his departure look like an escape, so the other party members would receive the lead without knowing that Kerben had agreed to it. Kerben accepted and asked for the same help if he ever found himself in Theo Harvey's position. The prisoner promised it.

Kerben gave [[Mobley]] the carrot tea recipe for the crew's evening in the mess. He considered making it more sedating, but decided that the tea itself would be sufficient and wanted to avoid putting the cook in danger or making him the focus of blame. Its natural effects dulled the drinkers while they played cards and enjoyed themselves. Kerben also accounted for [[Yalet Mora]], who would not be affected like the other crew members, by assigning him duties where he would not see the departure.

With the map left on the bed, Kerben arranged the wooden enclosure to look as though Theo Harvey had broken out. They shook hands, and the prisoner reached the Gheister and slipped away through darkness and fog. He intended to lie low, perhaps near the warrens or on an inconspicuous island, and did not give Kerben an exact destination. The crew was left to discover the apparent escape and its useful evidence.

#### Aeris's mortal imprisonment

After the palace audience, [[Ceril]] reflected on Obould's helplessness and humiliation. He thought that killing the captain might spare him further indignity and remove the hostage leverage, though he made no attempt to do so. Giving [[Vizier Jade]] Penumbra remained unacceptable to him. The possibility of restoring the sky outweighed participating in the bargain she had offered.

While Ceril tranced aboard the ship, he and [[Vokenar]] found themselves together in a strange black expanse. Silver dust crunched underfoot, and the sky overhead held no stars. Before them stood a great ring combining stone, flowing cloud, trapped water, and fire. Its center held a figure gathered from the stars absent above them.

Ceril recognized that the elements belonged to [[Stark]], but could not locate the place. It might be beneath the world, under the ocean, or in some remote expanse they had never encountered. The faint contours of its horizon suggested a real landscape. Druidcraft worked there and foretold wind and light rain despite the apparent absence of clouds.

The captive called the device [[Starfall]] and identified herself as [[Aeris]]. She resembled the goddess they had met, but lacked the overwhelming divine presence they expected. This was a mortal seed of Aeris, a concentrated portion of her soul held within the weapon. She explained that her body was the Penumbra, while this stolen form served as a battery awaiting further fragments.

A man had reached up and touched her decades earlier, then wanted her power for himself and denied it to anyone else. He had captured this portion and broken the rest into pieces. Her account matched what the party had just learned about [[Emperor Shen]], though she spoke of her captor without naming him in the encounter.

Ceril asked how she could be imprisoned when they had contacted her elsewhere. Aeris explained that her divine soul still flowed into the world as long as the sky remained. She had felt the party's healing of it and used some of that renewed divinity to reach them. After the latest repair, however, her captor had tightened her bonds and drained more power from the mortal form, leaving her weaker. This also explained the strain in her recent attempts to answer Ceril.

She had spent roughly sixty years in captivity, aging like a mortal, and had spent much of that time learning to use magic as mortals did. The effort required to bring them to her had nearly exhausted her. She believed they were present in their true forms, but did not fully understand the spell she had managed to cast or how long it would hold.

Vokenar asked whether banishment could free her. Aeris did not know where it would send her or whether she would survive, but was willing to have him try if it offered a chance. The encounter faded before any such attempt. She guessed that the location might be deep below or at the center of the world, without supplying a reliable route.

Ceril used Plant Growth on the ring's sparse vegetation. Apples, pears, citrus, and grain flourished, giving the captive something more substantial to eat. The other elements of the ring also seemed to become more orderly as the plants grew, making the care more than a gesture within an unreal dream.

Aeris described what she had seen when Starfall fired. Color gathered around her, became a beam, and shot toward the sky. Part of her physical form had been used to damage her own godhood. Her remaining divine power had supplied the weapon's first use; afterward it needed pieces of her body, the Penumbra. The device fused all four elements into a stream containing what would be needed to rebuild a world, making it a weapon unlike any the party knew.

Vokenar asked whether feeding Penumbra to [[Lyngbakr]] and repairing the sky could save her. Aeris believed that restoring her divinity would make her whole and might rescue the trapped mortal portion. Otherwise, she feared that restarting the world would become the only recourse.

He also asked about the [[Demi-Spell]]. Aeris said that power used for it would flow back into the cosmos to await a new world rather than repair the present sky. She had felt the party's efforts as healing and the Empire's as pain, but had not felt the Demi-Spell's actions or learned what its use of her power would bring. She could not assure them that those pursuing it would be trustworthy.

She directed Ceril to keep consulting the divine presence that witnessed events beneath the sky, using the means associated with his shield. The mortal captive and the goddess were separated enough that they could not freely share everything each knew.

Vokenar left a Continual Flame as a promise that they were still trying to help. Fire from Starfall's own elements wove into the light beside Aeris. It could not warm her, but it would remain a visible sign of their commitment in the darkness. A low, pulsing rumble surrounded the device, and approaching it too closely felt dangerous.

Aeris warned them not to underestimate her captor before they returned to the bunks. Ceril could still feel the expenditure of the magic he had used, supporting the encounter's reality. He also knew the specific kinds of plants now growing at the ring, which might become a clue for locating it. Vokenar wanted to keep strengthening the party and collecting Penumbra rather than attempt an unprepared confrontation with Emperor Shen. They went back to rest.

#### Domyx and the people who left the mountains

The next scene returned to Domyx's earlier visit to the [[Broyish Capital]], before the encounter with Aeris. He wanted to understand why so many Titanfolk lived there. Returning home would itself violate the rules under which he had descended, potentially exposing him to the punishment already threatened against [[Ulrich Fjoller]]. Understanding the people who had left might help him judge the society he was about to revisit.

Near the Dawn Palace, he met [[Fharan]]. The seneschal described Broy as a place for those who fell through the cracks of other societies. Humans had come when rising waters intensified competition with elves and dwarves; Titanfolk also arrived after failing to find a respected place in the mountains. The Empire offered belonging in exchange for allegiance.

Domyx found the account difficult to reconcile with a culture built around competition and excellence. Fharan recognized his noble connection to [[Clan Akathia]], whose rule he described as extending over Nornhof and the wider Titan region, and suggested that his position explained why he did not understand those denied similar opportunities.

Fharan said that the clan's supposedly restricted rites and history were more widely known than Domyx believed, particularly among people capable of leaving the mountains. Other Titanfolk had earned achievements too, he argued, but would never receive the same respect there. The conversation exposed how much Domyx's upbringing had concealed even about his own society.

The Empire's diversity did not erase its hierarchy. Fharan acknowledged that humans, as its founding people, occupied a higher tier than later arrivals. He nevertheless told Domyx that blood should not keep him from advancement in the capital if he worked for it. The promise appealed to Domyx, while remaining tied to imperial allegiance.

#### What the party found in the brig

In another scene preceding Aeris's visit, Domyx returned to the bunks and found Theo Harvey gone. He inspected the improvised enclosure before spreading an alarm. Pry marks and tooth marks made it appear that the prisoner had obtained a tool and worked through the wooden posts. Domyx could place the departure only within the time the party had been away.

He sought [[Raxxy]], who had been on watch. She seemed drunk or delirious, unlike her usual conduct while on duty, and admitted losing track of time in the mess after drinking Mobley's new carrot tea from Kerben's recipe. She had not seen Theo Harvey leave.

Raxxy found the document in the brig and gave it to Domyx. It marked The Carrot Cake on the rear side of the central mountain of [[Thalasia]], north of [[The White Drake]], with a particular direction from which a boat should approach. It repeated the opening window from the beginning of the fifth moon to the end of the ninth, while making clear that entering might still be difficult.

Theo Harvey's accompanying note thanked the party for not surrendering him to the League and presented the map as a gesture of good faith. It claimed the park was full of Penumbra. Domyx wondered whether the marked place was also where the prisoner had gone, but the note did not establish that.

When the group reconvened in the commons, Red Caesar announced that the Gheister was missing too. Domyx shared the map. Kerben knew what had happened, but did not reveal the arrangement.

Vokenar and Red Caesar questioned the lead's reliability. Theo Harvey had heard them discussing Penumbra and might have written what they wanted to hear to divert pursuit. Kerben's knowledge of Jack Harvey could lend credibility to a plan to collect the sky fragments for future use, but the collection had not been seen.

There were thirty-one days before the next opening, leaving time for other work. The party considered visiting Domyx's homeland first, a voyage estimated at about eight days, then returning toward the southern waters for resources and other obligations before entering the park.

The League's payments to Vizier Jade had also damaged their confidence in [[Lady Jacinthe]], who had previously denied knowing about Penumbra. They discussed questioning her, seizing supplies being held at The White Drake, or intercepting a delivery and disguising themselves as its bearers. Vokenar warned that provoking the League could trap them in another difficult confrontation.

Ceril remained reluctant to enter [[Tome Keeper's Pyramid]] without protection from the [[Tome of Satariel]]. He considered whether an offering or the capture of an evil, powerful wizard could help them bargain, but no such plan was carried out. They left the keeper for later and kept their commitments to the Indorras and [[Southport]] in mind.

#### Departure and the divided party

The party settled on going north first, then visiting The White Drake for information about the Penumbra dealings before making a southern circuit. Possible stops included [[Cutlass Cray]], the underwater siren, the maker of a map of all Stark known as [[Witness]], the black dragon connected to their original shipwreck and the young dragon at [[Castle Kaedon]], and the troll island the League wanted cleared.

The route did not bind every future decision. Vokenar still intended to accompany [[Trent Indorra]] and [[Ebbie Indorra]] toward Southport if necessary, and Ceril would be especially interested in going there if a Penumbra lead emerged. Buying scrolls at Cutlass Cray might also prepare them for the fiend they had deferred.

The Opal's new cannons required a master gunner and powder monkey. They considered awakening an actual monkey for the latter work, but recruited no such companion. Kerben made their immediate route official by stabbing the chart with Shark's Edge.

Red Caesar chose a separate course. He wanted to investigate the possibility of intercepting Penumbra deliveries and work on a convincing false piece as leverage against Vizier Jade. He teleported to The White Drake to undertake that business and wait for the ship's return. He did not accompany the northern expedition, and no completed imitation was established during this session.

#### Ice, aurora, and the hydra

The upgraded Opal traveled more swiftly than it had early in the campaign. A favorable wind carried it through the strait between the elven lands and Broy. Merchant vessels shared the waters with imperial military ships, some launching airships for patrols. The two sides respected territorial boundaries amid tension; there was no outright elven-imperial war in that stretch, and The Opal passed through without interception.

After several days, traffic fell away and the northern waters froze. The bulbous bow broke the ice, while Shark's Edge let Kerben choose warmer water and thinner patches, making the journey safer and faster. The hull still suffered wear from the passage. Vokenar's consecration helped keep life aboard bearable, though the top deck grew bitterly cold and the crew had to keep working.

An aurora shimmered over the ice on the seventh morning. Domyx grew solemn and nostalgic, remembering how he had crossed these waters on his descent: walking over ice, leaping between floes, then swimming through freezing straits before finding a ship. That journey had forged much of his endurance.

Rather than push The Opal all the way to shore, Vokenar prepared Water Walk so the party could leave it and cross the remaining water. Before they disembarked, the ship's disturbance broke the sleep of a creature beneath the ice.

Eight heads rose on the port side, followed by a great body armored in permafrost over dragon-like scales. The [[Rimefire Hydra]] shook the vessel as it emerged. Kerben's rifle shattered one head into ice, while Domyx went out onto the surface under Water Walk and brought the [[Gith Shard Glaive]] against the clustered necks.

The hydra's regeneration made removing heads dangerous: a lost one could return as two. It concentrated its biting attacks on Domyx, who endured the onslaught with his own restorative power and help from Ceril and Vokenar. Its body slammed against The Opal, driving ice into the hull despite Raxxy's attempt to steer clear.

[[Brim the Bullywog]] volunteered to use the new cannons. He struggled with precise shots but used a wider barrage and grape shot to tear into the creature and remove more heads. His effort demonstrated the weapons' value without filling the need for a trained master gunner.

Kerben applied black dragon acid through a poisoned shot. The continuing corrosion stopped the hydra from regenerating and prevented its missing heads from growing back. That turned the fight from an expanding threat into one the party could finish.

Ceril's moonlight and starry attacks shone through the aurora and refracted across the ice-covered body. Vokenar brought forth a celestial defender and joined the assault with divine radiance. With the hydra badly wounded, he released a holy fireball that burst it open from within. Kerben's acid continued to melt the remains, preventing another recovery.

The hydra was dead, but The Opal retained the damage from the ice and the collision. Vokenar kept the celestial defender present for the forthcoming journey ashore.

#### The Arctic Plains

After the fight, Raxxy offered to take the ship farther offshore while the party traveled, reducing the danger from other creatures near the coast. Vokenar agreed. The vessel could be repaired later; it remained usable.

Ceril shifted his starry form into the chalice constellation and healed Domyx, allowing the expedition to proceed without a long pause. Kerben left his animal companions aboard in the crew's care rather than expose them to the northern cold. Ceril, Domyx, Kerben, and Vokenar crossed toward the Arctic Plains with the celestial defender still accompanying them.

The temperature became punishingly cold. Kerben used Locate Animals or Plants to find the direction of caribou and similar creatures, reasoning that their tracks could lead through places with shelter and warmth. He scouted ahead while Domyx drew on memories of surviving his descent. Ceril became a ram better suited to the terrain.

Their precautions helped, but did not make the exposure harmless. The travelers weakened as they acclimated to the conditions. They pressed forward through rocky edges and snowy slopes rather than wandering blindly in search of a route.

#### The Tatzelwurm

Ahead of his companions, Kerben found dead caribou and the rest of the herd scattering. A huge white dragon, hard to distinguish from the mountain behind it, was finishing its meal. He took cover close to it, unseen.

Kerben set a mental Alarm at the corner his companions were approaching. Domyx recognized the familiar signal from the ship and stopped, understanding that Kerben was warning them not to advance. It could not deliver a spoken explanation.

Instead of withdrawing, Kerben chose to attack. He marked the dragon, prepared wyvern poison, and opened with powerful shots. The reports echoed between the peaks and dislodged small avalanches. The dragon dropped its meal and charged him, while his companions were also taken by surprise at the sudden battle.

Ceril identified it as the [[Tatzelwurm]], a unique white dragon that flew over and surveyed its northern territory. Its influence shaped the surrounding land, intensifying blizzards, avalanches, and the extreme cold they had endured. Defeating it would make the onward route less dangerous.

Ceril returned from ram form and summoned an aerial bestial spirit to attack from another direction. Domyx rushed forward and knocked the dragon down with his hammer. Vokenar approached with the celestial defender and tried to support the attack with fire.

Kerben followed his opening shots with Shark's Edge and another poison, although the latter did not incapacitate the dragon. The Tatzelwurm struck back with freezing breath and furious attacks, while Ceril healed Kerben and kept his summoned bird fighting.

Domyx defended his ally and toppled the dragon again. Ceril's bestial spirit delivered the blow that ended it. As the Tatzelwurm collapsed, the bitter weather eased: the party had removed the force making the surrounding winter so extreme.

The celestial defender gave Kerben one last healing touch before departing. Vokenar then used the Prayer of Healing stored in [[Sunset's Solace]] to let the party recover quickly within a shared restorative aura. The shortened pause left Ceril's bestial spirit present for a little longer.

Kerben looked for a hoard but judged that the dragon was far from any den and did not keep a great collection of treasure. Its remains were useful instead. He extracted the liver and combined material from the dragon with darkwood bark to make [[Tatzelwurm Gizzard Juice]], a cold-inflicting injury poison. They also packed dragon meat in snow to bring back to the ship.

A bird scouting for Ceril found a river leading uphill into Titanfolk territory. Blood from the slain dragon had turned its clear waters red, giving the route a vivid landmark. The party followed it for hours, with the summoned spirit eventually fading.

#### The Remorhaz's jaws

The river basin was warmer, but another danger awaited it. Trees ahead snapped and shifted as a huge creature surged out toward the party, trailing snow and dirt. Ceril identified the [[Remorhaz]], a rare northern predator with an insect-like body, fish-like fins, and an internal furnace fueled by constant eating.

It had smelled the travelers. Ceril knew it would try to swallow them one at a time, and its body radiated enough heat to endanger anyone close to it. He placed Moonbeam in its path and shifted into his dragon constellation. His growth had now given that starry form the ability to fly and hover, alongside its steadying effect on his magic.

The Remorhaz rushed toward Vokenar. Domyx used [[Iklwa Isondo]] to hook himself onto its head, putting himself in danger and drawing attention away from his companion. He followed with his hammer while enduring the creature's heat.

Vokenar raised a ring of radiant fire around himself and Kerben, protecting them while Kerben fired from within it. Ceril continued moving the moonlight across the attacking creature.

The Remorhaz turned and threw Domyx down toward its mouth. It bit him and held him in its jaws, leaving him caught as the chunk ended.

### Chunk 0003

#### Rescue from the Remorhaz

Domyx tried to force the jaws apart, but the Remorhaz swallowed him. Heat and digestive fluids burned around him, and he could neither see a destination for his cloud step nor move freely. He enlarged himself and tried to fight his way out with his hammer, without making the creature release him.

Kerben shot into the Remorhaz's side, punching a hole through which Domyx could briefly see daylight. Ceril continued attacking with moonlight, but the creature moved again and lunged at him. Vokenar's protection prevented the bite, though the surrounding heat broke Ceril's spell.

Inside, Domyx lost consciousness and approached death. Ceril's influence over fortune helped him survive long enough for his companions to act. They could not directly heal him through the enclosing body.

Vokenar struck with a blast of holy light, destroying the Remorhaz. Kerben immediately used his survival knowledge to carve through the corpse beneath its head. After working past its internal bones and flesh, he reached Domyx's arm. Exposing part of him gave Ceril a target for healing.

Ceril's chalice-enhanced magic woke Domyx just before Kerben pulled him the rest of the way free. Vokenar added further healing, and Domyx took a moment to recover on the ground. He had survived both being swallowed and the rescue.

The Remorhaz had no useful poison to harvest, but Kerben took its heat gland while it was still warm. It would help them endure the rest of the climb, even as its stored heat diminished. After restoring the injured travelers, they continued without another extended rest.

The mountains were becoming familiar to Domyx. He remembered his father's advice to leave the beasts alone and trust that they would leave him alone, but the ascent had been much harsher than that memory suggested. The party had no settled explanation for the difference.

#### Returning to Acathian Manor

At a certain height, the snow gave way almost abruptly to grassy hills, orchards, farmland, and flowers. The mountain realm's nature and magic made the change possible. Domyx reached the place where Ulrich Fjoller had intercepted him during his descent and searched it, finding no useful trace left behind. From there he could lead the way with confidence.

Worked paths, stairs cut into rock, and cleared valleys climbed toward palaces at the highest points of Stark. The party could see the sky as a faintly discernible flat plate separating the world from [[Arkadia]]. Here it looked intact, far from the damage that had defined their travels below.

[[Acathian Manor]], the golden home of the ruling clan, drew steady traffic. Domyx approached cautiously. His own return was unlawful, and Ceril, Kerben, and Vokenar would stand out as an elf, dwarf, and aasimar among a people deeply suspicious of outsiders.

Domyx explained his immediate purpose: his sister's beloved had been imprisoned, and his family's connection to the Empire had raised questions he could not ignore. He went into the manor alone, leaving a sketch of Ulrich Fjoller to help his companions look for the prisoner.

Kerben kept the others concealed. Vokenar searched for Penumbra through Locate Object and detected none within reach, while Ceril sent his familiar to survey the area. Their interest in the sky's fragments continued alongside helping Domyx. The surrounding sky's apparent wholeness also helped explain why the mountain society had remained so sheltered from the Cataclysm.

#### Charlotta Fjoller and Lorelai Lapis-Acathian

Domyx found [[Charlotta Fjoller]], Ulrich Fjoller's sister, who prepared food and handled repairs around the manor. She recognized him and warned that he should not be there.

Charlotta Fjoller explained that [[Domyx IV]] had become rigid about enforcing the laws after gaining power. He wanted to demonstrate that he would make no exceptions, even for her brother. Before his arrest, Ulrich Fjoller had told her that what he had discovered about the clan could bring it down. He knew their grandfather was monstrous, but had not learned enough to explain what he had done.

Meanwhile, Ceril's magma owl returned with a possible lead: a heavily guarded cavern into which prepared food was being delivered. It stood out among the mountain's open forums and separate buildings. Ceril chose to wait and share the information rather than risk exposing the outsiders by investigating on his own. The report did not yet establish that the cavern held Ulrich Fjoller.

Domyx told Charlotta Fjoller that Emperor Shen had touched Aeris and then broken the sky with Starfall. He saw the suppression of that history as protecting the clan from the consequences of its own crimes and isolation. The account shocked her.

[[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] emerged from hiding after hearing the conversation. She believed their father must have known more than he had ever told them. She described his rule as increasingly severe, even while he imagined himself well loved.

Her pregnancy remained hidden from him. She was determined to bring Ulrich Fjoller to safety and said they could no longer live there. He was held in the [[Prison of Frost]], not far away, and she explained the route.

Lorelai Lapis-Acathian expected that leaving with him would mean they could never return. Seeing that Domyx had survived below gave her hope that she could too, if he showed her how. Domyx agreed. He worried that the departure of the remaining heirs might affect their father's conduct, but would not make that concern a reason to abandon them.

#### Domyx rejects the clan

Domyx decided to confront his father. He took the medallion from his sash into his hand, preparing to cast away the visible mark of his noble status. That emblem was also how Fharan had recognized his lineage in Broy.

On the manor's exterior, Domyx IV initially smiled at the sight of his son, then remembered what the return meant. He challenged the violation of the clan's rules, but offered to conceal it, share an evening with Domyx, and send him back below without consequences.

Domyx demanded an explanation of his grandfather's much greater crimes and the other Akathians living outside the mountains. His father dismissed those relatives and justified the secrecy as preserving the traditions and sanctity of Titan society. History, he said, could hide its mistakes as easily as it could hide Domyx's return.

Domyx refused to accept the Cataclysm as a small mistake. He explained the events he had learned, using gestures and performance to make Starfall and its destruction vivid.

Domyx IV acknowledged that his father had been a monster and remained one. He nevertheless insisted that the people must be protected from the knowledge and that Domyx should leave and forget it. He blamed Ulrich Fjoller for awakening the curiosity that had brought his son to disaster.

Domyx would not forget. He argued that a people devoted to earning remembrance should not answer their history by erasing it, and cast the medallion onto the ground.

His father declared that he was no longer an Akathian or his son. Domyx IV departed in sorrow rather than attacking him. Domyx walked away having rejected both the family's imposed silence and his place in its noble structure.

When he rejoined his companions, he told them to call him simply Domyx. He saw himself as a pirate now and had no desire to return for further personal dealings with a society so fixed in its ways.

Lorelai Lapis-Acathian came with him and introduced herself to the group. She apologized for drawing them into the crisis, recalling the magical mirror through which she had first asked her brother for help. Vokenar turned their attention to the friend they still needed to rescue, and she offered to lead them to the prison.

### Chunk 0004

#### Freeing Ulrich Fjoller

Lorelai Lapis-Acathian guided the party to the Prison of Frost. Ulrich Fjoller recognized her and Domyx from behind a plane of magical energy. The absent clan emblem showed him that something had changed; Domyx confirmed that he now used his name without the noble designation.

Ulrich Fjoller could not break the barrier and did not understand its sorcery. He called it an abomination to a people who had traditionally avoided depending on magic, exposing a contradiction in the means used to enforce their laws.

Ceril recognized the force field and dispelled it, allowing Ulrich Fjoller to pass through. They did not have to fight the prison keeper nearby: their approach kept him outside the encounter. The mountains had been the harder obstacle, and the prison itself was not comprehensively locked down against people who had managed to reach it.

Ulrich Fjoller asked whether he and Lorelai Lapis-Acathian could survive in the world below. Domyx answered that it was safer than remaining there, while warning that the journey would be difficult. They left together.

The return to The Opal was swift and brought no further confrontation. The major creatures along the route had already been defeated. Ulrich Fjoller and Lorelai Lapis-Acathian joined the ship's company, bringing a second couple aboard alongside the Indorras.

The pregnancy prompted discussion of finding a midwife and perhaps arranging a nursery, but no such facilities or hire were completed. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian had not entered labor. With the rescue accomplished and the family safely aboard, the ship resumed its planned route south, where Red Caesar was waiting at The White Drake.

Kerben's bargain converted a dangerous prisoner into a potentially valuable lead, but its secrecy left the party unable to judge the information on the same terms he had. His connection to Jack Harvey helped make the collection plausible, while the others reasonably feared a diversion. Theo Harvey remained free with the ship's longboat, and the bounty was not delivered. The seasonal opening gave them a reason to use the intervening month rather than attempt immediate entry.

The encounter with Aeris transformed the effort to repair the sky into care for a captive who could age, starve, and die. Her divine presence and mortal seed were connected but could not freely exchange knowledge. She supported the party's healing work without promising that they could rescue her by any immediate spell, and without resolving whether the Demi-Spell's makers could be trusted. Ceril and Vokenar answered her suffering with food and light while accepting that they still needed information and strength.

The northern journey tested resources acquired over several sessions. The bow and Shark's Edge helped The Opal reach waters that ordinary ships could not safely traverse. The cannons mattered before a qualified gunner had been found, and Kerben's poison craft turned the hydra's regeneration into a manageable threat. Domyx endured much of the danger, including nearly dying inside the Remorhaz, but his survival depended on the coordinated rescue rather than his own strength alone.

That dependence contrasted with the ideals he confronted at home. Fharan had exposed the limits of a culture that recognized noble achievement while overlooking those who left it. Domyx IV then offered to hide his son's violation while defending the punishment of Ulrich Fjoller and the suppression of Emperor Shen's history. Domyx chose memory, accountability, and his relationships over the clan's protection. The result was a real rupture with his father, followed by the successful rescue of the friend whose warning had begun the search. Ulrich Fjoller and Lorelai Lapis-Acathian reached safety aboard the ship, while the larger fight over Penumbra and Starfall remained ahead.
