### Summary

This session focuses on the rescue and mystery surrounding [[Alamar]]. After clearing the exposed spires of demons, including [[Sunspite]], the party finds Alamar in a magical coma inside [[Castle Kaedon]]. [[Rasharan's Rock]], the former evacuation destination, no longer exists. [[Vlerro]] is responsible for the Sequestering. [[Tuna]], a sphinx, is connected to Alamar. The party's connection to [[Castle Kaedon]] and [[Lyngbakr]] deepens.
