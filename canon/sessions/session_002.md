# Session 002

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/sessions_001-0010|Source transcript]]

A flashback returned to [[Red Caesar]] at [[Heaven's Bulb]]. [[Master Lee]] warned him about the dangers outside and gave him [[Para and Bellum]], a pair of copper blades whose names recalled the saying "Si vis pacem, para bellum." His preparation belonged to a school that sought to make its members capable of inheriting a new world.

On the island, the party explored ruins with elven features that had been built by humans. The site included caves that might have served a funerary purpose. They encountered [[Kuo-Toa]], learned of their god [[Blibdoolpoolp]], and noted incense among their offerings.

### Summary

Red Caesar's earlier departure from Heaven's Bulb was recalled through Master Lee's warning and gift of Para and Bellum. The island ruins introduced the Kuo-Toa and Blibdoolpoolp before the subsequent dungeon exploration.

### Connections

- [[Heaven's Bulb]] and Red Caesar's preparation connect his personal history to the world changed by [[The Cataclysm]].
- [[canon/sessions/session_003]] records the map to a Temple of [[Sigil]], the defeat of Blibdoolpoolp, and [[Rella Kel'Navvi]] joining the party.
- The later temple visit in [[canon/sessions/session_008]] revealed human and Kuo-Toa statues, including a frightened-looking Kuo-Toa.
