# Session 011

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_011/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Vizier Jade's private visit

About a week before the descent into [[Darvinblast]], an alarm woke [[Red Caesar]] aboard [[The Opal]]. On deck he found [[Vizier Jade]], a human woman in eastern attire who had appeared without warning and was inspecting the ship. She introduced herself as a servant and voice of [[Emperor Shen]] of [[Broy]]. When Red Caesar questioned whether she was merely a projection, she touched his arm to demonstrate that she was physically present.

Vizier Jade already knew about [[Heaven's Bulb]] and the party's search for [[Penumbra]]. She urged Red Caesar to visit the [[Broyish Empire]], offering to continue the education he had received at Heaven's Bulb. She argued that survivors of [[The Cataclysm]] were stronger and more deserving inheritors of the new world, and presented his magical potential as a reason for seeking him out. Red Caesar questioned what she wanted in return, but she described her interest as a duty to cultivate people capable of shaping that world.

Her offer also carried a threat. She called his companions liabilities to him, Heaven's Bulb, and the Broyish Empire, promising to protect him from disaster while warning that she would come for them if they held him back. Red Caesar nevertheless asked about the practicalities of entering the Broyish Empire. Vizier Jade assured him that he would be admitted safely, claimed to have known [[Master Lee]] personally before and around The Cataclysm, and offered to discuss him further in Broy. She also explained that the Broyish Empire sought Penumbra as a source of energy and power, for purposes different from those of his companions.

At Vizier Jade's request, Red Caesar promised to keep their meeting secret. She departed by teleportation, leaving him unsettled enough to patrol the deck again before returning to sleep. Small disturbances among the objects she had inspected reinforced that the encounter had been real. By the time the party entered Darvinblast, he had still told none of his companions.

#### Vokenar's training during the fall

A second flashback returned to [[Vokenar]] forty years before the present, during his descent toward [[Stark]]. Confined in stone outside ordinary time and space, he was periodically awakened into an environment of clouds, stars, and elemental platforms to fight opponents constructed from the elements. His body felt trained by long practice, but he could not remember earlier awakenings; each renewed encounter with his teachers felt unfamiliar.

[[Crone]] demanded faster progress and insisted that he was not yet ready to join the people below. [[Sigil]] urged more patience and understanding, while [[Aeris]] acknowledged that they did not yet know what had happened to his world or the one beneath it. Their estimates of the time remaining before his descent ended differed, ranging from years or decades to another century. What they could prepare him for was an inevitable war, even though the wrong he would be sent to remedy was still uncertain.

Vokenar willingly offered himself as their weapon, reasoning that without their intervention he would already have died. Before resuming the fight against stronger elemental opponents, he gave the goddesses a phrase in another language to repeat at his next awakening, hoping it would remind him of his purpose and shorten the recurring explanations.

#### Ceril's watch in the mountains

While the others had been exploring the preceding dungeon, [[Ceril]] watched its entrance, which he had concealed beneath thick weeds and vines. A flying machine bearing imperial markings returned over the mountains after the party had seen it pass earlier. Ceril took the form of a mountain goat to avoid drawing attention and sent [[Nyquil]] to follow it.

The owl pursued the scout far beyond the mountains until it flew out over the sea. On returning, Nyquil conveyed images of a golden-haired, golden-bearded human in a turban and the Broyish Empire's white and gold colors. The pilot had appeared to search the land without finding his target. Nyquil interpreted the search as an effort to locate something hidden in the mountains, possibly more than the Darvinblast entrance.

Ceril then climbed to a vantage point and noticed an unnatural arrangement of terrain farther north. A powerful Mirage Arcana concealed a broad area from travelers and aerial observers alike, although he could not determine what lay beneath it. He returned to the entrance and marked the illusion's location on his map.

As he finished, a badly injured dwarf burst out of the dungeon and fled toward [[The Pit]]. Ceril sent Nyquil after him. The owl reported that the dwarf surrendered in town and was collected by members of [[Figma Brickfinger's Union]], but could not explain what happened to him afterward. Ceril later shared these observations with the party.

#### Entering the dwarven city

After resting, the reunited party continued through the doorway left by the defeated stone golem, with [[Zulu]] accompanying [[Kerben]]. Vokenar clearly remembered his training in a dream during the rest, while Red Caesar reflected privately on Vizier Jade's visit.

The descent led through increasingly broad caverns where collapsed side passages suggested that the dwarves had deliberately sealed their routes to the surface. Magma pulsed through the walls, and acrid fumes filled the passage. Vokenar created water, which Red Caesar shaped against escaping magma to cool it back into stone. This protected them from the worst of the heat, but the smoke remained hazardous. Kerben associated its smell with childhood excursions underground and was more accustomed to the poison than most of his companions.

Beyond a stone gate, the party entered a seemingly deserted public square. Kerben's stone sense revealed that its inhabitants were crowded into residences built into the surrounding walls. Their frightened shouting and hostility weighed on the newcomers. Vokenar reassured his companions and used Calm Emotions to keep fear and anger from overwhelming their purpose. Among the residents, some recognized Red Caesar as the mage who had previously broken the magic over another dwarf; word of that act offered a small opening in their distrust.

The party's immediate objective was to find [[Morel Chainsunder]], whom divine guidance had placed ahead of the search for Penumbra. Red Caesar chose the more heavily trafficked route, following marks left by wagons and equipment. It opened into another large civic space divided by pits of ash, magma, and smoldering waste.

Morel Chainsunder's voice rang through the walls, accusing the party of coming to take what belonged to the dwarves and ordering his followers to attack. Fighters emerged from the residences, including priests, spies, and [[Bat Rider]] knights. Vokenar urged the party to minimize casualties rather than deepen the population's fear. [[Domyx]] demonstrated that restraint by throwing one attacker back into his home instead of killing him. Kerben demanded in Northern that the defenders take them to Morel Chainsunder. Some fighters wavered, but the priests urged continued resistance, and the confrontation became a battle.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_011/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Mercy in the divided square

The first battle continued across the divided square. The party used force against the committed defenders while trying to make surrender possible. Red Caesar's lightning killed one of the priests, and Domyx's intimidation drove several ordinary fighters to abandon the fight and retreat into their homes, despite their fear of the magic watching them. Vokenar and Ceril supported their companions with healing and protective magic as the priests, poisoned blades, and mounted knights continued to threaten them.

Kerben deliberately subdued a spy without killing him, leaving him conscious but unable to continue fighting. The captive tried to drink acid rather than remain in the party's hands. Red Caesar knocked the container away, and the spilled liquid ate into the floor. A nearby fighter surrendered and argued that Morel Chainsunder would have to hear the newcomers out. Questioned by Kerben before being allowed to return home, he pointed toward a chapel to the south, where a service was expected later.

Vokenar began disarming and healing fallen enemies, offering to continue helping their friends if they stopped attacking. His words initially failed to reassure them: he did not speak their language, and his angelic presence frightened some of the people he hoped to comfort. He therefore combined Calm Emotions with Kerben's translation of his appeal. Several defenders, including a Bat Rider, stood down. The knight asked that his giant bat be spared, making clear that he wished to protect his mount even if the party chose to punish him.

Kerben bound the captured spy and kept him under guard, with Zulu helping to discourage resistance. The spy remained loyal to Morel Chainsunder and tried to warn the others against the party's influence, but could no longer command their attention. Vokenar's care for the wounded and Kerben's assurance that they sought the leader rather than the inhabitants had begun to change the encounter.

The fighting had not ended everywhere. Across the ash-filled ravine, Domyx still faced a group whose spy and remaining fighters resisted surrender. He disarmed and restrained the spy while Ceril turned his magic toward that struggle. The party had secured captives and won some defections, but still had to overcome this last pocket of resistance.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_011/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Survivors, testimony, and new allies

Red Caesar ended the remaining resistance with fire. Domyx's Stoic Laurels protected him from his companion's magic, but the fighters around him were killed. Vokenar treated the wounded who could still be saved, although some of the fallen were already beyond his help. A fallen knight's bat escaped without being pursued. The party rested alongside the surviving dwarves, and their unexpected care for defeated opponents softened some of the watching residents' hostility. The city remained fearful and antagonistic, but its pressure on the party had lessened.

The captured spy still refused to reveal Morel Chainsunder's secrets. Kerben tried his keyring's truth magic, but gained no answers. Rather than execute the prisoner or take him along, the party accepted the surrendered Bat Rider's offer to remain and guard him. The knight warned that defectors would have little safety among Morel Chainsunder's loyal followers.

Through Kerben, Vokenar offered the survivors the prospect of being welcomed by the dwarves above once the situation was resolved. The spy briefly broke his silence to challenge that account. He invoked a catastrophe in 1420 and cave-ins ten years earlier, accused [[Figma Brickfinger]] of being no better than the leader the party opposed, and expressed hope that Morel Chainsunder would take revenge. These were the captive's accusations; the party did not establish the underlying history during this exchange.

Two surviving fighters introduced themselves as [[Gammix]] and [[Tammix]] of the [[Steelfend Clan]]. They said they had fled underground hoping for a better life and had no family left. If the party could defeat Morel Chainsunder, they wanted to take up arms alongside it.

Before trusting the brothers' safety to that decision, Red Caesar examined the magic still affecting them. He found observation magic and a further enchantment that appeared capable of punishing disobedience. His dispelling removed both, and unexpectedly reached beyond the two dwarves in front of him. Gammix and Tammix explained that their whole clan had undergone the same ceremony. Red Caesar therefore had reason to believe that he had freed the wider Steelfend Clan, although its other members had yet to be found.

#### The market and the cost of defection

The brothers helped the party understand the city's routes. Following their advice, the group found a chain that raised a bridge out of the ashes. The direct southern approach to the chapel was barred by two magically sealed gates; Kerben's stone sense confirmed the second barrier and the sanctuary beyond. With the city on alert, the brothers feared that the expected service would be canceled. The party chose to backtrack through another passage rather than force that entrance.

They reached an abandoned market square whose residents shuttered their windows at the newcomers' approach. Red Caesar found a chest containing gold coins and a large amethyst. Vokenar objected to stealing from the people they were trying to liberate. Ceril treated the treasure as compensation for the trouble the dwarves had caused, and the group chose to take it into their shared stores. Vokenar left the decision to his companions while declining to participate in the theft, acknowledging that they saw their methods as a means to an end in a world different from the one he had known. Crone's lessons had emphasized justifying means by their ends, but he maintained his objection to taking the dwarves' property.

At the next intersection, residents above them overturned boiling oil onto the party and its new allies, shouting accusations of heresy and betrayal. Ceril used Mage Hand to tip the barrel back long enough to spare Gammix, but Tammix collapsed beneath the scalding oil. Kerben healed him and helped him back to his feet. Tammix thanked him and recognized that many people in the city would now treat him as an enemy; the attackers had condemned his entire clan.

#### The gathering atrium

The party continued into a broader atrium that Gammix and Tammix remembered as the city's parade and gathering space. They recalled an earlier period of hope after leaving Figma Brickfinger's community, when an underground future had seemed possible and the public space had been better cared for. Their recollections added another perspective to the spy's accusations and the city's present misery.

Morel Chainsunder's voice returned, calling on those still loyal to oppose the party and denouncing Figma Brickfinger's Union. More defenders emerged, including priests, spies, and Bat Riders. This time the residents closed their windows almost immediately, obeying warnings that the newcomers intended to kill them all.

Kerben again demanded that the defenders bring Morel Chainsunder forward, but the spy rejected him and was killed as the second battle began. Gammix and Tammix stood with the party against their former allies. Ceril brought moonlight down into the atrium, with the image of [[Crone]] appearing as a crescent overhead. Domyx advanced within that light unharmed by his companion's magic as the fighting spread through the gathering space.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_011/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### The atrium's last defenders

The atrium battle continued against defenders who remained committed to Morel Chainsunder. Gammix and Tammix supported the party with their crossbows, while Vokenar sustained his companions through the prolonged fighting. The party eventually overcame the remaining priests, knights, and fighters; fleeing bats were allowed to escape.

During the battle, Red Caesar gained and used Resilient Sphere to shelter himself behind a barrier covered in warding runes. He recognized the inscriptions as the same ones that had protected Heaven's Bulb from the surrounding waters. His new protection thus drew directly on the refuge and magical education that Vizier Jade had invoked during her private visit.

#### Refuge with the Steelfend Clan

After the fighting, Vokenar tended his companions, and Gammix and Tammix offered a safer place to recover. They led the party to the Steelfend Clan house, which occupied a wing of the complex. There the brothers rejoined a household of women, children, and other clan members. Their earlier statement that they had no family left was not explained.

The clan's matron spoke Western and welcomed the people who had brought the brothers back. She particularly thanked Red Caesar, confirming that the clan had felt the magic over it disappear when he dispelled the shared effect. What he had inferred from the brothers' ceremony was now confirmed: the wider Steelfend Clan had been released from Morel Chainsunder's watch. The matron gave the party deep-world coinage, some of which had previously been under his control, and asked them to spend it wisely above. She spoke hopefully of her people rejoining society.

Among the fallen defenders' belongings, Ceril found a map to the [[Far Helm Clan]] keep. It marked a nearby cavern where the clan had hidden valued treasure against the possibility that the deep-world venture failed. The map's bearer and his kin had died in the fighting, leaving no one from that group to return for the cache. Ceril shared the discovery with the party, giving them a new destination to investigate.

The party accepted the clan's hospitality and rested in its wing while other clans remained hostile elsewhere in Darvinblast. Gammix and Tammix asked to leave the traveling group and stay with their family to protect it. The party agreed, and Vokenar asked them to spread word that the newcomers were trying to help rather than harm the dwarves. By the session's close, the party had gained a liberated household and refuge, but had not yet confronted Morel Chainsunder or located the Penumbra it sought in the region.

### Summary

- [[Vizier Jade]] secretly approached [[Red Caesar]] aboard [[The Opal]], invited him to [[Broy]], promised safe entry, and threatened his companions if they obstructed his potential. He kept the meeting private.
- A flashback revealed [[Vokenar]]'s training during his fall, his lost memories of earlier awakenings, and his willingness to serve the goddesses against an anticipated war.
- [[Ceril]] and [[Nyquil]] investigated an imperial scout. Ceril located and mapped concealed terrain in the northern mountains and learned that an injured dwarf had surrendered to [[Figma Brickfinger's Union]].
- The party entered [[Darvinblast]] and fought its defenders while attempting to spare and heal those willing to stand down.
- [[Kerben]] captured a spy, and Red Caesar prevented the prisoner's suicide. The captive resisted questioning and made unverified accusations about [[Figma Brickfinger]] and earlier disasters.
- [[Gammix]] and [[Tammix]] joined the party; Red Caesar dispelled the shared magic over them and the [[Steelfend Clan]].
- The party took gold coins and a large amethyst from a market chest despite Vokenar's objection.
- Ceril protected Gammix during a boiling-oil ambush, and Kerben rescued Tammix after he collapsed.
- The party won a second battle in the gathering atrium. Red Caesar gained Resilient Sphere, using runes associated with [[Heaven's Bulb]].
- The Steelfend Clan confirmed its liberation, offered refuge, and gifted deep-world coinage. Ceril recovered a map to the [[Far Helm Clan]] keep.
- Gammix and Tammix stayed to safeguard their family. Morel Chainsunder and the local Penumbra remained the party's immediate objectives.

The private visit and the training flashback placed two different claims on the party's future. Vizier Jade sought to draw Red Caesar toward the Broyish Empire through education, shared connections, and protection backed by threats. Vokenar offered himself to divine service despite uncertainty about the worlds below and the war he was being prepared to face. His response in Darvinblast gave that commitment a practical form: he treated frightened inhabitants and defeated enemies as people who could still be helped.

The dwarven conflict unfolded through changes in trust as much as through victories. Intimidation, healing, translation, and calming magic persuaded some defenders to stop fighting, while Red Caesar's dispelling freed an entire clan bound through a shared ceremony. The brothers' defection exposed them to retaliation, making their eventual reunion and the clan's hospitality a significant outcome. The spy's testimony and the brothers' memories also complicated the apparent division between the surface dwarves and the deep-world cult without settling their historical grievances.

The party's liberating purpose did not erase its internal differences or the costs of its methods. Vokenar opposed the market theft, several committed defenders died, and much of the city remained hostile. At the close, the Steelfend Clan provided a temporary refuge and evidence that the wider population could be freed, while the map and Ceril's marked illusion offered distinct leads. Neither the cult's overall hold nor the regional search for Penumbra had been resolved.
