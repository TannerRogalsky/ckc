## Session 037

### Chunk 0000

- [[The Opal]] sails across the [[Gray Wastes]] toward [[Gaokerena]]. Imperial soldiers teleport aboard, but [[Vokenar]]'s consecration prevents their fiendish reinforcements from joining them. The party defeats the boarders and takes their leader alive.
- The captive reveals that [[Emperor Shen]] intends to fire [[Starfall]] at [[Sigil]] that day. A magical secrecy condition petrifies him as he divulges the plan.
- Vokenar contacts [[Crone]], who is recovering among Gaokerena's roots. She says Emperor Shen is delaying the shot to confront [[Domyx]], that the goddesses cannot intervene directly, and that [[Ceril]] will receive help through the tree. She also gives hope that [[Vizier Jade]] will resist Emperor Shen.
- The Opal anchors at the living land around Gaokerena. Ceril learns that the plants he grew to feed [[Aeris]] in session 024 restored the tree's lower growth and spread into an evolving forest with wildlife.
- Ceril opens a passage through Gaokerena to [[Arkadia]], where he and Vokenar meet Aeris and Sigil. Aeris grants [[Ceril's Star]], a lasting star whose prayer can suspend time and shelter the party for recuperation.
- The party reunites, shares food prepared by [[Kerben]], and follows human-sized and titan-sized tracks toward Starfall.

### Chunk 0001

- The party reaches [[Axis Mundi]], where [[Emperor Shen]] and [[Vizier Jade]] await beside the overgrown [[Starfall]]. Emperor Shen intends to destroy [[Sigil]] and grant [[Domyx]] divine power whether Domyx accepts it or not.
- Starfall makes Emperor Shen invulnerable. While Domyx challenges him, [[Red Caesar]] establishes a private telepathic bond with Vizier Jade and proposes rebuilding the [[Broyish Empire]] together.
- Vizier Jade asks Red Caesar to restrain her so that Emperor Shen must load the final ammunition himself. She deliberately accepts the restraint; Emperor Shen condemns her apparent failure and petrifies her. Her parting advice is to avoid repeating his mistakes.
- Emperor Shen feeds the final piece of ammunition into Starfall: the [[Antumbra]] the party planted in session 025. The sabotage destroys Starfall completely and ends his invulnerability before he can fire at Sigil.
- [[Farron Acathian II]] and [[Zohai Lapis]], the previously spared titan pair, return to defend Emperor Shen, lending him supernatural protection. Emperor Shen also summons the ghosts of fallen titans.
- The party defeats Farron Acathian II and the ghosts. Zohai Lapis and Emperor Shen remain in the fight.

### Chunk 0002

- [[Kerben]] kills [[Zohai Lapis]], ending the remaining ally's protection of [[Emperor Shen]]. [[Domyx]] defeats his grandfather, who calls on the [[Vanir]] before dying.
- [[Vokenar]] restores [[Vizier Jade]] from petrification. Her betrayal has cost her the magic Emperor Shen controlled; she warns that the old gods are about to return.
- Vizier Jade's permanent ward around [[Axis Mundi]] prevents magical escape as well as entry. The party prepares to face the returning gods.
- [[Entropie]] and [[Dunkelkalt]] manifest as hostile counterparts to the sister goddesses. Dunkelkalt repeatedly casts Domyx outside reality, while Entropie manipulates luck against the party.
- Kerben kills Dunkelkalt. The party remains engaged with Entropie, sustained by Vokenar's divine healing.

### Chunk 0003

- The party defeats [[Entropie]], with [[Domyx]] striking the final blow. Before dissipating, Entropie casts a Wish that brings forth the last [[Vanir]], the resurrected [[Emperor Shen]].
- The last Vanir rises in a colossal divine form resembling [[Domyx]] and [[Emperor Shen]], releasing a wave of force. Session 038 confirms that this is Emperor Shen's resurrected body. Four mysterious obelisks rise around the battlefield; their purpose remains unknown.
- The party faces the last Vanir with [[Vizier Jade]] and [[Tango]] sheltered nearby. [[Ceril's Star]] remains unused, and [[Aeris]]'s release from her mortal captivity has not yet been established.
- [[Kerben]] passes the [[Potion of Eels]] to [[Red Caesar]] for later use.

### Summary

The journey to Axis Mundi exposes both the urgency of Emperor Shen's plan and the limits of his control. His boarding force fails because The Opal's consecration excludes its fiendish support, but the surviving soldier confirms that the attack on Sigil is imminent. Crone explains why there is still time: Emperor Shen's pride demands that Domyx witness the destruction. She also directs the party toward help that Ceril unknowingly prepared months earlier.

Gaokerena's flourishing lower roots are the consequence of Ceril's attempt to feed Aeris during her imprisonment. His modest planting has become an entire living refuge in the Gray Wastes, restoring the world tree and linking him to its power. Passing through it brings Ceril and Vokenar to the goddesses. Aeris answers their coming danger with Ceril's Star, giving the party a means of withdrawing from the flow of time to recover when the need is greatest.

At Starfall, Domyx refuses the destiny his grandfather insists on giving him. Red Caesar offers Vizier Jade a future for Broy beyond Emperor Shen's ambitions, and she makes her resistance explicit. She arranges for Emperor Shen to handle the final ammunition himself, accepting petrification and the loss of her power. The final piece proves to be the Antumbra, deliberately reserved for this moment. Its detonation fulfills the party's long-delayed sabotage and strips Emperor Shen of Starfall's protection.

The resulting battle draws together enemies the party previously spared and the older divine conflict beneath the world. Emperor Shen's titan allies fall, and Domyx defeats him, but his death calls Entropie and Dunkelkalt back into the world. Vokenar frees Vizier Jade, who warns the party and can no longer rely on her former magic. Her lasting abjuration also prevents the party from simply departing the battlefield by planar travel.

The party defeats both returning gods, yet Entropie's final Wish brings forth the last Vanir, Emperor Shen's resurrected body, as confirmed in session 038. The old gods have returned to strike at the party for serving their enemies, Aeris, Sigil, and Crone. The colossal manifestation and four rising obelisks leave that conflict unresolved. Starfall is destroyed and its shot at Sigil prevented; Aeris's mortal fate remains unconfirmed, and Ceril still holds the unused blessing that may sustain the party through the confrontation.
