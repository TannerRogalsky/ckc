# Session 037

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_037/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### The emperor's boarding force

[[The Opal]] crossed the [[Gray Wastes]] beneath the world, skimming dunes of powder in the unfamiliar gravity. Occasional streams of water fell from above and disappeared into the dust. The air was thin but breathable, and [[Gaokerena]] gave the party a destination beneath the coast of the [[Broyish Empire]]. They rested during the voyage, expecting several more hours before reaching the tree.

[[Kerben]] and [[Ceril]] spotted imperial soldiers materializing aboard the ship, including attackers in the crow's nest. The newcomers struggled with The Opal's movement, and the party realized that their expected fiendish support had failed to arrive. [[Vokenar]]'s consecration of the ship excluded those reinforcements, leaving the mortal boarders to fight alone.

The attack threatened both the party and the ship's course. Kerben fought while tending the helm, and [[Domyx]] helped steady it before exploiting the weaker gravity to reach an enemy above the deck. [[Red Caesar]] struck across the boarding force with magic, while Ceril's moonlight and Vokenar's radiance helped break the assault. Domyx brought one of the officers down from the crow's nest. The party defeated the attackers and deliberately kept their leader alive; the others died of their wounds, and their bodies were put overboard into the dust.

Magic enabled the party to question the captive in Broyish. His loyalty to [[Emperor Shen]] survived the emperor's retreat from Broy. He confirmed that Emperor Shen had expected fiends to reinforce the boarding party despite [[Vizier Jade]]'s warning that the ship's consecration would prevent it. His account made the failed attack another instance of the emperor overriding advice.

The prisoner initially invoked an oath of secrecy, then disclosed that Emperor Shen intended to fire [[Starfall]] that very day, destroy the sun and [[Sigil]], and free mortals from the gods. As he gave away the plan, petrification spread upward from his feet. The party recognized a magical condition tied to revealing the truth rather than an ordinary injury. They moved the resulting statue aside in the ship's common area; they did not establish a means of freeing him.

#### Crone's counsel

The Opal continued toward Gaokerena, giving the party time to recover. Vokenar contacted [[Crone]] through his divine mantle, whose scales took on a silver appearance. He saw her lying among the world tree's roots, receiving restorative light, with [[Aeris]] and Sigil nearby. The two other goddesses were speaking, but Vokenar could not hear their conversation.

Crone was still weak and initially met him with her customary sharpness. Nevertheless, her treatment of him showed growing respect. She confirmed that the tree was the right destination and that the party could arrive before the shot. Emperor Shen was waiting for his grandson: his pride demanded Domyx's presence at the culmination of his plan. Without that desire, Crone said, Sigil would already have been destroyed.

The goddesses could not simply intervene in their own creation. Crone directed the party toward assistance that Ceril had unknowingly prepared, explaining that power would return to him through the tree he had nurtured. She also offered hope about Vizier Jade. Her will was too strong to remain indefinitely under Emperor Shen's control, though Crone did not promise exactly how or when her resistance would take effect. Her suggestion that Vokenar might eventually die in a greater battle remained a warning about the future rather than an account of anything that had happened.

#### The forest beneath the sky

[[Raxxy]] called attention to the land ahead. Gaokerena's canopy supported the lower sky, while its immense trunk descended to a living island in the Gray Wastes. Grass, moss, shrubs, and trees spread outward from its base, creating a striking interruption in the surrounding dust.

Ceril recognized the origin of the growth. Months earlier, he had planted and magically nourished seeds to feed Aeris during her captivity in Starfall. Those plants had taken root in the world tree's lower growth. With Aeris's power, a seemingly modest act of care had helped restore Gaokerena and generated a forest extending far beyond the original planting.

The new refuge had become an ecosystem. Flowers had diverged into unfamiliar forms, and insects, rabbits, hawks, and elk lived among the vegetation. Its inhabitants had passed through many generations in only a few months, adapting to the gravity beneath the world. The party could trace Ceril's magic in the growth, but neither its scale nor its rapid development had been his deliberate design.

The Opal anchored with its bow against the living land. When Ceril touched Gaokerena, golden light appeared at his fingers and a voice reached him through the tree. Its invitation extended through the connected life of plants across realms. A doorway opened in the trunk, allowing his passage through plants to cross into [[Arkadia]]. That passage depended on Gaokerena's extraordinary connection between worlds.

Ceril and Vokenar entered. Domyx, Red Caesar, and Kerben remained outside, where Kerben gathered roots and hunted game for a meal. Red Caesar supplied heat with small workings of magic, and Domyx helped with the cooking. Their practical preparations continued while the other two sought divine help.

#### A star for Ceril

In Arkadia, Ceril and Vokenar met Aeris and Sigil directly. Both had already survived seeing the goddesses in their true presence; the same safety could not simply be assumed for companions encountering them for the first time.

Sigil listened to their account of the journey, including the rescue involving the roc. Her surprise made clear that the goddesses were not following every mortal event with perfect knowledge. She considered the task and its dangers unsafe and questioned the wisdom of what had been asked of the party. Aeris defended mortal choice. She described the freedom and unpredictability of mortal souls as qualities that even divine beings could not fully control or foresee.

Aeris also explained the old resentment beneath the present conflict. The [[Vanir]] had opposed the sister goddesses, and their titan descendants had preserved that hostility. Emperor Shen's attack belonged to that much older struggle over the world and its creation.

Aeris gave Ceril a lasting place among the stars: [[Ceril's Star]]. Through prayer, he could suspend the passage of time and shelter his companions long enough to rest, eat, and recover before returning to the moment and place they had left. The blessing could be invoked even if he became unconscious. It offered a refuge during a crisis rather than a guarantee that the coming battle would be harmless. Sigil urged them to return safely.

Ceril and Vokenar rejoined their companions for the meal. Ceril renewed their protective feast, choosing protection against thunder and force for the confrontation ahead, while Red Caesar adjusted his own protection against lightning. They then followed wooded paths toward [[Axis Mundi]]. The tracks included both human-sized travelers and enormous titan footprints. Some smaller traces suggested earlier concealment, but the present route could be followed. The party left the living refuge and approached Starfall.

### Chunk 0001

#### An inheritance imposed

At Axis Mundi, the party found Starfall shining like a vast ring amid the trees. Ceril's growth had spread over and around the apparatus, giving the clearing the appearance of a dark forest beneath a golden sunset. Hot wind came from the weapon. Emperor Shen and Vizier Jade stood on opposite sides, with Vizier Jade holding the final ammunition.

Emperor Shen welcomed Domyx as the heir to the divine dynasty he intended to create. Destroying Sigil would transfer power to Domyx, whether Domyx consented or not. His vision left no room for his grandson to refuse the destiny arranged for him.

Domyx challenged his grandfather's claim to have made himself powerful. The emperor acknowledged the help of others but regarded obedience as the necessary price of his rule. He defended his early governance of Broy by claiming to have met his citizens' needs and won their loyalty. Other people touched by the sky, however, could not be allowed to exercise comparable power. His promised liberation would still place mortal lives under his authority.

Vokenar encouraged a sparring contest between grandfather and grandson to gain time. Domyx's first blow failed to injure Emperor Shen: Starfall made him invulnerable. The emperor answered with his staff, which Domyx blocked. Vokenar then encouraged him to speak about his history, prolonging the encounter while Red Caesar established a private telepathic bond with Vizier Jade.

#### Vizier Jade's choice

Red Caesar recalled Vizier Jade's earlier insistence that he survive and visit Broy. Their conversation returned to his search for a place where he could build something lasting. He criticized the elves' attachment to the past and future at the expense of the present, the dwarves' narrow attention to immediate concerns, and the League's uncertainty about its direction. Broy still offered possibilities, if Emperor Shen's ambition could be removed from its future.

Red Caesar proposed that he and Vizier Jade help build a new empire together. Vizier Jade agreed that they could make something better. Their exchange was private; it did not establish a public appointment or a commitment made by the whole party.

Vizier Jade then asked Red Caesar to stop her from loading the weapon. Emperor Shen needed to handle the final stone himself. Red Caesar bound her with magic, and she deliberately accepted the restraint while pretending to struggle. The ammunition fell near Starfall's threshold but did not roll into it.

Emperor Shen condemned her apparent failure and turned her to stone. Before their telepathic connection ended, Vizier Jade urged Red Caesar to preserve his dream without repeating the emperor's mistakes. Emperor Shen had once held a similar dream, she said, before a wrong turn changed what he became. Her acceptance of punishment made her resistance explicit, while her warning put responsibility for Broy's future back in Red Caesar's hands.

#### Antumbra destroys Starfall

Domyx tried to seize Emperor Shen and prevent him from loading the ammunition, but his grandfather's power dragged him along. The emperor fed the last stone into Starfall. It was [[Antumbra]], the altered stone Kerben had planted among The White Drake's Penumbra supplies. The final placement of the stone ensured that the emperor himself completed the fatal interaction.

The weapon's glow collapsed into darkness and trapped stars. Emperor Shen's hand was caught in the field before the destruction erupted outward. The blast separated him from Domyx, threw the party among the trees, and momentarily swallowed sound. Vizier Jade's statue remained fixed, driven farther into the ground.

Starfall was utterly destroyed. The intended shot at Sigil never occurred, and the protection that had made Emperor Shen invulnerable ended with the apparatus. His mask and robes were gone, exposing the familial resemblance beneath them. He could now bleed and be injured.

The victory immediately drew new opponents into the clearing. [[Farron Acathian II]] and [[Zohai Lapis]], the titan pair the party had spared near Broy, returned to defend Emperor Shen. Supernatural links from the two allies gave him further protection, though they did not restore Starfall's absolute invulnerability.

#### The mortal emperor's defenders

Domyx brought his new equipment into the fight, using the [[Kaboom Ring]] before closing with the [[Cestus of the Clear Sky]]. Ceril summoned moonlight, while Red Caesar successfully manifested a magical sword in the likeness of a small blade from [[Para and Bellum]]. Vokenar's dirge weakened the enemies and denied them healing. Kerben used the trees, ruins, and his exchanges of position with [[Tango]] to maintain a line of fire.

Emperor Shen summoned the spirits of six fallen titans. They surrounded Kerben and Ceril, and their assault drained Ceril's vitality. The fight forced the party to divide its attention between the living defenders and the dead.

Domyx killed Farron Acathian II amid Ceril's moonlight, breaking one of Emperor Shen's protective connections. Red Caesar then used the Akasha grenade recovered from the Tome Keeper's Pyramid to destroy four of the titan spirits. Vokenar drove away the remaining two with divine power. The captured energy had been expended, and the immediate threat from the ghosts ended.

Emperor Shen continued fighting. His lightning struck at Red Caesar and Ceril; Red Caesar's preparation helped him withstand it, while Ceril was badly hurt and lost his moonlight. Ceril turned to his healing chalice, and the others continued pressing the emperor and Zohai Lapis.

Kerben exchanged places with Tango to reach a ruined pillar of Starfall and fired on Zohai Lapis from that position. Domyx injured his grandfather directly. Emperor Shen met his restored mortality with a disturbing pleasure, recalling what it meant to be able to fail. Farron Acathian II and the ghosts were gone, but Zohai Lapis still sustained him, and neither surviving enemy had yet fallen.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_037/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Emperor Shen's death

The party continued the battle without withdrawing. Red Caesar's magical sword cut at Zohai Lapis while he attacked Emperor Shen's mind, and Vokenar restored Ceril enough to keep him in the fight. Kerben finally killed Zohai Lapis with his rifle. The titan's icy form broke apart, ending the emperor's remaining connection to a living defender.

Emperor Shen accused Domyx's friends of holding him back, comparing them to the assistance he now dismissed as a hindrance in his own life. Domyx answered through his actions, warning his companions when his grandfather prepared another lightning assault and helping them survive it. The emperor's demand for independence meant rejecting care; Domyx continued to fight as part of the group.

Red Caesar's withering magic, Ceril's starlight, Kerben's gunfire, and Vokenar's divine power wore Emperor Shen down. He blamed the sister goddesses and treated the party as vessels of Aeris, Sigil, and Crone rather than people choosing to oppose him.

As defeat approached, Emperor Shen called on the Vanir, naming [[Entropie]] and [[Dunkelkalt]]. Domyx struck the final blow to his chest and sent him against a rock. For a short interval, the emperor remained conscious, bleeding and unable to move. He held Domyx's gaze with an unsettling smile. The party used that interval to heal, but it was not a full rest. Emperor Shen then died.

#### Vizier Jade's warning and the closed battlefield

Vokenar freed Vizier Jade from petrification. He thanked her for what he understood as her part in bringing about Antumbra's success. Her release did not restore the powers Emperor Shen had controlled: he had sealed them away as the cost of her betrayal. She would have to begin again without her former magic.

Vizier Jade warned that Emperor Shen and the titans had conspired with the long-dead Vanir. Those gods regarded the sisters' creation as an affront and were about to return. Their emergence was imminent, and she no longer possessed the power with which to help resist them.

She attempted to escape by magic, but the attempt failed. Red Caesar considered withdrawing through planar travel and returning later. Vizier Jade explained that her permanent private sanctum enclosed the battlefield in both directions. The same protection that had kept magical travelers from reaching Axis Mundi prevented the party from leaving it that way. Ceril also wanted to finish the struggle rather than leave the divine task unresolved.

The companions made what preparations they could. Vokenar consumed Aramil's tincture to recover magical strength. Red Caesar used magic and potions to improve his movement and survival, and Domyx drank his potion of lummethood to strengthen himself. Red Caesar tried to shield Domyx's mind with the recovered Mind Blank scroll, but the attempt failed. The party remained in the clearing as the old gods manifested.

#### Entropie and Dunkelkalt

Entropie appeared as a dark counterpart to Aeris, with stars and hostile celestial power. Dunkelkalt combined contrasting forms in a winged, muscular body with two crowned heads: one cold and blue, the other dark and fiery. Together they stood against the three sister goddesses.

The party quickly discovered that familiar methods did not work equally against both. Dunkelkalt resisted fire, cold, and poison, but weapons could harm him. Entropie resisted the radiant and deathly energies normally central to several companions' magic, while other natural forces could wound him. Those discoveries changed how the party fought.

Domyx enlarged himself, leapt onto Dunkelkalt, and dragged him toward the ground. His sash softened the fall, and Kerben exchanged places with Tango to join the close attack. Dunkelkalt answered with Exodus Mundi, casting Domyx outside reality into a cold, suffocating field of stars. Domyx returned, but the god repeated the banishment to break his hold and disrupt the fight.

Entropie attacked the party's fortune as well as their bodies. He changed an unsuccessful strike into a dangerous hit against Ceril and later interfered with Kerben's aim. Ceril opposed that manipulation with his own omen of woe. Dark stars briefly held Red Caesar in place, while the changing threat forced the companions to keep adapting.

Red Caesar used a great magical hand to grapple Dunkelkalt, then turned its force and his fire against Entropie. Ceril considered whether the refuge of his star might allow a prolonged binding of a god, but the separation from the battlefield would prevent that plan. He kept the blessing in reserve and relied on healing and primal acid to remain useful against an enemy resistant to much of his customary magic.

Vokenar called down a moving celestial pillar. Its fire injured Entropie while its light restored the party, and his bond through the [[Ioun of the Gilded Savior]] helped carry that protection to Red Caesar. The combination allowed them to survive attacks that threatened to overwhelm individual companions.

Kerben fired on Dunkelkalt. Entropie spoiled one shot, but the next killed the two-headed god. As he fell, Dunkelkalt struck at Red Caesar and tried to cast him outside reality. Red Caesar resisted the final banishment, and Dunkelkalt sank away into the ground.

Kerben then drank the [[Potion of Fluid Adamantite]]. His body took on a metallic appearance and became more resistant to harm as he turned toward Entropie. Tango sheltered among the ruins, and Vizier Jade also remained under cover. One returning Vanir was gone; the party was still fighting the other, sustained by Vokenar's celestial light.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_037/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### Entropie's final wish

The fight narrowed around Entropie. Vokenar's hammer and divine power drew the god's recognition of Sigil's influence. Ceril continued attacking with nature and starlight, while Red Caesar's great magical hand supported the assault. Entropie's form shed sparks and stars as the party wore him down.

Domyx delivered the final blow. Entropie dwindled into a few remaining stars, but his defeat gave him time for one last act. He cast a wish that could not be countered, calling for Emperor Shen to rise again and become the last Vanir.

#### The last Vanir rises

The dead emperor's body was transformed into a colossal divine form. He was recognizably Emperor Shen, now bearing the youthful familial appearance that also connected him to Domyx. Feathery energy and immense power surrounded the resurrected figure. The party's victory over the old gods had produced another incarnation of the enemy they had just killed.

His emergence released a surge of force across the clearing. The protection Ceril had chosen for the feast helped the companions endure it, though the blast broke some of their maintained magic. Tango and Vizier Jade remained sheltered nearby. Four enormous obelisks rose around the battlefield, but their purpose was not yet established.

Ceril offered the refuge of his star, then held it for use during the coming battle rather than invoking it immediately. Kerben passed the [[Potion of Eels]] to Red Caesar for later use; Red Caesar had not yet drunk it. Vokenar's celestial presence still offered healing, and the party faced the renewed threat with their surviving preparations.

Starfall was destroyed, the shot at Sigil had been prevented, and both returning Vanir had fallen. Emperor Shen nevertheless stood before the party as the last Vanir. Their final confrontation was only beginning. Ceril's Star remained unused, and the session had not established Aeris's release from her mortal captivity.

### Summary

- Imperial boarders failed to seize The Opal; its consecration kept their fiendish support away. A captive disclosed the imminent shot at Sigil before a secrecy condition petrified him.
- Crone explained that Emperor Shen was waiting for Domyx and directed the party to help through Gaokerena.
- Ceril's planting for Aeris had restored Gaokerena's lower growth and created a living forest beneath the world.
- Aeris gave Ceril his own enduring star and a refuge where the party could suspend time to recover.
- Vizier Jade privately agreed to help Red Caesar build a better future for Broy and accepted petrification to force Emperor Shen to load the final ammunition himself.
- Antumbra destroyed Starfall completely and ended Emperor Shen's invulnerability before the weapon fired at Sigil.
- Farron Acathian II, Zohai Lapis, and the summoned titan spirits fell. Domyx killed Emperor Shen.
- Vokenar freed Vizier Jade, who had lost her former powers and warned of the Vanir's return. Her permanent ward prevented magical escape.
- The party defeated Dunkelkalt and Entropie, but Entropie's final wish resurrected Emperor Shen as the last Vanir.
- Four unexplained obelisks rose around the renewed battlefield. Ceril kept his star refuge in reserve, and Kerben gave Red Caesar the Potion of Eels for later use.

The journey brought consequences from earlier acts of protection into the final struggle. Vokenar's consecration saved The Opal from the boarders' fiendish support, and Ceril's attempt to feed Aeris had become a forest, a restored part of the world tree, and a route to divine assistance. The goddesses' disagreement about mortal danger and choice also challenged the idea that the companions were merely obedient instruments of divine will.

At Axis Mundi, Domyx resisted the inheritance Emperor Shen intended to impose on him. His grandfather promised freedom from the gods while insisting on obedience to his own dynasty and denying others comparable power. Domyx continued choosing his companions, even when Emperor Shen described their care as weakness. His killing blow ended the emperor's mortal life without settling the older conflict behind it.

Red Caesar's private exchange with Vizier Jade joined a political hope to an immediate act of resistance. She accepted the emperor's punishment so that he would complete the loading of Antumbra himself. Her warning about his former ideals qualified the promise of a new Broy: replacing the ruler would not, by itself, ensure that a new dream avoided the same mistakes. She survived the petrification through Vokenar's intervention, but her betrayal left her without her old powers.

The party's long-delayed sabotage succeeded decisively. Starfall ceased to exist, and Sigil survived the intended shot. The clearing nevertheless became a battlefield against the Vanir, with Vizier Jade's lasting ward preventing an easy withdrawal. The companions adapted their abilities to hostile divine powers and defeated both returning gods, only for Entropie's last wish to transform the fallen emperor into their final opponent. They ended the session alive and together, with a new means of recuperation still available, while Aeris's mortal fate and the outcome of the confrontation remained unsettled.
