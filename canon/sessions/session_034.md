## Session 034

### Chunk 0000

At [[The White Drake]], [[Obould]] and [[Lady Jacinthe]] confronted the cost of the [[Penumbra]] transfer that had prolonged Obould's captivity. [[Red Caesar]] revealed that the party had secretly planted [[Antumbra]] in that supply, and the group concluded that it would permanently annihilate [[Starfall]] if the weapon consumed it, though they could not yet confirm whether the sabotage had occurred.

Obould permanently resigned as captain of [[The Opal]], named [[Kerben]] his successor, and proposed to Lady Jacinthe with [[Obould's Wedding Band]]. She accepted, and they agreed to lead [[The League of New Stark]] together. [[Raxxy]] and most of the crew chose to remain with Kerben, while [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] and [[Ulrich Fjoller]] left the ship to prepare for their child's birth and seek a home associated with The White Drake or [[Southport]]. [[Vokenar]] gave [[Jack Harvey's Portrait]] to [[David Harvey]], who hung it aboard The White Drake.

The party stopped in [[Cutlass Cray]], where Kerben recruited [[Vorgan of the Stage]] as The Opal's performer. Red Caesar returned the [[Tomb of Lenth the Rugged]] to [[Pleasance MacLenth]] at [[Bookbinders Cray]], learning that her grandfather had returned to the Southport region late in life. The party acquired three powerful Ioun stones before sailing to [[Tome Keeper's Pyramid]].

The pyramid's flooded lower floor contained unusually shallow [[Akasha]], perhaps drawn upward after the sky's repair. The party survived the substance and several magical traps while a familiar voice taunted them from above. A trapped chest yielded two caches of valuable diamonds, but another ward briefly turned [[Domyx]] against his companions before Vokenar broke the domination.

### Chunk 0001

At the pyramid's summit, the party discovered that the fiend was [[Farraday]], Kerben's former employer and [[Jack Harvey]]'s old benefactor. Farraday's supposed crypt had been empty because he had survived as an [[Arcanoloth]], and Jack Harvey had used the false tomb to guide later explorers toward his hidden [[Penumbra]].

Kerben immediately destroyed the [[Tome of Satariel]], severing Farraday's ledger and material link to hell. After a battle dominated by competing magic and countermeasures, Kerben destroyed Farraday's body on Stark and forced him back to hell. Most of the tome was lost, but [[Red Caesar]] recovered three intact spells. Vokenar established the pyramid as a return point, leaving it available as a future base.

The party visited the [[Dawn Market]] in destabilized [[Broy]], where [[Emperor Shen]]'s flight had left the [[Broyish Empire]] in a militarily weakened power vacuum. [[Geoffrey the Younger]] crafted three reusable magical bullets for [[Kerben]], and the party revisited [[Qian Hu]]'s old-world relic shop. They then traveled to [[The Pit]], where [[Sigrid Forgewelt]] transformed Domyx's hammer into the [[Cestus of the Clear Sky]], a weapon that spreads a living image of the restored heavens across his body.

### Chunk 0002

The party brought [[Yalet Mora]] to confront [[Yalet Aurum]] near [[Brimbolyn]], but found the golden Galeb Duurr apparently crushed beneath a fallen moon fragment. The party refused to attack a helpless opponent. Yalet Aurum surrendered a massive golden brain to settle the feud and let his brother depart believing he had finally outlasted him.

After Yalet Mora left, Yalet Aurum admitted that the moon fragment had strengthened rather than killed him. He had staged his decline to give his insecure brother closure and intended to reshape the surrounding land once unobserved.

At [[House Kiirnodel]], [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] agreed to stop urging [[Keys Caeradel]] to cast the [[Demi-Spell]] while the party pursued its own solution. In exchange, the party accepted the task of stopping [[Boril Erendel]], whose altered spell would reincarnate the world's spirits into high elf bodies. With [[Ceril]] rejoining them, the party followed Rizolvir's map to [[House Erendel]] and found several duplicates of Boril Erendel, along with another created figure resembling Vokenar.

### Summary

Key events:

- [[Obould]] retired from [[The Opal]], named [[Kerben]] captain, and became engaged to [[Lady Jacinthe]].
- [[Raxxy]] remained with Kerben; [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] and [[Ulrich Fjoller]] left to prepare for parenthood.
- [[Vorgan of the Stage]] joined The Opal as its performer.
- [[Jack Harvey's Portrait]] was entrusted to [[David Harvey]] aboard [[The White Drake]].
- [[Red Caesar]] returned the [[Tomb of Lenth the Rugged]] to [[Pleasance MacLenth]].
- The party learned that [[Farraday]] was the fiend they had hunted and destroyed his body on Stark.
- [[Kerben]] destroyed the [[Tome of Satariel]], leaving only three recoverable spells.
- At the [[Dawn Market]], [[Geoffrey the Younger]] crafted three reusable magical bullets for [[Kerben]].
- The party acquired the [[Ioun of Crimson Dreams]], [[Ioun of the Emerald Hunter]], [[Ioun of the Gilded Savior]], and [[Cestus of the Clear Sky]].
- [[Yalet Mora]]'s feud with [[Yalet Aurum]] ended without bloodshed, and the party received a massive golden brain.
- [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] agreed to delay pressure to cast the [[Demi-Spell]] while the party confronts [[Boril Erendel]].
- The party reached [[House Erendel]] and discovered multiple Boril Erendel duplicates.

The session closed several long-running personal arcs while opening the immediate confrontation with Boril Erendel. Obould chose a future with Lady Jacinthe and entrusted The Opal to Kerben, transforming an acting command into permanent leadership. Farraday's defeat removed his last foothold on Stark and fulfilled the condition that had kept [[Keys Caeradel]]'s father bound to his demonic hunt, though Farraday himself still exists in hell.

The peaceful resolution between Yalet Mora and Yalet Aurum revealed an unexpected act of care beneath their rivalry. The final bargain at House Kiirnodel does not reconcile the party with the Demi-Spell; it buys time. Rizolvir's restraint lasts only while the party acts against Boril Erendel, whose laboratory already shows that his replacement-body project has moved beyond theory.

### Connections

- [[Obould's Wedding Band]], recovered from [[Xarag]], enabled his renewed proposal to [[Lady Jacinthe]].
- The [[Antumbra]] remains an unconfirmed but potentially permanent answer to [[Starfall]].
- Farraday's false crypt connected [[Kerben]]'s pre-Cataclysm past to [[Jack Harvey]]'s plan for preserving the [[Penumbra]].
- Destroying the [[Tome of Satariel]] releases [[Rakshasa]] from Farraday's surveillance and advances [[Keys Caeradel]]'s family reunion.
- The moon fragment strengthening [[Yalet Aurum]] shows that remnants of [[Crone]] continue to affect creatures and terrain.
- [[Vokenar]]'s high elf reincarnation and the duplicate at [[House Erendel]] both demonstrate that [[Boril Erendel]]'s [[Demi-Spell]] alterations are already changing bodies.
