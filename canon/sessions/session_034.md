# Session 034

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_034/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Obould chooses a life beyond the Opal

The party returned to [[The White Drake]] to see [[Obould]] and [[Lady Jacinthe]]. Their reunion brought the cost of the recent rescue into a more personal conversation. Obould had sworn to take Lady Jacinthe's place if she were endangered, and had expected his imprisonment to end his life. He had never expected the others to bring him back. Their success forced him to consider what he wanted to do with the life they had preserved.

Obould challenged Lady Jacinthe over the [[Penumbra]] she had sent to the Empire to save him. He believed she had endangered the world for a bargain his oath did not require her to make. She answered that she loved him and would make the choice again. He admitted that he would have done the same for her. Their disagreement exposed the danger of the exchange without undoing their commitment to one another.

[[Red Caesar]] then revealed the concealed [[Antumbra]] among the League's supply. Lady Jacinthe's snake-bodied ally protected their discussion with Nondetection, allowing Red Caesar to explain the sabotage without openly broadcasting it. The revelation changed the meaning of the shipment: Lady Jacinthe had unknowingly helped carry a weapon intended to destroy [[Starfall]]. Red Caesar hoped its eventual use explained [[Emperor Shen]]'s weakened position, but the party still lacked confirmation.

Red Caesar and [[Kerben]] reconsidered the likely effects. Ordinary Penumbra had been drawn away to repair the heavens, whereas the altered stone might have remained available for Starfall's next attempt to fire. Their assessment was that consuming Antumbra would annihilate both the sample and Starfall itself. The weapon's unique magical nature would make that destruction permanent, rather than leave a machine that could be repaired or replaced. Divination might establish whether either still existed; no such confirmation was obtained in this conversation.

Obould announced his own decision. He permanently resigned as captain of [[The Opal]] and appointed Kerben to lead it into whatever future awaited. Adventure had become a way to escape responsibilities elsewhere, he explained. The ship had mattered deeply, but it had never been his whole life. Kerben was moved to tears, while Red Caesar struggled with the unexpected finality of the change.

Obould then knelt and offered [[Obould's Wedding Band]] to Lady Jacinthe. She accepted his proposal. They intended to lead [[The League of New Stark]] together, making their future a shared commitment rather than another separation imposed by his voyages. This was their engagement; the celebration did not establish that a wedding ceremony had already occurred.

#### Those who stayed and those who left

[[Raxxy]] chose to remain aboard The Opal under Kerben. Finding her birthplace in [[Southport]] had not made it the home she wanted, and she felt that she belonged with the ship. Kerben valued her as a lookout and trusted her place in the crew. Most of the others also stayed.

[[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] and [[Ulrich Fjoller]] departed to prepare for their child's birth. The child was still expected in the coming months. Southport was discussed as a possible home, but Lorelai Lapis-Acathian preferred The White Drake, where the League's future offered both greater safety and continuing purpose. Ulrich Fjoller reflected on the changes among the Titans and the possibility that his father's clan might eventually have to adapt. Those hopes did not establish a reconciliation or reform within [[Clan Akathia]]. Before leaving, he embraced [[Domyx]].

[[Vokenar]] gave [[Jack Harvey's Portrait]] to [[David Harvey]], who hung it aboard The White Drake. The gift placed the recovered likeness with Jack Harvey's family, despite the different judgments David Harvey and [[Theo Harvey]] had made of their father. David Harvey also reported that [[Damien Ouranous]] had died when the moon was struck. Vokenar consecrated The White Drake as a sanctuary to which his recall magic could return the party.

Red Caesar withdrew from the celebration, and Domyx sought him out near the pantry. Red Caesar described Obould as the head of the only family he had known since leaving [[Heaven's Bulb]]. He understood Obould's right to choose a life with Lady Jacinthe, but had assumed that the captain would always be aboard The Opal. The change left him feeling as though a foundation had shifted beneath him. Domyx listened, acknowledged the hurt, and encouraged him to share it rather than carry it silently. Red Caesar accepted the support. Obould later called him back to drink with the others, praising the wizard as a great ally, and the reunion continued through the day.

#### Trade and a new performer in Cutlass Cray

The following day, the travelers left The White Drake. At sea, Red Caesar began to see Obould's retirement as a freedom they could support: their former captain now had a home they could visit, rather than a ship to which he was bound. The voyage brought the four companions to [[Cutlass Cray]]. Ceril had separated from them during their travels; his work elsewhere was explained when they reunited later in the session.

The neutral port was busy again. Imperial vessels were withdrawing from the waters, and a Broyish ship could conduct business at the docks without beginning another fight. Kerben brought the poisonous fungi he had collected in the [[Mana Sea]] to [[Gilder Savar]] at [[Savar Brews]]. In exchange for a rare purple-worm skin rug, the brewer fashioned Mana Worm Poison. Besides its poisonous effects, the preparation could make a spellcaster's attempted magic misfire dangerously against its user.

The companions used diamonds from the dragon's hoard to seek new equipment. They agreed that Ceril would have first claim on the next comparable cache. Domyx held his share for a weapon purchase elsewhere, since the port's offerings did not suit that purpose.

Kerben also searched for the entertainer his enlarged crew needed. At [[The Bad Hare Day Theatre]], he found [[Vorgan of the Stage]], an old member of [[40 Karats]]. Vorgan of the Stage was performing in a reenactment of the day the moon fell and Cutlass Cray defended itself. His role as [[The Wonder Hulk]] required an elaborate artificial costume. The production was reaching the end of its run, and the absence of [[Muudeep]] as the troupe's playwright had left him searching for new material. Muudeep was still alive and selling goods at [[The Garden]].

Kerben offered a berth aboard The Opal. Vorgan of the Stage wanted to experience the world, gather stories, and eventually help keep 40 Karats alive through new performances. He joined as an entertainer, with no claim that he had become a seasoned fighter. His arrival answered the crew's need for someone to sustain morale during long voyages and confinement within the ship's magical vessel.

#### Lenth the Rugged's remains and the Ioun stones

Red Caesar carried the [[Tomb of Lenth the Rugged]] to [[Bookbinders Cray]] on a floating disc and offered it to [[Pleasance MacLenth]]. She confirmed that [[Lenth the Rugged]] was her grandfather and welcomed the recovered tomb as a piece of family history. Its familiar scent recalled [[Deception by Lenth]]. Consulting her family histories, she described the late-life romance that had drawn him back toward Southport.

Pleasance MacLenth intended to keep the tomb on display in her library. Their negotiation included flirtation and a proposed document whose romantic implications Red Caesar declined; he said that his heart remained at sea. The transaction nevertheless returned the remains to Lenth the Rugged's family. Through a favor owed by The Wonder Hulk, Red Caesar received the [[Ioun of Crimson Dreams]], a protective stone whose elemental resistance could be changed.

Kerben acquired the [[Ioun of the Emerald Hunter]], which sharpened his agility and helped him follow a marked quarry. Vokenar received the [[Ioun of the Gilded Savior]], a golden stone that could share healing with a chosen companion and strengthen a decisive strike. He bonded it to Red Caesar. Its rapid orbit traced a faint figure eight between them, visibly linking their protection.

A discussion of the brewer clarified that Gilder Savar was the same human who had traveled with [[Ceril]] and [[Vanzia Vynnfae]] decades earlier. After escaping [[Brimbolyn]], he had chosen lichdom rather than let old age end his work. His portrait depicted his former living appearance; there was no separate surviving human brewer behind the lich's name.

#### The pyramid's defenses

The Opal sailed through the rocky eastern passage. Its reinforced hull helped it cross terrain that ordinary ships avoided, and the retreating navies left the route largely clear. Toward evening, the party found [[Tome Keeper's Pyramid]], docked nearby, and used a longboat to reach a hole blasted into its base.

Red Caesar had supplied the companions with healing potions from Tasha's Bubbling Cauldron. Those preparations became useful almost immediately. Ocean surges filled the pyramid's lower chamber, but the water contained [[Akasha]], the substance that could crystallize into Penumbra. Its presence so close to the surface was unusual. The suggestion that repairing the sky had drawn it upward remained an explanation to consider, rather than a demonstrated cause.

The companions had already crossed much of the chamber before recognizing the danger. The liquid burned and weakened them, and bones swept in with the waves showed what it could do to earlier visitors. Vokenar restored their strength, with the new Ioun stone also carrying healing to Red Caesar. They climbed toward the higher rooms.

Kerben recognized an etched trap in the next chamber and stopped short, but the others activated a Circle of Death ward. Darkness drained the room of color and alerted the pyramid's owner. An amplified voice celebrated the decay of a drowned empire. Kerben found it faintly familiar, although too much time had passed for him to identify its speaker. The party recovered before ascending again.

In a smaller room, they found a stone chest fused to the floor and secured by metal bars and locks. Kerben used his magical key ring to open it from a distance. Inside were two bags of diamonds; one was allocated to Ceril as promised, and the other went into the ship's hold.

Red Caesar also preserved a sample of the lower chamber's Akasha with Dust of Dryness. Condensing that substance altered the result: the pellet became an Akasha grenade capable of releasing a violent burst of radiant energy. It was acquired here, without being used during the exploration.

Withdrawing from the chest did not carry everyone beyond the room's next defense. Only its corners were safe, and a domination trap reached the party along the walls. The others resisted its influence, but Domyx did not. Holding the recovered diamonds, he turned toward his companions with blackened eyes and an intention to attack.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_034/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### The keeper was Farraday

Vokenar recognized the change in Domyx before it became an attack. His calming magic suppressed the domination, bringing Domyx back to his senses. The four continued into the pyramid's highest chamber.

The room was painted as a hellish city beneath an unfamiliar red sky, false constellations, and ten silver moons. A tall, masked figure waited beside a floating book. Kerben recognized his former employer, [[Farraday]], whom he had served before [[The Cataclysm]]. The keeper's identity also explained his connection to [[Jack Harvey]], who had held a higher place in the same enterprise.

Farraday had never been buried in the supposed crypt the party had investigated. Its empty tomb and the trail toward [[The Karat Cake]] had served another purpose. The account suggested that Jack Harvey had arranged guidance toward his hidden Penumbra for whoever eventually followed it, rather than establish that a mortal Farraday had died there.

The floating [[Tome of Satariel]] was Farraday's ledger of money, souls, and exchanges with hell. Its recorded victims and captured magical knowledge made it much more than an ordinary book. Before the party could negotiate, Farraday transformed his painted surroundings into a convincing illusion of hell and attacked their minds.

#### Breaking the ledger and the foothold

Kerben immediately targeted the tome. His shots shattered its spine and scattered its pages before the magical bond with Farraday could restore it. Destroying the ledger severed a vital connection between the fiend's work in hell and his presence on [[Stark]], depriving him of the power the book might otherwise have supplied.

Farraday's illusions also turned toward the dust and ruin of the old world, mocking the party's desire to recover what had been lost. Kerben broke their hold and recognized the painted walls as a constructed scene, much like the theatrical sets of 40 Karats. Domyx remained trapped in the false surroundings longer, but continued fighting until his blows broke that influence as well.

Red Caesar and Farraday contested one another's magic throughout the confrontation. Farraday repeatedly prevented Red Caesar from establishing stronger protections or empowering Domyx. Red Caesar eventually overcame those defenses in return, stopping an attempt to imprison Domyx in a magical maze and preventing a lightning attack against the group. Their struggle over which spells could take effect shaped the battle as much as the blows exchanged nearby.

Vokenar sustained the companions through the fiend's assaults and came close to falling himself. The Ioun of the Gilded Savior helped keep Red Caesar protected while the cleric tended the rest of the group. Domyx's hammer battered Farraday down and opened the way for Kerben's blades. The former employee, once repeatedly underpaid and dismissed, dealt the final blow to his old master.

Farraday's body on Stark was destroyed, ending his last foothold in the world. His magic returned him to hell, where he would have to rebuild his position from a lower rank. The victory removed his immediate presence; it did not establish that his soul had been permanently annihilated.

Among the tome's ruined magical writings, three spells remained intact: Contact Other Plane, Arcane Gate, and Mind Blank. Red Caesar recovered them, and the party also found a gem. They rested for a day after the confrontation. Before leaving, Vokenar consecrated the pyramid as another recall sanctuary. They considered repairing it into a future base, but had not yet made the dangerous structure into a settled home.

#### Shopping in a changed Broy

The travelers sailed north for roughly half a day to the [[Dawn Market]] in [[Broy]]. Emperor Shen's withdrawal had left uncertainty in the streets. Some citizens had valued imperial protection and still supported him, while others welcomed the change. Broy had survived earlier governments, including a shogunate, but its weakened military and absent ruler created a power vacuum. Rising crime and a coming revolutionary government were prospects described for this unsettled period, rather than an already established successor regime.

Red Caesar considered whether he might settle there and help shape its future, drawing on what he had learned of the old world's failures. The thought remained an aspiration. He had neither claimed an office nor been appointed to one.

Kerben retained his rifle and used treasure from the hold to commission special ammunition from [[Geoffrey the Younger]]. The craftsman produced three magical bullets named Past, Present, and Future. A missed shot would preserve a bullet's magic for another attempt. Geoffrey the Younger thanked the party on behalf of Broyish people who had opposed the old direction of the Empire, recalling his father's stories of the beautiful sky before the catastrophe.

The possibility of another visit to [[Qian Hu]]'s relic shop arose, but Red Caesar recalled his unpleasant conversation with its proprietor and declined to pursue it. The party then went to the dwarvish market at [[The Deepworlders Delve]] to find Domyx's new weapon.

[[Sigrid Forgewelt]] had heard of the repaired sky, although she had not yet gone aboveground to see it. Domyx offered the hammer she had previously made for him, together with his diamonds. She fashioned the [[Cestus of the Clear Sky]], a powerful gauntlet designed around the hand with which he had touched the heavens. Its wrapping left his blue palm exposed, making his established connection to the sky central to the new weapon.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_034/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### A weapon shaped by the heavens

The cestus's full effects became apparent as Domyx attuned to it. Its outward form was simple leather wrapping, but it channeled force through his strikes and could turn his restorative sky power into a further blow. The blue of his palm spread across one side of his body and took on the appearance of the actual heavens, rather than merely resemble paint. The change made his connection to the restored sky visible wherever he traveled.

Before the party left, [[Beryzoz Helmscar]] asked Red Caesar how his former teeth had served him. Red Caesar reported that [[Beryzoz's Teeth]] had spoken from the mouths of the dead, delivering both truths and lies. He discussed buying another set when he acquired more money. After Red Caesar excused himself and closed the door, Beryzoz Helmscar smashed out his current teeth. No second set was transferred to Red Caesar in this scene.

#### The brothers' last confrontation

The party brought [[Yalet Mora]] into the woods near Brimbolyn to confront [[Yalet Aurum]]. The route Red Caesar had followed earlier was now difficult to recognize. Pieces of [[Crone]] had fallen across the continent, forming new obstacles and terrain. Locating magic helped him recover the way, and the travelers passed beneath two great moon fragments leaning against one another.

Yalet Aurum appeared to have been crushed by another fragment. Half his great head lay beneath the fallen stone, and he claimed that he was dying. Yalet Mora nevertheless demanded the fight he had wanted for so long, urging the party to kill his weakened brother.

Vokenar refused to take part in killing someone who appeared unable to defend himself. Domyx likewise rejected a victory over a helpless opponent. Red Caesar tried to reassure Yalet Mora that he had always possessed the potential to grow, but the younger brother remained focused on compensation for years of humiliation.

Yalet Aurum offered his golden brain. The immense, brain-shaped mass rolled from his mouth, and Domyx stopped it before it could roll away. The gold went into the ship's hold. Yalet Aurum told his brother to leave him to look at the restored sky during his final moments, expressing regret that he had not been a better brother.

Vokenar tried healing and stone-shaping magic, but neither changed the apparent wound. Yalet Mora eventually thanked Red Caesar for bringing him there. He was satisfied to believe that his brother would die even without being beaten in a fair fight, and returned to The Opal.

Once Yalet Mora was beyond hearing, Yalet Aurum told the four companions the truth. The moon fragment had strengthened him rather than injured him. He was healthy, more powerful than before, and had staged his decline to give his younger brother a sense of victory. He asked the party to take care of Yalet Mora, while continuing to boast that he surpassed his brother even in morality.

Yalet Aurum intended to reshape the surrounding land once the visitors had gone. He also said that he could feel the moon's influence slowly lifting and expected Crone eventually to become whole again. His account explained the failed attempts to heal him, but his predictions of the moon's recovery and his own invulnerability remained his claims. The party left with the gold and with knowledge Yalet Mora did not share: the feud had ended through a deception, without either brother being killed.

#### A bargain at House Kiirnodel

The companions used Red Caesar's teleportation circle to return to [[House Kiirnodel]]. [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]], now leading Brimbolyn, asked why people who had repaired the heavens were still traveling in search of further work. Red Caesar explained the remaining danger posed by Emperor Shen and the possibility that their achievement could still be undone.

Their discussion exposed the difference between their larger purposes. Rizolvir Kiirnodel wanted [[Boril Erendel]] stopped because his interference would make the [[Demi-Spell]] restore only elves in a new world. Such a world, he argued, would fail without the combined strengths of humans, dwarves, and other peoples. Removing that obstruction would advance the Order's project, even if the party intended to preserve the existing world instead.

Red Caesar rejected the idea that the Demi-Spell was an acceptable safeguard. He regarded its execution as among the worst possible outcomes, comparable to the Emperor's destructive plans. Nevertheless, he was also opposed to Boril Erendel. He offered to defeat the wizard if Rizolvir Kiirnodel would allow the party more time before any attempt to enact the spell.

Rizolvir Kiirnodel acknowledged that he would have preferred casting it as soon as [[Keys Caeradel]] finished it. Keys Caeradel, [[Feronia Caeradel]], and their allies had chosen otherwise, even though the spell might restore those killed when the moon fell. The death of Keys Caeradel's close friend weighed on that decision. Rizolvir Kiirnodel believed that further delay imposed a cost on those grieving losses the party's restoration could not reverse.

They nevertheless shook hands on a limited agreement. Red Caesar's group would act against Boril Erendel, and Rizolvir Kiirnodel would stop pressing Keys Caeradel to cast the Demi-Spell for a while longer. The bargain bought time; it neither reconciled their purposes nor bound the elves to abandon the spell permanently.

Rizolvir Kiirnodel warned that Boril Erendel possessed extraordinary magic despite the resources the elves had stripped from him. He attributed that power to the wizard's supposedly purer lineage and considered him stronger than [[Queen Caeradwyn]] had been. Those were Rizolvir Kiirnodel's judgments. Red Caesar answered that Boril Erendel lacked the companions standing behind him. Rizolvir Kiirnodel provided a map to [[House Erendel]].

#### Reunion at the altered house

Ceril rejoined the group before the expedition. During their earlier separation, he had come to Brimbolyn to help remedy the damage from the fallen moon, including moving its fragments. Domyx also chose Spurgruhn as his new surname, continuing the separation from the Akathian identity he had renounced.

The five followed the forest route toward House Erendel. Its old stone structure was surrounded by luminous magical growth, and a force field replaced missing portions of its decaying dome. As they approached, three figures resembling Boril Erendel emerged from concealed positions with magic hovering over their hands. Another figure resembled Vokenar's current form. The sight gave the party immediate evidence that the wizard had populated his refuge with copies, but their nature and his plans awaited the confrontation.

### Summary

- Obould permanently resigned command of The Opal, appointed Kerben captain, and became engaged to Lady Jacinthe.
- Raxxy and most of the crew stayed with Kerben. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian and Ulrich Fjoller departed to prepare for parenthood.
- Vokenar gave Jack Harvey's Portrait to David Harvey and established The White Drake as a recall sanctuary.
- Red Caesar revealed Antumbra's placement. The party assessed its potential to destroy Starfall permanently, without confirming its use.
- Kerben recruited Vorgan of the Stage, commissioned Mana Worm Poison, and acquired the Ioun of the Emerald Hunter. Red Caesar returned Lenth the Rugged's tomb to Pleasance MacLenth and received the Ioun of Crimson Dreams; Vokenar acquired the Ioun of the Gilded Savior.
- The pyramid yielded diamonds and an Akasha grenade. The party destroyed the Tome of Satariel and Farraday's body on Stark, recovering three intact spells and consecrating a possible future base.
- Kerben received magical ammunition at the Dawn Market, and Sigrid Forgewelt made Domyx the Cestus of the Clear Sky.
- Yalet Aurum surrendered his golden brain and staged his death to satisfy Yalet Mora. The party refused to kill an apparently helpless opponent and learned that both brothers remained alive.
- Red Caesar secured a temporary delay in Rizolvir Kiirnodel's pressure for the Demi-Spell. Ceril rejoined the group, Domyx chose a new surname, and the party reached House Erendel's waiting duplicates.

The session carried several long-running obligations toward endings while changing what the party's success meant for its members. Obould's rescue made retirement possible, and his decision gave Kerben an enduring command rather than temporary responsibility during another captain's absence. Red Caesar's grief showed the personal cost of that welcome outcome. Domyx's support and the possibility of visiting The White Drake allowed the bond to continue in a different form.

The renewed port traffic and Broy's uncertainty placed those personal changes within a wider transition. The sky had been restored, but institutions and loyalties had yet to settle around the new world. Red Caesar began considering a role in that future while continuing to pursue threats that might erase it. Antumbra remained central to his hope of ending Starfall, though the party's assessment here did not prove that the weapon was gone.

Farraday's defeat removed an old employer's hold on Kerben and an active fiendish presence on Stark. Yalet Mora's desired vengeance ended more ambiguously: the party preserved a defenseless-looking life, and his brother supplied a false ending to their rivalry. The final negotiation was similarly limited. A shared wish to stop Boril Erendel brought opponents of the Demi-Spell into cooperation with one of its advocates, but only bought time for the larger disagreement. The party closed the session reunited and facing the copies outside House Erendel.
