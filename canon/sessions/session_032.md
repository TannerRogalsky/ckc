# Session 032

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_032/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Emperor Shen's captive heir

[[Domyx]] awoke in the magical cage where [[Emperor Shen]] had confined him. He could not tell how much time had passed. Memories of earlier awakenings blurred into a cycle of pain, unconsciousness, and renewed demands. Cold water brought him back to awareness, and his grandfather, still wearing the half-broken mask damaged by [[Kerben]], demanded that they begin again.

Emperor Shen knew that Domyx had rejected his family name, but wanted him to accept the [[Broyish Empire]] as his inheritance. He contrasted his grandson with Domyx's father, whom he considered too weak to reach the heavens. He claimed to have gathered the sky's power at its full strength and regarded Domyx as the unexpected descendant capable of continuing that achievement. Domyx remained defiant, refusing to let suffering decide his allegiance.

[[Vizier Jade]] questioned the value of repeating the same torture and persuasion. Emperor Shen refused to have her enthrall Domyx: he wanted a willing grandson and successor rather than a puppet. He described the survivors of the Empire as a population Domyx could govern according to his own judgment, kindly or ruthlessly. His offer presented authority over others as the answer to Domyx's wish for control over himself.

The emperor claimed that he had killed [[Lyngbakr]], taken the Penumbra, and made repairing the sky impossible. He also reported that the lagoon settlement had been destroyed and imperial armies were advancing through League, elven, and Order territory. These assertions were part of his coercion, and his account of Lyngbakr contradicted the turtle's successful ascent in the preceding session.

The cage was moved on a floating disc through the [[Dawn Palace]] to a high marble area overlooking [[Broy]]. Domyx noticed that a waterfall from the heavens was gradually narrowing. He kept that sign to himself. Emperor Shen opened an ornate coffin containing [[Vokenar]]'s body, using the dead divine warrior as evidence that the gods had sacrificed their own servant to a futile task.

Emperor Shen also offered an account of their ancestry. He said that the titans had existed when the sister goddesses were born, that giants and gods had once fought as Aesir and Vanir, and that his people had surrendered to divine power. He presented his current war as a way to end the gods' interference and reclaim what might once have belonged to them. Domyx challenged the need to destroy the sky rather than accept that history as justification.

#### The mirror's account of abandonment

Vizier Jade urged the emperor to dispose of Vokenar's body and pursue the surviving companions before they regrouped. Emperor Shen instead wanted to display his victory to the population. He directed her to use her scrying mirror to see what the crew was doing.

The mirror showed an apparent meeting of [[Red Caesar]], Kerben, and [[Obould]] aboard [[The Opal]]. In that presentation, Red Caesar insisted that they rescue Domyx, while the others argued that Starfall's imminent attack made Axis Mundi the priority. The apparent Obould claimed that Vizier Jade intended to kill Domyx and that the rescue might already be too late. The image ended with a decision to leave him captive while pursuing the weapon. This scene was later explicitly identified as false; it was the account shown to Domyx, not the crew's actual conversation.

Emperor Shen used the image to insist that Domyx's companions would not come for him. He also claimed that Ceril was dead, treating the elf's departure into the heavens as another loss. Domyx still refused to accept the proposed succession. The emperor described himself and Vizier Jade as people who had lost former families, friends, and belonging, and who hoped for a future through him. Vizier Jade grew visibly disgusted with the exchange, then cast Sequester and returned Domyx to a dreamless, timeless sleep.

#### Alamar's reunion in Arkadia

The scene shifted to [[Ceril]] and [[Alamar]] in [[Arkadia]], walking from its drained ocean basin into hills beneath unfamiliar constellations. Gray, sandy wastes lay behind them where the sea had emptied through Stark's broken sky. Above the basin, yellow meadows, cyan clouds, and strange plants made the new world beautiful but unfamiliar.

Lyngbakr had remained behind to rest after returning the Penumbra to the heavens and sealing the ocean floor. He told them to seek [[Aeris]], whose body they had healed. Ceril considered exploring and charting the stars if the goddesses had no further work for him.

Alamar recognized three people near a farmhouse and ran ahead to greet them: [[Vlerro]], [[Igden]], and [[Tyson Cromwell]], all dead figures from old Kaedon. Vlerro had preserved him through Sequester so that he could survive into a future where the sky might be repaired. Igden had served as parliamentarian of Kaedon's senate, enforcing diplomacy among competing groups before dying when the waters rose. Tyson Cromwell had been a father figure who helped turn Alamar away from the path of [[King Maniasis]]. The precise circumstances of Tyson Cromwell's escape from an earlier imprisonment were not settled in this conversation.

The reunion placed Alamar's work in a history Ceril had never fully shared. Asked about his elven connection, Alamar explained the family bloodline's association with [[Queen Caeradwyn]] and the entanglement of inherited magic and corruption. He thanked the people who had helped him become something different from his father.

Alamar chose to remain with his old companions while Ceril continued toward the goddesses. Their conversation became dreamlike as he settled into Arkadia's altered sense of time. He thanked Ceril and encouraged him to go on, treating the old world's trials as something he could finally leave behind.

#### Encounters among the dead

Ceril's Augury favored continuing east toward a towering pillar in the distance. The answer felt familiar and divine. He understood that Arkadia was a separate plane from Stark: they had crossed a physical opening between them, but ordinary travel now required much greater magic. The powers that had threatened or interfered with him below did not extend into this place in the same way.

The route crossed the dead ocean bed, where decayed shadows of former life remained. Ceril became a bird and flew above it. He noticed two figures walking together and listened from the air. One was the winged [[Damien Ouranous]], the other the silvery [[Witness]].

Witness had finished charting Stark and was now exploring Arkadia. He described Vizier Jade as his creator, grateful that she had given him life. He remembered her desire for peace and the repeated wars that drew her back into work she did not want. Damien Ouranous said that Emperor Shen had killed him. He did not know all that had followed his death, but recognized the emperor as Vizier Jade's current master.

Farther on, Ceril encountered [[Illidrielle Gandara]] with two other dead members of [[The Order of Seasons]]. She recognized the bird as someone more than an ordinary animal and called him down. She remembered only a few moments of screaming before her death, which occurred on the day the moon was shattered. Ceril explained that event. Falling debris was a likely explanation, but she did not know exactly what had killed her.

Illidrielle Gandara said that [[Keys Caeradel]] had completed the [[Demi-Spell]] before her death. Red Caesar's rapid Penumbra-making method had helped, but Keys Caeradel had honored the party's request not to cast it. She spoke warmly of her longtime friend and believed he had mourned her deeply. His restraint mattered to her: simply bringing her back would not have justified disregarding everything still at stake.

She gave another reason for the delay. [[Boril Erendel]] was preparing elven bodies so that those entering the new cycle would all return as elves. If Ceril went back to Stark, she asked him to stop Boril Erendel, explicitly preferring his death. She also asked him to tell Keys Caeradel that she thought well of him and would see him again someday. Ceril left with both a message and a renewed warning about the Demi-Spell's corruption.

#### The goddesses' remaining charge

The distant pillar proved to be a vast golden tree with varied bark, leaves, and fruit, surrounded by a court and ivory towers. Ceril could not identify it as an ordinary species. [[Sigil]] emerged, and her presence overwhelmed him with light until he could neither see his surroundings nor move. She touched him and relieved the blindness, allowing him to see Aeris and the badly injured [[Crone]] nearby.

Sigil was devoting her strength to Crone's recovery. She explained that life and death formed a union: as long as one of the two younger sisters survived, she could help restore the other. Crone remained wounded rather than extinguished, and Ceril did not need to replace Sigil's healing work.

Aeris reminded Ceril that she had originally given him two responsibilities: restore her body and protect the boy she had brought into the world. The repaired sky fulfilled the first task, but Vokenar had died. Sigil could not return him from where they stood. Aeris believed Ceril could do so below if he recovered the preserved body and chose suitable magic before it deteriorated.

Although the sky looked whole from Stark, residual passages in Aeris's recovering body were still closing. They offered a brief route back. Ceril proposed Reincarnate and asked for time to prepare it. Aeris created a day for him to rest and change his magic without allowing corresponding time to pass elsewhere. Crone urged him to bring back her bravest warrior quickly.

The goddesses left his subsequent course to him, but charged him to prevent another assault upon them. Ceril then leapt through a remaining opening toward Stark.

#### The crew's actual decision

During his descent, Ceril saw smoke and warfare across the eastern waters near Broy, [[Brimbolyn]], [[The White Drake]], and [[Cutlass Cray]]. Alamar's lagoon was burning, and the invaders had driven its people away. This established the refuge's destruction and displacement without settling every inhabitant's fate. Ceril directed his fall toward The Opal.

Meanwhile, the narrative returned to Red Caesar, Kerben, and Obould for their actual conversation, explicitly distinguishing it from the earlier mirror scene. Obould now argued for rescuing Domyx, whom he believed Emperor Shen would keep alive as a prospective heir. Red Caesar saw Broy as a chance to address both the captive and Starfall: the Empire had reached Axis Mundi somehow, and might have an access point or people who knew the route. With its armies fighting elsewhere, entering the capital might be easier than waiting.

Red Caesar also told Obould about the [[Antumbra]] planted in the imperial Penumbra supply. He hoped that the next firing would produce an explosion or disable Starfall. That remained an untested expectation, not proof that the weapon had already been destroyed.

Obould examined the treasure recovered from [[Xarag]], retrieving his [[Orkland Pin of Courage]] and the wedding band intended for [[Lady Jacinthe]]. The ring brought back their earlier decision to postpone marriage while pursuing different lives. He asked to stop at The White Drake to address that unfinished relationship before resuming command. Kerben continued captaining The Opal.

They considered their other vessels, including [[The Hideous Truth]], as ways to approach without drawing attention to the familiar Opal. For the moment they sailed toward The White Drake, with the war ahead making the route dangerous.

Ceril descended to the deck, briefly becoming a bird to arrest his fall before landing in elven form. He told the crew that Vokenar was dead and that the goddesses required recovering his body. Kerben and Obould supplied what they had seen before escaping the beach. Red Caesar suspected that the body and Domyx would be held near one another in Broy. Ceril rejoined them, agreeing that the restored sky did not end their work. Alamar remained in Arkadia.

#### A missing ship and a new symbol

Near The White Drake, Kerben went ashore early to fish while the others remained aboard. When he looked for The Opal afterward, it had disappeared. He searched unsuccessfully, then examined the egg through which he called his animal companions. A new symbol, labeled Bravo and shaped like a ship, had appeared among the familiar creatures.

Activating it released The Opal, initially a small streak of magic that expanded into the whole vessel upon touching the sea. Kerben returned to his fishing. The scene then shifted to the crew's experience inside the egg: Ceril, Red Caesar, and Obould had awakened amid kaleidoscopic walls around their ship. They feared imperial magic had trapped them. Obould fired the cannons at the enclosure without effect, and their perspective ended as the mysterious containment began to change.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_032/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Carrying the Opal

The enclosure faded through bright colored light, returning the crew to the ocean. Kerben came back with a substantial catch and explained the ship symbol he had found in his egg. The vessel and everyone aboard had apparently entered the same magical space as his companions.

He tried activating the power again, but it only wheezed. Red Caesar detected a limit and suggested that it might renew at dawn. The source of the new ability remained uncertain; the park expedition was a possible explanation rather than a confirmed cause.

The discovery offered a way to approach Broy aboard The Hideous Truth while keeping The Opal and its crew concealed and ready for escape. Obould endorsed that plan, then left for The White Drake with the recovered wedding band. He told them that they could contact him there or return through its teleportation circle. His departure to Lady Jacinthe was established; the result of their reunion was not yet shown.

#### Vizier Jade spares Domyx

Domyx awoke to another repetition of Emperor Shen's demands. The emperor again claimed that the sky was irreparable and that Domyx's companions were gone. When he left Vizier Jade to continue the interrogation, she described the exhausted pattern herself: torture, resistance, sleep, and another angry failure to change his mind.

Domyx asked what she wanted. Vizier Jade said that she had made a foolish pact and no longer possessed the freedom she once had. She had formerly led a guild and spent time creating things for a better world. War, divine intervention, and struggles for power repeatedly destroyed that work. Her martial usefulness came from necessity rather than a love of fighting, and she remembered only a short stretch of peace amid centuries of conflict.

Domyx considered pretending to accept the succession in order to escape. Vizier Jade asked what he would do if he actually ruled years later. His answer was uncertain: he wanted people to realize their own potential, as he wanted to become the best version of himself. It did not become a worked-out program for governing the Empire. The exchange clarified that self-direction mattered more to him than authority over others.

Emperor Shen returned after seeing that the companions were coming. He abandoned persuasion and ordered Vizier Jade to kill Domyx, declaring him no grandson. Domyx told him that achievements should serve growth rather than public recognition, and that the emperor had stopped trying to become better.

Vizier Jade cast Feign Death under an illusion resembling her killing magic. Domyx felt his body go limp and his heart stop, but understood that the apparent death was a deception. Emperor Shen accepted it, grieving and raging over the failed Akathian succession. He blamed Vizier Jade and ordered her not to speak until he chose otherwise. Her inability to answer demonstrated how directly his commands constrained her.

#### Recruiting a diversion in Broy

Red Caesar, Ceril, and Kerben transferred to The Hideous Truth, carrying The Opal and its crew within the egg. Before entering Broy, Red Caesar prepared fresh Penumbra and fire-breathing potions for the three rescuers. Those preparations did not themselves create the incendiary animals or widespread sabotage he had considered.

Red Caesar's citizenship helped them enter the [[Broyish Capital]]. Oni watched the steel city, but nobody immediately stopped them. They sought [[Naomi Ue]], who reported unexpectedly low security and Vizier Jade withdrawing some palace guards from the streets. She also described Vokenar's corpse being paraded as evidence that the Empire could defeat divine servants, a spectacle the population had celebrated.

Naomi Ue could not provide a direct route to Starfall. Its beam emerged through the mountains from underground, but the openings healed or collapsed soon afterward. Following the visible attack path had therefore failed to reveal an entrance. Penumbra shipments had ended, apparently because the Empire had accumulated sufficient fuel. Red Caesar thought transport workers might still know the route, but no such person was located here.

Naomi Ue offered a quiet thieves' approach to the palace. Red Caesar instead asked for a diversion nearby, making clear that civilians should not be killed. She was reluctant to expose her contacts or the population to danger, and required money rather than Penumbra. They discussed manipulating an Oni's protections and allegiance, but did not carry out that option.

When Red Caesar pointed to the restored sky, Naomi Ue showed him what the city's people could actually see: the same old broken heavens. A Mirage Arcana concealed the repair above Broy. Revealing the true sky would provide both a signal and a grievance around which the diversion could gather.

Red Caesar hired her people to be ready near the palace. On the sky's change, they were to make a brief disturbance against imperial authority and withdraw into the shadows. After haggling, Naomi Ue accepted gold, [[Jade's Compass]], and The Carrot Cake plans the party had recovered. She promised a return worth the payment while insisting that their meeting had never happened.

#### Removing the false sky

The three rescuers rose in their balloon amid Broy's civilian air traffic. The illusion had been placed so high that the balloon reached its natural limit before Detect Magic could locate it. Red Caesar used Gust of Wind to push the craft higher, while Ceril took over detecting the magic.

They reached the illusion and could finally see the healed sky above it. Red Caesar dispelled the Mirage Arcana as the balloon drifted over the Dawn Palace. Below, Naomi Ue's arrangements took effect. The commotion combined staged protest with a genuine revolt, giving visible form to grievances rather than simply making the citizenry look indiscriminately violent.

The party abandoned the balloon for the descent. Ceril flew in his dragon starry form and carried Red Caesar down; Kerben held Tango, who brought him safely toward the palace.

#### The emperor retreats to Starfall

Before the rescuers reached Domyx, Vizier Jade opened his cage and started to leave. Emperor Shen returned, distressed that the population was turning against him and rushing the palace. He ordered a retreat to Starfall to prepare the final attack, declaring that they should not return to the surface until it was done.

He permitted Vizier Jade to speak again. She reminded him that the survivors should have been dealt with earlier, then sent Domyx a short private message. A part of her wished his hopes could succeed; she urged him to use the time she had given him, while saying she could not stand against the emperor.

Vizier Jade teleported herself and Emperor Shen away as the rescuers entered. She saw the arriving companions before departing, but did not stop them. Domyx remained on the floor under Feign Death, with the cage open. Red Caesar immediately recognized that he was alive and told him to get up.

Domyx explained how Vizier Jade had spared him and reported that the pair had gone to Starfall. He had no direction or coordinates to offer. Red Caesar examined their departure for a trail, but their Teleport left no circle or accessible route to follow. Reaching the palace had not solved the journey to Axis Mundi.

Ceril found Domyx badly depleted by physical and psychological torture. Greater Restoration reversed the lasting damage, and chalice-enhanced healing improved his remaining injuries. Domyx then guided the others along the routes Emperor Shen had shown him.

Outside, the adoring crowd of his earlier captivity had become a riot as news spread that the restored heavens had been hidden from them. High above the city, the waterfall into the volcanic caldera had dwindled almost to a rivulet. At the palace's pinnacle, the four companions reached the ornate coffin and opened it to find Vokenar's body.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_032/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Escaping with Vokenar

The body had been preserved by Gentle Repose. The decorated setting showed signs of public visits, confirming that it had served as a display of imperial power rather than merely a private prisoner's remains.

Ceril needed time for Reincarnate and wanted to leave enemy territory first. Domyx carried Vokenar over his shoulder. Ceril used Transport via Plants on a willow, mixing his materials in water upon his shield and pouring them over the tree. The water blurred its surface into a passage. He entered first, followed by Kerben, Red Caesar, and Domyx with the body.

The Opal's presence in Kerben's egg meant that the whole crew could travel with them without returning to a distant anchorage. They chose the [[Temple of Sigil]] on [[Otyugh Isle]] as a peaceful place for the ritual, emerging through rhododendrons. The temple had been tidied since their earlier visit, suggesting care by the people they had freed from petrification. The west was quiet compared with the war they had left.

#### A return constrained to elven form

Ceril laid Vokenar on Sigil's altar and offered treasure for the costly ritual. He invoked the goddess and began the next part of his charge. The offerings and old body dissolved into golden smoke; a luminous humanlike shape formed as flesh and bones gathered in brilliant light.

When the light faded, Vokenar had returned in a high elf body. His skin was paler, his ears pointed, and the wings of his former appearance were absent. Ceril recognized something wrong with the process. Reincarnate should have drawn among possible forms, but chance had not selected this one. Only elven bodies were available within the cycle. Illidrielle Gandara's warning about Boril Erendel was already affecting rebirth, rather than remaining merely a threat for the eventual casting of the Demi-Spell.

Vokenar remembered Vizier Jade killing him and then this awakening, with no experienced interval between them. He was disoriented and asked where they were and what had happened. The others told him that the sky had healed, but Emperor Shen and Starfall still threatened it.

Looking upward, he admired the heavens they had worked to restore. He accepted the new form, saying that whatever body he had, he would use it to help his companions and others. He thanked them for returning for him when they could have left him dead. The party was whole again, though its understanding of the cycle's corruption had deepened.

#### A descent beneath the world

The companions debated visiting the demon at Hell's Passage, pursuing Boril Erendel, or addressing [[Mana Sickness]] in Southport. Red Caesar still wanted a route to Axis Mundi and proposed [[The Pit]] first. They used its teleportation circle to reach the dwarven city.

The Deepworlders had integrated into its society as a group specializing in divination, alongside its mining, construction, and trade interests. A great fragment of Crone had fallen near the palace. The dwarves had built around it and tunneled into its base, incorporating the moon rock into new housing. Its transformation showed the city's response to the disaster without establishing that nobody had been harmed by the fall.

Red Caesar learned that [[The Funnel]]'s deeper excavation was overseen by [[Courteous Cam]] and [[Otto]], rather than directly by [[Figma Brickfinger]]. The party descended wooden lifts and pulley systems far below the sea to a mixed workforce of dwarves, gnomes, halflings, humans, and a half elf.

Courteous Cam said Otto had reached the bottom of Stark, where solid ground opened into air and a vast black abyss. The route had collapsed around the breakthrough and would have to be reopened. Neither knew the name Axis Mundi. The apparent emptiness was what they had seen, not a completed survey of everything beneath the world. Courteous Cam also described Otto as a warforged who had developed a soul, answering Red Caesar's assumption that he was simply a machine.

Red Caesar explained that repairing the sky had not removed the emperor or the weapon that broke it. Courteous Cam agreed to divert the excavation toward reopening the route, estimating several weeks of work. The crew already used earth-moving magic, explosives, and advanced methods. Red Caesar offered assistance and proposed teaching them Penumbra-based power, but no workable new system was established. Courteous Cam doubted that learning and implementing it would shorten the job.

Mana Sickness was also slowing the workers. Healers from the south had helped, yet the illness kept returning. Red Caesar recalled the [[Deep Roses]] used against it and offered to address that problem while the digging continued. Courteous Cam would send word as the route neared readiness. Even reaching the abyss might leave them far from their actual destination; reopening the descent was the first step, not proof of a direct passage to Starfall.

#### Southport's welcome

The party weighed the remaining choices. Ceril wanted Boril Erendel stopped, Red Caesar remained interested in the demon, and several companions favored treating the sickness before more people died. Keys Caeradel still held a completed Demi-Spell, but neither speculation about his death nor confidence in his honor settled what he might later choose. They voted to go to Southport.

After several days sailing through fog, they approached alongside refugee boats from Alamar's lagoon and other threatened places. Southport offered an alternative to joining either the League or the Empire, gathering humans and other peoples into a relatively independent refuge.

[[Mayor Yoris]], a very young man in military regalia, organized the arrivals along the beach. He greeted the party and cast protective magic against poison and disease before they went ashore. Looking between the restored sky and the newcomers, he said that [[Janeera]] had foretold the arrival of the people who would heal the heavens. The session ended with that welcome; investigating Southport's illness remained ahead.

### Summary

- Emperor Shen tried to force Domyx into imperial succession through captivity, torture, false claims, and a fabricated mirror scene showing supposed abandonment by his companions.
- In Arkadia, Alamar reunited with Vlerro, Igden, and Tyson Cromwell. Ceril overheard Damien Ouranous and Witness, then learned from Illidrielle Gandara that the Demi-Spell was complete but uncast and Boril Erendel was constraining future bodies.
- Aeris, Sigil, and the injured Crone sent Ceril back to recover Vokenar. Aeris gave him time to prepare without delaying events below.
- The crew's real discussion favored going to Broy. Ceril returned to The Opal, and Kerben discovered that his egg could carry the vessel and crew.
- Obould left for The White Drake with the recovered wedding band to seek Lady Jacinthe.
- Vizier Jade secretly used Feign Death to spare Domyx after Emperor Shen ordered his execution.
- Red Caesar paid Naomi Ue with gold, Jade's Compass, and The Carrot Cake plans. The rescuers dispelled Broy's false sky, triggering a diversion and uprising.
- Emperor Shen and Vizier Jade teleported to Starfall. Vizier Jade left Domyx's cage open; the party restored him and located Vokenar's preserved body.
- Ceril escaped through plants with the companions and body, then reincarnated Vokenar at the Temple of Sigil. The forced high elf form exposed corruption already active in the incarnation cycle.
- Courteous Cam and Otto agreed to reopen the Funnel's collapsed descent into the abyss beneath Stark, requiring several weeks.
- The party sailed to Southport to address Mana Sickness during the excavation. Mayor Yoris protected and welcomed them on Janeera's prediction.

The companions recovered their two lost members through different changes in allegiance and capability. Domyx resisted the emperor's claim that blood and strength entitled him to obedience. Vizier Jade's private reprieve gave that resistance a chance to survive, even while her pact kept her within Emperor Shen's command. Ceril's return from Arkadia renewed the responsibility to Vokenar and supplied the magic that made his recovery possible. Kerben's egg then allowed the rescuers to carry their ship and people through a departure that would otherwise have stranded them far from their resources.

Exposing the false sky gave the repair a political consequence within Broy. The emperor's public claim to mastery depended on concealing what others had accomplished and displaying a dead divine servant. Removing the illusion and recovering Vokenar challenged both symbols. The resulting uprising forced a retreat from the palace, but did not end imperial power or reveal a usable route to Starfall. Vizier Jade's intervention complicated her role without undoing Vokenar's killing or the other harm she had supported.

The restored party now faced dangers beyond the broken heavens. Boril Erendel's influence was already narrowing reincarnation, Keys Caeradel possessed a finished Demi-Spell, and Emperor Shen had withdrawn to prepare another attack. The Funnel offered a possible entrance beneath Stark, with an uncertain journey beyond and a delay the party could not eliminate. Choosing Southport turned that waiting period toward a spreading illness that affected both refugees and the workers needed for the descent. The closing welcome established a new place to seek answers and help, rather than an accomplished cure.
