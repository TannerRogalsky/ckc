# Session 019

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_019/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Crossing to the aasimar memorial

The party continued the same day's exploration of [[Castle Kaedon]], with the northern arcane-locked door still unopened. Broken platforms to the south and west also remained unsearched. The sea was becoming rougher, and [[Ceril]] expected a storm that night followed by worse weather the next day.

The travelers considered ways to cross the gaps. [[Domyx]] could not use the [[Iklwa Isondo]] as a means of pulling himself toward distant structures, and its magnetic return did not solve the route. [[Kerben]] instead proposed using the [[Mangonel]] to launch him to the southern platform. Domyx agreed, relying on his own climbing and cloud magic for the return.

Kerben calibrated the machine for Domyx's weight using its mechanism and his navigational knowledge. The launch carried Domyx across successfully, though the landing bruised him. The mangonel remained intact. On the far platform, he opened a crank-raised door and explored the ruins of a structure partly reclaimed by flowering plants.

White marble statues with great wings stood inside. A sign in Western identified them as a memorial to aasimar heroes, apparently honoring some former pact or heroic effort. Domyx found amethysts set into one statue's eyes and recovered the gems into the shared hold.

As he turned back toward his companions, the statues pulled free of their bases and took flight. They retained their angelic shapes, with flowers still hanging from their feet, but had become hostile marble gargoyles. The rest of the party saw them rise behind Domyx and warned him. Ceril also heard movement underground near the companions on the original platform, suggesting a second threat.

#### Permanent bridges and a hidden attacker

Rather than fight alone on the isolated platform, Domyx dived toward the sea and used his cloud jaunt just before impact to break the fall. He swam to the nearer cliff and began climbing, while some gargoyles descended after him.

Kerben's stone-sensing awareness revealed that these particular statues were made of single, brittle pieces of marble. Thunder could break them more effectively than ordinary attacks. Red Caesar used that knowledge to blast the approaching creatures, while Kerben fired across the gap. Vokenar watched for the movement Ceril had heard behind them.

A [[Zorn]] emerged through the flagstones. Its body was made of stone, soil, and pebbles, with three arms, three legs, three eyes, and one great central mouth. The ground briefly softened around it, then closed again as it moved. It attacked Vokenar and vanished beneath the floor, where Kerben could continue tracking it through the stone.

Ceril recognized its ability to glide through earth without leaving an open tunnel. The creature sought precious metals and gems, and could potentially be lured or satisfied with them, but its hidden movement made it difficult to confront. Ceril used Plant Growth on the fungal network beneath the ground, thickening it enough to impede the Zorn.

The same magic transformed the exposed terrain. Seaweed growing up the pillars spread into net-like connections between nearby platforms, while grass, vines, and fungi provided new climbing supports. Domyx found a shelf fungus large enough to stand on and used the growth to reach the party again. The bridges were permanent, although they did not extend to every distant island.

The entangled Zorn had to surface to continue attacking. It spoke in a language Domyx understood, regretting its departure from [[Axis Mundi]] and complaining about the difficulty of life above ground. The party continued fighting both it and the gargoyles. Red Caesar's thunder, Kerben's weapons, and Ceril's light shattered the remaining statues until only the subterranean creature remained.

#### The Zorn's bargain

Kerben tracked the Zorn's slowed movement while the others prepared to strike when it surfaced. Vokenar's weapon brought [[Akasha]] into contact with it, briefly dematerializing part of its elemental body. The creature begged Domyx to let it return home and offered to surrender gold gathered from the castle.

Vokenar questioned whether releasing it would merely allow another attack later. Domyx argued that killing it might yield its treasure anyway, and used that threat to demand what it had swallowed. The Zorn coughed up its coins, which emerged unusually clean and polished after passing through its rocky body. The party recovered roughly fifty pounds of gold into the hold.

The creature pointed at Vokenar's weapon and accused them of using a force that destroyed elemental matter, including the oceans. It considered that more evil than its own conduct. The accusation remained the Zorn's judgment, but its fear of Akasha was unmistakable. Domyx accepted its departure after receiving the treasure. Kerben sensed it burrowing down the cliff into deep stone, and the party did not pursue it.

#### The senate's meeting room

Ceril's bridges let the party reach a western platform more easily. Roses had spread through the broken structure, where large tables suggested a meeting space for displaying goods or discussing public matters. Engraved chairs carried the names Rodgard, Bloomshield, [[Clockwork]], and [[Esperanto]].

Those labels suggested representation from families, notable individuals, and other communities. They offered physical evidence of the broad senate that had followed the monarchy, rather than another royal household. Red Caesar considered the seats potentially ambassadorial, while the party reflected on how many voices the old republic appeared to have admitted. The chairs yielded knowledge rather than a useful treasure to carry away.

Through the northern doors, they entered what looked like a former guard post. Kiosks, ruined turnstiles, and places for weapons marked its old function. Ceril noticed that damaged equipment had not merely broken: acid had dissolved its edges, rounded the room's corners, and left portions of the ceiling drooping.

He stopped the others before they advanced too far. Black puddings gathered out of the acid-damaged space, and [[Severed Hands]] emerged deeper in the room. The party began another fight, with Red Caesar attacking the hands, Domyx holding them back, and Kerben initially choosing gunfire to avoid dividing the puddings. The encounter remained underway at the chunk boundary.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_019/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Clearing the ruined checkpoint

Kerben decided to press the attack with his blades despite the black puddings' tendency to split. He divided one into smaller threats, but the acid also corroded his ordinary scimitar. The creatures crowded toward him while the crawling hands moved around the party.

Ceril answered with Maelstrom, drawing on whirlpools he had observed during his time at sea. Water filled the center of the room and pulled creatures toward its vortex. The spell gathered the severed hands into a roiling mass and helped the companions destroy the divided puddings. Domyx could endure Ceril's magic, while Red Caesar moved out of the dangerous water.

Vokenar's holy light, the party's ranged attacks, and Domyx's returning trident finished the surviving creatures. Ceril entered the whirlpool himself before ending it, drawing the water back toward his shield. Nothing useful remained to recover from a room whose contents had been consumed by acid.

The companions took a short rest and checked the remaining exposed terrain. Most of what had once stood there had already fallen into the sea, and they felt they had found what could be found. Kerben summoned an additional land beast to accompany Tango before the party opened the route into the castle proper.

#### The entrance foyer

Kerben used his magical keyring on the northern arcane-locked door. Seven heraldic crests surrounded the entrance. One matched the emblem on [[Alamar]]'s clothing; others suggested a shield, crossed blades, and coins. The party associated them with the groups that had governed the former republic.

Inside lay a secure foyer with a red carpet leading through a portcullis and down stairs. The descent confirmed Kerben's earlier discovery that much of the castle extended under the ground they had explored. Roses had also grown into the interior.

The party failed to recognize every danger before advancing. Scorpions, a carrion crawler, and severed hands appeared among the overgrowth. What had looked like rumpled carpet beneath the portcullis was a [[Flabbergast]], an undead body pinned under the gate. It tore itself free and rose to attack.

Kerben sent Tango and the summoned beast ahead, drawing the creature's attention while he fired from cover. Its necrotic influence frightened the animals, and its stench made close approach dangerous. Tango was struck down while trying to fly away, while the summoned beast escaped its reach. Red Caesar identified additional risks, including paralytic claws, and the party favored holy light against it. Domyx confronted the scorpion while the others worked around the foyer's pillars.

The Flabbergast eventually directed its fear at Kerben himself and advanced toward Red Caesar. Red Caesar blocked its claw with protective magic, while Domyx broke free of the scorpion and helped drive the undead creature into reach. Kerben steadied himself despite the fear and shot it down. Its frightening influence and stench immediately subsided.

Ceril cleared the remaining hands, and Kerben's summoned beast continued helping after recovering from its fear. The last carrion crawler briefly paralyzed Red Caesar, but he recovered and Vokenar killed it with his sword. The foyer was clear.

This time the party stopped for the night. They moved the bodies aside and set alarms, while Red Caesar created Leomund's Tiny Hut. The enclosed foyer offered a defensible place to sleep, with Kerben's keyring available to secure the entrance further. Their overnight rest also ended the lingering vitality loss from the specters. They remained inside Castle Kaedon rather than traveling away to another refuge.

#### Flashback: Lady Jacinthe and Obould's oath

The transcript then returned to the earlier visit to [[The White Drake]], before the castle expedition. During that visit, Red Caesar, Vokenar, and Domyx had stayed overnight at [[Lady Jacinthe]]'s invitation. Vokenar enjoyed foods beyond the limited variety aboard their own ship, and Domyx joined a lively group playing Titan's Tarot. Red Caesar sought the private conversation he had wanted with their host.

Lady Jacinthe left the banquet and retired toward her quarters. Red Caesar followed and knocked; she invited him in. She had been drinking and appeared less guarded, although she retained her composure. Red Caesar explained that curiosity sometimes overcame his tact and asked about the history she shared with [[Obould]].

She told him that she and Obould had once intended to marry. Their connection had rested on a shared appetite for adventure and ambition rather than a quiet domestic life. Her adventure became the creation of [[The League of New Stark]], bringing kingdoms together as the world divided and the [[Broyish Empire]] expanded. Obould remained better suited to piracy and war.

His violence had become distasteful to people she wanted to bring into the League. Lady Jacinthe had therefore ended the engagement. She believed he had softened since taking the party into his crew. Red Caesar questioned whether the change was more a practical adjustment: he thought Obould could still act as a cutthroat if it served his interests. She entertained the possibility that their similarities ran deeper than their different circumstances allowed them to express.

When she rejected the engagement, she also refused his ring and told him to keep his treasure. Obould left her an oath instead. If harm came to her, he would go in her place; if she would die, he would die first. He had said that he could not bear a Stark without her.

Lady Jacinthe described the promise as binding him to death and leaving her with sorrow. In her account, he had cursed her with that sorrow, while she had cursed him with the oath to die. She warned Red Caesar that its consequences would eventually become visible: Obould would leave whatever useful work he was doing aboard [[The Opal]] to save her and the League.

Red Caesar reconsidered his judgment of the captain and acknowledged the romance in that promise. He then asked how Lady Jacinthe's personal purpose related to the people joining her organization. Did they all have to share her goal? She wanted to rebuild what had been torn down and hand the resulting world over to free peoples. Their conversation continued into the next chunk.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_019/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Flashback continued: voluntary service and Starfall

Lady Jacinthe contrasted her purpose with the other powers seeking control of Stark. In her view, the elves threatened freedom by undoing the world, while the eastern Empire threatened it by forcing humans into a single unthinking collective. Those were her assessments of their projects, not new proof of every possible outcome.

Red Caesar asked what the League would require if the Empire escalated: would its members and Obould's crew be conscripted? Lady Jacinthe said they would not. Their agreements were voluntary, offering safety, cooperation, and mutual protection. She hoped a stronger League could establish secure laws and contracts resembling the old republics without forcing everyone into one purpose.

Red Caesar doubted that volunteers could withstand the emperor's concentrated determination and warned that she might be placing dangerous faith in them. Lady Jacinthe defended the League's dispersed organization. A single tall tower could collapse, but the League's forces and communities were spread widely.

Before she retired, Red Caesar requested access to any imperial mage or engineer the League might capture who understood the great cannon. Lady Jacinthe said that one such mage had already been taken. He resisted and was killed, but had disclosed the weapon's name: [[Starfall]].

Her account described it as ancient, long held by the Empire, and said to have fallen from the heavens before Stark. Red Caesar received that information as encouraging and thanked her. The conversation supplied a name and a reported history for the suspected weapon, without establishing its location or providing a surviving engineer to question.

#### The next morning in the castle

The narrative returned to the castle foyer on the morning after the party's rest. A storm raged outside, with rainwater flowing beneath the door, confirming Ceril's forecast. Kerben chose [[Zulu]] as his companion for the next stage instead of Tango.

The party raised the portcullis and descended toward the larger entrance below. The seven heraldic symbols formed a star-like arrangement around it. As they approached, Ceril perceived a magical threat that the others had not noticed. A faint glow became, in his mind's eye, an image of deep water overwhelming the party.

He recognized the Mark of the Home as a curse that would make its victims dangerously unlucky. Before it swept over them, he used dispelling magic to protect Red Caesar, whom he judged especially vulnerable. The others faced its influence, and Kerben succumbed. The curse weakened his ability to resist subsequent dangers even though he had not fully understood what happened.

Ceril considered whether to preserve his strongest restorative magic or use it immediately. Because Kerben repeatedly faced dangerous situations and the party still had a dungeon ahead, he judged leaving the curse in place more costly than removing it. He prepared Greater Restoration as a draught in his shield and offered it to Kerben, describing its taste as strawberry and rhubarb. Kerben drank, and the curse was lifted.

The companions then entered a pentagonal chamber with four onward doors. Benches lined the walls, red, white, and yellow roses had spread through it, and brass frames remained where paintings had been torn or faded away.

Circles appeared on the floor as Domyx advanced into the room. Pentacles formed within them, recalling the summoning effects encountered in the castle's upper rooms. [[Bearded Devil]]s arrived: humanoid fiends with rubbery purple skin, black topknots, glaives, and barbed tentacles sprouting from their faces.

The session ended with the devils present and the confrontation about to begin. The party had reached its second day inside the castle, cleared the entrance, and overcome the curse, but had not yet fought this new group or recovered the Penumbra it sought.

### Summary

- Kerben launched Domyx to an isolated platform with the mangonel. Domyx recovered amethysts from an aasimar memorial before its statues animated as gargoyles.
- Ceril's Plant Growth impeded an earth-gliding Zorn and created permanent bridges and climbing supports between nearby platforms.
- The party shattered the gargoyles and accepted the Zorn's surrender of roughly fifty pounds of gold. It fled after Vokenar's Akasha-bearing weapon and Domyx's threat forced its bargain.
- An old meeting room's engraved chairs suggested representation by the Rodgard and Bloomshield families, Clockwork, and Esperanto in Kaedon's republic.
- The party cleared black puddings and severed hands from a ruined checkpoint, then rested briefly and opened the arcane-locked castle entrance.
- They defeated a Flabbergast and other creatures in the foyer and stayed there overnight under alarms and Red Caesar's hut.
- A flashback to the earlier White Drake visit revealed Lady Jacinthe's broken engagement to Obould, his oath to die in her place, and her commitment to voluntary League membership.
- In that private conversation, Lady Jacinthe supplied the name Starfall and an imperial prisoner's account of its ancient origin.
- The next morning, Ceril protected Red Caesar from the Mark of the Home and removed the curse from Kerben.
- The party entered a pentagonal chamber where bearded devils appeared. Their battle and the Penumbra search remained unfinished.

The expedition changed both the party's access to the ruins and its picture of the civilization that had occupied them. Ceril's growth created lasting routes across the nearby broken terrain, while the senate's named seats gave the republican history a physical setting. The Zorn encounter also revealed a subterranean home and a fear of Akasha's destructive effect. Its surrender ended the immediate attack without killing it, but did not establish how it would behave after leaving.

Lady Jacinthe's flashback supplied personal stakes for the alliance discussed during the earlier visit. Her political project and Obould's maritime ambitions had separated them, yet his promise still bound their futures. Red Caesar learned that the captain could abandon the crew's immediate needs to protect her. The discussion of voluntary contracts placed that private obligation beside the League's broader refusal to conscript its people.

At the close, the travelers were still in Castle Kaedon. Their night's refuge was the cleared foyer, and the storm made continued exploration indoors sensible. Ceril's intervention removed a dangerous burden before the new day's fighting, but the summoned devils showed that the deeper rooms remained hostile. Starfall now had a name in the record, while its full history, location, and relationship to the catastrophe remained unsettled.
