# Session 026

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_026/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Vokenar between worlds

The session first returned to [[Vokenar]]'s departure from [[The Opal]], roughly two weeks earlier. At the full moon, [[Crone]] descended onto the deck in her largest and most imposing form, carrying a bastard sword and covered in demon blood. She demanded that Vokenar resume his training. His months on [[Stark]] had made him stronger, but he doubted that strength was yet enough for the dangers ahead.

Crone criticized the gentler preparation he had received from [[Sigil]]. She explained that the membranes separating the worlds had grown thin and that she was holding demons back at their boundaries. [[Aeris]] had once helped her, but was still weakened and recovering. Vokenar offered his assistance, took Crone's hand, and rose with her through the moon's overwhelming light.

She brought him into the space between worlds where he had spent decades during his descent from [[Arkadia]]. There, clouds supported them as though solid, while demons materialized around them. Vokenar fought beside Crone and sparred with her between those encounters. She attacked from unexpected directions and refused to let him lower his guard. During one exercise, she even fought alongside demons against him, forcing him to face both their attacks and her own.

Sigil watched and sometimes seemed ready to intervene. The sisters argued privately about Vokenar's preparation, but he overheard parts of their exchanges in Heavenly. Crone wanted a more effective warrior and accused Sigil of giving him too many breaks. Sigil defended the kindness, judgment, and ethics she had taught him. Without those qualities, she argued, Vokenar might never have accepted the mission to restore the sky at all.

Among the stars above them, Vokenar could see Aeris. She was unconscious and resting, yet appeared stronger than during their earlier journey together. Sigil explained that the Penumbra the party had returned from [[Castle Kaedon]] had brought Aeris to the point where she could begin healing herself. Left undisturbed, that recovery might eventually restore the sky even without more help, though it would take a very long time. Further [[Penumbra]] would accelerate it.

Crone objected to any reassurance that encouraged delay. New harm could interrupt that recovery, and Vokenar's work remained urgent. Seeing Aeris himself renewed his commitment. He told Crone to keep training him for as long as necessary and send him back when she thought him ready.

#### The attack on Crone

During their sparring, Crone invited Vokenar to deliver his strongest strike. His old Akasha blade passed her defenses, but the result was far worse than an ordinary training injury. Pearlescent blood poured from her, she cried out, and both Sigil and Aeris reacted in alarm. Sigil immediately told Vokenar that he had not caused what was happening. Something had struck Crone from Stark.

Crone grew frail and rapidly aged as the wound continued bleeding. Vokenar offered divination to find the attacker. He initially considered [[Vizier Jade]], but her magical defenses had frustrated earlier attempts to see her. Instead, he focused his scrying on [[Emperor Shen]].

The vision showed a dark space that felt underground, with rolling clouds, ice, and streams of molten metal. Emperor Shen stood beside [[Starfall]]. A moon appeared centered within its structure. Vokenar watched the Emperor throw another large piece of Penumbra into the device: its stars dispersed into the outer ring, and a tremendous beam shot toward the moon. Crone's worsening injuries coincided with the attack.

#### The broken moon above the Opal

Meanwhile, it was night aboard the Opal. [[Kerben]] was at the helm, struggling with unusually still air. [[Ceril]] and [[Domyx]] were resting, while [[Red Caesar]] continued his studies. Kerben first saw a pencil-thin white line crossing the heavens from the east. As it widened, he discerned red, green, blue, and yellow within it, merging into white at the edges. He associated those colors and its strange natural power with the four elements.

Kerben stopped the ship and hurried to bring Ceril onto the deck. By the time they returned, the streak had faded and the moon was broken. The blast had been silent. Ceril recognized the weapon's effect as the same kind of attack that had torn the sky during [[The Cataclysm]], and feared that Crone had been killed. The moon was not wholly gone: a damaged, crescentlike core remained among the displaced fragments.

They considered what would follow. Moon debris could strike settlements and the sea, causing casualties and enormous waves. Ordinary tides might change, and the moon's relationship to time raised questions they could not yet answer. These were assessments of the danger, rather than reports of every consequence already occurring.

From the direction and position of the shot, Kerben placed the strike above [[Brimbolyn]] in western elven territory. This made it an attack on the elves as well as on Crone. The falling debris would threaten the Empire's enemies below. Ceril feared for people he had once called his own, despite his long resentment toward their rulers. They discussed northern territories where high elves might escape, including the region from which [[Aramil Kiirnodel]] had originally come.

Sailing to Brimbolyn would take too long to respond to an immediate disaster, though Red Caesar's teleportation offered another possibility. The crew also needed to consider its own safety. Kerben thought about sheltered water, while Red Caesar argued that a ship capable of riding a swell could fare better in open water than near a shore where it broke. They feared for [[Cutlass Cray]] and for [[Alamar]] in the lagoon, but had no report establishing damage to either place.

#### Crone's instruction

Between worlds, Crone's condition worsened. Her body seemed to sink into the silvery blood surrounding her. Sigil said her sister had been attacked in a way she could not heal and told Vokenar to return to Stark. She began asking him to find the fallen pieces of the moon.

Crone interrupted. She insisted that Vokenar remain focused on Aeris and not waver from that mission. He promised to collect moon fragments if an opportunity arose, while continuing the work of restoring the sky. Crone smiled through her suffering and slumped back into the pool. Sigil stayed with her and sent Vokenar to his companions. Vokenar left Crone critically wounded in Sigil's care.

Kerben had assembled the crew in the commons to discuss the broken moon. [[Raxxy]], [[Ulrich Fjoller]], [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]], and the others were there when Vokenar returned in a beam of light. He told them what he had witnessed and relayed Crone's instruction.

Their religious understanding offered a reason for that priority. Aeris was the eldest sister, born from the world's original chaos and emptiness, and had created the moon and sun. If she regained her full power, she might be able to create a new moon or help resolve Crone's injury. The party regarded that as a possibility supporting their mission, not an outcome already secured.

Domyx argued that stopping the weapon would prevent further harm while they sought a cure. Red Caesar agreed that Starfall had to be broken and Aeris freed. Ceril suspected that Sigil might be the Empire's next target now that both of her sisters had been attacked.

Ulrich Fjoller asked who had fired the weapon. Vokenar looked toward Domyx, confirming that it was his grandfather. Lorelai Lapis-Acathian contemplated the enormous suffering Emperor Shen had caused. Domyx noted how difficult it became to think of victims as individual people when their numbers were so great. He supported bringing Emperor Shen to justice despite their family connection.

Vokenar believed that neither Emperor Shen nor Vizier Jade should be spared after such crimes. Red Caesar cautioned that revenge could not become their guiding purpose. Their central task remained restoring Aeris and the sky, even if stopping the perpetrators formed part of that work.

#### Keeping the route concealed

The attack occurred on the first day of the fifth moon, when [[The Carrot Cake]] was predicted to open. The party wondered whether the coincidence mattered. Ceril observed that an earlier attack on the moon might have interfered with the opening indefinitely, but they did not yet know how its damaged state affected the park's magic.

Vokenar asked what evidence they had that the place existed. Kerben had collected admission tickets since the early stages of their travels, and [[Theo Harvey]] had provided its location and opening information. The tickets supported the account, although they were not direct observation of the park in its current condition.

They considered going straight to the Penumbra hoard before another power found it. Ceril instead urged them to finish their nearby eastern errands first. If someone was watching the party, an immediate journey to the secret park could lead that observer to it. Raxxy supported confronting the dragon that had damaged the Opal and taking back Obould's treasure. The dragon's island was already close to their course; the trolls lay farther along, and Cutlass Cray could serve as a stop before the park. The dangerous visit to the keeper of the [[Tome of Satariel]] remained deferred.

Ceril also chose to attend to the moon disaster. He wanted to chart the fragments, warn [[The Garden]] and others through Sending, and check first on the elves in Brimbolyn. He asked Red Caesar to prepare travel to [[House Kiirnodel]], with a later reunion at The White Drake or Cutlass Cray. Red Caesar agreed. Concern for the elves took precedence over Ceril's bitterness toward them, and the ensuing island expedition consisted of the other four companions.

#### Xarag's Island

The next evening, the Opal rounded [[Xarag's Island]]. The sea was still quiet despite the disaster overhead. Luminescent algae surrounded the cliffs, and mist hung over glowing pools on the dark island. Nearby was the place where [[Xarag]] had broken open the Opal's hold at the beginning of the voyage.

Red Caesar prepared acid-resistance potions in his cauldron, and the four explorers drank them before confronting the black dragon. Kerben used the ship's lights and the terrain to identify repeated claw marks on a sloping approach from the water. Those signs suggested a nest on land. They considered whether the spread of Akasha in the depths had made the sea less hospitable to creatures that once lived there.

When visible tracks faded, Red Caesar's Locate Creature guided them onward. They found the dragon coiled in the darkness, awake rather than sleeping. Black dragon eggs were also nearby, but Red Caesar's magic distinguished the adult from its clutch. Kerben's knowledge of the species suggested a territorial predator unlikely to have friendly allies waiting beside it.

Kerben concealed himself with his rifle while his flying companion Tango accompanied the advance. Domyx and Vokenar approached more directly, and Red Caesar remained separate from Kerben's position. Xarag recognized the scent of their orc captain and taunted them for coming back for treasure he claimed to have spent.

Domyx engaged the dragon while Kerben opened with a devastating shot through the roof of its mouth. Red Caesar followed with lightning. Xarag answered with acid and clouds of black gadflies that harried Kerben, but the party's preparations reduced the danger of its breath.

Vokenar conjured a spiritual weapon in the form of Crone's bastard sword. It shimmered through the phases of the moon before striking the dragon, bringing his mentor's imagery into the fight. Domyx kept Xarag occupied and rallied his companions. The dragon's renewed acid attack badly hurt Vokenar and disrupted the sword as the confrontation continued into the next chunk.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_026/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### The dragon's hoard

Xarag continued attacking Vokenar with corrosive magic, but Vokenar healed himself through divine power and called Crone's sword again. Red Caesar's acid-resistance potions had kept the dragon's breath from overwhelming the party. Domyx pressed the close fight while Kerben recovered his aim despite the insect swarms.

Kerben's next decisive shot struck near the dragon's neck. Xarag fell into the glowing algal water, thrashed, and died. The party had finally defeated the creature that had wrecked their ship and stolen its cargo.

Kerben examined the body and found a way to preserve two remaining acid-resistance potions from Red Caesar's batch. By adding prepared dragon scales and material from its acid gizzard, he made them stable beyond the next use of the cauldron. The salvage produced lasting protective supplies rather than a new acid poison.

Domyx searched the water around the island while the others rested. He found a small gap where water flowed into a cliff and swam inside. A glimpse of gold revealed the dragon's hoard. He returned to guide the others into the hollow so they could recover the cargo together.

They brought back two hundred pounds of gold, five units of mithril, and bags of gems. Red Caesar recognized the gems as possessions of the Opal and Obould. Much of the mithril appeared to exceed the amount formerly carried aboard; another wreck, perhaps an elven vessel, was suggested as its source rather than established.

Two smaller objects mattered particularly to Obould. The [[Orkland Pin of Courage]] commemorated his service in southern sea battles to save orc tribes decades earlier. [[Obould's Wedding Band]] lay in its box on satin, bearing an inscription. Its presence raised the possibility that he had meant to give it to someone, but this scene did not identify a recipient or establish a marriage. The recovered goods went into the Opal's hold. Their captain remained absent, and the party hoped to return his personal possessions to him if he could be rescued.

#### Imperial balloons over the Opal

As the explorers rowed back, they heard the Opal's newly installed cannons firing. Domyx accelerated their return and leaped aboard; Tango flew up with him, and the others followed. [[Yalet Mora]] was at the helm, extending his arms and growing another pair to maneuver the ship. Ulrich Fjoller operated the cannons, and [[Brim the Bullywog]] fired the arbalest.

Three [[Gun Balloon]]s bearing [[Broyish Empire]] markings hovered overhead. The crew was trying to bring them into range while keeping the Opal clear of their weapons. Imperial soldiers began leaping from the gondolas onto the deck as the party returned. The attack had caught the ship during the explorers' absence.

Domyx seized one boarder and threw him into the sea. The man survived and began working his way back toward the ship. Vokenar struck two balloons with fire, bringing one down into the water as its envelope tore. Crew from the falling craft jumped onto the Opal, adding to the attackers already aboard.

Ulrich Fjoller and Brim the Bullywog continued firing while Yalet Mora handled the helm. The reinforced hull withstood some of the incoming fire, but the attack still caused serious damage. Red Caesar climbed the rigging and used Steel Wind Strike to reach another gondola, where two imperial men occupied the cramped space. The remaining pilot continued attacking the Opal even with Red Caesar beside him.

Domyx moved to protect Ulrich Fjoller when boarders threatened him. Vokenar flew toward the other surviving balloon and invoked divine power to place its pilot under Geas. He commanded the man to fly north without turning back. The pilot obeyed, raising the balloon and leaving the battle. That craft escaped under compulsion rather than being destroyed or captured.

#### Taking a balloon and clearing the deck

In the gondola, Red Caesar used a resilient sphere around himself to force the two imperial men out of the available space. One bailed with some control; the other fell more awkwardly. The expanding sphere also displaced equipment, tipped a brazier of burning oil, and interrupted the balloon's air supply. The craft descended against the stern of the Opal, damaged but recoverable, with Red Caesar protected inside his sphere.

The fight continued on deck. Domyx urged Ulrich Fjoller to leave his exposed post. His new brother-in-law gave him an encouraging pat before withdrawing. Domyx held a boarder in place while Vokenar descended among the attackers with Spirit Guardians appearing as the weapons of the three goddesses. The divine aura hindered the soldiers and struck those caught within it.

Red Caesar left the fallen gondola and swept across the boarders with another Steel Wind Strike. Kerben bound one attacker with ensnaring magic that animated the ship's ropes into nautical knots. Domyx killed the man he held, and Vokenar combined healing with the continuing protection of his divine weapons.

The remaining resistance collapsed. A blade within Vokenar's guardians brought down the specialist who had fought in the gondola. Red Caesar killed the soldier who broke out of Kerben's ropes, and Kerben shot the last boarder. The assault ended with one balloon lost in the sea, another flying north under its pilot's compelled obedience, and the third in the party's possession.

The captured balloon needed repairs, but could be made usable with the resources aboard. Full repairs to both it and the damaged Opal required supplies from port. Its capture gave the party a means of traveling above the sea, which they immediately recognized as useful if the waves became more dangerous.

Yalet Mora praised Red Caesar's performance and reminded him of his promise to help kill [[Yalet Aurum]]. Red Caesar said he had not forgotten. The reminder kept their earlier obligation alive; this session did not resolve the brothers' conflict. The crew began putting the captured craft in order, and the party rested.

#### The next League errand

The next nearby task was clearing two beach trolls from an island sought by [[The League of New Stark]]. The creatures prevented local people from safely using the area. The party chose the longboat and an approach through the shallows rather than risking their newly captured balloon.

Ceril had returned from his visit to the elves. He took Vokenar aside to seek divine guidance about their danger, the falling pieces of Crone, and what should happen next. He wanted to know whether Crone could still answer and to consult both her and Aeris. The conversation's answers were not presented here. Ceril and Vokenar remained away from the troll expedition, leaving Kerben, Domyx, and Red Caesar to go ashore with Zulu.

Red Caesar supplied healing potions for that group, which would otherwise be without either of its usual healers. Fog obscured the low islands and sandbars ahead. They also considered how changes in the tides might expose more land after the moon's damage, though their immediate task remained finding the trolls.

### Chunk 0002

#### Two beach trolls

The trolls charged as the three companions reached the island. Their cargo nets, seaweed, sand, and woven shells had helped conceal them against the beach. Kerben fired at one while Zulu moved forward and Domyx met its attack.

Transel's brother, Shreddle, remained in the fight. These beach trolls did not share the usual vulnerability to fire and acid. Wounded flesh flowed back together like clay, and the party needed another way to prevent that regeneration. Red Caesar chose to charm [[Transel]], taking one attacker out of the immediate fight while the others concentrated on his brother.

Transel did not understand Red Caesar's Western, but responded to the spell by approaching him as a friend. He spoke Runic, allowing Domyx to interpret. Transel begged for a boat off the little island, saying he had eaten everything there. His request to leave offered a possible solution beyond killing both trolls.

Meanwhile, thunder and radiant attacks failed to stop the other troll's recovery. Red Caesar tried Shocking Grasp, and its electricity continued moving through the creature's body. When Domyx struck it down afterward, the flesh melted into a puddle instead of reforming. Lightning had overcome the regeneration.

Transel asked whether Domyx was a friend of the mage who seemed to have been sent by the gods to free him. He wanted a raft or their longboat so he could leave. His statement expressed how he interpreted Red Caesar's arrival; it did not establish that another mage had imprisoned him.

#### A raft for Transel

Before giving up their boat, Red Caesar wanted to know whether Transel could offer anything from the island. Transel said he had already eaten its secrets, then reached through his claylike chest and pulled out an amethyst. He had kept the gem because he knew other people valued such things, hoping to trade it for passage. He handed it over, and the party stored it with their goods.

They decided to build a raft while the charm still encouraged cooperation. Domyx helped Transel fell palm trees and gather usable wood. Kerben found materials to bind the pieces together. Red Caesar used Bigby's Hand to move and arrange the logs, allowing them to finish before the spell's influence ended.

Transel climbed aboard and asked for his dead brother's femur to use as an oar. With the bone in hand, he rowed away. The League's immediate problem was resolved through two different outcomes: one troll had been killed, while the other had received the means to leave. The party retained its longboat and continued toward Cutlass Cray before visiting The Carrot Cake. The dangerous demon encounter farther north remained deferred.

#### Waves and a protected port

On the voyage, the consequences of the moon's destruction finally reached them directly. An enormous swell rose ahead of the Opal. Kerben brought the ship straight into it rather than allowing the water to strike at an angle, then steered over its crest. The improvements to the vessel were essential to surviving the passage. When Red Caesar took his turn at the helm, he likewise guided the Opal through the waves.

The companions feared that Cutlass Cray's boardwalks must have been torn apart. Instead, they found the town intact. [[The Marid]] checked their paperwork as usual and asked whether they had seen the great wave after the moon exploded. His water magic had kept it away from his port.

They could still see enormous swells approaching, but the Marid bent the water outward before it reached the town. At one point it rose like a cross section of the sea, with fish visible within it, yet remained held off from the boardwalks. The danger was real; the city's protector had prevented it from becoming a disaster there.

After the party docked and entered town, Transel arrived separately on his rickety raft. He greeted them with a broad wave rather than attacking after the charm had ended. He went to [[The Brine & Bodak]] and became its new bartender, filling a vacancy the party's earlier recruitment had created. Their decision to help him leave the island had brought him a place in the town they now shared.

### Summary

- Vokenar trained with Crone and saw Aeris recovering between worlds. Sigil said the returned Penumbra had enabled Aeris to begin healing herself, while Crone insisted the mission remain urgent.
- Emperor Shen used Starfall against the moon, gravely wounding Crone and breaking the moon above elven territory. Kerben saw the elemental beam; Vokenar witnessed Emperor Shen through scrying.
- Crone instructed Vokenar to prioritize Aeris. He returned to the crew, and they kept restoring the sky as their central purpose while planning to stop further attacks.
- Ceril went to check on the elves. The other four killed Xarag and recovered the Opal's gold, mithril, gems, Obould's courage pin, and his boxed wedding band.
- Imperial gun balloons attacked the Opal. The party defeated the boarders, compelled one pilot to fly north, and captured another craft; ship and balloon still needed full repairs.
- Ceril returned and consulted the gods with Vokenar. Kerben, Domyx, and Red Caesar killed one beach troll and built a raft for the charmed Transel in exchange for an amethyst.
- Kerben and Red Caesar steered the upgraded Opal through enormous swells. Cutlass Cray survived under the Marid's protection, and Transel arrived to become bartender at the Brine & Bodak.

The moon attack made the danger of Starfall immediate again. Antumbra had been planted, but its delivery and use remained outside the party's knowledge, and the Empire could still fire the weapon. Vokenar's training ended in a crisis he could not solve where he stood. Crone's suffering and her insistence on Aeris redirected his urgency toward the same work he had already undertaken, rather than a separate search that would abandon the sky.

The crew's discussion also clarified how that mission related to justice. Domyx accepted the need to stop his grandfather, and Vokenar saw killing the perpetrators as warranted. Red Caesar kept the group's attention on restoration rather than revenge. Ceril's choice to check on the elves showed a parallel response: their present danger mattered despite his old grievances.

The dragon's death repaired an earlier loss and recovered evidence of Obould's personal history while he remained captive. The immediate attack on the Opal then exposed the cost of leaving the crew under threat. Yalet Mora, Ulrich Fjoller, and Brim the Bullywog had kept defending the vessel until the explorers returned. The party emerged with a new aerial craft, but also with damage that required a port's supplies.

Transel's fate provided a different ending to a hostile encounter. Charm opened a conversation, practical assistance removed him from the island, and his later greeting showed that the contact had not ended in renewed violence when the spell expired. At Cutlass Cray, the party found refuge from the waves and a place to repair and prepare. The Carrot Cake remained their intended destination, Crone's recovery remained unsettled, and stopping Starfall was more urgent than ever.
