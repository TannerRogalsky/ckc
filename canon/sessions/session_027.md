# Session 027

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_027/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Preparing in Cutlass Cray

The recovered dragon treasure gave the party resources to prepare for [[The Karat Cake]]. Its predicted opening had passed, and [[Kerben]]'s long pursuit of [[Jack Harvey]]'s clues had finally supplied a route there. The tickets he had found in his former employer [[Farraday]]'s supposed crypt now led toward a place where Jack Harvey had hidden broken sky.

Before leaving [[Cutlass Cray]], the companions replenished supplies and improved their equipment. At [[The Wonder Hulk]]'s shop, Kerben bought three feather tokens: one could produce a swan boat, another a large multicolored bird capable of carrying passengers or cargo, and the third a balloon. He exchanged mithril and his black periapt for them.

[[Ceril]] obtained gem-encrusted vessels for Heroes' Feast and visited [[Savar Brews]] for emergency supplies. He hoped for help with dangerous movement, particularly surviving a fall into a chasm. The proprietor, [[Gilder Savar]], recognized him immediately, though Ceril did not recognize the lich before him.

Gilder Savar addressed Ceril as a former traveling companion and projected an illusion of his old human appearance. They had escaped [[Brimbolyn]] together with [[Vanzia Vynnfae]] before the Cataclysm. Gilder Savar explained that perfecting his craft required more time than a human life allowed: the best drinks might take centuries to mature. He had chosen undeath so he could remain long enough to perfect them.

Ceril agreed to catch up, but kept the details of Aeris and the journey to The Karat Cake private. He did tell Gilder Savar that Vanzia Vynnfae was alive, had joined the Broyish Empire, and had recently helped the party. Their conversation restored contact between two survivors of an old escape without resolving how Gilder Savar had passed from his earlier mortal peril into his present condition.

Gilder Savar supplied Ceril with a potion of feather fall rather than the more costly means of sustained flight. Ceril also purchased an invulnerability potion and earrings of wakeful birdsong, which would briefly restore him if he collapsed and then break. He spent some of the summer spice among his goods on those preparations, keeping other gems and metal in reserve.

#### Red Caesar's purchases

[[Red Caesar]] sought scrolls containing Rary's Telepathic Bond, Planar Binding, and Greater Invisibility. He intended to copy their spells into his book rather than retain them only as single-use scrolls. Planar Binding would give him another possible approach to the dangerous fiend guarding the [[Tome of Satariel]], though the party had not yet chosen to face that creature.

At the Wonder Hulk's shop, Red Caesar also bought dust of dryness and a corrosive powder for attacking ordinary metal. He purchased three immovable rods, and the merchant added five pipes of smoke monsters, with different colors of smoke. Red Caesar distributed the pipes so every party member had one, adding another small theatrical flourish to the billowing cloaks they already possessed.

The Wonder Hulk proposed exchanging Red Caesar's lightning wand for the [[Wand of Blighting Bolts]]. Red Caesar already had several ways to produce lightning; the new wand would give him access to withering necrotic magic and a more flexible range of uses. He considered the trade and ultimately accepted it, paying with the old wand, a sapphire, jade, and mithril. Later, as the party approached the park, he explicitly noted that he had switched his wand to necrotic magic.

#### Vokenar and the ship

At [[The Church of the Thirty Lights]], [[Vokenar]] established a sanctuary for Word of Recall. He also designated [[The Opal]], whose earlier consecration made it a fitting refuge. These preparations offered ways to return to the church or the vessel in danger, under the understanding of the magic established in the campaign.

The church had retrieved pieces of the shattered moon. From one of them, its craftspeople fashioned the [[Moon Blade]] for Vokenar. Despite its name, the weapon was a versatile warhammer. Its connection to [[Crone]] made it especially meaningful after the attack he had witnessed. It could wield destructive moon-associated power against spellcasters, and its moonlight could weaken resistance to fire and cold.

Vokenar paid with valuables rather than surrendering his older Akasha blade, which he expressly wanted to keep available. The new weapon supplemented his resources instead of erasing the earlier one. He also purchased enchanted cannonballs that added fiery force to the Opal's guns and additional healing scrolls.

The companions discussed making the vessel harder to intercept. Ceril contributed an amethyst toward a feather token that could conceal the ship and everyone aboard it for a day. This ghost-ship token was a consumable resource, not a permanent change to the Opal.

Vokenar then invested in a further improvement for sailing. A magical apparatus using an air elemental became the ship's genie engine, generating favorable wind when natural conditions were poor. Its primary purpose was to drive the sails, though the elemental could be given a more independent form if needed. The addition improved the ship's reliability on the coming voyage.

Kerben completed his own major purchase with [[The Ascot]], a scarf woven with the appearance of different animal pelts. It allowed him to communicate continually with animals and exchange positions with a nearby companion. The item suited the way he worked with his beasts and could move either him or an animal out of danger.

The Opal received repair work while docked, addressing damage from the northern voyage and the imperial balloon attack. The conversation about recruiting a powder monkey did not establish a new hire.

#### Old acquaintances and the hidden vessel

[[Domyx]] checked on [[Transel]] at [[The Brine & Bodak]]. The troll was being introduced as a trainee and trying to fit in with the staff. A hobgoblin helped teach him to serve drinks and deal with customers. Transel still spoke only Runic, leaving a language barrier with the other workers, but Domyx found him making an effort rather than causing trouble.

[[The Marid]] gave the party a fuller account of the wave that had threatened Cutlass Cray. The sea had risen above the whole city. For hours, his protective dome kept the water away while people could stand on the docks and see fish swimming overhead. His magic explained why the town had survived a swell far larger than its buildings.

Red Caesar also asked about [[Lenth the Rugged]]. [[Pleasance MacLenth]] knew her father chiefly through stories and had never met him in person. His fate remained uncertain; the discussion suggested divination as a possible later inquiry rather than establishing his death.

The party sailed toward The Karat Cake, using the genie engine and their other improvements to make good progress despite unfavorable natural winds. The journey took three days. On the way, they checked the grotto where [[The Hideous Truth]] had been concealed. The vessel remained where they had left it, now more thoroughly covered in vines and undergrowth. They chose to retain that camouflage and postpone the work of clearing it for a future launch.

#### The revealed approach

The route carried them among the high southern peaks. Ceril and Kerben remembered the ridge as a boundary in old Stark between the lush northern territories and the dry southern lands. [[Esperanto]] had flourished beyond it despite the desert, with goblins helping establish the city. Mountains that had once appeared to be an unoccupied wall now provided many hidden inlets among their exposed heights.

The Opal maneuvered through sharp rocks until it reached an approach that had only recently lost its concealing illusions. A smaller docking place led into a larger lagoon cut into the mountains. The artificial shape suggested a former approach or staging area that the flood had submerged. They chose a sheltered place to leave the ship.

Overhead, the canopy mixed green leaves with red, orange, and yellow foliage, while flowers from apparently different seasons grew together. Farther up the path, painted structures, tethered balloons, and brilliant magical lights appeared above the hills. An orange route led toward an enormous carrot shape. The entrance displayed the stylized carrot-and-diamond emblem of [[40 Karats]], the company Kerben remembered.

The lights made the newly exposed park conspicuous. The companions recognized that its location might not remain secret for long. Before entering, Red Caesar supplied the healing potions he had prepared during the voyage, and Ceril provided Heroes' Feast. He made a familiar shipboard stew with carrots, gathered ingredients, and spices, giving the meal protective strength against fear and poison. [[Zulu]] joined the feast with the party.

#### The entrance's defenders

The five companions and Zulu advanced along the carved passage. Illusory drums, a tuba, and a saxophone played a lively waltz. Their music softened and the images faded when anyone approached, keeping the performance at a comfortable distance. Tents and places to tether horses lined the way beneath steep cliffs and tangled vegetation.

Kerben found the setting both familiar and wrong. Jack Harvey had intended a visible, inviting spectacle, but signs had been removed and much of the display was diminished. Kerben understood this as more than simple neglect: after the world changed, Jack Harvey had been converting an amusement park into a fortified refuge for his stockpiled Penumbra. Its present form reflected that repurposing as well as the decades of deterioration afterward.

Ancient magic and Kerben's unease kept the group alert. As rain turned the ground to mud, humanoid figures rose from it and from around the tents. Their orange clothing looked like ruined uniforms. Mummies and revenants surrounded the visitors, the latter appearing as amalgams of multiple bodies.

A rabbitlike figure sat ahead on a bench, drinking tea from a fine white saucer. Red Caesar recognized it as a [[Magen]], a wizard-created construct containing a reproduced part of its maker rather than an ordinary mechanical servant. The rabbit welcomed them to 40 Karats and announced the beginning of the dance.

Vokenar turned two of the mummies away. They retreated toward the seated Magen, which refused to help them. Domyx and Vokenar engaged the revenants while Ceril called a crab and fought in his archer constellation. An illusory audience appeared to cheer the confrontation. The entrance battle was still underway when the chunk ended.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_027/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Through the entrance

Red Caesar swept through the undead with Steel Wind Strike, allowing Kerben and Zulu to press the weakened attackers. Vokenar used the Moon Blade against a revenant. The two turned mummies accepted the Magen's advice to leave and sank back into the rain-softened mud.

The remaining revenants temporarily paralyzed Domyx and Vokenar. Ceril defended himself with his shield and continued fighting while Kerben fired from concealment. The protections of the feast had helped against fear, but the party still had to contend with the undead's other powers. Together they destroyed the remaining attackers, with Ceril's light and crab helping finish the last revenant.

Domyx recovered his movement, and the group continued without resting. The Magen introduced itself as one of Jack Harvey's many reproductions, charged with watching The Karat Cake. Kerben remembered Jack Harvey's interest in filling the park with such constructs, and the guide explained that part of Jack Harvey's mind existed within its own.

#### A constellation of resorts

The Magen described an amusement park spread across Stark rather than confined to the midway. Teleportation circles linked distant resorts, letting visitors see places across the world for one ticket. [[Magic Hat]] had once offered a hotel, but its repurposed role was more like a bunker. [[Hole Shebang]] contained festivities and games; other destinations included a train and a southern island arena where visitors competed to become [[King of the Hole]]. The guide did not know how every area had fared during the intervening decades.

Kerben asked for carrot tea, and the Magen poured him a cup included with admission. The drink gave him a sudden desire to remain and enjoy the place forever. He shook off that feeling and continued with the others.

Domyx asked why the park opened only during part of the year. The Magen explained that running it required immense energy and that business during the rainy off-season could not justify the cost. Jack Harvey had been willing to run at a loss, but his own superior had persuaded him otherwise. The explanation added a commercial history to the opening cycle without identifying that superior.

Asked for a map, the guide did not provide one. It did create a playful illusion marking where the party stood. When Vokenar asked about Penumbra, the Magen recalled Jack Harvey burying unusual material but did not claim direct knowledge of what it was. From the party's description and the midway's continued operation, it concluded that Penumbra was probably the remaining power source.

That raised a problem for Vokenar: taking the material to restore the sky might shut the park down. The possibility of substituting manufactured Penumbra was raised, but no such arrangement was made. The party left the guide and continued exploring.

#### Trees and a new ambush

The next boulevard passed painted stalls, tables, and benches beneath the cliffs. Higher up, tangled vines and thorny growth had reclaimed the terrain. At the party's level, however, the trees remained beautifully maintained. Ceril recognized specimens from many parts of old Stark, all fruiting together despite their different climates. He touched their bark and gathered fruit and seeds, accumulating a collection for future planting.

To Domyx, the park offered a strange composite of the world he had known chiefly through stories. It displayed an inviting but artificial version of that lost landscape. He explored the stalls while the others watched the surroundings.

One stall still shone with lights and played music, but a rustling above drew Vokenar's attention first. He warned the party before a [[Cloaker]] dropped from the canopy. Two brightly colored [[Flame Skull]]s emerged from concealment, and a pile of scrap beside Red Caesar rose into [[Hooksy the Clown Automaton]]. The Cloaker appeared to have summoned the others with infrasound.

Hooksy's chained limbs and nailed-on clown face resembled a broken attraction placed into defense mode. Despite its name and metal exterior, it was not an ordinary construct. Red Caesar's protective magic applied to the other kinds of threat rather than to Hooksy, and he repositioned toward the Cloaker. Domyx exchanged places with Ceril through his cloud magic and took up his glaive near the skulls.

The Cloaker's terrifying moan failed to overwhelm those protected by the feast. Its physical attack was another matter: it wrapped itself around Red Caesar, enclosing him in its thin, living membrane.

#### Freeing Red Caesar

The Cloaker had attached itself closely enough that attacking it would also hurt Red Caesar. Ceril noticed its veins hooking into him and changed from attacking to healing him. Vokenar concentrated on the other enemies instead of risking his companion.

Red Caesar countered a Flame Skull's fireball before it could strike the group. He then escaped the Cloaker with Misty Step, leaving it clamped around empty space. With him free, the party could attack the creature without sharing the harm with him.

Ceril's crab tore into Hooksy and exposed flesh, blood, and musculature beneath the metal and ceramic mask. Kerben's shots broke away more of its plating. Vokenar struck the fiendish skulls with divine radiance, while the Moon Blade's influence weakened their resistance to cold without overcoming their immunity to fire.

Domyx used his Gith Shard weapon to cut down both Flame Skulls and Hooksy, its dark, space-colored energy extending between his targets. The Cloaker survived and tried to reach Ceril. Red Caesar blasted it through the branches with Thunderwave, tearing its membrane, but it still attacked and divided its appearance into overlapping images.

Ceril dispelled those images and struck the creature as they converged. Vokenar drained its strength, and Kerben finally shot it out of the air. The party took a short rest after defeating the four attackers, with Vokenar also tending injuries.

Red Caesar salvaged Hooksy's ceramic clown mask, still covered in blood and flesh. He kept it among his possessions and proposed wearing it on the back of his head to confuse predators. Other masks remained in the stall, but this one carried the history of the creature they had just killed.

#### Mind the Mimic

The operating stall offered a game called Mind the Mimic. Animated gloves showed the visitors several chests, briefly revealing one as full of treasure and the others as toothy monsters before restoring their identical appearance. The boxes were physical, while their transformations were illusions. The game allowed the group one free attempt.

The companions selected Kerben for his keen observation, and Red Caesar supported him with Guidance. Kerben followed the correct chest through increasingly complex rounds as more gloves and boxes joined the shuffle. In the final round, the game transferred gold and false teeth between containers, breaking his certainty.

He had nevertheless advanced far enough to earn four portions of treasure: gold and platinum coins shaped around the park's carrot emblem, marked with 40K on one side and a rabbit on the other. The winnings went into the shared hold. Once the rewards were delivered, the stand's remaining power drained away and it shut down.

The group searched the nearby stalls and continued. Ceril's gathered seeds formed a substantial collection from the boulevard's trees, another kind of value preserved from the park.

#### The breathing tent

As they approached a large tent, Ceril and Kerben noticed its walls expand and settle as though it were breathing. The others were still taking in the surroundings when the front opened and drew air inward beneath a brilliant glow.

The [[Haunted Living Tent]] attacked. Kerben moved clear of its reaching support and fired into the structural points he could identify. Ceril called Moonbeam onto it, ending his crab's presence as he turned to the larger threat. They began damaging the tent before the rest of the group could fully react. Its nature and the destination behind that light were revealed in the next chunk.

#### Inside the tent

The tent opened wider, revealing rows of teeth around a spiraling light. Kerben, Red Caesar, and Vokenar were drawn inside. To those outside, their bodies separated into points of light and vanished into its mouth. Ceril resisted the first pull and remained with Domyx and Zulu.

The three trapped companions appeared in a pleasant little house with sofas, lamps, a table, bay windows, and a green landscape beyond them. Another Magen sat drinking tea. It welcomed company but warned that the visit would not be pleasant for its new guests.

The furniture immediately came alive, striking them in a swirling assault. Kerben examined the windows and apparent door, finding that the attractive surfaces were false: the glass felt like wood, and there was no ordinary exit matching the courtyard outside. He began cutting into a corner where the support felt different. His attacks opened dark holes in the cheerful interior.

Outside, Ceril's moonlight and other attacks pierced the tent, while Domyx slashed it with his glaive. Those openings released brilliant, furnace-like light and uncomfortable hot wind. The two sides could damage the same creature, but could not simply see or reach one another through an ordinary room.

Red Caesar recognized that the house occupied an extra-dimensional space. Teleportation Circle and Word of Recall would not bring them back across that boundary. Plane Shift might have done so, but they had no available means to use it. Destroying the creature remained their practical way out, though Red Caesar could not yet know precisely what its collapse would do to them.

He enlarged Vokenar, who used his new weapon and a conjured moon-associated sword to break into the supports. Kerben kept cutting at the same vulnerable structure. Their blows widened the holes, revealing emptiness beyond the interior rather than the world they had left.

#### Breaking the house

The tent pulled Ceril inside on its next attempt, ending his Moonbeam outside. Domyx and Zulu were now the only companions remaining in the courtyard. The Magen greeted the additional guest as furniture continued attacking the others.

Ceril's intervention, Vokenar's protection, and Red Caesar's own defenses helped keep the trapped group from being crushed against the walls. Kerben realized that the growing holes opened into the Astral Plane, offering no safe ordinary escape. Ceril attacked the floor with Shillelagh while the others worked on the surrounding structure.

Domyx climbed onto the tent, away from the direction of its sucking mouth, and cut down through its roof. The damage he caused met the openings Kerben had made from within. The tent began to break apart and rapidly deflate.

Inside, the house dissolved into starry particles. The seated Magen disintegrated first, saying goodbye. Red Caesar noticed a portrait, and Kerben recognized Jack Harvey's likeness and the painting's value. Together they seized [[Jack Harvey's Portrait]] before the remaining interior vanished.

The trapped companions returned to the real world, holding the painting inside the collapsing tent. Domyx stood beside them on its deflating material. Everyone had escaped, and the portrait was placed in the Opal's hold. After healing, they chose to continue rather than take another short rest.

#### Four paths and a sealed gate

Beyond the tent lay a broad courtyard. Kerben and Ceril watched for further threats while the others examined it. A heavy gate stood to one side, its seam and visible wards indicating that it could open but was secured by powerful magic. The 40 Karats emblem formed part of that protection. Four distinctive lamps beside the gate remained unlit.

At the courtyard's center, four gold-rimmed holes opened into darkness. Signs identified their destinations: Magic Hat beneath a magician's top hat, Hole Shebang beneath electrical arcs and a roller coaster, King of the Hole beside a hill-and-carrots motif, and [[Hole on Wheels]] with its colorful train.

Red Caesar was immediately drawn to the electrical imagery of Hole Shebang. Before the party finished investigating, he jumped toward its portal. Magic briefly held him motionless above it, then drew him out of the courtyard. Vokenar's enlargement ended when Red Caesar departed.

The others questioned another Magen. It explained that Jack Harvey had placed one lamp in each of the four destinations. They needed to light all four to enter his inner sanctum beyond the gate. There he had secured prized possessions, including the large rocklike pieces the Magen had watched him gather while the world was falling apart. Jack Harvey had intended to keep them from being taken.

The guide expanded its account of the destinations. Magic Hat's hotel was probably infested with undead. King of the Hole combined an island resort with a coliseum. Hole on Wheels was a flying train overlooking the desert around Esperanto, Jack Harvey's old home. Hole Shebang housed technological marvels, games, and ideas for future improvements to the park. These were the guide's descriptions, not reports of places the party had yet explored.

Vokenar considered dividing the party, but the Magen warned that earlier visitors who separated had not returned from their chosen holes. Their remains might still lie within. Domyx favored following Red Caesar rather than leaving anyone to face a destination alone, and Vokenar went ahead into Hole Shebang.

#### Securing the approach and entering Hole Shebang

The guide knew of no ready mechanism for sealing the courtyard and suggested alarms instead. Kerben set an Alarm at the entrance, while Domyx moved a table and helped arrange a barricade against wandering threats.

When Domyx lifted the table, papers and abandoned adventuring equipment fell from it: a backpack, an old knife, and leather armor. They might have belonged to one of the unsuccessful visitors, though no body there settled the owner's fate. Among them was a [[Ranger Scroll]] containing Conjure Barrage. Domyx gave it to Kerben, who could use its two inscriptions to call a burst of ammunition.

After those preparations, the remaining companions followed Red Caesar and Vokenar through Hole Shebang. They emerged one by one from a barrel into a cavern vestibule. Copper wiring powered hanging lanterns; the illumination was electrical rather than magical. A folding metal lattice gate blocked the view farther inside.

The barrel they had used bore the name [[City Hole]], identifying the route back to the main courtyard. Other barrels led to Magic Hat, Hole on Wheels, and King of the Hole. City Hole named that returning destination, not the cavern in which they now stood.

Red Caesar examined the portals and recognized the teleportation-circle sigils underlying them. Having seen their markings, he could use Teleportation Circle to reach those destinations independently if the park's portals became inaccessible. The group had gained another means of navigating the resort's connected sites.

They prepared for a short rest in the vestibule, with Vokenar proposing Prayer of Healing to shorten it. Domyx intended to use his storm-proofing potion for the electrical destination, but held off until the next stage rather than consuming it at the session's close. The five companions were reunited at the gate into Hole Shebang, with four lamps still to find and light.

### Summary

- The party spent its recovered wealth on equipment and ship resources. Kerben acquired feather tokens and the Ascot; Red Caesar acquired spell scrolls, powders, immovable rods, party pipes, and the Wand of Blighting Bolts.
- Ceril reunited with the lich Gilder Savar, bought protective supplies and feast components, and contributed a ghost-ship token. Vokenar obtained the Moon Blade at the church, established recall sanctuaries, and funded enchanted ammunition, healing scrolls, and the Opal's genie engine.
- Domyx checked on Transel, and the Marid described protecting Cutlass Cray beneath a dome as the sea rose above it. The Hideous Truth remained hidden under growing vegetation.
- The group sailed three days to the newly revealed park, The Karat Cake, and entered after Ceril's Heroes' Feast. Its orange-uniformed undead attacked; the party defeated the remaining defenders after two mummies withdrew.
- A Magen described Jack Harvey's linked resorts and inferred that Penumbra sustained the midway. Ceril collected seeds, Kerben won gold and platinum carrot coins, and Red Caesar kept Hooksy's mask after another ambush.
- The living tent trapped four companions in an extra-dimensional house. Attacks from within and Domyx's work outside destroyed it, and Red Caesar and Kerben saved Jack Harvey's Portrait.
- The party learned that four lamps opened Jack Harvey's inner sanctum. After Red Caesar jumped ahead, they secured the courtyard, gave Kerben an abandoned Ranger Scroll, and reunited in Hole Shebang's electrically lit entrance cavern.

The preparations gave the party more than stronger weapons. Recall sanctuaries, feather tokens, an invisible-ship option, and the genie engine widened their choices for travel and retreat. Ceril's meeting with Gilder Savar also restored a connection to his earlier life. Gilder Savar's pursuit of mastery through undeath contrasted with the urgent, present work that Ceril kept partly private.

The Karat Cake brought Kerben's old employment and the party's effort to restore the sky into the same place. Its spectacle survived, but its defenses, abandoned uniforms, and hostile occupants showed what decades of isolation had made of Jack Harvey's refuge. The Magen preserved part of his mind and knowledge while remaining limited in its understanding of what powered the park and what had happened in distant sections.

Exploration demanded care beyond defeating enemies. Red Caesar's escape from the Cloaker prevented attacks on it from harming him, while the living tent required those inside and outside to damage the same structure from separate spaces. Amid those threats, the companions preserved seeds, art, equipment, and old currency. Their search was already recovering pieces of the vanished world as well as approaching the Penumbra they needed.

At the close, none of the four lamps had been lit. The courtyard's gate remained sealed, and the party was together beyond the Hole Shebang portal, preparing to investigate its technological interior. Red Caesar had learned the circle markings connecting the destinations, while the Alarm and barricade provided limited protection behind them.
