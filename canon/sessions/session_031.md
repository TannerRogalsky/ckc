# Session 031

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_031/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Kerben preserves the ship

The opening returned to [[Kerben]]'s defense of [[The Opal]] while his four companions were still completing [[The Carrot Cake]]'s challenges. Imperial vessels had found the ship, and it had already taken damage by the time he returned in the longboat. [[Ulrich Fjoller]], [[Brim the Bullywog]], [[Yalet Mora]], and the other crew members worked its upgraded weapons, while Kerben took the helm. His priority was to keep the crew alive rather than attempt to defeat the approaching fleet.

Kerben ordered Yalet Mora to operate the bilge pump and identified the most dangerous vessel, partly concealed behind the leading escort. Damaged steering ropes made turning The Opal harder than expected, but he brought its weapons to bear. The combined attack disabled enemy munitions, and the great arbalest broke the vessel's mast. Its inability to sail gave the crew a brief reprieve while the pump slowed the water entering their own hull.

Brim the Bullywog reminded Kerben of the ghost-ship feather token that could conceal The Opal for a day. Ulrich Fjoller welcomed the chance to protect [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]] and the crew. With a natural breeze now sufficient for sailing, they could also release the air elemental from the genie engine against the enemy crews. Kerben considered boarding and taking the crippled vessel; Brim the Bullywog volunteered to cross over with others and the elemental. The exchange did not establish a completed capture or what they recovered.

Kerben then recognized dozens of sails farther east. More imperial galleons were approaching, making a sustained engagement hopeless. Before retreating, he used one of his Conjure Barrage scrolls: spectral musketmen appeared alongside the crew and fired across the nearby enemy deck, throwing defenders into the sea. The remaining escorts retaliated, further damaging The Opal. Brim the Bullywog urged him to activate the token and get the vessel to safety.

Kerben consumed the ghost-ship token, making The Opal disappear from outside view. Its position beside the crippled enemy vessel might cause later arrivals to mistake that wreck for their target, but this remained a possible advantage rather than a witnessed imperial mistake. He ordered the crew west, away from the immediate fighting and toward the waters near [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]]. He stayed aboard for several hours before returning to the park entrance late in the day, where he concealed himself and rested overnight. His growth to a more accomplished hunter and scout accompanied that rest.

#### Following the imperial landing party

The next morning, an imperial boat landed near the entrance. Soldiers searched the trees with lanterns and forced a manacled [[Theo Harvey]] ahead of them. Kerben remained hidden and used Speak with Animals to send [[Tango]] scouting. He overheard Theo Harvey claiming that he had guided the earlier visitors while blindfolded and could not identify them. Theo Harvey also insisted that the park had probably already been looted. His escort distrusted the story and pushed him onward.

Tango identified the [[Imperial Xihe]] farther offshore by its marked masthead. He suspected that the Empire's ruler or fleet commander might be aboard and warned that it could arrive within hours. Kerben followed the landing party into The Carrot Cake rather than fight it alone. The soldiers had killed the helpful [[Magen]] near the entrance. Theo Harvey's constant conversation distracted them enough for Kerben to move ahead through the stalls and find cover near the great vault door.

All four lamps were lit, but the imperials could not open the door. Theo Harvey used that failure to reinforce his claim that the earlier visitors had taken everything and that continuing would waste their time. Their commander refused to leave. Kerben watched an imperial spellcaster examine the barrier, keeping his weapons ready while waiting for his companions.

Meanwhile, after their rest aboard [[Hole on Wheels]], [[Ceril]] prepared Heroes' Feast for the four travelers, and [[Vokenar]] renewed Water Walk. [[Red Caesar]] inscribed the teleportation sigils and returned them to the midway. Vampire spawn were already attacking the imperials there. Their arrival drew an angry exclamation from [[Fharan]]: he had understood [[Vizier Jade]] to have shut down the portals. That accusation identified her involvement through his testimony, although the party had not witnessed her sabotage aboard the train.

#### Reunion at the vault door

The five companions reunited in the middle of the conflict. Fharan recognized Red Caesar as the imperial citizen he had previously admitted to the palace. Red Caesar attacked the imperial force with Steel Wind Strike, while Ceril turned his magic against the undead. Theo Harvey encouraged Fharan to concentrate on the vampire spawn, falsely presenting the newcomers as their allies. For a time, that deception diverted imperial attention from the party.

Red Caesar determined why the door remained closed to the soldiers: its magic recognized those who had completed the lamp challenges. A successful participant could open it by touching the carrot emblem. The imperials lacked that qualification, although sufficiently powerful dispelling or deceptive magic might offer another route. The lit lamps alone had not given every visitor access.

An imperial scion struck Red Caesar with Harm, draining his vitality and leaving his body visibly diminished. Kerben used Knock through the Curator's keyring to break Theo Harvey's manacles, then gave him his balloon feather token. Theo Harvey thanked him and prepared his escape. He confirmed that the Penumbra lay beyond the door, warned that Vizier Jade was coming, and said he intended to hide among the dwarves, whose language he knew. He had not yet departed when the fighting resumed.

Domyx attacked the undead threatening them, and Vokenar's radiant magic devastated enemies from both hostile groups. Red Caesar killed an imperial warlord, but remained vulnerable after the scion's curse. Examining the vault door, he also realized that its seal was holding back the ocean. Opening it without preparation could unleash a dangerous flood into the midway.

Fharan eventually saw through Theo Harvey's diversion and ordered the soldiers to attack the party. The surviving vampire spawn continued attacking the imperials, leaving a confused struggle around the door. The scion stunned Ceril and ended his Moonbeam, while an imperial warrior struck the already weakened Red Caesar. The chunk ended with Red Caesar badly wounded and the vault still closed.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_031/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Defeating Fharan's force

Red Caesar collapsed as the battle continued. Domyx fought free of a vampire spawn and turned on the scion responsible for draining him. Kerben shot at that spellcaster while Tango harried Fharan. Theo Harvey activated the balloon token and rose out of the fighting, completing the escape Kerben had prepared in the preceding chunk. His intended refuge remained the dwarven lands; this scene did not establish his arrival there.

Vokenar used a divine healing scroll to restore Red Caesar to consciousness, then brought down radiant daylight on the hostile force. The scion died calling out that she had failed Vizier Jade, and undead caught in the light were destroyed. Red Caesar killed the remaining warlord and directed his companions to preserve Fharan for questioning.

Fharan warned that failure in the Empire left him little chance of surviving even if the party wished to spare him. He nevertheless continued fighting, using Phantasmal Killer to bring Red Caesar down again. Vokenar restored him a second time, and Domyx knocked the seneschal to the ground.

Red Caesar detected a lethal magical contingency attached to Fharan, intended to prevent his capture, and successfully dispelled it. Ceril then used Greater Restoration on Red Caesar, removing the scion's drain on his vitality. These actions solved different problems: disabling the contingency made interrogation possible, while Ceril's restoration reversed the lasting harm to his companion.

Fharan's final Chain Lightning defeated Red Caesar's attempted counterspell and struck the party. Red Caesar fell unconscious for the third time, and Vokenar collapsed as well. Kerben aimed to incapacitate rather than kill the seneschal, and Domyx finally subdued him. Ceril's chalice-enhanced healing restored the companions, including both fallen spellcasters. None of the party died in this battle.

#### A dying prisoner's answers

Although the contingency had been removed, Fharan was bleeding from severe wounds. Ceril and Kerben prepared an herbal mixture using their knowledge of plants and poisons. It dulled his pain and brought him into a dreamlike state in which he could answer questions, but they did not magically heal his injuries. The party had only a few minutes to learn what he knew.

Fharan said [[Obould]] was still alive and was being brought to the site by [[Emperor Shen]] and Vizier Jade. They would arrive before noon. He also identified [[Sigil]], the sun, as [[Starfall]]'s next target, and argued that Red Caesar's talents could achieve far more under imperial guidance.

When asked about mind-reading, Fharan could not explain Vizier Jade's power. He knew only what she chose to share and described her abilities as beyond his understanding. The discussion left innate power or a supporting item possible, without establishing either explanation.

Ceril asked whether [[Aeris]] could be freed. Fharan claimed that the world and the goddess were approaching their end and that the Empire would remake the world in its own image. He assured the companions that they could all be welcomed into that successor world. Ceril and Domyx rejected this reassurance. His account expressed the imperial position; it did not prove that rescue was impossible or that the Empire's promised future would occur.

Fharan died of his wounds after the questioning. Red Caesar considered using [[Beryzoz's Teeth]] to question his corpse, but decided that they had no further questions worth pursuing and did not cast the spell. Domyx threw the body through [[Hole on Wheels]] portal, where it fell behind the moving train and was left inaccessible to them.

#### Preparing to enter the vault

The party discussed how to open the pressure-sealed door without being swept away. Red Caesar observed that leaving it closed would still obstruct ordinary imperial access, but Ceril doubted that its protections would reliably stop Vizier Jade. With the prisoner warning of her imminent arrival, recovering the hoard became urgent.

Red Caesar explained that his newly discovered [[Obvolvo Caelum]] could make even a vast store of Penumbra portable. They also needed a way to leave with it. A teleportation circle could not carry them directly to The Opal, which had no matching circle. Vokenar instead proposed Word of Recall, having established the ship as a sanctuary.

They considered resting for Red Caesar to recover more magic, then chose to proceed with healing and preparation rather than take a short rest at the door. His vitality had already been restored, and Vokenar's recall provided an escape independent of the park's routes. Their next task was to contain the water and reach Jack Harvey's hoard before the arriving fleet could intervene.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_031/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Opening the flooded vault

Kerben told the others that The Opal was west near Lyngbakr's lagoon. Vokenar tended to their injuries while they prepared the entrance, but this pause did not become a short rest.

Red Caesar first tried Mage Hand and then Knock on the great door. Neither worked: a participant needed to touch the emblem with their own hand. Vokenar shaped stone into a protective barricade, while Ceril shaped the water around them. Domyx volunteered to open the seal.

When Domyx touched the carrot, Jack Harvey's recorded magical voice welcomed him to the vault. The lamps went dark, the door opened, and ocean water burst into the midway. Their preparations protected them from the initial surge, which continued flowing around the entrance. The hazard came from the post-Cataclysm sea pressing against an old structure rather than from a deliberate attack by its absent owner.

Beyond the entrance lay another chamber with two sealed doors. Water poured through ceiling openings until it filled this airlock. Water Walk initially held the companions near the rising surface, while Ceril provided Water Breathing for the whole group, including Tango. Once they could continue, they descended ramps and stairs into a submerged vault crowded with reddish-brown seaweed and stone pillars. The vegetation obscured their sight, encouraging them to stay close.

Vokenar noticed warm pockets within otherwise cold water and suspected another presence. Red Caesar found Penumbra growing across the walls. Its dust and crystals responded to his hand, gathering toward him. He used Obvolvo Caelum to draw the material into a small, dense sphere whose outward size remained almost constant as the hoard accumulated. Even early in the collection, it rivaled their previous finds combined.

Kerben and Domyx discovered [[Jade's Compass]], an elaborate treasure with gold, jade, gemstones, and Vizier Jade's name engraved upon it. Red Caesar found no active enchantment. Years of water damage suggested that it had been in the vault long before the current landing, but the compass alone did not prove that Vizier Jade had personally visited. Remembering Theo Harvey's account of her missing servant gave the party another possibility.

#### The Tyrant's confusion

In a deeper chamber, they found [[The Tyrant]]: an enormous undead beholder-like head with a central eye, a great mouth, and withered eyestalks. Penumbra clung to its body as well as the surrounding chamber. The companions moved through the seaweed to engage it, keeping Tango farther back. Kerben used his shark-tooth blade in the flooded space, and Ceril summoned a powerful crab to help.

The Tyrant called telepathically for Vizier Jade, saying she had cast him out and begging for her aid. No answer was heard. Its eye rays and teeth remained dangerous despite its confused appeals. Domyx survived a disintegration ray, while Kerben was slowed. Red Caesar recognized that the great central eye projected a negative aura in which healing would not work, making the companions' positions especially hazardous.

Red Caesar hastened Domyx. Vokenar manifested a sword associated with [[Crone]], which the Tyrant recognized as a piece of his divine mother. It grieved over what had happened to her and accused its attackers of destroying both the world and its mothers. Vokenar tried to explain that Vizier Jade and Emperor Shen had caused that harm and that the party was trying to stop them. His words briefly diverted the creature's hostility, but did not settle it; its accusations and attacks remained disconnected by madness.

Domyx rallied his companions with a zealous battle cry, helping them resist the Tyrant's magic, before a ray paralyzed him. A death ray struck Kerben severely enough to stop his heart briefly, but he survived without falling dead or requiring revival. Ceril could not use healing within the negative aura and continued supporting the attack with his crab. Red Caesar considered a magical way to reach the creature's mind as the fight continued into the next chunk.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_031/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### Learning where Starfall was hidden

The Tyrant repeatedly demanded to know how anyone had reached Starfall, which it believed had been securely hidden. Kerben's ensnaring magic and the party's continued pressure exhausted its protections against hostile spells. Kerben recovered from the slowing ray, only to fall under the creature's charm, while Domyx remained paralyzed and Vokenar was driven into the seaweed.

Ceril and the summoned crab brought the Tyrant close to defeat. Red Caesar then successfully cast Charm Monster, ending its hostility instead of delivering a killing blow. Domyx's paralysis faded, and the companions brought Kerben back to his senses. The change gave them a brief chance to question a creature that had been too distraught to speak coherently during the fight.

The Tyrant said that it existed at Vizier Jade's pleasure and expected her to destroy it. It described Starfall as the core of [[Stark]], formed from the world's four elements when the world itself was born, and as a failsafe preceding [[Genesis Mundi]]. It also said that the weapon had created it and that it was an embodiment of its energy. It did not understand how Vizier Jade had reached what it regarded as a guarded secret.

Vokenar persuaded it to reveal the location: beneath the world, at its center, in [[Axis Mundi]]. As it gave that name, an unseen outside force snapped its jaw, tore its body apart, and condensed the remains into a small knot of bone. The party did not witness the source of that force. The destruction followed its warning about Vizier Jade's control, but her responsibility was an inference rather than a visible act in the chamber.

The companions now had a concrete lead to Starfall. They finished gathering the hoard, which contained several times more Penumbra than all their earlier discoveries combined. Jack Harvey's long effort to preserve the broken sky had finally yielded material that Red Caesar could carry out in one hand.

#### Returning to the hidden Opal

Vokenar healed Kerben, then insisted that they recover before returning to a ship that might still be in battle. His Prayer of Healing provided a brief short rest in the flooded vault. Ceril dismissed the crab so that Word of Recall could carry the five companions and Tango together.

Vokenar's recall wrapped the group in feathers and light and returned them to The Opal. It remained invisible to outside observers. Its hull was damaged and still under repair, but [[Raxxy]] reported that no crew member had been lost and that they had reached the lagoon. Vokenar moved through the vessel with Aura of Vitality, healing those still hurt. [[Mobley]] had made a welcome stew from ingredients Kerben had left him, and the surviving crew was in good spirits.

The companions discussed repairing the vessel and investigating Axis Mundi. Red Caesar associated an underground destination with the dwarves and their digging. Ulrich Fjoller noticed the condensed Penumbra and urged them not to delay its delivery to Lyngbakr.

#### Alamar's changed refuge

The settlement at Lyngbakr Lagoon had expanded markedly since their departure. Watchtowers, larger buildings, and busy construction surrounded an anchorage containing human, dwarven, and elven boats. Red Caesar recognized connections to several small settlements, including [[Southport]]. The community resembled the mixed refuge [[The League of New Stark]] might have wanted to build, but it had developed through [[Alamar]]'s own efforts, without becoming an established League outpost. Domyx still saw no settlers from his titan people.

Ceril was greeted by [[Rella Kel'Navvi]], his blink dog, who ran and teleported around him in delight. The reunion brought him back to the companion he had left at the lagoon. The conversation also established that [[Rochella Golf]] had left the ship and was now in Southport; the party did not visit her here.

Alamar asked whether they had completed the sky-repair task. The tiny sphere in Red Caesar's hand initially seemed an inadequate result for their long absence. Red Caesar explained that it held concentrated Penumbra and identified the Broyish Empire, Emperor Shen, and Vizier Jade as the agents behind the sky crisis and the attack on the moon. Vokenar supported the explanation and acknowledged the work Alamar had done for the settlement.

Alamar said that refugees fleeing the waves after Crone's destruction had forced the community to abandon its secrecy and expand. Imperial balloons had followed, prompting him to install large harpoons on the watchtowers. He wanted gunpowder weapons and doubted that the island could survive a naval assault.

His own confinement had not ended. He could move freely about the lagoon, but a trial voyage farther out to sea had caused him to fade as though he were only partly present. He wondered whether he was a projection bound to [[Castle Kaedon]], even though the castle no longer stood. He did not provide a settled explanation for that condition. Once he understood how much Penumbra the party had brought, he welcomed their success and the prospect of healing the sky.

#### The fleet reaches the lagoon

Ceril began calling Lyngbakr through Speak with Animals, but the great turtle did not immediately answer. Tango hurried to Kerben with another report: the Imperial Xihe had reached the shore, troops were landing, and the settlement was in danger. The flagship had sailed past the still-invisible Opal. Its crew had moved the vessel farther out to sea without being discovered.

Kerben sounded the alarm. Red Caesar had to remain with the condensed hoard until its delivery, so he and Ceril went into the water to find Lyngbakr. Kerben, Domyx, and Vokenar moved to the shore to delay the invaders. Alamar initially accompanied them, but the sight of numerous longboats and troops landing along the coast overwhelmed him. He turned back toward the settlement, hoping to protect its inhabitants.

Emperor Shen and Vizier Jade approached the three companions, with high-ranking imperials bringing Obould behind them, bound, gagged, and locked in a great cage. Vizier Jade cast Tongues for the emperor. He demanded the Penumbra, declared that his agents had checked Jack Harvey's empty vault, and threatened to kill Obould and destroy the settlement if the material was withheld.

Vokenar answered that they had no Penumbra to give him and asked about Starfall in Axis Mundi. The revelation surprised the emperor. Vizier Jade considered whether her missing Tyrant had reached the place and disclosed its secret; they had believed the servant only lost. Vokenar continued the conversation to give Ceril and Red Caesar time.

Domyx proposed exchanging places with Obould through Cloud Jaunt, sacrificing his own freedom to reverse the hostage situation. Kerben readied his rifle against Emperor Shen. These plans had begun at the end of the chunk; their results followed in the next.

### Chunk 0004

[[chunks/session_031/chunk_0004|Source transcript]]

#### Rescuing Obould and losing Domyx

Kerben's shot struck Emperor Shen after the emperor signaled Vizier Jade not to intervene. It broke away half his mask and revealed a youthful face almost identical to Domyx's. The appearance contradicted the frail, decrepit figure the companions had previously understood him to be. Blood ran from the wound, but he ordered Vizier Jade to separate and capture them.

Kerben directed his next shot at Obould's manacles, freeing his hands while leaving him inside the cage. Obould tore off his gag and immediately asked whether The Opal survived. Kerben said it did, though barely. The captain warned that there was no reasoning with their enemies and that the danger exceeded what they understood. He urged a retreat to the ship so they could regroup.

Domyx used Cloud Jaunt to exchange places with him. Obould emerged free beside his companions, while Domyx appeared inside the cage. Obould called Kerben to lead him back to The Opal, and the two withdrew. Domyx tried to force the bars apart but could not escape. Emperor Shen mocked the attempt and declared that his grandson would return to the Empire to be disciplined.

#### Vokenar's last stand

Vokenar stayed between the invaders and the settlement. He challenged Emperor Shen's purpose and refused to let him advance on the island's people. The emperor threatened to kill everyone there, then follow the companions from island to island until everything they loved had been taken away. Only afterward, he said, would the remaining Starfall shot be fired into the heavens.

Vokenar raised a Wall of Fire through the supporting troops, separating them from the emperor, Vizier Jade, and the caged Domyx. Emperor Shen ordered Vizier Jade to deal with the remaining opponent and suggested sending Vokenar back to the ruined world of [[Arkadia]]. Her Banishment failed when Vokenar drew on his discipline against hostile magic and forced himself to remain on Stark.

Emperor Shen seized Domyx's cage and departed with him through a column of golden light. Before leaving, he claimed that the companions seeking an ally were pursuing someone he had already killed. The parallel search would soon disprove the implication that Lyngbakr was gone. Domyx's immediate destination was not shown, although the emperor had declared his intention to take him back to the Empire.

Alone with Vizier Jade, Vokenar asked why she wanted to destroy gods when she belonged to the world she threatened. She said that she had lived for a long time and once fought for it much as the companions did. Wars, deaths, and people fighting for competing gods had exhausted her hope. She accused the gods of treating mortals as toys and wanted the cycle ended.

Vokenar rejected that conclusion, insisting that hope remained. Vizier Jade allowed that his victory might prove him right, then chose a killing word rather than another attempt at banishment. She shed a tear as darkness overcame him. Vokenar died, and she departed with his corpse. No rescue or return was established in this scene.

#### Finding Lyngbakr

Meanwhile, Ceril and Red Caesar continued searching underwater, unaware of the shore confrontation's outcome. Calling Lyngbakr had not brought a response. Red Caesar used Locate Creature, and they followed its guidance deeper beneath the great Cataract. After a prolonged search, they found the giant turtle hidden far below the falling water.

Lyngbakr explained that a man had tried to kill him and driven him into hiding. He had escaped, keeping the Penumbra already within him safe. He did not name his attacker in this conversation. He also described the growing power to fly that earlier deliveries had given him, which had destroyed the castle on his back as he rose toward the heavens.

With more Penumbra, he could reach the sky and complete the repair, sealing Stark from the world beyond. Ceril asked whether he could accompany that ascent. Lyngbakr agreed. Red Caesar dismissed the sphere holding the hoard, releasing black, star-filled material into the water. Layers poured outward without seeming to reduce the center until the sea around them resembled the heavens. Lyngbakr drew the material into himself and moved with increasing energy, finally announcing that he held enough to heal the entire sky.

Ceril regarded his mission as complete and intended to continue toward Arkadia. Red Caesar chose to remain on Stark. He planned to return to [[Figma Brickfinger's Union]] and ask for a route down to Axis Mundi: leaving Starfall intact would allow the same destruction to happen again. Lyngbakr heard the sounds of war above and urged him to return to those who might need his help.

#### The whole sky

Lyngbakr carried them toward the surface, but first needed to collect Alamar. The turtle explained that Alamar was bound to the place and would not survive being left behind. At the lagoon, as battle spread and boats encircled the refuge, Red Caesar disembarked and Alamar hurried aboard. Alamar wished him safety, warning that seemingly every force in the world wanted him dead. The exchange did not establish the settlement's final fate.

Ceril and Alamar rode Lyngbakr as he rose through the falling water and into the air. Black fragments streamed from his back into wounds across the sky. Small gaps above the dwarven lands, League territory, and the elves healed first; then the great opening above the lagoon filled piece by piece. The remaining space narrowed to barely enough room for the turtle.

Lyngbakr passed through with his two passengers, and the sky sealed beneath them. The black material turned blue as it completed the heavens. Red Caesar, born after the sky had broken, looked up at a whole blue sky for the first time in his life. The repair was accomplished, even though the shattered moon and Starfall remained separate problems.

The hidden Opal later recovered him. Kerben and Obould were safe aboard, and their reunion brought embraces and the sharing of what had happened. The ship still faced uncertain next steps. Ceril had departed into the heavens with Lyngbakr and Alamar, Domyx was Emperor Shen's prisoner, and Vokenar was dead in Vizier Jade's custody. Restoring the sky had secured one great victory while leaving the surviving companions to confront the weapon beneath it.

### Summary

- Kerben and the crew preserved The Opal through a naval assault, using its upgraded weapons, a Conjure Barrage scroll, the air elemental, and the ghost-ship token to escape west invisibly.
- At The Carrot Cake, Kerben freed Theo Harvey and gave him a balloon token. Theo Harvey escaped, intending to hide among the dwarves.
- The reunited party defeated Fharan's force. Red Caesar removed his lethal contingency; the dying seneschal disclosed Obould's imminent arrival and named Sigil as Starfall's next target.
- The party safely opened the flooded vault. Red Caesar gathered Jack Harvey's immense Penumbra hoard into an Obvolvo Caelum sphere, and Kerben and Domyx found Jade's Compass.
- Red Caesar charmed The Tyrant after the companions exhausted its defenses. It identified Axis Mundi as Starfall's location and described its own connection to the weapon before an unseen force destroyed it.
- Vokenar recalled the party to the damaged, invisible Opal and healed its surviving crew. Alamar's lagoon had grown into a mixed refuge threatened by the arriving Imperial Xihe.
- Kerben broke Obould's restraints; Domyx exchanged places with him, enabling the captain's escape but becoming trapped. Emperor Shen carried Domyx away.
- Vokenar remained to delay the attackers, resisted banishment, and challenged Vizier Jade's despair. She killed him and took his body.
- Red Caesar delivered the hoard to Lyngbakr. The turtle repaired the entire sky, carrying Ceril and Alamar through the final opening before it sealed.
- Red Caesar returned to The Opal with Kerben and Obould, intending to seek a route to Axis Mundi. Starfall remained intact, and the lagoon's eventual fate was not shown.

The recovery of Jack Harvey's hoard joined preparation, discovery, and fulfillment. The park's challenges had granted the party access that the imperial landing force lacked, while Red Caesar's newly developed Obvolvo Caelum made its enormous store transportable. The flooded vault also yielded the first concrete location for Starfall. The Tyrant's confused mourning and brief cooperation revealed a connection between the world's original elements, its destructive failsafe, and a servant the Empire could no longer reliably use.

Kerben's decisions kept the ship and its people available when the expedition most needed them. He chose concealment and retreat against the fleet, rescued Theo Harvey without confronting the entire landing party alone, and helped recover Obould without surrendering the Penumbra. Domyx's exchange made that last rescue possible at the cost of his own freedom. Vokenar's stand bought time for the others, but left him isolated before Vizier Jade's killing magic. Their losses were established outcomes, while the emperor's promised destination and the later disposition of Vokenar's body remained unknown.

The restored sky fulfilled Ceril's immediate purpose and allowed him to leave Stark with Lyngbakr and Alamar. It did not undo the moon's destruction, free Aeris from Starfall, or remove the weapon capable of attacking the heavens again. Red Caesar's refusal to depart turned the campaign toward that remaining danger. By the close, a whole sky stood above Stark, but the party was divided, a refuge was under assault, and the surviving companions aboard The Opal had to decide how to reach Axis Mundi and recover those taken from them.
