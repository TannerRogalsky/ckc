# Session 038

### Chunk 0000

- [[Emperor Shen]] has been resurrected as the last [[Vanir]] by [[Entropie]]'s Wish. His presence drains life across the [[Gray Wastes]], and four rib-shaped stone pillars sustain his power: the Stele of Apotropaism, Stele of Menace, Stele of Solemnity, and Stele of Serenity.
- [[Vizier Jade]] explains how to destroy the stelae permanently. [[Red Caesar]] and [[Vokenar]] begin stripping away their protections.
- Emperor Shen recognizes [[Domyx]] as his grandson and imprisons [[Kerben]] in a magical maze.
- [[Ceril]] invokes [[Ceril's Star]], drawing everyone into an astral refuge and freeing Kerben. [[Aeris]] helps the party renew its magic; from outside the three planes, they see the moon healing as [[Crone]] recovers.

### Chunk 0001

- The party returns to the battlefield. Emperor Shen compels [[Domyx]] to strike down [[Red Caesar]], then releases Domyx and claims that loyalty to his friends makes him weak. [[Vokenar]] restores Red Caesar.
- Emperor Shen identifies the stelae as his bones and the plane as his body. As his supports fall, the lower sky breaks apart.
- [[Ceril]] dissolves the final stela, removing the remaining protections. Red Caesar casts Disintegrate at Emperor Shen as great pieces of the sky fall around them.

### Chunk 0002

- [[Red Caesar]]'s spell destroys [[Emperor Shen]] completely. The wasting influence ends and fertile soil returns, but the sky continues collapsing. [[Stark]]'s oceans pour into the [[Gray Wastes]].
- [[Vokenar]] leads the retreat to [[The Opal]]. [[Kerben]] stores the ship with the party, [[Vizier Jade]], and the crew aboard. Vokenar stays behind and banishes Kerben, carrying everyone else, back to [[Cutlass Cray]].
- Over the following weeks, the oceans recede and drowned lands reappear. Cutlass Cray settles on exposed mountaintops. Attempts to contact Vokenar fail.
- [[Obould]] and [[Lady Jacinthe]] marry aboard [[The White Drake]] near the former [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]]. The surviving companions attend with League guests and representatives from [[Broy]]. The couple later have half-orc children.
- [[Theotropa]] invites Ceril to help restore emerging lands. He contributes dense, fertile jungle before retiring to Ceril's Star, where he can live outside the ordinary passage of time.
- Red Caesar and Vizier Jade establish Broy's new governing charter, with succession safeguards and limits on personal rule. Red Caesar erases the [[Demi-Spell]] and uses its blank pages for the charter. Vizier Jade submits to imprisonment for her crimes under the new laws.
- [[Theo Harvey]] gathers surviving [[40 Carats]] performers, and Kerben rebuilds the troupe. Obould confirms him as The Opal's permanent captain and gives him a [[Spelljammer]]. Kerben visits [[Jack Harvey]]'s newly accessible grave near [[Esperanto]], then eventually sails into the stars in later life.
- Domyx returns to [[Thalasia]], where the houses share authority and other peoples live among the titans. [[Charlotta Fjoller]] directs him to [[Domyx IV]], who apologizes. Father and son reconcile while carrying a heavy log together.
- Vokenar awakens in [[Arkadia]] in his youthful body, retaining his experience. [[Aeris]], [[Sigil]], and Crone welcome him; [[Alamar]], [[Illidrielle Gandara]], and [[Damien Ouranous]] greet him. He accepts Crone's offer of long training for a future campaign in the deeper hells.
- The epilogue confirms [[Southport]]'s recovery, [[Lyngbakr]]'s return to Arkadia, and [[Witness]]'s work mapping that plane. [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]] rules the elves as king, and [[The Academy]] is repurposed.
- The [[Rakshasa]] remains in a crystal worn by [[Feronia Caeradel]]. [[Keys Caeradel]] seeks a new purpose away from elven society; his later destination and work remain uncertain.
- [[Beryzoz Helmscar]] settles his ship-theft grievance with Kerben through a pass to the revived troupe. [[Xander MacLenth]]'s passage to the afterlife is confirmed. [[Kilosaurus]] is believed to have died, without a witnessed death.
- Lyngbakr Lagoon is revealed as a volcanic caldera. [[Octavia Crayborne]] is identified as Cutlass Cray's mayor and a descendant of its builders.

### Chunk 0003

### Summary

The final confrontation tests the bonds that brought the party to [[Axis Mundi]]. Resurrected as the last Vanir, Emperor Shen tries to isolate Kerben and turn Domyx against his companions. Ceril's Star preserves the group, and Vokenar restores Red Caesar after Domyx's compelled attack. The companions destroy the stelae sustaining Emperor Shen, allowing Red Caesar to end his transformed body. The victory ends the divine threat and the plane's wasting influence, but it cannot stop the lower sky from collapsing.

Vokenar secures the others' escape by sending Kerben home with the entire ship and its passengers in his possession. Alone below, Vokenar realizes the flooding can relieve the world above. Stark's retreating oceans expose the old lands, making recovery possible without rewriting reality. His companions cannot reach him for weeks, but he ultimately awakens young again in Arkadia, welcomed by the restored goddesses.

The companions choose different futures. Red Caesar erases the Demi-Spell and uses its paper to build a lawful future for Broy with Vizier Jade, who accepts accountability for her past. Ceril helps life return before withdrawing to his timeless star. Kerben reunites the 40 Carats company before eventually exploring other worlds. Domyx returns to a changed homeland and begins a more equal relationship with his father. Vokenar takes up training with Crone for future dangers. Their shared mission is complete, while the world and the people they saved continue to grow.
