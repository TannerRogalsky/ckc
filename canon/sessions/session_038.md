# Session 038

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_038/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### The last Vanir's supports

[[Emperor Shen]], resurrected by [[Entropie]]'s wish as the last [[Vanir]], rose over the clearing at [[Axis Mundi]]. His presence changed the [[Gray Wastes]]. Color faded from the forest [[Ceril]] had grown, and the plane's ancient hostility to living things reasserted itself. [[Red Caesar]] recognized that remaining there would drain the companions' vitality and their capacity to recover. Where the emperor's feet dragged across the ground, fertile earth became barren gray dust.

[[Vizier Jade]] explained the four rib-shaped pillars that had emerged with him. They were stone, despite their resemblance to bones, and their glowing inscriptions sustained his powers. The Stele of Apotropaism protected him against magic, the Stele of Solemnity strengthened his resistance to weapons, and the Stele of Menace and Stele of Serenity supplied further protections and power. Breaking them through ordinary attacks would leave pieces that could reform. Magic capable of altering or wholly destroying the stone could remove a support permanently.

[[Vokenar]] began breaking one of the supports while his celestial light continued healing the party. Emperor Shen dispelled that sustaining presence despite Red Caesar's attempt to prevent it. The companions then faced the god's attacks without the moving refuge of healing that had carried them through the preceding battle.

[[Kerben]] fired on the emperor, and [[Domyx]] closed to stagger him and draw his attention. Emperor Shen recognized his grandson even in his transformed state. His golden, obscured face produced beams of light, while shocks through the ground swept across the clearing. Ceril's protective feast helped the party withstand the thunder.

Red Caesar disintegrated the Stele of Menace, removing it entirely. Vokenar flew to the Stele of Solemnity and reshaped its stone, ending the protection it gave against their weapons. Ceril summoned a flying beast to attack the remaining magical protection and took a hovering starry form to avoid the shocks through the ground.

The emperor concentrated much of his violence on Red Caesar and Ceril. He told Red Caesar that Vizier Jade had wanted him to join their new world. Domyx tried to stop his grandfather from striking his companions, but even when he pushed the colossal figure back, the god's reach remained dangerous. His attempt to grapple the emperor failed, and Emperor Shen drove him into the dust while dismissing him as the child he had left decades earlier.

The Potion of Eels Kerben had given Red Caesar was now helping him withstand the assault. Ceril and Vokenar repeatedly healed him, and Red Caesar enlarged Domyx to strengthen the challenge to his grandfather. The companionship Emperor Shen mocked kept the group in the fight.

#### Refuge beyond the three planes

Emperor Shen cast Kerben into a magical maze. The dwarf found himself alone in a pocket dimension of concentric stone passages. His familiarity with stone and skill in investigation did not immediately reveal an exit, and his attempt to follow the passages left him trapped. The party had lost a crucial fighter while the others were being worn down.

Ceril invoked [[Ceril's Star]]. Its refuge gathered the companions from their different locations, including Kerben from the maze. With its prisoner removed, the magical labyrinth ceased to exist.

They stood in an astral space outside [[Arkadia]], [[Stark]], and the Gray Wastes. From there they could see the vast disk of their world, the distant sun, and the broken moon beginning to draw together as [[Crone]] healed. An hour could pass for them without advancing the confrontation below. The wasting power of the Gray Wastes could no longer drain them while they rested.

[[Aeris]] came to Ceril and explained another benefit of the refuge. The companions could combine their lesser reserves of magic to renew a stronger working. That assistance let them recover the power needed for the struggle and, ultimately, for the escape that followed.

The party healed and prepared to return. Kerben summoned a crab-like bestial spirit to accompany him, while Vokenar set aside his bond to Crone's warhammer and returned to the Akasha blade. The rest also ended several temporary enhancements they had carried into the confrontation. Their return would require fresh effort, but the star had restored the group and freed Kerben without allowing Emperor Shen time to advance his attack.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_038/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### The attack on their loyalty

The companions returned to their places in the clearing. For Emperor Shen, almost no time had passed; he had felt their presence disappear for an instant and now found them renewed. Kerben rushed back into the fight with his blade and whip, drawing black blood from the divine body. There was something within the god's form that could still be killed.

Domyx rallied his friends with a battle cry and kept his grandfather's attention on him. Vokenar moved between the remaining supports while sustaining the others through healing. His connection through the [[Ioun of the Gilded Savior]] let him restore Red Caesar even from far across the clearing. Ceril attacked with moonlight and continued trying to break the Stele of Apotropaism.

The emperor's injuries coincided with more debris falling from the sky above. Each blow seemed to damage the plane as well as the figure within it. The party's route to victory was beginning to destabilize the world beneath Stark.

Emperor Shen then dominated Domyx. He first ordered his grandson to kill Kerben, but Kerben exchanged places with [[Tango]] and moved out of reach. The emperor changed the command: Domyx was to kill Red Caesar.

Domyx struck his friend while tears ran down his face. The forced assault overwhelmed Red Caesar and left him unconscious. Emperor Shen released his control and presented the violence as proof of his grandson's power. He claimed that loyalty to friends had suppressed Domyx and that joining him would restore his strength. He then beat Domyx himself, demanding respect.

Vokenar brought Red Caesar back through their healing bond. Red Caesar could see that Domyx was no longer under his grandfather's control; he struck him with a reprimand before returning his attention to the enemy. The compelled attack had hurt the party, but it did not make Domyx choose Emperor Shen over his friends.

The emperor also tried to banish Vokenar to Arkadia, telling him he did not belong in this plane. Vokenar caught a glimpse of home but resisted the expulsion and remained with the others.

#### Removing the last protection

The battle continued amid worsening falls from the lower sky. Kerben used Tango's exchanges of position to recover and return to the attack. When he worried about leaving Vizier Jade exposed, she explained that Emperor Shen considered her powerless and was concentrating on winning rather than taking further revenge on her.

The emperor offered Kerben a place in his promised world, where dwarves could have held power instead of humans. Kerben continued fighting. Domyx's blows and the dwarf's weapons wore down the divine form while Vokenar worked to remove the remaining stone supports, using the Akasha blade on shattered remnants.

Ceril's shield from Aeris became an important defense against the repeated beams. One reflected ray punched another hole in the sky above the Gray Wastes. Even a successful defense could contribute to the damage spreading through the plane.

Emperor Shen warned Ceril away from the last stele, calling the supports his bones and the world his body. Ceril finally dissolved the Stele of Apotropaism with primal acid. Unlike the earlier unsuccessful attacks, the melted stone could not reform. The emperor's remaining protections were gone.

The destruction also accelerated the sky's collapse. Enormous pieces fell around the battlefield, threatening to crush anyone caught beneath them. With the final support removed, Red Caesar cast Disintegrate at Emperor Shen. The beam reached an opponent who could no longer rely on the stele's resistance to magic.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_038/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Victory and Vokenar's escape plan

Red Caesar's spell punched through Emperor Shen's black-blooded form and consumed it outward from the center. His lower body disappeared, then his shoulders, until only his head remained. He gave Domyx a final farewell and acknowledged the world passing to his grandson's generation. The disintegration then took the last of him.

Nothing remained of the last Vanir. His wasting influence ended immediately, and the damaged ground regained color and fertility. Small shoots began appearing even in the clearing blasted by Starfall's destruction. Vizier Jade rejoined the companions.

The sky continued falling. Ceril and Vokenar saw the holes spreading until the ocean above began pouring through them. The immense waterfalls recalled [[The Cataclysm]], when water from Arkadia had flooded Stark. Now another boundary had broken, and Stark's water was rushing into the Gray Wastes.

Vizier Jade could not provide planar escape. Red Caesar considered the recovered Arcane Gate scroll as a way to cross an obstacle during the retreat, but Vokenar had a plan that required reaching [[The Opal]]. The party ran back through the forest with Vizier Jade. The ship was several miles away, and the gathering water had not yet reached it. Red Caesar understood that a backed-up wave might sweep across the land once the flood arrived.

At the ship, Vokenar directed the others and Vizier Jade aboard with the crew. He kept Kerben outside with him. Kerben restored The Opal to its small magical container, putting the entire vessel and its passengers in his possession.

Vokenar placed a hand against Kerben's chest and banished him back to his native plane. The dwarf carried the stored ship, every person aboard it, and their possessions to safety. The magic renewed in Ceril's refuge had made that final rescue possible.

Vokenar remained alone in the living forest as the waters rose. Looking at the space beneath the second sky, he realized it could contain all the water added to Stark during the Cataclysm. The flood below could let the oceans above retreat and expose the old lands. Their journey had created a way for the world to recover without restarting its history. With his companions gone, Vokenar sat and waited.

#### The changed world and the wedding

Several weeks passed. Attempts to contact Vokenar through the companions' magic failed. The record did not establish what happened to him during that interval.

Kerben and the stored ship had arrived at [[Cutlass Cray]]. As the oceans receded, islands began revealing themselves as mountaintops, and people moved toward newly exposed land. Parts of the floating city settled onto mountains beneath it. Its residents rearranged their homes and businesses to make the changing terrain habitable.

[[The White Drake]] moved with the changing waters and anchored near the former [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]], close to the site of [[Castle Kaedon]] and the old Cataclysm. The companions were invited to [[Obould]] and [[Lady Jacinthe]]'s wedding. Ceril attended despite his dislike of parties, and Domyx, Kerben, and Red Caesar joined the celebration. Guests included allies of [[The League of New Stark]] and visitors from [[Broy]].

After the ceremony, the newlyweds greeted those who had come. Vizier Jade attended with the Broyish contingent and looked after its people. Her return had left them with questions about Emperor Shen's abandonment and uncertainty about what government would follow him. The invitation also offered a chance to improve relations with the League.

#### Ceril's restoration and retirement

[[Theotropa]] approached Ceril at the wedding. She praised the trust she had placed in him and asked about Vokenar and the goddesses. Ceril said their hard mission seemed complete, but Vokenar had not returned with them. Theotropa answered that beings from Arkadia ultimately return there; her reassurance did not explain how Vokenar's own return would occur.

She offered Ceril a place in [[The Garden]] and asked for help restoring the emerging lands. Their fertile ocean deposits could support farms, and human, elven, orcish, and dwarven communities would need assistance as they expanded. Ceril was wary of that expansion and said that what he needed most was time.

He chose to spend some time replanting before retiring. His contribution favored dense, productive jungle that could sustain life while making it harder to cover the land with permanent structures. It expressed his concern for the living world without making him the planner of its new settlements.

Ceril then withdrew to his star as a hermit. Beyond Crone's control of time, the refuge allowed him to live indefinitely and potentially revisit the world in later centuries. When [[Genesis Mundi]] eventually came, he could watch the renewal from outside it or enter the new world and begin again. He had not settled that distant choice. His ending established retirement and a lasting refuge rather than a final departure from every possible future.

#### Red Caesar and Broy's charter

Red Caesar and Vizier Jade returned to their plans for Broy. He proposed that she serve initially as its public figurehead while he helped design and manage its new order. He had studied the failures and achievements of societies before and after the Cataclysm and wanted institutions that could survive for centuries.

One central safeguard concerned succession. There should always be someone able to take responsibility when a leader failed or departed, without giving the successor unlimited dictatorial power. Red Caesar recalled [[Alamar]]'s withdrawal from responsibility as a warning. He wanted rule guided by a durable legal framework rather than the will of one powerful person. Although a republic was suggested as a description, he did not adopt that label for his proposal.

Vizier Jade trusted his judgment and agreed to help restore Broy. She also said that her own long life included crimes for which she would have to pay. Her immediate leadership was a means of rebuilding, with responsibility eventually passing onward.

Red Caesar still possessed the [[Demi-Spell]]. Its magic offered the possibility of undoing the campaign's losses by restarting reality, but it would also erase what the companions had achieved. He chose to erase the spell itself and preserve the paper. On the cleared pages, he wrote the charter of the new Broy.

The charter was established and displayed as the framework of the new order. Vizier Jade then submitted to its judgment. Her past conduct placed her in violation of the laws they had written, and she accepted years of imprisonment in a Broyish jail as repayment. Their shared hope became an actual political settlement, and her accountability became part of it. The conversation proposed roles for Red Caesar without separately confirming a formal appointment to every title discussed.

#### Kerben, 40 Carats, and the stars

At the wedding, Obould brought [[Theo Harvey]] to Kerben. Theo Harvey thanked him for the balloon that had saved him from the imperial guards at [[The Carrot Cake]]. Since escaping, he had met Obould and gathered surviving members of [[40 Carats]] from Cutlass Cray and The Garden.

He offered Kerben the chance to put the company back together. Kerben accepted and chose that work as his immediate future. Rebuilding the troupe would let Theo Harvey see it in its former spirit and bring the surviving performers together.

Obould thanked Kerben for caring for The Opal and confirmed that he was its permanent captain. He also gave him a [[Spelljammer]], a geode-like device with iridescent crystal within a rocky exterior. Southern orcish guilds had originally supplied it to Obould, who had kept it until the ship could withstand its power. Mounted aboard The Opal, it would allow Kerben to leave their plane and explore the astral and other worlds.

As the oceans drew back, places Kerben had known became accessible again. He found [[Jack Harvey]]'s grave near [[Esperanto]], where it had remained beneath the sea, and paid his respects. He successfully reunited 40 Carats and spent the next part of his life leading it.

In later years, old and gray, Kerben returned to The Opal and sailed into the stars. He went in search of unfamiliar worlds, animals, and landscapes. The record established that departure without fixing his ultimate destination or fate. Vokenar's consecrations remained enduring enchantments aboard the ship, though the Spelljammer would temporarily suspend them during astral travel.

#### Domyx and his father

Domyx returned to [[Thalasia]] to understand what had changed after his grandfather's death. He wanted to learn how titan society might move beyond its hidden history and restrictive traditions. His rejection of his lineage did not prevent him from caring about the people who lived there.

The mountains were full of new industry and repair. Younger titans carried timber uphill, watermills turned, and human, halfling, gnomish, and dwarven communities occupied the foothills. Some members of those peoples walked openly among the titans. The isolation Domyx had known was giving way to cooperation with newcomers.

[[Charlotta Fjoller]] greeted him with a hug near [[Acathian Manor]]. Domyx remained uncertain about rejoining his old house, but welcomed the changes she described. No single house ruled any longer; the houses shared authority. She told him that the people could manage their affairs without needing him as a new leader, but someone at home did need him.

[[Domyx IV]] received his son with an apology for decades of mistreatment and foolishness. His father acknowledged the corruption of their lineage and Emperor Shen's influence on his life. When his own father died, he had felt a great weight lift, then anger and guilt about his relief. Domyx also told him of Farron Acathian II's death, which brought further grief.

Domyx IV said the people were learning to decide for themselves rather than obey tradition. He would have to change too. He asked Domyx to tell him everything that had brought him home, then invited him to carry the other end of a great log.

Father and son shouldered the burden together. Neither could manage it alone, and either abandoning his part would bring the other down. They descended toward the changed titan world while Domyx began his story. Their reconciliation was grounded in apology and mutual responsibility; it did not require Domyx to inherit Emperor Shen's proposed dynasty.

#### Vokenar's homecoming

Vokenar eventually awakened in Arkadia. He did not know whether weeks, years, or decades had passed. His body was youthful again, restored to the form in which he had lived there before being sent to Stark. The experience and knowledge acquired during the campaign remained with him. The precise manner of his passage from the flooding Gray Wastes was not described.

[[Sigil]], Aeris, and Crone welcomed him home. Arkadia's oceans were still lower than he remembered, but clouds and rain were replenishing them. Crone had recovered enough to guide their restoration.

Alamar greeted Vokenar with [[Tuna]] nearby. He welcomed the peace of Arkadia after a life of fighting, confusion, and misplaced trust in Stark. His final trust in the party had given him some wisdom before he left the old world. He expected to remain in Arkadia until the natural renewal, understanding that the Demi-Spell would not be cast.

[[Illidrielle Gandara]] hurried over and asked whether Vokenar's companions had survived. He could only tell her that he had sent them away with the last of his magic and believed they were safe. She accepted that the spell she had worked on would remain unused. [[Damien Ouranous]] also greeted him, returning to a home he had never truly known. He hoped to study at [[Academia Lux]] and join the warriors Arkadia would need, and Vokenar offered to show him around.

Aeris told Vokenar what had become of his friends. Red Caesar was helping reshape Broy's future, Ceril had a home in the stars after contributing to the returning life below, Kerben would eventually sail beyond the sisters' knowledge and power, and Domyx had returned to a cooperative homeland. Aeris was helping Ceril's restoration from afar and hoped that the world around him would express her gratitude.

Vokenar had spent his mission longing to return, yet home now felt unfamiliar. He could not simply resume the life he had left as though the campaign had never happened. Crone recognized the continuing purpose in him and offered a century of training together for an assault on the deeper hells beneath the Gray Wastes, where a pit fiend was gathering an army.

The other sisters urged that he be given time to recover as well. Vokenar accepted Crone's challenge. Even in his younger body, he caught her sword with the ease of his retained experience and charged into their first exchange. His service would continue, but the proposed campaign in the hells remained a future commitment.

#### Other established endings

The closing account settled several consequences beyond the companions' own epilogues. [[Southport]] fully recovered from its magical illness following the party's work with [[Janeera]] and the local community. Obould and Lady Jacinthe later had half-orc children.

[[Beryzoz Helmscar]] used divination to discover the repeated thefts of his ships, including Kerben's part in them. He confronted the dwarf, and they settled the grievance through a season pass to the revived 40 Carats.

When the waters withdrew from Lyngbakr Lagoon, they revealed the rim of a volcanic caldera. Emperor Shen's attack had killed residents, but many had escaped. The place no longer resembled its former island lagoon. [[Lyngbakr]] had returned to Arkadia and was swimming in its recovering ocean.

[[Xander MacLenth]]'s passage to the afterlife was confirmed. [[Kilosaurus]] was thought likely to have died, but no witnessed or certain death was established.

The elves adopted a monarchy under [[Rizolvir Kiirnodel]], a ruler who listened to his subjects. [[The Academy]] was retired from its original purpose and repurposed. Its long effort to produce the Demi-Spell had proved unnecessary once the world's restoration could proceed another way.

[[Witness]] reached Arkadia after charting Stark and crossed paths with Ceril during his ascent. He began charting that plane as well. Travel beyond their cosmology was a possibility rather than an established subsequent journey.

[[Octavia Crayborne]] was identified as Cutlass Cray's mayor and a descendant of its builders. She expressed herself through music.

The [[Rakshasa]] remained contained in a crystal now worn by [[Feronia Caeradel]]. [[Keys Caeradel]] sought a new purpose away from elven society. Suggestions that he might help Southport, explore the emerging lands, or begin another spell remained possibilities; none supplied a confirmed later destination or achievement.

### Summary

- Vizier Jade explained the stelae sustaining Emperor Shen's final Vanir form and how to destroy them permanently.
- Ceril invoked his star, freeing Kerben from a magical maze and sheltering the party outside the three planes. Aeris helped renew their magic while the battlefield remained suspended.
- Emperor Shen forced Domyx to strike down Red Caesar. Vokenar restored him, and the companions continued fighting together.
- The party removed the stelae, and Red Caesar disintegrated the last Vanir. His wasting power ended, but the lower sky continued collapsing.
- Vokenar sent Kerben to Cutlass Cray with The Opal, its crew, Vizier Jade, and the other companions stored aboard. He remained below as the flood gathered.
- Stark's oceans began retreating, exposing old lands and settling Cutlass Cray onto mountains. Obould and Lady Jacinthe married aboard The White Drake.
- Red Caesar erased the Demi-Spell and used its paper for Broy's governing charter. Vizier Jade helped establish the settlement and accepted imprisonment for her crimes.
- Ceril helped restore life to the revealed lands before retiring to his star.
- Kerben rebuilt 40 Carats, visited Jack Harvey's grave, and eventually left for the stars with The Opal and Obould's Spelljammer.
- Domyx returned to a more open titan society and reconciled with Domyx IV.
- Vokenar awakened young again in Arkadia, retained his experience, and accepted Crone's training for a future struggle in the deeper hells.

Emperor Shen's final effort to break the party relied on the same view of loyalty that Domyx had rejected throughout the campaign. The emperor treated other people as sources of power or obstacles to it. He isolated Kerben and made Domyx injure Red Caesar, but he could not turn those imposed actions into a lasting division. Ceril's refuge, Vokenar's healing, Domyx's sustained opposition, Kerben's attacks, and Red Caesar's final spell together made victory possible.

The destruction of the last Vanir did not restore the lower sky. Its collapse instead opened space for the water that had submerged Stark since the Cataclysm. Vokenar secured his companions' escape and remained below, recognizing that the flood could return land to the world above. Their inability to reach him made his absence real during the first weeks of recovery. His later homecoming was established, while the manner and duration of his return remained uncertain.

The epilogues favored rebuilding over rewriting. The Demi-Spell was erased rather than cast, preserving the world's losses and achievements while converting its paper into a framework for Broy's future. The companions chose distinct lives: political responsibility, restoration and retirement, performance and exploration, family reconciliation, and continued divine service. Their mission was complete without resolving every later possibility or requiring each society to take the same political form.

The goddesses were well in Arkadia, and the moon and oceans were recovering. The record did not separately describe the fate of Aeris's imprisoned mortal body. Ceril's choice about a later renewal, Keys Caeradel's next purpose, Kerben's ultimate destination, and Vokenar's proposed future campaign remained open within the completed story.
