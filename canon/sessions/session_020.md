# Session 020

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_020/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### The invisible master of the castle

The battle against the [[Bearded Devil]]s summoned at the end of [[session_019]] continued in [[Castle Kaedon]]'s pentagonal chamber. [[Red Caesar]] recognized two dangers beyond their weapons: the devils' poisonous beards could temporarily prevent healing, while their glaives could leave infernal wounds that continued bleeding. [[Vokenar]] suffered one of these wounds in his shoulder. [[Domyx]] resisted the lingering wounds but was affected by the beard poison. Red Caesar accelerated Domyx with Haste, while [[Ceril]] used his luminous star form and Vokenar brought divine radiance against the fiends. [[Kerben]] and [[Zulu]] joined the attack.

The devils treated the fighting as a game and laughed when one of their companions fell. Their destruction left ash rather than recoverable bodies or weapons. One survivor spoke in Hellish to an unseen ally, who strengthened it with magic. Red Caesar could hear the intervention but could not counter a caster he could not see.

Vokenar's See Invisibility revealed the observer: a large, boar-bodied [[Nalfeshne]] with enormous tusks and bluish fire around its form. Vokenar addressed it in Heavenly, and the demon answered in the same royal tongue. It called him an old friend, but the exchange established no actual history between them. The Nalfeshne claimed the castle as its new possession and said it belonged in Hell, lamenting that it had been hoisted into the party's world. This was the occupier's assertion, rather than a verified account of the castle's origin.

The Nalfeshne promised more surprises and expected the party to contend with its servants before facing it. Ceril also understood Heavenly, which allowed him and Vokenar to communicate apart from their companions. Although Ceril considered exposing the invisible demon with his magic, he concentrated on the visible enemy instead. Domyx destroyed the last bearded devil. Vokenar advised against forcing an immediate confrontation with their much more formidable master, and the Nalfeshne teleported away.

With the chamber clear, Red Caesar explained that ordinary magical healing could close Vokenar's infernal wound. Vokenar used Healing Hands to do so. The party also discussed why a demon was commanding devils despite the rivalry between their kinds: the Blood War did not prevent opportunistic alliances away from their home planes. The cooperation they had witnessed did not imply that all fiends shared one allegiance.

#### Choosing the northern passage

The party examined the chamber's roses and empty art frames, then considered its several exits. Kerben's Stone Cunning detected a repeated pattern of movement to the north, like a patrol, although that movement eventually stopped. Its disappearance did not establish that the next room was safe; stationary creatures and newly summoned enemies could evade this kind of warning.

A small room to the southeast offered a possible refuge. It was an old storage closet with liquor racks, a ladder to a second level, and solid walls. The group could barely fit inside, and there was no other exit, but they considered securing its entrance with magic if they needed somewhere to hide or recover. They inspected it without taking refuge there.

The party chose the northern route along the red carpet. A smaller wicket door within a larger doorway stood open, allowing them to proceed. Red Caesar now hastened Vokenar, whose remaining See Invisibility exposed the creatures waiting beyond: [[Wraith]]s mounted on [[Nightmare]]s. Their early discovery prevented the hidden patrol from simply taking the party by surprise.

The nightmares were intelligent, fiery steeds capable of crossing into the ethereal realm and passing through barriers. Their riders also benefited from their protection against fire. The party understood such creatures as mercenaries that could serve different masters; their association with the Nalfeshne was plausible, though not independently established by the encounter.

Domyx used the [[Iklwa Isondo]] to pull a wraith away from its mount, while Ceril's Moonbeam and Vokenar's holy light pressured the undead. Kerben fought alongside Zulu, whose magical spurs allowed the companion to harm the supernatural steeds. The greater danger fell on Kerben: a mounted wraith reached him and repeatedly drained his vitality. Healing kept him alive, but the attacks left him with substantially diminished strength and capacity to recover.

The party eventually brought down both steeds and their riders. Domyx destroyed one wraith, and Ceril finished the last with magically transformed claws. The visible threats were gone, but their victory did not reverse Kerben's life drain. Ordinary healing could restore him only within his reduced condition.

Ceril had already used the restoration magic available to him to remove Kerben's curse that morning, and Vokenar had not prepared Greater Restoration. Lesser Restoration would not address the wraiths' injury. Vokenar therefore healed Kerben as far as he could, without claiming to cure the underlying loss. The party continued without taking a short rest.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_020/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Barricades and the falling apothecary

Still weakened, Kerben summoned a bestial spirit in the form of a crab to support Zulu and help keep enemies away from him. Red Caesar searched the cleared room and recovered a piece of mithril among its rusted metal bars.

The northern door presented a different obstacle: an arcane lock secured it, and a physical barricade on the far side held it shut as well. Kerben's magical keys might deal with the enchantment, but that alone would not remove the obstruction. The group left the passage unopened and returned to the pentagonal chamber to explore the right-hand route.

Vokenar told the others what he had seen and identified their invisible adversary as a Nalfeshne. Red Caesar explained the creature's particularly dangerous qualities, including its ability to perceive hidden opponents, its magic, and its capacity to overwhelm enemies with supernatural fear. Concealment alone would not be a reliable answer to it.

Along the right-hand route, the party reached a damaged room with cracked masonry and a collapsed wall. Water and refracted light entered through the broken structure, and roses still grew among the ruins. Red Caesar judged the damage to be recent, despite the protective magic that should have helped preserve the castle. He suspected the fiendish occupation was interfering with those abjurations, but the cause remained an inference.

Domyx disturbed the unstable flagstones, bringing more of the ceiling down upon him. The falling debris came from the apothecary the party had visited above. A [[Potion of Soothing Gaze]] dropped into Domyx's lap amid the collapse. Its magic could heal the drinker and extend healing to others the drinker looked at. Red Caesar claimed the potion for his inventory; the party did not consume it during this scene.

#### The dungeon and another ambush

Another doorway led into the castle's prison. An anteroom contained old books, gaming materials, and a rusted weapons rack. Beyond it were iron-barred cells divided by deteriorating partitions, with skeletons inside. These were people who had been left behind when the castle flooded. Red Caesar removed a full set of platinum teeth from one skull, recovering valuable metal from the dead.

Before the group could investigate the prisoners' histories, Ceril heard the sound of chalk tracing summoning marks. A [[Chain Devil]] and a bearded devil appeared among the cells and partitions. The unexpected summons forced the party to fight within the dungeon rather than proceed directly to questioning its dead.

The chain devil's supernatural gaze briefly frightened Domyx. He moved out of its sight and dealt with the bearded devil instead, while Red Caesar used Vampiric Touch against the chain devil. Kerben's crab spirit and Zulu helped contain the enemies, and Ceril and Vokenar supported the attack with luminous and divine magic.

Their invisible adversary was nearby again. Vokenar could make out the Nalfeshne in an adjoining room and saw that it was bolstering the chain devil. He chose to concentrate on the immediate fight instead of provoking the stronger demon. Domyx overcame the chain devil's next attempt to frighten him, and Ceril's divination helped him land the blow that ended it. Its chains collapsed as the creature fell.

The Nalfeshne immediately answered with a Fireball that engulfed the party and their companions. Red Caesar again lacked a visible caster to counter. The blast destroyed Kerben's summoned crab, but Zulu survived. Vokenar warned the demon that they were getting closer to confronting it directly. The Nalfeshne mocked the damage it had inflicted, then teleported away in invisible blue fire.

Red Caesar considered drawing healing from Zulu through Vampiric Touch and spoke of having harmed the companion too severely. The ensuing discussion stressed that feeding on Zulu would provoke the animal and require an explanation to Kerben. The record then proceeded to a short rest with Zulu alive and later fighting normally; it did not establish a lasting death or a resurrection in this scene. The party recovered in the cleared prison before resuming its investigation.

#### The drowned republic's prisoners

A room adjoining the cells appeared to have served the prison's guards or warden. Its scorched tapestry had little remaining value, but a bulletin board still preserved some prisoner names in the language of [[Westerness]]. Domyx recognized [[Clockwork]], identified as a member of the [[Dancing Blades]]. The name had also appeared among the heraldic associations in the senate room explored in the preceding session. Finding it on a prison list raised a question about how someone associated with power had also been held in the dungeon. The list did not establish that any particular skeleton belonged to Clockwork.

Ceril examined and took down a surviving map of Westerness and its surrounding lands. It recorded the forests, farmland, and topography that had since become ocean floor. For the party's navigation, it offered a view of the old landscape beneath their present sea routes and could join the maps kept aboard [[The Opal]]. It also made the scale of the drowned world's ecological transformation visible.

Kerben found a further hoard of Kaedonite coins beneath fabric on a table. Some bore bite marks, and gaps in the pile suggested that a creature had eaten part of the stash. The party connected this to the [[Zorn]] they had encountered earlier, which had surrendered swallowed coins to Domyx. They recovered the remaining money, although the identification of the creature responsible remained a reasonable inference.

Vokenar offered a prayer for the people who had died in the cells, asking that the gods give them better justice than the world had provided. He did not regard a prison sentence as sufficient reason to deny the dead care. The prayer heartened him, and he felt that the sister watching over him had not lost sight of him even here.

Red Caesar then used [[Beryzoz's Teeth]] to cast Speak with Dead on the skeleton from which he had taken the platinum teeth. The animated man explained that he had not been a prisoner. He had been meeting a client in the cell as a defense advocate when the water rose and the doors trapped them inside. He was not a lawyer by profession, but a senator who had put himself at risk to defend this person.

The unnamed senator had represented the Dancing Blades. Asked about [[Alamar]], he said that his small guild would not have survived without Alamar's intervention: the former kingdom had been trying to root it out. His testimony added a personal account of what the republic had changed for a group formerly threatened by the monarchy.

Ceril urged Red Caesar to ask about surviving wealth. The senator's final answer confirmed that he had hidden riches, but the questions ended before he gave a location. The party gained no usable directions to that treasure. The magical teeth returned to Red Caesar, who kicked the skeleton apart before leaving.

#### The stained-glass passage

The group continued through the right-hand doorway into a long corridor that led south toward an earlier room. Stone supports alternated with panels of stained glass depicting rolling green countryside beneath differently colored skies. Seaweed and barnacles grew outside the windows, and the light passing through them filled the hall with shifting colors. The passage lay against the exterior of the hill into which this part of the castle was built.

Vokenar heard something rattling at the northern door. A large [[Hezru]] entered through it, while two more bearded devils were summoned at the southern end of the corridor. These were a new confrontation, separate from the chain devil's attack in the cells.

Domyx held the southern approach against the devils. Red Caesar protected himself against extraplanar enemies with Protection from Evil and Good, using an anointed component from [[Heaven's Bulb]]. He described the ward as a diffuse field extending from his body, rather than the hard shell of his other protective magic. It visibly repelled some of the Hezru's attacks.

The Hezru resembled a large, orange, bull-horned toad and carried a nauseating stench. Ceril summoned his own bestial spirit crab to help fight it, replacing the lost support with a new creature under his control. Kerben kept his distance where possible, firing into the struggle while Zulu remained active. Vokenar brought holy fire against the demon. The fight was still underway when the chunk ended.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_020/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### The rainbow hall's defenders

The party continued fighting along the stained-glass corridor. Domyx prevented the two bearded devils from freely reaching his companions, while Red Caesar's protective field helped him hold against the Hezru. Ceril and his summoned crab joined the attack despite the creature's sickening odor. When the Hezru leapt toward Vokenar, the cleric's armor, shield, and divine protection held against its assault.

Vokenar established a Guardian of Faith in the hall. Its glimmering sword took on the many colors pouring through the stained glass, making the spectral defender seem part of the room's rainbow light. Domyx struck one bearded devil hard enough to destroy it and drive it through a window into the ocean below. He then pulled the other into the guardian's reach with the Iklwa Isondo. The guardian cut that devil down.

With the southern enemies gone, the party surrounded the Hezru. Kerben attacked it with his blades and brought Zulu into the fight. Vokenar delivered the finishing stroke with his [[Akasha]] blade. The visible enemies had all fallen, but the Nalfeshne had again been watching for the moment after the fight.

#### The hidden master's ambush

Ceril sensed the danger and withdrew from the room just before an unseen Lightning Bolt tore down the corridor. The blast struck Domyx, Red Caesar, and Zulu. Domyx remained standing, but Red Caesar and Zulu collapsed. The Nalfeshne then retreated through a door to hide, escaping another direct confrontation. This departure was a retreat through the castle, rather than the teleportation it had used after the prison battle.

The party attended to the fallen instead of immediately pursuing the demon. Kerben healed Red Caesar and used the magic of his bond with Zulu to restore his companion. Vokenar followed with Aura of Vitality, healing Red Caesar, Domyx, and the others. Red Caesar proposed taking vitality from Zulu again, but the recovery actually established in this scene came from Kerben and Vokenar's magic. Zulu and Red Caesar were restored without an established death. Kerben's ordinary wounds could be healed, while the earlier wraiths' draining injury still limited him.

The group's accumulated experience would allow further growth after their next long rest, but they had not yet taken that rest. There was still unexplored dungeon between them and a secure ending to the expedition.

#### Choosing to wear down the Nalfeshne

They reconsidered whether to pursue the Nalfeshne immediately through the northern door or return to explore the left side of the castle. Vokenar considered the advantage of stopping the demon before it could summon more defenders. The alternative was to force it to spend more of its magic and servants in separate encounters, rather than meet it with all those resources intact.

The party settled on the latter approach. This was a plan to wear down the Nalfeshne's available resources, rather than proof that its supply of fiends was already exhausted. They returned to the foyer to approach the left wing. The Guardian of Faith remained in the stained-glass corridor, and Ceril's summoned crab was still present. They did not take another short rest before the session ended.

Their enemy remained alive and concealed somewhere deeper inside Castle Kaedon. The barricaded northern passage and other unopened doors still separated them from unexplored rooms, and they had not yet recovered the [[Penumbra]] needed for [[Lyngbakr]]. The group closed the session healed enough to continue, but carrying Kerben's unresolved life drain and a growing awareness of how the Nalfeshne exploited each apparent victory.

### Summary

- The party defeated the bearded devils summoned in the pentagonal chamber and identified their invisible overseer as a Nalfeshne claiming Castle Kaedon.
- Wraiths riding nightmares were destroyed, but their life drain left Kerben weakened beyond the reach of the healing presently available.
- Red Caesar recovered mithril, platinum teeth, and a Potion of Soothing Gaze. Ceril took an old map of Westerness, and the party recovered another hoard of Kaedonite coins.
- The dungeon's prisoner list named Clockwork. An unnamed Dancing Blades senator described dying while defending a client and credited Alamar with saving his guild from the former kingdom.
- The party defeated a chain devil and a bearded devil in the prison, then a Hezru and two bearded devils in the stained-glass corridor.
- The Nalfeshne intervened with Fireball and Lightning Bolt after its servants fell. Kerben's summoned crab was destroyed, and the later lightning felled Red Caesar and Zulu before their companions restored them.
- The party returned to the foyer to explore the left wing, intending to deplete the demon's resources before confronting it directly.

Castle Kaedon's exploration became a contest with an enemy that preferred concealment and attrition to a fair confrontation. The Nalfeshne's taunts advertised its claim over the place, while its interventions demonstrated how dangerous that claim had become. Each cleared room gave the party access to another part of the old castle, but the demon used the aftermath of those victories to attack and escape.

The discoveries also recovered a more complicated history of the drowned republic. The prison held people trapped by the rising water, including a senator whose presence there was an act of advocacy rather than punishment. His praise for Alamar and the survival of the Dancing Blades added another perspective to the party's earlier account of the monarchy's replacement. The map preserved the former land beneath the sea, while Clockwork's name connected the dungeon to the political associations already visible elsewhere in the castle.

The party's responses to the dead and to its companions exposed different priorities. Vokenar sought justice and divine care for the prisoners; Red Caesar pursued information and valuables, then damaged the senator's remains. The suggestion of drawing life from Zulu also put recovery at odds with Kerben's bond to his companion. In the final emergency, however, the party's actual coordinated healing restored Red Caesar and Zulu. They ended together in the foyer, prepared to continue the search, with their strongest enemy still at large and Kerben's deeper injury unresolved.
