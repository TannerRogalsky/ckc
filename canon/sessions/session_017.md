# Session 017

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_017/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Westerness beneath the water

The party traveled aboard the [[Gheister]] to the newly exposed portions of [[Castle Kaedon]]. [[Lyngbakr]] had risen as far as it could, but its head and parts of the surrounding terrain still lay below the waves. Looking down through the water, the travelers could see the encrusted trees of an ancient woodland, streets, and occasional buildings of [[Westerness]], the human city that had surrounded the castle.

For [[Red Caesar]], the sight brought the history he had studied into the visible world. [[Kerben]] remembered visiting Westerness before [[The Cataclysm]], whereas [[Ceril]] knew of it but had never been there. The submerged ruins therefore carried different associations for the three companions: remembered streets, a place once known by reputation, and a civilization encountered through books. Nature had reclaimed much of what had once been described to Ceril as an evil empire in the west.

The castle island's perimeter rose in steep cliffs, but broken stone and collapsed sections formed a ramp from the water. The party landed there and climbed toward the interior. Bright sunlight illuminated the exposed buildings while sea fog remained mostly below them. From the approach they recognized the turrets and portions of the study and ballroom explored on their earlier visit. The rising island had opened a lower courtyard that had formerly been underwater, rather than simply offering another passage through those familiar rooms.

#### The preserved arena

[[Domyx]] led the way into a broad courtyard, now identifiable as the [[Castle Kaedon Arena]]. Bleachers surrounded a circular playing area with a wooden post at its center. A raised box with a throne-like seat overlooked the grounds, and stables and commercial kiosks stood along the walls. Domyx's knowledge of human history suggested a competitive sporting venue, although he could not identify the game once played there.

Despite their long immersion, many wooden structures remained surprisingly intact. The contrast with the overgrown city outside drew attention to the castle's magic. Red Caesar used Detect Magic and found a stronger protective aura than on their earlier visit. It spread across the grounds without an obvious central source. He connected its renewed strength with the [[Penumbra]] that had been fed to Lyngbakr and with [[Alamar]]'s increased ability to move away from the castle.

The magic also carried an unfamiliar, otherworldly quality. Red Caesar could recognize its protective character, but not identify a specific being or plane behind it. The connection to Penumbra explained why it seemed more strongly fueled; it did not settle every question about the castle's preservation, animated objects, and undead.

Kerben sent [[Tango]] to scout overhead and used Speak with Animals to understand his report. The bird described a western stretch of land, disconnected pieces to the south, and stairs supported on pillars that connected to structures the party had previously entered. Those stairs were no longer straightforwardly accessible from the exposed ground. Tango's reconnaissance gave them an outline of the island's broken layout, and he remained overhead to watch for changes while the party explored.

Ceril noticed that the stands were not empty. Bones and occasional intact skeletons remained among the bleachers, and another set occupied the raised box. The party could not tell whether that last person had been a noble spectator or an official overseeing the event. Beneath the bones, damp sludge mingled with seaweed. Ceril recognized that some of it was unnatural, and it began to move as the travelers entered the courtyard. Dark material on the playing surface likewise gathered into a larger ooze.

#### The remains turned hostile

Ceril warned the others as the old human remains coalesced into hostile creatures. Some became ochre jellies; the biomass on the court formed a black pudding, associated with the athletes who had once occupied it. Gray [[Psychic Goop]] in the stands had developed from remains in which brain matter predominated.

Ceril's knowledge helped the party avoid making the encounter worse. Cutting attacks and lightning could divide some of the oozes into additional creatures, while the black pudding's acid threatened ordinary equipment. The psychic creatures presented a different danger: magical attacks against them could send pain back through a mental connection. Concealment would also prove less effective than usual, because they could locate minds without clearly seeing bodies.

Ceril raised a wall of fire to delay creatures approaching from the stables. The barrier gave the others time to confront the oozes already among them. [[Vokenar]] used holy light, Domyx relied on his hammer rather than a cutting weapon, Kerben fired from a distance, and Red Caesar drew energy from the creatures through necrotic magic. Those methods reduced the risk of splitting the jellies and steadily broke down the closer threats.

The psychic goops remained in the bleachers and attacked together, pressing painfully against their opponents' minds. Kerben's attempts to hide did not prevent them from finding him, and Ceril likewise discovered that moving out of sight did not sever their influence. Vokenar helped protect his companions while the party destroyed the black pudding and most of the nearby jellies.

Ceril climbed to the raised seating and took on his starry archer form to answer the creatures above them. Domyx also pushed into the stands, breaking through the seating as he struck. By the end of the chunk, the physical oozes had mostly been cleared, but the last nearby jelly and several psychic goops remained. The arena battle was still underway.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_017/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Clearing the arena and recovering its treasures

The remaining psychic goops concentrated much of their assault on Ceril. Their combined influence produced an oppressive hum and increasingly severe mental pain. Vokenar moved toward his elevated position and healed him, while Domyx and Kerben continued reducing the creatures in the stands. Red Caesar finished the last ochre jelly near the party and then helped destroy the final psychic threat.

As the goops fell, their collective pressure weakened. The survivors could no longer exert the same combined strength. The party eventually cleared the arena, and Vokenar gave Ceril further healing before they moved on.

Domyx searched the kiosks that had once sold food to spectators and found [[Kaedon]] coinage among them. Each coin carried the profile of [[King Maniasis]], Alamar's father. The discovery prompted recollection of the king's madness and poisoning by [[Queen Caeradwyn]]'s elven blood, linking an ordinary piece of the arena's commercial life to the troubled royal history behind the castle.

One of the nearby stables had served as a wardrobe rather than an animal stall. Most of its clothes had deteriorated, but a ringmaster's motley vestment remained vividly pink, green, and blue. The party judged it an important surviving piece of Kaedonite art. The garment itself was not magical; the castle's preservation appeared to explain its condition. They added it and the recovered coins to the ship's shared hold.

#### The western grounds

Rather than rest in the cleared courtyard, the party continued west. Their route passed beneath the castle's connecting structures and down onto another exposed plateau. Cliffs overlooked the ocean, and the great cataract formed the backdrop to the western grounds. Stables, enclosed wooden buildings, and old cultivated fields stood ahead. The planting rows were still recognizable, but thick seaweed had replaced the former crops.

Stiff wind drove the sea into choppy waves and carried sheets of mist across the island from the waterfall. The Gheister had already struggled with the approach in those conditions. Visibility returned quickly between the passing curtains of fog, yet Vokenar found the bright, outwardly cheerful surroundings unsettling.

Red Caesar heard a scuttling rattle and noticed small black shapes moving through the overgrown field. They initially resembled spiders, but their five crawling extremities resolved into fingers and thumbs. A swarm of [[Severed Hands]] was moving toward the party. Almost simultaneously, the stable doors opened and two [[Mezzoloth]]s emerged. The fiends had been crouching inside; standing upright, they revealed beetle-like bodies, four arms, and tridents.

#### Fiends beside the cliffs

Kerben's gunfire and binding magic caught one mezzoloth, but the creature escaped its restraints by dissolving into liquid metal and reappearing beside him. The other seized Vokenar in a powerful claw. Their hard shells resisted the party's attacks, and their resistance to magic made them harder to stop than the oozes.

Red Caesar extended his ward to protect companions and used thunderous force against the severed hands. Some of the swarm fell from the cliff; another blast drove the remaining hands toward the edge, where Kerben cut away the fingers still holding on. The scattered remains dropped into the sea below.

Domyx struck the mezzoloth holding Vokenar and tried to jar its grip loose, but could not free him. Vokenar's attempt to banish the creature also failed. Its trident was especially dangerous: mercury shifted inside the weapon as it moved, lending a heavy, booming force to its blows. Vokenar remained trapped and increasingly endangered despite Red Caesar's protection.

Kerben killed the fiend that had advanced on him and turned toward the remaining opponent. Domyx continued trying to bring it down, but it still held Vokenar at the chunk's close. The swarm and one mezzoloth had been defeated; the second fiend remained an immediate threat.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_017/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Vokenar's collapse and the stables

The surviving mezzoloth struck Vokenar down. Before it could turn on someone else, Domyx retaliated with his hammer and smashed the creature into the ground, ending the battle. Vokenar still needed urgent help. Red Caesar had no immediate healing to offer, but Kerben reached him and used Cure Wounds to restore him to consciousness. Vokenar then helped heal the others, and the group continued exploring without settling down to rest.

The stables contained silvered hackamores, bridles, and other fittings for show horses. These were intact enough to recover as treasure and were added to the shared hold. Ceril searched for surviving traces of the animals themselves, but found none: the water had swept away the biological remains, leaving only their equipment. The discovery emphasized another difference between the old world and the flooded present, where horses had become much less common.

#### The granary's dangerous air

The party chose the smaller of the nearby buildings and opened both doors together. Inside lay a granary with large grain bins and barrels stored in its corners. Opening the sealed space released a rush of alcohol vapor. Grain left for decades had fermented into old malt liquor, and its fumes had filled the building.

The air made several companions cough and suffer from its intoxicating, poisonous effects. Vokenar and Ceril were particularly affected. It also created an obvious fire hazard, which Red Caesar recognized immediately. The opening doors disturbed giant scorpions among the barrels, a swarm of parasitic [[Sturges]] on the ceiling, and filthy carrion crawlers.

Ceril identified the bat-like sturges as blood-feeding creatures that could attach themselves to victims. Kerben recalled that carrion crawlers could paralyze prey with their tentacles. Between the fumes and the infestation, the seemingly ordinary storehouse had become another dangerous obstacle.

Red Caesar withdrew through the doorway and sent a fireball into the far side of the room. The alcohol vapor amplified the burst, carrying it across a broader area and consuming the fumes. The attack badly injured several creatures while clearing the air. Domyx could endure his companion's magic and remained inside to hold the approaching infestation away from the others.

He cut down the sturge swarm, but the remaining creatures crowded around him. Several enormous scorpions caught him in their claws and stung him while the carrion crawlers tried to immobilize him. Vokenar answered with a burst of holy light, further thinning the creatures. Kerben fired from the doorway, killing a crawler and one of the scorpions clinging to Domyx.

Domyx began wrestling the last scorpion, embracing the drunken mood of the encounter even after the vapor had burned away. His companions briefly thought the fight was effectively settled, but the creature continued resisting. Ceril finally drove a luminous arrow through it. Domyx did not immediately notice that it was dead and continued grappling with the inert body for a short while before the party regrouped.

#### Survivals of the old world

After clearing the granary, the party finally rested. Red Caesar used the pause to search and examine the building's magic. Some of the contents remained drinkable despite the dangerous fumes: he found [[Kaedonite Black Ale]], which the party added to the shared hold alongside its other discoveries.

Kerben asked about Kaedon's enemies before the Cataclysm, prompting a review of its last years. The kingdom had been helping make peace between elves and dwarves and had few remaining organized enemies. Human and orcish encampments between Kaedon and [[Esperanto]] still caused problems, but their opposition was diminishing as trade routes were being established. The catastrophe had overtaken a world that was approaching peace, rather than one simply consumed by continuous war.

The party ended the session resting near the cleared granary. The arena and western grounds had yielded coins, art, horse fittings, and surviving ale, but no new Penumbra had yet been recovered from the castle. Much of the exposed complex remained unexplored.

### Summary

- The party returned to Castle Kaedon aboard the Gheister and saw Westerness's submerged streets and buildings beneath the water.
- Red Caesar found the castle's protective magic stronger than before, consistent with the added Penumbra and Alamar's expanding range. Its otherworldly quality remained unexplained.
- Tango mapped the broken island's exposed layout, and the party entered its preserved sporting arena.
- The former spectators' and athletes' remains had become hostile oozes. The party cleared them, with Vokenar healing Ceril after sustained psychic attacks.
- Domyx recovered coins bearing King Maniasis's profile; the party also found a preserved ringmaster's vestment.
- On the western plateau, they defeated severed hands and two mezzoloths. One fiend struck Vokenar unconscious before Domyx killed it and Kerben revived him.
- The stables yielded silvered horse fittings. A nearby granary contained dangerous alcohol fumes, giant scorpions, sturges, and carrion crawlers.
- Red Caesar ignited the vapor, and the party cleared the infestation before resting and recovering drinkable Kaedonite Black Ale.
- They recalled Kaedon's peace efforts and developing trade with Esperanto before the Cataclysm. The castle exploration continued without a new Penumbra recovery.

The newly exposed grounds brought the old human civilization into closer view. Westerness had largely become an underwater ruin, but the castle's stronger magic preserved unexpected pieces of everyday life: sporting facilities, concession money, bright ceremonial clothing, equestrian equipment, and a brewing tradition. Those survivals complicated the picture of a place defined only by a mad king or a curse. The closing account of Kaedon's peace efforts likewise distinguished its later direction from the oppressive history Ceril had once heard about.

Preservation had also allowed threats to endure or develop. Human remains had become coordinated psychic creatures, fiends occupied the stables, and neglected grain had turned a storehouse into a toxic environment. The party survived through shared protection and healing as well as force. Vokenar's collapse and Kerben's rescue were the most immediate personal consequences. By the close, they had made a safe resting place in the granary and recovered several treasures, while the search for Penumbra and the deeper explanation of the castle's magic remained unfinished.
