## Session 033

### Chunk 0000

[[Mayor Yoris]] explained that [[Deep Roses]] cured individual cases of [[Mana Sickness]], but victims continued to become infected. Through the summoned spirit of [[Janeera]], the party learned that fallen [[Penumbra]] had reacted with an ancient green dragon named [[Ninki Nanka]], turning the dragon into the contagion's source. Ninki Nanka had once defended the elves of [[Windsurf]] and might be another victim of the curse.

[[Red Caesar]] reunited with [[Lodestar]], a fellow former student of [[Heaven's Bulb]] and sister of [[Ebbie Indorra]]. Lodestar warned the party to preserve Greater Restoration for someone beyond their group. Janeera also revealed that [[Witness]] had entrusted her with part of his world chart before leaving Stark, but the fragment had been lost with a failed Southport expedition in the [[Mana Sea]].

The party received [[Green Slaadi Whiskey]] and [[Crone's Contempt]] from Janeera's collection, then entered the Mana Sea with [[Victor, the Amphibious Beast]]. They endured the swamp's magical sickness, recovered [[Rahmadi]]'s cloak and other treasure from a dead expedition member, and survived poisonous fungi and a vast bird swarm before discovering drider webs and an ambush led by a drow cultist.

### Chunk 0001

The party defeated the drow cultist and one drider while driving another away. [[Ceril]] discovered that the attackers had been drawn from the underground by a layered magical or psychic command linking them to an unseen master.

After recovering two restorative tinctures, the party encountered moss golems sustained by the Mana Sea's poisonous atmosphere. Fire and cold stripped away the constructs' growth and prevented them from overwhelming the group.

### Chunk 0002

The party destroyed the moss golems and entered deeper brackish water, where [[Kuo-Toa]] musketeers attacked under magical compulsion. The party broke several free without killing them, revealing that the same hidden master controlled both the Kuo-Toa and the earlier drow.

An invisible [[Aboleth]] emerged beneath the water, seized several party members, and fed upon their memories. Ceril's Starry Wisp exposed it, allowing the party to fight the creature directly while the freed Kuo-Toa escaped.

### Chunk 0003

[[Kerben]] killed the Aboleth after [[Domyx]] restrained it and pulled him free. The party then reached [[Ninki Nanka]]'s cave, which was saturated with lethal poison. [[Vokenar]]'s Arcane Eye found the injured dragon, the failed expedition, the missing half of the [[Chart of the Witness]], and the dragon's abandoned hoard.

[[Red Caesar]] enclosed Ceril in a protective sphere and sent him through the cave. Ceril emerged beside the terrified dragon and removed its Penumbra-born curse with Greater Restoration. The poisonous outflow stopped immediately, ending the source of Mana Sickness. Ninki Nanka thanked Ceril, abandoned the hoard, and departed to protect the dragon's people.

The party recovered the chart fragment, a potion of flight, diamonds, and the [[Tomb of Lenth the Rugged]]. Back in Southport, Red Caesar decoded and recombined the complete Chart of the Witness. Its topography revealed deep ocean trenches but no direct path to [[Axis Mundi]], supporting the belief that the world's interior lies beyond a planar boundary.

Lodestar reported that [[Emperor Shen]] had weakened, imperial forces were retreating, and the eastern war would remain quiet until at least the next moon. Southport installed voice pipes and expanded dormitories aboard [[The Opal]], while eleven local deckhands joined the crew. The party chose to pursue [[Farraday]] next, then confront [[Boral Erendel]] in elven territory.

### Summary

Key events:

- [[Janeera]] identified [[Ninki Nanka]] as the continuing source of [[Mana Sickness]].
- [[Lodestar]] reunited with [[Red Caesar]] and foresaw the need to preserve Greater Restoration for the dragon.
- The party crossed the [[Mana Sea]], defeated an [[Aboleth]], and freed several enthralled [[Kuo-Toa]].
- [[Ceril]] removed Ninki Nanka's Penumbra-born curse, ending new Mana Sickness infections.
- The party recovered and completed the [[Chart of the Witness]].
- The party acquired [[Rahmadi]]'s cloak, [[Green Slaadi Whiskey]], [[Crone's Contempt]], a potion of flight, diamonds, and the [[Tomb of Lenth the Rugged]].
- [[The Opal]] gained voice pipes, expanded dormitories, and eleven new deckhands.
- [[Lodestar]] reported that [[Emperor Shen]] and the [[Broyish Empire]] are temporarily weakened.
- The party prepared to advance to level 13 and chose [[Farraday]] as its next target.

The Southport expedition turned a recurring plague into a solvable recovery effort. Deep Roses remain necessary for existing victims, but Ceril's restoration of Ninki Nanka means the city no longer faces endless reinfection. The dragon's history also reframed a presumed monster hunt as the rescue of an old protector corrupted by Penumbra.

Completing the Chart of the Witness closes a long-running exploration thread while refining the route toward Axis Mundi. The chart offers no simple physical entrance, so the dwarves reopening [[The Funnel]] remain the party's strongest lead. Lodestar's news creates a temporary strategic window: with the Empire inactive, the party can pursue Farraday and Boral Erendel before returning to Starfall.

### Connections

- Ninki Nanka's curse confirms that [[Penumbra]] can transform living creatures and their environments in unpredictable ways.
- The Aboleth's domination explains why unrelated drow and Kuo-Toa attacked with the same unnatural coordination.
- The completed Chart of the Witness links Janeera's pre-Cataclysm history to the fragment found in the [[Southern Archipelago Castle]].
- The [[Tomb of Lenth the Rugged]] reconnects the legendary pirate to [[Pleasance MacLenth]] and [[Xander MacLenth]].
- The temporary imperial retreat gives the party time to address [[Farraday]] and the [[Demi-Spell]] before confronting [[Starfall]].

### Theory: Antumbra Sabotage

**Unconfirmed theory:** [[Emperor Shen]] may be weakened because he attempted to charge or fire [[Starfall]] with the [[Antumbra]] that [[Kerben]] planted among the [[Penumbra]] aboard [[The White Drake]]. The contaminated fuel was designed to spread through Starfall like a bomb and cause catastrophic damage. Shen and [[Vizier Jade]] fled directly to Starfall to prepare their final attack, but no subsequent shot was witnessed; [[Lodestar]] soon divined that Shen had lost access to former magic and could not act until at least the next moon. This sequence suggests the Antumbra may have detonated during charging, damaging Starfall and either injuring Shen or severing his access to the weapon. No source has yet confirmed that the sabotage occurred.
