# Session 033

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_033/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### A town that could not stay cured

[[Mayor Yoris]] welcomed the companions as people whose reputation had reached [[Southport]]. He recognized [[Kerben]] as [[The Opal]]'s captain, while [[Obould]] remained away at [[The White Drake]]. The settlement was a comparatively quiet refuge beyond the eastern war, but its people still suffered from [[Mana Sickness]].

The [[Deep Roses]] delivered through the freed priests of the [[Temple of Sigil]] had cured many individual cases. The problem was reinfection. Something in the [[Mana Sea]] continued spreading the illness, undoing the benefit of treatment. War had also disrupted Southport's food routes. The supply network from [[The Garden]], strengthened by Kerben's logistical work and [[Ceril]]'s cultivation, had helped keep the town fed through the shortages.

[[Red Caesar]] had understood Mana Sickness chiefly as a danger of unprepared spellcasting. Mayor Yoris said that this could still be a way to contract it, but noncasters and elderly people were now becoming ill as well. The outbreak had worsened during his young lifetime after a long quieter period. A druid could maintain a regular supply of Deep Roses, but that would demand ongoing work without removing the contagion.

The mayor directed them to [[Janeera]], the seer who had predicted their arrival. She believed that a creature called [[Ninki Nanka]] was the source. He warned that the Mana Sea's wild magic interfered with divination and other spells, and that experienced spellcasters could still contract the sickness themselves.

#### Familiar faces in Southport

Moving through the town, they found people whose earlier troubles had brought them into the party's path. [[Rochella Golf]] had reunited with her family and was doing well. One of the priests restored from stone was adjusting to life outside the temple, covering his pale skin against the sun.

[[Trent Indorra]] and [[Ebbie Indorra]] were there with their many children, now lively enough to run and play. Ebbie Indorra's sister, [[Lodestar]], was also present. Red Caesar recognized her as a former student of [[Heaven's Bulb]], where she had studied divination while he pursued abjuration.

They spoke in Southern about leaving the school and finding a place among unfamiliar peoples. Lodestar said [[Master Lee]] had encouraged her to make a life beyond Heaven's Bulb because her power exceeded what it could contain. Following the stars had eventually brought her back to her sister. She welcomed a later opportunity to reminisce with Red Caesar.

Before parting, Lodestar gave him a warning for the expedition: preserve Greater Restoration until someone outside the party needed it. Red Caesar passed that guidance to the companions. Finding an additional scroll would have been useful, but Southport had too few scribes and spellcasters to supply one.

#### Janeera's testimony and the missing chart

Mayor Yoris led them to an overgrown, largely unused manor. In its den, he prepared a cauldron and read an invocation from a scroll. Smoke and shimmering essence formed the face of Janeera, an elderly orc woman who was already dead. Her advice to the young mayor came through this prepared contact rather than through a living resident of the house.

Janeera had arranged means of communication before her death. She had initially watched the travelers through interest in other orcs, including their captain, then recognized the larger significance of their work on the sky. She had foreseen their arrival months earlier.

Her account connected the plague to fallen [[Penumbra]]. Creatures had swallowed pieces of it, and its reaction with their bodies altered them. Ninki Nanka, a great green dragon, had become the dominant afflicted creature and the source of contagion. Its poisonous nature and lair had been amplified by that magical transformation. Wild magic prevented her from locating it precisely through divination.

Mayor Yoris admitted that he had sent warriors into the Mana Sea on her advice. They had not returned. He blamed himself for their deaths and refused to send another group without greater confidence that it could survive. They had carried the missing part of [[Witness]]'s chart, now presumed lost somewhere along their approach to the dragon.

Janeera had received that part of the chart decades earlier. Witness had mapped Stark by crossing both mountains and ocean depths, undeterred by the rising water. Other warforged hunting him forced him into hiding and altered how he worked, but he eventually completed the map. He entrusted portions to people he trusted, including Janeera and the people who had sheltered him, then left Stark to chart another plane. Her own death in the rising waters had not ended the usefulness of the chart or her advice.

The party already owned the other portion. Recovering the lost expedition's copy could therefore serve both the plague investigation and its wider need to understand the world. Mayor Yoris urged them to move quickly through the Mana Sea, where spellcasters might intensify their exposure to the contagion.

#### Preparing to rescue a possible victim

[[Vokenar]] used Legend Lore to learn more about Ninki Nanka. The dragon had lived around [[Windsurf]] since before [[The Cataclysm]]. It had once made peace with the local elves and a copper dragon, defending their lands. Its history suggested that the current source of disease might itself be a victim, giving Lodestar's warning about restoration a possible meaning.

The party sold the [[Boston Golf]] automaton to his granddaughter, who lived in Southport and could afford to recover the family heirloom. The proceeds supplied valuable materials for protective feasting. Ceril prepared Heroes' Feast for all five companions and [[Victor, the Amphibious Beast]], the crocodilian partner Kerben chose for the swamp.

Mayor Yoris offered four potions from Janeera's collection, two before they left and two upon their return. Red Caesar took [[Green Slaadi Whiskey]], a volatile restorative capable of healing or harm and, very rarely, something as extraordinary as a wish. Kerben received [[Crone's Contempt]], which could empower a burst of weapon attacks. The remaining rewards stayed in Southport.

The companions set out by rowboat toward the dark dome of the Mana Sea's canopy. Insects skimmed the water, while interlocked trees and growth obstructed the oars. Eventually the crowded vegetation made the boat unusable. Vokenar's Water Walk allowed the group to continue on the surface rather than swim through the murk.

#### Crossing the poisoned forest

Within the canopy, darkness, humidity, and magical fog surrounded them. The sickness struck at their vitality despite their preparations. Red Caesar and Vokenar temporarily lost their sight, their eyes turning inky black. Kerben supplied restorative herbs for Red Caesar while Vokenar restored his own sight. The party recognized how easily these hazards might have defeated the earlier expedition.

Kerben and Ceril spotted an overgrown corpse, apparently from an earlier adventuring group. It wore a valuable cloak and carried a ruby. Red Caesar recognized the cloak's association with [[Rahmadi]], the legendary southern figure who had led people out of the desert during the flood. They recovered the cloak and gem without identifying the corpse as Rahmadi himself.

The forest formed cave-like spaces from matted wood and vines, supporting fungi and meadows above the drowned ground. Poisonous spores filled one chamber before the companions recognized the danger. Heroes' Feast helped them withstand the exposure, but did not make the passage harmless. They also collected mithril and diamonds embedded in the old wooden walls.

A vast flock then rushed through the area, tearing leaves and wood as it swept across their path. Kerben and the crocodile sheltered beneath a shelf fungus. Vokenar raised Spirit Guardians to protect the group, while Red Caesar used Gust of Wind to divert birds that escaped that defense. The others braced behind shields or endured the passage. After a few minutes the flock passed, and Vokenar tended the crocodile's injuries.

They chose to continue without resting. The route became choked with old, damp webs. Kerben recognized that the strands were not ordinary vines, and Ceril associated them with driders and the drow. Water Walk kept them above the swamp, but the netting still obstructed progress.

Two driders descended around the group, one ahead and one behind. A drow cultist called from farther away, invoking Lolth in Old Elfish and directing the ambush. Ceril spotted the rear threat and warned Kerben. Domyx engaged the attackers as the chunk ended, while the damp webs prevented easy clearance by fire.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_033/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### The drow's unseen master

Red Caesar caught the rear drider in Resilient Sphere, separating it from the fight. It slowly rolled after Ceril through the tangled terrain. Domyx confronted the forward drider while the cultist attacked from beyond it. His [[Gith Shard Glaive]] let him reach her through the intervening space.

Ceril tried using a fire-breathing potion against the webs, but their dampness prevented the attempted clearance. Vokenar used the Misty Step available in his new elven form to enter the fight and brought his newly learned weapon magic against the cultist. Kerben supported with his rifle and sent the crocodile forward, where the crocodile seized and twisted at the cultist's leg.

While the others fought, Ceril studied the captive drider. Drow and driders should have been underground, especially after the flood made remaining there more attractive. Their presence here suggested an outside power had drawn them to the surface. Detect Magic revealed a magical or psychic link from the drider to its immediate leader and then to something higher. It was not simply a conventional spell placed on the captive.

Ceril could not question it through Speak with Animals: the creature's drow nature and Old Elfish speech required different communication. The link nevertheless gave him a warning of an unseen master beyond the cultist.

Red Caesar killed the forward drider, but the cultist's spells continued. Harm threatened Vokenar, followed by punishing fire and cold. A Cone of Cold overcame Red Caesar's attempted counterspell and brought down both Vokenar and the crocodile. They were unconscious rather than dead. Domyx used fire breath against the cultist and kept her occupied until Kerben shot her down.

The party considered what to do with the remaining drider. Red Caesar contemplated crushing it within the sphere, but did not do so. Ceril left the captive and returned to find his companions fallen. His chalice-enhanced healing restored Vokenar, and the crocodile was resummoned. The surviving drider recognized its unfavorable position and fled as the confinement ended. The party did not pursue it.

Vokenar used healing stored in his shield to help the wounded companions, while Domyx drew on his ability to recover under pressure. They did not take a short rest. Searching the fallen attackers yielded two doses of Aramil's Tincture, which Ceril and Vokenar used to replenish their magic.

#### Moss that grew through poison

The next stretch reached raised marshland, briefly offering easier movement. Three masses of mud and moss then rose from the ground as golems. Ceril understood their constructed nature as a corruption of living material put under another will.

He identified the danger of the surrounding poison. The golems absorbed it and regenerated through the Mana Sea's ambient atmosphere, and their growing bodies could become increasingly difficult to contain. Fire and cold offered effective ways to strip their growth away. Clearing the air might interrupt their recovery, but the poison was environmental and would flow back into the space.

Domyx held their attention while Ceril and Vokenar brought fire against them. Kerben kept his rifle working from farther back and directed the crocodile around the heated area. His passage through the swamp had also allowed him to gather unusual poisonous mushrooms for later use.

Red Caesar considered dispersing the gas, then chose direct fire against the constructs. Domyx's fire-breathing preparation remained useful, and Ceril used another potion before raising a Wall of Fire across their positions. The golems enlarged as the moss gathered into them. The chunk ended with the growing constructs still attacking through the flames.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_033/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Finishing the golems

Ceril's Wall of Fire continued burning the moss, while Domyx prevented a golem from simply retreating through it. Vokenar added a ring of frost, exposing the constructs to both elements. Kerben withdrew the crocodile from the hottest ground and supported the attack with rifle fire.

Red Caesar's Fireball destroyed one golem. Domyx brought down another, and the crocodile caught the last as it moved toward the rear of the party. The crocodile's bite finished it before it could deliver a final attack. The companions regrouped and healed, but again chose to press onward without a short rest.

#### Breaking the musketeers' compulsion

The enclosed, cave-like forest gave way to taller trees above deep brackish water. The Mana Sea had grown over land and forests drowned by the rising ocean, so threats could move beneath a surface the travelers scarcely disturbed.

Vokenar detected an underwater ambush in time to warn the party. [[Kuo-Toa]] carrying muskets surrounded them with striking coordination. His radiant magic broke the compulsion over two of them. They lowered their guns and prepared to flee instead of continuing the attack.

Ceril recognized the same greater power behind the earlier drow. The controller was nearby, but hidden. Kerben's attacks freed another musketeer, and Domyx tried to break the others' control rather than simply kill them. Red Caesar spread Magic Missile among the distant Kuo-Toa, applying enough pain to shake several more back to themselves. The party allowed those freed to dive away.

Some musketeers remained enthralled and continued firing, while an invisible creature reached out from underwater. Its tentacles dragged Kerben and Ceril beneath the surface. Red Caesar's Shield initially kept it from doing the same to him.

The creature reached into Ceril's memories, replaying both his earlier life and the present campaign in a painful rush. It shared and consumed their knowledge without erasing them. Ceril changed into a crab to wriggle free, then resumed his normal form to cast Starry Wisp at the location he had found.

#### The Aboleth revealed

The spell struck and outlined the hidden creature with starlight. The party could now see an [[Aboleth]], a great tentacled aberration whose psychic control explained the drow's displacement and the musketeers' unnatural cooperation.

Vokenar placed a Guardian of Faith nearby. Its protection injured the Aboleth and helped break more Kuo-Toa from control. Domyx freed further musketeers with the glaive, and the released creatures continued fleeing. The remaining gunfire became a secondary danger beside the creature beneath the water.

Kerben briefly forced himself free, but the Aboleth caught him again, along with Ceril and Red Caesar. Red Caesar fought back through Vampiric Touch, recovering some vitality while choosing to stay close enough to drain his captor. The creature drew on his memories as well, then attempted to make Domyx a replacement for the servants it was losing. Domyx resisted its domination.

Ceril tried concentrating on painful memories to discourage the intruder. Instead, it visibly enjoyed anguish as much as pleasure. He shifted toward dull memories of island life, but even those offered practical knowledge the creature valued. The attempts exposed its appetite without ending its access.

Vokenar healed his companions while they were held underwater, and the guardian continued wearing down their captor. His attempted Banishment failed against the creature's resistance. Domyx entered the water to strike it and was caught in another tentacle, which let it examine memories of his own accomplishments.

Domyx then enlarged himself, escaping the grip through his greater size. He wrapped one arm around the Aboleth to restrain it and used the other to pull Kerben free. Red Caesar remained within reach for his draining magic, and Ceril was still caught. The Aboleth could not escape Domyx's hold or successfully recapture Kerben. Blood and ooze spread through the water as the fight continued into the next chunk.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_033/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### Ending the Aboleth's rule

Vokenar continued supporting the attack, and Kerben used the opening Domyx had given him. Freed from the tentacle, he brought his blades against the restrained Aboleth and killed it. The creature's final words regretted that it could not return to space with the world it had learned from their memories.

The last Kuo-Toa musketeer had fled by the time the party surfaced. The companions had ended the controller's hold without hunting down its surviving servants. They finally took a short rest, using Vokenar's stored Prayer of Healing to recover quickly before continuing toward the dragon's cave.

#### A cave no one could safely reach

Following Janeera's directions brought them to an actual cavern beyond the forest's wooden enclosures. Toxic gas poured from its entrance, far denser than the atmosphere they had already endured. Red Caesar tried Gust of Wind, but could not clear enough of it to make the approach safe.

Domyx tested the passage by running in and back. He suffered badly before reaching the dragon and returned knowing that ordinary endurance would not carry even him through the whole distance.

Vokenar sent Arcane Eye ahead. It found the previous expedition's bones, the missing chart portion clutched by one of the dead, and an unused potion of flight. Farther in, Ninki Nanka cowered in a corner, wounded and exhausted, with black blood at its mouth. Poison radiated magically from its entire body and filled the cave, including the ceiling. There was no safer alternate entrance.

The dragon's hoard contained bags of diamonds and a gilded tomb bearing [[Lenth the Rugged]]'s name in Western. The lid was partly open, revealing old bones and tattered clothing. Recovering those things depended first on reaching the frightened creature without being overcome.

#### Ceril lifts Ninki Nanka's curse

The companions decided to protect Ceril inside Red Caesar's Resilient Sphere. Ceril prepared Freedom of the Winds as a way to escape afterward if needed. Domyx helped push the sphere down the long passage, giving it momentum while its protection kept the poison out.

Ninki Nanka turned toward the arriving elf and begged him to make death quick if he had come to kill. When the sphere ended, Ceril briefly suffered the surrounding toxin, then cast Greater Restoration to remove the curse.

The change was immediate. Poison stopped streaming from the dragon, and the foul atmosphere cleared behind Ceril in a wave. Ninki Nanka looked around in surprise, asked what he had done, and recognized that both its own suffering and the contagion had ended. Ceril identified himself while acknowledging that he had not acted alone.

The grateful dragon wanted to leave and protect its people. It invited them to look around, abandoning the hoard. Ceril flew alongside it for part of the return passage, then landed near the party as Ninki Nanka rushed out into the wider world. Its precise destination and the identity of those it meant to protect were not established here.

The party gathered the diamonds, [[Tomb of Lenth the Rugged]], missing chart, and potion of flight. They intended to return the tomb to [[Pleasance MacLenth]], Lenth the Rugged's granddaughter. Kerben was proposed as the flight potion's recipient because it could help him scout or shoot from above, but it was not consumed in this scene.

#### Recombining Witness's work

Back in Southport, the companions received lodging and spent the evening among the people they had helped. Red Caesar and Lodestar reminisced, and they visited the Indorra family. The next day they reported their success to Mayor Yoris and Janeera.

Mayor Yoris gave them the two promised remaining potions. Domyx received Red Slaadi Whiskey, a restorative that exchanged lasting vitality for immediate recovery, and a potion that temporarily increased strength. He did not drink either during the return conversation.

Red Caesar retrieved the chart portion kept aboard The Opal and decoded the two parts together. The completed [[Chart of the Witness]] represented the world's heights and depths, so the old mountains and lowlands could be read against the islands remaining above the sea. Witness's long labor had produced a map of both the drowned landscape and the remaining surface.

It did not mark [[Axis Mundi]] or show an ordinary entrance to it. Deep ocean trenches might offer places to begin digging, but the apparently continuous ground also suggested that the hollow realm below lay beyond a planar boundary. The companions considered a relationship like that between Stark and Arkadia. This was an interpretation of the chart and their existing clues, rather than a directly surveyed route.

#### Three days of care and rebuilding

Mayor Yoris thanked them and offered hospitality for as long as they needed. Ending the source meant Southport could recover, but existing victims still faced months of treatment. Vokenar wanted to help those he could while also keeping the wider threat in view.

The mayor offered work on The Opal in gratitude. The crew provided mithril for an acoustically enhanced voice-pipe system that would allow messages and warnings throughout the vessel. The town could also expand the dormitories, though it had no entertainer to spare for the requested common-room role.

Lodestar then brought further divination. Emperor Shen was resting and weakened, apparently lacking access to magic he had previously possessed. Imperial troops were falling back, the navy was retreating, and the eastern war was abating. She believed that even a longer stay would not worsen matters before the next moon. She did not know the reason for the emperor's weakness.

The report gave them confidence to remain while the work was completed. In three days, townspeople installed the voice pipes and tripled the bunks. Domyx helped carry heavy components, and the Indorra children joined the communal effort. Eleven local deckhands volunteered, were assessed for suitability, and joined the ship. Together with earlier recruits, the assorted deckhands now numbered twenty, in addition to the named crew. Red Caesar requested common red shirts for the new arrivals.

Vokenar used the interval to heal townspeople and established Southport as a holy return point. The companions' recent experience and recovery also brought them to level 13, with their new capabilities still to be developed.

#### Choosing the next expedition

The pause left several paths open: Boril Erendel's interference with rebirth, the eastern fiend in the pyramid, and the eventual descent through [[The Funnel]]. The party also recalled the promise concerning [[Yalet Mora]]'s brother. None of those remembered obligations was completed by the Southport work.

They chose to begin the next journey at The White Drake, where they could see Obould and Lady Jacinthe. Carrying The Opal inside the egg would make a teleportation-circle journey there practical, followed by sailing toward [[Tome Keeper's Pyramid]]. The intended sequence then continued to elven territory and Boril Erendel, whose laboratory was now understood to be beneath Brimbolyn rather than at The Garden.

The session ended with the ship improved, the local plague's source removed, and the chart complete. The planned departure had not yet been played through, and Lodestar's respite did not mean that Starfall or the Demi-Spell danger had disappeared.

### Summary

- Mayor Yoris explained Southport's repeated reinfection, dwindling food supplies, and reliance on Deep Roses and the Garden's network.
- Red Caesar reunited with Lodestar, who warned the party to save Greater Restoration for someone beyond its own members.
- Janeera's summoned spirit connected the contagion to Ninki Nanka's reaction with fallen Penumbra and described the failed expedition carrying the other chart portion.
- Vokenar's Legend Lore identified Ninki Nanka as a former defender of Windsurf, supporting an attempt to help it.
- The party funded Heroes' Feast by selling Boston Golf's automaton to his granddaughter. Red Caesar took Green Slaadi Whiskey, and Kerben received Crone's Contempt.
- With the crocodile accompanying them, the travelers endured the Mana Sea's sickness, recovered Rahmadi's cloak, ruby, mithril, and diamonds, and survived poisonous fungi and a bird swarm.
- They defeated a drow cultist and one drider; the other fled. Ceril detected a psychic command extending to an unseen master.
- They destroyed three moss golems, then freed enthralled Kuo-Toa rather than pursuing the released servants.
- Ceril exposed the Aboleth with Starry Wisp. Domyx restrained it and freed Kerben, who killed it.
- Ceril reached Ninki Nanka within a protective sphere and removed the dragon's curse. The poisonous outflow stopped, and the dragon departed.
- The party recovered the missing chart, a flight potion, diamonds, and Lenth the Rugged's tomb. Domyx received the remaining two Southport potions.
- Red Caesar decoded the full Chart of the Witness; it provided surface geography and possible digging clues, without a direct Axis Mundi entrance.
- Southport improved The Opal's communications and dormitories and supplied eleven new deckhands. Vokenar healed residents and established a return sanctuary.
- Lodestar reported an unexplained imperial weakening and retreat. The companions planned to visit The White Drake, then the eastern pyramid and Boril Erendel.

The expedition resolved the difference between treating illness and ending its source. The Deep Roses remained valuable, but reinfection had made a sustained recovery impossible. Janeera's knowledge, Vokenar's lore, and Lodestar's warning redirected the party from simply confronting a dangerous dragon toward freeing an afflicted protector. Ceril's restoration accomplished that purpose, while the others' scouting, protection, and endurance made his approach possible. The dragon's gratitude and departure were established; its later work was left open.

The swamp also separated the plague from a distinct exercise of control. The Aboleth had drawn drow from their usual refuge and used the Kuo-Toa as coordinated servants. Discovering that influence let the party release many of its opponents and confront the controller directly. Its appetite for memory made the fight invasive without erasing the companions' pasts, and Domyx's rescue of Kerben turned that knowledge into a decisive opening.

Southport's response gave the success lasting practical consequences: renewed relationships, a recovered world chart, a stronger ship, and more people willing to join it. Those gains did not instantly cure every resident or reveal the route beneath Stark. The chart still required interpretation, the Funnel still required work, and Lodestar's account left the cause of Emperor Shen's weakness unknown. At the close, the companions had time and resources to pursue the pyramid and the corruption of rebirth while the town began its slower recovery.
