# Session 015

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_015/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Kerben's time aboard the ship

While his companions completed their visit to [[Continental Stark]], [[Kerben]] remained aboard [[The Opal]], fishing and talking with [[Obould]]. Their conversation considered whether the ship's new purpose, pursuing [[Penumbra]] and following instructions from the stars, offered something better than their earlier piracy. Kerben remembered the world before the rising waters and described the opportunities created by its upheaval: familiar places had been broken apart, new islands had appeared, and each voyage could reveal something different.

Obould acknowledged the practical advantages their newer companions had brought. [[Ceril]]'s negotiations had secured legitimate permission to sail in waters previously closed to them. Yet the captain could not personally hear the gods who supposedly directed the mission. He likened them to the unseen wind filling a sail, something he hoped was real while having to trust the people who claimed to understand it.

[[Raxxy]] called from the lookout when a vessel with red sails and black markings approached. Elven patrols inspected it before allowing it onward. Once it came alongside The Opal, a well-dressed goblin in a tall black hat crossed the gangplank and introduced himself as [[Gunk Grodley]] of the [[Goblin Traders]]. He invited Kerben to inspect their wares, including goods that would be illegal within elven lands.

The traders sold magical ammunition, accessories, potions, and scrolls. Gunk Grodley directed Kerben to his wife in the hold for poison-making. Kerben had begun learning the craft but had not yet made a poison himself. Working with her equipment and preserving ingredients, he turned his recovered wyvern glands into [[Wyvern Poison]], trading other goods and gold for the assistance. The result could be applied to a weapon or ammunition for a later attack.

He also acquired [[Lolth's Sting]], a dark, oily poison that weakened its victims and could render them unconscious. In exchange, he gave the merchant his bulette eggs. She wanted to raise the creatures as shipboard defenders, imagining them as appealing animals rather than considering the danger their growth posed to a vessel. Kerben warned her about that risk but made the trade, since he could not realistically raise them aboard The Opal either.

Gunk Grodley then offered the [[Subpoena Deuces Mercator]], a reusable magical summons that would call the Goblin Traders to find its user at sea. It could carry a short request so that they knew what supplies to bring, though their arrival would still take several days. Kerben acquired it with a valuable item from the shared hold. The summons offered a way to trade without returning to port and a possible means of seeking assistance if the crew were stranded. The goblins departed before the other travelers returned.

#### A new crew member and two private commitments

The returning party shared the results of its visit to [[Brimbolyn]]. Its agreement with [[The Order of Seasons]] had left room for both sides to work, without making them firm allies. [[Brim the Bullywog]] leaped aboard and introduced himself to Kerben as the newly hired tinkerer. Happy to leave the elven jungle, he demonstrated his leap again by reaching the crow's nest directly from the deck.

The crew reconsidered its planned circuit. Cutlass Cray had been intended as a shopping stop, but recent trades had reduced that need. They also chose to pass [[The White Drake]] rather than call on [[Lady Jacinthe]] while her request concerning two beach trolls remained unfinished. [[The Garden]] offered more immediate business: the request to visit the [[Rakshasa]], concern about [[Boril Erendel]], and the possibility of another Penumbra fragment. Forecasts prepared by Ceril and Kerben still favored the wider circuit, and the ship set a course toward the Garden.

During the first evening, [[Yalet Mora]] invited [[Red Caesar]] to drink in the mess and discuss his visit to [[Yalet Aurum]]. He confirmed that the golden-headed figure was his older brother. Galeb Duurr shaped bodies for themselves from stone; even Yalet Mora's present body was not the one with which he had begun. He described his brother's superiority as bullying and an act, while Red Caesar admitted an interest in the gold enclosed within the monument.

The two agreed to grow stronger and eventually confront Yalet Aurum. Yalet Mora wanted to defeat him and claim his golden head as his own, turning their rivalry into a shared prospective venture. They toasted the plan. No confrontation or transfer of the gold took place during this conversation.

On the second day, Obould summoned [[Domyx]] privately to his quarters. He distinguished Kerben's expertise as first officer and the quartermaster's management of the crew from the responsibility of giving the whole ship direction. If the captain died, he wanted a successor who could negotiate, understand the crew, and lead it toward a purpose. He designated Domyx as the person who would inherit The Opal.

Obould connected that decision to the hidden relative Domyx sought. He had heard that the ancestor might be involved in the Cataclysm and suspected that he knew who the person was, but did not disclose an identity. He stressed that he could not foresee the future and was not planning to die. He was preparing for uncertainty and ensuring that Domyx would have a ship for the search.

The captain also credited Domyx's arrival as the beginning of the crew's new direction, followed by Ceril's guidance through the stars and [[Vokenar]]'s conversations with the gods. Before that, Obould had lived more in the moment, hoping simply to improve his ship and recruit a more impressive crew. He now believed that he already had the best of both and intended to follow the course the party chose. Domyx's succession was recorded in the captain's logbook to settle any dispute after his death; Obould remained in command.

#### The boarding alarms

Later that night, Kerben and Red Caesar woke together as several of the alarms they maintained around the ship sounded at once. The simultaneous warning was more concerning than the occasional alarm triggered by a wandering crew member or animal. Kerben chose to investigate before raising a general alert, hoping to identify anyone already moving about rather than obscure that information by waking everyone. Red Caesar accompanied him toward the top deck.

Gunshots sounded before they reached it, waking the others. Above, Raxxy was wounded and holding an unconscious crew member, while another hid nearby. Hobgoblin boarders had spread across the deck. At the bow, an injured Obould was tending another fallen deckhand under the threat of several guns. The intruders included well-armed veterans and a first mate whose clothing indicated rank. Once Kerben and Red Caesar appeared, the attackers turned their attention toward them.

Red Caesar opened the defense with fire, scattering several boarders. The strengthened, laminated deck installed to carry [[Kilosaurus]] could withstand that blast better than the ship's earlier structure. Kerben confronted the first mate and nearby pirates, but was injured as the others emerged from below. The enemy vessel had pulled clear to starboard after its gangplank was removed.

Red Caesar enclosed Obould and his patient in a Resilient Sphere. The captain embraced the deckhand so that both would fit within its protection, giving him time to save the crew member's life. Kerben withdrew toward Raxxy, who borrowed his pistol while helping the wounded. Ceril and Vokenar joined the defense with magic, and Domyx struck a veteran through the deck into the mess below, damaging the ship while eliminating the attacker.

Kerben noticed that the deckhand beside Raxxy was still alive but bleeding out. He healed her and told Raxxy to take the injured below, then check for further casualties. The captain likewise stabilized his patient. Red Caesar released the sphere so that Obould could carry the deckhand into his quarters. The crew had recovered from the surprise, but the last boarders were still fighting on deck.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_015/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### The last boarders and the pursuit

[[Red Caesar]] blasted one remaining veteran overboard with Thunderwave. The first mate was still standing. [[Kerben]] considered killing him or disabling him for a bargain, while Red Caesar suggested that a survivor could carry stories of the failed attack and discourage future boarders. Kerben was concerned that the other vessel might still contain enough pirates to return and proposed taking it rather than merely sending its crew away.

[[Ceril]]'s guiding light and [[Domyx]]'s hammer brought the first mate down, leaving him unconscious and wounded. [[Obould]] returned to confirm that none of The Opal's crew had died, a better result than their earlier encounter with pirates. He proposed putting the surviving attacker on a raft with a mark identifying The Opal, leaving his survival to the sea and using him as a warning. The party did not establish his later fate.

[[Vokenar]] healed Kerben and Domyx, and Ceril treated Obould before the pursuit. The captain asked them to recover the enemy vessel without destroying it if possible. They boarded the [[Gheister]] and crossed rough water as the rain intensified. The smaller craft was better suited to rivers than to the open storm, but the crossing was short enough to manage.

Ceril saw no hobgoblins on the enemy ship's upper deck. Its furled sails and strange heading suggested that it was drifting rather than being actively piloted. Nevertheless, he heard slow, deep breathing from below. The party secured the Gheister and boarded, then descended toward the hidden occupant.

#### The Warg Lord

Below decks stood the [[Warg Lord]], an enormous gray-furred creature with a bear-and-wolf body and a goblinlike face. It rushed the newcomers before they could question it. Ceril's Moonbeam stripped away the goblin features, revealing a more wholly bestial form beneath them.

The creature appeared both angry and afraid, like a cornered animal. Its howl shook the vessel and cracked the boards, filling the party with a terror that was more than ordinary sound. Red Caesar collapsed unconscious, and Kerben and Domyx struggled against the fear. Vokenar revived Red Caesar through his healing aura, allowing him to support Domyx with hastening magic while their companions continued the fight.

The Warg Lord dissolved into sheets of rain and re-formed among them. It declared in Fae that the ocean offered only fear and that the Cataclysm would swallow the party as it had swallowed the creature. Its poisonous bite made the struggle more dangerous, while its shifting form made the cramped hold difficult to escape. Vokenar's protective radiance and Ceril's healing kept their companions alive.

Kerben moved back to find room for his gun. Despite the lingering terror, he steadied himself and placed a shot through the creature's forehead. The Warg Lord fell, trying and failing to utter another howl. The party had overcome the vessel's apparent leader and could now inspect what it had captured.

#### The problem of another ship

After tending their injuries, Kerben and Red Caesar searched the galley. Some crates had gone overboard when the Warg Lord's howl shook it; the party recovered them through cantrip magic and found chunks of gold inside. The vessel was considerably smaller than The Opal and had no papers, identifying colors, or useful log. Its existing Fae name referred to the end of the world.

Ceril suspected that the hobgoblins had taken it from earlier owners in much the same way they had tried to seize The Opal. The party found no record that identified those owners or established their fate. Taking possession was possible, but operating another vessel presented a practical problem: their available crew could not sail all their ships effectively.

The nearby White Drake offered a possible place to seek aid, sell the galley, or arrange its care. The party also considered using Lady Jacinthe's banner to call League vessels to them. Kerben was uneasy about the unknown earlier ownership and about returning to the League before completing its request. Red Caesar argued that salvage rights governed such claims and that the League would not necessarily demand restitution. The debate remained open as they considered whether to sell the vessel, tow it, or preserve it for later use.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_015/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### A concealed reserve and the Garden's fleet

The party chose to keep the captured galley as a reserve rather than sell it or stretch its crew across a third vessel. [[Red Caesar]] suggested the name [[The Hideous Truth]]. His knowledge of the nearby islands directed the search, and [[Kerben]] and [[Domyx]] found a jungled grotto with canopy extending over the water. Besides concealing the ship from passing vessels, the cover would help hide it from imperial aerial reconnaissance.

Kerben gathered foliage and cut material to lay over the hull. [[Ceril]] questioned the need to cut down trees, but the group completed the concealment and marked the location for a possible return. Kerben saw a spare ship as useful protection against the damage The Opal so often sustained. The party did not yet have the people or resources to make the captured vessel part of its active fleet.

After the crew recovered from the attack, it again voted to continue directly to [[The Garden]] and pass The White Drake. More vessels gathered around the Garden than on the earlier visit. A departing supply boat carried seeds, plants, and livestock toward a settlement. The druids were expanding their work beyond the sanctuary, helping surviving communities grow crops and sustain themselves.

The party divided its business there. Kerben investigated the trade routes and cargo of the growing network. Vokenar searched for Penumbra. Red Caesar went to see Boril Erendel, while Ceril and Domyx first sought the Rakshasa in the inner caverns.

#### The Rakshasa's confinement

The [[Rakshasa]] received Ceril and Domyx in a comfortable, silk-draped chamber, smoking from a hookah. He recognized Domyx's connection to [[Clan Akathia]] and said that he had visited its lands and met Domyx's father. He had once been eager to travel, but now seemed content to wait as the waters rose more slowly.

Ceril said that they had come on behalf of [[Keys Caeradel]]. Mention of his son drew a strong emotional response before the Rakshasa turned away to hide it. He suspected that Keys Caeradel's mother might also be involved. He doubted that their Demi-Spell would work and described both as too stubborn to be argued out of it. Ceril explained that the party wanted to make the spell unnecessary.

The Rakshasa connected the world's eventual end with Genesis Mundi and wondered whether that renewal might offer him atonement. He did not care greatly when it came. His more immediate problem was that he could not safely visit his family. Other beings had escaped from hell alongside him, and he was charged with destroying them so that their souls would return to the underworld.

He had exposed that purpose to his enemies, who would unite against him if they detected his movements. Leaving the Garden could bring the threat to his family as well. Waiting for the demons to come within his reach had achieved little, and he could not openly seek them.

He therefore asked the party to find and kill the demon who kept the [[Tome of Satariel]]. Through the book, that enemy could watch his movements and summon the others against him. Both concealed themselves, each knowing that a mistake might expose them to destruction. Removing the watcher might at least allow the Rakshasa to visit his son, though it would not discharge his entire duty against the escaped demons. Ceril accepted the search, and Domyx welcomed the prospect of confronting a fiend.

#### Theotropa's bargain

Kerben's questions brought him to [[Theotropa]], who explained that the Garden had begun building a fleet of its own. Cyan-and-green banners distinguished its vessels. Supplies and livestock could now reach more communities, but the dangerous waters might force the druids to seek protection from [[The League of New Stark]]. Feeding people could become more costly if that aid required payment.

Vokenar's locating magic led him to a Penumbra fragment hidden behind Theotropa's chambers. She admitted that it had recently come into the Garden's possession and was nearly drained. The druids had studied whether its remaining power could improve their cultivation, but she hesitated to use a resource whose source and cost she did not understand. If its energy came from the sky, drawing more from it might merely take strength away elsewhere.

Vokenar asked what would persuade her to give it to the party for the repair mission. Theotropa trusted divine voices in principle, but could not hear the conversations on which their claimed instructions rested. She wanted tangible progress and greater safety for the druid waters before placing much hope in the undertaking.

She offered a specific task. A [[Storm Phoenix]] to the south had lost its senses after the Cataclysm and needed to be destroyed so that its elemental power could be reborn. Its original form was an amber core, to which it would return upon defeat. The party should cast that core into the sea rather than bring it back. She would know when the task was done and would give them the Penumbra in exchange. Vokenar accepted and also pledged what help the crew could provide against threats encountered near the Garden.

Theotropa asked about communities that might need supplies. Vokenar mentioned the people at [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]], whom the ship could not visit constantly. She was unwilling to commit a route into potentially dangerous waters, but might reconsider when their safety improved. No regular delivery agreement was established.

#### Boril Erendel's proposed bodies

Red Caesar found [[Boril Erendel]] working on a spell in a room with a view over the islands. Ceril joined him after the Rakshasa meeting. Boril Erendel said he preferred this setting to the Academy's constrained working arrangements, which left little room for art and poetry. The paintings nearby reproduced landscapes without much life, but he valued having time for pursuits beyond research.

He explained that his separation from the other researchers was also ideological. The Demi-Spell recycled existing elements and forms. Where Keys Caeradel allowed some randomness in the next world, Boril Erendel wanted stricter control. Away from their supervision, he could develop additions that he believed would remove evil more thoroughly.

His intended change was that every spirit should be born into a perfected high elf body. He regarded other forms, including dwarves, orcs, and humans, as inferior accidents of birth. Giving everyone the same powerful, magical form would, he claimed, remove the differences that produced fear and war.

Red Caesar challenged the assumption that an imperfect researcher could be trusted to decide what perfection meant. Boril Erendel insisted that he had done the work and that no purer form existed. He said he had already created thousands of empty high elf bodies, ready for spirits to inhabit in the next world. The visitors did not inspect or independently verify that claim.

Red Caesar said he would pass the plans to Keys Caeradel and seek his response. If Keys Caeradel rejected them, Boril Erendel was prepared to proceed on his own, though he hoped the tiefling would be more receptive than House Kiirnodel. Red Caesar remained outwardly civil, but afterward admitted that he had barely resisted setting the office on fire.

Out of earshot, Ceril called the researcher a poisonous influence whose involvement magnified the Demi-Spell's dangers. The visit had exposed more than a disagreement about saving the existing world: a member of the project intended to impose a single ancestry on all its future inhabitants. Neither visitor acted against him during the meeting.

#### A proposed reversal and Crone's counsel

Ceril called the group together and proposed changing the purpose of its search for the book-keeping demon. Rather than eliminate the Rakshasa's watcher to enable a reunion, they could try to work with that enemy to capture the Rakshasa. A captive father could become leverage to force Keys Caeradel to halt the Demi-Spell. Ceril also raised assassination if necessary to secure that result.

Domyx was surprised by the turn, although he agreed that locating the demon was an initial step whichever purpose they chose. Vokenar opposed the Demi-Spell but feared that such a plan could turn the existing accord into open war with the elves. Ceril believed the conflict already existed in substance. Red Caesar argued that ignoring the threatened renewal also carried consequences. They sought divine advice before undertaking the proposed coercion.

Theotropa provided a room for the night. Under the full moon, Vokenar called [[Crone]]. To his sight, the walls peeled away toward a vast moonlit opening, and the goddess entered in chainmail, dragging a blood-soaked greatsword. Exhausted from battle, she leaned onto a bed. His companions could not see her, but real blood soaked the bedding and later marked her passage across the floor.

Crone reproached Vokenar for drawing her away from increasingly fierce battles. Asked whether they had time to continue the Penumbra search before the Demi-Spell was completed, she said they did, but warned that dying was a greater danger than simply running out of time. They remained weak and needed to grow. The spell's power exceeded even the goddesses' scope, and she condemned mortal ambition that reached beyond any willingness to accept limits.

Vokenar also asked how much Penumbra was needed to restore the sky. Crone explained that [[Aeris]] could heal her own wounds as a living body could, but the damage had left her too weak to do so. They did not need every fallen piece, only enough power to restore that capacity. Crone could not tell him the necessary amount.

When he raised the plan to capture or kill the Rakshasa, she called using him as a bargaining piece clever. The demons' struggle belonged to the wider blood war of hell, and she advised approaching their watcher through force rather than expecting reasonable cooperation. Sparing its life could become part of a bargain to lead it to the Rakshasa. She described the Rakshasa as weakened by life on Stark and accustomed to deals in place of the violence of hell. Her advice supplied a possible method, not an accomplished alliance or captive.

Vokenar relayed the answers to his companions and used their final question to ask whether the elves could be trusted to refrain from renewal if the sky were repaired. Crone said no. In her judgment, they wanted to witness Genesis Mundi and inherit the next world rather than leave the present one to a new generation. The warning challenged the party's confidence in the accord, but remained her assessment of the elves' intentions.

Vokenar apologized for taking her from battle and handed back her sword. Crone accepted the apology and left, with the blood remaining visible to the others. Afterward, the group considered the proposed leverage again. Vokenar urged that they first fulfill Theotropa's bargain and obtain her Penumbra peacefully, before any action involving the Rakshasa might change the Garden's attitude toward them.

The party rested and reached ninth level. Domyx developed more forceful strikes that could drive enemies back or impede their movement. The spellcasters and Kerben also considered how to extend their capabilities before the next voyage. No kidnapping, assassination, or bargain with the Rakshasa's enemy took place that night.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_015/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### New capabilities and the southern search

As the party completed its preparations, [[Kerben]] continued developing his rogue training and agility. [[Red Caesar]] gained Circle of Power, Bigby's Hand, and Teleportation Circle. The last allowed him to use the encodements received at [[House Kiirnodel]]. He also learned circle destinations at [[The White Drake]] and the dwarvish market in [[The Pit]]. Although he could now return to report [[Boril Erendel]]'s plans, he chose to delay that visit.

[[Ceril]] prepared Commune with Nature for the search south of [[The Garden]]. Rather than relying on a nearby creature alone, he could draw on the collective knowledge of plants, animals, and the landscape. Over several days, the ship entered humid tropical waters amid an archipelago whose submerged land made navigation difficult.

Through the spell, Ceril learned where the shallow waters lay and where fresh water could be found on nearby islands. He also discovered abundant plantains and stopped to collect some. The knowledge might prove useful to the Garden's supply work as well as his own gathering of life from different regions.

The same communion tracked a powerful flying creature whose movements were far faster than the ship. Trees and animals directed him toward a bird that appeared to become lightning as it passed from place to place. Flashes between the clouds slowed into a circling shape, then leaped toward nearby land before the [[Storm Phoenix]] settled on The Opal's crow's nest. It was aggressive and prepared to attack.

#### The lightning's release

[[Vokenar]] tried to draw the creature's attention toward an empty portion of deck and away from the crew. Its attacks could send lightning arcing between people standing near one another, making separation a protection for the group. [[Domyx]] used the [[Iklwa Isondo]] for the first time in battle, pulling the flying phoenix nearer the deck with its returning trident. Red Caesar tried to enclose it in a Resilient Sphere, but it escaped before the magic could hold it.

Kerben watched the intervals when the phoenix re-formed between its lightning movements and placed shots into its body. Ceril demonstrated Freedom of the Winds, using sailcloth to take to the air in a birdlike flight. When the creature attacked him, he vanished in streaks of light and returned to the deck, ending that flight to evade it. Vokenar joined the struggle on his angelic wings and left a guardian's radiance beside Red Caesar, discouraging the phoenix from striking there.

Domyx kept the creature close enough to engage, then drove it away again to avoid the electrical jolts that traveled through his weapon. Red Caesar's missiles broke more of its form apart, releasing feathers and amber dust. Kerben's final shot brought it out of the air. Its body dispersed, leaving a single amber core on the deck, where a bolt of lightning struck as it landed.

Kerben wanted to retain the core and perhaps raise a phoenix rather than cast away a valuable creature. Vokenar insisted on fulfilling Theotropa's instructions, both to obtain the Penumbra and to avoid provoking their hosts before deciding what to do about the Rakshasa. The core's future hatching was uncertain, and Red Caesar considered a phoenix a dangerous shipboard companion. The others took the amber from Kerben and threw it overboard. He watched where it went, but did not retain it.

As soon as the amber touched seawater, it became a vertical lightning bolt that shot into the sky and split in many directions. The power dispersed into the heavens rather than remaining as an egg to recover. Ceril connected the release to the weather of recent decades: lightning had become increasingly rare, even while rain and snow continued. Destroying the phoenix's concentrated form had returned some of that elemental balance to the wider world.

#### Theotropa's reward and another watcher

Several days later, the party returned to the Garden. [[Theotropa]] already knew that the task was complete. Lightning had struck one of its woodlands, and the returning storms brought the possibility of wildfires and the new growth that followed them. She explained that this particular phoenix had not been meant to retain the elements in its body; the lightning was freer in the heavens. Its madness and formation had been part of the disruption caused by [[The Cataclysm]].

She gave the party the Penumbra as promised. During their absence, someone had apparently searched for it from the air, concealed by a cloudlike appearance and traveling on a device. Her magic had sensed the searcher's purpose, but she had not identified the visitor. The fragment's protection against detection had prevented its discovery. Vokenar suspected a scout of the [[Broyish Empire]], connecting the account with the airborne reconnaissance they had already encountered.

Theotropa offered to reserve any further Penumbra she acquired for the party. She had no wish to drain its unexplained power, and their success gave her reason to keep it from other seekers. Vokenar renewed his promise to address threats to the Garden's waters when the crew encountered them. The relationship had gained a practical basis in fulfilled work, while a regular supply route to the lagoon remained unconfirmed.

The party considered where to go next. Returning to [[Castle Kaedon]] was the clearest continuation of the repair effort. They discussed the ancient black dragon [[Xarag]], whose earlier attack had cost them their treasure, but did not attempt that confrontation. They also considered looking west for [[Southport]], both to learn more of the map and to bring [[Rochella Golf]] toward the family she hoped to find.

### Chunk 0004

[[chunks/session_015/chunk_0004|Source transcript]]

#### The western route and the lagoon

The ship made an arc toward the possible location of [[Southport]] while continuing toward [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]]. The party did not find the landmass or see enough traffic to guide it there, and a larger detour would have delayed the return. [[Kerben]] used the supply-route information obtained from [[Theotropa]] to establish the general direction for a later trip. [[Rochella Golf]] remained with the crew; the journey had not reached her destination.

After roughly two weeks at sea, the party arrived at a settlement that had grown during its absence. Huts now housed the people left there. An oil-burning alarm triggered a chain of lights leading into the settlement, and its defenders approached armed and mounted on [[Kilosaurus]]. [[Alamar]] recognized the returning friends and dismounted. They had been gone longer than he expected, and [[Lyngbakr]] had finished digesting their earlier Penumbra feeding weeks before.

The party brought the new fragment to the feeding chute rather than leave it unsecured. Alamar had fully excavated the passage and surrounded it with the blue, ever-burning lights made during the earlier visit. The Penumbra was sent down into the water for Lyngbakr. The settlement's improved system made the repeated feeding easier to carry out.

Alamar demonstrated another defense by turning a crank that raised a canvas dome covered with foliage. From overhead, it could make the inhabited area look like an ordinary part of the island. The party reported the apparent airborne search at the Garden, reinforcing the value of hiding from imperial scouts.

The mention of the Garden surprised Alamar. A druid sanctuary had existed in his own time under a kindly woman also named Theotropa. [[Vokenar]] told him that a woman of that name still led it and explained the unsuccessful attempt to arrange supplies for the lagoon. Alamar's recollection suggested a connection across the ages, but the conversation did not establish how the two accounts related.

#### The castle rises again

With the additional feeding, Lyngbakr rose farther. The party could see the bases of the turrets explored before, more of the island beneath them, and separate pillars of land ascending around the main mass. A much larger portion of [[Castle Kaedon]] was now accessible.

Alamar sensed fiends and undead within it, describing a return of the evil he had known there in childhood. [[Red Caesar]] asked why those forces occupied his former home. Alamar replied that the castle's curse was literal, as was the burden of curses that had followed his own life.

He traced much of the history to [[Queen Caeradwyn]] and her long ambition. Her death had not ended her influence. Followers had continued in her name, and women had repeatedly claimed to be her reincarnation. He described a madness spreading from [[Brimbolyn]] into the other lands and wondered whether the Cataclysm had in some sense been beneficial. Vokenar challenged that thought on behalf of the many people it had killed.

Red Caesar asked Alamar to accompany them and help undo the curse. Alamar declined, believing himself more likely to hinder than aid the expedition. He would remain to protect the lagoon and conceal it if another scout passed overhead. His mood was more subdued than on the previous visit, even as his work had made the settlement safer.

The party left The Opal and took the [[Gheister]] toward the newly exposed castle. Lightning, restored through the phoenix's release, flashed behind its silhouette. The session ended on that approach; the next expedition inside had yet to begin.

- The party identified a general route toward [[Southport]] without reaching it, returned to the expanded lagoon, fed [[Lyngbakr]], and set out toward a further-risen Castle Kaedon.

The session developed the ship as both a community and a resource. Obould's succession decision recognized Domyx's capacity to give it direction, while the defense showed how healing and protection could preserve the people behind that future. The captured galley offered a new asset, but the decision to hide it acknowledged the limits of their current crew. Brim the Bullywog's arrival and Kerben's trading expanded what they could attempt without instantly resolving those limits.

The Garden tested the accord established in Brimbolyn. The Rakshasa's request began as a way to reunite a family, then became a possible instrument of coercion after Boril Erendel revealed his ambition for the next world. Ceril and Red Caesar saw the proposed uniformity as a more urgent reason to stop the Demi-Spell. Vokenar shared that opposition while questioning the consequences of turning a tenuous agreement into war. Crone's counsel made leverage seem possible and challenged trust in the researchers, but did not turn the proposal into a completed act.

The phoenix task supplied a different kind of restoration. Returning its lightning to the wider world fulfilled a bargain, improved trust with Theotropa, and demonstrated a repair that preserved the existing world. Kerben's desire to keep the creature brought that collective benefit into conflict with his interest in a powerful companion. The party chose the release and acquired another fragment without violence toward the Garden's inhabitants.

By the close, the lagoon had become a defended settlement and the castle had risen farther, but the mission was still incomplete. The amount of power Aeris needed was unknown, the Rakshasa remained where he had been, Boril Erendel's plan had not been reported back or stopped, and Rochella Golf had not reached Southport. The party's immediate choice was to continue exploring Castle Kaedon, while several newly clarified moral and personal commitments remained unresolved.
