## Session 015

### Chunk 0000

While the rest of the party completes business in elf country, [[Kerben]] remains aboard [[The Opal]] with [[Obould]]. They discuss the crew's new direction, the rising oceans, and whether the ship's divine errands and Penumbra hunting are better than old piracy. A red-sailed goblin vessel approaches after passing elven inspection, and Kerben meets [[Gunk Grodley]] of the [[Goblin Traders]]. Kerben trades for poison work and acquires [[Wyvern Poison]], [[Lolth's Sting]], and the [[Subpoena Deuces Mercator]], a reusable way to summon the goblin traders at sea.

When the party returns from [[Continental Stark]], [[Brim the Bullywog]] joins the crew as tinkerer and lookout. [[Yalet Mora]] privately tells [[Red Caesar]] that [[Yalet Aurum]] is his older brother and pitches a future plan to train up and defeat him. [[Obould]] privately summons [[Domyx]] and names him heir to [[The Opal]] if Obould dies, recording the decision in his logbook.

### Chunk 0001

That night, hobgoblin pirates board [[The Opal]]. [[Raxxy]] and [[Obould]] are injured in the initial attack, but the party rallies and drives the boarders off without losing crew. [[Obould]] proposes sending the surviving hobgoblin adrift as a warning and sends the party aboard the [[Gheister]] to chase the hobgoblins' second ship.

The second vessel appears adrift. Below decks, the party finds the [[Warg Lord]], a massive fey creature with a bear-wolf body and goblin-like face. [[Ceril]]'s Moonbeam strips away the goblin features, revealing its true beast form, and the party destroys it.

### Chunk 0002

The party renames it [[The Hideous Truth]] and hides it in a jungled grotto under foliage because they do not yet have enough crew to operate a third ship.

The party sails to [[The Garden]], now busier and more organized as a druid supply hub for nearby settlements. [[Domyx]] and [[Ceril]] meet the [[Rakshasa]] in the inner sanctum. The Rakshasa recognizes Domyx's Akathian heritage and reacts emotionally when Ceril says he came on behalf of the Rakshasa's son. He explains that he is trapped by a duty to hunt escaped demons and asks the party to find and kill [[Farraday]], who keeps the [[Tome of Satariel]] and can track him if he leaves hiding.

[[Kerben]] investigates the Garden's supply routes while [[Vokenar]] finds a drained [[Penumbra]] chunk in [[Theotropa]]'s possession. Theotropa agrees to give it to the party if they destroy the [[Storm Phoenix]], an elemental being driven mad by [[The Cataclysm]]. Meanwhile, [[Red Caesar]] and [[Ceril]] visit [[Boril Erendel]], who reveals a plan to use the [[Demi-Spell]] to reincarnate all spirits into high elf bodies. Red and Ceril are horrified by the plan and its implications.

That night, [[Vokenar]] summons [[Crone]] under the full moon. Crone appears exhausted and blood-soaked. She confirms there is still time to stop the [[Demi-Spell]], but warns that death is the greater danger. She reveals that [[Aeris]] can heal the sky herself if given enough power; the party does not need every [[Penumbra]] piece, only enough to restore Aeris's strength. Crone also warns that the elves will not abandon the Demi-Spell even if the sky is repaired.

### Chunk 0003

The party hunts the [[Storm Phoenix]] south of the Garden. [[Ceril]] locates it through Commune with Nature, and the party destroys it aboard [[The Opal]]. Its amber core is thrown into the sea, becoming a lightning bolt that returns lightning to the heavens. When the party returns, [[Theotropa]] confirms storms have returned to the region and gives them the drained [[Penumbra]]. She also warns that a [[Broyish Empire]] scout searched for the Penumbra while the party was gone.

### Chunk 0004

The party considers future routes, including [[Southport]] for [[Rochella Golf]], but continues toward [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]]. [[Kerben]] uses Theotropa's supply-route information to identify Southport's general direction for later travel.

After roughly two weeks, the party returns to [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]]. The settlement has grown, and [[Alamar]] has built alarms, a feeding system, and a camouflage dome to hide the island from aerial reconnaissance by the [[Broyish Empire]]. He reveals that a druid garden also existed in his own time under a woman named [[Theotropa]], suggesting a deeper connection across ages.

The party feeds the drained [[Penumbra]] to [[Lyngbakr]], raising more of [[Castle Kaedon]] from the sea. More turrets and surrounding pillars emerge, exposing twice as much castle to explore. [[Alamar]] senses hellish and undead forces within and traces the castle's curses to [[Queen Caeradwyn]]'s ambition and bloodline magic. Alamar stays behind to guard the lagoon while the party takes the [[Gheister]] toward the risen castle.

### Summary

- [[Kerben]] acquires [[Wyvern Poison]], [[Lolth's Sting]], and the [[Subpoena Deuces Mercator]].
- [[Brim the Bullywog]] joins [[The Opal]], and [[Obould]] names [[Domyx]] his heir to the ship.
- The party survives a pirate attack, defeats the [[Warg Lord]], and captures [[The Hideous Truth]].
- The [[Rakshasa]] asks the party to destroy [[Farraday]]'s foothold so he can safely visit [[Keys Caeradel]].
- [[Boril Erendel]] reveals his plan to reincarnate all spirits as high elves through the [[Demi-Spell]].
- Destroying the [[Storm Phoenix]] restores lightning and earns [[Theotropa]]'s drained [[Penumbra]].
- Feeding [[Lyngbakr]] raises more of [[Castle Kaedon]], and the party departs to explore it.

The party's growing network brings new crew, a captured ship, and a clearer route toward restoring the sky. At [[The Garden]], the Rakshasa's confinement and Boril Erendel's forced-reincarnation plan expose personal and ideological conflicts within the Demi-Spell project. [[Crone]] confirms that [[Aeris]] can heal once sufficiently restored, but warns that the elves will continue pursuing their reset. The victory over the Storm Phoenix advances the party's alternative, providing the Penumbra that opens more of Castle Kaedon.

### Connections

- [[Yalet Mora]]'s proposed feud with [[Yalet Aurum]] links the party's new crew to its earlier elven journey.
- [[Keys Caeradel]]'s request to see his father becomes a hunt for Farraday and the [[Tome of Satariel]].
- Boril Erendel's revelations deepen the disagreement over the Demi-Spell established at [[The Academy]].
- The expanded [[Lyngbakr Lagoon]] and newly raised castle continue the party's efforts to repair the [[Hole in the Sky]].
