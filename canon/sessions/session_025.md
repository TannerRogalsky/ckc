# Session 025

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_025/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Red Caesar's work at the Academy

While his companions traveled north to rescue [[Ulrich Fjoller]], [[Red Caesar]] pursued the possibility he had raised before their separation: manufacturing [[Penumbra]] and altering it to sabotage [[Starfall]]. He could travel between [[Broy]] and [[The White Drake]] through their teleportation circles. For the research itself, he returned alone to [[House Kiirnodel]] in [[Brimbolyn]] and sought the facilities of [[The Academy]]. The account followed his work through the northern expedition and the eleven days his companions subsequently spent returning south.

Red Caesar's hypothesis drew on both elven research and his upbringing in [[Heaven's Bulb]]. Maintaining the compound's machinery had taught him how pressure could hold back enormous quantities of water. He now suspected that Penumbra was an exceptionally dense form of [[Akasha]], produced naturally where the Mana Sea pressed against the sky beneath [[Arkadia]]. If he could reproduce that compression, a small existing fragment might help initiate the process. He considered dwarven machinery, but the Academy offered the magical expertise and experimental equipment he needed.

[[Keys Caeradel]] was working alone when Red Caesar arrived. Keys Caeradel initially hoped the visit meant Red Caesar had accepted the [[Demi-Spell]], but Red Caesar still wanted an alternative to replacing the world. The two could nevertheless cooperate. Keys Caeradel's project needed Penumbra to accelerate [[Genesis Mundi]], and a reliable means of producing it would serve that purpose even if Red Caesar intended a different use. Keys Caeradel agreed to provide laboratory access in exchange for their sharing the method and dividing production equally.

The laboratory already held Akasha, but containing it was a problem in its own right. Ordinary vessels could not reliably restrain the substance: it might evaporate, pass through matter, or deaden the energy around it. Special metal-tempered glass provided insulation. Keys Caeradel also explained why naturally forming Penumbra was so difficult to imitate. The dense material resulted from immense pressure, and even an entire lake of liquid Akasha might yield only a small piece. The Academy could collect the Akasha that fell with rain and direct it into a reservoir, giving their experiments the volume they required.

Red Caesar disclosed his second aim rather than concealing it from his collaborators. Genuine Penumbra could help the elven project; a separately altered sample could poison the Empire's device when it drew on the material's energy. He did not intend to contaminate the elves' own supplies. Keys Caeradel recognized both the danger to years of Demi-Spell work and the advantage of weakening [[Broyish Empire]] before it further damaged the sky. Reports of growing military tension and clashes across the strait gave that concern urgency.

[[Illidrielle Gandara]] joined the discussion and proposed a division of work. Red Caesar would solve the synthesis, Keys Caeradel would alter the resulting material, and Illidrielle Gandara would conceal the alteration with illusion. They could oppose the same threat without agreeing about the world's eventual future. Red Caesar remained determined to preserve the existing world, while the elves still saw new Penumbra as a resource for the Demi-Spell.

#### Penumbra and Antumbra

Red Caesar used a resilient magical sphere as a sealed experimental boundary and adapted enlargement and reduction magic to compress what lay inside it. Their trials examined pressure, volume, containment, and temperature rather than simply shrinking an ordinary object. With Keys Caeradel's assistance, Red Caesar eventually concentrated a lake's worth of Akasha into a small sphere. The material became jet black, with the familiar stars visible within it: they had successfully made Penumbra.

The breakthrough came after roughly ten days of work. The collaborators called the process the Caesarean method. It offered a repeatable way to manufacture the scarce material, though it depended on substantial facilities and a large Akasha supply. Their estimates concerned production over several days; they had not created a portable device that could immediately make unlimited stones.

Only after producing genuine Penumbra did Keys Caeradel and Illidrielle Gandara prepare the sabotaged sample. The alteration remained separate from the material reserved for their own work. They named the result [[Antumbra]]. Its danger lay in an unstable condition within the compressed Akasha, concealed beneath an apparently ordinary exterior. Drawing on its energy was expected to release that instability into the consuming system, behaving more like a destructive infection or internal collapse than a conventional poison.

Red Caesar expected Antumbra to damage Starfall, but neither he nor his collaborators had tested it in the Empire's machine. He had not yet heard [[Ceril]]'s account of the divine prisoner inside that device. The research established a means of sabotage, not proof of its eventual effects. Red Caesar thanked the elves and carried the prepared sample back to The White Drake. After he left, Illidrielle Gandara privately remarked to Keys Caeradel that the synthesis might be the best development the Demi-Spell could have hoped for. That conversation was presented apart from Red Caesar's knowledge.

#### Crew and reunion

During the return voyage, [[Kerben]] continued managing [[The Opal]] as acting captain and found work for its new passengers. [[Trent Indorra]] had sailed on Broyish vessels and understood rigging, repairs, and the demands of shipboard work. Although modern cannons were newer to him, Kerben appointed him master gunner. [[Ebbie Indorra]] could tend injuries and poisoning, and the crew agreed to provide space for a sickbay. She also offered care for the pregnant [[Lorelai Lapis-Acathian]].

Ulrich Fjoller lacked sailing experience, but could keep records and update charts. [[Domyx]] helped him learn the ship's routines, while Lorelai Lapis-Acathian wanted useful work rather than simply remaining a passenger. These arrangements gave the rescued couple a place aboard the Opal. A dependable powder monkey was still needed; Kerben treated keeping the cannons' powder dry and safe as a serious responsibility. The large triceratops remained in the lagoon with [[Alamar]], rather than accompanying the northern voyage.

At The White Drake, the five companions reunited and Red Caesar showed them Antumbra. Kerben also reported [[Theo Harvey]]'s escape to [[David Harvey]]. He did not reveal the private bargain by which he had helped the prisoner leave. David Harvey regretted losing the captive and his information, and no bounty was paid. Nevertheless, Theo Harvey's departure removed the Empire's immediate foothold on the island, making it possible for the Harengon to reclaim their warrens. David Harvey intended to send people back and deal with Theo Harvey himself later. He also challenged Theo Harvey's account of [[Jack Harvey]], insisting that his father had been trying to save the world.

The party needed a route from their single altered stone to Starfall. The White Drake served as a diplomatic headquarters, with frequent visiting ships and increasing military traffic, rather than an ordinary trading port. Waiting to identify one particular delivery might consume time they did not have. [[Vokenar]], accompanied by Ceril, used locating magic to find a small stock of Penumbra in the ship's hold. Since the League's supply was being traded to the Empire, placing Antumbra there offered another approach.

Red Caesar tried magical alarms as a way to monitor activity, but a mage dispelled them. Vokenar proposed planting Antumbra directly among the stored stones. Red Caesar and Ceril hesitated about leaving its timing to others. [[Obould]] remained a hostage, and Ceril knew that the endangered piece of [[Aeris]] inside Starfall was more than an impersonal source of power. Sabotage might help them, but they wanted guidance about the risks before sending it beyond their control.

#### Divine guidance and Vokenar's departure

Vokenar consulted [[Sigil]]. She told him that Obould was in less danger than appearances suggested and that [[Vizier Jade]] had further tricks concealed within the situation. Her answer complicated the hostage crisis without explaining those tricks or guaranteeing Obould's safety. Waiting to produce more Antumbra was possible, but it was not the course the party immediately took.

[[Crone]] pressed Vokenar to return to training. She warned that he would fail in the coming battle unless he accepted the preparation she demanded, even if it kept him away for weeks. The sisters' disagreement was presented privately. Vokenar left to train with Sigil and Crone before the others began their southern errands; his companions did not receive a clear account of where he had gone or when he would return.

Ceril then sought Aeris through communion. Rather than appearing only as the weakened mortal prisoner of his earlier encounter, her presence emerged amid darkness and stars. She spoke Ceril's name, and he questioned her about the party's choices. Aeris indicated that Vokenar was safer away from the party for the moment, that intercepting a delivery would not be quick enough to matter, and that using Antumbra would not harm the trapped seed of herself. She urged them to pursue Red Caesar's plan and confirmed that Obould was alive. Her presence faded before the surrounding stars collapsed again.

Ceril conveyed those answers to the others. They now had a reason to act despite uncertainty about precisely when the Empire would receive the stone. The guidance addressed the danger to Aeris and the need for haste; it did not show them the eventual operation of Starfall.

#### Planting Antumbra

Red Caesar made Kerben invisible, with Antumbra concealed inside his coat. Domyx drew attention in the mess hall by talking openly about Titan culture and its failures. His audience gave Kerben some cover, but two guards still stood outside the locked hold. Kerben needed a separate distraction to move them.

He positioned [[Zulu]] at a different locked doorway, leading to the meat stores, and used his curator's keyring to open it with Knock. Zulu acted as though he had broken through the door himself, then began eating the stored meat. The apparent escaped dinosaur drew the guards away from the actual hold.

Kerben picked the hold's lock and opened the door only far enough to reach inside. The Penumbra lay beyond a barred division. He threw Antumbra toward it; the stone struck a bar with an alarming noise, but landed inside beside the other pieces. He had successfully placed the altered material with the League's supply. He had not witnessed a transfer to the Empire or established when Starfall would consume it.

To finish the diversion, Kerben revealed himself and acted as though he were bringing his unruly companion under control. [[Damien Ouranous]] restrained Zulu and criticized Kerben's handling of the animal, contrasting it with his own smaller triceratops. Damien Ouranous required Kerben to help clean the raided stores and said he would send an invoice for the meat. Kerben helped with the cleanup, returned Zulu safely to the Opal, and told Red Caesar that the planting had succeeded.

#### The priests of Otyugh Isle

After the sabotage, the Opal sailed for [[Otyugh Isle]]. Five days later, with nine days remaining before the predicted opening of [[The Carrot Cake]], the party returned to the [[Temple of Sigil]]. It still held twelve petrified priests and nineteen petrified Kuo-Toa. Their frozen poses preserved the interruption of their conflict by the cockatrice the party had previously encountered.

Ceril, Red Caesar, and Domyx examined the priests' clothing and religious equipment to identify someone likely to help restore the others. Red Caesar recognized signs of abjuration, and the group selected [[Father Warrick]], who appeared to be a senior cleric. Ceril freed him with Greater Restoration. Father Warrick's ash-pale complexion initially resembled the stone around him, but he was alive and could speak.

Father Warrick had survived [[The Cataclysm]] before leaving [[Southport]]. He and his fellow priests had taken vows of poverty and sought a peaceful home among the island's druids. Kuo-Toa had arrived to claim the same temple, and the cockatrice had interrupted their confrontation. Father Warrick had lost decades to petrification; he had not been turned to stone before the Cataclysm. He wondered whether he should have stayed in Southport with [[Janeera]], where they had once had a good life together.

Using his own restorative magic, Father Warrick freed several more priests. The scene did not establish that every priest or any Kuo-Toa had yet been restored. He intended to reopen the temple and contact Southport, while eventually awakening the Kuo-Toa one at a time. He hoped to teach them about Sigil and believed that people could change. The party questioned how he would handle renewed hostility, but his proposed gradual restoration remained an intention.

Red Caesar asked whether the freed clergy would travel aboard the Opal. Ceril insisted that they first be told about [[Mana Sickness]] in Southport. On learning of the plague, Father Warrick wanted to hurry to the mainland and help its people. Ebbie Indorra hoped they could save her children, whose illness had driven her and Trent Indorra to search for [[Deep Roses]].

Trent Indorra and Ebbie Indorra left the ship with the priests on Otyugh Isle, near the route back to Southport. Trent Indorra proposed teaching Ulrich Fjoller the gunner's work before leaving; the conversation did not establish a completed transfer of that office. [[Rochella Golf]] also chose to go with them. Southport had been her intended destination since joining the crew after the Cutlass encounter, and she had only now heard about the plague. She thanked the party and departed to seek her home. Their departure did not yet show arrival in town, reunion with the Indorras' children, or a cure.

#### Growth aboard the Opal

The party rested and advanced in their abilities. Kerben's growing wilderness training made him a stronger swimmer and climber and strengthened his animal companions. Domyx gained the endurance to keep fighting through injuries that would ordinarily bring him down, a development connected to his recent ordeal inside the remorhaz. Ceril acquired Transport via Plants, Heroes' Feast, and Sunbeam. He considered the new light magic in terms of drawing sunlight through the world's broken sky.

Red Caesar added True Seeing, Contingency, and Tasha's Bubbling Cauldron to his repertoire. The cauldron gave him a new way to supply the group with potions, beginning with a batch that conferred giantlike strength. Its products would be replaced when he created a new batch. He planned to fashion the gilded ladle required for this magic from the gold recovered from [[Yalet Aurum]]'s head.

#### Preparing for the southern waters

Red Caesar fashioned the gilded ladle from Yalet Aurum's gold. He also prepared a likeness of himself with amethyst eyes for Contingency. A sapphire was set aside for possible future use with Drawmij's Instant Summons; that spell remained a future prospect rather than an ability he had already learned.

The Opal sailed south through the archipelago. Ruined human structures and old lighthouses recalled the world Red Caesar had first glimpsed when leaving Heaven's Bulb. The compound itself lay farther south, beyond the portion of the sea they were now exploring. Ceril's new plant travel offered a future means of reaching places such as the gardens he had tended, but the group continued this journey by ship.

Following directions from [[The League of New Stark]], they approached the siren's waters near two sharp rocks, one bearing a carved woman's figure. Sailors associated the place with wrecks, maddening song, storms, and lightning. Red Caesar used his cauldron to provide water-breathing potions for the four companions and a possible additional helper. Vokenar remained away at his training.

Underwater, the party found a dense garden containing the Deep Roses the Indorras needed. Ceril recognized the plants, encouraged their growth, and gathered a supply before the confrontation. Finding the medicine advanced the effort to help Southport; its collection did not itself cure anyone there.

#### The Bane Siren

Red Caesar's locating magic guided the group through the submerged vegetation. Kerben and his shark went ahead, with Ceril and Domyx following and Red Caesar keeping a protected position behind them. The [[Bane Siren]] was bound to the garden in a manner resembling a dryad's connection to a tree. The flooding of the Cataclysm had twisted and enlarged the creature into its present threat.

Domyx feared the possibility of being turned against his companions more than the danger to himself. The siren's silver-eyed gaze did charm him, preventing him from attacking her directly. It did not give her control over the entire party or cause Domyx to assault his friends. Instead, while caught by the enchantment, he helped Kerben's shark hold the siren in place.

Kerben's shark, Sierra, maintained her grip despite the siren's efforts to escape. Kerben's improved swimming let him fight effectively beneath the water. Red Caesar raised Circle of Power to protect his companions against the creature's magical influence, but the siren's song operated differently from that magic. Ceril and Red Caesar resisted its lure while Ceril called a crab companion and fought in his archer constellation.

The siren repeatedly tried to break free and renew her enchantments. She recognized Red Caesar as a powerful human and marveled that her magic could not harm him. Kerben and the shark kept her occupied while Ceril provided healing and the others wore down her resistance. Kerben finally killed the Bane Siren with Urumi. Her final shriek failed to overwhelm the group, and Domyx's temporary attachment to the creature ended without his having harmed a companion.

Ceril returned to gathering Deep Roses after the fight. The nearby wrecks had long since been stripped of other valuables, but he found a dead sailor holding a damaged locket containing lapis lazuli. The sailor appeared to have drowned after being lured into the garden. The locket went into the ship's stores; the account did not establish that the body was recovered aboard.

#### Witness's abandoned refuge

After six days of sailing and rest, only three days remained before the predicted opening of The Carrot Cake. The party reached the [[Southern Archipelago Castle]] described by [[The Garden]] as the former home of [[Witness]]. The construct had mapped [[Stark]] before the flood and later lived with humans who sheltered in this castle. Something had driven him out.

Red Caesar supplied stronger healing potions during the voyage. The four companions and Zulu entered the castle through waters fouled by a red algal bloom and the smell of decay. Inside, the human-scale passages forced Domyx to stoop. Black, sootlike pollen hung in the air, and bodies of different ages lay among damaged walls. The dead showed injuries from concentrated heat and crushing blows.

Red Caesar initially considered whether a beholder might explain the destruction, but the evidence did not settle that theory. The cramped rooms and absence of other kinds of magical injury gave them reasons to doubt it. The victims' wounds would find a different explanation when they encountered the castle's surviving occupant.

Using [[Beryzoz's Teeth]], Red Caesar questioned a corpse in Southern, his native language and the language the dead inhabitant understood. The corpse said a new, wicked construct had appeared, killed the people there, and driven out their ally Witness. The inhabitants had taken shelter after the flood because Red Caesar's people had been unable to help them.

The corpse indicated Red Caesar's aspirant earrings when identifying Heaven's Bulb. Asked what had happened, it said the compound's people had tried to help but had been too weak. This was testimony of a failed rescue rather than a refusal to assist. Red Caesar shared the information about Witness and the murderous construct with his companions, but withheld what he had learned about his own community. The party had no confirmed location or fate for Witness.

#### The chart and the poisoned air

Kerben opened the armory with his keyring and found fifty pounds of mithril. The castle's inhabitants had retained the valuable metal while using steel weapons. Their ruined refuge suggested they had resisted the attacker, though the group had no full account of that battle. The mithril was recovered for the shared hold.

As the party moved farther inside, the black pollen caused painful headaches and an increasingly oppressive psychic effect. Zulu collapsed, and Kerben restored his companion. Red Caesar repeatedly used Gust of Wind to clear the air, while the group relied on their healing preparations to continue toward the inner rooms.

The clearing wind revealed the [[Chart of the Witness]]. It occupied its full surface, but showed only part of the information required to read the complete map. The missing portion appeared to require a matching chart or overlay brought together by magic. The party had recovered an incomplete representation, rather than a sheet simply torn in half. They intended to keep it with their maps aboard the Opal.

Kerben also found an unopened [[Potion of Fluid Adamantite]] among bottles left by the inhabitants. Red Caesar identified it as a powerful means of resisting harm and accepted it when Kerben offered it to him. The potion remained available; it was not consumed during the ensuing battle.

#### PAXO

A cloaked metallic humanoid ambushed Kerben and Ceril from behind. Its smooth, reflective faceplate and fluid movements distinguished it from an ordinary armored person. The construct identified itself as [[PAXO]] and announced that remaining lifeforms had to be eradicated. Its heat beams matched the concentrated burns found on the castle's victims.

Ceril raised a wall of fire to protect himself and Kerben while they drew back toward Domyx and Red Caesar. PAXO endured the flames and tried to disable the spellcasters. Domyx engaged it with his hammer, while Red Caesar caught it in Bigby's Hand. The conjured hand had the appearance of crackling mechanical force, and its grip bent the construct's metal body.

Ceril used his newly learned Sunbeam, flooding the confined room with light and temporarily blinding PAXO. The beam also scorched the hand holding it, but the restraint lasted. PAXO retaliated with heat and a supernatural slowing effect. One beam badly burned Ceril's neck and face; his constellation-assisted healing restored him, and the apparent scar unwove as the stars gathered around the injury. Domyx rallied the others with his battle cry while Kerben and Zulu pressed the attack.

The construct repeatedly tried to escape Red Caesar's grip, but the group's combined pressure broke it down. Kerben wedged his shark-tooth weapon beneath the faceplate and pried it loose. At the same moment, Bigby's Hand crushed the exposed body into a twisted mass of metal. PAXO was destroyed, ending the threat that had killed the refuge's inhabitants.

Kerben and Red Caesar examined the recovered faceplate. It was an unfamiliar alloy resembling mercury or quicksilver combined with gold, silver, and other fine metals. Electricity could alter its shape and appearance, producing effects resembling illusions without magical casting. The party stored it as a potentially valuable curiosity. PAXO's creator, origin, and reason for coming to the castle remained unknown.

On the way out, Zulu drew Kerben to a side room they had overlooked. It held a treasury of one hundred pounds of [[Esperanto]] coins, apparently saved for a time when the old world's money might again be useful. The coins joined the mithril, chart, faceplate, and other recovered goods in the Opal's hold.

#### The next destination and an earlier conversation

The party continued toward [[Xarag's Island]], where the black dragon [[Xarag]] had attacked the Opal at the beginning of their voyage. They wanted to recover the treasure lost under Obould's command as well as settle that earlier encounter. The three-day journey brought them to the island at the date when The Carrot Cake was predicted to become accessible. Its opening was understood from their information, not observed directly at this location. The companions rested before the next confrontation.

The session ended with a flashback to Red Caesar's youth inside Heaven's Bulb. [[Master Lee]] asked what Red Caesar hoped to become once he no longer needed the compound and it no longer needed him. Red Caesar described life there as being part of a machine, valued for what one could produce. Outside, he wanted to understand how magic, people, and society fitted together.

Master Lee asked whether he would still want that knowledge if it revealed a broken world or truths too difficult to bear. Red Caesar compared understanding the world to understanding the gearbox that kept a pump running. He did not require the world to be beautiful; he still needed to know how it worked. Master Lee said Red Caesar had learned everything he could teach him and expressed his pride.

Above them, the compound's ceiling had been built to imitate an outdoor sky. A crack Red Caesar had watched widening over days and weeks now admitted a beam of real sunlight. The scene belonged to his earlier life, closing the session with the same drive to understand that had informed both his experimental work and his private encounter with the dead refugees.

### Summary

- Red Caesar, Keys Caeradel, and Illidrielle Gandara developed a method of manufacturing Penumbra, then prepared Antumbra as a separately concealed means of sabotaging Starfall.
- Aeris supported the plan and said it would not harm her trapped seed. Sigil complicated the party's understanding of Obould's captivity, while Vokenar left for training with Sigil and Crone.
- Kerben used invisibility and a diversion with Zulu to plant Antumbra beside The White Drake's stored Penumbra. Its delivery and use by the Empire were not witnessed.
- Ceril restored Father Warrick, who freed several more priests. The clergy, the Indorras, and Rochella Golf left toward Southport, with the plague still unresolved.
- The four remaining companions defeated the Bane Siren and gathered Deep Roses. At Witness's former refuge they recovered his incomplete chart and destroyed PAXO.
- Red Caesar privately learned that Heaven's Bulb had tried and failed to help human flood refugees. The party recovered mithril, a lapis lazuli locket, PAXO's faceplate, Esperanto coins, and the Potion of Fluid Adamantite, which Red Caesar received.
- They reached Xarag's Island as The Carrot Cake's predicted opening date arrived. A closing flashback connected Red Caesar's desire for understanding to Master Lee's teaching.

The synthesis changed what the party could attempt. Penumbra had been a scarce resource they hoped to retrieve or redirect; Red Caesar's work made it something that could be produced, with consequences for both the attempt to repair Stark and the elves' competing plan to replace it. Cooperation with Keys Caeradel and Illidrielle Gandara therefore served immediate common interests while preserving their disagreement about the Demi-Spell. The altered sample gave the party a way to strike at Starfall without openly supplying the Empire with usable power.

Divine guidance eased their fear for Aeris, but the sabotage's practical outcome remained beyond their sight. Obould was alive, Vizier Jade's intentions were still opaque, and Vokenar had accepted training under a warning about the next battle. The remaining four companions proceeded with the southern obligations they could address directly: restoring the petrified clergy, obtaining the Indorras' medicine, and searching for Witness's map.

Those errands brought both help and loss into sharper focus. Father Warrick returned to a world changed by decades of absence, while the medicine for Southport remained aboard the Opal rather than in the hands of its patients. Witness's refuge revealed people who had survived the flood only to be murdered later. Their testimony also challenged any simple account of Heaven's Bulb's conduct. Red Caesar kept that discovery private even as he continued to act on the curiosity and responsibility Master Lee had encouraged.

The session closed with new supplies, one incomplete chart, and the knowledge that Witness had been forced away rather than confirmed dead. The Opal was at Xarag's Island, the lost treasure remained to be recovered, and the predicted opportunity to enter The Carrot Cake had arrived elsewhere.
