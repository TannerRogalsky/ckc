# Session 028

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_028/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### Scouting the electrical district

The party remained in the [[Hole Shebang]] entrance chamber, where [[Red Caesar]] had learned the portal's sigils. Beyond the barred service doors lay another part of [[The Carrot Cake]]. Their immediate task was to find how this district could illuminate one of the four lamps sealing [[Jack Harvey]]'s inner grounds.

Red Caesar sent an Arcane Eye through a small opening used by the electrical wiring. Following narrow passages, it emerged into sunlight beside a sandy, partly pebbled shore. Rock overhangs enclosed stretches of the coast, while colorful birds and dense vegetation occupied the island's interior. [[Ceril]] recognized the plants, and [[Kerben]] identified the local animals as subtropical. Together their observations suggested that this attraction stood well south of their familiar maps, perhaps near [[Esperanto]]. They had inferred a broad region rather than determined an exact location.

The eye followed wires along the cliffs and inspected a piano stage, barrels, and broken magical puppets. Those puppets had no obvious electrical connections; the attraction combined magical animation with its conspicuous electrical machinery. Elsewhere, rubber tubes marked what appeared to be a balloon game.

Wires branched through the island and converged beneath heavy brambles in a central rocky enclosure. There the installation's exposed poles repeatedly arced with electricity. A large lever stood beyond them. Red Caesar compared the arrangement to an irrigation system and proposed that the breaker might divert energy elsewhere, perhaps toward their lamp, rather than simply shut everything down. His scouting revealed no active enemies, but the abandoned-looking mechanisms gave little assurance that the district was safe.

#### Toys, ribbons, and living lightning

The companions opened the service doors and entered with [[Zulu]]. [[Domyx]] noticed a separate little island and a tower that the eye had not explored. Before they could investigate it, Ceril felt a gathering static charge like an approaching storm. The seemingly inactive scenery began to move.

A [[Roger Ribbons]] formed near the piano: a winged mass of string and twine, with a red skull and crosslike spikes. Animated toys spilled from barrels in two swarms, continually trying to repair their damaged bodies. A [[Lightning Elemental]] and a smaller [[Death Spark]] emerged around the wires.

Roger Ribbons caught Kerben with its threads, reeled him toward itself, and impaled him against its spiked head. Zulu chewed through the bindings and freed him. Red Caesar and [[Vokenar]] answered with fireballs, destroying one toy swarm before it could rebuild and burning away parts of Roger Ribbons' hairlike material. Domyx swept through the toys with his [[Gith Shard Glaive]], while Ceril brought Moonbeam down on them and healed Kerben.

The toys remained capable of repairing themselves under the moonlight. More dangerously, the electrical creature's discharge restored them instead of hurting them. The enemies' shared surroundings therefore supported their survival: leaving the living lightning active allowed it to replenish the damaged swarm.

An electrical pulse paralyzed Red Caesar, Vokenar, Domyx, and Ceril. Kerben, positioned away from them, remained mobile. Roger Ribbons bound the helpless Red Caesar and pulled him closer, while the Death Spark harried Kerben. Kerben used a Lesser Restoration scroll on Red Caesar, restoring his movement and freeing him from the bindings. Having weakened Roger Ribbons with his own attacks, Kerben left Red Caesar an opening to finish it with Magic Missile.

The creature collapsed into feathers, twine, thorns, and pearly bloodlike material. Its monstrous appearance concealed an assembled, animated body rather than an ordinary corpse. Domyx and Ceril recovered from their paralysis, and the companions turned against the remaining electrical enemies.

Kerben destroyed the Death Spark. Zulu bit the Lightning Elemental and suffered an electrical backlash, while Red Caesar's Mind Sliver helped expose it to Ceril's moonlight. Ceril dispersed the elemental with Moonbeam. Without its replenishing electricity, the remaining toy swarm succumbed to Kerben, Zulu, and the moonlight.

Afterward the party rested briefly. Vokenar used Prayer of Healing, and the others recovered before continuing. Their inspection of the piano and barrels found broken, inactive toy parts rather than another prize or useful mechanism.

#### The Sky High Striker and the Tower of Treasures

Domyx swam to the little island he had noticed earlier. Its Sky High Striker was a carnival strength test, with a seesaw mechanism, a tall scoring tower, and a bell at the top. Blue and white clouds decorated it, and the red-and-gold wooden mallet bore the park's forty-carat carrot emblem. Its sign offered one play per party, with better prizes for stronger strikes.

Domyx called the others over to watch and enlarged himself before wielding the mallet. His strike nearly reached the bell. Rather than resent falling short of perfection, he said the result left room for growth; a perfect score would have disappointed him by leaving nothing further to reach.

Confetti erupted, and a scroll materialized in a prize hatch. Red Caesar identified it and took custody. It contained Expeditious Retreat, Fireball, Phantasmal Killer, and Synaptic Static, together with a spell attributed to Melf whose full name was not established in the conversation.

The companions returned across the water. Domyx preferred swimming, while the others could use Vokenar's water-walking magic. Red Caesar proposed circling along the shore to reach the breaker from the other side before deciding how much more of the island to explore.

Along that route they found Al-Qadif's Tower of Treasures, an automated bottle-knocking game. A mechanical portrait explained that a single powerful strike could win a substantial gold prize. Kerben's rifle seemed well suited to the challenge. Red Caesar considered whether tampering with the electrical supply might improve their chances, but did not reroute the machine or carry out that proposed interference.

Kerben instead used the last of his [[Tatzelwurm Gizzard Juice]] on a bullet. He concealed himself beneath the water, then surfaced to take aim. The resulting cold blast cleared every bottle and earned the maximum reward: six portions of the park's gold, equivalent to the advertised three hundred pounds. The winnings went into the party's shared holdings.

With those games completed, the companions approached the electrical hub. They had identified a plausible way to advance the gate objective, but had not yet reached or moved the lever.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_028/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Redirecting the power

At the electrical enclosure, Red Caesar attempted to reach the breaker with Mage Hand. The corner concealed the lever from him, preventing the simple remote solution he had hoped for. He also tested whether Shocking Grasp might influence the arcs. It drew an electrical response rather than providing a safe way through; he could potentially take the discharge himself to give someone else an opening.

The companions considered other tools, including the immovable rods, but found no workable means of grounding or bypassing the installation with them. Domyx volunteered to cross it directly. He ran through the first arcs, endured a glancing discharge, and used Cloud Jaunt to finish the journey to the switch.

When he toggled the lever, the electrical arcs sputtered out and the lights went dark. Vokenar supplied magical light so the party could see. The result fit Red Caesar's theory that the power had been diverted, though they would need to return to the midway to confirm whether a gate lamp had actually lit.

#### The lute player and the second Roger Ribbons

Shutting down the electrical hazards did not disable every animated defender. More toys appeared, accompanied by another Roger Ribbons and two Death Sparks. A lute began to sound around a corner before a [[Mary Andrews]] construct joined the fight.

Domyx engaged Roger Ribbons with his hammer. Kerben kept his distance and shot at Mary Andrews, while Red Caesar drank a potion of increased speed and used Steel Wind Strike. The enemies' defenses prevented that opening assault from ending the confrontation. Ceril added guiding light and attacks from his starry form.

Mary Andrews' music could injure and charm listeners. Its performance was not itself a spell that Red Caesar could simply counter. When it tried to control Domyx, his protective pendant broke to defeat the charm. He spent that safeguard rather than relying on an enduring immunity.

Roger Ribbons then used its threads to draw Mary Andrews behind cover, protecting the musician from Kerben's line of fire. Domyx answered with [[Iklwa Isondo]], throwing the spear and hauling Mary Andrews back into reach. When the enemies tried to repeat the maneuver, he reeled the musician back again.

Red Caesar used the [[Wand of Blighting Bolts]] against Roger Ribbons. Its formerly vivid threads withered to gray, demonstrating the necrotic power he had acquired before reaching the park. Kerben continued shooting the lute player, and Zulu and Vokenar helped destroy one of the Death Sparks.

Roger Ribbons caught Ceril and dragged him toward itself. Domyx broke the hold by destroying Mary Andrews and then Roger Ribbons with his hammer. Freed from the threads, Ceril finished the toy swarm with starry arrows, and Domyx destroyed the remaining Death Spark.

Domyx tried to restore himself through his nimbus, but the healing was slight. Red Caesar gave him a spare healing potion, which he drank, and Vokenar also tended him. Domyx nevertheless asked them to leave him partly injured: he wanted to test the resilience that became important when he was pressed to the brink.

With the breaker moved, they could have returned to the midway immediately. Red Caesar raised that option, but Domyx strongly favored investigating the island's remaining attractions. The companions chose to continue without another rest.

#### Phase cats in the burned passages

Deeper inside, smoldering debris blocked parts of their route. The ruined toys and ambient magic coalesced into two [[Smoke Elemental]]s. Four [[Phase Cat]]s also appeared, moving through solid surfaces and hunting together. These cats seemed to be local wildlife that had moved into the abandoned park, rather than another set of Jack Harvey's manufactured performers.

Red Caesar struck several cats and one elemental with Steel Wind Strike. The cats retaliated by displacing their targets unpredictably, repeatedly teleporting him around the area and onto the sea. The party's water-walking magic prevented those sudden relocations from plunging him below the surface.

One smoke creature forced a poisonous tendril down Domyx's throat. Ceril used his ability to turn an ill omen aside, undoing one dangerous attack. Another burst of toxic smoke blinded Vokenar and killed two phase cats. The creatures sharing the encounter were therefore vulnerable to one another; their attacks did not form a coordinated defense of the park.

Kerben withdrew to maintain his firing position and killed another cat. Zulu attacked the remaining one. Ceril took on his starry chalice aspect and used Mass Cure Wounds. Spores and pollen became part of the restorative magic, and fresh grass and flowers sprang up through the burned debris. The healing reached the companions nearby and Zulu, but Kerben was too far away to benefit.

Domyx used the Gith Shard Glaive against the remaining cat and cleaved through the first Smoke Elemental, dispersing it. Vokenar tried to fight through his temporary blindness. Red Caesar then raised a powerful Gust of Wind against the surviving smoke.

The current battered and scattered the elemental rather than destroying it at once. It also pushed Vokenar, while the others braced themselves against it. The smoke tried to reach Red Caesar and choke him, but could not overcome the wind. The last phase cat continued teleporting Domyx and Zulu until Kerben shot it.

Ceril's starry arrows and Vokenar's deathly magic weakened the surviving elemental. Vokenar's sight returned, and Red Caesar swept the wind across the remaining smoke, finally blowing it out toward the ocean. The party continued along the crevasse without stopping to rest.

#### Pool and Smack a Bodak

An interior room offered a different sort of encounter: a friendly [[Magen]] beside a billiard table. It proposed a game of eight-ball and offered to match wagers. Vokenar accepted the game but put no money at stake.

While Vokenar played, Kerben investigated Smack a Bodak. Artificial, foul-smelling bodak heads rose from the machine for him to strike. Red Caesar used Guidance to sharpen his attention to the sounds and movements of the game. As it accelerated, Kerben took a second mallet and continued with both hands. He struck every head and won another six portions of gold for the shared holdings. The magical game still functioned despite the redirected electrical supply.

At the billiard table, Ceril discreetly helped Vokenar by using magic to tilt the playing surface. Even with that assistance, Vokenar lost. The Magen admitted that it had intended to lose deliberately and lure its opponent into a more profitable second round, but Vokenar's play had made even that difficult. They did not take up another game.

#### The southern stage

The companions proceeded toward the southern side of the island, where the balloon game and another stage remained unexplored. Domyx inspected barrels as they approached, but junk also rolled down from an overhang. Toy swarms spilled into their path, another Mary Andrews took the stage, and Death Sparks and a second [[Hooksy the Clown Automaton]] joined the ambush.

Red Caesar wore the salvaged Hooksy mask over his face. Its bloody, fleshy remains made the disguise grotesque, but wearing it did not prevent the island's defenders from attacking.

Domyx charged the new lute player with his hammer, knocking it down as the others engaged the surrounding enemies. The confrontation was still underway when the chunk ended.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_028/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Domyx tests his endurance

Kerben used the water beside the stage to maintain a distant firing position and shot into Hooksy's flesh beneath its metal covering. Ceril contributed starry light and his enchanted staff, but Hooksy caught him with its hooks and pulled him closer.

Mary Andrews attempted Tasha's Hideous Laughter against Domyx. Unlike the earlier musician's non-spell performance, this was magic Red Caesar could counter, and he stopped it. He also summoned Bigby's Hand to fight the electrical enemies and the musician.

Domyx rallied his companions. His encouragement helped Red Caesar resist Mary Andrews' charm and helped the conjured hand land a blow. Ceril destroyed another Death Spark, and Kerben's shots finished Hooksy.

The toy swarms crowded Domyx while he continued attacking Mary Andrews. He asked the others to let him reach the limits of his endurance rather than heal him immediately. Vokenar wanted to help, but respected that request and healed Red Caesar instead.

Twice Domyx was driven to the brink of collapse and remained conscious through his relentless rage. He used those sudden recoveries to intimidate Mary Andrews, whose head snapped around unnaturally as it screamed. The automaton remained dangerous and continued striking him, but its fixation gave Red Caesar's conjured hand opportunities to attack.

Having demonstrated his resilience, Domyx asked for healing. He then smashed Mary Andrews' head free, destroying the musician. Kerben and Zulu eliminated one toy swarm, with Zulu swallowing its last toy.

The remaining toys kept attacking. This time Domyx's endurance failed, and he fell unconscious. Red Caesar moved aside to leave an opening, and Vokenar used a fireball against the cluster. Domyx's protective laurels kept him from being burned even while he lay helpless among the toys.

Ceril broke apart one swarm with his enchanted staff. Kerben then shot the final toys, ending the fight. Before Domyx's condition could worsen, Ceril healed him and brought him back to consciousness. Domyx had survived two near-collapses and a later incapacitation; none of those moments established his death or a resurrection.

#### Recovery and the last attraction

The party took a short rest after the battle. Zulu recovered as well, and Vokenar gave Domyx additional healing. Domyx then drank the remaining healing potion Red Caesar had prepared for him earlier. This was separate from the spare potion Red Caesar had already given him at the breaker.

The companions discussed how much longer they could continue before a long rest. Ceril thought they could still handle further danger, while Red Caesar acknowledged that much of his more powerful magic was spent. He remained willing to accompany the group and contribute through the abilities he still had. They had not yet taken a long rest or committed to leaving the park.

Only the balloon attraction remained in this district. As Domyx, Vokenar, and Zulu rounded its corner, rubber seals over the tubes began inflating a row of balloons. Before anyone could play, darts detached themselves from the wall behind the visitors and flew toward the targets on their own.

The animated darts attacked the companions on the way to the balloons. Domyx and Zulu were struck by poison-bearing darts, but the Heroes' Feast they had shared before entering the park mitigated the poison. Vokenar escaped the shots aimed at him.

Darts that missed the visitors continued into the balloons and registered points with the machine. When the trap finished, it paid out two more portions of gold. The party added those winnings to its shared treasure, completing the island's remaining attraction through an involuntary, hazardous game.

#### One lamp lit

The companions returned through [[City Hole]] to the main midway. They wanted to verify that their work on the breaker had advanced the central objective rather than merely darkened the electrical district.

One of the four lamps beside Jack Harvey's sealed door was now lit. The result confirmed that redirecting the Hole Shebang's energy had supplied the gate. Three lamps remained to be illuminated.

The park also gave the party a plush bodak toy sprayed with the creature's characteristic smell, a parting souvenir of the games. No individual recipient was singled out.

They considered their next destination. Domyx wanted to be fully rested before attempting the [[King of the Hole]] arena. The [[Hole on Wheels]] train offered another possibility, but the [[Magic Hat]] was a hotel and might provide a place to stop and recover. Red Caesar supported that possibility, despite the reminder that their earlier encounter with a welcoming cottage had proved dangerous.

The party chose the Magic Hat. The session ended at the midway with that plan established, one lamp lit, and the hotel still ahead of them.

### Summary

- Red Caesar's Arcane Eye traced the Hole Shebang's coastal passages and electrical wiring to a breaker beyond hazardous arcs.
- The party defeated ribbon creatures, animated toys, living lightning, lute-playing automata, phase cats, smoke elementals, and another Hooksy.
- Domyx won a five-spell scroll from the Sky High Striker; Red Caesar identified it and took custody.
- Kerben spent his last Tatzelwurm Gizzard Juice to win the maximum Tower of Treasures reward, then won another substantial prize at Smack a Bodak.
- Domyx reached and toggled the breaker, shutting down the electrical arcs and diverting power toward Jack Harvey's sealed gate. His anti-charm pendant was consumed during the ensuing battle.
- Domyx tested his endurance in the final stage fight, eventually fell unconscious, and recovered through Ceril's healing.
- The balloon trap injured visitors but paid additional gold. The party also received a scented plush bodak souvenir.
- Back at the midway, the companions confirmed one lamp was lit and chose the Magic Hat as their next destination.

Hole Shebang revealed how entertainment, machinery, and danger had become inseparable in the abandoned park, The Carrot Cake. Games still recognized scores and dispensed prizes, friendly Magen remained willing to play, and living defenders activated among scenery that had appeared ruined. Even cutting the electrical supply did not end all the magic sustaining the district. The toys' repair, their replenishment by lightning, and the Smoke Elementals rising from burned debris made the environment itself part of the danger.

The companions nevertheless found a concrete way to advance their search for Jack Harvey's hidden sky. Red Caesar's scouting supplied the route and theory; Domyx's willingness to cross the arcs turned it into action; the illuminated lamp later confirmed the result. Their optional exploration yielded considerable gold and a scroll, while costing consumable safeguards and leaving them in need of a longer recovery.

Domyx's experiment with his own resilience also exposed its limits. His companions initially respected his wish to fight while injured, then cared for him when he finally collapsed. That recovery depended on ordinary healing after the enemies were defeated. The group ended the session together at the midway, with three gate objectives still outstanding and the hope that the Magic Hat could offer both progress and shelter.
