# Session 030

### Chunk 0000

[[chunks/session_030/chunk_0000|Source transcript]]

#### The fourth arena round

The companions resumed [[King of the Hole]] with three rounds completed. The apparent arrival announced as [[Kerben]] at the previous session's close was clarified as another [[Hooksy the Clown Automaton]], misidentified by the announcer. Kerben and [[Zulu]] had not rejoined the contestants; [[Red Caesar]], [[Vokenar]], [[Ceril]], and [[Domyx]] remained the four participants.

Vokenar's divinely aided rest had restored the group before this challenge. Red Caesar's Haste had ended, though his longstrider potion still helped him move around the island.

The fourth required round again divided the battlefield. On the northern side, Red Caesar and Vokenar faced a [[Gorgon Head]] and two [[Phase Cat]]s. Near the lighthouse, Domyx confronted three Hooksies while another Gorgon Head threatened Ceril above him.

Vokenar met the northern gorgon with his [[Moon Blade]]. Red Caesar joined him and used Vampiric Touch, draining vitality from the cats while maintaining the wards and shields that kept him in the fight. The combination gave him a way to replenish himself without abandoning the defense of their side of the arena.

At the lighthouse, Ceril summoned a flying bestial spirit in the form of a heron. The bird attacked a Hooksy and scraped away its porcelain mask. Beneath it was an eyeless, faceless mass of pulsing flesh, with no meaningful distinction between front and back. The increasingly varied encounters exposed how much living material these apparent automata contained.

Domyx chose an improvised weapon. He tore a carrot-shaped beacon from the ground, hurled its pointed stake through a Hooksy, and punched the impaled creature backward into the sea. The beacon worked loose as the creature fell. The announcer supplied crowd noises through its carrot microphone, though no actual spectators appeared.

The maneuver did not end the southern assault. Hooksies pulled Domyx back and forth with their hooks while the gorgon climbed toward Ceril's perch. Its gaze caught Ceril, and stone began creeping up his body.

Ceril used Dispel Magic before the transformation completed. Through his druidic magic, he drew the encroaching stone back toward the ground; a bloom appeared as the stone cracked away. Freed, he attacked the gorgon with starry light and directed the heron upward to help him.

#### Petrification and a dangerous descent

On the northern side, the other gorgon caught both Vokenar and Red Caesar in its gaze. Vokenar used Lesser Restoration on himself, then moved closer to his companion. Red Caesar chose to continue draining a cat and trust his own endurance. He resisted the petrification before it became complete, a result associated with the physical discipline he had learned alongside magic under [[Master Lee]].

The gorgon repeatedly threatened Vokenar with stone, forcing him to cleanse himself again. Their fight became a prolonged effort to hold out against its poisonous attacks while the cats displaced them around the ground.

At the lighthouse, Ceril's heron was destroyed by the southern gorgon. Domyx was also close to collapse under the Hooksies' combined attacks. His nimbus helped him endure, and he asked Ceril to let him use his resilience rather than immediately interrupt the experiment with healing.

Ceril called down Moonbeam against the gorgon, then drank his feather-fall potion and leapt from the lighthouse. The potion let him reach the ground safely and quickly enough to help Domyx. He brought the moonlight into the ground-level fight and changed to his starry chalice aspect to heal him.

Domyx had twice remained conscious through blows that nearly overwhelmed him. With Ceril now beside him, he destroyed the remaining Hooksies and turned his spear against the airborne gorgon. [[Iklwa Isondo]] pulled it lower, and his later throw finished it. None of those Hooksies released the nested offspring seen in the preceding round.

The northern battle continued after the southern threats were gone. Vokenar and Red Caesar eliminated the cats, but the gorgon still pressed them. Ceril and Domyx began moving toward their allies. Before they reached the fight, Red Caesar finally brought the head down, ending the fourth round while Vokenar remained dangerously wounded but conscious.

#### Choosing the bonus challenge

The group reunited on the pinnacle. They had completed the required arena sequence and earned access to its lamplighter.

The announcer offered the optional final challenge as a different contest: defend the hilltop together. All four would begin near its central beacon. Leaving the pinnacle's vertical space would bring psychic punishment, although flight directly above it was allowed.

Ceril immediately favored accepting the extra challenge. Vokenar proposed using the Beacon of Hope scroll to strengthen healing, but the group chose a short rest first, saving that option for later. They recovered on the hilltop before beginning the bonus round.

### Chunk 0001

[[chunks/session_030/chunk_0001|Source transcript]]

#### Defending the pinnacle

After the fourth victory, the central beacon's mechanism revealed the switch that could redirect the arena's power. The party could have used it and left. Instead, they prepared for the promised bonus.

Ceril enchanted his staff with Shillelagh. Red Caesar summoned Bigby's Hand and used it to press the single button needed to start this final challenge.

The [[Magen]] announcer cast Mirage Arcana. The ocean appeared to rise rapidly until it met the hilltop, leaving the contestants surrounded by water. They recognized the flooding as an illusion. Water Walk offered no route across an unreal sea, and crossing the imposed boundary would still subject them to the arena's punishment.

Two [[Shambling Mound]]s, four [[Death Spark]]s, and a [[Smoke Elemental]] attacked. The Sparks restored the Mounds with their electricity, repeating the replenishing combination from earlier rounds.

Domyx struck the vines and smoke with his [[Gith Shard Glaive]]. Red Caesar used Steel Wind Strike and his conjured hand, while Ceril's starry attacks and Vokenar's divine magic helped weaken the attackers.

One Mound failed to engulf Domyx, but the other pulled Ceril into its electrified vines. The Smoke Elemental tried to attack Red Caesar; Domyx intercepted it with a glaive strike and destroyed it.

Domyx then knocked a Mound off the pinnacle. The arena's boundary hurt the displaced enemy as well, though the creature climbed back into the fight. Red Caesar answered the clustered enemies with fire and a blow from Bigby's Hand.

Ceril could still use his staff from inside the vines, but his blindness and confinement hindered him. Vokenar used Burning Hands against the electrical enemies even though the blast also reached Ceril. Ceril accepted that risk as the group tried to remove the creatures replenishing the plants.

#### Three companions engulfed

The Mounds' attacks tightened the situation. Vokenar was pulled into the same mass holding Ceril, while the other Mound swallowed Red Caesar. Ceril and Vokenar found themselves briefly sharing confinement, recalling that they had been the first two companions to meet after the Cataclysm.

Domyx used Cloud Jaunt to exchange places with Ceril. Ceril appeared outside the vines, while Domyx entered the plant beside Vokenar. The Mound had made no fresh effort to restrain the substituted Domyx, and he could step back out rather than remain trapped.

Red Caesar escaped his own confinement with Misty Step. His blindness inside the Mound had not prevented him from directing Bigby's Hand, which continued striking the enemies.

Freed, Ceril destroyed one Mound with his enchanted staff. Vokenar remained inside the other and used Radiance of Dawn. Light burned through the tangled vegetation from within, reaching both the plant and the electrical creatures around it.

Domyx eliminated the remaining Sparks, ending their healing support. Red Caesar weakened the last Mound with Mind Sliver and the hand. Vokenar's radiance continued cutting through it.

When the Mound reached toward Ceril, Domyx struck it down. At the same moment, he grasped Vokenar's hand and pulled him to safety as the vines collapsed. The illusory water lowered, and the announcer proclaimed that the four contestants had won every challenge, including the optional bonus.

#### A prize and an urgent messenger

The companions asked for their additional reward. Offered wealth, fame, or power, they chose a magical gift for Domyx. Red Caesar suggested something that would help with his repeated brushes with collapse.

The Magen created the [[Hopping Mad Sash]] by tearing off its own rabbit tail, enchanting it, and presenting it deferentially. The resulting sash or championship belt bore a heart-shaped buckle and a rabbit tail. It improved the wearer's leaping and could release a forceful shockwave when the wearer was brought to the brink of defeat. It did not establish the guaranteed survival from a knockout that Red Caesar had initially proposed.

The party flipped the arena's lamplighter and returned to [[The Karat Cake]]'s midway. Three of the four gate lamps were now visibly lit. Kerben still had not returned.

Instead, [[Tango]] descended from overhead with a hurried note. Kerben had written only that they should speak with Tango. Ceril used Speak with Animals and relayed the bird's account to the others.

Several boats had attacked [[The Opal]] and nearly capsized it. Kerben had reached the ship in time to help the crew repel that first force, but more enemies were approaching. A larger [[Broyish Empire]] force could reach them within roughly a day. Kerben would remain aboard to hold the attackers back while the others continued.

Tango also described a consequence of the shattered moon. Every night had become dark enough for Imperial forces to try slipping past the elves and [[The Order of Seasons]]. Those allies were making efforts to keep the Empire at bay, but darkness complicated their defense.

The companions considered whether to stop and recover enough to gain further strength. Tango's report instead encouraged them to finish the remaining attraction and return to help the ship.

#### Jack Harvey's legacy and the train

They asked a Magen guide about [[Hole on Wheels]]. It described a scenic train traveling between mountain peaks above the [[Esperanto]] region, once giving passengers views of the desert city's minarets, lights, and surrounding landscape. Its route looped, allowing visitors to remain aboard for as long as they wished.

Red Caesar also asked what had happened to Jack Harvey. The guide knew that he had resigned and left the park and its stored possessions in [[Farraday]]'s care. Given the time that had passed and Jack Harvey's mortal nature, it supposed he was long dead. That was an inference, not an account of his witnessed death. Farraday, by contrast, was not mortal and did not age conventionally; the guide did not know his precise nature.

Vokenar used the Beacon of Hope inscription from the card-game prize, then healed the group with Mass Cure Wounds. Tango also benefited before departing. The companions entered Hole on Wheels with renewed health but limited magic.

They emerged from a painting inside a moving train. Other paintings in the same car depicted the park's districts and served as return portals. Through the door toward the caboose, the sea rushed past. The guide's old desert panorama had been replaced by open ocean after the flood.

Ceril sent his owl familiar outside to scout. It found seven cars moving in a broad loop over the water. The front car had a large glass pane, a frightened Magen conductor, and a horrifying vampire in control. Other vampire servants occupied the cars and exterior platforms.

The owl also found the final lamplighter among the controls at the front. The train was called the Quick as a Bunny Express. No Penumbra was identified aboard; this district's immediate purpose was to supply the remaining lamp.

The group considered taking different routes over the roof or through the cars. Remembering the arena's cost when they were separated, they chose to stay together and advance along the interior.

#### Opening the train fight

Red Caesar cast Protection from Evil and Good on Domyx before they proceeded. The spell would help protect their leading fighter from supernatural attacks and control.

Behind the first bar, Red Caesar found two tins of preserved carrot tea and a scroll bearing two Mass Healing Word inscriptions. He kept the scroll. The training at [[Heaven's Bulb]] had given him enough familiarity with divine magic to use it, even though the earlier prize's clerical spells were beyond what he could learn into his spellbook. The tea joined the party's shared treasure.

Domyx forced open a side door and encountered a cambion waiting to attack. Its screech drew revenants from farther up the train. The undead wore faded nutcracker-like coats with large gold buttons, perhaps remnants of an earlier role defending the train.

The cambion attempted to dominate Domyx, but Red Caesar's protection had already made the attempt ineffective. Domyx held the doorway with his hammer, while Vokenar and Red Caesar joined the attack.

Ceril summoned another bestial spirit, this time a large crab, and supported it with starry arrows. One revenant fell, but the cambion and the remaining attackers still contested the narrow cars as the chunk ended.

### Chunk 0002

[[chunks/session_030/chunk_0002|Source transcript]]

#### Blades through the train

More enemies moved down the train: vampire-linked human familiars, a vampire spawn, and another cambion. The first cambion attempted to banish Domyx. Hands reached up through a hellish opening beneath him, but he resisted with the help of Red Caesar's protective magic.

Vokenar had been watching the approaching enemies through the open doors. He raised Blade Barrier along the train's interior. Blades punched through the ceiling in sequence, opening skylights and tearing doors and frames apart. The spell changed the battlefield, forcing attackers toward windows, outer platforms, and the roof.

Ceril destroyed the remaining revenant, blasting it through a window into the sea. His crab helped finish a familiar. Domyx grappled another human familiar and forced him into the barrier while Red Caesar shocked him. Ceril questioned the brutality of what they were doing, even as the fight continued.

The enemies tried to escape the blades through the windows. The second cambion attacked with a fireball that injured Vokenar and also caught its fellow cambion. Vokenar lost the barrier, but its holes and shattered doors remained. Ceril maintained the crab despite further attacks on him.

The party used healing words to recover while continuing the advance. Ceril's light drove the first cambion from the side of the train, and the crab finished the familiar Domyx had restrained.

Domyx struck an attacker through a window and knocked him away into the sea, where the train's motion quickly left him behind. He also used Iklwa Isondo to pull a spawn down from the roof. The surviving cambion's attempt to dominate him failed against the same protection as before. The companions overcame the remaining guards, with Domyx driving the last attacker out through a ceiling opening.

They pressed onward without resting, keeping Ceril's crab and Red Caesar's protective spell active.

#### Memories of old Stark

Red Caesar found an old travelogue on a bench as they moved through the cars. It recorded journeys around [[Stark]], including Esperanto and Windsurf, with portraits and paintings of the places visited. He collected the surviving keepsake.

Farther ahead, he noticed a framed rendering of The Karat Cake's midway. It combined an architectural view with decorative imagery and depicted Jack Harvey's final achievements for the park. That artwork joined their shared holdings.

The remaining enemies had withdrawn toward the front. Its door was locked and barricaded. Domyx's first kick failed to break through, warning the defenders and giving them time to reposition. Further kicks finally opened the way.

The Magen conductor cowered beyond them, near the controls and lamplighter. The vampire lord waited with spawn and familiars.

#### The Nightbringer and the sunlight

The [[Vampiric Nightbringer]] had no face beyond a screaming mouth. White, angelic wings spread from its back, and its head moved in abrupt, unnatural shifts. Wounds released pale feathers rather than ordinary blood.

Domyx engaged the servants with his glaive. A spawn caught him, and the Nightbringer took advantage of his confinement to drain his vitality. The loss weakened him beyond the immediate injury and would remain until he could recover properly.

Ceril and Red Caesar understood that direct sunlight could suppress the vampire's regeneration. Vokenar used the Slow spell stored in his shield to hinder the spawn and lord, reducing the pressure on Domyx while the others sought a way to expose them.

Red Caesar examined the five-spell scroll Domyx had won at the Sky High Striker. He explicitly identified its previously incompletely named spell as Melf's Acid Arrow. Reading that inscription, he blasted through the front window. Wind drew shattered glass inward, frightening the conductor, while sunlight began entering the cabin and interfering with the vampires' recovery.

Domyx struggled to break the spawn's hold and rallied his companions. The Nightbringer answered with Harm against Ceril, sending black energy from its wings. Ceril resisted the worst of that draining magic and maintained his summoned crab.

Ceril first imagined tearing open a tunnel above the train, but learned that there was no tunnel: its roof stood directly beneath the open sky. He changed his plan to opening more of that roof. After using Misty Step to reach the front, he prepared to cast from outside.

The Magen conductor mistook his arrival for relief and leapt out ahead of the train. It vanished beneath the moving cars. Its survival was not established.

Meanwhile, Domyx fought while still grappled, and another bite further drained him. The Nightbringer cast Dominate Person on Red Caesar and took control of his will. Red Caesar nevertheless continued maintaining the protection on Domyx; no commanded attack on a companion had yet occurred.

Ceril took his starry dragon aspect and flew outside the front. He read his Call Lightning scroll, releasing the crab as he changed the magic he was sustaining. Lightning tore a larger opening through the roof, bathing the Nightbringer in sunlight and suppressing its regeneration more thoroughly.

Vokenar struck the Nightbringer with the Moon Blade. The blow broke its hold over Red Caesar before Red Caesar could be forced to attack him. Freed, Red Caesar turned his own attack against the vampire.

#### Dragged beneath the cars

Domyx used a forceful glaive strike to throw the spawn grappling him off the train. The spawn pulled him down with it.

He caught at the side but failed to hold on, then was dragged beneath the moving cars. Battered by the train's underside, he reached for successive gaps until he found a grip near the caboose. He hauled himself back aboard, badly hurt but conscious.

The Nightbringer had remained in the front car. With the spawn gone, Domyx began running back through the train toward his companions, while their fight against the lord and its remaining familiar continued.

### Chunk 0003

[[chunks/session_030/chunk_0003|Source transcript]]

#### The vampire's defeat

Ceril brought further lightning down on the Nightbringer and its last familiar, killing the servant. Vokenar and Red Caesar continued fighting the lord under the sunlight pouring through the damaged roof.

As Domyx passed the car with the portal paintings, he sensed that something had changed. He was too intent on returning to the battle to determine what was wrong. He drank his last newly brewed healing potion and continued forward.

The vampire kept attacking, but the sunlight prevented it from restoring what the companions took from it. Ceril's lightning, Vokenar's weapon, and Red Caesar's repeated shocks brought it close to disintegration.

Domyx finally reached the front. His last glaive attack destroyed the Nightbringer. It broke apart into pale feathers, which blackened and drifted out through the hole in the ceiling.

The companions regrouped beside the final lamplighter. Their path to the controls was clear.

#### The fourth switch and the broken paintings

Red Caesar wanted to time the power diversion so that the train would be near where they had entered, anticipating that flipping the switch might stop it. He studied the controls and found that he could accelerate it considerably or bring it to a rapid halt.

He increased its speed. Ceril added a jolt of lightning to the electrical supply, and the train tore around its ocean loop, throwing high sheets of water from either side of the front. The companions enjoyed the extraordinary ride despite the danger they had just escaped.

When Red Caesar judged that they had returned near the starting point, he flipped the final switch. Power was diverted toward the midway. All four lamplighters had now been used, and Jack Harvey's sealed door was unlocked elsewhere in the park.

The party returned to the paintings to go back. Ceril tried to pass through the midway's image and found only its ordinary surface beneath his hands. The other paintings had also lost their portal magic.

Red Caesar and Ceril examined the change. The underlying circle markings remained usable as destinations for teleportation magic, but the enchantments that had let visitors step through the paintings were gone. They concluded that someone had used dispelling magic stronger than their own, then departed by casting Teleportation Circle.

That was their reconstruction of the sabotage, not an act they had witnessed. They did not yet identify the intruder. Domyx's earlier unease suggested that the change had occurred while they were occupied farther up the train.

Red Caesar could restore their route with Teleportation Circle after recovering his magic. He could not cast it immediately. The opened vault was therefore accessible elsewhere while the party was stranded far away on Hole on Wheels.

#### A forced rest and further growth

The four companions accepted that they would have to rest aboard the train overnight. They settled it to a gentler pace and used the motion as a background to sleep.

The long rest restored Domyx's drained vitality. The four advanced to their twelfth level. Domyx chose Durable, strengthening his ability to recover from serious injury and fitting his repeated tests of endurance. Ceril improved his agility. Vokenar strengthened his divine judgment and his capacity to protect others with warding flares.

Their recovery solved the immediate shortage of magic, but the delay gave whoever had interfered with the paintings time to act near the opened door.

#### Intruders in Heaven's Bulb

During the rest, Red Caesar entered a recurring dream of Heaven's Bulb. This time it was unusually vivid: familiar passageways came with the remembered irritation of summer flies against his skin. More strangely, he appeared as his present, experienced self rather than reliving the scene as an apprentice.

Nearby, a younger Red Caesar spoke with Master Lee. Master Lee directed the apprentice's attention to the man he would become. The two versions met, and Red Caesar recognized that this was more than an ordinary dream.

He understood that magic had shaped his memories into the surrounding space, and that someone else had entered it. Before he could conceal himself, [[Emperor Shen]] and [[Vizier Jade]] emerged from a passage.

Emperor Shen praised Vizier Jade's idea and the power her magic had brought to his Empire. He initially seemed unsure whether the present Red Caesar was a conscious visitor or merely part of the memory.

Speaking within Red Caesar's hearing, Emperor Shen said that the party's actions had granted access to the Penumbra. The Empire did not yet know where to find it and needed to search while the companions were incapacitated. The conversation revealed an intention to exploit their enforced delay; it did not establish that Imperial forces had already claimed the vault.

Vizier Jade warned that Red Caesar might remember the intrusion. His alarm showed despite his attempt to appear calm, and she recognized him as aware within his own dream.

The intruders considered probing the memory by attacking the younger Red Caesar and Master Lee. When Emperor Shen drew his katana and moved toward them, Red Caesar stepped between the Emperor and those figures. Emperor Shen held the blade at his neck, and Red Caesar challenged him to act.

Vizier Jade discouraged a pointless confrontation inside the memory and directed attention toward more recent events. Emperor Shen then invoked leverage in the real world: [[Obould]], the captive captain, would not live to see another dawn.

Red Caesar growled at the threat. Vizier Jade and Emperor Shen departed to continue their search through his memories. Obould's execution was threatened, not shown, and the extent of what they learned remained unknown.

#### Completing Obvolvo Caelum

With the intruders gone, Master Lee asked Red Caesar to explain what had happened. Red Caesar wanted to warn and protect the figures in his memory, but he was also searching for an explanation himself.

He cast True Seeing on his younger self. The dream was real enough for casting to expend his actual magic. Moving electrical sparks appeared in the whites of the apprentice's eyes.

The younger Red Caesar could now perceive the present wizard's thoughts, spells, and unfinished research. He saw something the older researcher had overlooked: the physical fractures in the material itself.

A distant crack in Heaven's Bulb's false ceiling drew his attention. The younger self had watched that crack before, wondering whether the elements were breaking into the hidden conclave. Looking at it with the present wizard's knowledge, he identified Mending as the missing component of the Penumbra work.

Red Caesar had already brought together Otiluke's Resilient Sphere, reducing magic, and his research into converting [[Akasha]] to Penumbra. Mending supplied a way to address the smallest physical breaks and smooth the change between liquid and solid states.

The two versions completed [[Obvolvo Caelum]]. The new spell could condense Penumbra instantly at any size, making it portable for the eventual task of returning it to the sky. Its principle also promised to improve the existing Akasha-to-Penumbra synthesis. Red Caesar understood it so thoroughly that casting it felt as simple as an ordinary, practiced gesture.

No sky was repaired and no Penumbra was transported within the dream. The achievement was the completed, lasting magic and his knowledge of how to use it.

Red Caesar began waking, wondering which other memories Vizier Jade and Emperor Shen had entered. The party still needed to leave the train, reach the opened vault, and respond to the threat against Obould.

### Summary

- The fourth required King of the Hole round ended with both battlefield groups surviving repeated petrification threats. Ceril spent his feather-fall potion to descend from the lighthouse and help Domyx.
- The party accepted and won the optional hilltop defense. Domyx rescued Ceril through Cloud Jaunt and pulled Vokenar from the final Shambling Mound.
- Domyx received the Hopping Mad Sash, and the arena's power lit the third gate lamp.
- Tango reported that Kerben and the Opal had repelled an Imperial attack, with a larger force approaching.
- The Magen guide said Jack Harvey had retired from stewardship and entrusted the park to his nonmortal benefactor, Farraday.
- On Hole on Wheels, the companions collected preserved tea, a healing scroll, a travelogue, and a framed midway rendering.
- They fought through the train's supernatural occupants. Sunlight suppressed the Nightbringer's regeneration; Domyx survived being dragged beneath the cars by a spawn and returned to finish the lord.
- Red Caesar flipped the fourth lamplighter, unlocking Jack Harvey's door. The party then discovered that someone had dispelled the train's return portals.
- A long rest restored the companions, including Domyx's drained vitality, but left the opened vault exposed during their delay.
- Vizier Jade and Emperor Shen invaded Red Caesar's memories to search for the newly accessible Penumbra. Emperor Shen threatened Obould's life.
- True Seeing on Red Caesar's younger dream-self revealed Mending as the missing principle in his research, completing Obvolvo Caelum.

The final attractions advanced the party's objective while increasing the danger around it. The arena rewarded cooperation after repeatedly forcing separation, and its optional finale let Domyx turn his mobility into direct rescues. The train offered another coordinated fight, with Ceril's summons, Vokenar's protective magic, and Red Caesar's wards helping them reach the controls despite dwindling resources. Opening the roof and windows made sunlight an ally against the vampire lord.

Those victories did not secure Jack Harvey's hoard. Tango's account placed the Opal under growing Imperial pressure, while the train's disabled paintings separated the adventurers from the door they had just opened. Their enforced rest restored their strength and marked further growth, but someone else could exploit the interval. The saboteur's identity and actions at the vault remained unsettled.

Red Caesar's dream gave that delay both danger and consequence. Emperor Shen and Vizier Jade exposed their search and a direct threat to Obould, then moved through memories Red Caesar could not fully protect. In the same invaded space, his earlier and present selves solved the practical problem his research had missed. Obvolvo Caelum gave the party a way to carry Penumbra at the scale their mission demanded. The session ended with that breakthrough learned, the train still their immediate obstacle, and the newly accessible vault awaiting them elsewhere.
